Joy has a healing power that no prescription can match. A hopeful heart, laughter with friends, and a reason to get up in the morning can steady the body and calm the mind in ways medicine alone cannot. This does not mean happiness replaces treatment, but that our inner state shapes how we recover and endure. When we are happy, we sleep better, we eat better, and we face hard days with more strength. Guarding your happiness is therefore not selfish; it is a form of care for yourself and for everyone who depends on you.
