Lennon asks us to change what we count. Years pass whether we enjoy them or not, and tears are part of every life, but neither measures how well we have lived. Friends do. Smiles do. A life rich in friendship and laughter is a full life, whatever the calendar says. The quote is a gentle warning against keeping score the wrong way — chasing birthdays, achievements, or possessions while neglecting the people and moments that actually make us glad to be alive. Measure your life by warmth and connection instead, and you will find you are richer than you thought.
