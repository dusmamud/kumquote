This line treats happiness as something earned rather than something chased. When you live by the standards you truly believe in, a quiet satisfaction follows naturally, because your actions and your values finally agree. The reward is not applause or wealth, but the calm of knowing you did what was right by your own measure. That is why the highest right matters: a lesser standard brings a lesser peace. Happiness, in this view, is the interest paid on a life lived honestly, day after day, choice after choice.
