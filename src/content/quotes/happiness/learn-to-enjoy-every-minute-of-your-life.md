Nightingale warns against postponing joy until some future milestone arrives. The promotion, the house, or the perfect moment may never come, and life keeps passing while we wait. Happiness is available now, in ordinary minutes, if we pay attention to them. Being happy now is not naivety; it is a skill. It means noticing what is already good instead of fixating on what is missing. The future will bring its own moments, but they will be enjoyed only by someone who has practised enjoying the present.
