Disney reminds us that happiness depends less on circumstances than on perspective. Two people can live through the same day and feel it entirely differently, because each one interprets it in their own way. When we wait for the world to make us happy, we hand our mood over to luck. When we treat happiness as a way of seeing, we reclaim some control. Hardship is real, and this is no call to pretend otherwise. It simply says that attention, gratitude, and interpretation shape how life feels, and a hopeful outlook can be practised like any habit.
