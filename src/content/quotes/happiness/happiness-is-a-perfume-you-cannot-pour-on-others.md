Kindness returns to the giver. Emerson's image of perfume captures how happiness spreads: when you lift someone else's spirits, some of the fragrance stays on you. Generosity is not a sacrifice of joy but a source of it. The act of encouraging another person, sharing good news, or offering help creates warmth on both sides. Selfish happiness is fragile because it depends on getting. Shared happiness is sturdy because it grows through giving. Pour joy on others freely, and you will find your own hands scented with it.
