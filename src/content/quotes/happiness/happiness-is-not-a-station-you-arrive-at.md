Runbeck warns against treating happiness as a destination that begins once life is finally sorted. If joy waits for the promotion, the house, or the perfect circumstances, it keeps moving one step ahead of us, and the travelling itself feels like a delay. But happiness can be a way of moving through the day: noticing, appreciating, and meeting people with warmth along the road. The journey is not the obstacle to a happy life; it is the happy life. How we travel matters more than when we arrive.
