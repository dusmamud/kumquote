Virgil praises understanding as the root of happiness. Fear often comes from not knowing why things happen; we imagine threats in the dark. Learning the causes of things, how nature works, why people act as they do, replaces fear with clarity. A person who understands is less easily shaken by rumour, superstition, or panic. Knowledge brings a calm steadiness that no wealth can buy. The happiest people are often those who have taken the time to understand their world instead of merely reacting to it.
