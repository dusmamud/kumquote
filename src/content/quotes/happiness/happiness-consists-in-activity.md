Happiness rarely arrives when we sit still and wait for it. Like a stream, it stays fresh only while it keeps moving, fed by new effort, new learning, and new connections with others. A stagnant pool may look calm, but it slowly turns stale, and so does a life without motion. Activity does not mean constant busyness; it means engagement, the quiet satisfaction of making, helping, and growing. When we throw ourselves into work we care about, joy follows naturally. The happiest days are usually the ones where we were too absorbed to notice how happy we were.
