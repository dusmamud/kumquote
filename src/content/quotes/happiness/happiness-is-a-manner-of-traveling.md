We treat happiness like a destination, a station we will reach once the job, the house, or the relationship is secured. Runbeck corrects this: happiness is the manner of traveling, the way we move through ordinary days. Two people can take the same road and have entirely different journeys, depending on attention, gratitude, and patience. If happiness is a way of traveling, it is available now, on this stretch of road. The destination matters less than how we carry ourselves along the way.
