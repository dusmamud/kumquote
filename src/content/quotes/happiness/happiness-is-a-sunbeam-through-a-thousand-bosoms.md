Porter pictures happiness as light that is never used up by sharing. A sunbeam can pass through a thousand hearts and still shine as brightly as before; in fact, when it meets a kindred heart, it reflects back brighter still. This answers the fear that giving joy away leaves us with less. Unlike money or time, happiness multiplies when it is passed along. A kind word, a shared laugh, or a moment of encouragement costs the giver nothing and leaves both people warmer. Joy, unlike most things, grows by being given away.
