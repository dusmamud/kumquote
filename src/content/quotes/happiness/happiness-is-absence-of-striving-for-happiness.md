The harder we chase happiness, the further it seems to run. Constantly measuring our mood and demanding joy from every moment turns life into a test we keep failing. Peace arrives when we stop striving and simply live: doing the work in front of us, enjoying a meal, listening to a friend. In those unforced moments, contentment slips in without announcement. This is not giving up on a good life; it is letting the good life happen. Release the demand to feel happy, and you may discover that happiness was waiting in the quiet space left behind.
