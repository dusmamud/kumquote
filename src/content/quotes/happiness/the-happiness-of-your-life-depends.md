Marcus Aurelius, writing as both emperor and Stoic, knew that outward fortune could not guarantee peace. What we think about, day after day, shapes how life feels far more than what happens to us. A mind trained on grievances finds reasons to suffer everywhere, while a mind trained on gratitude finds steadiness even in hard times. We cannot always choose our circumstances, but we can practise choosing our thoughts, noticing them, and gently steering them. This is slow work, but it is the only work that truly changes our experience.
