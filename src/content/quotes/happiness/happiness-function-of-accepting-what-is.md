Erhard reduces happiness to a single practice: accepting reality as it stands. Most misery comes from the gap between what is and what we insist should be, a gap we widen every time we argue with facts. Acceptance closes that gap at once, not by giving up but by starting from the truth. From there, change is still possible, but it begins with clear eyes. When what is becomes enough for now, the mind stops fighting and happiness has a place to stand.
