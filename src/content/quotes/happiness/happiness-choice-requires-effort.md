There are days when happiness comes easily, and days when it does not come at all. Choosing happiness in such moments means doing something small and deliberate: going for a walk, calling a friend, or refusing to replay a grievance. Effort does not mean pretending everything is fine. It means taking responsibility for the direction of our attention. Happiness is not always effortless, but it is often available to those willing to reach for it, even when reaching feels difficult.
