Happiness is not a single ingredient but a way of living fully. Feeling deeply means letting joy and sorrow both reach us, instead of numbing ourselves to life. Enjoying simply means finding pleasure in a meal, a walk, or a conversation without needing more. Thinking freely keeps us honest, and risking life means daring to try, to love, and to fail. Finally, to be needed gives our days weight; we matter to someone. Together these capacities form a rich life. Happiness follows not from avoiding pain, but from embracing life completely, with an open heart and an engaged mind.
