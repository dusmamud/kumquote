Joy and sorrow are often treated as opposites, but Gibran sees them as two sides of the same heart. The very depths that sorrow carved out are what allow joy to fill us so fully. A person who has never grieved deeply may never know how high joy can rise. Our hardest experiences widen us, giving happiness more room to live. This does not make suffering welcome, but it gives it meaning. When joy comes, it often arrives through the same doorway sorrow once used, and the heart that has felt both is the richer for it.
