Rowling refuses the idea that dark times cancel joy entirely. Darkness in this line is not a lack of feeling but a lack of attention: the light is already there, waiting for a small deliberate act. Turning it on can be as simple as calling a friend, stepping outside, or remembering something true about yourself. Happiness here is not loud celebration but a quiet refusal to let the hardest season have the last word. Even a small light changes a room, and one remembered kindness can change a day.
