We spend our lives trying to change our circumstances, convinced that a new job, a new city, or more money will finally make us happy. Carnegie says the real lever is elsewhere: our thoughts about what we have. The same situation can make one person miserable and another content, depending entirely on the story each tells about it. Circumstances matter, but they are not the deciding factor. When we change the way we think about our lives, our lives change with it. Happiness, in the end, is an inside job.
