Waiting for happiness to arrive on its own rarely works. We can think, plan, and hope for a better life, yet nothing changes until we act. Action does not guarantee happiness, because effort can fail and plans can go wrong. But without action, happiness has no soil to grow in. Doing something, even a small step, creates movement, learning, and possibility. It breaks the stillness of worry and opens doors we could not see from the chair. James reminds us that happiness is not passive. It is built through trying, making, helping, and moving, one honest action at a time.
