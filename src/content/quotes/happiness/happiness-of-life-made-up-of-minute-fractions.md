A happy life is not built from rare, dramatic events but from tiny moments that pass almost unnoticed: a kiss, a smile, a kind word, a sincere compliment. Each one is small on its own, yet added together they form the warmth of a whole life. We forget these fractions quickly, which is why we must learn to notice them while they happen. Give a kind look to a stranger, thank someone properly, or say the compliment you were thinking. These small charities cost nothing and return more than they give, quietly composing the happiness we remember.
