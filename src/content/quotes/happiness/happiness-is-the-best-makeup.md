Barrymore says that joy changes how a person looks more than any product can. A happy face carries light in the eyes, ease in the smile, and a looseness that no cosmetic can copy. The point is not about appearance for its own sake but about what shows on the outside when the inside is well. People notice warmth before they notice features. The practical advice is simple: take care of your inner life first, and the outer life will follow, brighter and more natural than any disguise.
