Goethe pictures happiness as something that never stays still, rolling away just as we reach for it. The chase itself becomes the point. We imagine happiness waiting in the next job, the next city, the next achievement, yet each time we arrive, it has moved on. This does not mean the pursuit is foolish. Running after the ball keeps us growing, learning, and reaching. The wisdom lies in enjoying the run instead of resenting the distance. If we stop demanding that happiness sit still and let us catch it, we can find joy in the motion itself, in the effort, the hope, and the small moments gathered along the way.
