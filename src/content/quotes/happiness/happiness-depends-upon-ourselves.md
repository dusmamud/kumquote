Aristotle places happiness in our own hands, not in luck, wealth, or the approval of others. If happiness depends on us, then it is something we build through the choices we make each day: how we respond to setbacks, what we value, and the habits we practise. This is both demanding and liberating, because it removes the excuse that happiness is out of reach. It does not promise that life will be easy, only that our inner state is ours to shape. We can begin today by choosing gratitude, patience, and purpose over complaint and comparison.
