Roy T. Bennett warns against treating happiness as a reward for later. We keep telling ourselves we will be happy after the promotion, after the move, after the hard season passes — but the finish line keeps moving, and the present keeps slipping away unused. Happiness postponed is happiness denied, because the future arrives only ever as another present moment. The invitation here is not to ignore real problems, but to stop making joy conditional on everything being perfect. If happiness must wait for ideal conditions, it will never come. Now is the only time it can happen.
