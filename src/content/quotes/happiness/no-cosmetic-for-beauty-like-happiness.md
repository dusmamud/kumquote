Beauty is often treated as something applied from the outside, a matter of creams, clothes, and careful styling. Yet none of those can match the radiance of a genuinely happy person. Happiness softens the face, brightens the eyes, and gives movement a lightness that no product can imitate. It is visible to others before a word is spoken. The most attractive quality a person can carry is contentment, and unlike cosmetics, it costs nothing and never wears off by the end of the day.
