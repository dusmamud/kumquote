Lincoln cuts through every excuse we make about happiness with a blunt truth: it is largely a decision. Our circumstances vary, and some lives carry heavier burdens than others, yet within any situation there is room to choose our stance toward it. People who decide to be happy find reasons to smile; people who decide to be miserable find reasons to complain. The mind follows the direction we give it. Making up your mind to be happy is not denial — it is a quiet act of strength, choosing light even when the world offers plenty of reasons not to.
