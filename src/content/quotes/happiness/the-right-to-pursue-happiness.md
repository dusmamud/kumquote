Franklin reminds us that no law or government can hand us happiness. The right to pursue it is guaranteed, but the catching is our own work. Happiness is not delivered; it is built through choices, effort, and attention. Waiting for someone else to supply it leaves us disappointed. The quote carries a practical challenge: stop expecting the world to make you happy and start doing the things that create happiness. Pursue meaningful work, nurture relationships, and practise gratitude. The chase itself, done well, becomes the reward.
