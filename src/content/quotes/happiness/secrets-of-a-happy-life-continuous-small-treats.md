Murdoch finds happiness in the small and the repeatable rather than the grand and the rare. A happy life, in her view, is not built from milestones alone but from a steady rhythm of little kindnesses we offer ourselves: a favourite tea, a walk, ten quiet minutes with a book. These treats are not indulgence; they are maintenance, the way we keep our spirits fed between larger joys. Waiting for one big event to rescue us leaves most days empty. Small pleasures, taken regularly, quietly add up to a life that feels good to live.
