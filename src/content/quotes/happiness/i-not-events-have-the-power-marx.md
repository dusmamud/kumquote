Events will do what they do: traffic jams, bad news, and rude strangers are beyond our control, but our response to them is not. By claiming that power first thing in the day, we refuse to hand our mood to circumstance and remind ourselves that attitude is a choice we renew daily. This is not denial of hardship but a practical kind of freedom. We cannot choose every event, yet we can choose whether it rules us. Starting the day with that ownership sets the tone for everything that follows.
