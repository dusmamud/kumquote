We often treat happiness like a destination we will reach once the promotion lands, the house is bought, or the problems end. But every arrival only reveals a new horizon, and the chase never truly ends. Harris reminds us that happiness is a direction we walk, not a place we arrive. It lives in the daily choices: how we speak to people, what we notice, and whether we keep moving toward what matters. A traveler heading the right way can be content long before arriving. Choose the direction each morning, and the journey itself becomes the reward.
