Much of our unhappiness begins with comparison. We watch other people's careers, homes, and highlight reels, then quietly decide our own lives fall short. Caring what others think is natural, but when their opinions start running our choices, life becomes a performance instead of a life. Happiness grows when attention turns inward, toward what we truly value and what we are trying to build. That does not mean ignoring others or living selfishly. It means refusing to let applause or judgment decide our worth, and choosing instead to live by honest standards of our own.
