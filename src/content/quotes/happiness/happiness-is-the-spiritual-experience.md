Waitley describes happiness not as a mood that visits us, but as a spiritual practice measured minute by minute. Love, grace, and gratitude are not grand gestures reserved for special days; they are small choices made constantly, in how we greet people, forgive mistakes, and notice what is good. A minute lived with love is a happy minute, whatever else the day holds. This view makes happiness available now, not after some future achievement. It asks nothing expensive or rare, only attention and an open heart. Live enough minutes this way, and a happy life quietly assembles itself from them.
