Habits shape our days far more than grand decisions do. A person who practices noticing good things, smiling at strangers, and counting blessings will slowly become happier, almost without trying. Happiness, like any habit, grows stronger with repetition and fades when neglected. We do not need a perfect life to begin; we need only a small daily practice, repeated with patience. Over months and years, these tiny choices compound into a temperament. The happiest people are rarely those with the easiest lives, but those who have trained themselves, day by day, to meet life with gladness.
