We tell ourselves we will be happy after the promotion, the move, or the holiday, and in doing so we mortgage the present to an imagined future. But life is lived minute by minute, and the minutes we spend waiting are gone for good. Enjoying each minute does not mean ignoring problems; it means refusing to let the future steal today. Happiness is available now, in small things, if we practice noticing them. The time to be happy is not someday but this very moment, while it is still ours.
