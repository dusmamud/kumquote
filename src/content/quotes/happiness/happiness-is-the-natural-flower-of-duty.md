Duty sounds like a burden, yet it often blooms into joy. When we do what we ought to do, keeping a promise, caring for family, doing honest work, we earn a quiet satisfaction no indulgence can match. Happiness that comes from duty is sturdy, because it rests on having lived well, not on luck or mood. Like a flower, it grows naturally from a healthy root: a life aligned with responsibility. Those who chase pleasure alone often find it wilting in their hands, while those who serve faithfully discover happiness growing beside them, unforced and lasting.
