We often wait for happiness to happen to us, as if it were weather we cannot control. But happiness is usually a decision made in small moments: choosing gratitude over complaint, kindness over bitterness, presence over worry. Circumstances matter, yet two people in the same situation can live entirely different emotional lives, depending on what they choose to notice. Making happiness a conscious choice does not mean denying pain; it means refusing to let pain have the final word. Each morning offers a fresh decision, and each choice slowly builds a happier life.
