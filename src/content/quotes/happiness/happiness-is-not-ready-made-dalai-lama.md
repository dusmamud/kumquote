Most of us treat happiness like weather — something that happens to
us when conditions improve: a better job, a bigger house, a quieter
year. The Dalai Lama reverses the order. Happiness is not delivered
by circumstances. It is produced, slowly, by what you do with the
circumstances you already have.

That makes the quote useful on an ordinary Tuesday, not just on a
retreat. Ask what action today would make you a little happier by
evening — a walk, a call to a friend, one kind thing done without
being asked — and do it. Nobody will hand you a ready-made happy
life. But a life with a few deliberate happy actions in it, repeated
daily, starts to look a lot like one.
