Wilde's wit carries a serious observation: people leave happiness behind them or take it with them, depending on who they are. Some brighten every room they enter, and their absence is felt as a loss. Others drain a room, and the mood lifts only when they leave. The difference is rarely talent or wealth; it is generosity of spirit, the habit of listening, and the refusal to make everything about oneself. We each decide, daily, which kind of person we will be to the people around us.
