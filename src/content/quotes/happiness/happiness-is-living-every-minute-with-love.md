Waitley describes happiness as something lived minute by minute, not stored up for later. Love, grace, and gratitude are not grand achievements; they are small choices repeated through an ordinary day. To live with love is to pay attention to people. To live with grace is to meet difficulty without bitterness. To live with gratitude is to notice what is already good. Happiness does not arrive after everything is fixed. It grows inside the present moment when we meet it openly. Every minute offers the materials — we only have to use them.
