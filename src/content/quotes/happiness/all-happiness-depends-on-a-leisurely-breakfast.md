John Gunther finds happiness in an unhurried morning, and the joke carries a serious point. A leisurely breakfast means the day has not yet seized you — no rushing, no alarms, no inbox. It is a small daily proof that time can belong to us, not only to our obligations. Happiness rarely arrives as a grand event; it hides in ordinary moments we refuse to hurry through. The way a day begins often decides how it feels, and a slow start is a quiet act of self-respect. Protect the morning, and the rest of the day often follows.
