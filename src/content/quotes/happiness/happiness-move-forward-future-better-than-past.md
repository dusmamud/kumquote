Ziglar ties happiness to hope rather than to present comfort. The future does not need to be perfect; it only needs to hold the promise of improvement. That belief gives strength in hard times, because today's trouble is read as a step rather than a sentence. Moving forward matters more than arriving, since every step taken in hope makes the next one easier. Happiness, then, is less about where you stand and more about the direction you face, and the confidence that better days are ahead.
