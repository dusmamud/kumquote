Ford connects happiness to accomplishment, not to comfort or leisure. A life of ease without effort grows empty, while even difficult work satisfies when it produces something real. Accomplishment need not be grand — finishing a task, helping a neighbor, learning a skill all count. The realization matters: noticing that our effort made a difference. Happiness grows from the sense that our days add up to something. Work done well, with purpose, is one of the most reliable sources of lasting contentment.
