Two people can live through the same rainy week and tell entirely different stories about it. One sees ruined plans, while the other sees a good excuse for soup, books, and rest. Downs is right: circumstances matter far less than the attitudes we bring to them. Attitudes are not fixed traits; they are habits we can choose and strengthen. Gratitude, humor, and patience can be practiced like any craft. We may not control the weather of our lives, but we always control the windows we open to it. Choose the attitude, and the same life feels better.
