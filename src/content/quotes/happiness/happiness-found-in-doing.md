We tend to believe happiness waits inside the next purchase, the next promotion, or the next possession, yet the thrill of owning things fades quickly. Doing is different. Possessions sit still, but action moves us forward and gives our days shape. The quote reminds us that happiness is less like a trophy on a shelf and more like a rhythm we enter. We find it not by gathering more, but by engaging fully with the life already in front of us.
