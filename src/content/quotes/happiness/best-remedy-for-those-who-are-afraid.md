Anne Frank wrote this while confined indoors, which gives the advice its weight. She had learned that four walls can shrink a mind until fear and loneliness fill every corner. Going outside breaks that spell: the sky is larger than any worry, and nature asks nothing of us while offering calm in return. A walk does not solve every problem, but it changes the scale of them. Fresh air, open space, and the simple rhythm of moving remind us that the world is wider than our troubles, and that we belong to it.
