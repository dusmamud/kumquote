Aristotle places happiness at the centre of human life, not as a pleasant extra but as its very point. Everything we pursue — wealth, honour, friendship, knowledge — we pursue because we believe it will make our lives good. Happiness is the final goal behind every other goal, the one thing we want for its own sake. This does not mean chasing pleasure moment by moment; it means living well, acting with purpose, and growing into the best version of ourselves. A life aimed at happiness is a life aimed at meaning, not merely at comfort.
