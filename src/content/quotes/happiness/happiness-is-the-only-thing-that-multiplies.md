Most good things diminish when divided: money spent is money gone, and time given is time lost. Schweitzer points out that happiness breaks this rule. Shared joy does not shrink; it grows, because the happiness we give returns to us in the other person's smile, and in the bond that forms between us. Hoarded happiness, by contrast, fades quietly, unused. Generosity with our gladness — celebrating others, spreading encouragement, offering warmth — costs nothing and creates more of what it spends. This is a rare and generous arithmetic: the more happiness we give away, the more we find ourselves holding.
