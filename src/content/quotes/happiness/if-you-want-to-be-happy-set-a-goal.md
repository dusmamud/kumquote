Andrew Carnegie links happiness to having a goal worthy of our whole selves. A goal that commands our thoughts gives the mind a centre, so worries lose their grip. One that liberates our energy turns restlessness into momentum, because effort finally has somewhere to go. And one that inspires our hopes gives the future a shape worth walking toward. Happiness, in this view, is not found in comfort or leisure, but in purposeful striving. Choose a goal big enough to deserve you, and happiness follows as a by-product of the chase.
