Emerson captures a lovely truth: kindness returns to the giver. When we pour happiness onto others — a compliment, a helping hand, a moment of attention — some of it always lands on us too. Generosity is not a sacrifice of our own joy but a way of multiplying it. The image of perfume is perfect, because perfume cannot be shared without scenting the hand that gives it. If you want more happiness in your life, the shortest route may be to give some away first.
