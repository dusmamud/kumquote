Bennett puts happiness back where we can reach it. Circumstances matter, but two people can meet the same day and live it completely differently, and the difference is usually the lens they bring. A mindset is not pretending that hard things are easy; it is choosing what to notice, what to make of setbacks, and how to speak to yourself while you face them. Attitude is a daily practice, not a lucky trait. When we tend it, ordinary days start to feel lighter, and even difficult ones leave room for gratitude and hope.
