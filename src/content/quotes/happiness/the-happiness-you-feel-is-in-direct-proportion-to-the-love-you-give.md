Oprah Winfrey describes happiness as a simple proportion: the more love we give, the more happiness we feel. Love kept locked away does nothing for anyone, least of all for the person holding it back. But love given freely — attention, kindness, encouragement, time — returns to the giver as warmth and purpose. This is why generous people so often seem the happiest: they have discovered that happiness is not a prize to be won, but a harvest from what we plant in others. Give love away, and there is suddenly more of it.
