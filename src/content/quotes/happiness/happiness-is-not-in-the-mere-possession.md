Money can buy comfort, but it cannot buy the feeling that comes from making something real. Roosevelt points to a deeper source of happiness: the satisfaction of effort, growth, and achievement. When we set a goal, work hard for it, and finally reach it, the joy belongs to us in a way no purchase ever could. Possessions fade and lose their shine, but the pride of having created, solved, or overcome something stays with us. That is why the happiest people are often the busiest builders of their own lives.
