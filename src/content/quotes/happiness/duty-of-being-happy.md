Happiness is often treated as a lucky accident, something that happens to us when circumstances finally align. Stevenson turns that idea on its head, calling happiness a duty we owe ourselves and others. It is a responsibility, not a reward. When we choose to be cheerful, we bring lightness into our rooms, our work, and our relationships. A happy person lifts everyone nearby. This does not mean denying sadness, but refusing to let gloom become a habit. Joy, practiced deliberately, becomes a quiet gift we give to the world around us every single day.
