Keller reminds us that happiness is not a prize waiting in the next job, the next purchase, or someone else's approval. Outside things can please us for a while, but they shift and fade, and chasing them leaves us always reaching for the next thing. Real happiness grows from how we meet life: the gratitude we practise, the meaning we find in small moments, and the peace we build inside ourselves. When the source is within, it travels with us through hard days as well as good ones.
