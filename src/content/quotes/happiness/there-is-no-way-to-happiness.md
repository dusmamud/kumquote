We often treat happiness as a destination that lies beyond the next promotion, purchase, or milestone. Thich Nhat Hanh gently corrects this: the path itself must contain happiness, or we will never arrive. If we rush through our days in a state of stress and resentment, no future reward will suddenly make us content. Peace has to be practised now, in ordinary moments — a meal eaten slowly, a walk taken without hurry, a conversation given full attention. When the way we live is already kind and calm, happiness is not delayed.
