A difficult childhood does not have to define an entire life. This quote argues that joy is not locked in the past; it can be recovered at any age. An adult can still learn to play, to wonder, and to laugh without apology. Healing old wounds means refusing to let early hardship write the ending of your story. The past explains where you started, but it need not decide where you finish. By choosing curiosity and lightness now, you reclaim the freedom that childhood represents. It is never too late to begin again.
