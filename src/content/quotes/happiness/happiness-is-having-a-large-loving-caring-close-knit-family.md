Burns delivers his wisdom with a wink: family is a deep source of happiness, and so is a little distance. Love is easier to enjoy when it is not crowded by daily friction, and absence has a way of sharpening affection. The joke lands because it holds a real truth — closeness needs breathing room, and the people we love most can also be the ones who test our patience. Happiness in family life is often a matter of balance: near enough to care, far enough to stay fond.
