Effort here does not mean frantic striving or constant self-improvement. It means the quiet, daily work of showing up: keeping promises to ourselves, tending relationships, learning from mistakes, and choosing habits that support a good life. None of this guarantees happiness, and luck still plays its part. Yet people who invest in their own growth tend to find that gladness arrives as a by-product, not as a gift. The consequence follows the effort, the way a harvest follows the planting.
