Dior links beauty to the inner life rather than to appearance alone. A face lit by genuine happiness carries a warmth that no cosmetic can copy, while unhappiness can dull even the finest features. The quote suggests that beauty is not something we put on but something that shines out when we feel at ease with ourselves. That is why the same person can look radiant one day and tired the next. Happiness, in this view, is the real source of glow. Looking after our inner state is therefore not vanity; it is the deepest form of self-care.
