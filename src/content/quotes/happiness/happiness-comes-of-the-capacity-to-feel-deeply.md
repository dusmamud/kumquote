Storm Jameson lists happiness as a set of capacities, not circumstances. It grows from feeling deeply instead of numbing ourselves, from enjoying simple things instead of always wanting more, from thinking freely instead of borrowing opinions, and from risking something that matters. The last item is the most telling: to be needed. Happiness is not only about receiving; it comes from knowing someone counts on us. A life that is felt, simple, free, brave, and useful leaves little room for emptiness. Happiness, in this view, is something we practice.
