Most people chase happiness by rearranging their circumstances, hoping the next job, city, or achievement will finally make them glad. Yet the secret lies closer to home: learning to like what is already in front of us. Liking what we do transforms ordinary work into something meaningful, because attention and care change the experience itself. Happiness does not wait at the end of a long road; it grows in the daily practice of finding value in the task at hand.
