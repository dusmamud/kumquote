Happiness rarely arrives through the front door we keep watching. We plan for it, chase it, and set conditions for it, yet it slips in sideways, through ordinary moments we never thought to guard. A conversation, an unexpected kindness, a quiet morning — these are the open doors. Barrymore's image reminds us that happiness favors the receptive rather than the striving. Leave room for surprise. Stay open to people, to detours, to unplanned joy, and happiness will find its way in when you least expect it.
