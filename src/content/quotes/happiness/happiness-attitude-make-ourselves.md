Misery is not the easy option, even though it can feel like the default. Complaining, worrying, and resenting all take energy, and they quietly drain the very strength we need. The quote is not claiming that pain can be wished away. It is pointing out that our inner stance is a real choice, and that both attitudes cost us something. Since the work is equal either way, we might as well invest it in the version of ourselves that feels strong.
