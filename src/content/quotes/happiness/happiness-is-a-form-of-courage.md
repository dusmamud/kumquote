Jackson's short sentence reframes happiness as something brave rather than something easy. It takes courage to be happy when the world gives ample reasons for gloom — to hope after disappointment, to laugh after loss, to trust again after being hurt. Cynicism is the safe posture; it asks nothing of us. Happiness asks us to stay open, to risk caring, to believe that good is still possible. Choosing joy in difficult times is not naivety. It is one of the bravest acts a person can perform.
