Martha Washington states a hard-won truth: our happiness depends far less on what happens to us than on how we meet it. Circumstances change without asking, but our disposition — the habit of looking for what is workable, the choice to stay cheerful — is ours to keep. This is not pretending that hardship does not exist; it is refusing to let hardship have the last word. Determination turns happiness from a lucky accident into a practice, one that holds steady even when the situation does not.
