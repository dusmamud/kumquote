A life with nothing to look forward to feels flat, however comfortable, while even modest anticipation can brighten an ordinary day. Change itself need not be grand; a planned journey, a new project, or a coming reunion is enough to lift the spirit. This is why routines without prospect grow stale and why hope is a practical necessity rather than a luxury. Happiness, then, is partly the art of keeping a hopeful horizon in view, so that today is warmed by the promise of tomorrow.
