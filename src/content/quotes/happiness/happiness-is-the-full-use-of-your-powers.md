Kennedy defines happiness as excellence in action. It is not found in idleness or indulgence, but in using your abilities fully toward something worthwhile. When skill meets purpose, effort itself becomes satisfying. A person stretched by meaningful work feels alive in a way that comfort alone never provides. The quote challenges the idea that happiness is rest; instead, it is engagement. Find the work that calls on your best powers, give it your full attention, and happiness follows as the natural companion of a life well used.
