Chopra points to a simple source of unhappiness: the fight against what is already happening. Much of our suffering comes not from events themselves but from refusing to accept them, replaying them, and wishing them away. When resistance drops, the same day feels lighter, because energy once spent on struggle becomes available for living. Acceptance does not mean approval or passivity; it means meeting life as it arrives. In that openness, one good moment can follow another without interruption. That is happiness in its simplest form: moments allowed to arrive and pass without a fight.
