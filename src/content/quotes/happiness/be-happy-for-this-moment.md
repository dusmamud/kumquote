Khayyam reminds us that life is not lived in some distant future when everything finally falls into place; it is lived right now, in this ordinary moment. Waiting for perfect conditions only postpones joy, because conditions are never perfect. When we notice the present fully — the light, the people around us, the simple fact of being alive — happiness stops being a goal we chase and becomes something we already hold. This moment, with all its imperfections, is the whole of life.
