Happiness is contagious. A person who is genuinely content carries a lightness that spreads to everyone around them — in a smile, a patient reply, a willingness to help. You do not need grand gestures to lift others; your own mood sets the tone of the room. This gives happiness a purpose beyond ourselves: caring for your own joy is also a gift to your family, friends, and colleagues. When you nurture what makes you happy, you quietly give others permission to do the same. One happy person can change the atmosphere of a whole home.
