Happiness is not a prize waiting at the end of a long road, but something that grows out of how we spend our days. Aristotle reminds us that a good life is built through action: learning, creating, helping, and striving toward what matters. When we sit still and wait for joy to arrive, it rarely does. But when we engage fully with our work, our relationships, and our purpose, happiness follows naturally as a companion to effort. So do not chase happiness directly. Live actively, choose worthwhile pursuits, and let contentment emerge from a life well used.
