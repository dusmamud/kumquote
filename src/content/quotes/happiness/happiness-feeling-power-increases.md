Nietzsche describes happiness not as comfort but as the sensation of growing stronger. We feel most alive when we meet a difficulty and discover we can handle it, whether the difficulty is a hard task, a fear, or a habit we are trying to change. Comfort alone rarely satisfies us for long; it leaves us restless, because nothing is being tested or gained. The quote suggests that happiness arrives in the struggle itself, in the moment resistance gives way. That reframes hard times completely.
