Lynd exposes a quiet condition hiding inside our generosity. We say we want others to be happy, yet we often mean happy on our terms, following paths we understand and approve. Real care lets people choose their own shape of a good life, even when it looks strange to us. A parent, a partner, or a friend may find joy in places we would never look. Happiness shared on someone else's terms is still happiness. Letting go of our approval may be the kindest thing we can offer the people we love.
