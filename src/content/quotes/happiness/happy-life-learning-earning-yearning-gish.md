Gish packs a whole philosophy of happiness into three verbs. Learning keeps the mind alive and growing, so that curiosity never hardens into boredom. Earning gives dignity and independence, the quiet satisfaction of contributing something of value to the world. Yearning keeps the heart reaching forward, holding a dream or a hope that makes tomorrow worth waking for. Together they balance the mind, the hands, and the heart, leaving no part of life idle. A happy life, in this view, is not a destination but a rhythm of growth, work, and longing, renewed every day.
