The Dalai Lama links two things we usually keep apart: feeling well and being well. We chase health through food, sleep, and exercise, yet overlook how worry, anger, and resentment wear the body down day after day. A calm and kindly mind is not a luxury on top of health; it is part of health itself. Happiness lowers the strain we carry, helps us rest, and gives us energy for others. Caring for our inner state, then, is as practical as any medicine, and its benefits show in the body too.
