Way reminds us that happiness is not the absence of problems but a shift in attention. A perfect life does not exist; every life carries flaws, setbacks, and unfinished business. Waiting for everything to be fixed before allowing ourselves joy means waiting forever. Happiness begins when we decide to look beyond what is broken and notice what is still good, still working, still worth loving. This is not denial or pretending that difficulties do not matter. It is a deliberate choice of focus. We can hold our troubles honestly and still choose to let the light in through the cracks.
