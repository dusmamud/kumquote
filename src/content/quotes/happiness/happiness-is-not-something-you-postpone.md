Rohn warns against treating happiness as a reward for later. Many people tell themselves they will be happy after the promotion, the house, or the holiday, yet each milestone only moves the goalpost further away. Happiness postponed becomes happiness denied, because the future never quite arrives as imagined. Designing it for the present means building small joys into ordinary days: a walk, a conversation, a moment of gratitude. The present is the only time we actually live in. If happiness cannot be found there, no future version of life will supply it.
