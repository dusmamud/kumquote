Wealth that depends on getting more is never secure, because there is always something else to want. Cicero argues that true riches come from contentment with what we already have. A person who is satisfied with their life is rich in a way no fortune can match, and no market crash can take away. Contentment is not giving up ambition; it is refusing to let desire run the whole show. When we appreciate what is already ours, we stop chasing and start enjoying. That quiet satisfaction is the most durable wealth anyone can hold.
