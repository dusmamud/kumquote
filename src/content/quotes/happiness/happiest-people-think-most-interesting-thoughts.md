Happiness follows the mind's diet. People who fill their thoughts with interesting ideas, questions, and wonder rarely have time to dwell on petty grievances. A curious mind finds delight everywhere: in a book, a conversation, a walk through an unfamiliar street. Boredom, by contrast, breeds complaint, because an empty mind turns inward and magnifies every small discomfort. The happiest people are not necessarily the richest or the luckiest; they are the ones whose inner life is rich. Cultivate interesting thoughts, and happiness will quietly follow wherever you go.
