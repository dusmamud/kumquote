Thich Nhat Hanh insists that joy is not waiting in some distant future but is already present, hidden in plain sight. We miss it because our minds race ahead to the next task or backward into regret, leaving the present moment unattended. Attention is the key that unlocks it: a breath, a cup of tea, sunlight on a wall, all hold happiness for anyone willing to notice. This teaching reframes joy as a practice of seeing rather than a reward for achieving. When we slow down and truly arrive where we are, we find the present was full all along.
