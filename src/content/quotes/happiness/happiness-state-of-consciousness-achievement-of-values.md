Rand ties happiness to living by what we actually value. In her view, it is not a mood that strikes at random but the feeling that follows when our actions line up with our deepest commitments. That is why borrowed goals leave us empty even when we reach them: the achievement belongs to someone else's values, not ours. The question to ask is not how to feel happier in the abstract but what we truly care about and whether our days serve it. When effort and values point the same way, satisfaction follows naturally.
