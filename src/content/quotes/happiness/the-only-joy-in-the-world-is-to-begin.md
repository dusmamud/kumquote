Pavese's sentence is a small rebellion against waiting. We often imagine joy will arrive after the hard part — after the project launches, the money arrives. But the beginning itself is the joy: the first page, the first step, the moment an idea stops being a wish and starts being real. Beginnings carry a particular energy that no later stage can match, because everything is still possible. If you keep postponing the start, you postpone the joy too. Do not wait for the perfect moment to feel glad. Begin now, and let the joy of beginning carry you forward.
