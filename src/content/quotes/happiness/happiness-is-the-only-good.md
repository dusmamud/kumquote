Ingersoll refuses to postpone happiness to some future date. Not tomorrow, not after the next milestone, but now, and not somewhere else, but here. The formula he offers is strikingly simple: make others happy, and happiness follows. Kindness is not a detour from our own joy; it is the road itself. A compliment given honestly, a burden shared, or a door held open changes two moods at once. When we stop waiting for perfect conditions and start spreading a little warmth today, happiness stops being a distant prize and becomes something we practice daily.
