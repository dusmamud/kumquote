A destination always moves further away the closer we think we get, because each achievement soon feels ordinary. Treating happiness as a journey changes the question entirely. Instead of asking when we will finally be happy, we can ask whether today holds anything worth noticing. The walk itself becomes the point: the conversations, the small wins, the quiet mornings. We do not have to wait for life to be perfect before enjoying it. The road we are already on is where happiness lives.
