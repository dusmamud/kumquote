Helen Keller knew more than most about a world beyond sight and sound, and her words carry unusual weight because of it. The finest things in life — love, trust, wonder, belonging — have no shape we can hold and no price we can pay. They live in how we feel and how we connect with others. Keller invites us to stop measuring life by what we can see or own. True richness is felt, not counted, and it is available to anyone willing to notice it.
