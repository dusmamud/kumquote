Repplier reminds us that happiness cannot be imported from anywhere else. No place, no person, and no possession can deliver it to us ready-made. If we cannot find contentment within ourselves — in our thoughts, our work, our way of meeting the day — then changing the scenery will not help. The search for happiness elsewhere is endless because the destination keeps moving. But when we cultivate a quiet satisfaction inside, we carry it wherever we go. Happiness is an inner skill, practised daily, not a prize waiting in another city.
