Runbeck reframes happiness as a way of moving through life rather than a place to reach. If happiness is a destination, we will always be waiting for arrival, and every delay will feel like failure. But as a manner of traveling, it becomes a quality we carry into each day: how we notice, how we treat others, how we meet setbacks. This shift frees us from the tyranny of someday and lets us practice joy in the ordinary miles, here and now, wherever the journey leads.
