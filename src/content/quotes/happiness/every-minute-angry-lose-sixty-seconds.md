Anger feels justified in the moment, yet it charges us for the time it occupies. A minute spent resenting someone is a minute that cannot hold laughter, calm or gratitude. The loss is easy to miss. Minutes add up into hours and days of life spent rehearsing grievances. Letting go is not the same as approving what happened; it is choosing not to pay for it twice. We cannot control what provokes us, but we can decide how long we lend our attention to it. Guarding our happiness means spending minutes on what restores us.
