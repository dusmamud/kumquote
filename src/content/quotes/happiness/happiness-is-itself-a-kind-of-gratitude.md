Gratitude and happiness are not two separate things; they are two names for the same open-hearted way of seeing the world. When we notice what is already good in our lives — the people who care for us, the small comforts, the chances we have been given — joy follows on its own. An ungrateful mind can own everything and still feel empty, while a thankful mind can find richness in very little. The secret is attention. Happiness does not come from adding more to our lives, but from finally appreciating what is already there.
