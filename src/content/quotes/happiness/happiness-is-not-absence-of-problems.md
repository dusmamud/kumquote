Waiting for a problem-free life means waiting forever, because problems are part of every life. Happiness belongs instead to those who learn to face difficulties with steadiness and hope. Each problem handled well builds confidence, and confidence makes the next problem feel smaller. This is a skill, not a talent: break the trouble into steps, ask for help when needed, and keep moving. The happy person is not the one with no storms, but the one who has learned to sail through them. Strength grows every time you deal with a problem instead of running from it.
