This quote places happiness squarely in the realm of choice. We cannot control everything that happens to us, but we can decide what to carry and what to release. Holding on to every slight, every disappointment, and every fear makes the mind heavy; choosing to let go creates space for lighter things. It means welcoming what nourishes us and refusing what poisons us. When we remember that this power of choosing is always ours, happiness becomes less about perfect circumstances and more about the quiet, daily act of deciding what deserves a place in our lives.
