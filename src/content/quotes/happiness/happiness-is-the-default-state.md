Ravikant turns the usual search for happiness upside down. We tend to treat joy as something to earn by adding a better job, a bigger home, or more approval, yet each gain soon feels normal and the wanting starts again. He suggests happiness is not the reward at the end of that chase; it is what remains when the chase quiets down. The feeling that something is missing is often a habit of comparison, not a fact. Let go of the missing piece, and the present moment is enough.
