Most good things diminish when divided, but happiness breaks that rule. A joy shared with a friend does not halve; it grows, because delight multiplied by another person's delight becomes something larger than either felt alone. This is why celebrations feel empty in solitude and why a kind word can brighten two days at once. Generosity with happiness costs nothing and returns everything. When we share our gladness, we invite others into it, and their response deepens our own. Happiness hoarded slowly withers; happiness given away keeps doubling, endlessly.
