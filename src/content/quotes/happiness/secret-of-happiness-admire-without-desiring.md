Desire turns admiration into hunger. We see something beautiful — a life, a possession, a talent — and immediately want it for ourselves, which converts appreciation into envy. Sandburg's secret breaks that chain: admire without desiring. We can love a sunset without owning the sky, celebrate another's success without resenting it, enjoy beauty without possessing it. This frees us to take pleasure in the whole world rather than only in what we can claim. Admiration without grasping is one of happiness's quietest skills.
