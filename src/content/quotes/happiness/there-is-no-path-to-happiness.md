This line turns happiness from a destination into a way of travelling. We often treat joy as something waiting at the end of an achievement, a purchase, or a change in circumstances, and so postpone it. Thich Nhat Hanh reminds us that happiness lives in the path itself — in how we walk, breathe, listen, and pay attention right now. When each step is taken with presence and kindness, the journey stops being a means to an end. Happiness is not something we arrive at later; it is something we practise daily, and the road becomes the reward.
