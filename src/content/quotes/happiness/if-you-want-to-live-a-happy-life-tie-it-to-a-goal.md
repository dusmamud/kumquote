Einstein points to a steady anchor for happiness: a goal of our own. People change, leave, or disappoint us, and things break, age, or lose their shine, so tying our joy to them makes it fragile. A goal, by contrast, gives each day direction and turns effort into progress we can feel. It does not have to be grand; learning a skill, building something useful, or growing kinder all count. When happiness rests on purpose rather than possession, it survives setbacks and keeps renewing itself.
