Gandhi points to a simple test for happiness: do your thoughts, words, and actions agree with each other? Much of our unease comes from the gap between them — saying one thing while believing another, or promising ourselves change while acting the same way. That inner conflict is exhausting, even when no one else notices it. Harmony, by contrast, feels light, because there is nothing to hide and nothing to pretend. When we align what we think with what we say and do, peace follows, and happiness stops feeling so far away.
