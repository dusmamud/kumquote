Pollock pictures happiness as a resting place between two kinds of excess. Too much brings clutter, noise, and the weight of maintaining it all; too little brings hunger, worry, and the ache of wanting. The middle ground is where life is workable: enough to be comfortable, not so much that it owns you. Finding that station is a personal task, because the right amount differs for everyone. The wisdom lies in noticing when you have arrived there and choosing, for once, to stay.
