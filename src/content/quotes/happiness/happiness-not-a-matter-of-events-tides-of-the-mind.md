Meynell compares the mind to the sea, with tides that rise and fall regardless of what happens on shore. Events come and go, but our inner weather decides how we receive them. The same news can feel like a disaster on a low tide and a challenge on a high one. This does not mean events are irrelevant; it means they are not the whole story. Tending the mind, through rest, attention, and honest company, changes the tide. Happiness then depends less on arranging life perfectly and more on the state in which we meet it.
