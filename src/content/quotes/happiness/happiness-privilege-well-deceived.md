Swift is being wry rather than cynical here. Much of our contentment rests on the stories we tell ourselves, the small illusions that soften harsh truths and let us carry on with hope. A person who sees every flaw in every situation may be accurate, yet rarely at peace. Happiness often comes from choosing which truths to dwell on and which to set aside for a while. This is not a call to live in denial, but a reminder that a gentle self-deception, like believing a friend means well or that effort will pay off, can be a kindness we give ourselves. Guard your perspective, because the lens you look through shapes the life you feel you are living.
