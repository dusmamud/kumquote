Thucydides links happiness to freedom, and freedom to courage. The chain is simple: without courage, we cannot be free, and without freedom, happiness cannot breathe. Fear keeps us in narrow lives — afraid to speak, to change, to try, to leave. Courage does not remove fear; it lets us act in spite of it. Each brave step widens the space in which we live. Happiness, then, is not a lucky accident. It is the reward of people who dare to live on their own terms, and who refuse to let fear write the rules of their days.
