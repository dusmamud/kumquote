We often search for joy in new possessions, bigger houses, and grander experiences, as if happiness were something to buy. Wagner reminds us that joy lives inside us, not in the objects around us. Two people can stand in the same place and feel entirely different things, because joy is a quality of the heart, not of the scenery. When we cultivate an inner life — attention, gratitude, wonder — ordinary moments begin to shine. The world does not need to change for us to feel joy; we need only learn to carry it within ourselves.
