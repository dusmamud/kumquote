Shaw turns happiness into a matter of fairness. We gladly receive joy from others — kindness, humour, patience — yet we often forget to give any back. Happiness is not a resource to consume passively; it grows when it circulates. A word of encouragement, a small act of help, a moment of attention — these are the ways we produce happiness for others, and in doing so, we earn our share. The happiest people are rarely the ones who take the most. They are the ones who contribute, and find their own joy in the giving.
