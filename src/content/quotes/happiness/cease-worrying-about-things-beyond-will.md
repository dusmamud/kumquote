Epictetus, a Stoic philosopher, gives happiness a single clear condition: stop worrying about what you cannot control. So much of our anxiety is spent on other people's opinions, the past, the future, and events that were never ours to decide. None of that worry changes the outcome; it only steals the present moment. What we can control is our own judgement, our effort, and our response — and that is plenty. Letting go of the rest is not giving up; it is choosing where your energy goes. Peace arrives when we stop demanding that the world obey our will.
