Condorcet names the thief that steals most happiness: comparison. Measured against someone else's highlight, any life looks thin, because we compare our ordinary days with their best moments and forget the struggles behind them. Yet no two lives share the same starting point, the same burdens, or the same aims, so the comparison is never fair. Enjoying our own life means judging it by what matters to us: our health, our people, our small daily pleasures. Put the measuring stick down, and contentment has room to grow.
