Lasting happiness is not handed to us; it is earned through striving. The joy of reaching a goal comes from the struggle that led there, the problems solved and the skills gained along the way. Creative effort adds a second reward: the pleasure of seeing something exist that did not exist before. When life feels flat, the cure is often a challenge worth taking on. Choose a goal that stretches you, work at it steadily, and let the thrill of progress lift your mood. Achievement turns ordinary days into stories you are proud to tell.
