We imagine happiness waiting at the end of some road, as if the right job or the right city will finally unlock it. But destinations keep moving, and people who arrive often find the feeling missing. Runbeck says the secret is in how we travel, not where we end up. A journey walked with attention, gratitude, and good company feels rich even on ordinary days. The manner matters: patience instead of rushing, curiosity instead of complaint, kindness instead of scorekeeping. Travel well, and happiness stops being a faraway place and starts riding along beside us.
