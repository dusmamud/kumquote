Fields delivers his advice with a wink, treating a smile as a small chore to be done and finished rather than a grand emotional project. The joke hides a useful truth: a smile at the start of the day costs nothing, yet it softens our face, our mood, and the way others meet us. We do not need to feel cheerful first; the act itself can lead the feeling, like warming up before a performance. By getting the smile over with early, we stop waiting for the day to give us a reason and instead give the day one.
