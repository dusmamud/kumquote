A choice means we can decide, again and again, how to meet the day. A skill means we can get better at it with practice, the way we improve at anything worth doing. That is hopeful, because it puts happiness within reach of ordinary effort. We can train our attention, our gratitude, and our responses until they become steady habits. Happiness, then, is not a prize reserved for the fortunate few. It is something we build, slowly and deliberately, one decision at a time.
