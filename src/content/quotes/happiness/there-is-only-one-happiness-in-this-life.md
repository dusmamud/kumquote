Sand reduces happiness to a single thing: loving and being loved. Wealth, fame, and success can please us for a while, but they leave a hollow feeling when no one shares them. Love, by contrast, gives ordinary days meaning and carries us through hard times. The quote does not demand grand romance; it includes family, friendship, and quiet affection. To love means opening ourselves to another person, and to be loved means being truly seen. That mutual bond, Sand says, is the one happiness that makes a whole life feel complete.
