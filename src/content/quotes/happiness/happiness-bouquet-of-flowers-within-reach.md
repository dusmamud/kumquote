Goddard compares happiness to arranging flowers you can actually reach. The gardener who longs only for distant blooms walks past the beauty growing at her feet, while the one who gathers what is near comes home with something lovely. The art lies in seeing value in the ordinary: a friend, a meal, a morning's work, a quiet hour. Happiness does not require a rare life, only a willing eye. Gather what is within reach today, and the bouquet will be fuller than you expect.
