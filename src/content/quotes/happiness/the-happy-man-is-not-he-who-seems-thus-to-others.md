Appearances deceive. A person can look successful and envied while feeling hollow inside, and another can seem ordinary while feeling deeply content. This line says happiness is judged by the one living the life, not by the audience watching it. Chasing the appearance of happiness, through status or display, misses the point entirely. Real contentment is an inner verdict: does this life feel good to the person living it? Measure your life by your own honest answer, not by applause.
