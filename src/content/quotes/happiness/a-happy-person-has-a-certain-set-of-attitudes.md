Two people can live through the same events and feel entirely differently about them. The difference lies in attitude, not circumstance. A happy person is not someone who has been spared difficulty; it is someone who has learned to meet difficulty with perspective and grace. Attitudes are built from small daily choices: what to notice, what to forgive, what to let go. Circumstances matter, but they are only part of the story. When you change how you interpret your life, the life itself begins to feel different.
