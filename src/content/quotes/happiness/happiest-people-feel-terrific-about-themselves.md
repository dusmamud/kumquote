Tracy connects happiness to something unfashionable: total responsibility. When we blame luck, other people, or circumstances, we hand away our power and wait helplessly for rescue. Accepting responsibility does the opposite; it returns the steering wheel to our hands. We cannot control everything, but we can always control our response, our effort, and our next step. That control breeds self-respect, and self-respect quietly grows into feeling terrific about ourselves. Happiness, then, is not luck or personality. It is the natural result of owning our lives fully, flaws and all.
