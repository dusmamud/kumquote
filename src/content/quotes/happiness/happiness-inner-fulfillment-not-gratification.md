Ricard draws a line between fulfillment and gratification. Outward desires never end: each thing gained soon asks for another, so chasing them is a road without a destination. Inner fulfillment works the other way; it grows from contentment, kindness, and a mind at ease with itself. That kind of happiness does not depend on the next purchase or the next praise. It is steady because its source is within reach at all times, in the way we meet each moment rather than in what the moment gives us.
