Madame de Staël argues that feelings alone, however noble, do not make a life happy; they must be turned into something done. Love that never acts stays a daydream, and good intentions that never move stay a burden on the conscience. The deepest satisfaction comes when what we feel finds its way into the world — a kindness delivered, a work finished, a truth spoken. Action gives feeling its shape, and in that shaping we discover a happiness that thought alone could never provide.
