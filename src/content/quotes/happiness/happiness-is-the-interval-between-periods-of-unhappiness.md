This wry view reminds us that happiness is not a permanent condition but a welcome pause between difficulties. Life will always bring trouble, loss, and disappointment; no one escapes the hard stretches. Yet between them come stretches of ease, laughter, and ordinary peace, and those intervals are the real substance of a good life. If we only count happiness as endless bliss, we will always feel cheated. But if we recognize these calm spaces for what they are, we can savor them fully. Wisdom lies in cherishing the quiet intervals instead of resenting the storms around them.
