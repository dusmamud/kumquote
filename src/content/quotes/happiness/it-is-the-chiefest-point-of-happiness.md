Erasmus places self-acceptance at the very centre of a happy life. So much unhappiness comes from the quiet war we wage against ourselves — wishing we were taller, richer, cleverer, or someone else entirely. That refusal of our own nature leaves no room for peace, because the person we are is the only life we will ever get to live. Willingness to be what we are is not resignation; it is the ground on which real growth stands. Accept first, then improve.
