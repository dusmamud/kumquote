Anger feels like punishment aimed at someone else, but Buddha turns the arrow around. Holding anger keeps our own mind burning long after the original offence has passed, and the one who suffers most is usually the one who holds it. The other person may never even know. Letting go is not approval of what happened; it is refusing to keep injuring ourselves over it. Release the anger, and you release yourself from a punishment you were never meant to keep serving.
