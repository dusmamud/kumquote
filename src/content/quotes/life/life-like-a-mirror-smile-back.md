A mirror does not decide what to show us; it reflects what we bring to it. Life works the same way. Bring suspicion and gloom, and the world seems hostile. Bring warmth and a smile, and people respond with openness more often than we expect. This is not naive optimism — bad things happen regardless — but attitude shapes far more than we admit. A smile is a small investment with a large return: it softens strangers, eases tension, and invites kindness. Smile at life first, and watch how often life smiles back.
