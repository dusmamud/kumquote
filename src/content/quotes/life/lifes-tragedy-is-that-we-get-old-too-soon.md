Youth has the energy and old age has the understanding, and Franklin mourns that they rarely arrive together. When we are young, we have the strength to act but not the wisdom to choose well; when we are wise, the body has already slowed. The tragedy is not ageing itself but the timing. The remedy is to borrow wisdom early: listen to those who have lived longer, learn from their mistakes instead of repeating them, and treat time as the one resource that never returns. Get wise while you are still young enough to use it.
