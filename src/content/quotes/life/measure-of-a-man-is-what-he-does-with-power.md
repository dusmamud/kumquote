Power reveals character more honestly than any test, because it removes the excuses that hide who we really are. Without power, people can claim virtue cheaply; with it, their choices show the truth. Do they use power to help others or only themselves? Do they listen, or do they command? Do they protect the weak or exploit them? The measure of a person is not how much power they gain but what they do with it once it is theirs. Power is a trust, not a prize. Used well, it lifts others; used badly, it exposes the user.
