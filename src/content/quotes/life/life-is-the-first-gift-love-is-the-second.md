Piercy counts three gifts, in order, and the order matters. Life comes first, because without it there is nothing else. Love comes second, because a life without love is only existence. But understanding is the third gift, and it is the one that makes the first two last. Love without understanding fades into habit or conflict; life without understanding drifts into confusion. Understanding is what turns a gift into something we can keep. Cherish all three, but remember that the third is what protects the other two.
