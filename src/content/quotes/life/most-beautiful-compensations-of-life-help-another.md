Helping others is never a one-way gift. When you sincerely try to lift someone else, you grow too: you learn patience, you practise compassion, and you discover your own strength. The helper often gains as much as the helped — a clearer sense of purpose, deeper relationships, and quiet joy. This is one of life's fairest arrangements. You do not need to choose between your own good and the good of others, because the two are tied together. Try to help someone today, and you will find that you have helped yourself as well.
