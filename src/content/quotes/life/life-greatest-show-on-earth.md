We already hold the ticket — the only question is whether we will watch the show or sleep through it. Life offers wonders daily: a sky full of stars, a stranger's kindness, a child's laugh, a meal shared with people we love. None of it requires a fortune or a special occasion. The greatest show on earth is not somewhere else; it is here, today, in front of us. The tragedy would be to hold the ticket and never look up. Pay attention. The performance is already underway, and we have front-row seats.
