We treat what we hear as fact and what we see as truth, but Marcus Aurelius warns that both are filtered through human minds. Every report carries someone's opinion, and every view carries someone's perspective. Remembering this does not make truth impossible; it makes us humbler about our own certainty. It invites us to listen longer, judge slower, and stay open to being wrong. Most arguments shrink once both sides admit they are holding perspectives, not the whole truth. Wisdom begins with knowing the difference between seeing something and seeing all of it.
