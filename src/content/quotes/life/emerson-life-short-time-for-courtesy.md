We excuse rudeness by saying we are too busy, as if kindness were a luxury that only fits into a slow day. Emerson turns that excuse around: life is short, and that is exactly why courtesy matters. A kind word or a moment of patience costs almost nothing, yet it can change the whole tone of someone's day. We remember the people who treated us well in passing far longer than we remember their schedules. Courtesy is not wasted time; it is the smallest, cheapest way to make a brief life better for everyone it touches, including ourselves.
