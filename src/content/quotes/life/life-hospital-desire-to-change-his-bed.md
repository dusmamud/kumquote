Human discontent has a comic side, and this image captures it. Everyone believes a different bed, a different job, a different city would finally bring rest, yet the restlessness travels with them. The desire to change beds is rarely about the bed at all; it is about the hope that a new setting will fix an old feeling. Change can help, but it cannot do the inner work for us. Until we understand what troubles us, every new place becomes another bed to complain about. The cure begins not with moving, but with looking honestly at what we carry.
