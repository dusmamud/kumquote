Hardship can leave two very different marks. Bitterness keeps the wound open, replaying what was unfair and blaming others for what went wrong. Growth, by contrast, asks what the difficulty taught you: patience, strength, humility, or compassion for others who suffer. Dan Reeves reminds us that pain itself does not decide the outcome; your response does. You can honour what was lost while still choosing to learn from it. Let hardship refine you rather than define you. When you emerge better, not bitter, you turn a painful chapter into a source of wisdom for the life ahead.
