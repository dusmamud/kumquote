Life is short, and the question is what to spend it on. Pleasure fades, possessions wear out, and applause is forgotten, but work done for something larger than yourself keeps living after you are gone. It might be a child raised well, a kindness that changed someone's path, a piece of work that still helps people, or an idea that outlives its thinker. Spending life this way does not require fame; it requires choosing purpose over comfort, again and again.
