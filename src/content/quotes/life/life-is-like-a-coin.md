A coin can be spent only once, and Dickson turns that plain fact into a quiet challenge. Every day is a spending of the one currency we cannot earn back, and the choice of what to buy with it is ours alone. We can spend life on worry, on grudges, on waiting for a better moment, or we can spend it on the people, the work, and the small joys that make the expense feel worthwhile. There is no refund and no exchange, but there is also no wrong purchase that cannot be followed by a wiser one.
