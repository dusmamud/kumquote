Giving up feels reasonable when progress is invisible. You have worked hard, seen little result, and wonder if continuing is foolish. Yet many breakthroughs come just after the hardest point, when the person who keeps going finally reaches what the person who stopped never saw. Failure is often not a sign that success is impossible; it is a sign that you were close and did not know it. You cannot always tell how near you are. That uncertainty is exactly why persistence matters. Before you quit, remember how many people gave up one step too soon.
