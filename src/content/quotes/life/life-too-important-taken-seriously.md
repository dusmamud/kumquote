Wilde delivers a paradox with a smile: the very importance of life is the reason not to take it too seriously. Treating every setback as a tragedy and every duty as a burden drains the joy from the years we are given. A lighter touch — humour, play, and the willingness to laugh at ourselves — is not frivolity but wisdom. Life matters enormously, and that is exactly why we should enjoy it rather than merely endure it. Taking life lightly is not the same as taking it lightly in value; it is how we survive it gracefully.
