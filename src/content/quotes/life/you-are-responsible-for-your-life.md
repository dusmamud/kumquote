Responsibility can sound heavy, but it is also the most freeing idea in this line. If you are responsible for your life, then you are also the one who can change it. Blame feels easier because it hands the power to someone else, yet that same handover leaves you waiting for others to fix what only you can fix. Owning your life does not mean denying hardship or pretending everything is your fault. It means claiming the choices that are yours: your responses, your habits, your next step. Your life is yours. Own it, and it starts to move.
