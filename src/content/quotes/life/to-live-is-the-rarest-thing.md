Existing is easy: eat, sleep, work, repeat. Living is rarer, because it requires attention, choice, and courage. Most people drift through their days on routine, never asking what they truly want or what makes them feel alive. Wilde's sharp observation is a warning against a life of mere existence. To live means to notice, to feel deeply, to create, to love, and to take part in your own story rather than watching it pass. The difference between existing and living is the difference between a life endured and a life embraced.
