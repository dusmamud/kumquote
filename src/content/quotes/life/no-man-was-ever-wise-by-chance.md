Wisdom never arrives by accident. It is earned through attention, study, reflection, and the hard lessons of experience. People become wise by asking difficult questions, listening carefully, and thinking about what their experiences actually mean. Chance can bring good fortune, but it cannot bring understanding; understanding requires deliberate effort. If wisdom feels out of reach, the reason is usually not a lack of talent but a lack of practice. Read widely, reflect honestly, learn from mistakes instead of repeating them, and wisdom grows the way any skill grows — slowly, steadily, and on purpose.
