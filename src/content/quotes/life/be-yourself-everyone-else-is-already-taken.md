Imitating others is a losing game, because the original will always do it better. Wilde's witty line carries serious advice: the only role nobody else can fill is your own. Pretending to be someone else wastes the one thing that makes you irreplaceable, your unique combination of experiences, talents, and quirks. Authenticity is not only more honest but more effective; people trust and remember those who are genuinely themselves. The world already has everyone else. What it needs, and what only you can offer, is you, exactly as you are.
