Life gains meaning in three movements: finding what we are good at, growing it with care, and then giving it away. Viscott's sequence matters because each stage depends on the one before it. A gift undiscovered stays hidden; a gift developed but hoarded helps no one. It is in the giving — teaching, serving, creating for others — that our abilities find their full purpose. The question is not only what talent you carry, but what you will do with it. Discover it, develop it, and let it serve the world.
