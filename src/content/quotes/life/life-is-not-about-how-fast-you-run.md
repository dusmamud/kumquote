Speed and height impress people, but Komori measures life by a different skill: the bounce. Everyone falls — the project fails, the relationship ends, the plan collapses. The fast runner and the high climber fall too, and what matters then is how quickly and how fully they recover. Bouncing well means learning from the fall instead of being defined by it, and returning to the race with something new. A good bounce is not about avoiding the ground; it is about using the ground to rise again.
