Wonder is not something we outgrow; it is something we stop noticing. The world does not become less magical as we age — our attention simply grows duller, crowded out by routine and hurry. The magic is still there: in light on water, in a stranger's kindness, in the way a familiar street looks after rain. It waits patiently for us to slow down and sharpen our senses again. Growing up need not mean growing numb. The people who keep their sense of wonder are not naive; they are simply still paying attention.
