A blank canvas can be frightening, because every mark feels permanent and every choice feels like a risk. But a life left blank is the only true waste, while a life painted boldly is beautiful even when some colours clash. Throwing paint means trying things, making mistakes in public, loving without guarantees and learning in the open. Not every stroke will work, and that is the point: a full canvas holds the evidence of a life truly lived. Do not wait for the perfect brush or the perfect plan. Pick up the paint and begin.
