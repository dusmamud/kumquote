You cannot discover who you are by only repeating what others say. Finding yourself means pausing, asking your own questions, and thinking honestly about what you believe and what you want. The crowd offers ready-made answers, but they rarely fit a single life. Independent thought takes courage, because it may lead you away from popular opinion. Yet it is the only way to live a life that is truly yours. Think for yourself — question, reflect, decide — and in that thinking you will slowly meet the person you really are.
