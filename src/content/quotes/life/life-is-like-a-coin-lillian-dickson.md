Dickson's coin makes the economics of a lifetime impossible to ignore. Money can be earned again, but time spent is spent for good — every hour goes to something, whether you choose it deliberately or let it slip away by default. There is no single correct purchase, only the question of whether you chose it. Spend the coin on what you would be glad to have bought. A life spent deliberately, on what you truly value, is the only purchase that never feels wasted.
