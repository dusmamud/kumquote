A wave looks like one thing, but it is never the same water twice. Tyndall's image captures life exactly: we seem continuous, yet every moment brings new thoughts, new cells, new circumstances. Nothing stays still, and that constant change is not a flaw but the nature of being alive. Clinging to a fixed version of ourselves or our lives fights against how reality works. The wave keeps its shape by always moving, and so do we. Accept the flow, and change stops feeling like loss and starts feeling like life itself.
