La Bruyere captures the two ways of watching the same life unfold. Those who feel deeply experience its losses as tragedy, because they live inside every moment. Those who think step back and see the absurdities, the ironies, the small follies that make the same events almost funny. Neither view is wrong; both are incomplete. The wise person holds both at once, feeling fully without drowning and thinking clearly without growing cold. Life is tragic when we lose what we love, and comic when we notice how seriously we take ourselves.
