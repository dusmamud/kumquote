Life is shorter than we think, and no one else will sweeten it for us. The responsibility, and the freedom, is ours: to choose joy where we can, to be kind, to savour small pleasures, and to let go of grudges that sour our days. Delany lived a long life and knew that sweetness does not come from circumstances but from how we meet them. You cannot add years to your life, but you can add sweetness to your years, and that is entirely up to you.
