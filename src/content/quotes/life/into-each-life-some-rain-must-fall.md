No life is free of trouble. Everyone, however fortunate they seem, has known loss, disappointment, or pain. The rain falls on every life, and that fact is both sobering and comforting: we are not singled out, and we are not alone in our struggles. Expecting a life without rain only makes the storms harder to bear. This quote invites us to accept hardship as a normal part of living, not as a personal failure. When we stop asking why me, we can start asking what now, and that question leads forward.
