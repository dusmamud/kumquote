Silence is often mistaken for emptiness or weakness, but it is one of the strongest things a person can practice. In silence we hear our own thoughts clearly, notice what matters, and gather ourselves before acting. Speaking less also means listening more, and listening is where understanding begins. People who can sit comfortably in silence are rarely pushed around by noise, pressure, or other people's urgency. This quote reminds us that strength is not always loud; sometimes it is the quiet confidence of a mind at rest.
