Life rarely deals us the hand we would have chosen. We compare our cards with other people's and feel the game is rigged against us. Stevenson's wisdom is that the game was never about the cards. A poor hand played with patience, courage, and judgment can win more than a strong hand played carelessly. What matters is not what we were given but what we do with it. The person who plays a hand well learns resourcefulness, timing, and grace under pressure. Character is not measured by the luck of the draw, but by the quality of the play.
