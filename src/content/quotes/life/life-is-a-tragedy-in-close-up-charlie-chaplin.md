Chaplin, whose whole art turned misfortune into laughter, explains his method in one sentence. Up close, any life looks tragic: the losses, the embarrassments, the plans that fell apart. Step back far enough, though, and the same events take on a comic shape — the stumble, the misunderstanding, the grand plan undone by a banana peel. Distance does not erase pain, but it can shrink it to a size you can carry, and sometimes even laugh at. When a day feels tragic, try the long shot. The comedy may already be there, waiting for room.
