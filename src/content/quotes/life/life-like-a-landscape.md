When we are inside a difficult season, everything feels close and confusing — like standing in the middle of a forest, unable to see the shape of it. Only later, with distance, does the pattern become clear: why that job ended, why that relationship mattered, how that hardship changed us. This is why patience matters in hard times. We cannot always understand our life while we are living it. Sometimes we must simply keep walking, trusting that the view from further on will make sense of the landscape we are crossing now.
