The same event can feel like a disaster or a lesson, depending on the thoughts we bring to it. Marcus Aurelius understood that while we cannot control most of what happens to us, we can always choose how we interpret it. A mind trained toward gratitude finds reasons to be thankful; a mind trained toward complaint finds reasons to suffer. Our thoughts are not passive weather but tools we can shape, and the quality of our inner life decides the quality of our outer one. Change your thoughts, and you change your life.
