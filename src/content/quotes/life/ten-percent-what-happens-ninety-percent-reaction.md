We cannot choose most of what happens to us: the traffic, the diagnosis, the rejection, the sudden change of plan. What we can always choose is our response, and Swindoll claims that response is ninety percent of life. Two people can face the same event and live entirely different stories, because one meets it with bitterness and the other with resolve. Attitude is the one part of life that stays in our hands no matter what. You may not control the ten percent, but the ninety percent is yours.
