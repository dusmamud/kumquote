Half-hearted effort produces half-hearted results, and it leaves us wondering whether we ever truly tried. Going with all our heart does not mean being reckless; it means being fully present in whatever we have chosen. When we commit completely, ordinary work gains dignity and ordinary places gain meaning. Even a task we did not choose can become worthwhile when we bring our whole selves to it. The destination matters less than the spirit we carry there. Wherever life sends us next, let us arrive completely — not divided, not distracted, not holding back.
