Life is short, and Hepburn treats it as an occasion worth dressing for. The party is not about vanity or showing off; it is about showing up with care, joy and presence. Too often we wait for a special day to wear the good clothes, speak the kind words or live fully, as if ordinary days did not count. But every day is part of the celebration. Dress for it — bring your best self, your warmth and your courage — and the ordinary becomes something worth remembering.
