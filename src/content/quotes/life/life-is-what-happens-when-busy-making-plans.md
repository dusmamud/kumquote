We spend much of our lives preparing for a future that keeps moving, while the present quietly passes by. Plans are useful, but they can become a way of postponing joy, connection, and attention until conditions are perfect. John Lennon reminds us that life is already happening in ordinary moments: a shared meal, a walk, a conversation, a laugh. If you wait until everything is settled to start living, you may wait forever. Make plans, but do not miss the life unfolding inside them. Pay attention today, because today is the only day you can actually live.
