Treating life as a puzzle to be solved keeps us forever analysing, planning, and waiting for the right answer. Kierkegaard invites us instead to live it directly: to feel, to notice, to participate in the present moment rather than standing outside it. Experience cannot be replaced by explanation; no amount of thinking about joy equals a single moment of actually feeling it. The richness of life is found in engagement, not in analysis. When we stop trying to solve life and start experiencing it, we discover it was never a problem at all.
