Life does not make us bigger through comfort; it makes us bigger through experience. Every job, journey, failure, and friendship adds something to who we are, even the experiences we would not choose again. A person who has lived widely carries more understanding, patience, and depth than one who has stayed narrow. The point is not to collect experiences like souvenirs, but to let them change us. Each one stretches us a little further, and over a lifetime those small stretchings add up to a much larger self.
