Life is not a picture to be kept clean and safe; it is a canvas waiting for bold colour. Kaye encourages throwing paint freely, which means trying things, making messes and refusing to live cautiously. A careful life stays tidy, but stays small. Bold strokes bring risk, mistakes and embarrassment, yet they are also what make a life vivid and memorable. The canvas is large and the time to paint on it is limited, so holding back wastes the opportunity. Be generous with effort and courage, and let the finished picture show a life fully lived.
