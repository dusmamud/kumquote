Life does not come with good roads guaranteed, and waiting for perfect conditions means never leaving at all. The journey includes rough stretches, wrong turns and poor lodging, yet it still has to be travelled, because staying put is not an alternative. Every mile teaches something the map could not: patience with discomfort, resourcefulness when plans fail, and gratitude for the easy stretches when they come. The quality of the journey is not decided by the roads but by the traveller. Pack lightly, keep walking, and let the rough parts become part of the story.
