Adventure sounds exciting until we realize it means risk, uncertainty, and the chance of failing. Roosevelt names the fear that stops most people: not fear of danger, but fear of living itself, of stepping into a full life with all its unknowns. A safe life avoids that fear by avoiding life, and that is the worst bargain of all. The adventure is not optional; it is what life is. Accept the fear as part of the journey, and take the step anyway. Living fully is the only way to answer a life that is, itself, a great adventure.
