A life does not need easy goals to be meaningful. In fact, the deepest purpose often comes from reaching toward something larger than ourselves: a question we cannot yet answer, a task we cannot yet complete. The attempt itself stretches us, teaches us, and gives our days direction. Even if we never fully arrive, the striving makes the life worthwhile. Do not settle only for what is safe and certain. Ask the big question, attempt the hard task, and let the reach itself become the meaning of your existence.
