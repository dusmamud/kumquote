A piano does not decide what music comes out; the player does. Lehrer's image turns life into the same kind of instrument. The keys are the days we are given, the same for everyone, more or less. What we get depends on how we play: with attention or carelessness, with courage or fear, with practice or neglect. Some blame the instrument for a tune they never learned. But the piano is neutral, and life is mostly neutral too. It returns what we put into it. Play with care, keep practicing, and even an ordinary life can produce a beautiful sound.
