The past can feel like a weight and the future like a fog, and between them the present slips by unnoticed. Roosevelt's image gives each its place. Yesterday is history, finished, useful only for what it can teach. Tomorrow is a mystery, unwritten and unknowable, useful only for what it can inspire. Today is the gift, the one day we can touch, choose in, and live. We cannot rewrite yesterday or control tomorrow, but we can decide what today will hold. A life well lived is a long series of todays, each one received as the gift it is.
