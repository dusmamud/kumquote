If life is only about arriving somewhere, then most of it is wasted waiting. Emerson reminds us that the road itself is the point: the people met along the way, the lessons learned from detours, the slow changes in who we are. Destinations are brief moments, quickly replaced by new goals. The journey, by contrast, is everything we actually live. To enjoy the journey is to value ordinary days, not just milestones. Walk slowly enough to notice, and the travelling becomes the reward.
