A life without falling is not a real life; it is a life that never attempted anything difficult. Everyone stumbles — in work, in love, in health, in plans that collapse. What separates a remarkable life from an ordinary one is not the absence of failure but the habit of rising after it. Each time we get up, we prove to ourselves that a fall is an event, not an identity. The glory lies in the return, again and again, until rising becomes who we are. That is a kind of greatness available to anyone willing to stand back up.
