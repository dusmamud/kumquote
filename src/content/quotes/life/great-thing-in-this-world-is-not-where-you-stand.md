Your current position matters far less than the direction you are heading. A person who starts low but moves steadily upward will go further than someone who starts high but drifts downward. Life is not a single snapshot; it is a journey, and the trend of your steps decides where you arrive. So do not be discouraged by where you stand today. Ask instead which way you are moving. Small steps in the right direction, repeated daily, will carry you further than any single advantage ever could.
