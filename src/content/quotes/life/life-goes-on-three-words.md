Frost compresses a lifetime of wisdom into three words: it goes on. Grief, failure, embarrassment, and loss all feel permanent while we are inside them, yet time keeps moving and so do we. The sun rises after the worst day, and slowly, almost without noticing, we begin again. This is not cold comfort — it is the deepest kind. Whatever has happened, life has not ended; it continues, and with it our capacity to heal, to try again, and to find new meaning. The going on is itself the hope.
