Swindoll's arithmetic is deliberately lopsided: the events of life get only ten percent, because they are mostly outside your control. The other ninety — your response — is where your life is actually decided. Two people can face the same setback and live entirely different stories afterward, and the difference is rarely the event itself. You cannot always pick what happens, but you can almost always pick your response. That ninety percent is the largest freedom most of us will ever own.
