We spend most of our energy worrying about events we cannot control, yet those events are only a small part of life. Swindoll's famous ratio reminds us that our reaction matters nine times more than what happens to us. Two people can face the same setback and live entirely different stories, depending on whether they respond with bitterness or with resilience. We cannot choose every circumstance, but we can always choose our response, and that choice shapes our happiness, our relationships, and our future. Life is ten percent event and ninety percent attitude.
