Holmes uses the mirror to make a point about the mind's power to colour experience. A person who walks into the world expecting hostility will find plenty of evidence for it, while one who expects goodwill will notice kindness everywhere — and both will believe the world simply is that way. The mirror does not invent what you see, but it returns the attitude you brought to it, sharpened. If you dislike what life keeps showing you, check what you have been thinking into it. Change the thought, and the reflection changes with it.
