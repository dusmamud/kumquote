Wayne states a blunt fact and then makes it blunter. Life is hard for everyone — that part is not optional. But foolish choices add a second layer of hardship that could have been avoided: the ignored advice, the repeated mistake, the shortcut that costs more than the long way. Wisdom does not remove the difficulty of life, but it stops us from multiplying it. The quote is a joke with a lesson inside: learn from experience, listen to people who know more, and do not make a hard life harder than it has to be.
