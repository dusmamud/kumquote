Butler's line is dry, funny, and quietly true. Life uses us up a little at a time, through work, worry, joy, and age, and tiredness is the slow tax on living fully. Yet the tiredness is also evidence of a life spent doing things, not a life left unused. The trick is to get tired doing what matters rather than what merely drains. Rest is part of the process too, not a failure of it. Spend your energy well, rest without guilt, and let the tiredness be the honest price of a life truly lived.
