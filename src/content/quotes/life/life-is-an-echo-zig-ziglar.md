An echo never invents anything; it only returns what was sent. Ziglar's image is a warning and a promise at once. The kindness we send out comes back as kindness, sometimes from unexpected directions. The sharp word, the careless act, the cruelty, these return too, often louder than we sent them. We cannot control what others send toward us, but we have control over what we send out. That is why the wisest people are careful with their words and generous with their help. Life listens more closely than we think, and it answers in the same voice we used.
