The same rain falls on everyone, but not everyone experiences it the same way. Some people rush through it, head down, seeing only an inconvenience. Others pause, feel it on their skin, and find something alive in the moment. The difference is not the weather; it is attention. Life is full of moments like rain — ordinary, unchosen, easily missed. We can hurry past them, or we can actually feel them. Being present turns even a grey day into something real. The choice is always ours: merely get wet, or truly feel the rain.
