Jobs said this to graduates, but it reads like a warning to everyone
with a calendar full of other people's priorities. Time is the one
budget that never gets a refund, and every yes to someone else's
agenda is a no to your own — quietly, without a receipt.

The honest way to use it is an audit, not a dramatic resignation.
Look at last week. Which hours were yours, and which were spent
performing a life someone else designed? You do not need to change
everything at once. Reclaim one hour a day for the work, the person,
or the rest that is actually yours. A life is built from those hours,
not from the ones you gave away.
