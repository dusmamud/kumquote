People complain that life is short, yet Seneca argues the problem is not the length of life but how much of it we waste. Years slip away in distraction, worry, and busyness that serves no real purpose. A life used well, even a shorter one, can feel full and complete, while a long life squandered on trifles feels empty at the end. Time is the one resource that cannot be earned back, so spending it deliberately is the highest form of wisdom. Use your days on what matters, and you will find that life was long enough after all.
