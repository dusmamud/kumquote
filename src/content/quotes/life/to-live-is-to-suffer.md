Nietzsche refuses to pretend that life can be lived without pain, and instead offers something more useful than comfort: meaning. Suffering itself is unavoidable, but meaningless suffering is crushing, while suffering that serves a purpose can be borne, even transformed. Survival, in this view, is not merely enduring; it is the active work of finding the meaning inside the hardship, the lesson, the love, the cause that makes the pain worth carrying. This turns the sufferer from a victim into a creator, someone who shapes what their pain means. Life does not ask whether we will suffer.
