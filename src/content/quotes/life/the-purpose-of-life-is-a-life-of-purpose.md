A life without purpose can still be comfortable, but it rarely feels complete. Purpose does not have to be grand — it can be raising a family well, building something useful, or simply being the person others can count on. What matters is that your days point somewhere, that your choices add up to more than a list of errands. When you know what your life is for, even hard days feel worthwhile, because the effort belongs to something bigger than the moment. Purpose turns a stretch of years into a story worth telling.
