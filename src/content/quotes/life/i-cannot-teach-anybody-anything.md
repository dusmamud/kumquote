Real learning cannot be poured into a person like water into a cup. A teacher can ask questions, share ideas, and point the way, but understanding only happens when the learner starts thinking for themselves. That is why the best teaching feels less like instruction and more like awakening. A good question can do more than a hundred answers, because it starts the mind working. So do not wait to be taught everything. Ask, wonder, test ideas, and think deeply. The moment you begin to think, you begin to truly learn.
