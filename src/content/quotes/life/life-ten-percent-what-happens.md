We cannot control most of what happens to us: the traffic, the weather, other people's choices, or sudden bad news. What we can always control is our response. That response, multiplied across a lifetime, shapes our happiness far more than the events themselves. Two people can face the same setback and live completely different lives, because one reacts with bitterness and the other with patience. Swindoll's numbers may be approximate, but the principle holds: your reaction is where your power lives, so choose it carefully.
