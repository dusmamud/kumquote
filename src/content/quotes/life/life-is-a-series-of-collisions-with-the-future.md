We imagine life as a straight road we have already mapped, but it is really a series of collisions with a future we cannot predict. People, chances, and losses arrive unannounced and change our direction. What matters is not the sum of what we have been, but what we yearn to be, the direction we choose after each collision. Every unexpected meeting is a chance to become someone new. Do not fear the collisions. Meet them with open eyes, let them reshape you, and keep walking toward the person you most want to become.
