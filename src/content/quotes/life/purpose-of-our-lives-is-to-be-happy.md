With so many measures of a good life, wealth, status, achievement, it is easy to forget the simplest purpose: happiness. The Dalai Lama does not mean shallow pleasure or constant excitement. He means a deeper contentment that comes from kindness, gratitude, and a calm mind. Happiness grows when we care for others, accept what we cannot change, and appreciate what we already have. If a choice brings lasting peace and helps others, it is probably aligned with your purpose. Let happiness guide your decisions, not as an indulgence, but as a direction.
