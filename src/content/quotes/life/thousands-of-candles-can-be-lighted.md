The Buddha uses a simple image to correct a common fear: that giving away our happiness will leave us with less. A candle loses nothing by lighting another; its flame does not shrink, and the room only grows brighter. Happiness works the same way. Shared joy is not divided joy; it multiplies, lighting new candles that go on to light others. The person who guards happiness like a scarce resource misunderstands its nature. It is not a possession to be hoarded but a light to be passed along, and the passing is what keeps it burning.
