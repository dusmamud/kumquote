We search for life's meaning in grand moments, the milestones, the achievements, the days that change everything. Holmes points somewhere quieter: life is a great bundle of little things. Morning coffee, a child's laugh, a walk with a friend, a page before sleep. None feels important alone, and that is why we overlook them. But these small things are the fabric of our days, and our days are the fabric of our lives. A life made of small good things, noticed and enjoyed, is not a small life. It is the richest kind, and it is available every single day.
