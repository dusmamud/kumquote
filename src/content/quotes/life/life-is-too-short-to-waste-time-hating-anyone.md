Hatred is expensive, and life is too short to pay its price. Brett's lesson is that every hour spent hating is an hour stolen from living — from joy, rest, friendship and the things that actually feed us. The person we hate often goes on untouched, while we carry the weight alone. Letting go does not mean forgetting or excusing harm; it means refusing to give it more of our limited days. Choose to spend your short life on what builds you up, not on what wears you down.
