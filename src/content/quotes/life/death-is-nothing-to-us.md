Fear of death steals joy from the living, and this short sentence takes that fear apart with simple logic. When we exist, death is not present; when death is present, we no longer exist. So there is no moment in which we actually meet it and suffer it. The dread we feel is not of death itself but of the idea of it, a shadow cast over days that are still ours. Once that is understood, the present becomes lighter. Life is for living now, not for spending in fear of an end we will never experience.
