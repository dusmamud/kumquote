De Beauvoir attacks the habit of postponing change. We tell ourselves we will start on Monday, next month, next year — as if the future were a reliable friend. But the future is a gamble: health, circumstances, and energy are never guaranteed. Today is certain; it is the only day you actually hold in your hands. Acting now does not mean acting perfectly — it means refusing to bet your life on a tomorrow that may never arrive as you imagine. Change begins when you stop waiting for permission from a future date and start with the day you already have.
