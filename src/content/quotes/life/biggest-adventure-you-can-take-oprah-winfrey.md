Most adventures are chosen by circumstance, but the greatest is chosen by us: to live the life we dream of. That sounds simple until we try it. It means disappointing some expectations, leaving safe paths, and admitting what we truly want. Winfrey's words remind us that a dreamed life is not found; it is lived, day by day, in small acts of honesty. The adventure is not in the destination but in the daily courage to be ourselves. Anyone can take it, and no one can take it for us. The only ticket is the decision to begin.
