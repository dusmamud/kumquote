The same life can feel like a burden or a festival, and the difference is not in the circumstances but in the eyes that see them. The wise find celebration in ordinary things — a morning's light, honest work, a shared meal — because they have learned to notice what is already good. The unwise wait for grand occasions and miss the small joys passing by every day. Wisdom here is not cleverness but attention: the habit of looking at life with gratitude instead of complaint.
