We spend so much of life planning the next stage that we miss the one we are in. The perfect job, the right time, the future milestone, all of them keep our attention fixed ahead, while the real living happens quietly in the background. Lennon reminds us that life does not wait for our plans to be ready. It unfolds now, in ordinary days and unplanned moments. Plans are useful, but they are not the point. The point is to notice the life happening around you while you are busy arranging it.
