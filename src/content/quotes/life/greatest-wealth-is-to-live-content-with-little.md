True wealth is not measured by what you own but by how little you need to be content. A person who is satisfied with little is free: free from the endless chase for more, free from the fear of losing it, and free to enjoy what is already present. The desire for more has no natural stopping point, so those who measure wealth by possessions are never rich enough. Contentment, by contrast, turns whatever you have into enough. Simplicity is not poverty; it is the deliberate choice to value peace over accumulation.
