Dickinson treats living itself as an astonishing event, so full of wonder that it leaves little room for distraction. The startling quality of life is easy to miss when days are crowded with routine, worry and small grievances. Yet attention reveals how extraordinary ordinary existence is: breath, light, change, feeling, other people. The quote is an invitation to meet life with open eyes rather than rushing past it toward the next task. Wonder is not a luxury reserved for rare moments; it is available in any moment that is truly noticed. Living fully begins with paying attention.
