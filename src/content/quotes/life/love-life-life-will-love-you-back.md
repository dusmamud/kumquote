Life often mirrors the attitude we bring to it. Approach it with suspicion and resentment, and it seems to return the same coldness. Approach it with warmth and curiosity, and small gifts begin to appear everywhere: a kind stranger, a beautiful morning, an unexpected opportunity. This is not magic; it is attention. Loving life means noticing what is good instead of only tallying what is wrong. That habit changes what we see, what we attract, and how we respond. Love life first, and life has a way of loving us in return.
