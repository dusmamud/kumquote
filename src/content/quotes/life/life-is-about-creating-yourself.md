Many people wait to discover who they are, as though identity were hidden somewhere to be found. George Bernard Shaw offers a more active view: you create yourself through your choices, habits, and commitments. Every book you read, skill you practise, kindness you offer, and challenge you accept shapes the person you become. You are not fixed by your past or your circumstances. If you do not like who you are becoming, change what you do each day. Identity is not a treasure to uncover; it is a work of art you build, one deliberate decision at a time.
