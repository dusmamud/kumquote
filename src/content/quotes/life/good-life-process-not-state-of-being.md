A good life is not a place you arrive at and then simply stay. It is a direction you keep moving in, a process of growing, learning, and becoming a little more yourself each day. People who treat happiness as a destination often feel disappointed, because no achievement permanently fixes life in place. The direction matters more than the distance covered: are you moving toward honesty, kindness, and curiosity, or drifting away from them? A life pointed the right way can hold setbacks without losing its meaning. Keep walking in the right direction, and the process itself becomes the reward.
