A flower is beautiful on its own, but its honey is the sweetness hidden within it, made only when bees gather and work. Hugo suggests that life resembles the flower and love is its honey: the richest part of living is not the surface beauty but the sweetness love produces. Love gathers ordinary days into something nourishing and memorable. Without it, life may still look fine, yet its deepest flavour is missing. The image is gentle rather than demanding. It simply shows what love adds, and lets the reader feel the difference.
