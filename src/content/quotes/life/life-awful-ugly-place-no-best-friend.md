Life can be hard in ways that have little to do with money or success. Without a best friend, even ordinary days feel heavier, because there is no one who knows you, no one to call when things go wrong or right. A best friend turns small moments into shared ones and makes trouble easier to carry. The world does not become ugly because it lacks beauty; it feels ugly without connection. Friendship is not a luxury added to life, it is part of what makes life livable. Hold your friends near, and never take their presence for granted.
