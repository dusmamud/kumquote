Much of life's difficulty comes from getting this balance wrong: we cling to what has already ended, or we release too soon what still needs our care. Holding on matters when something is precious — a relationship, a value, a promise worth keeping. Letting go matters when holding on only causes pain — an old hurt, a finished chapter, a version of ourselves we have outgrown. Wisdom is knowing which moment calls for which. The balance is not fixed once but adjusted daily, with attention and honesty, until we learn to hold lightly and release gracefully.
