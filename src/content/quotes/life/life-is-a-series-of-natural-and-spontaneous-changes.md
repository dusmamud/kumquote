Change arrives whether we welcome it or not, and resisting it only adds a second layer of suffering to the first. Seasons turn, people grow, plans shift, and nothing stays exactly as it was. When we fight these natural changes, we exhaust ourselves holding on to what has already passed. Acceptance is not surrender; it is the wisdom to move with the current instead of against it. Let the change come, adapt to it gently, and you will find that sorrow fades faster when you stop feeding it with resistance.
