We cannot control the weather, and we cannot control much of life either. Rain falls whether we approve of it or not, and fighting it only leaves us wet and frustrated. The wise response is acceptance: let the rain fall, take shelter if you can, and wait. This quote is about the art of not struggling against what cannot be changed. Acceptance is not defeat; it is the clarity to save our energy for the things we can actually influence. Sometimes the best thing to do about a problem is simply to let it be.
