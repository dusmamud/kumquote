Plans are useful, but life rarely waits for them. While you are preparing for the future, the present is already happening — in small conversations, ordinary days, and unexpected turns. If you spend all your attention on what comes next, you can miss the life unfolding right now. This does not mean you should stop planning. It means you should also pay attention. Notice the people beside you, the moment you are in, and the chances that appear without warning. Life is not only the destination you planned; it is everything that happens on the way there.
