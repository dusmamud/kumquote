Enemies are expected to speak against us, so their words rarely wound as deeply as we fear. What stays with us is the silence of friends: the moment someone who could have spoken stayed quiet, looked away, or chose comfort over courage. That silence teaches a hard truth about loyalty, which is not tested when agreement is easy but when standing up costs something. Friendship asks for a voice, not just affection. Remember this when you are the friend watching: your words in a difficult moment may be what someone carries with them for years.
