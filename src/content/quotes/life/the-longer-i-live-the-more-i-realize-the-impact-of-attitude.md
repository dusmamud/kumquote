We spend years trying to change our circumstances, convinced that a new job, city, or relationship will fix how we feel. But the same events land differently on different people, and the difference is usually attitude. Attitude decides whether a setback becomes a lesson or an excuse, whether criticism becomes guidance or insult. It is the one thing we can choose even when we cannot choose what happens to us. The longer we live, the clearer it becomes: life is shaped less by events than by the attitude we bring to them.
