A long life is not automatically a full one. What counts is how much living you pack into the years you have: the love you give, the work you care about, the risks you take, and the people you help. Someone who lives boldly for fifty years may leave a richer mark than someone who merely passes time for ninety. Lincoln's words ask us to measure life by depth, not length. Fill your years with life, with purpose and joy and courage, because the number of years is never fully in your hands, but how you live them is.
