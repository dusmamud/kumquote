Many of the things we want most — a raise, a second chance, help, forgiveness — are sitting on the other side of a single question we are afraid to ask. Silence feels safe, but it guarantees nothing changes. Asking takes courage because it opens the door to hearing no. Yet a no costs far less than a lifetime of wondering what might have happened. The people who get more from life are not always the most talented; often they are simply the most willing to ask. Speak up, and give life a chance to say yes.
