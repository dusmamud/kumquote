The past cannot be changed and the future cannot be controlled, yet our minds spend most of their time in one or the other. Dwelling on old regrets brings sorrow, and dreaming anxiously about tomorrow brings fear, while the only moment we can actually live is this one. The Buddha's advice is practical: bring your attention back to now, to the task, the breath, the person in front of you. The present is where life happens, and a mind that rests here finds a peace that neither past nor future can offer.
