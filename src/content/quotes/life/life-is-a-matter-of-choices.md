Our lives are not shaped in a single dramatic moment but in the small choices we repeat each day. Maxwell's line reminds us that every decision — how we spend an hour, what we say, what we tolerate — leaves a mark on who we are becoming. This is both sobering and hopeful: we cannot change yesterday's choices, but today's are still ours to make. Choose carefully, because each choice is a brushstroke. Over time, those strokes add up to the portrait of a life, and it is one we are painting now.
