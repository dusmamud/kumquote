The truth has a way of returning, however carefully it is buried. Buddha names three things that cannot stay hidden forever: the sun, the moon and the truth. Light is patient; it does not argue, it simply rises again each day. Lies and secrets demand constant effort to maintain, while truth needs only time. This is a warning against deception and a comfort for those waiting for honesty to win. In the end, reality comes out — and those who live by the truth have nothing to fear from its arrival.
