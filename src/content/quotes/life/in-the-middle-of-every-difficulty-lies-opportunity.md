Difficulty and opportunity are often the same moment seen from two sides. A problem forces us to think differently, learn new skills, and discover strengths we never needed before. The setback that blocks one road may open another that we would never have noticed in easy times. This does not mean trouble is pleasant; it means trouble is useful. When you face a hard moment, ask what it might be teaching or opening for you. Inside every difficulty, if you look closely enough, an opportunity is waiting to be found.
