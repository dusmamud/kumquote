Birth gives you a life, but purpose gives it a direction. Many people drift for years, busy and successful by every outside measure, yet quietly unsure what any of it is for. Then one day something clicks — a calling, a cause, a craft, a person to serve — and suddenly the scattered pieces of their life make sense. That second day is the real beginning. Everything before it was preparation; everything after it has meaning. If you have not found your why yet, keep looking. It is the most important discovery a life can hold.
