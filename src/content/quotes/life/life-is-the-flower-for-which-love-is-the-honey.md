Hugo pictures life as a flower and love as the honey inside it. A flower without honey is still beautiful, but it has not fulfilled its purpose; it exists to be gathered, shared, and turned into something sweet. In the same way, a life without love can be busy and impressive yet feel empty. Love is what gives our days their flavour — the care of family, the warmth of friendship, the devotion to a cause. Without it, success tastes flat. With it, even ordinary days carry a quiet sweetness. The flower blooms once; gather the honey while it lasts.
