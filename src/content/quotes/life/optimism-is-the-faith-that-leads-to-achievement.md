Hope is not idle wishing; it is the faith that makes action possible. Without optimism, we never take the first step, because we have already decided the effort is pointless. With it, we try, we practise, and we persist through failure. Confidence does not guarantee success, but its absence guarantees that nothing is attempted at all. Optimism is therefore practical, not naive: it is the working faith that lets us begin. Keep hope alive, and achievement has a chance to follow.
