We treat success as a distant destination, a single great achievement somewhere in the future. But life is really a chain of small moments, and each one is a chance to live fully: a conversation, a task done well, a kindness offered. To live each moment is to succeed, because a life well lived is simply a collection of well-lived moments. Do not rush past today while chasing tomorrow's triumph. Slow down, pay attention, and give this moment your best. String enough such moments together, and you will find you have built a successful life.
