A gift is not meant to be stored away; it is meant to be used and shared. Life gives us the privilege of being here, the opportunity to grow, and — with those — the responsibility to give something back. Becoming more is not about collecting achievements for ourselves. It is about growing into someone who can contribute: a kinder neighbor, a wiser parent, a more generous friend. The more we grow, the more we have to offer. That is the real return — not what we keep, but what we become and give.
