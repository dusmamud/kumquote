Life is not easy for anyone; Curie knew that better than most. She worked in a shed, handled materials by hand, and was overlooked again as a woman in a field that did not want her. Yet her answer was not complaint but perseverance, and confidence in herself. She believed each person is gifted for something, and that the gift must be attained. Difficulty is not a sign to stop; it is the price of doing something worth doing. We cannot control how hard life is. We can control whether we keep going, and whether we believe our work matters.
