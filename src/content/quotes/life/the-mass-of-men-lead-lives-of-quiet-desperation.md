Many people look fine from the outside while feeling empty inside. They follow routines they never chose, chase goals they never wanted, and call it a life, all while a quiet voice keeps asking whether this is all there is. The desperation is quiet because admitting it feels shameful, and because everyone else seems equally resigned. But noticing the emptiness is the beginning of freedom. A life becomes your own the moment you stop performing it for others and start asking what you actually want. Desperation ends where honesty begins.
