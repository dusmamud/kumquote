Eyes can see the world, but only vision gives life direction. A person may have perfect sight and still drift without purpose, while a person without sight can live with clear goals and deep meaning. Vision is the ability to imagine a better future and to move toward it with intent. Without it, days pass in a blur of routine. With it, even hardship becomes part of a larger story. The real blindness is not in the eyes; it is in a life without direction. Hold on to vision, and your steps will always have somewhere to go.
