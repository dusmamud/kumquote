It is strange how easily we postpone living. We wait for the perfect time, the safer job, the smaller fear — as if life were a rehearsal and the real performance were coming later. It is not. This is the performance, and the clock is running. Living does not require grand adventures; it requires showing up for the life we already have. Say the thing, take the trip, call the friend, start the project. Shortness is not a threat. It is an invitation to stop waiting and to live now, while now is still ours.
