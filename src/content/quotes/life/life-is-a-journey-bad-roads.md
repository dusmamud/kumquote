No one gets to choose smooth roads for their whole journey. Life brings rough patches, poor conditions, and detours no one planned for, and yet the journey continues whether we like it or not. The choice is not whether to travel but how: with complaint or with courage, with bitterness or with patience. Goldsmith reminds us that the road's condition is not the measure of the traveller. Keep moving through the bad stretches, because they are part of the journey, not the end of it, and better roads often lie ahead.
