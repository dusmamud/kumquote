Big goals frighten us because we see them whole: the mountain, not the path. Brown's advice is to shrink the goal until it fits in a step. A baby step is laughably small, and that is its power. It asks so little we cannot reasonably refuse it, and once taken, it proves we can move. One step becomes two, two become a habit, a habit becomes a journey. Progress announces itself; it accumulates quietly, one small step at a time. Anyone can take a baby step, whatever the goal. Baby steps, added over time, are how every distance is crossed.
