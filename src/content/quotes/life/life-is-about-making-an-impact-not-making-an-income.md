An income keeps you alive, but an impact is what makes a life feel alive. Money matters — it pays the bills and buys freedom — yet a life measured only by earnings ends up feeling empty even when the numbers are large. Impact is different: it is the difference you make in other people's lives, the problems you solve, the people you lift. It does not require wealth or fame; a teacher, a neighbour, a parent all make impacts that ripple for years.
