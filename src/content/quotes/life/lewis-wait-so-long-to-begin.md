We speak of life as short, yet the greater loss is how much of it we spend waiting — waiting for the right moment, the right amount of money, the right permission, the right version of ourselves. While we wait, the years pass anyway, and the life we meant to begin stays a plan. Beginning does not require perfect conditions; it requires only a decision to stop postponing. The tragedy is not the ending, which comes to everyone, but the long delay before the start. The remedy is simple and urgent: begin now, with what you have, where you are.
