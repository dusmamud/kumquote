Circumstances hand each of us different material, but the making is ours. Two people can face the same situation and build entirely different lives from it, because attitude, choices and daily habits shape what raw events become. Blaming fate hands away the only power we actually hold, while accepting responsibility returns it. This does not deny hardship or luck; it simply insists that what we do with them matters. Life is not something that happens to us while we wait. It is something we are always making, one decision at a time.
