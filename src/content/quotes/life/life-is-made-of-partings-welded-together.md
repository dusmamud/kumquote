Every life is stitched together from hellos and goodbyes. We part from childhood, from places, from people, and from versions of ourselves, and each farewell leaves its mark on who we become. Dickens saw that parting is not an interruption of life but its very fabric; the moments of leaving shape us as much as the moments of arriving. Rather than fearing goodbyes, we can honour them as proof that something mattered enough to miss. Each parting welds a new piece into our story, making the whole stronger and more complete.
