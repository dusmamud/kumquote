We cannot control most of what happens to us: delays, illness, other people's choices, or sudden change. What we can control is our response. Charles Swindoll puts the balance at ten percent events and ninety percent reaction, because the same setback can break one person and strengthen another. A calm pause before reacting, a search for what can be learned, and a choice to act with dignity can transform a bad day. Your attitude is the one part of life that remains yours in every circumstance.
