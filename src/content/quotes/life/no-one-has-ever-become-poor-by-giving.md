Giving is usually counted as a loss. We give away time, money, attention, or care, and the arithmetic seems simple: less for us, more for them. But Anne Frank saw the other side of the calculation. What we give does not disappear; it returns as connection, gratitude, and a fuller life. The people who give freely are rarely the ones who feel poor — they are the ones who feel rich in relationships and purpose. Poverty of spirit comes not from giving too much but from holding on too tightly.
