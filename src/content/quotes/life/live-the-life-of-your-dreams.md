Most adventures are journeys to faraway places, but the biggest one is the journey toward the life you dream of. It takes more courage than any trip, because it asks you to leave the safety of what is familiar and build something new. The life of your dreams is not handed to you; it is chosen, day after day, through the work you do and the chances you take. That is what makes it an adventure: uncertain, demanding, and deeply rewarding. Take it, because no other journey will matter as much.
