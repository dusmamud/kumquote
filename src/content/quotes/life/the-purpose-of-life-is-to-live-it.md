Roosevelt refuses to reduce life's purpose to a single achievement or duty. Instead, the purpose is living itself, tasted fully and without fear. Experience is the material of a meaningful life: new places, new ideas, new people, new challenges. Fear narrows that material, keeping us in familiar routines that feel safe but slowly shrink us. Reaching out eagerly does not mean being reckless; it means choosing curiosity over comfort. A life rich in experience becomes rich in understanding. The invitation is simple and brave: say yes more often, and let life teach you through living it.
