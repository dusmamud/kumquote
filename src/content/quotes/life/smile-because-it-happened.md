Endings hurt, and it is natural to grieve them. A friendship that faded, a season that passed, a chapter that closed — the sadness is real. But grief is only half the story. The other half is gratitude that the good thing existed at all. We were lucky to have it, even briefly. Smiling because it happened does not erase the loss; it honors the gift. It shifts the focus from what we no longer hold to what we were given. Every ending carries this quiet choice: cry for the loss, or smile for the having had.
