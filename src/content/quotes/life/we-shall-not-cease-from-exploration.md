Exploration is not always about going somewhere new. Sometimes the longest journeys lead us back to where we began — home, family, the familiar street — and only then do we finally see it clearly. Experience changes the traveller, not the destination. The place has not moved, but we have: we return with wider eyes and a deeper understanding of what was always there. That is why the search never really ends, even when it circles back. To explore is to keep learning, and the greatest discovery is often a new way of seeing the old one.
