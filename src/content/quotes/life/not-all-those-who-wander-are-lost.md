Wandering has a bad reputation. We are taught that a straight road is the only respectable route, and that anyone without a fixed destination must be lost. But some of the most meaningful paths are winding ones. Exploring, changing direction, and taking the longer road are not signs of confusion — they are how we learn what the world contains and what we actually want. A person who wanders with open eyes is gathering experience, not wasting time. Being lost means having no sense of direction at all.
