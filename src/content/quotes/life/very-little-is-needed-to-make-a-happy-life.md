Happiness is often chased through possessions, status, and approval, yet it quietly lives much closer to home. Marcus Aurelius reminds us that a happy life requires very little from the outside world; it depends mostly on how we think about what we already have. When we stop demanding more and start appreciating enough, contentment becomes available immediately, without waiting for circumstances to change. Simplicity is not deprivation but freedom: fewer needs mean fewer disappointments. The happiest people are not those who have the most, but those who need the least.
