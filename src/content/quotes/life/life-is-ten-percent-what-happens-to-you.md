We cannot control most of what happens to us. Accidents, delays, other people's choices, and sudden changes arrive without asking. What we can control is our response, and that response shapes almost everything that follows. Two people can face the same setback and live very different lives, simply because one chooses patience and the other chooses bitterness. Your attitude is the one part of life that is truly yours. Guard it well, respond with calm and courage, and the ten percent you cannot control will never decide your whole story.
