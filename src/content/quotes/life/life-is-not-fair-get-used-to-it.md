Fairness is a hope, not a rule of nature, and this blunt line strips away the illusion that life owes us even treatment. Some people start ahead, some face hardship through no fault of their own, and complaining about the imbalance changes nothing. Acceptance is not surrender; it is the clear-eyed starting point for doing something useful. Once you stop waiting for the world to be fair, you can focus on what is actually in your hands: your effort, your choices, and your response. Life may not be fair, but it still leaves room to build something good.
