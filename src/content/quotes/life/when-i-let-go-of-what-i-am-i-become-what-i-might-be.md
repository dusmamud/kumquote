We hold tightly to who we think we are: our job, our reputation, our old habits, our image of ourselves. That grip feels safe, but it also traps us. Growth requires releasing the old identity so a new one can form. Letting go is not losing ourselves; it is making room for who we might become. The person we could be is often waiting on the other side of the identity we refuse to drop. This quote reminds us that change begins with release, and that our future self is already waiting to emerge.
