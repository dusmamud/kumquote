Hard times feel endless while we are inside them. The night seems permanent, and it is hard to believe morning will come. But darkness has never once lasted forever; every night in history has ended with a sunrise. This quote does not promise that hardship will vanish quickly, only that it will end. Hope is not naive optimism but the memory of how life works: seasons change, wounds heal, and difficult chapters close. When we are in the dark, the most useful thing we can do is hold on until the light returns, because it always does.
