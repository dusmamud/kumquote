The idea that you only live once is often used to justify reckless choices, but Mae West gives it a wiser meaning. One life is enough if it is lived with intention, joy, and courage. Doing it right does not mean doing everything; it means choosing what matters, loving deeply, laughing often, and not wasting years on regret or fear. Live in a way that you can look back on without wishing you had been braver, kinder, or more present when it mattered most.
