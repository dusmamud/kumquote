We are all given more gears than we ever use. Most people live in the same comfortable gear — the same routines, the same skills, the same safe opinions — and never discover what the higher gears can do. The higher gears are harder to reach: they demand effort, practice, and the willingness to feel awkward for a while. But they are also where the speed is, where life moves faster and further. A bicycle ridden only in first gear still moves, but it never shows what it was built for.
