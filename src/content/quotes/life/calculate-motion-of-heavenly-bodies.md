Newton could predict the movements of planets with perfect precision, yet he lost a fortune in the South Sea Bubble — and this remark is said to have followed that loss. The planets obey laws; people do not. Crowds panic, chase fashions, and abandon reason in ways no equation can capture. The line is a humbling reminder that intelligence in one field does not transfer to another, and that human behaviour is the hardest system of all to predict. The smartest minds fail when they mistake people for planets.
