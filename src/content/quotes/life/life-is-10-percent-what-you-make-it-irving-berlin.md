We cannot choose most of what happens to us. Traffic, a diagnosis, a delay, another person's decision, these arrive uninvited and often unfair. Berlin's arithmetic puts the real power elsewhere: only ten percent is what happens, ninety percent is how we take it. Two people can face the same setback and live two different stories, because of the response they chose. Attitude is the one part of life no circumstance can take without our permission. We may not control the event, but we always control the meaning we give it, and that meaning decides what happens next.
