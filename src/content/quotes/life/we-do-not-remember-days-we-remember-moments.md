Our memories are not made of ordinary days but of moments that stood out: a laugh, a sunset, a kind word, a sudden realisation. Pavese reminds us that life's value is measured in these bright fragments, not in the slow passage of time. This is both a warning and an invitation: if we drift through our days on autopilot, we will have few moments worth remembering. But if we pay attention, seek connection, and embrace small joys, we fill our lives with memories that last. The days will pass anyway; the moments are ours to create.
