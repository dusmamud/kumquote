Some feelings are too large or too tangled for words. Grief, joy, longing, and awe often sit beyond what sentences can carry. Music reaches them directly: a melody can say what a paragraph cannot, and a song can name a feeling we did not know we had. That is why music matters in every culture and every age. It gives voice to the inner life when language runs out. This quote honors music as more than entertainment; it is a second language, one that speaks exactly where words fall silent.
