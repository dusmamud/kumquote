Life has a way of humbling even the most confident among us. Plans fail, strengths prove limited, and moments arrive when we must admit we were wrong or ask for help. Barrie calls this a lesson rather than a punishment, because humility is something life teaches slowly, through experience rather than argument. Each setback gently corrects the idea that we control everything. The quote offers comfort as well as warning: if humility is the lesson, then our failures are not wasted. They are the curriculum, shaping us into people who listen better, judge less, and treat others with greater kindness.
