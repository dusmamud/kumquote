A successful life is not built by copying someone else's path. It begins with discovering what you are meant to do — the work, the service, or the craft that fits your talents and values. That discovery takes honesty and time, and it often comes through trying, failing, and listening to yourself. Once you find it, the second part is simple but demanding: do it. Commit fully, work steadily, and refuse to be pulled away by distraction or fear. Purpose plus action is the secret. Find what is yours to do, then give it your whole life.
