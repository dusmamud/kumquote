Whatever our age, most of our story is still unwritten. The pages behind us hold lessons, but the pages ahead hold possibilities we cannot yet imagine — people we have not met, places we have not seen, versions of ourselves we have not become. That is a reason for hope, not fear. A book is not judged by one chapter, and neither is a life. Keep turning the pages: stay curious, stay open, and let the next chapter surprise you. The best parts of the story may be the ones you have not read yet.
