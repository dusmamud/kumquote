Searching for ourselves by looking inward alone can become a circle: the more we examine our own feelings, the more lost we feel. Service breaks that circle. When we help someone else — listening, carrying, teaching, or simply showing up — our attention moves outward, and in that outward movement we discover what we are actually good for. Purpose rarely announces itself in quiet reflection; it shows up in the middle of useful work. The person we become through serving others is often more real than the person we were trying to find.
