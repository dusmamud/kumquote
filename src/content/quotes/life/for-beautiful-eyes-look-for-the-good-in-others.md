Real beauty, Hepburn suggests, is not applied from outside but practised from within. Beautiful eyes are not a matter of shape or colour; they come from looking for the good in people instead of their faults. Beautiful lips are not shaped by cosmetics but by the habit of speaking kindly. And poise — the calm confidence people admire — comes from knowing we are never truly alone, that we belong to something larger than ourselves. The advice turns beauty into a daily practice rather than a possession.
