Nature never hurries, yet everything gets done. Trees grow slowly, rivers carve stone over centuries, and seasons arrive exactly when they should. We, by contrast, rush everything and wonder why we feel exhausted. Patience is not laziness; it is trust in a process that works on its own schedule. This quote invites us to slow down and match nature's pace: to let things grow, to wait for the right season, and to believe that steady, unhurried effort accomplishes more than frantic haste ever will.
