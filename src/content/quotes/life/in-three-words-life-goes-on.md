Loss, failure, and disappointment can make it feel as though life has stopped. Robert Frost offers a gentle truth that is both sobering and hopeful: life goes on. Time does not wait for our grief to finish, and the world keeps turning even when our hearts are heavy. That continuity can feel cruel at first, but it also carries a promise. New mornings arrive, new people enter our lives, and new possibilities appear. You only need to trust that movement itself, one day at a time, will slowly carry you forward again.
