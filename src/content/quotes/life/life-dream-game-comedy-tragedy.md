The same life looks completely different depending on who is living it. The wise treat it as a dream — fleeting, strange, not to be taken too seriously. The fool treats it as a game to be won or lost. The rich can afford to laugh at it, while the poor feel its weight as tragedy. The quote is funny and sad at once, because it is true: our circumstances color everything. Yet it also hides a choice. We cannot always change our circumstances, but we can choose the eyes we see them with. Wisdom is choosing the dream.
