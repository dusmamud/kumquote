Life asks us to hold two opposite skills at once. Sometimes we must make things happen: plan, push, decide, and take responsibility for the outcome. At other times we must let things happen: wait, trust, release control, and allow events to unfold in their own time. Neither skill alone is enough. Those who only push exhaust themselves fighting what cannot be forced, and those who only wait never begin. Wisdom is knowing which dance the moment requires. Learn both steps, and life becomes less a struggle and more a rhythm you can move with.
