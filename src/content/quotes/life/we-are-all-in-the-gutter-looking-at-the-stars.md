Everyone faces hardship, disappointment, and moments when life feels like the gutter. What separates people is not their circumstances but where they choose to look. Some stare down at the mud and see only misery, while others lift their eyes to the stars and find hope, beauty, and direction. Looking up does not deny the difficulty; it refuses to let difficulty define the whole of life. Hope is a choice of attention, and those who keep their gaze on the stars find the strength to climb out of the gutter one step at a time.
