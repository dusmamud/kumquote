A life lived without reflection is a life lived on autopilot, following habits and expectations without ever asking whether they are right. Socrates believed that questioning your beliefs, your choices, and your values is what makes life meaningful. Examination is not self-criticism; it is curiosity about your own life. Why do I believe this? What do I truly want? Am I living by my own values or someone else's? Asking these questions is uncomfortable, but it is the price of a life that is genuinely yours.
