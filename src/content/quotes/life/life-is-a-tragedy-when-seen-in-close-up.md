Seen up close, life is full of small tragedies: losses, embarrassments, and plans that fall apart. But step back, and the same events often look different, even comic, because distance reveals the pattern we could not see while we were inside it. Time gives us that long shot, turning today's disaster into tomorrow's story. This does not make pain unreal; it makes it survivable. When life feels tragic, try widening the frame. The scene that hurts now may one day be the one you laugh about, and that change of view is a kind of wisdom.
