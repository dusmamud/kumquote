We spend much of life waiting for conditions to improve: waiting for the storm to pass, the crisis to end, the perfect moment to arrive. But life does not pause while we wait, and the rain keeps falling whether we dance or not. Learning to dance in the rain means finding joy and movement inside difficult seasons instead of postponing happiness until they end. The storm may not lift soon, but your life is happening now. Step into it, keep moving, and discover that even grey days can hold music if you choose to hear it.
