Dirksen's bluntness makes the point unforgettable: only the dead and the incapacitated never change their minds. Life moves, facts change, and a thinking person changes with them. Refusing to update an opinion is not loyalty; it is a refusal to learn. Changing your mind is evidence that new information reached you and that you had the honesty to let it matter. Growth is not a single decision but a habit of revision. Stay open, stay curious, and let your views grow as your life does.
