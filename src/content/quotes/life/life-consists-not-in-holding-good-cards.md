Billings uses a card game to say something about fairness. Nobody chooses the hand they are dealt — not their family, their health, or their luck. Complaining about the cards changes nothing, and envying someone else's hand changes less. What matters is how skilfully we play what we have: the patience to wait for the right moment, the courage to take a chance, the grace to lose a round without losing our temper. People with modest hands often win by playing them well, while brilliant hands are wasted by careless players. Judge a life by the playing, not the dealing.
