Life does not come with a promise to be easy, and pretending otherwise only makes the hard parts feel like personal failure. Everyone faces difficulty; that is the shared price of being alive. The quote's dark humour is also its comfort: since life is hard for all of us, we can stop asking why it is hard for us and start asking what we will do with the difficulty. Accepting the hardness frees us from resentment. We can then meet each day honestly, with less complaint and more courage, and find our own way through.
