We count our lives in years, but we remember them in moments. A sunset that stops us mid-step, a laugh that arrives out of nowhere, the first time we hold someone we love — these are the seconds that stay with us. They cannot be scheduled or bought; they can only be noticed. That is the quiet warning inside this quote: a life spent rushing from task to task collects many breaths and very few moments. Slow down enough to be present, and ordinary days begin to hold extraordinary seconds. Those are what we are really living for.
