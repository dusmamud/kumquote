We measure life in money, titles, and possessions, but Robbins says the real measure is the people around us. A rich life with cold relationships feels empty, while a modest life with warm ones feels full. Relationships decide how our days feel: who we laugh with, who listens when we struggle, who celebrates when we win. Everything else is background. If the quality of life depends on the quality of relationships, then tending them is not a luxury but the main work. Invest in people, and the life improves with them.
