Santayana strips away the romance. Life is not a show for our entertainment, nor a banquet laid out for our pleasure. It is a predicament — a situation we find ourselves in, full of limits, risks, and choices we cannot avoid. That sounds bleak, but it is also freeing. Once we stop waiting to be entertained or served, we start taking responsibility for how we live. A predicament demands judgement, patience, and courage. The feast may be smaller than we hoped, but the person who meets their predicament lives more fully than the one waiting for the curtain to rise.
