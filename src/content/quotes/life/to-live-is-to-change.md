Newman treats change not as a threat but as the definition of living. A person who never changes their mind has stopped learning, and a life that never changes direction has stopped growing. Perfection, in his view, is not a fixed state we reach once but a habit of honest revision — admitting mistakes, updating beliefs, and letting new experience reshape us. Every change is a death of who we were and a birth of who we might become. The people we admire most are rarely the ones who stayed the same; they are the ones who kept becoming.
