Difficulty and opportunity are often the same situation seen from different angles. When things go wrong, most people see only the problem, but hidden inside every crisis is a chance to learn, to innovate, or to grow stronger. Einstein knew that breakthroughs rarely come from comfortable times; they come when old ways stop working and we are forced to think differently. The opportunity is not always obvious, and finding it takes patience and courage. But those who look for the opening inside the difficulty often discover that the hardest moments were the most valuable ones.
