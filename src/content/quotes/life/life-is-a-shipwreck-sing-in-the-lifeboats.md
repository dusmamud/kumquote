Voltaire's image is darkly funny and deeply true: life will wreck our plans, sink our certainties, and leave us clinging to whatever floats. But even in the lifeboat, we have a choice. We can sit in silence and despair, or we can sing, finding joy, humour, and companionship in the midst of disaster. Singing does not fix the shipwreck, but it keeps our spirits alive and reminds us that we are still here, still together, still capable of delight. The ship may be gone, but the song is ours, and it is worth singing anyway.
