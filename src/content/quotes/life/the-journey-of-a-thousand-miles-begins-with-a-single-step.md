A thousand miles sounds impossible when we stand at the start, and the size of the goal can stop us before we begin. But every long journey, whether it is learning a language, building a career, or changing a habit, is made of single steps, and each step is small enough to take. The first step matters most because it breaks the stillness and proves movement is possible. We do not need to see the whole road, only the next step. Begin, and the distance takes care of itself, one step at a time.
