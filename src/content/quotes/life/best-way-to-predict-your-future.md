Waiting for the future is a passive act. We watch, we hope, and we guess at what might arrive, as if tomorrow were weather we cannot influence. But the future is not a forecast; it is a construction site, and our daily choices are the materials. Every habit we build, every skill we learn, and every relationship we tend becomes part of what is coming. People who create their future do not have special knowledge of what lies ahead — they simply refuse to leave it to chance.
