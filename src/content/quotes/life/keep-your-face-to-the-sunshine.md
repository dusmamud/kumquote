Where we fix our attention shapes what we see. Keller's image is simple: face the light, and shadows fall behind you, out of view. This is not pretending that darkness does not exist; it is choosing what to look toward each day. Gratitude, hope and purpose act like sunshine, filling the mind so that fear and complaint have less room to grow. We cannot always change the weather of our lives, but we can choose our direction. Turn toward the light, and the shadows will take care of themselves.
