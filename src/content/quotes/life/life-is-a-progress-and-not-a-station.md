Holmes reminds us that life is movement, not arrival. We often treat happiness as a destination — a promotion, a house, a milestone — and feel lost when we reach it and the feeling fades. But growth has no finish line; each stage prepares us for the next. The value of a day is not in where it leaves us but in what it teaches us. When we accept that we are always becoming, setbacks stop feeling like failures and start looking like part of the road. Keep walking, keep learning, and let the journey itself be enough.
