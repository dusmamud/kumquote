Endings feel like losses, but they are also the soil in which beginnings grow. Every new chapter in a life — a new job, a new home, a new way of living — follows the closing of something else. We resist endings because they hurt, yet without them nothing new has room to appear. The end of one season is not the end of the story; it is the turning of the page. When something in your life closes, look for what it has made possible rather than only for what it took away.
