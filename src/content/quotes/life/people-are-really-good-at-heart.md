These words were written by a young girl hiding from cruelty, in circumstances that gave her every reason to believe the opposite. That is what makes them extraordinary. Faith in human goodness is easy when life is kind; it costs almost nothing. But to hold on to that belief while surrounded by evidence against it is an act of courage. Anne Frank chose to believe that the cruelty she saw was not the whole of humanity, and that choice has outlived her by generations.
