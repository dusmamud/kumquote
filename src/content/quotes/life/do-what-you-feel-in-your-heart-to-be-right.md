Criticism is unavoidable. Whatever we do, someone will find fault with it — too bold, too cautious, too early, too late. Since the criticism comes either way, it makes no sense to let it decide our actions. The only sensible course is to follow our own conscience and accept the criticism as the price of having a position at all. People who shape their lives around avoiding disapproval end up with neither approval nor integrity; they simply drift. Doing what we believe is right does not silence the critics, but it gives us the knowledge that we acted honestly.
