Much of our stress comes not from life itself, but from the complications we add: overthinking, comparing ourselves with others, chasing approval, and filling every hour with noise. Confucius points to a simpler truth. Eat well, rest enough, speak honestly, care for others, and do your work with attention. When life feels tangled, ask what can be removed rather than what should be added. Simplicity is not laziness; it is clarity. By letting go of unnecessary worries and obligations, you create space for what truly matters: health, relationships, and a quiet sense of purpose.
