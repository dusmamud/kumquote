Following the crowd is comfortable because the road is already paved and the destination is known. But a life lived entirely on other people's paths is never fully our own. This quote urges us to step off the marked trail and go where no path exists yet. That takes courage, because there is no map and no guarantee. But the people who leave trails are the ones who discover new places, new ideas, and new ways of living. A meaningful life is not found by following footsteps; it is made by leaving our own.
