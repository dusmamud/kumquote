A life needs a task larger than itself, and Moore adds a twist: the task must be impossible to finish. A goal we can complete leaves us empty afterwards, but an endless task gives every day its direction. The sculptor never truly finishes his work; each piece teaches him something that sends him back to the stone. That is the secret — not a destination but a devotion that outlasts any single achievement. Choose work so big that a lifetime is not enough for it, and you will never run out of purpose.
