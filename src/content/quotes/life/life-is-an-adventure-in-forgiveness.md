Forgiveness is often described as something we do for others, but it is really an adventure we take for ourselves. Carrying old wounds keeps us tied to the past, replaying injuries that have already happened. To forgive is to step into unknown territory, letting go of the story that someone owes us pain for pain. It does not excuse what happened, and it does not require forgetting. It simply frees our hands and our hearts for the life still ahead. The adventure is in discovering how light we feel once we finally set the burden down.
