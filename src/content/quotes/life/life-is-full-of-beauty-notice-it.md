Beauty is easy to miss when we rush through our days with our eyes on our worries. It hides in small things: morning light, a child's laugh, rain on a window, a stranger's kindness. Noticing it is a choice, a small act of attention that changes how the whole day feels. The world does not become more beautiful because we notice it; we become more alive because we do. Slow down for a moment and look around. Life is full of beauty, quietly waiting for someone to notice it, and that someone can be you.
