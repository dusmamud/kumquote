A closed door holds our attention because loss feels louder than possibility. Keller warns that staring at what ended can blind us to the door that has already opened beside it. Grief and disappointment deserve their time, but they should not become our permanent address. New chances rarely arrive with a fanfare; they wait quietly, noticed only when we lift our eyes. Turn from the closed door, look around with fresh attention, and step toward the opening that is already there.
