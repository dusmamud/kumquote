Life does not come with a manual, and the only way to learn it is to try things. Each experiment — a new skill, a new place, a new way of working — teaches something, whether it succeeds or fails. People who experiment widely gather more knowledge about themselves and the world than those who stay safely in routine. A failed experiment is not wasted time; it is a result, and results are how wisdom is built. The fear of being wrong keeps many people from ever finding out what is right for them.
