Meaning is not hidden somewhere waiting to be found, like a lost object. It is made, day by day, out of what we choose to care about. The work we take seriously, the people we love well, and the problems we help solve — these are the raw materials of a meaningful life. Waiting for meaning to arrive on its own leaves us empty, because meaning follows action, not the other way around. When we give our days a purpose, even a small one, the days return the favour by feeling worthwhile.
