Predictability feels safe, but a fully predictable life would be dull. Surprise, change, and uncertainty are what give life its flavour — new people, unexpected chances, lessons you never planned to learn. Trying to control everything only creates anxiety, because life will not obey your plans. The wiser approach is to welcome uncertainty as part of living. Be prepared, but stay open. Let the unexpected teach you, stretch you, and sometimes delight you. A life without surprise may feel secure, but it would lose the very things that make it worth living.
