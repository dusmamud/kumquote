Silence in the face of wrongdoing feels safe, but it has a cost. Each time we stay quiet about something that matters — an injustice, a lie, a harm done to someone weaker — a small part of our own life dims, because we have chosen comfort over conscience. A life is not measured only by its years but by whether it stood for anything. Speaking up can carry real risk, yet history moves because ordinary people refused to stay silent. Use your voice while you have it; that is what keeps a life truly alive.
