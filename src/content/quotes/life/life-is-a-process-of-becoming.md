We often treat ourselves as finished products, judging who we are by a single moment or mistake. But life is a process of becoming, not a fixed state. Each day adds a new layer: something learned, something survived, something understood for the first time. The states we pass through, even the painful ones, are part of the combination that makes us who we are. Give yourself permission to be unfinished. Growth is slow and rarely visible while it happens, but every day you are becoming someone a little different, and a little more yourself.
