Hard experiences feel like interruptions, as if life were supposed to run smoothly. Ford offers a kinder reading: each experience, even the hard one, makes us bigger. The failure that taught patience, the loss that taught what we value, the embarrassment that taught humility. We rarely recognize growth while it happens; it looks like trouble, not education. Only later do we see the chapter added something we could not have gained otherwise. Life is not a series of obstacles in the way of our growth. The experiences are the growth, and each one leaves us a little larger than before.
