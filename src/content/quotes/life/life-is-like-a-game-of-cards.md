Life rarely hands us the circumstances we would choose. We are born into families, times, and situations we did not select, and chance keeps dealing unexpected cards. Nehru's image reminds us that freedom does not lie in controlling the deal, but in how we respond to it. Our choices, attitude, and effort shape what the hand becomes. Blaming fate wastes the game; playing it well turns even a poor hand into something meaningful. The power is not in the cards we hold, but in the way we choose to play them.
