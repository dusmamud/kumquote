Our lives are not shaped by one grand decision but by the thousands of small choices we make every day: what to eat, whom to call, whether to try, whether to quit. Each choice seems tiny alone, yet together they become the whole of who we are and where we end up. Camus reminds us that this is empowering rather than frightening, because it means our future is being written right now, in the choice we make next. We cannot undo past choices, but we can always make a better one today, and that is how a life changes.
