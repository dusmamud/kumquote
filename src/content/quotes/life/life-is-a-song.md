Sai Baba refuses to let life be only one thing. It is a song, a game, a challenge, a dream, a sacrifice, and love, all at once, and each face of it asks something different from us. Sing when there is music, play when there is a game, rise when there is a challenge, and give when there is a need. The quote is a call to participate fully rather than to watch from the sidelines. Life is not a problem to be solved but a series of invitations, each one asking for our wholehearted yes.
