A good life is not made of endless pleasure, and it is not made of endless hardship either. It is made of balance: knowing how to enjoy small pleasures fully without demanding that they last forever, and knowing how to endure difficulty without letting it harden you. The art lies in the proportion. People who chase only enjoyment grow restless when it fades, while people who only endure forget why they are enduring. Enjoy the little that is good today, carry the hard parts with patience, and let both shape a life that is steady rather than extreme.
