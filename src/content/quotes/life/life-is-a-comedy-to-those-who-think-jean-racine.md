Two people can live the same day and describe two different lives. One tells it as a comedy, noticing the absurdity, the small mercies, the lucky turns. The other tells it as a tragedy, counting losses and slights. Racine's line suggests the difference is not the events but the mind that meets them. Thinking means stepping back far enough to see the whole pattern, including its humor. Feeling means staying close to the wound. Both are human. But a life that can be thought about, laughed at, and learned from is easier to keep living.
