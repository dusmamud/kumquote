A bicycle stays upright only while it moves. Stop, and balance becomes impossible. Life works the same way. When you stop learning, growing, and moving forward, you begin to wobble — doubt grows, fear takes hold, and problems feel larger. Movement does not have to be fast or dramatic. A small step, a new habit, a fresh idea can restore your balance. Keep moving toward what matters, even slowly, and stability follows. Life rewards motion. If you feel unsteady, do not freeze. Pedal forward, gently and steadily, and balance will return.
