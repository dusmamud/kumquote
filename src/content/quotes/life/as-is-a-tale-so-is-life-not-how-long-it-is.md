A story is not judged by its length, and neither is a life. What matters is the quality of the days: the love given, the work done honestly, the courage shown, and the moments truly lived. A short life filled with purpose can be richer than a long life spent drifting. This idea frees us from counting years and invites us to fill each day well instead. Live deeply rather than merely long. When the tale of your life is told, let it be a good one — not because it was long, but because it was well lived.
