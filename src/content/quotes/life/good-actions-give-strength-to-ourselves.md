Doing good is not only a gift to others; it strengthens the person who does it. Each good action builds confidence, steadies the conscience, and makes the next good action easier. And goodness spreads: people who witness a generous or brave act are more likely to act generously or bravely themselves. Kindness is contagious in the best way, moving from one person to another without ever running out. You do not need a grand gesture to begin; a small act of help, honestly given, starts the chain.
