We count our lives in years, as if more time automatically meant more living, yet a long life can be shallow and a shorter one deeply full. Depth comes from attention, love, and courage — from how much we feel and give, not how many days we accumulate. A single day lived with open eyes can hold more life than a year of drifting. This line invites us to stop measuring by the calendar and start measuring by richness: the quality of our days, the honesty of our relationships, and our engagement with the world.
