Kay, a pioneer of personal computing, offers a practical alternative to fortune-telling: stop guessing what will happen and start building it. Prediction is passive — it waits for the future to arrive and hopes to be right. Invention is active — it chooses a future worth having and works toward it, piece by piece. The career you want, the skill you need, the life you imagine — each begins as an invention, a small act of making today. Build the future you would like to predict, and prediction becomes unnecessary.
