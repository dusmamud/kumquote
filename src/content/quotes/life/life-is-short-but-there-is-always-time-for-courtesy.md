Life is short, and it is easy to treat other people as obstacles in our hurry. But courtesy costs almost nothing and changes almost everything: a kind word, a held door, a moment of patience. These small acts are how we tell other people they matter. They also change us, making us more attentive and less self-absorbed. In a brief life, the way we treat others may be the most lasting thing we leave behind. Courtesy is not old-fashioned politeness; it is respect made visible, and there is always time for it.
