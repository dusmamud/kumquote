Failure only counts as failure when you stop. Every attempt that does not work still teaches you something: a wrong turn, a flawed assumption, a method to discard. Edison understood that each dead end narrowed the path to the answer, turning mistakes into information. Most people quit after a few tries and call the result failure, but the inventor keeps going until the list of wrong ways finally reveals the right one. Reframe your setbacks this way, and every effort becomes progress, no matter how many attempts it takes to get there.
