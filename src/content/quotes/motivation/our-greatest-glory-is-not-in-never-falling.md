A life without falls is a life without risks, and a life without risks achieves very little. Confucius teaches that glory is not found in avoiding failure but in the act of rising after it, again and again. Each recovery makes us stronger, wiser, and more certain of our own endurance. People admire those who never fall, but they are inspired by those who fall and rise, because that story proves that setbacks can be survived. Our falls are not the end of our story; they are the chapters that make the rising worth celebrating.
