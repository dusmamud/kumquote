Happiness is often treated like a destination we might stumble upon, a lucky break, a perfect set of circumstances that will arrive. Kimball disagrees. A happy life is not found lying somewhere waiting to be picked up; it is built, choice by choice, in the ordinary hours of an ordinary day. It is made of small decisions: to be kind when it is easier to be sharp, to notice what is good instead of tallying what is missing, to do work that matters. None of these moments feels dramatic. Together, they become a life that feels like home.
