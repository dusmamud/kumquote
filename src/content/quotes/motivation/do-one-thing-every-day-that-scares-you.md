Fear shrinks when we face it and grows when we avoid it. This daily challenge is not about reckless risk but about deliberately stepping beyond comfort, one small act at a time. Speaking up, trying something new, or admitting a mistake all count as brave steps that stretch what we believe we can do. Each time we survive the thing we feared, our confidence expands and the next fear feels smaller. Courage is not the absence of fear; it is a practice, built day by day, until boldness becomes a habit.
