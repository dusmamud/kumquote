We spend a lot of energy wishing the wind would change: the timing, the market, the other person, the luck of the draw. Dean's image reminds us that the wind was never ours to command. What is ours is the sail, our attitude, our preparation, our response. A skilled sailor reaches the same destination in any weather, not by fighting the wind but by working with it. Life works the same way. We cannot choose every condition we meet, but we can always adjust our approach. That shift, from wishing to adjusting, is often the difference between drifting and arriving.
