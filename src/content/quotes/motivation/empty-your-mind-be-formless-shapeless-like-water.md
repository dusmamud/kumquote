Water has no fixed shape, yet it can wear down stone. It flows around obstacles instead of breaking against them, and it takes the shape of whatever holds it. This quote asks us to be like that: flexible rather than rigid, responsive rather than brittle. When plans collapse or circumstances change, the rigid person shatters while the adaptable person finds a new way forward. Emptying the mind means letting go of fixed ideas about how things must happen. Strength, in this view, is not hardness but the ability to adapt without losing yourself.
