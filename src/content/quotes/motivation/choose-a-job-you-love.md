Work takes up most of our waking lives, so the difference between loving it and enduring it is enormous. When we love what we do, effort feels like engagement rather than exhaustion, and even difficult days carry meaning. Often it is about finding the part of the work that matters and growing it. Passion does not remove hard days, but it changes our relationship with them. The quote invites us to choose work that fits who we are, because a life spent doing what we love rarely feels like a life spent working.
