Big changes rarely arrive all at once. Like water filling a jug one drop at a time, progress is usually the quiet sum of small efforts repeated day after day. A page read, a minute of practice, a kind word, none looks like much on its own, yet together they fill the vessel. This is hopeful news for anyone who feels behind, because it means you do not need a dramatic breakthrough to begin. You only need the next small drop. Keep adding them patiently, and one day you will look back and see how full the jug has become.
