Defeat is an event, not a verdict. Everyone loses sometimes — the job, the match, the election, the argument — and the loss itself says nothing final about who you are. What decides the ending is what you do next. The person who stands up, learns the lesson, and tries again is still in the game, no matter how many times they have fallen. Quitting is the only true defeat, because it closes the door on every future you might have built. So lose if you must, but never let a loss be the last word.
