We call people lucky when good fortune seems to find them, but look closer and there is usually a long period of quiet preparation behind the moment. Opportunity arrives for many, yet only the prepared can act on it in time. Preparation is the part we control: learning the skill, doing the work, staying ready when nothing is happening. Luck, then, is not random at all but the meeting point of readiness and timing. Instead of waiting for fortune to smile, we can spend our waiting time preparing, so that when the door opens we are already standing there.
