Putting off a duty does not make it disappear; it only moves it to a day when it may be harder to face. The problems we avoid today grow quietly in the background, gathering interest like an unpaid debt. Responsibility cannot be outrun, only postponed, and postponement rarely makes the task smaller. Facing it now, while it is still manageable, is usually the kinder choice for our future selves. Tomorrow will arrive whether we are ready or not. The work we do today decides whether we meet it prepared or still running from what we refused to handle.
