A life lived only within easy reach is a small life. The things we can already grasp ask nothing of us, and they give back only what we already have. Reaching beyond our grasp is what pulls us forward — it is the reason we learn, practise, and attempt things that might fail. Ambition is not arrogance; it is the recognition that we are meant to grow past our present limits. If our goals never stretch us, they are not goals at all, merely errands.
