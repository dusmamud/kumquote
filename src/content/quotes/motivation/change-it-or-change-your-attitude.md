Life hands us two kinds of problems: the ones we can fix and the ones we cannot. Wisdom is knowing which is which — and refusing to waste our energy on the wrong one. When change is possible, we should act: speak up, move on, rebuild, try again. When it is not, the only freedom left is the attitude we bring to it. That is not surrender; it is strength. Complaining about what we cannot change only chains us to it. Choosing our response, even in hard circumstances, keeps our dignity and our peace intact.
