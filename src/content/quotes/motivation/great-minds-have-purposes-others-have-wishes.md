A wish costs nothing. We can hold a hundred of them at once, admire them from a distance, and never change a single habit because of them. A purpose is different: it asks for a plan, a schedule, and a sacrifice. It turns a vague longing into a direction and then into daily work. The difference between the two is not talent or luck but the willingness to commit. Wishes wait for the world to change; purposes change the person who holds them.
