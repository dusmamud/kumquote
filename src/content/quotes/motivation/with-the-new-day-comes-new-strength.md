Each morning arrives with a gift we rarely notice: a fresh supply of strength and a mind wiped a little cleaner than the night before. Yesterday's failures do not have to set today's limits, because the new day brings new resources to meet them. Roosevelt, who rebuilt her public life again and again, understood that renewal is not rare but daily. We do not need to wait for a new year or a new chapter; the next sunrise is enough. The strength we lacked yesterday may be there this morning.
