There are moments when strength runs out and the only option left is to hold on. This line does not promise that the struggle will end soon, nor does it pretend that hanging on is easy. It says that endurance itself is a victory. A knot is a stubborn act of refusal, a decision not to let go when letting go would be simpler. Hard times pass more often than they feel permanent, and the people who survive them are the ones who refused to quit one more day. Hold tight, breathe, and wait for the rope to steady.
