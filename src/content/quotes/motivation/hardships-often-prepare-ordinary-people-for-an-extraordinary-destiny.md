Hard times feel unfair while you are inside them, yet they often teach what easy times cannot. Struggle builds patience, courage, humility, and strength. It forces you to discover resources you did not know you had and to depend on what truly matters. Ordinary people do not become extraordinary by avoiding difficulty; they grow through it. The hardship itself is not the destiny, but the preparation for it. If you are facing a hard season now, do not assume it is wasted. It may be shaping you for something greater than you can yet see.
