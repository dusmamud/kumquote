Emerson gives fear a job: it marks the exact place where growth is waiting. The things we are afraid to do are rarely trivial; they are the honest conversation, the bold attempt, the step into the unknown that our life actually needs. Fear, in this reading, is not a warning to retreat but a signpost pointing forward. Doing the feared thing shrinks it. Each time we act in spite of fear, the fear loses a little authority, and our confidence gains ground. Courage is a practice, built one frightening act at a time. Start with the smallest fear today.
