Achievement begins in the mind, long before it appears in the world. Hill's famous principle links three steps: first we imagine a goal clearly, then we believe it is possible, and only then can we achieve it. Without the vision, there is nothing to work toward; without belief, the vision dies at the first obstacle. Belief is not wishful thinking but the fuel that sustains effort through difficulty. When conception and belief align, the mind finds ways, resources, and persistence that doubt would never discover. What we truly believe we can do, we usually find a way to do.
