This line cuts through the habit of half-hearted effort. Saying we will try often gives us a quiet escape route, a way to fail without fully committing. Real progress begins when that escape route disappears and we decide to act completely. Trying is not the same as doing; it keeps one foot outside the door. When we remove the option of a half attempt, our focus sharpens, our energy gathers, and our chances improve. Commitment does not guarantee success, but it changes how we show up.
