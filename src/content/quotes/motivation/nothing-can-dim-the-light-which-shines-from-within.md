Some lights depend on the weather outside, and some burn from a source no storm can reach. Angelou points to the second kind: the courage, kindness, and hope that live inside a person. Circumstances can take away comfort, status, or applause, but they cannot switch off what a person carries within unless that person lets them. When the world grows dark, the answer is not to wait for someone else to shine a light, but to protect the one already burning. An inner light is not loud; it simply refuses to go out.
