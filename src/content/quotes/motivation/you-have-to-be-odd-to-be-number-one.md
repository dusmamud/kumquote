Being first means being different, and different always looks odd at first. Those who reach the top rarely get there by copying everyone else; they get there by trusting their own strange ideas and refusing to blend in. Fitting in feels safe, but it guarantees an average result, while standing out carries the risk that makes greatness possible. Dr. Seuss built a career on delightful oddness, and his words remind us that our quirks are not flaws to hide but the very qualities that set us apart. Embrace being odd; it may be your greatest advantage.
