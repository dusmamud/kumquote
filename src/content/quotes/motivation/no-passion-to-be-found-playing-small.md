Playing small feels safe, but it starves the soul. Mandela knew from prison and from presidency alike that a life lived below its capacity is a quiet tragedy, not a modest virtue. The talents you were given were not meant to be hidden; they were meant to serve something larger than yourself. Refusing to shrink is not arrogance, it is honesty about what you can contribute. Dream bigger, stand taller, and let your life be large enough to matter. The world does not need smaller versions of you; it needs the fullest one.
