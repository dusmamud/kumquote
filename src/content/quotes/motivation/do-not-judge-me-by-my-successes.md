Success can be misleading. A smooth record may simply mean the path was easy, while a life of falls and recoveries shows something deeper. Mandela asks to be measured by resilience: how many times he rose after being knocked down, and whether he kept his purpose intact. Falling is not the opposite of achievement; it is often the price of attempting anything that matters. The real question is not how often we stumble, but whether we stand up again, learn, and continue with our head held high.
