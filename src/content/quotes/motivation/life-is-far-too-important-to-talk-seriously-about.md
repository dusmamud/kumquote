Life is serious enough on its own; treating every moment with solemnity only makes it heavier. Humor gives us distance from our troubles and reminds us that most of what worries us will look small in a year. This quote is not an excuse to avoid responsibility, but a reminder that lightness is a form of wisdom. People who can laugh at themselves recover faster, connect more easily, and endure hardship with grace. Taking life less seriously does not mean caring less; it means carrying our cares with a lighter step and a freer heart.
