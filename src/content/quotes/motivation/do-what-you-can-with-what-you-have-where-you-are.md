Waiting for perfect conditions is the quietest way to waste a life. You will never have all the money, all the time, or all the certainty you want, and the people who move forward are the ones who start anyway. Use the tools already in your hands, the hour already in your day, and the place you already stand. Small honest effort beats grand plans that never leave the notebook. Begin with what is available, learn as you go, and let each step create the resources the next step needs. Progress belongs to those who act now.
