Goldwyn punctures the myth of luck as a lottery that falls on the chosen few. In his experience, luck follows effort the way a shadow follows a walker: the harder you work, the more often you find yourself in the right place at the right time. Hard work multiplies the chances of a fortunate encounter, sharpens the skill to recognize it, and builds the reputation that brings opportunities to your door. What looks like luck from outside is usually preparation meeting a moment, and preparation is something we control. If you want better fortune, do not wait for it.
