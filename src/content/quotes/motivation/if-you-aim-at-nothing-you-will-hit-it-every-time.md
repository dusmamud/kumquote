A life without a target drifts, not because effort is missing but because effort has nowhere to land. Ziglar's warning is blunt: aimless days reliably hit exactly what they aimed at, which is nothing. A goal does not need to be grand to be useful; it only needs to be clear enough to guide today's choices. When the target is known, every small action gains a direction and setbacks become corrections rather than defeats. Choose a target, even a modest one, and watch scattered effort turn into steady progress.
