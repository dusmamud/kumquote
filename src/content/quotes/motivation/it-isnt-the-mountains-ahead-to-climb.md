We usually blame the big obstacles for our exhaustion, but Ali points to the small irritation we ignore. A pebble in the shoe does not stop the climb, yet every step rubs a little more, until the whole journey feels unbearable. Small resentments, unfinished tasks and unspoken worries work the same way: they drain energy quietly, day after day. The wise move is to stop early and remove them, however trivial they seem. Deal with the small thing now, and the mountain becomes far easier to climb.
