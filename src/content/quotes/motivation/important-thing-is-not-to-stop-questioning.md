Curiosity is the engine of every discovery, and this line defends it simply: keep asking. The moment we stop questioning, learning stops too, and certainty hardens into habit. Questions open doors that answers alone cannot, because a good question reframes the problem and reveals what we had been missing. Children ask without embarrassment, and the wisest adults keep that habit alive. You do not need to have all the answers to move forward; you need the willingness to wonder, to probe, and to admit what you do not know. Stay curious, and the world keeps teaching you.
