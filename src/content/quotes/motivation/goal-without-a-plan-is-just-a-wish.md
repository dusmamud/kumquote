Wishes are pleasant, but they have no engine. A goal becomes real the moment it gets a plan: a first step, a date, a measure of progress. Without that, even the sincerest dream stays in the realm of someday. Planning is not the enemy of dreaming; it is the way dreams get built. Break the big goal into small actions, schedule the first one, and do it. The plan does not need to be perfect — it needs to exist. A wish waits. A goal with a plan moves. That is the entire difference.
