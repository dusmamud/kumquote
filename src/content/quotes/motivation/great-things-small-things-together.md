We admire great achievements from a distance and imagine they arrived all at once. Up close, they are always a collection of small things: one brushstroke, one page, one practice session, repeated until the whole appears. Van Gogh knew this from his own work — a painting is thousands of tiny decisions. The lesson is freeing: we do not need a grand gesture today. We need one small thing done well, then another. Bring the small things together, day after day, and the great thing builds itself almost without our noticing.
