Disney's line turns dreaming from idle wishing into the first step of doing. A dream gives direction: it names what you want well enough that you can plan, practise, and begin. Without it, effort scatters in every direction and nothing gets finished. With it, even small daily actions add up because they point somewhere. The warning inside the quote is gentle — a dream alone changes nothing, and the doing is where the promise is kept. So hold the finished picture in mind, take the smallest practical step toward it today, and let the next step appear after that.
