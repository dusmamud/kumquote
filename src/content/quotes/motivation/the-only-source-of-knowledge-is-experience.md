Books and lectures can hand us information, but Einstein insists that real knowledge arrives only through experience. Reading about swimming is not swimming; hearing about failure is not failing. Experience is what turns a fact into understanding, because it adds the weight of having lived it. That is why the same advice means nothing at twenty and everything at forty. We should still study and listen, but we should not mistake information for wisdom. The deepest learning comes from doing, and doing is the only teacher that cannot be skipped.
