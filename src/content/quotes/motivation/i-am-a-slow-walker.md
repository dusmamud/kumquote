Lincoln offers a modest, stubborn kind of hope: speed does not matter, direction does. A slow walker who never turns back will still arrive, while a fast walker who keeps reversing course goes nowhere. Progress is measured not by how quickly we move but by whether we keep moving in the right direction. The quote forgives our pace and demands only persistence. It speaks to anyone who feels behind, watching others sprint ahead and wondering whether slow progress counts. It does. Every step forward, however small, is permanent ground gained. The only true failure is turning back.
