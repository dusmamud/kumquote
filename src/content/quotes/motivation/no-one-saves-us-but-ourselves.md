Others can guide us, encourage us, and show us the way, but no one can walk our path for us. Real change begins when we stop waiting to be rescued and take responsibility for our own steps. This is not a harsh idea; it is a freeing one. It means the power to grow is already in our hands. We do not need permission, perfect conditions, or a saviour. We need the willingness to begin and the courage to keep walking. Each step we take ourselves makes us stronger, wiser, and more truly free.
