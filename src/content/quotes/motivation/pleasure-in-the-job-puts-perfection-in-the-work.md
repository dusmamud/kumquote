When you enjoy what you are doing, the quality of your work rises on its own. Pleasure is not a distraction from good work; it is the fuel that makes careful, patient effort feel natural. A person who loves the craft notices small details, keeps going when the task gets hard, and returns to it willingly the next day. That is why the finest work often comes from people who genuinely like the work itself, not only the reward at the end. Find the part of the task that gives you joy, and excellence will follow.
