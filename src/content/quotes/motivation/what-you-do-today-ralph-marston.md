Tomorrow has a way of absorbing our good intentions. We tell ourselves we will start then, change then, try then. But tomorrow never arrives as a day we can act in; it is always today when the moment comes. Marston's point is that the future is not a distant place but the sum of what we do now. Each action today is a deposit into the days ahead. A walk, one honest conversation, a single page written. None of them looks like much alone. Together they rewrite the weeks ahead, and the future we were waiting for begins to arrive.
