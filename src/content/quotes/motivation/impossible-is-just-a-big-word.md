Calling something impossible is often a way of giving up before starting. The word sounds like a fact about the world, but it is usually a fact about our willingness to try. Ali saw through this: the people who declare things impossible are often the ones who benefit from things staying as they are. Real change begins when someone refuses the label and explores the power they already have. The world they were given is not the world they must keep.
