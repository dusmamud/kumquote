Fear and doubt grow loudest exactly when something matters most, and they make a convincing case for waiting. But importance is itself the reason to act, not the reason to delay. When a cause is worth your effort, the odds against you become part of the work, not a verdict on it. You may fail, and the cost may be real, yet standing aside guarantees the outcome you feared. Choose the thing that matters, accept the risk honestly, and do it anyway, because some efforts are worth making even when success is uncertain.
