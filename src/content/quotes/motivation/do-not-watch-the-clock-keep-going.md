Watching the clock makes hard work feel longer and progress feel slower. Time passes whether you act or not, so the real question is what you will do with it. The clock does not complain, hesitate, or look back; it simply continues, one steady tick at a time. That is a useful model for any long task. Instead of counting the minutes until you finish, give your attention to the next small action in front of you. Keep going through boredom, doubt, and delay. Steady effort, repeated daily, quietly carries you further than urgency ever will.
