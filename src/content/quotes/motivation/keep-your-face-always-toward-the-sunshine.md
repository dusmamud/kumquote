You cannot always remove the shadows from your life, but you can choose which way you face. Turning toward hope, gratitude, and possibility does not deny hardship; it keeps hardship from becoming your whole view. When you focus on what is good and what can grow, the dark parts lose their power to lead you. Shadows are real, but they fall behind when light is in front. This is not about pretending everything is fine. It is about direction. Face the sunshine, and let the shadows stay where they belong, behind you.
