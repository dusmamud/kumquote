Ambition is the spark and work is the fuel, and this line insists that neither works alone. A dream without ambition never leaves the mind, and ambition without effort never leaves the starting line. Many people wait for the perfect moment, the right resources, or a burst of confidence before they begin, yet beginnings are usually small and imperfect. The first step matters more than the perfect plan, because effort turns a vague wish into a real project. Ambition gives direction, work gives motion, and together they carry an idea from thought into reality, one step at a time.
