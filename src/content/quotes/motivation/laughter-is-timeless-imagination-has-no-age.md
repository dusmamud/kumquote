Childhood is not the only season for laughter, imagination, and dreams; these things do not expire with age. Laughter keeps the heart light when life grows heavy. Imagination lets us see beyond the facts in front of us and picture what could be. Dreams give our days direction and our efforts meaning. Together they form a kind of inner youth that no calendar can take away. This quote invites us to protect those qualities deliberately, because a life that keeps laughing, imagining, and dreaming stays open, hopeful, and alive at every age.
