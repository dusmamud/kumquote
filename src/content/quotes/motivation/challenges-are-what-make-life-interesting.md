A life without challenge sounds comfortable, but comfort alone leaves us unchanged. It is the hard assignments, the steep climbs, and the problems we did not choose that force us to grow beyond what we thought possible. Interest comes from the stretch, and meaning comes from the overcoming. When you meet a difficulty, you are being offered raw material for a better version of yourself. Do not wish the challenge away; meet it, learn from it, and let the victory over it give your days their depth and colour.
