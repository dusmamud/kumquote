Talent, luck, and resources all matter, but they rarely decide who finishes first. Many gifted people stop at the first serious setback, while less gifted people keep going and eventually pass them. The difference is usually not ability but endurance. Quitting feels reasonable in the moment, especially when progress is slow and nobody is watching. Yet every meaningful achievement is built from the decision to continue one more day. This quote reminds us that persistence is a skill we can practice, and that refusing to quit is often the whole advantage.
