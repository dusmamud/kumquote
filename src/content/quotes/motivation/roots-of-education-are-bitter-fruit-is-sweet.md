Learning often feels hard at the start. New ideas confuse us, practice is slow, and progress seems invisible. That bitterness is real, but it is only the beginning of the story. The person who stays with the difficult part eventually tastes the fruit: understanding, skill, confidence, and the freedom that knowledge brings. Every subject that once felt impossible becomes a strength once the roots take hold. So do not quit when study feels bitter. The sweetness comes later, and it belongs to those who endure the early struggle.
