Thoreau calls this the most encouraging fact he knows, and the encouragement is in the word conscious. We are not passengers in our own lives; we can choose, deliberately, to rise. Elevation does not happen by accident or by waiting for luck. It happens when a person decides what a better life looks like and works toward it on purpose, day after day. The ability is unquestionable, he says, which means the only real question is whether we will use it. Hope here is not a feeling but a fact we can act on.
