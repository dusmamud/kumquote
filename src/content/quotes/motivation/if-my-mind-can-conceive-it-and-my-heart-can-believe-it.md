Achievement follows a simple order: first the mind imagines it, then the heart believes it, and only then does the body work to make it real. Without a clear picture in the mind, effort has no direction. Without belief in the heart, effort dies at the first setback. But when imagination and belief stand together, hard work has a purpose and setbacks become lessons instead of endings. This is not magic; it is focus. See the goal clearly, believe it is yours to reach, and then let your daily actions carry you toward it.
