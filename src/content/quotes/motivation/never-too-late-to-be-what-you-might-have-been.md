Eliot speaks directly to the quiet regret many of us carry: the dream set aside, the path not taken, the years we think we wasted. Her answer is gentle and firm — the door is not closed. Age, past mistakes, and lost time do not disqualify us from becoming who we hoped to be. What matters is not when we start but that we start. The person we might have been is still available to us, waiting in the choices we make today. It is never too late, because the future is still unwritten.
