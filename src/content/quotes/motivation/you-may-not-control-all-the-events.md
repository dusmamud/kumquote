Hard events arrive uninvited, and no amount of planning can keep every one of them away. Angelou draws a clear line between what happens to us and what it makes of us. We cannot always choose the circumstance, but we can choose whether it shrinks us into bitterness or becomes material we grow through. Refusing to be reduced is not denial; it is a quiet decision to keep our dignity, curiosity and hope intact. Pain may shape us, but it does not get to decide who we become.
