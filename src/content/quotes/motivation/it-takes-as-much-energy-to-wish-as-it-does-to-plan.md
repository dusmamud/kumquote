Wishing and planning draw on the same energy, yet only one of them moves anything forward. Roosevelt's observation is a gentle warning against spending our best hours dreaming about a life we never start building. A wish stays comfortable because it asks nothing of us; a plan asks for decisions, effort and the risk of failing. Both begin in the mind, but the plan continues into action. If the energy is already being spent, spend it on the thing that can actually change your days.
