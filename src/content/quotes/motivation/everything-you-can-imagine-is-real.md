Imagination is the first draft of reality. Every invention, building, and work of art existed first as an image in someone's mind before it existed in the world. Picasso's words remind us that imagining is not idle daydreaming but the beginning of creation itself. What we can picture clearly, we can begin to build, test, and shape into something tangible. Limiting our imagination limits what we can achieve, while daring to imagine more opens doors we did not know existed. The real is not only what we see, but what we dare to see.
