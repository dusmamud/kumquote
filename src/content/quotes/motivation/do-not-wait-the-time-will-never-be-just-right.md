Waiting for perfect conditions is the most common way dreams die quietly. There will always be one more thing to learn, one more reason to delay, one more fear to overcome before starting. Hill's warning is blunt because the trap is so familiar: the perfect moment never arrives, and those who wait for it watch others pass them by. Starting imperfectly is far better than waiting perfectly, because action creates the very conditions we were waiting for. Begin now, with what you have, and let the timing take care of itself.
