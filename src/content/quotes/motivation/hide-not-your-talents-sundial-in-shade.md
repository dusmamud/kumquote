Talent kept hidden helps no one, and this image makes the point with quiet wit. A sundial in the shade cannot tell the time, however well it was made, and a gift unused is much the same. People often hold back their abilities out of modesty, fear, or the belief that the moment is not right. Yet talents grow stronger through use, not through safekeeping. Sharing what you can do serves others and sharpens your own skill at the same time. Step into the light, put your abilities to work, and let them do what they were made to do.
