Most dreams die not from impossibility but from waiting — waiting for the perfect moment, the perfect plan, or permission that never comes. Beginning is the hardest part because it turns a vague wish into a real commitment, and once you start, the path begins to reveal itself. Small first steps create momentum, and momentum attracts help, ideas, and opportunities that waiting never would. You do not need to see the whole road to take the first step; you only need enough courage to move. Begin now, with what you have, and let the work teach you the rest.
