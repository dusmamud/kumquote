Mornings are easy to waste on dread — the alarm, the inbox, the list of problems waiting. Marcus Aurelius offers a different way to begin: notice that being alive at all is a privilege. Breathing, thinking, enjoying, loving — these are not background details of life; they are life itself. Starting the day with that awareness changes everything that follows. Problems do not disappear, but they shrink beside the simple fact of existence. Gratitude is not a luxury for good days. It is a discipline for every morning, especially the ordinary ones.
