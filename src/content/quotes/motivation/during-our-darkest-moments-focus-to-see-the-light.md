Hard times narrow our vision. When things go wrong, the mind fixates on what is broken, and hope can feel like a luxury we cannot afford. Yet darkness is exactly when focus matters most, because the way out is rarely obvious — it has to be looked for. A small opening, a kind person, a forgotten skill: the light is usually smaller and quieter than we expect. Looking for it does not deny the difficulty; it refuses to let difficulty be the whole story. Keep your eyes open in the dark, and you may find the path forward.
