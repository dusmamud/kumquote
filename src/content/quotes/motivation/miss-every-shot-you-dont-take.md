Not trying guarantees failure, while trying at least opens the door to success. People avoid taking the shot because they fear missing, but the math is unforgiving: a shot never taken has no chance at all. The great scorers in any field are not the ones who never miss; they are the ones willing to miss often enough to score. Every attempt carries risk, but inaction carries certainty of nothing. So take the shot. Even a miss teaches you something, and the next attempt will be better for it.
