Frost's line is short because the truth is simple and we still resist it. When trouble arrives, our first instinct is to go around it, postpone it, or pretend it is not there — and each of those detours only stretches the trouble out. Going through hurts, but it ends, while going around keeps you lost. The next time you catch yourself circling a problem, remember that the exit is not behind you or beside you. It is straight ahead, on the far side of the thing itself.
