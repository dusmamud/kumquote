Obstacles look enormous when attention drifts away from the goal, and this line explains why. A problem grows in the mind the moment focus shifts from the destination to the difficulty. The same setback feels small when you remember what you are working toward, and huge when you stare only at what stands in the way. Keeping your eyes on the goal does not remove the obstacle, but it puts it back in proportion. Difficulties are real, yet they shrink when measured against a purpose. Stay fixed on where you are going, and the road becomes easier to walk.
