Wanting to win is common; almost everyone wants the prize, the recognition, and the result. What is far less common is the willingness to do the quiet, unglamorous work beforehand. Preparation is where contests are actually decided, long before anyone is watching. It means practising when motivation is low, studying the details others ignore, and repeating the basics until they hold under pressure. Desire without preparation is only a wish. When the moment arrives, we do not rise to the level of our hopes; we fall to the level of our preparation.
