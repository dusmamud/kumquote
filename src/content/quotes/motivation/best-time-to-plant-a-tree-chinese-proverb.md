We often delay starting something because we feel we have already missed our chance. The years we wish we had begun are gone, and that regret can quietly become another excuse to wait. This proverb cuts through that thinking with gentle honesty. The past cannot be reclaimed, but the present is always available to us. A tree planted today still grows, still gives shade, still becomes something worth having. The same is true of a skill, a habit, a dream, or a repair. Begin now, however small the start, and let time do what only time can do.
