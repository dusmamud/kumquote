Regret and preparation are both ways of spending time on the future — but only one of them helps. Regret looks backward at what we failed to do; preparation looks forward at what we can still do. An athlete knows this well: no amount of wishing after a race can replace the training that should have happened before it. The same is true for exams, careers, relationships, and health. The work we do today is the regret we avoid tomorrow. Look ahead, prepare honestly, and let the future find you ready.
