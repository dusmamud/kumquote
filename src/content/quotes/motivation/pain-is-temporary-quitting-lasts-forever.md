In the hardest stretch of any effort, the discomfort feels permanent. The burning muscles, the doubt, and the urge to stop all insist that this is how it will always feel. But pain passes. It fades with rest, with time, and with the finish line behind us. Quitting, on the other hand, leaves a different kind of mark — the memory of having stopped, which can follow us into the next challenge and the one after that. When we keep going, we trade a temporary ache for a lasting proof of what we can endure.
