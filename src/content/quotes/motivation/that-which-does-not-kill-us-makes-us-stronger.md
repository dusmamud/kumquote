Hardship is often treated as proof that something has gone wrong, yet the hardest seasons are frequently the ones that make us stronger. What does not destroy us forces us to adapt, to learn new strength, and to discover resources we did not know we had. This is not a promise that pain is pleasant or that every wound heals cleanly. It is a reminder that survival itself changes us. If you are still standing after a difficult time, you are not the same person who entered it. You are stronger, wiser, and harder to break.
