We are taught to think of ourselves as small — one person among billions, one drop in a vast sea. Rumi reverses the image entirely. The drop contains the ocean: the same depth, the same salt, the same power, only in a smaller vessel. Our smallness is an illusion of scale. Inside each person is the full range of human capacity — to love, to create, to endure, and to change the world around them. When we stop seeing ourselves as insignificant, our actions change too.
