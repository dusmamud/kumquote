A clear purpose changes how hardship feels. When you know why something matters, the difficulties in front of you stop looking like walls and start looking like the price of something worth having. People endure long hours, setbacks, and criticism when their reason runs deep, while those without a reason quit at the first obstacle. Purpose does not remove pain, but it gives pain a place to sit, so it no longer rules every decision. Find your why first, and the how becomes a problem you can solve rather than a burden you must carry.
