The word impossible carries its own contradiction, and that small trick of language points to a larger truth about how we see our limits. Most barriers begin as assumptions we never test. When a goal looks unreachable, the first step is not a grand leap but a quiet refusal to accept the label. Belief does not remove the work, yet it changes what we are willing to attempt. Once we treat a challenge as possible, we start looking for a way through instead of a reason to stop, and that shift alone often opens the door.
