Yesterday has already happened; replaying it cannot change a single moment of it. Yet so many of us live there — rehearsing old mistakes, nursing old wounds, wishing for old chances. Every hour spent in yesterday is stolen from tomorrow. A better future needs our attention in the present: the plan we make today, the skill we practice today, the apology we offer today. The past deserves reflection, not residence. Learn from it, thank it for its lessons, and then turn around. Tomorrow is built by people facing forward.
