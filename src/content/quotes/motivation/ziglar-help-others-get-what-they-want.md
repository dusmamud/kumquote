Ambition is usually pictured as a solo climb, yet the surest path upward runs through other people. When we help others reach their goals, we build trust, skill, and goodwill that return to us in ways no amount of self-promotion can match. Generosity is not the opposite of success; it is one of its engines. People remember who helped them, and they open doors for those who did. The paradox is generous and practical at once: focus less on what you can extract from the world and more on what you can give, and the world tends to give back.
