The future is not a distant event that arrives on its own; it is built from the choices you make today. Waiting for the perfect moment is a quiet way of giving your future away. Gandhi points to the power hidden in ordinary days. You do not need a grand plan to begin. You need one honest action now, then another tomorrow. Small, deliberate choices repeated over time become the life you will one day look back on and recognise as your own making.
