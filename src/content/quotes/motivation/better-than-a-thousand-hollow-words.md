Words are easy to produce, but only a few of them truly help anyone. A thousand clever sentences can pass through a conversation and leave nothing behind, while a single honest, gentle word at the right moment can calm a worried mind or heal a strained relationship. The value of speech is not measured by how much we say, but by what our words do for the person hearing them. Before speaking, it is worth asking whether our words will bring clarity, comfort or peace. If they will not, silence is often kinder. Speak less, and mean more.
