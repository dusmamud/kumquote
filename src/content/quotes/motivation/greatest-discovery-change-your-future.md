We cannot always change our circumstances, but we can always change our attitude toward them, and that change reshapes everything that follows. A person who sees obstacles as lessons learns faster; a person who sees setbacks as temporary recovers sooner; a person who expects good outcomes works harder to create them. Attitude is the lens through which the whole future is viewed, and changing the lens changes what we notice, what we attempt, and what we achieve. The future is not fixed by the past; it is shaped by the attitude we choose today.
