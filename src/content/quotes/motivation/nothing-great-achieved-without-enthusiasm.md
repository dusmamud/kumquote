Great work needs more than skill and planning; it needs genuine enthusiasm. Without energy and excitement, even a good plan slowly cools into routine, and routine rarely produces anything memorable. Enthusiasm is what carries people through the dull middle of a long project, when the first excitement has faded and the end is still far away. It is contagious too: teams, readers, and customers can feel it, and they respond to it. Talent matters, but talent without fire stays flat. Bring real feeling to the work, and the work itself begins to rise.
