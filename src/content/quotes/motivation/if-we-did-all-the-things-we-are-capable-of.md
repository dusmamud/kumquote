We often live far below what we are capable of, not because ability is missing but because we rarely test its full reach. Edison's line is a challenge to stop settling for the familiar and to attempt the work that feels slightly beyond us. Capability grows through use: each hard problem attempted, each skill practised, each fear faced reveals strength we did not know we had. The surprise is not that we succeed every time, but that trying stretches us further than comfort ever could. Aim higher than feels safe, and you may astonish even yourself.
