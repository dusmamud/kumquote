Fear shrinks when understanding grows. Most of what frightens us is not danger itself but the unknown around it, and the unknown loses its power the moment we study it closely. Curie's life proved this: she worked with invisible forces others feared, turning mystery into knowledge through patient experiment. When you feel afraid, ask what you do not yet understand, then go and learn it. Understanding does not remove every risk, but it replaces panic with clarity. Courage is rarely the absence of fear; it is the decision to understand first, and then act with open eyes.
