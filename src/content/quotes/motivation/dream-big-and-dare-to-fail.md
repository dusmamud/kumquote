Small dreams ask little of us and return little in kind, while big dreams stretch our imagination and demand our best effort. Daring to fail is the price of that stretch: anyone who aims high will sometimes fall short, and that is not a disgrace but the natural cost of ambition. The real failure is shrinking your dreams until they are safe enough to guarantee success, because then you succeed at something too small to matter. Aim beyond what feels comfortable, accept that stumbles will come, and let the size of the dream pull you forward.
