Rand flips the usual question on its head. Most of us ask for permission: who will let me, who will approve, who will open the door. She suggests the better question is who could possibly stop a person who has decided to move. Permission-seeking hands our future to other people's opinions, while determination takes it back. The quote is not a promise that the path will be easy; it is a refusal to wait for approval before starting. Obstacles will appear, but they are different from permission.
