Great achievements rarely arrive in a single dramatic blow. They are built from small, repeated efforts that seem insignificant on their own. Every day you practise, every page you read, every honest attempt adds up quietly, like an axe falling again and again on a mighty tree. The lesson is patience: do not despise the small step because the goal looks huge. Keep striking, keep showing up, and trust the compound effect of steady work. What looks impossible today becomes inevitable when small strokes are repeated long enough.
