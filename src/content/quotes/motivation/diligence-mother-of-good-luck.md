Luck looks like chance from the outside, but Franklin insists it has a mother, and her name is diligence. The people called lucky are usually the ones who prepared longest, worked hardest, and showed up most often. Their good fortune is not an accident but the visible result of invisible effort. Waiting for luck is a poor plan; working diligently is a better one, because effort creates the conditions luck needs to appear. Be diligent in small things every day, and luck will start to look like something you earned.
