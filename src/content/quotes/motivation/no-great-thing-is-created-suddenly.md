Epictetus compresses a lifetime of wisdom into seven words. Great things - a skill, a body of work, a character - are not born in a single inspired moment but assembled slowly, out of days that look ordinary. The suddenness we imagine belongs to the finished product, never to the making of it. This is freeing for anyone standing at the beginning of something hard: slowness is not failure, it is the method. A tree does not hurry, and neither does mastery.
