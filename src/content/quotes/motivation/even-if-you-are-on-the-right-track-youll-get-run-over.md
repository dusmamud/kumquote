Being right is not the same as moving. A good plan, a good job, or a good idea all lose their value the moment we stop walking. The world does not pause for people who have chosen correctly; it keeps coming, and standing still on the right track is still standing still. Rogers turns direction into a joke with a sharp edge: correctness is only the beginning, and motion is the rest. The track matters, but so do the steps. Keep moving, even slowly, because progress belongs to those who stay in motion.
