Belief is not empty optimism; it changes how you act when a goal feels distant. When you genuinely believe a thing is possible, you prepare more seriously, you persist after setbacks, and you notice opportunities that doubt would have hidden. Doubt, by contrast, makes every obstacle feel like proof that you should stop. Roosevelt reminds us that confidence is the first half of the work, not a reward for finishing it. You do not need certainty about every step. You need enough belief to take the first step, learn from it, and take the next one with steadier courage.
