Williams, who has won and lost at the highest level, moves the definition of a champion away from the trophy cabinet. Wins are partly luck, timing, and form on a given day; recovery is character. Anyone can look strong while winning, but falling reveals what a person is made of — whether they blame, hide, or quietly get back to work. Judge yourself less by your record and more by your response. The speed and grace of your recovery is the truest measure of your strength.
