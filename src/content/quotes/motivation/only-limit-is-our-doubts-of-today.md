Doubt is the quietest limit we face. It does not stop us with force; it persuades us to aim lower, wait longer, and settle sooner. Roosevelt understood that tomorrow is shaped less by circumstances than by what we believe is possible today. A clear vision, held with courage, gives direction to effort and patience to endure setbacks. When doubt speaks, ask whether it is protecting you from real danger or merely protecting you from discomfort. Replace it with a practical next step. The future belongs to those who refuse to let today's uncertainty decide what tomorrow can become.
