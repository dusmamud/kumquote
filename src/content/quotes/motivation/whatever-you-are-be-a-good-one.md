Greatness is not about the job title but about how well you do the job you have. Lincoln's line refuses the excuse that ordinary work deserves ordinary effort. Whatever you are, a teacher, a farmer, a clerk, a parent, be a good one. Excellence is available in every role, and it is measured by care, honesty, and pride in the work, not by fame or pay. People remember how well you did what you did far longer than what your position was. Take whatever life has given you to do, and do it as well as it can be done.
