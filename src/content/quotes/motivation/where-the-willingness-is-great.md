Difficulties are not fixed in size; they shrink or grow depending on the will we bring to them. A task that looks impossible to the unwilling looks merely hard to the willing, and willingness turns a wall into a problem to be solved. Machiavelli's insight is that the real obstacle is often inside us, not in the work itself. When we genuinely want the outcome, we find patience, help, and new routes. When we do not, every small bump becomes a reason to stop. Strengthen the willingness first, and the difficulty follows its lead.
