We cannot control most of what happens to us, but we can control the attitude we bring to it, and that changes everything. The same setback looks like a dead end to a hopeless mind and a lesson to a hopeful one. Attitude shapes what we notice, how we interpret events, and whether we keep trying or give up. Changing your attitude does not magically fix the world, but it changes the person facing the world — and that person then acts differently, chooses differently, and creates a different life.
