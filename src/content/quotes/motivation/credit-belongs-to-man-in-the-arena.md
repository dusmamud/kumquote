Credit belongs to those who try, not to those who watch and criticize from a safe distance. Anyone who attempts something difficult will stumble, fail, and fall short, again and again, because effort and error travel together. The person in the arena knows the dust, the sweat, and the sting of defeat, yet keeps striving anyway. Critics risk nothing and therefore learn nothing, while the doer grows stronger with every attempt. This is a defense of daring itself: it is better to fail while trying greatly than to live safely without ever entering the fight.
