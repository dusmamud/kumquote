Every skill that looks effortless today once felt clumsy and confusing. The first page, the first workout, the first awkward conversation — all of them are hard because they are new, not because they are beyond us. Difficulty at the start is not a verdict on our ability; it is simply the price of entry. Once we push through the early resistance, the same task begins to feel natural, even enjoyable. The hard part is rarely the work itself. It is the decision to begin while we are still bad at it.
