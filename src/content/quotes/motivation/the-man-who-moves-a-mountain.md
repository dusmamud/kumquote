Mountains look impossible from the valley floor, yet every mountain is moved one stone at a time. Big goals fail most often not because they are too large, but because we wait for a moment when we can carry the whole mountain at once. That moment never comes. Progress belongs to the person who picks up the first small stone today, then another tomorrow, without ceremony or applause. Small efforts compound quietly until the landscape itself has changed. Start small, stay steady, and let the stones add up.
