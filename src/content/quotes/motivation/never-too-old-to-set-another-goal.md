Age is often used as an excuse to stop growing, but curiosity and ambition have no expiry date. A new goal at any stage of life brings energy, learning, and a reason to look forward. C. S. Lewis reminds us that dreaming is not only for the young. Experience actually makes later dreams wiser, because you know your strengths and your limits. Begin where you are, with what you have, and let a fresh goal prove that growth is still possible.
