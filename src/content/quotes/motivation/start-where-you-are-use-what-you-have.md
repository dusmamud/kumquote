Waiting for perfect conditions is one of the most common ways people delay their dreams. There will always be a missing skill, a lack of money, or a better time in the future. Arthur Ashe cuts through that excuse with a practical invitation: begin with your present place, your present tools, and your present ability. Small beginnings create momentum, and momentum creates learning. You will find better resources as you move, and your confidence will grow with each step. You do not need everything to start. You need the willingness to use what is already in your hands today.
