We often search for peace in the wrong places: a new job, a new city, more money, or someone else's approval. But peace is not something the outside world can hand to us. It grows inside, from a calm mind, honest living, and letting go of what we cannot control. When the inner state is steady, outer troubles lose their power to shake us. When it is restless, no comfort is ever enough. The work, then, is inward. Sit quietly, breathe, and tend your own mind first. Peace found within stays with you wherever you go.
