Every great change asks us to leave something familiar behind. The shore is safety: the job we know, the routine we trust, the life that feels certain. Crossing the ocean means accepting a stretch where land is out of view and there is nothing to hold except the decision itself. That is frightening, but it is the only way to reach a new place. Courage is not the absence of fear; it is choosing to sail anyway, trusting preparation and persistence to carry you across. No new shore is reached by those who refuse to leave the old one.
