We often dismiss small steps because they do not look like progress, yet almost everything lasting is built this way. A little saved each day becomes security, a little practice each day becomes skill, and a little kindness each day becomes a reputation. The danger is not starting small but never starting at all, waiting for a grand gesture that never arrives. Patience turns tiny deposits into something substantial. Trust the slow arithmetic of daily effort, and keep adding a little, day after day, until a lot quietly appears.
