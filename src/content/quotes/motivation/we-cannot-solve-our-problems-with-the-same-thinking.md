We try to fix our problems with the same habits, assumptions, and shortcuts that created them, then wonder why nothing changes. A problem and its solution rarely live at the same level of thinking. To move forward, we must be willing to question what we assumed, learn something new, and see the situation from a fresh angle. That is uncomfortable, because it asks us to admit our old approach failed. But clinging to familiar thinking only repeats familiar results. Change your thinking first, and the problem will begin to change with it.
