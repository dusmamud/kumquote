Everyone gets knocked down. Setbacks, rejections, and failures visit every life without exception, and no amount of planning can keep them away entirely. What separates those who move forward from those who stay stuck is not the absence of falls but the decision to rise after them. Getting up is a choice made in a hard moment, when staying down feels easier and more justified. Each time we stand again, we prove to ourselves that a fall is an event, not an identity.
