Two thousand years before motivational posters, Virgil stated the bargain plainly: luck sides with those who act. Fortune does not reward the careful watcher as reliably as the person willing to step forward and risk something. Boldness does not guarantee success, but it creates the openings that luck needs to find us. The timid plan, endlessly refined in safety, never meets the chance encounter or the opportunity that only appears to those already in motion. This is not a call to recklessness but to courage, the willingness to commit before every doubt is resolved.
