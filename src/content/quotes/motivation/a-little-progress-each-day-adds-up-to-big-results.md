Big change rarely arrives in one dramatic moment. More often it is the quiet sum of small steps taken day after day, each one too modest to notice on its own. A page read, a minute exercised, a single kind choice — none of these feels like transformation, yet together they compound into something unmistakable. The trouble is that we judge our efforts by the day, when we should judge them by the season. Progress asks for patience rather than intensity. Keep moving a little, keep showing up, and trust that the accumulation is working even on the days when nothing seems to change.
