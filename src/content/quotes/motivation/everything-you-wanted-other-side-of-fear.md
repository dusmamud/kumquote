Fear is often a signpost, not a stop sign. The job you want, the conversation you need, the skill you admire, and the life you imagine usually sit just beyond a moment of discomfort. Fear tries to protect you by keeping you where you are, but comfort rarely produces growth. When you act despite fear, the imagined danger usually shrinks, and your confidence grows from the evidence of surviving it. You do not need to feel fearless first. Take one small brave step, then another, and discover how much of what you wanted was waiting on the other side.
