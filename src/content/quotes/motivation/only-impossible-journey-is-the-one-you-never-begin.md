Every journey that was ever finished began with a single uncertain step. The only journey that is truly impossible is the one that never starts, because a dream left in the mind can never become real. Fear, doubt, and waiting for the perfect moment keep many good plans locked away. But the first step does not need to be big or perfect; it only needs to happen. Once you move, the path teaches you what to do next. Begin today, however small the start, and the impossible quietly becomes possible.
