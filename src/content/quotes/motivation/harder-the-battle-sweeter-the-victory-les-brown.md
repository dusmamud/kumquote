Brown reframes difficulty as the ingredient that gives success its flavour. An easy win is quickly forgotten because it cost nothing and therefore means little; a hard-won victory stays with you because you know exactly what it took. When a task feels harder than expected, that is not a sign you chose wrong; it may be a sign the result will matter more. Keep going through the hard part. The sweetness you are working for is measured by the battle, not despite it.
