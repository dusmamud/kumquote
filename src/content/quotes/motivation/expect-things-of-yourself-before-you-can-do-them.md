Expectation is the quiet starting line of every achievement. Before a skill is built or a record is broken, someone must first believe the outcome is possible for them. Low expectations shrink effort, because we rarely train hard for a result we do not think we deserve. Jordan's point is that standards are set inside before they show outside. Expect more of yourself and your preparation changes: you practise longer, recover faster, and refuse shortcuts. The demand you place on yourself becomes the floor you stand on, not the ceiling you reach for.
