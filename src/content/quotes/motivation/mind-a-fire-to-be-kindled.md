Real learning is not the passive collection of facts, like water poured into a jar. It is the lighting of something already inside you: curiosity, wonder, and the urge to understand. A filled vessel can sit still and grow stale, but a fire spreads, warms, and transforms everything it touches. The best teachers do not hand over answers; they spark questions that keep burning long after the lesson ends. Guard that fire. Read widely, ask bold questions, and let your own interest lead you, because a mind set alight will teach itself for a lifetime.
