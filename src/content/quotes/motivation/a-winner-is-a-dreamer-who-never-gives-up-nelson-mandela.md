Winning is usually pictured as talent or luck, something a few people are born with. Mandela offers a different picture: a winner is simply a dreamer who refuses to quit. The dream gives direction, but it is the refusal to stop that does the real work. Every winner has a long list of failures behind them, days when quitting would have been reasonable and sensible. What separates them is not that they never fell, but that they kept standing up. Hold on to the dream, keep going one more day, and the distance between dreaming and winning begins to close.
