Your beliefs about yourself act like a prophecy. If you are convinced you can succeed, you try harder, persist longer, and notice opportunities others miss. If you are convinced you cannot, you hesitate, give up early, and confirm your own doubt. The task in front of you does not change, but your mind decides how much of yourself you bring to it. That is why the first victory is always internal. Choose the belief that serves you, because whichever one you hold will shape the outcome more than talent or luck ever will.
