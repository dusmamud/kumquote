Fear of failure never fully disappears, so waiting for it to vanish means waiting forever. Success belongs to those whose desire outweighs that fear — not people without fear, but people who want the goal more than they dread the fall. When desire is stronger, fear becomes background noise instead of a wall. The trick is to feed the desire: picture the result clearly, remember why it matters, and take one small step. Desire grows with action. Let it grow larger than the fear, and the fear will stop making your decisions.
