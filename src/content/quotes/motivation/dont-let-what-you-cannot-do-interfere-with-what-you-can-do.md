It is easy to get stuck thinking about the skills we lack, the resources we do not have, or the doors that seem closed to us. This quote reminds us to turn our attention to what is already within reach. We all have something we can do today, however small it may seem. Progress comes from using the ability we already possess, not from mourning the ability we wish we had. When we act on what we can do, our limits often turn out smaller than we feared, and each small step builds confidence for the next one.
