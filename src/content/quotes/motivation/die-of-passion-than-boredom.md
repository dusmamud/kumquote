Boredom feels safe, but it is a slow kind of dying. A life with no passion — no project that excites us, no cause that moves us, nothing we care about deeply — may be comfortable, but it is hollow. Passion, by contrast, is risky: it asks for energy, it invites failure, it can burn us. Van Gogh chose the burn. He would rather feel everything, even the hard parts, than feel nothing at all. The choice is ours too. A passionate life is not always easy, but it is always alive. Boredom promises safety and delivers emptiness.
