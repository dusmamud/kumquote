Hope is not idle wishing; it is the mind picturing a better future while fully awake to the present. Like a dream, it shows us what could be, but unlike a dream, it belongs to daylight, where we can act on it. Hope gives direction to our effort, because we work hardest for a future we can already see in our mind. Even in difficult times, holding on to a clear picture of what is possible keeps us moving forward. Dream while you are awake, and then let that vision pull your actions toward it, one step at a time.
