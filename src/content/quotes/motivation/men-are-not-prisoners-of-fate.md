Circumstances can limit us, but Roosevelt argues that the tighter prison is the one we build in our own minds. Fear, doubt, and old beliefs lock more doors than fate ever does. People stay in jobs, relationships, and routines they have outgrown, not because escape is impossible, but because they believe it is. The mind that accepts its chains never tests them. Freedom begins with questioning the bars: are they real, or only familiar? Change the thinking, and the cell opens. The key was in your pocket all along.
