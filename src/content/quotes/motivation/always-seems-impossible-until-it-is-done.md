Before a great achievement, the task often looks impossible because no one has finished it yet. The distance, the obstacles, and the doubts all seem larger from the starting line. But impossibility is often a feeling, not a fact. Mandela knew this from a life spent pursuing freedom against overwhelming odds. When you persist, what once seemed beyond reach becomes a memory of hard work completed. Do not judge a goal by how it looks at the start; judge it by what steady effort can make real.
