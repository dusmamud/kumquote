Failure gets all the attention, but it is not the real danger. A failed attempt at least teaches us something — what does not work, what needs changing, what we are capable of surviving. The missed chance teaches us nothing at all, except regret. Every opportunity we never take closes quietly, without drama, and we rarely notice how many have passed. Years later, people seldom regret the things they tried and failed at; they regret the chances they were too afraid to take. Fear of failure is understandable, but it is the wrong fear.
