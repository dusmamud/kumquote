Self-doubt speaks in a convincing voice. It tells us we are not talented enough, not ready enough, not the kind of person who can do this thing. The usual response is to wait until the voice goes away, but it rarely does. This quote offers a different strategy: act anyway, and let the action answer the doubt. Painting, writing, building, or trying despite the inner critic is itself the proof the critic was wrong. Each time we create in spite of fear, the voice grows quieter. Courage is not the absence of the voice; it is painting while it talks.
