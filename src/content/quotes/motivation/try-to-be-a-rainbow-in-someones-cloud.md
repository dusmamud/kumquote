Clouds are the hard days — the grief, the disappointment, the stretches when nothing feels bright. A rainbow does not remove the cloud; it appears inside it, changing how the whole sky looks. Being that rainbow for someone else costs little: a kind word, a patient ear, a small act of encouragement at the right moment. We often underestimate these gestures because they feel too small to matter, but to the person under the cloud, they can matter enormously. We cannot always fix someone's weather, but we can always bring colour to it.
