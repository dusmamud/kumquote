Timidity treats every choice as final, but Emerson reminds us that life is an experiment, and experiments are allowed to fail. The scientist does not fear a wrong result; the result itself is the knowledge. So it is with a career change, a new skill, or an honest conversation. We learn by trying, and the trying matters more than getting it perfect the first time. Fear keeps us in the laboratory of our own thoughts, while action puts us in the world. Be bold enough to test, and let the outcome teach you.
