Dreaming is only the beginning; the real work is walking toward the dream with confidence. Many people wait for permission, for certainty, or for the perfect time, and in waiting they never live the life they imagined. Thoreau urges the opposite: move in the direction of your dream now, trusting that the path will appear as you walk it. Confidence does not come before the journey, it grows during it. Step by step, the imagined life stops being a fantasy and becomes the life you are actually living.
