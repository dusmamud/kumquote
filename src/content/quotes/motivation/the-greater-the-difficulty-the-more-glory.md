The victories we remember are the ones that demanded everything we had. Difficulty is not a sign that a goal is wrong for us; it is often the thing that makes the achievement meaningful. When a task resists us, it forces us to grow sharper, stronger and more patient than we were before. The struggle itself becomes part of the reward, because we know what it cost. So when the path gets steep, do not turn back in search of something easier. Climb anyway, and let the size of the challenge match the pride you will feel at the top.
