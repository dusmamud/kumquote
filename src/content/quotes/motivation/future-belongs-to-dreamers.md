Dreams are not idle wishes; they are the first draft of the future. When you believe in a dream deeply enough, it begins to shape your choices, your habits, and the risks you are willing to take. Belief turns a distant hope into a plan, and a plan into daily work. The future does not arrive by accident. It is built by people who can picture something better and refuse to let go of that picture. Hold on to the beauty of your dream, because that belief carries you through the long stretch between imagining and achieving.
