Self-trust sounds simple, but it is one of the hardest things to practice. The world constantly tells us what to think, want, and become, and listening to our own inner voice takes courage. Emerson claims that every heart vibrates to the same iron string, meaning that genuine conviction rings true to others too. When you speak from real belief rather than borrowed opinion, people feel it. Trusting yourself does not mean ignoring advice; it means testing everything against your own judgment. The string is already inside you. Listen for it, and let it guide you.
