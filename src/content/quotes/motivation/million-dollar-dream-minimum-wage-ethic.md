Big dreams are free, but they are never cheap. Everyone can picture a better life; far fewer are willing to pay for it with early mornings, honest effort, and patience when results are slow. The gap between what we want and what we are willing to do is where most ambitions die. This is not a judgment — it is arithmetic. Results follow effort the way harvest follows planting. If the dream matters, the daily work must match it. Raise the effort first, and the dream stops being a fantasy and starts becoming a plan.
