Creativity is not a tank that runs dry with use; it is a muscle that grows stronger every time it is exercised. Many people save their ideas, afraid of wasting them, and so the ideas never arrive at all. But each act of making opens new doors: one poem suggests another, one sketch reveals a fresh way of seeing. Angelou knew that creative energy multiplies in the using. The more you write, paint, build, or imagine, the more ideas come to you. So do not hoard your creativity. Spend it freely, and watch it return with interest.
