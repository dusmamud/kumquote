We often treat motivation as a one-time spark that should carry us forever, then feel betrayed when it fades by Tuesday. The comparison to bathing makes the point with humor: some things only work when they are repeated. A short talk, a good book, or a morning reminder can refill the tank, but the tank empties again, and that is normal rather than failure. The wise response is a small daily practice that keeps the flame alive. Motivation is less like a switch we flip once and more like hygiene we tend every day, quietly and without drama.
