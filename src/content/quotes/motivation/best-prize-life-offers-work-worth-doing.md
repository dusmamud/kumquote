The greatest reward is not wealth or applause but the chance to spend your energy on work that matters. Hard work feels different when the task is worthy: effort becomes satisfying instead of draining, and difficulty becomes part of the reward rather than an obstacle to it. Many people chase easy comfort and end up bored and restless, while those who take on meaningful challenges find energy they did not know they had. The privilege lies in the combination — hard work plus worthy work.
