Waiting for permission is the slowest way to live. This quote challenges us to stop asking whether others will approve of our ambitions and instead to trust our own judgment and move forward. Permission-seeking hands our dreams to people who may never understand them, while self-belief keeps the decision where it belongs: with us. Obstacles will still appear, and critics will still speak, but neither can stop someone who has already decided to act. The real question is never who allows us, but whether we allow ourselves.
