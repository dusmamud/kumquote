Wishing alone changes nothing, and this line is a plain reminder of that truth. Dreams, plans, and good intentions stay frozen until effort warms them into motion. Talent without work is only potential, and luck without work is only a rumour. The world rewards those who show up, practise, fail, adjust, and try again. Work does not have to be grand or dramatic; small, steady action done daily compounds into real change. When progress feels slow, the answer is rarely a better plan. It is usually more honest effort, applied consistently, until the work itself begins to work for you.
