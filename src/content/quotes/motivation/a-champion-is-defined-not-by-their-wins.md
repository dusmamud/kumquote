Williams, whose career was built on comebacks as much as on titles, moves the definition of a champion away from the trophy shelf and toward recovery. Wins are visible and celebrated, but they tell only half the story. The real measure is what happens after a fall: the injury, the loss, the season that goes wrong. Anyone can look strong while winning. The champion is the person who gets up, adjusts, and returns, sometimes slowly, sometimes painfully, but always moving forward. We do not need a flawless record to be champions of our own lives.
