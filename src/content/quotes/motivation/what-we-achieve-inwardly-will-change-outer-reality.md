Change rarely starts with circumstances. It starts inside, with the way you think, what you believe you deserve, and the habits you quietly keep when nobody is watching. Inner growth is slow and invisible, which is why so many people give up on it, but it is the only change that lasts. A calmer mind makes better choices, a braver heart takes better risks, and a clearer sense of purpose shapes better days. Work on the inside first, and the outside world will slowly rearrange itself to match the person you have become.
