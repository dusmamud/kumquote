Normal is a safe hiding place. It asks us to smooth our edges, quiet our unusual ideas, and match the crowd so nobody looks twice. But the very things that make us different are usually the source of our greatest strengths — the odd interest, the unusual way of thinking, the talent that does not fit the template. Trying to be normal trades that away for acceptance that costs too much. Amazing is not a destination for the few; it is what happens when a person stops apologising for who they are and lets their real qualities show.
