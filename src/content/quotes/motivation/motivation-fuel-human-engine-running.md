Ziglar compares motivation to fuel because both are consumed by use and must be topped up. A car does not run forever on one tank, and a person does not stay driven on a single burst of enthusiasm. The quote makes daily renewal normal rather than a sign of weakness: reading, talking with encouraging people, remembering your reasons, and taking small wins all refill the tank. Waiting to feel motivated before acting gets the order backwards, since action itself often creates the energy. Treat motivation as maintenance, not magic, and the engine keeps running through ordinary days.
