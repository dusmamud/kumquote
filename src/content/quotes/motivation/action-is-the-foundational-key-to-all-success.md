Ideas alone change nothing; it is action that turns thought into reality. Many people wait for the perfect plan, the perfect moment, or complete certainty before they begin, and in waiting they lose the very progress they hoped for. Picasso knew that creation starts with a single stroke, not with endless preparation. Every achievement, from a painting to a business, begins the same way: someone decides to act despite uncertainty. Small steps build momentum, and momentum builds results. The foundation of success is not talent or luck, but the willingness to begin.
