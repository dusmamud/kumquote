Big goals feel overwhelming when we stare at the whole distance at once. This quote reframes endurance as a series of small, manageable efforts rather than one endless struggle. Each short race has its own finish line, its own small victory, and its own chance to rest and begin again. By focusing only on the next stretch, we keep moving without being crushed by the size of the journey. Persistence, then, is not a single heroic act but a habit of starting over, one step at a time, until the distance is behind us.
