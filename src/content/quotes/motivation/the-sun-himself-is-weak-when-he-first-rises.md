Beginnings are rarely impressive. The first light of morning is thin and weak, and the first steps of any new effort feel just as small. Yet strength is not something we start with; it is something we gather as the day goes on, through steady work and stubborn persistence. Do not judge your effort by its pale first hour. Keep moving through the morning of your task, and you will find your courage growing with every hour. Weak starts are not a verdict. They are simply how every strong finish begins.
