Failure gets blamed for many ruined dreams, but this quote argues that the real problem is aiming too low. A person who fails while reaching for something great has still stretched, learned, and grown. A person who aims low and succeeds has only confirmed their own smallness. Low aims protect us from disappointment, but they also guarantee a small life. It is better to miss a high target than to hit a trivial one. The quote challenges us to set goals that frighten us a little, because only worthy aims can produce a worthy life.
