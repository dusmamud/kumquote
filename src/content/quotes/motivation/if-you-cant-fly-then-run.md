Progress rarely looks heroic. Most days it looks like moving a little when a leap is impossible, and refusing to stop altogether when even walking feels hard. King's image gives permission to shrink the step without shrinking the direction: run when you can, walk when you must, crawl when there is nothing else left. What matters is the forward motion, however small, because stillness is what truly defeats a dream. Keep moving, and the distance you cover will surprise you in the end.
