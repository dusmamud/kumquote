A setback is not the same as a defeat. Every attempt that falls short still teaches something: what does not work, what needs adjusting, what to try next. The only real ending comes when we stop trying altogether. As long as we are still learning and still moving, the story is unfinished. This reframes failure completely. It stops being a label we wear and becomes a moment we pass through. The question is never whether we stumbled. It is whether we are willing to stand up, dust ourselves off, and take the next step.
