Surviving is a worthy achievement, but Angelou asks for more: a life lived with warmth, courage and delight. To thrive is to bring passion to our work, compassion to our relationships, humour to hard days and style to the way we carry ourselves. It is not about luxury or ease; it is about refusing a small, grey version of life when a fuller one is possible. Every day offers a choice between merely getting through and genuinely living. Choose the fuller one, and let your days show it.
