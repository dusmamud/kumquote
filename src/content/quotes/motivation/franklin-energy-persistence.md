Talent and luck get more attention than they deserve, while steady energy and refusal to quit quietly decide most outcomes. A difficult task rarely falls to one brilliant stroke; it yields to repeated effort, applied day after day, long after the excitement fades. Persistence is not stubbornness for its own sake but the patience to keep returning to the work with fresh energy. When we commit to showing up, small gains accumulate into something that once seemed out of reach. The lesson is simple and demanding at once: bring your energy, keep going, and let time do the rest.
