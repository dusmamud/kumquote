Van Gogh describes a life lived at full intensity in three short sentences. Seeking means never settling for easy answers; striving means working even when the work goes unrecognized; and being in it with all your heart means holding nothing back. He painted without wealth or fame, driven only by the need to create. That kind of wholehearted commitment is rare because it costs so much, yet it is also what makes a life feel meaningful. Whatever you seek, seek it honestly. Whatever you strive for, give it your whole heart.
