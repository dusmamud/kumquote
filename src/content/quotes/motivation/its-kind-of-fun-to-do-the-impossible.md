Most people treat the impossible as a wall, but this quote treats it as an invitation. The things once called impossible, from flight to instant messages across the world, were achieved by people who found the challenge exciting rather than discouraging. Seeing difficulty as play changes everything: it loosens fear, invites creativity, and keeps us going when the work gets hard. Fun is not the opposite of serious effort; it is often what sustains it. When we enjoy the stretch, we attempt more, learn faster, and sometimes do what others said could not be done.
