Speed is the wrong measure for most meaningful goals. A slow learner who keeps studying, a writer who adds one page a day, and a runner who never misses a short walk all move further than talented people who quit. Progress compounds quietly, and consistency turns small efforts into a new identity over time. This quote frees you from comparing your pace with others. Your only real duty is to keep moving. Rest when you must, adjust your plan when it fails, but do not abandon the direction. Slow and steady is still moving forward.
