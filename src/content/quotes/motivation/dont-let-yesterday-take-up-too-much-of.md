Yesterday is gone, but it has a way of lingering, replaying mistakes, regrets and missed chances until they crowd out the present. Rogers warns against giving the past that much rent-free space. Today is the only day in which anything can actually be done, and every hour spent reliving yesterday is an hour stolen from it. Learning from the past is wise; living inside it is not. The past should be a teacher, consulted briefly and then set aside. Guard today fiercely, because it is the raw material of tomorrow, and it deserves your full attention.
