Every achievement quietly reveals the next challenge waiting beyond it. Mandela, who climbed many hills in his life, warns against expecting a single summit where struggle ends. Each hard-won height gives a clearer view, and with it, new hills to climb. This is not a reason for despair but for steady patience: progress is a series of climbs, not one final arrival. Rest, enjoy the view, and then begin again. The journey itself is the life, and each hill crossed makes the next one possible.
