Your thoughts are the quiet architects of your life. What you dwell on shapes what you notice, what you attempt, and how you respond when things go wrong. A mind filled with fear finds reasons to stop; a mind filled with purpose finds ways to continue. This is not wishful thinking, it is the simple fact that attention directs action, and action builds character. Guard your thoughts the way you would guard the foundation of a house, because everything you become is first built there, in the mind.
