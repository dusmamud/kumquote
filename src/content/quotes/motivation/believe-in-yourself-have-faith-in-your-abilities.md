Self-belief is not arrogance; it is the quiet trust that you can learn, adapt, and handle what comes. Without it, even great ability stays unused, because doubt talks you out of trying. With it, ordinary ability goes further, because you keep going when others stop. Faith in yourself does not mean ignoring your weaknesses; it means believing they can be improved with honest effort. The first person who must believe in your dream is you, since no one else can carry that conviction for you. Trust your abilities, act on that trust, and let the results confirm it.
