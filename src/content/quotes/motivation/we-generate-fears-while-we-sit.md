Fear grows in stillness. While we sit and think, the mind rehearses every danger, exaggerates every risk, and builds obstacles that do not exist. Action breaks that spell because it replaces imagination with evidence: the first step is usually far less frightening than the hours spent dreading it. Doing something — anything — shrinks the fear, and each small success makes the next step easier. You cannot think your way out of fear; you must move through it. Start before you feel ready, and let action teach you that the danger was mostly in your head.
