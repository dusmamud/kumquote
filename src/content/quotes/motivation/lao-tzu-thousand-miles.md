The distance between where we are and where we want to be can feel so vast that we never start at all. This ancient line shrinks the whole journey down to one manageable act: a single step. It does not ask for the full route, the perfect plan, or certainty about the ending. It asks only for movement, however small. Each step after that becomes easier because the ground behind us proves we are capable of progress. Big changes are built from ordinary steps taken in order, and the only step we truly control is the next one.
