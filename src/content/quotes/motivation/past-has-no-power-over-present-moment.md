The past feels powerful because the mind replays it so often, yet it exists now only as memory. This line reminds us that yesterday cannot reach into today unless we hand it the keys. Mistakes, regrets, and old wounds shape us, but they do not have to rule us. Each present moment is a fresh chance to choose differently, to act with a clear head instead of an old story. Freedom begins when we stop arguing with what already happened. The past is finished; the present is open, and what we do with it is still entirely ours.
