This famous line is a lesson in contrast. The butterfly is light, graceful, and hard to catch; the bee is small, quiet, and impossible to ignore once it strikes. Together they describe a way of moving through challenges: stay light on your feet, avoid wasted motion, and act decisively when the moment comes. Strength alone is not enough, and neither is speed alone. The two must work together — patience and timing on one side, power and precision on the other.
