Edison knew failure from the inside — thousands of attempts stood behind every invention that worked. His point is that quitting is not a neutral act; it is the one decision that guarantees the outcome you fear. One more try costs little and keeps every possibility alive, while giving up closes the door for good. Most breakthroughs arrive just past the point where stopping feels most reasonable. When you want to quit, ask whether you have truly run out of ideas or merely run out of patience. Usually it is patience, and patience can be renewed.
