Ziglar answers a common complaint with a joke that is also a strategy. Nobody expects one bath to keep them clean forever, yet we expect one inspiring speech or one good morning to carry us for months. Motivation is not a fuel tank you fill once; it is hygiene, something you renew in small doses every day. Stop waiting for a permanent surge of enthusiasm that will never arrive. Instead, build a small daily ritual that tops motivation up, and trust the ritual more than the mood.
