Helping others is one of the surest ways to grow ourselves. When we lift someone else, we step outside our own worries and discover a sense of purpose that no personal achievement alone can provide. Encouragement, guidance, and generosity return to us as confidence, gratitude, and deeper connection with the people around us. The act of giving reminds us of our own strength and worth, because we cannot offer what we do not possess. In lifting others, we rise too, carried upward by the very kindness we chose to extend.
