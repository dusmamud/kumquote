We often say we have no time, but the hours are the same for everyone. Ziglar's point is that the real shortage is not time but clarity about what matters. Without direction, even a full day scatters into small distractions and half-finished tasks. With a clear aim, the same twenty-four hours can carry real progress. The fix is simple to state and hard to practise: decide what deserves your attention before the day decides for you. Direction turns ordinary time into a life moving somewhere worth going.
