Larson asks us to look inward before we look at the obstacle. Whatever stands in our way — doubt, rejection, or a task that feels too large — it is outside us, while the resources to meet it are already inside: our resilience, our creativity, and our will to keep going. Believing in yourself is not arrogance; it is a clear-eyed recognition of what you carry. Obstacles feel enormous when we forget our own size. Remember what lives within you, and the obstacle shrinks to its true proportions: something you can face, and pass.
