Nobody gets through life without mistakes, and pretending otherwise only makes things worse. What matters is what happens after the mistake. Admitting it takes courage because it means facing embarrassment, disappointment, and sometimes real consequences. But admission is also what makes repair possible. A mistake we own can teach us; a mistake we hide will repeat itself. Forgiveness, from others and from ourselves, follows honesty far more easily than it follows excuses. This quote reminds us that courage is not never failing, but facing our failures openly and learning from them.
