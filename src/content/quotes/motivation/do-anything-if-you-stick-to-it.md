Sticking with something sounds simple, yet it is where most dreams quietly end. The first burst of excitement fades, progress slows, and the work becomes ordinary. That is the moment that separates wishing from achieving. Helen Keller knew this better than most: she built a remarkable life not in one dramatic leap but through years of patient, daily effort. Talent helps, and luck helps, but endurance is the force that carries both across the finish line. If we refuse to quit, time becomes an ally instead of an enemy.
