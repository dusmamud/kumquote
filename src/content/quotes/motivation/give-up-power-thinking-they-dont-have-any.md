Power is rarely taken from us all at once. More often, we hand it over quietly by deciding we have none. We tell ourselves our voice does not matter, our effort will not change anything, and someone else is better placed to act. Each of those thoughts feels modest, even realistic, but together they add up to surrender. The truth is that influence begins small: a choice, a conversation, a refusal to stay silent. Nobody needs permission to matter. The moment we act as if our choices count, they begin to count.
