Dreams rarely die because they are impossible; they die because fear convinces us not to try. Failure itself is survivable: it stings, teaches, and passes. But the fear of failure stops us before we begin, and that is the only true defeat. Coelho's shepherd in The Alchemist learns that the treasure was never as distant as his doubts made it seem. Every dream worth having carries the risk of falling short, and that risk is the price of the pursuit. When fear whispers that you might fail, answer it by starting anyway.
