It is easy to feel small in a large world and conclude that your efforts do not matter. But influence rarely announces itself. A teacher encourages a student, a neighbour offers help, a worker does a job carefully, and those small acts ripple outward in ways no one can fully trace. William James invites you to live as though your actions count, because they do. You may never see the full result, yet someone else may carry your encouragement for years. Meaning grows when you act with care, even without applause, proof, or any guarantee of recognition.
