Aiming at perfection can sound like a recipe for frustration, since perfection itself can never be reached. Lombardi's insight is that the chase is the point. Aiming high pulls effort, discipline and standards upward, and even falling short lands far beyond where a modest aim would have stopped. Excellence is what remains when perfection stays out of reach. The quote gives permission to pursue an impossible standard without despair, because the pursuit itself produces the result. Set the bar beyond reach, run at it honestly, and excellence is the prize you catch.
