Fear guards the door to almost everything worth having. The job you want, the conversation you need to have, the life you imagine for yourself, all of them sit just beyond the discomfort of trying. Fear makes the risk look larger than it is and the reward smaller than it will be. But fear is not a wall; it is a threshold. The moment you step through it, you find that the worst you imagined rarely happens, and the growth you gain is real. What you want has been waiting on the other side all along.
