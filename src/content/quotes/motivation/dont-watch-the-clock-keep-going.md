Watching the clock makes every minute feel longer and every task feel heavier. Progress rarely arrives on a schedule we can see, and counting the hours only feeds impatience. The clock, meanwhile, never argues or complains; it simply moves forward, second by second, without stopping. That is the model to follow. Keep working steadily, without measuring each moment against your expectations. Momentum builds quietly. One day you look up and discover how far the steady ticking of effort has carried you, while those who only watched the time are still waiting.
