Greatness is rarely a giant leap. It is usually a small habit repeated when nobody is watching: one more rep, one more draft, one more careful check, one more kind word. Ordinary people stop when the work is good enough, while extraordinary people add the little extra that turns good into memorable. That extra costs almost nothing, yet it compounds over weeks and years into a gap no one can close by luck alone. You do not need a new talent or a new life. You only need to give a little more than expected, every single day.
