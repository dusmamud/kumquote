Fear is loud, and it disguises itself as common sense, warning us to stay safe, stay small, and stay where we are. Bennett contrasts that voice with the quieter pull of our dreams, which point toward growth and the life we actually want. Being pushed means reacting to anxiety; being led means choosing direction. The quote does not deny that fear exists, but it refuses to let fear drive. When dreams set the course, fear becomes a passenger rather than the driver, and each step forward proves that the dangers were smaller than the life waiting beyond them.
