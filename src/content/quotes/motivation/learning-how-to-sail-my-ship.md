Storms are not a reason to stay in harbour; they are the very conditions in which a sailor learns the craft. Alcott's image reminds us that confidence does not come before the challenge but through it. Every rough stretch of life teaches a new skill: how to steady yourself, adjust your course, and keep moving when the wind turns against you. You do not need a calm sea to begin. You need a willingness to learn while the waves are high. Skill grows in difficulty, and the person who keeps sailing through storms eventually stops fearing them altogether.
