Fear is often a compass pointing straight at the thing that matters most. The conversation we keep avoiding, the project we keep postponing, the risk we keep circling - these are rarely unimportant. They frighten us precisely because they carry real stakes and real possibility. Ferriss turns fear from a stop sign into a signal: if something scares you, look closer, because growth is probably hiding on the other side. Doing the feared thing does not guarantee success, but avoiding it guarantees stagnation. Courage is choosing to act while the fear is still there.
