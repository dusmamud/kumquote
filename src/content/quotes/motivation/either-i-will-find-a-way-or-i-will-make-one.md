Big goals often look impossible from the starting line, and it is tempting to wait for a clear road to appear. But waiting rarely creates opportunity. This quote is about refusing to accept that the absence of a path means the absence of a way forward. When no route exists, determination itself becomes the route. People who build, lead, and change things rarely find ready-made paths; they cut them. The message is simple and demanding: stop asking for permission from circumstances, and start creating the conditions you need, one deliberate step at a time.
