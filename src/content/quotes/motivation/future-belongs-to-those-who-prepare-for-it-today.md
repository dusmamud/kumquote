The future is not something that simply happens to us; it is something we build with today's choices. Those who study, practise, save, and plan while others wait will find themselves ready when opportunity arrives. Preparation is the quiet work done before anyone is watching, and it decides who is ready when the moment comes. Waiting for the future to arrive unprepared is like showing up to a race without training. The time to prepare is always now, because tomorrow's success is decided by what we do today.
