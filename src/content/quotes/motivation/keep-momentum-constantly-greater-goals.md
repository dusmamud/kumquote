Momentum fades when there is nothing left to reach for, and this line offers a simple remedy: always keep a bigger goal on the horizon. Finishing one achievement feels wonderful, but without a next target, energy drains away and old habits creep back. Greater goals do not have to be grand or dramatic; they only need to stretch you a little further than before. Each new aim gives your days a direction and your efforts a reason. Progress loves a moving target, so when one chapter closes, open another, and let the pursuit keep you moving forward.
