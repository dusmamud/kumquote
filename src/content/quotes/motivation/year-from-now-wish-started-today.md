A year will pass whether we act or not, and that is exactly what makes this line sting. We often wait for the perfect moment, the right mood, or more confidence, while the calendar keeps moving without us. Lamb asks us to picture ourselves twelve months ahead, looking back and wishing we had simply begun. Starting today does not require a grand plan — only a first small step. The future we want is built by the person who begins now, not by the person who keeps waiting for someday.
