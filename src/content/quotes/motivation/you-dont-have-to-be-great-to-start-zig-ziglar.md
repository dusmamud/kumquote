Waiting to feel ready is the most common way good plans die quietly. Ziglar reverses the order we assume: greatness is not the ticket that lets you begin, it is the result of beginning badly and improving. The first attempt will be clumsy, the second a little less so, and skill grows only from those repetitions. Every expert was once a beginner who refused to wait. So lower the bar for today — start small, start rough, start now. The greatness you are waiting for is built on the far side of the first step, not before it.
