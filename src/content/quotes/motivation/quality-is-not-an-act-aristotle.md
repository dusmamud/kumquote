Aristotle refuses to let quality be a performance we put on for special occasions. A single brilliant day proves nothing; what you do on ordinary days, when nobody is watching, is who you actually are. Habits are the small choices repeated until they stop feeling like choices at all, and they quietly decide the standard of your work. That is good news: you need no heroic effort, only a better routine repeated faithfully. Choose one small standard to keep every day — finishing properly, checking your work, showing up on time — and let repetition do the heavy lifting.
