A rough stone becomes a gem only through grinding, pressure, and friction, and human character is shaped in much the same way. Comfort alone rarely teaches anything lasting; it is difficulty that reveals our weaknesses and forces us to grow beyond them. Trials strip away illusions, test our patience, and build the quiet strength that smooth days never can. When hardship arrives, it helps to see it as the polishing wheel rather than a punishment. The friction is not ruining you; it is removing what is rough and unnecessary.
