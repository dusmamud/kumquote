Determination is the quiet decision to keep going after the applause stops and the setbacks pile up. Failure only becomes final when we agree that it is, when we stop trying and call the ending permanent. Until that moment, every defeat is simply information about what to change next. Strong determination does not deny the pain of losing; it refuses to let losing have the last word. If your will to succeed stays stronger than your fear of failing again, failure cannot overtake you. It can only delay you, and delays can be endured.
