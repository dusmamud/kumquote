Motivation is hard to manufacture from nothing, and willpower alone runs dry quickly. Sandberg points to a steadier source: work that connects to something you genuinely care about. When a task matters to you, effort feels less like a chore and more like a choice, and setbacks are easier to absorb because the goal still means something. This is practical advice for anyone feeling stuck: instead of hunting for tricks to push yourself, change what you are working on, or find the part of it that matters. Care first, and the energy to keep going tends to follow.
