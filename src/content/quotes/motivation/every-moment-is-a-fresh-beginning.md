We carry yesterday's mistakes and tomorrow's worries into the present, and they crowd out the one moment we can actually change. A fresh beginning does not require a new year, a new job, or a new city; it requires only this moment, used well. Whatever happened before is finished, and whatever comes next has not yet been written. You can begin again right now, quietly, without permission or announcement. Each moment offers a clean page. Take it, and start writing something better with the time you have.
