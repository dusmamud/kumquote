Dreaming has its place, but the sea does not part for spectators. Tagore pictures a person standing at the shore, studying the waves, waiting for the perfect moment to cross — and never leaving the beach. The crossing begins with a step into the water, not with a better plan or a calmer sea. Most of what we want lies on the far shore, and the only way there is through the effort itself. Staring is comfortable; swimming is not. But no one has ever reached the other side by watching.
