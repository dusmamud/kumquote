Hard seasons tempt us to stop and build a home inside them, as if pausing will make the difficulty pass. It will not. The only way out of a dark stretch is through it, one step at a time, even when the next step is all you can see. Keep going does not mean rushing or pretending the pain is small; it means refusing to let a hard chapter become the whole story. Movement itself creates hope, and each step forward proves that you are still capable of moving. So keep walking, and let the far side find you.
