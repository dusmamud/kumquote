The prize at the end of a goal is smaller than the person the pursuit creates. Chasing a goal teaches patience, discipline, and courage, and those qualities stay with you long after the trophy gathers dust. Ziglar's point is that goals are tools for shaping character, not just checklists for collecting achievements. When you aim at something hard, you are forced to grow into someone capable of reaching it. So choose goals that stretch you. What you earn matters, but who you become while earning it matters far more.
