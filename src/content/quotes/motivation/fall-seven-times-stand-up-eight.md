Falling down is not the failure; staying down is. This old proverb reminds us that setbacks are a normal part of any worthwhile pursuit, and what defines us is not how many times we stumble but how many times we rise again. Each fall teaches something the smooth road never could: patience, humility, and the quiet strength that grows only through struggle. Resilience is not about avoiding pain or pretending that failure does not hurt. It is about choosing, again and again, to stand up, dust yourself off, and take the next step forward.
