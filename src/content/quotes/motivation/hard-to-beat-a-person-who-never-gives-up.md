Talent can be matched, luck can change, and skill can be learned, but persistence is almost impossible to defeat. A person who refuses to quit keeps coming back after every loss, and each return makes them harder to beat. Opponents may win a round, but they cannot win the contest against someone who simply will not stop. That is why endurance matters more than a fast start. You do not need to be the strongest or the smartest in the room. You only need to be the one who keeps showing up, keeps trying, and refuses to give up.
