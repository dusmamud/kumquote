Criticism is the unavoidable price of doing anything that matters. The moment you create, lead, speak or try, someone will find fault with your effort, and that is not a sign of failure. It is proof that you are visible and engaged with the world. The only way to escape judgement entirely is to withdraw from life altogether, which costs far more than any harsh comment ever could. So accept criticism as part of the work, listen for the useful part, and keep going. A life free of criticism is also a life free of contribution.
