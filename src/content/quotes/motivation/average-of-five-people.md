The people around us shape us quietly, day after day. Their habits, their standards, their way of talking about problems — all of it rubs off on us without our noticing. That is why choosing company is not a small decision. Spend time with people who are curious, disciplined, and kind, and those qualities start to feel normal. Spend time with people who complain and coast, and the same thing happens in reverse. We cannot always choose everyone around us, but we can choose where we linger, whose advice we seek, and whose example we follow.
