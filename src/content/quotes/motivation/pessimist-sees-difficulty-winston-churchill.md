The same event can be two different stories. A setback is proof that the plan was doomed, or it is the lesson that makes the next attempt stronger. Churchill draws the line between these two views with a sentence. The pessimist and the optimist are not living in different worlds; they are looking at the same world through different habits of mind. One habit counts the obstacles and stops. The other counts the openings and moves. Optimism here is not pretending everything is fine. It is the practical discipline of searching every difficulty for the opportunity hidden inside it.
