The past can weigh on us with its regrets, and the future can worry us with its unknowns, but neither of them decides who we are. What matters most is the strength, character, and courage carried inside. That inner life is larger than any setback behind you and any challenge ahead. When you trust what lies within, the past becomes a lesson instead of a chain, and the future becomes an opportunity instead of a threat. Circumstances change constantly, but the person you are inside is the one constant you can rely on.
