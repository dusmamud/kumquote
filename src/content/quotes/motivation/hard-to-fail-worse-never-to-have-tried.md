Failure stings, but it also teaches, and that makes it far better than never trying at all. A failed attempt leaves you with experience, sharper judgement, and a clearer sense of what to change next time. Never trying leaves you with nothing but the same question, repeated for years: what if I had? The regret of inaction quietly grows heavier, while the pain of a failed attempt fades and turns into wisdom. Courage is not the absence of fear or the guarantee of success; it is the decision to act anyway.
