Reputation is what others think of you; character is what you actually are. Reputation can be built by image and lost by rumour, but character is built quietly, choice by choice, and no one can take it from you. Chasing applause leads to compromise, while guarding character leads to peace. People may misunderstand you for a while, but a strong character earns trust in the end. So spend less energy managing what others think, and more energy becoming someone worth thinking well of. Character first; reputation will follow on its own.
