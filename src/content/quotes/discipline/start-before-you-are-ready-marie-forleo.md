Readiness is a comfortable illusion. We tell ourselves we will start when we know more, when we feel confident, when the timing is right. Forleo's advice cuts through the delay: start before you are ready. Readiness is not a condition for beginning; it is a result of beginning. The first attempt teaches what no amount of preparation can. The beginner who starts today will be further along in a month than the perfectionist who is still preparing. You will never feel fully ready, and that is fine. Start now, learn as you go, and let readiness catch up with you.
