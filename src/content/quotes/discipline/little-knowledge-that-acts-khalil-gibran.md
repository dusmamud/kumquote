Gibran draws a line between knowing and doing that most of us cross in the wrong direction. We collect advice, courses, and plans, and call that progress — but knowledge that never acts is decoration, not power. A single small fact, applied today, beats a library of unopened wisdom. Discipline is the bridge between the two: the decision to act on what you already know, however little it is. You need the resolve to use the lesson you already hold. Learn less, do more, and let the doing teach you the rest.
