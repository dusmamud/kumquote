It is tempting to wait for the key to arrive from outside — a better job, a lucky break, a person who changes everything. Rohn's line removes that comfort: the key is you. Our future is mostly shaped by our own choices, habits, and willingness to learn. Blaming circumstances feels honest, and sometimes circumstances are genuinely hard, but they are not the deciding factor. The person who takes responsibility for their own growth gains something no circumstance can give: control over the direction of their life. The better future does not start with a new situation.
