Self-respect is not built by praise, success, or the approval of others. It is built in the quiet moments when nobody is watching and you choose to act the way you believe you should. Each time you keep a promise to yourself, your trust grows; each time you break one, it shrinks a little. That is why self-control sits at the centre of dignity. A person who cannot control their behaviour eventually stops respecting themselves, no matter how much the world applauds them. True self-respect is earned daily, through the small discipline of doing what you said you would do.
