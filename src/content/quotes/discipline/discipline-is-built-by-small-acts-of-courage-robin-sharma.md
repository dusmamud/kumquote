Discipline sounds like a grand, stern thing, a matter of iron will. Sharma says it is built far more quietly: by consistently performing small acts of courage. The courage to get up early, to say no to a distraction, to do the hard thing first, to keep a promise to yourself. None of these acts feels heroic. But repeated daily, they become the foundation of a disciplined life. Courage is not only for battlefields; it is for ordinary mornings. Each small act proves to yourself that you can be trusted. And a person who trusts themselves can do almost anything.
