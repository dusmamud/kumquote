Knowing the right thing and doing the right thing are two different skills, and the gap between them is where most lives stall. We collect advice, read books, and make plans, then wait for a better moment to begin. But knowledge without application is only decoration, and willingness without action is only a wish. The bridge between knowing and doing is a single decision, made today, in this moment. Stop gathering information you will not use. Take what you already know, apply it now, and let action teach you the rest.
