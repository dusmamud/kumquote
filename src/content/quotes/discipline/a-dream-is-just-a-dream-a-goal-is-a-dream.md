A dream on its own is pleasant to think about, but it asks nothing of us. The moment we attach a plan and a deadline, the dream changes character. It stops being a wish and becomes a commitment, something we can measure ourselves against. Planning forces us to break a large ambition into steps we can actually take, and a deadline keeps those steps from drifting into someday. Discipline lives in that gap between dreaming and doing. It is the daily choice to follow the plan even when enthusiasm fades. Anyone can imagine a better life; the disciplined few give that imagination a date.
