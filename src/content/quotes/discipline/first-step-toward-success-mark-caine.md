Caine says the first step toward success is not a skill or a plan, but a refusal. Your starting environment — your town, your family, your circumstances — hands you a script and expects you to play it. Accepting that script is comfortable, and it is also a cage. Discipline begins the moment you decide the cage is not your destiny: you read, practise, save, and build your way out, one small act of refusal at a time. Refuse the cage, and the first step is already taken.
