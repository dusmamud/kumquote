When life gets hard, our first wish is usually for it to get easier: less work, less pressure. Rohn suggests a different wish. Don't wish it were easier; wish you were better. The difficulty is not the enemy; it is the training ground. An easier life would leave us weaker, less skilled, less ready. A better self, on the other hand, can handle whatever comes. The wish to be better is the only wish we can actually grant ourselves, through practice, patience, and honest effort. Stop asking the world to be easier. Start asking yourself to be better.
