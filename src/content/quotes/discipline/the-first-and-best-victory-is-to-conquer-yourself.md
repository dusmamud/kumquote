Before we can win anything in the outside world, we must first win the battle inside ourselves. Our impulses, fears, and habits are the real opponents, and they are harder to defeat than any rival, because they know us perfectly. Conquering yourself does not mean crushing your feelings; it means learning to direct them, so that anger, desire, and laziness serve your goals instead of sabotaging them. Every other victory depends on this one. A person who cannot control their own appetites will lose even with great talent, while a person who masters themselves can build steadily toward anything.
