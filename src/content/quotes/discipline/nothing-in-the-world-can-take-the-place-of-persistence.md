Coolidge's famous passage strips away every excuse. Talent will not take its place — the world is full of talented people who never finished anything. Genius will not — unrewarded genius is almost a proverb. Education will not — the world is full of educated failures. Only persistence and determination are omnipotent, he insists. It is bracing because it removes the comfort of blaming your gifts or your schooling. You may not be the most talented or the best educated, but persistence is available to anyone. Keep going when others stop, and you will outlast almost every advantage they started with.
