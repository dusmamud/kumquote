Rohn presents a choice we cannot avoid, only choose between. Discipline hurts — early mornings, honest limits, saying no to comfort. But regret hurts more, and it lasts longer, arriving when the chance to act has already passed. The pain of discipline is light because it is chosen and temporary; the pain of regret is heavy because it is forced on us by time. Since suffering of one kind is unavoidable, Rohn asks us to pick the lighter weight. Choose discipline now, and spare yourself the heavier pain later.
