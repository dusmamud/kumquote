Stress often feels like something that happens to us, but this quote points to a power we forget we have: the ability to choose our thoughts. We cannot always change the situation, but we can change what we dwell on. Focusing on the worst outcome feeds anxiety; focusing on the next useful step calms it. This is not pretending problems do not exist; it is refusing to let one thought dominate the mind. The weapon against stress is already in our hands: the choice of which thought to follow.
