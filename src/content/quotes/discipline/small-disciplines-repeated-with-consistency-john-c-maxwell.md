We dream of great achievements arriving in a single dramatic moment. Maxwell disagrees. Small disciplines repeated with consistency every day lead to great achievements gained slowly over time. The page written each morning, the walk taken each evening, the money saved each week. None of them looks like much on its own. But consistency has a quiet power: it compounds. What is small today becomes significant in a year, and remarkable in five. The secret is not intensity but regularity. Do the small thing, every day, without fail, and time will do the rest of the work for you.
