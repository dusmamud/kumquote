Talent feels like an advantage, but it is common. Plenty of people can write a good sentence, play a good game, or solve a hard problem once. What is rare is the willingness to do the unglamorous work, day after day, for years — the drafts, the practice, the revisions nobody sees. Talent without work stays a promise; work without talent can still build something real through persistence. King, who wrote for years before anyone noticed, knew this from experience. The world is full of talented people who never finished anything.
