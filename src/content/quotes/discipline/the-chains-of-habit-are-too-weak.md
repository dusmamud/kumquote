Johnson describes how habits trap us gently. Each repetition adds a link to a chain so light we barely notice it — one extra drink, one skipped workout, one hour lost to distraction. Because the chain feels weightless, we tell ourselves we can break it whenever we like. But links accumulate silently, and by the time the weight is obvious, the habit has hardened into something that resists every good intention. The lesson is to act early. Break a small chain today, while it is still light, and you will never have to wrestle with the heavy one tomorrow.
