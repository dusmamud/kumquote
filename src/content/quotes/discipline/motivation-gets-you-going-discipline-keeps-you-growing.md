Motivation is a wonderful spark: it launches projects and fills notebooks with plans. But sparks fade, and by the second week the excitement is usually gone. That is where discipline takes over — the quiet decision to keep going when you do not feel like it. Growth is not made in the inspired moments but in the ordinary ones, repeated faithfully. Motivation starts the journey, but only discipline carries it to the destination. Do not depend on feeling inspired; build the habits that work even when inspiration is absent, and growth will continue long after the spark has died.
