Waiting does not make fear disappear; it feeds it. The longer we delay, the larger our doubts grow, until the task feels impossible and we never begin. Carnegie knew the antidote: action, even small and imperfect, breaks the cycle. Each step we take proves to ourselves that we can handle the next one, and confidence grows from evidence, not from wishes. Courage is not the absence of fear but the result of acting despite it. If you want to feel brave, do not wait for confidence to arrive; act first, and confidence will follow.
