Doing the right thing in public is easy, because applause and reputation reward it. Integrity is tested in private, where nobody will ever know what you chose. That is when character is real: the choice made without witnesses, without praise, and without fear of getting caught. A person who acts rightly only when watched is performing, not living with integrity. The discipline of integrity is quiet and daily, built from small unseen decisions. Over time, those decisions become who you are. Be the person who does right even when no one is watching.
