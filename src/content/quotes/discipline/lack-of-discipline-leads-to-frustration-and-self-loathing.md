Discipline is often sold as a punishment, but Chapian shows its absence is the real punishment. Without discipline, we break our own promises, miss our own deadlines, and watch our goals drift further away — and then we feel the frustration of knowing we caused it. Self-loathing grows in that gap between what we intended and what we did. Discipline closes the gap. It is not about harshness; it is about keeping faith with ourselves. The freedom we want comes from the structure we keep.
