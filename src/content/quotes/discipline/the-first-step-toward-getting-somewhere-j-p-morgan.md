We often wait for the perfect moment to change: the right job, the right money, the right sign. Morgan's advice is simpler. The first step toward getting somewhere is to decide that you are not going to stay where you are. No plan is needed yet, no map, no permission. Only the decision. Once the decision is made, the next step becomes visible, and then the next. Staying where we are feels safe, but it is also a decision, one that keeps us exactly where we started. Decide to move, and the road begins to appear.
