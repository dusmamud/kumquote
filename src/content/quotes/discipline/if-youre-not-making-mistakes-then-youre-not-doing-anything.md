Mistakes are not proof of failure; they are proof of action. The person who never makes a mistake is usually the person who never tries anything new. Doers stumble because they move, they test, and they learn by doing. Each mistake teaches a lesson that caution alone could never teach. So do not fear mistakes — fear standing still. If your work includes errors, it also includes effort, courage, and growth. Keep doing, keep learning from each misstep, and let your mistakes become the record of a life fully attempted.
