Quitting once feels like a relief, and that relief is the danger. Lombardi warns that quitting is not a single event but a habit that forms the moment it works. The first time we quit, we teach ourselves that leaving is an option; the second time, it becomes the easy answer; the third time, it is the default. Persistence works the same way in reverse — each time we stay, staying gets easier. The habit we build is the one we practise. So practise finishing, especially when quitting feels easiest.
