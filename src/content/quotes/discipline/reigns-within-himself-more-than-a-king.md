A king rules a country, but the hardest kingdom to rule is the one inside us. Passions flare, desires pull, fears whisper — and most of us obey them without thinking. The person who masters these inner forces has achieved something no crown can give: freedom. Self-rule is not about suppressing feelings; it is about choosing which ones get to decide. When anger, greed, or fear run the show, we are servants, however powerful we look. When we rule ourselves, we are free. That is why Milton calls self-mastery greater than kingship.
