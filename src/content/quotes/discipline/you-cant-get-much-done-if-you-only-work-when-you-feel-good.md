Motivation is unreliable. Some days we wake up eager; other days we feel flat, tired, or uninspired. If we only work on the good days, we will accomplish very little. Discipline means working anyway, especially on the days when we do not feel like it. This quote is honest about the reality of effort: feelings follow action more often than action follows feelings. Once we start, energy often returns. The people who get things done are not those who always feel good, but those who work whether they feel good or not.
