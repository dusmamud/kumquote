Roosevelt takes heroism apart and rebuilds it as something ordinary: one step, then another. We imagine courage as a sudden transformation, but it usually arrives quietly, in the act of meeting the next thing in front of us. Each small confrontation teaches the same lesson — the dreaded thing is rarely as dreadful as it looked from a distance, and we are stronger than we thought. So stop waiting to feel heroic before you begin. Take the step in front of you, meet what comes up, and let the heroism accumulate behind you, unnoticed.
