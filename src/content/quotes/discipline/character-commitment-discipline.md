Achievement is not one act but a chain of three. Character gets you out of bed, because who you are decides whether you rise or stay comfortable. Commitment moves you into action, because a promise to yourself turns intention into motion. But only discipline carries you through, because the middle of any task is long and the first excitement fades. Ziglar's sequence is a map of the whole journey: start with character, continue with commitment, and finish with discipline. Miss any link, and the chain breaks.
