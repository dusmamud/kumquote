Every action starts as a thought. Before the hand reaches for the distraction or the mouth speaks the angry word, the mind has already made its choice. That is why discipline is not mainly about willpower in the moment; it is about training the thoughts that come before the moment. A person who watches their thinking — who notices the excuse forming and replaces it with a better thought — finds their actions following naturally. Control the inner conversation first, and the outer behaviour will obey. Master your thoughts, and you master your life.
