Talent without a plan is like a powerful engine with no steering. The genius may have brighter ideas, but without direction, those ideas scatter in every direction and arrive nowhere. The so-called idiot with a plan moves slowly and steadily toward one goal, and steady movement beats scattered brilliance almost every time. A plan turns talent into results by giving it order, priorities, and a next step. You do not need to be the smartest person in the room. You need a plan, the discipline to follow it, and the patience to let it work.
