We admire people who know a little about everything, but mastery belongs to the person who has practised one thing deeply. Ten thousand different kicks, each done once, teach far less than a single kick repeated ten thousand times. Repetition is not boring when it is done with attention; it is how skill sinks into the body until it becomes instinct. Choose the skill that matters most to you, and practise it with patience and focus. Depth beats breadth. The person who has mastered one kick is the one to respect, and the one to become.
