Every meaningful goal asks for a price, and Rohn points out that the price is charged either way. Discipline hurts in small, daily doses: early mornings, hard practice, saying no to comfort. Regret hurts later, all at once, when the chance has passed and cannot be recovered. The pain of discipline is voluntary and temporary; the pain of regret is involuntary and lasting. Framed this way, discipline stops looking like punishment and starts looking like the cheaper, kinder option. Choose the pain you can control today, or accept the pain that will control you tomorrow.
