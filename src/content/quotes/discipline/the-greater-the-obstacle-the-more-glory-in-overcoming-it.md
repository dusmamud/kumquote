Easy victories are quickly forgotten, but the ones that cost us something stay with us forever. Molière ties glory to the size of the obstacle: the harder the climb, the greater the honour in reaching the top. An obstacle is not only a barrier; it is also the measure of the achievement. When we face something truly difficult, we should remember that the difficulty is what will make the victory worth telling. Do not wish the obstacle smaller — rise to meet it, and let the glory match the effort.
