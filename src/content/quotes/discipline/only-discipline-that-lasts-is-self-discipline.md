Phillips draws a sharp line between discipline imposed from outside and discipline that comes from within. Rules, coaches, deadlines and other people can push us for a while, but that pressure fades the moment nobody is watching. Only the discipline we choose for ourselves survives bad moods, setbacks and quiet days when no one would notice if we stopped. That is why self-discipline matters more than talent or motivation. It is the steady, private decision to keep going, and it is the only kind of discipline that stays with us for life.
