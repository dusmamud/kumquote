Talent opens doors, but discipline walks through them every day. A coach sees this clearly: the most gifted athlete on the team will lose to the disciplined one, because games are won in practice, not on game day. The same rule holds everywhere — in business, in study, in health, in art. Discipline is the unglamorous work of showing up, following through, and doing the right thing when nobody is watching. Without it, success is impossible, period. With it, even modest talent can go a very long way.
