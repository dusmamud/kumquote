Holtz leaves no room for exceptions: without self-discipline, success is impossible, full stop. Talent can open a door, luck can offer a chance, and good advice can point the way, but only discipline carries you through the long work between the start and the finish. Every achievement requires doing things you do not feel like doing, again and again. The people who succeed are not those who never feel resistance; they are those who act despite it. Self-discipline is the bridge between wanting success and actually reaching it.
