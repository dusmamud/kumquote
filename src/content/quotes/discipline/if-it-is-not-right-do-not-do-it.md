Discipline begins with two simple rules that cover almost every choice: do only what is right, and say only what is true. Most regrets come from violating one of them — acting on impulse, or speaking words we do not believe. The test is easy to apply and hard to pass, because it demands a pause before every action and every sentence. But that pause is the whole of self-discipline: the moment between impulse and act where character is decided. Live by these two rules and life simplifies.
