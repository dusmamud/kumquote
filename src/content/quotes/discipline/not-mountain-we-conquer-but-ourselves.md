Hillary, the first to stand on Everest's summit with Tenzing Norgay, insists the mountain was never the real opponent. Mountains do not fight back; they simply exist, indifferent and immense. The true contest is within: against fear, exhaustion, doubt, and the voice that says turn back. Conquering ourselves — our limits, our excuses, our smallness — is the victory that makes the summit possible. Every great achievement works the same way: the outer challenge is real, but the decisive battle is always the inner one.
