Hard luck is real. Some people start with disadvantages they did not choose, and no honest philosophy can pretend otherwise. But complaining about luck changes nothing, and waiting for it to change is a plan that never arrives. Hard work is the only reliable answer, because it is the one factor fully within our control. It does not guarantee that luck will turn, but it guarantees that we will be ready when it does — and often, the work itself creates the luck. Opportunities appear to people who are already moving.
