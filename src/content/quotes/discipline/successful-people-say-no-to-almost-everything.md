Success brings opportunities, and opportunities are the real danger. Buffett says the difference between the successful and the really successful is the word no. Saying yes to everything spreads attention so thin that nothing gets the focus it deserves. Really successful people protect their time fiercely, turning down good offers to keep room for the great ones. Discipline is not only doing the right things; it is refusing the wrong ones. Learn to say no, and your yes will finally mean something.
