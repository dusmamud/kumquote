There is no shortcut to strength. Muscles grow only under resistance, skill grows only through practice, and character grows only through difficulty. Hill's word only is deliberate: comfort never produces growth, no matter how much we wish it would. Continuous effort matters more than occasional bursts, because body and mind adapt to what they do repeatedly, not once. Struggle is not a sign that you are failing; it is the mechanism of improvement itself. When you accept that growth requires friction, hard days stop feeling like punishment and start feeling like proof that you are moving forward.
