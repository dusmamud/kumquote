Roosevelt's claim is bold, but his own life supports it. He was a sickly child who built himself into a soldier, explorer, and president through sheer disciplined effort. Self-discipline does not guarantee every dream, yet it opens doors that talent and luck alone cannot. It turns a vague wish into a daily practice, and a daily practice into a capability. Most things that seem impossible are simply things no one has yet disciplined themselves to do. With self-discipline, the range of what you can attempt grows enormously.
