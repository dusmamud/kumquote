Rohn treats discipline the way a builder treats a foundation: invisible, unglamorous, and the thing everything else depends on. Talent, luck, and opportunity can raise a beautiful structure, but without discipline underneath it, the first storm brings it down. The foundation is laid in daily choices — showing up, finishing, keeping small promises — that nobody applauds and everybody notices later. Many people chase the visible parts of success while neglecting the base, then wonder why nothing holds. Start with the foundation. A disciplined life can carry a great deal of success; an undisciplined one cannot carry much of anything.
