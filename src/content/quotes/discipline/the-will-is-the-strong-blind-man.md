Schopenhauer pictures two figures who need each other: the will, strong but blind, and the intellect, clear-sighted but lame. Will alone charges forward without direction; intellect alone sees the path but cannot walk it. Discipline is the partnership between them — the strong blind man carrying the lame man who can see. When our drive and our understanding work together, we move with both power and purpose. Neither is enough alone. The lesson is to train both: strengthen the will, sharpen the vision, and let each carry the other.
