Try and triumph are nearly the same word — separated by just a little extra effort, a little "umph." That tiny difference is where most success lives. The person who tries once and quits is close to the person who triumphs; the gap is not talent but one more push. One more call, one more revision, one more early morning. It is a playful quote with a serious point: the distance between failure and success is often much smaller than we think. Add a little umph, and try becomes triumph.
