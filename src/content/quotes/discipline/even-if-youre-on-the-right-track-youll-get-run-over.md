Being on the right track is not enough. A good plan, a good idea, or a good start means nothing if you stop moving. Life keeps moving forward, and standing still on the right road is just another way of falling behind. Progress needs steady motion: small steps, taken again and again, even when the way is slow. Do not confuse choosing the right direction with arriving. Direction matters, but only motion gets you there. So if you are on the right track, keep walking. The track rewards those who move, not those who sit.
