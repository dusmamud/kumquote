Fear of failure keeps more people from success than failure itself ever does. It convinces us not to try, not to risk, not to expose ourselves to judgement — and in that safety, nothing grows. The paradox is that failure is not the opposite of success; it is the road to it. Every attempt that falls short teaches us something the next attempt needs. Athletes understand this instinctively: the missed shot is part of the game, not the end of it. To succeed, we must stop treating failure as a verdict and start treating it as a step.
