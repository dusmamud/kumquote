Conquering an enemy takes strength and strategy, but it is still an outer battle, fought in the open with allies at your side. Overcoming your own desires is a lonelier fight: the opponent knows all your weaknesses, never retreats, and attacks at your weakest moments. It takes more courage to say no to yourself — to appetite, anger, laziness, and fear — than to face any rival. The person who masters their desires gains something no victory over others can give: freedom from being ruled by impulse.
