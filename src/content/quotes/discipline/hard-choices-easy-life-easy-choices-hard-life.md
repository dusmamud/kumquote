Jerzy Gregorek compresses a lifetime of wisdom into eight words. The hard choice — waking early, telling the truth, saving money, having the difficult conversation — costs comfort today, but it quietly buys an easier life tomorrow. The easy choice feels kind in the moment, yet its bill always arrives: debt, regret, broken trust, lost health. We do not get to choose whether life will be hard, only when. Choose the hard thing now, while you still can, and the future becomes lighter. Choose ease now, and hardship will choose you later.
