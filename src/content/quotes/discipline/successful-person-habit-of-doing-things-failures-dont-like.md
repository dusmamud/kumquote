Success is rarely about talent alone; it is about willingness to do uncomfortable things repeatedly. E. M. Gray observed that successful people do not necessarily enjoy hard tasks more than others. They have simply formed the habit of doing them anyway: waking early, practising deliberately, having difficult conversations, and finishing what they start. The difference is a habit, not a gift. Choose one necessary but unpleasant task today, do it, and repeat it tomorrow. That habit will quietly separate you from the crowd.
