Patience is not passive waiting; it is disciplined waiting, the refusal to grab at the wrong thing now when the right thing is still coming. Impatient people trade their future for a quick reward, then wonder why their goals keep slipping away. The patient person works steadily, endures the delay, and arrives at the outcome they actually wanted. Almost everything worth having — skill, wealth, trust, health — is built slowly, and patience is the price of admission. Learn to wait well, and you will find that what you want has been waiting for you too.
