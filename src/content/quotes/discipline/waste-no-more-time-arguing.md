Talking about goodness is easy; being good is the hard part. We can spend years debating principles, reading advice, and planning self-improvement — all while changing nothing. Marcus Aurelius cuts through that with a single command: stop arguing and be one. Character is not built in conversation; it is built in the small choices of an ordinary day. Keep the promise, tell the truth, do the work, help the person. Nobody needs a perfect theory of goodness. They need one honest action, repeated daily, starting now.
