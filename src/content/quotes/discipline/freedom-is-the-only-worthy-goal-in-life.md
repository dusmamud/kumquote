Most of our worry comes from trying to control things beyond our reach: other people's opinions, the past, the future, the weather. Freedom begins when we stop grasping at those things and focus on what is actually ours: our judgments, our choices, our responses. This quote defines freedom not as doing whatever we want, but as being untroubled by what we cannot change. The person who masters this distinction is free in any circumstance. Discipline, here, is the practice of letting go of everything outside our control.
