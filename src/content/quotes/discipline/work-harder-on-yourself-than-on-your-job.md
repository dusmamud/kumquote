A job pays for the work we do today; working on ourselves pays for the work we will be able to do tomorrow. Most people pour all their energy into their job and none into the person doing it — then wonder why their income, their options, and their satisfaction stop growing. Skills, health, character, and knowledge are the real assets; the job is only where those assets are rented out. When we improve the person, the work improves as a side effect, and so do the rewards.
