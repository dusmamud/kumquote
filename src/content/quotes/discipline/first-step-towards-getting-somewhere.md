Every journey begins with a decision, not a step. Before we can move toward a better place, we must first decide that staying where we are is no longer acceptable. That decision is the true first step, because without it no amount of planning or effort will follow. Morgan's insight is that change starts in the mind: the moment we firmly resolve to leave the familiar behind, the path forward begins to appear. Many people wait for the perfect plan before deciding, but the decision itself creates the momentum. Decide first, and the steps will follow.
