Failure rarely arrives as a single catastrophe. More often it grows from small errors repeated daily: the skipped workout, the ignored budget, the promise postponed. Each one seems harmless alone, but together they compound into a life we did not intend. This quote is a warning and an encouragement. The same compounding works in reverse: small good choices repeated daily build success. The difference between failure and success is often just which small errors, or small disciplines, we repeat. Choose them carefully, because they add up.
