Carnegie's rule is the simplest cure for procrastination ever written. The hard job is the one we circle all day, doing small tasks around it while it grows heavier in our minds. Do it first, while energy is fresh, and two things happen: the dread lifts, and the rest of the day feels lighter because the worst is behind you. Order matters more than effort here. Start tomorrow with the hardest job on your list, and watch how much smoother everything after it becomes.
