The easy reps build nothing new; they only repeat what the muscle can already do. Growth begins in the last three or four, when the body protests and the mind wants to stop. That small area of pain is where champions are made, because it is where most people quit. Discipline is the decision to keep going when comfort says stop, one more rep, one more step, one more day. The pain is not the enemy; it is the signal that you have reached the edge of what you were. Push past it, and you become someone stronger.
