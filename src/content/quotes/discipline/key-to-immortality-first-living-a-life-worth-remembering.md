We cannot control how long we are remembered, but we can control whether our life is worth remembering. Immortality, in this sense, is not about living forever; it is about living so fully and so honestly that our influence outlasts us. A life worth remembering is not necessarily a famous one. It is a life spent on things that matter — work done with care, people loved well, courage shown when it was easier to hide. People are remembered for how they lived, not for how long.
