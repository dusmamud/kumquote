Luck is often just preparation meeting opportunity. Gary Player noticed that the harder he practised, the more often fortune seemed to favour him, and the connection was no coincidence. Practice sharpens skill, and skill turns chance moments into successes that look like luck from the outside. The unprepared call it luck when someone succeeds; the prepared know it was earned through hours of unseen work. If we want more good fortune, the most reliable way to attract it is to practise more. Luck follows effort far more often than it finds idleness.
