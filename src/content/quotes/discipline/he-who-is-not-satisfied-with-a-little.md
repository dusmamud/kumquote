Desire is a moving target: the person who cannot be satisfied with a little will not be satisfied with a lot, either. Epicurus saw that contentment is a skill, not a circumstance. If we train ourselves to appreciate small things — a good meal, a quiet morning, a friend's company — then enough is always within reach. But if we need more to feel satisfied, the finish line keeps moving no matter how much we gain. Satisfaction begins with learning to value a little. Without that, nothing will ever be enough.
