This old Icelandic saying ties discipline to the one thing that outlives us: our name. A life without discipline may still be comfortable, but it drifts — promises broken, work left half done, appetites obeyed without question. At the end, little is worth remembering. Discipline, by contrast, builds the record that earns respect: the kept word, the finished task, the hardship endured without complaint. Honor here is not applause; it is the quiet judgement of a life lived on purpose. The proverb is stern because the stakes are real. Live with discipline, and the honour takes care of itself.
