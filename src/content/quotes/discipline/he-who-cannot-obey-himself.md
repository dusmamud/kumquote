Self-command is not a restriction on freedom; it is the price of it. A person who cannot govern their own impulses, habits, and appetites will eventually be governed by something else: other people's demands, passing moods, or the loudest voice in the room. Discipline is what keeps that from happening. When you can tell yourself what to do and then actually do it, nobody else gets to decide your life for you. The choice is simple and stark. Either you learn to command yourself, or the world will command you, and it will rarely have your best interests at heart.
