Procrastination dresses itself up as patience, but it is usually just delay. Pickens cuts through the excuse with simple logic: if a thing can be done now, now is the best time to do it. Waiting adds nothing but risk, because conditions rarely improve on their own and opportunities quietly expire. The present moment is the only one we truly possess, and using it well is the essence of discipline. Do not wait for a better time, a clearer sign, or a perfect mood. The best time is whenever the thing can be done, and that time is now.
