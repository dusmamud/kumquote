Happiness is often chased in the wrong direction: more money, more praise, more things. Buddha points inward instead. A disciplined mind brings happiness, because a mind that has been trained does not chase every passing desire or fear. It can sit still, choose carefully, and let go of what does not serve it. Discipline here is not punishment; it is freedom from the mind's restless habits. An untrained mind is blown about by every wind. A disciplined one stands steady, and in that steadiness there is a quiet happiness that no purchase or praise can give.
