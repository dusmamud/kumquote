Maria Montessori argues that real discipline cannot be forced from outside — it must grow from freedom within. A child ruled only by fear and punishment obeys while watched and rebels when unwatched; nothing inside them has changed. But a child free to choose meaningful work, to move, to concentrate without interruption, gradually learns to govern themselves. That self-government is true discipline: chosen, not imposed. The same holds for adults. Rules can restrain us, but only freedom can teach us to restrain ourselves — and discipline that comes from liberty is the only kind that lasts.
