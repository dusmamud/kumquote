A lasting habit needs three things at once, and most failed habits are missing one of them. Knowledge tells us what to do and why it matters; without it, we act blindly. Skill gives us the ability to do it; without it, good intentions collapse under difficulty. Desire supplies the reason to keep going; without it, even a skilled person quits. Where the three meet, behaviour becomes steady and almost automatic. That is why willpower alone rarely works: it is only desire, unsupported.
