We love the story of the sudden breakthrough, the masterpiece painted in a single night. Van Gogh, who painted over two thousand works, knew better. Great things are not done by impulse, but by a series of small things brought together. Impulse may start the work, but daily practice, small corrections, patient layering finish it. Talent without that patience is only a spark. With it, the spark becomes a fire. Whatever you are building, do not wait for the grand gesture. Do the small thing today, and again tomorrow, and watch them gather into something great.
