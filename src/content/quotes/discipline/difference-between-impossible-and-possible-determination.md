Impossible and possible are often the same task seen through different levels of determination. What looks impossible to someone who has already given up can look like a challenge to someone determined to find a way. Determination does not remove obstacles, but it changes our relationship with them: instead of proof that we should stop, they become problems to solve. Lasorda's insight reminds us that the boundary between the two words is drawn by our will, not by the world. With enough determination, yesterday's impossible quietly becomes today's possible.
