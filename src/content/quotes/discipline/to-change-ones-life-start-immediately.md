We delay change by waiting for the perfect moment, the right mood, or a sign. This quote allows none of that. Start immediately, do it boldly, and make no exceptions. The flamboyance matters: a half-hearted start is easy to abandon, while a bold one commits us. No exceptions means no bargaining with ourselves when it gets hard. Change fails most often in the gap between deciding and doing. This quote closes that gap entirely: decide, and then act at once, without conditions.
