Adler corrects a misunderstanding of freedom. We usually think of it as the absence of rules — doing whatever we want. But a mind ruled by impulse, habit, fear, or fashion is not free; it is simply obedient to whatever shouts loudest. True freedom requires discipline: training that lets you pause, think, and choose for yourself rather than react. Discipline does not imprison you; it is what sets you free from your own worst tendencies. The disciplined mind can say no to the immediate and yes to the important. That is real freedom — not doing everything, but choosing well.
