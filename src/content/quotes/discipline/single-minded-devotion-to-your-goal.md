A mission fails most often not from lack of talent but from divided attention. We chase several goals at once, and none of them gets our full energy. Single-minded devotion means choosing one goal and giving it everything: our time, focus, and persistence. This quote is about the power of concentration. When all our effort points in one direction, even modest ability can achieve remarkable things. Devotion is not obsession; it is clarity. Know what matters most, and refuse to let lesser things steal your attention from it.
