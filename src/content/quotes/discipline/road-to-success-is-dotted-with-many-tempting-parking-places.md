On the way to any worthwhile goal, comfortable resting spots keep appearing. A little success, a little praise, a little money — each one invites you to stop and settle. These parking places feel safe, but they are traps for people who stop too soon. The road to real success continues past them, and only those who keep driving reach the end. Rest when you must, but do not park permanently at the first pleasant stop. Keep your eyes on the destination, enjoy the journey, and resist the temptation to settle for less than you set out to achieve.
