The mightiest warrior is not the one who defeats armies but the one who defeats himself: his anger, his greed, his fear, and his laziness. External enemies can be outnumbered or outmanoeuvred, but the enemy within is always present, knows all your weaknesses, and never surrenders easily. Conquering yourself requires more courage than any battle, because there is no glory in it and no one watching. Yet it is the victory that makes all others possible. Master yourself, and you will have beaten the hardest opponent there is.
