Dreams are pleasant company, but they do not build anything while we sleep in them. Valéry's joke is a wake-up call in the literal sense: the best way to make a dream come true is to wake up and start working on it. The dreamer who never wakes keeps the dream perfect and untouched, because reality would demand effort and risk. But a dream that stays a dream is only a comfort. Wake up, open your eyes, and trade the pleasant sleep for the harder — and far better — work of making it real.
