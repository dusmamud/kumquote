Washington, who led an outnumbered army to victory, knew that discipline was worth more than numbers. A small force that trains together, obeys together, and holds together under pressure can defeat a larger one that does not. Discipline turns weakness into strength — not by making individuals superhuman, but by making a group reliable. The same is true beyond the battlefield: a team, a family, or a single life held together by discipline becomes formidable. It is the soul of any effort, the invisible force that makes ordinary people capable of extraordinary things.
