Talent is a head start, not a finish line. A gifted person who coasts will eventually be overtaken by an ordinary person who trains every day, because skill grows through practice, not through potential alone. This quote is a warning to the talented and an encouragement to everyone else: what matters most is not where you begin but how consistently you work. Talent without effort fades, while effort without talent still builds real ability over time. The contest is never really talent against hard work; it is discipline against complacency, and discipline wins.
