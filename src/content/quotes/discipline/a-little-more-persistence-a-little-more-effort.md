Failure often looks final, but it is rarely as hopeless as it seems. The difference between failure and success is sometimes only a little more — a little more persistence when you want to quit, a little more effort when you feel exhausted. Many people stop just before the turning point, never knowing how close they were. Persistence does not require a huge leap; it asks for one more step, one more try, one more day. That small extra effort can transform what seemed lost. When failure feels certain, try a little more before you decide it is over.
