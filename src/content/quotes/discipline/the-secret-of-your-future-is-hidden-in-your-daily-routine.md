We look for the secret of our future in grand plans and sudden breakthroughs, but it is hiding in plain sight: our daily routine. What you do every morning, every evening, and every ordinary hour is quietly writing the next chapter of your life. A routine of learning builds wisdom, a routine of care builds health, and a routine of neglect builds regret. The future is not a mystery to be solved but a harvest to be grown. If you do not like where your routine is taking you, change the routine, and the future will follow.
