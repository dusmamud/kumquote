Easy effort does not build strength; only the hard part does. The repetitions that hurt are the ones that count, because they are the ones that push past comfort and force growth. Anyone can do the easy reps, but champions are made in the painful ones, where the body wants to quit and the will says one more. This applies far beyond the gym. The study session after tiredness, the honest conversation after fear, the extra hour after everyone leaves — those are the reps that count. Growth lives on the far side of discomfort.
