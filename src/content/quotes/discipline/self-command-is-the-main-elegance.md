True elegance is not found in clothes, manners, or wealth. It lives in the quiet ability to govern oneself, to choose a measured response instead of a hasty reaction, and to hold steady when feelings pull in every direction. A person who commands their own impulses carries a dignity that no outward display can match. Self-command shows in small moments: the pause before speaking, the patience to listen, the restraint to walk away. That inner order is the deepest form of grace, and it is available to anyone willing to practice it.
