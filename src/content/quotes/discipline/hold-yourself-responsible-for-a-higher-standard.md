It is easy to meet the standard others set for us, because it is usually modest and forgiving. The harder and more valuable task is to set a higher standard for ourselves than anyone else would dare to expect. That standard becomes a private promise: to do our best work, keep our word, and act with integrity even when nobody is watching. Holding ourselves to it builds self-respect that no praise can give. Do not wait for others to raise the bar. Raise it yourself, meet it daily, and let the habit of excellence become who you are.
