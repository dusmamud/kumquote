Habits begin as small choices we barely notice, repeated once, then again, until they become part of who we are. At first we shape them, choosing what to practise and what to allow. But sooner or later the habit takes over and begins to shape us, deciding what we do when we are tired, tempted, or distracted. That is why the small daily choices matter so much. Choose your habits deliberately, while they are still yours to choose. Once they are formed, they will carry you, for better or worse, long after the choosing is done.
