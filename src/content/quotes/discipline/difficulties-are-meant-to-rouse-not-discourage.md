Difficulties arrive in every life, and their purpose is not to break us but to wake us up. Like resistance training for the body, conflict strengthens the spirit: it forces us to think harder, endure longer, and discover resources we did not know we had. Discouragement is the easy response, but it mistakes the purpose of the trial. The struggle itself is the training ground, and the strength gained there stays with us long after the difficulty passes. When hardship comes, do not ask why it happened to you.
