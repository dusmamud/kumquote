Destiny is not decided in grand ceremonies but in small, quiet moments — the second you choose to speak up, to start, to say no, to keep going when quitting tempts you. Each decision is a fork in the road, and the road you are on today is the sum of the forks you chose yesterday. That is both a warning and a comfort: the past cannot be undone, but the next decision is always yours to make. Shape your destiny one decision at a time, and choose as if the future is watching — because it is.
