Life does not leave a vacuum: if you do not decide your own direction, someone else will decide it for you. A boss, a trend, a circumstance, or drift will quietly take the wheel. Discipline is the act of keeping your hands on it. It means choosing your priorities, setting your standards, and doing the work that keeps those choices real. Nobody hands you control of your destiny; you take it by acting deliberately every day. The alternative is not freedom but a life shaped by other people's plans, and that is a price few would willingly pay.
