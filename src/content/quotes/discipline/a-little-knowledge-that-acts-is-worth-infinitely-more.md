Knowledge alone changes nothing. A library of ideas, never used, is worth less than one small truth put into practice today. Action is what turns knowing into becoming: the lesson applied, the skill practised, the kindness actually done. Many people wait until they know enough to begin, but enough never arrives, and the waiting becomes the habit. Start with the little you already know, and use it. Each act teaches more than a shelf of unread books, and the knowledge that acts grows stronger with every use.
