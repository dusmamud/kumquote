Anyone can keep going while they feel strong. The real test arrives when strength is gone, the body exhausted and the mind begging to quit. Dempsey defines a champion not by talent or trophies but by that single act of rising anyway. Getting up when you can't is not about ignoring pain; it is refusing to let pain decide for you. Discipline lives in that gap between what you feel able to do and what you do regardless. Champions are not people who never fall, but people who rise one more time than they fall.
