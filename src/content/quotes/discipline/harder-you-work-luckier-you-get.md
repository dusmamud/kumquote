People often call successful people lucky, as if fortune simply smiled on them. Player, one of golf's greats, knew better: the harder he practised, the more often the ball seemed to bounce his way. Hard work does not guarantee success, but it creates conditions where good fortune can find you. Preparation turns random chances into real opportunities, and persistence keeps you in the game long enough for those chances to arrive. Luck favours the prepared, not the idle. If you want more luck in your life, the most reliable way to get it is to work harder than everyone expects.
