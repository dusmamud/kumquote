Obstacles usually feel like proof that we should stop, but Marcus Aurelius turns that idea upside down. The very thing blocking your path is the material your progress is made of. A difficult boss teaches patience, a failed plan teaches better judgment, and a closed door forces you to find a stronger route. When you treat resistance as training instead of defeat, every setback becomes useful. Instead of asking why this happened to you, ask what it demands of you. Answer that question honestly, and the obstacle stops being a wall and becomes the road itself.
