Freedom is often thought of as the absence of external limits, but Epictetus teaches that true freedom begins inside. If your desires, fears, and habits control you, you are not free, even when no one else restrains you. A person mastered by anger, addiction, or approval cannot choose wisely, because impulses decide for them. Self-mastery means pausing before reacting, choosing values over urges, and taking responsibility for your own mind. That inner freedom cannot be given by anyone else; it must be practised daily. Master yourself, and no external circumstance can take your freedom away.
