Every choice carries a cost, and Rohn weighs the two costs honestly. Discipline is uncomfortable in the moment: early mornings, saying no, doing the work when nobody notices. But that discomfort is light, measured in ounces, and it passes. Regret is different. It grows heavier over the years, fed by every opportunity missed and every promise broken to yourself. The quote does not pretend discipline is easy; it simply asks which weight you would rather carry. Choose the small, daily weight now, and you avoid the crushing one later.
