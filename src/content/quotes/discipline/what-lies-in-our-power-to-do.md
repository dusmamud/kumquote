Freedom is often pictured as the power to do anything, but real freedom includes the power to refuse. Every choice carries a second, hidden choice: the choice not to act. A person who cannot say no to a habit, an impulse, or a demand is not truly free, however many options they appear to have. Strength grows when we recognise that restraint is also an action. Choosing what not to do shapes a life just as surely as choosing what to do, and often more decisively.
