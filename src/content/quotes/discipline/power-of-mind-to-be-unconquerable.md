Fortune can take almost everything: wealth, health, reputation, comfort. What it cannot take, Seneca says, is a mind that refuses to be conquered. The power of the mind is not in controlling events but in controlling its response to them. A person who has trained the mind to stay steady cannot be defeated by circumstance, because defeat requires surrender, and surrender is a choice the mind can refuse. Build that inner strength through practice, reflection, and discipline. The world will test you, but an unconquerable mind answers every test the same way: I remain.
