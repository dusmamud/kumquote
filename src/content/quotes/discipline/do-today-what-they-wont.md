Success is often decided by a simple trade: doing today what others refuse to do, so that tomorrow you can do what they cannot. The early mornings, the extra practice, the hard conversations, and the disciplined choices are all things others avoid. But each one buys a capability they will never have. Johnson's formula is not about talent; it is about willingness. Pay the price now, in effort and discipline, and the reward arrives later as ability. Do today what they won't, and tomorrow will belong to you.
