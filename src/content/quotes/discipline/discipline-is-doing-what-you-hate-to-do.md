Everyone can do the parts they enjoy; discipline is measured by the parts they do not. The boring training, the early mornings, the repetitive practice — these are the moments where most people quit, and where champions are made. Doing unpleasant work with full effort, as if you loved it, changes the work itself: resistance fades, focus sharpens, and what felt like a burden becomes a source of pride. Attitude is a choice, and this line demands the hardest version of it. Do not wait to feel like it.
