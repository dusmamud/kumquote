Waiting feels safe, but Epictetus treats it as the real danger. Every day spent tolerating less than your best is a day you will not get back, and the habit of waiting becomes its own kind of prison. Demanding the best for yourself is not arrogance; it is self-respect, the refusal to accept a smaller life than you are capable of living. The question has no comfortable answer, because any honest answer is: too long already. Stop waiting for permission, for the perfect moment, for someone else to start. Demand your best now, and begin.
