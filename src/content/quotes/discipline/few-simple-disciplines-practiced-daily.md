Success looks complicated from the outside, but Rohn reduces it to something anyone can do: a few simple disciplines, repeated daily. Read a little, exercise a little, save a little, practise a little, and do it again tomorrow. None of these acts is impressive on its own, but together, over time, they compound into extraordinary results. The difficulty is not in knowing what to do but in doing it every day, especially on the days you do not feel like it. That is discipline, and it is the whole secret.
