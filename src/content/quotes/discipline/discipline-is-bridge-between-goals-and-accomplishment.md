A goal without discipline is only a wish. You can dream clearly, plan carefully, and talk passionately, yet nothing changes until you act consistently. Jim Rohn calls discipline the bridge because it connects where you are with where you want to be. Each disciplined choice, waking early, practising daily, saving regularly, is one plank on that bridge. Without it, the gap remains, no matter how beautiful the goal looks from the other side. Build the bridge one small action at a time. Discipline turns intention into progress and progress into accomplishment, step by steady step.
