Theory is useful, but it is weightless. You can read a hundred books about swimming and still sink the first time you enter the water. A single ounce of action — one real attempt, one real mistake, one real lesson — teaches more than a ton of reading. Action exposes what theory hides: the gaps, the friction, the reality of the task. That is not an argument against learning; it is an argument for learning by doing. Study enough to start, then start. The doing will teach the rest.
