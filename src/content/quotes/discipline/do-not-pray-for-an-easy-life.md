An easy life sounds appealing, but it rarely produces strength, wisdom, or character. Bruce Lee understood that difficulty is not an obstacle to a good life; it is often the training ground for one. Instead of asking for problems to disappear, ask for the strength to face them with courage and patience. Endurance is built through practice: showing up when you are tired, staying calm under pressure, and learning from failure. A difficult life faced with strength can become a meaningful one. Do not wish the mountain away. Become strong enough to climb it, one steady step at a time.
