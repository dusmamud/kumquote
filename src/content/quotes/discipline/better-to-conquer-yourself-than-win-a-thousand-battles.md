A thousand battles can be won with armies, weapons, and luck — and still leave the winner restless, angry, or afraid. Conquering ourselves is a different kind of victory, because its prize cannot be taken away. No enemy can steal self-control, no defeat can undo patience, and no fortune can remove the peace that comes from mastering our own desires. The battles we fight outside ourselves are often easier than the ones inside, which is why most people prefer them. But the inner victory is the only one that truly belongs to us.
