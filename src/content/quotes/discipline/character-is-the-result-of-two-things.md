Character is not born fully formed; it is built from two daily ingredients — how you think and how you spend your hours. Your mental attitude decides what you notice and how you respond: with bitterness or gratitude, with fear or courage. Your use of time decides what you become: hours given to learning and honest work build a different person than hours given to complaint and distraction. Both are choices, repeated daily, and together they quietly shape who you are. If you want a better character, do not look for a dramatic transformation.
