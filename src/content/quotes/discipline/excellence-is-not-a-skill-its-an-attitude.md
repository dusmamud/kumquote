Excellence is often mistaken for talent, but it is really a way of approaching the work. Skills can be learned, but attitude decides whether you practise carefully, finish properly, and care about the result. Two people with the same skill will produce very different work if one brings care and the other brings indifference. Attitude is the choice to do your best even when no one is watching and even when the task is small. Choose excellence as your attitude first, and the skills will follow. Great work begins in the mind, not in the hands.
