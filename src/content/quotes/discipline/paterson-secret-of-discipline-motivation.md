We often treat discipline as pure willpower, a grim struggle against ourselves, but Paterson points to a kinder secret: a strong enough reason makes discipline almost effortless. When the purpose matters deeply — a child to provide for, a dream worth chasing, a value worth living — the hard actions stop feeling like sacrifices and start feeling like steps. Motivation does not remove the work, yet it removes the inner argument about whether the work is worth doing. The lesson is practical: instead of fighting yourself, strengthen your reason, and discipline will follow on its own.
