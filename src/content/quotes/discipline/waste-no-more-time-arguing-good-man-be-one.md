Talking about goodness is easy, and arguing about it can fill a lifetime without changing anything. Marcus Aurelius cuts through all of that with a simple command: stop debating and start being. Character is not built in conversation but in action, in the small choices made when nobody is watching. Each day offers fresh chances to be honest, patient, and brave, and each chance taken makes the next one easier. The argument about what a good person should be ends the moment you become one. Be the example, and let your life do the talking.
