Wanting to win is common; almost everyone wants it. Far rarer is the willingness to prepare: the early mornings, the repetition, the study of weaknesses, the boring fundamentals. Preparation is where winning is actually decided, long before the contest starts. This quote separates wishers from winners. The wishers talk about victory; the winners build it quietly, day by day. If we want to succeed at anything, the question is not how badly we want it, but how seriously we are willing to prepare for it.
