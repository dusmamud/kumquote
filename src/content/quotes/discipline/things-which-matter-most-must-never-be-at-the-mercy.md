Every day offers a hundred small demands, and the least important ones shout the loudest: messages, chores, distractions, and other people's emergencies. The things that matter most, like health, relationships, and meaningful work, rarely shout at all. They wait quietly, and if we let the trivial take their place, we end the day busy but empty. Protecting what matters is a daily act of discipline. Say no to the small thing, guard time for the great thing, and refuse to let the urgent crowd out the important.
