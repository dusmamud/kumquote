Success is not the product of one quality but of several working together. Powell lists the ingredients plainly: striving for excellence, working hard, learning from failure instead of fearing it, staying loyal to people and principles, and persisting when others quit. Remove any one of them and the result weakens; hard work without learning repeats the same mistakes, while persistence without loyalty builds on sand. The formula is demanding but honest, because lasting achievement is never accidental. It is built daily, through the disciplined combination of effort, humility, and the refusal to give up.
