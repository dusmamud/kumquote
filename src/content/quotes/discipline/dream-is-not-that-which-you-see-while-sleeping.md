There are two kinds of dreams: the ones we have while sleeping, which vanish by morning, and the ones that keep us awake, which demand action. This quote is about the second kind. A real dream is not an escape from life but a purpose that will not let us rest until we pursue it. It disturbs our comfort because it asks us to become more than we are. If a dream does not keep you up at night, it may be only a wish. A true dream is a calling that refuses to be ignored.
