Discipline rarely feels good while it is happening. Waking early, saying no to temptation, practicing when we would rather rest — all of it feels like pain in the moment. That is why so many people quit: they judge discipline by how it feels now instead of what it produces later. But discipline is training, not punishment. Like an athlete's workout, it hurts in the moment and strengthens over time. The harvest comes later: steadiness, peace, and a life that holds together under pressure. Endure the training now, and enjoy the harvest later.
