Character is not built in dramatic moments; it is built quietly, in how we handle desire each day. Everyone wants what is easy, pleasant, or immediate, and the discipline of desire is choosing the better thing over the easier one. That choice, repeated hundreds of times, becomes the background of who we are. A person who cannot say no to a craving will struggle to keep promises, finish hard work, or stay loyal under pressure. Locke reminds us that self-control is not a restriction on life but the skill that makes a trustworthy character possible.
