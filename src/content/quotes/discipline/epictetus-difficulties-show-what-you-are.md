In comfort, we can believe almost anything about ourselves — that we are patient or brave — because nothing has tested the claim. Difficulty strips away illusion and shows the truth: our real character appears in how we respond to trouble. That revelation can be uncomfortable, but it is also a gift, because we cannot improve what we have not seen. Epictetus does not ask us to enjoy hardship, only to use it as a mirror. Each difficulty teaches us something about who we are, and that knowledge is the first step toward becoming who we wish to be.
