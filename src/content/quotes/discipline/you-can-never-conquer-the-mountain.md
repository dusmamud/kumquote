Whittaker was the first American to climb Everest, and his lesson is humbling: the mountain does not care whether you reach the top. It stays where it is, indifferent, and the only thing that changes is the climber. Fear, doubt, exhaustion, and the urge to quit — these are the real opponents, and they live inside us. Every great challenge works the same way. The mountain, the deadline, or the opponent is only the setting; the victory is always over ourselves. Conquer yourself, and the mountain takes care of itself.
