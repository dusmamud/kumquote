Jim Rohn reminds us that pain is unavoidable either way, so the only real choice is which kind we accept. Discipline hurts in small, daily doses: early mornings, hard workouts, saying no to comfort. Regret hurts far more, and it arrives all at once, when the chance to act has already passed. The first pain is chosen and temporary; the second is forced on us and lingers. Choosing discipline means paying a small price now to avoid a much larger one later. Both roads demand something — only one gives something back.
