The work itself is the same for everyone: early mornings, repetition, sacrifice, and discomfort. What separates winners from losers is not the difficulty of the work but the attitude toward it. Winners see discipline as a trade they are glad to make, because they understand what it buys. Losers see the same effort as a penalty imposed on them, something to endure or escape. The moment you start resenting the work, you have already decided to lose. But when you learn to love the discipline itself, the effort stops feeling like a cost and starts feeling like the path.
