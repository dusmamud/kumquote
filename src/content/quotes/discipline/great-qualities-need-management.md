Talent and good qualities are only raw material. Without management, they can do as much harm as good: intelligence without discipline becomes arrogance, courage without judgement becomes recklessness, and passion without control becomes destruction. La Rochefoucauld reminds us that having a gift is not enough; you must also govern it. The management of your qualities, knowing when to use them and when to restrain them, is what turns potential into character. Greatness is not in what you have, but in how well you manage it.
