The mind is either a servant or a master, and there is no neutral ground between the two. Left unguided, it replays old fears, magnifies small problems, and drags attention toward whatever is loudest. Governed well, it becomes a tool that serves clear thinking and steady action. Ruling the mind does not mean suppressing every feeling; it means noticing thoughts, choosing which ones to follow, and refusing to be pulled along by the rest. That daily practice is the foundation of freedom and peace.
