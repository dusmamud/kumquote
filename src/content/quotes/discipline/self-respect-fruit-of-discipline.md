Heschel connects two things we usually treat as separate: discipline and dignity. Self-respect is not granted by praise or success; it grows from the inside, each time we keep a promise to ourselves or say no to an impulse that would diminish us. Every act of self-mastery, however small, adds a little more dignity. Conversely, every surrender to what we know is beneath us quietly erodes it. Discipline, then, is not punishment — it is the way we earn our own respect, one honest choice at a time.
