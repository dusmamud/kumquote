We cannot control every thought that enters our minds, but we can choose which thoughts we feed. A thought we entertain grows; one we dismiss fades. This quote places moral growth in that daily choice. The highest stage of character is not never having a bad thought, but refusing to let it take root. Self-control begins in the mind, long before it shows in action. By guarding our thoughts, we gradually shape our habits, our character, and eventually the whole direction of our lives.
