We dream of a different life while repeating the same daily routine, then wonder why nothing changes. But a life is not transformed by a single decision; it is transformed by the small things we do every day. Change one habit, and you change the direction of your future. The difficulty is that daily change feels too small to matter, so we dismiss it. Yet a year of small daily changes adds up to a different person. If you want a different life, do not wait for a new year. Change something you do today, and keep changing it tomorrow.
