Every meaningful achievement asks for the same trade: give up something small now to gain something larger later. The person who can delay gratification can study instead of scrolling, save instead of spending, and practice instead of relaxing, and those small refusals compound into real success. Those who cannot wait take the quick reward every time, and the quick reward is rarely the one that matters. Discipline is simply the ability to keep the future in mind while the present tempts you. Master that one skill, and most other skills become far easier to build.
