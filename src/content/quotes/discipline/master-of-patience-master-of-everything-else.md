Patience is the master skill because nearly every other skill depends on it. The patient person can wait for the right moment, endure setbacks without panic, and keep working when results are slow to appear. Impatience, by contrast, rushes decisions, abandons good plans too early, and turns small problems into crises. To master patience is to master yourself, and to master yourself is to master almost everything else life will ask of you. It is not passive waiting; it is steady, deliberate strength. Learn to wait well, and the rest becomes far easier to handle.
