Warren Buffett warns that habits do not arrive as chains — they arrive as threads. One skipped workout, one small overspend, one little lie: each feels weightless on its own. But repeated daily, the threads braid into something that holds us fast, and by the time we notice the weight, breaking free takes far more effort than forming the habit ever did. The lesson is to act early, while the chain is still light. Good habits work the same way in reverse: tiny, almost invisible deposits of discipline compound into a strength that later feels unbreakable.
