Worthwhile achievement needs no magic formula, only three plain essentials. Hard work supplies the effort; stick-to-itiveness supplies the staying power when results are slow; common sense supplies the judgement to work on the right things in the right way. Leave out any one of them and the effort falls short: hard work without sense wastes energy, and sense without persistence quits too early. Together, they are enough for almost any goal. You do not need genius or luck to begin. Bring honest effort, refuse to quit, and use good judgement — and worthwhile things will follow.
