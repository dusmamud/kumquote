Successful people do not enjoy hard things more than anyone else; they have simply built the habit of doing them anyway. Waking early, practising daily, having the difficult conversation, finishing the boring task: these are things almost no one likes, but they are the things that separate success from failure. Gray's insight is that success is not about talent or luck but about a habit, the willingness to do the uncomfortable work consistently. Build that habit, and you will find yourself doing what others avoid, and getting what others never reach.
