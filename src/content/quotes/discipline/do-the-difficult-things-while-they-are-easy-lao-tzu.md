We put off difficult things, waiting for a better time, a clearer head, a stronger will. Lao Tzu's advice is to do them while they are easy, before they grow. A problem handled today is a task; left alone, it becomes a crisis. A thing begun while small is a simple step; left until large, it becomes overwhelming. The wise do not wait for things to become hard. They act early, when the effort is small and the result is large. Timing is the art: do the difficult while it is easy, and the great while it is small.
