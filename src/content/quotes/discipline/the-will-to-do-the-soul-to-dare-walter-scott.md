Dreams without action are only wishes, and wishes fade. Scott pairs the two things that turn a wish into a deed: the will to do and the soul to dare. The will is the decision to begin, the resolve to take the step even when the outcome is uncertain. The soul to dare is the courage to risk failure, embarrassment, disappointment. Together they are enough to move mountains, or to move us. Most people have one or the other. Those who finish what they start have both, and use them, in acts of courage that add up to a life.
