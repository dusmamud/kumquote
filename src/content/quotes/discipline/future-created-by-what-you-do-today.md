Tomorrow is a comfortable place to store our plans, because tomorrow never arrives — it is always one day away. The future is not built in the future; it is built today, in the small choices we make right now. What we eat, what we practice, what we save, what we learn — each of today's actions is a brick in tomorrow's house. Waiting for a better day to start only delays the result. The future belongs to people who act today, even imperfectly. Start now, and tomorrow will take care of itself.
