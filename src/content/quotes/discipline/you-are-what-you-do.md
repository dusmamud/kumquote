Intentions are cheap. We all have impressive plans for who we will become — once things calm down, once we have more time, once we feel ready. But identity is not built from intentions; it is built from actions. The person who exercises is an exerciser, whatever they claim about next Monday. The person who keeps promises is trustworthy, whatever they meant to do. Our actions are the only honest biography we have. If we want to be someone different, the path is simple and strict: do different things, starting today.
