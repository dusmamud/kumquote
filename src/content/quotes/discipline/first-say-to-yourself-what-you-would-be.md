Most people act first and think later, then wonder why their lives feel scattered. Epictetus reverses the order: decide who you want to be, name it clearly, and let that decision choose your actions. If you would be a disciplined person, then do what disciplined people do, even when it is inconvenient. Identity is not discovered by accident; it is built by doing, one choice at a time, in the direction of the person you said you would be. Decide first. Then do. The life you want is on the other side of that simple order.
