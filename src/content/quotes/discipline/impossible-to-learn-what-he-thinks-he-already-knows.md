The biggest obstacle to learning is the belief that we already know. When we think we understand something, we stop listening, stop questioning, and stop growing. A beginner's mind, by contrast, stays open and curious. This quote is a warning against intellectual pride. Real learning requires the humility to admit ignorance, even about things we have done for years. The moment we say I already know, we close the door. The moment we say I might be wrong, we open it again, and growth walks through.
