Greatness is not a gift handed to the talented; it is a door opened by self-discipline. Talent without discipline wastes itself on distractions, while ordinary ability, guided by discipline, compounds day after day into extraordinary results. Self-discipline is the key because it unlocks everything else: the habit of finishing, the patience to practise, the courage to keep going when motivation dies. It is a small key — just the daily decision to do what you know you should do — but it opens a very large door.
