Mistakes are inevitable; what defines a person is what happens next. First comes the bigness to admit the mistake — no excuses, no blame, just honest ownership. Then comes the intelligence to study it: what went wrong, what was misunderstood, what should change. Finally comes the strength to correct it, which is often the hardest part, because correction costs time, pride, and comfort. Many people stop at the first step, and some never take it at all. But those who walk all three steps turn every error into an education.
