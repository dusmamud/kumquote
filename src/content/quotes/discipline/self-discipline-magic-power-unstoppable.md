There is no secret trick to becoming unstoppable — only the daily habit of doing what you said you would do. Self-discipline looks like magic from the outside because its results seem impossible: the book finished, the business built, the body transformed. From the inside, it is far less mysterious: small promises kept, one after another, until they add up to something extraordinary. Motivation starts the journey, but discipline finishes it. Anyone who masters self-discipline becomes very hard to stop — and that power is available to anyone willing to practice it.
