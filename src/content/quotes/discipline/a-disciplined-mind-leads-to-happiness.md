The Dalai Lama traces both happiness and suffering to the same source: the state of our minds. An undisciplined mind chases every worry, replays every slight, and magnifies every fear, turning small troubles into long suffering. A disciplined mind notices those same thoughts without being ruled by them — it can pause, choose a kinder interpretation, and let go of what does not serve it. This is not suppression, but training: like a muscle, attention grows stronger with practice. Happiness, in this teaching, is less about changing the world than about training the mind that meets it.
