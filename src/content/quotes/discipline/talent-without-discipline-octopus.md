Talent without discipline is a funny image because it is also a true one: plenty of ability, moving in every direction at once, and getting nowhere. An octopus on roller skates has eight arms and no control, just as a talented person without discipline has gifts and no direction. Talent promises a great deal, but only discipline delivers it, by focusing ability on one goal and working at it steadily. Without that focus, talent flails and entertains but never arrives. Give your talent the discipline it needs, and it will finally go somewhere.
