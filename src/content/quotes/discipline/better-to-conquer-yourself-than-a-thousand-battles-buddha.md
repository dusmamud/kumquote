The Buddha measures victory by a harder standard than the battlefield. Winning a thousand battles against others proves strength, luck, and numbers — but it leaves the winner unchanged inside, still ruled by anger, craving, and fear. Conquering yourself is the one victory that changes the victor: mastering your impulses, your temper, your appetites. The quote reframes every struggle as an inner one. Your real opponent is not the rival across the field; it is the part of you that would rather quit, rage, or indulge. Win there first.
