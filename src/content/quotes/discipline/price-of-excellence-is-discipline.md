Everything has a price, and excellence is no exception. Its price is discipline: the daily effort, the denied shortcuts, the steady practice when no one is watching. Mediocrity also has a cost, though it is paid later, in disappointment: the quiet regret of knowing you could have done more. Ward's comparison makes the choice clear. You will pay one way or the other, so choose the price that buys something worthwhile. Pay for excellence with discipline now, rather than paying for mediocrity with disappointment for the rest of your life.
