Anyone can be disciplined for a day. The real test is the ordinary Tuesday when nobody is watching and the excitement has worn off. Lives are not shaped by rare bursts of effort but by the small things repeated until they become who you are. The person who reads ten pages every night, saves a little every month, or trains three times a week will quietly outgrow the person who waits for motivation. Consistency is the unglamorous engine of every transformation. Do the small thing again today, and let repetition do the heavy lifting.
