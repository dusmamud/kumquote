Kennedy calls self-discipline magic because its effects look impossible from the outside. The person who trains daily, saves steadily, and keeps promises to themselves seems to have some secret advantage — but the secret is only the habit of doing what they decided, whether they feel like it or not. Discipline compounds quietly: small kept promises build trust in yourself, and that trust lets you attempt bigger things. Build the habit in small doses — one promise kept today, another tomorrow — and watch how quickly the magic starts to look like a superpower.
