Most of us fill our calendars first and then wonder why the important things never happen. We prioritise the urgent, the loud, and the immediate, while the things that truly matter wait quietly for a space that never comes. The key is to reverse the order: decide what matters most, put it on the schedule first, and let everything else fit around it. This is a discipline, not a trick, because it requires saying no to good things in order to protect the best ones. Schedule your priorities, and your days will finally reflect your values.
