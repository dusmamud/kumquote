Starting is easy; enduring is the hard part. Anyone can begin a diet, a course, or a new habit with enthusiasm, but enthusiasm fades, and what remains is the daily choice to continue. Endurance is the discipline of staying when quitting would feel easier, of holding the course through boredom, doubt, and discomfort. The final victory rarely belongs to the fastest or the most talented. It belongs to the one who is still standing at the end, because they refused to stop. If you can endure, you can win — slowly, quietly, and completely.
