Growth requires admitting we do not know, asking questions others might mock, and trying things we may fail at publicly. The fear of looking foolish keeps many people frozen where they are, protecting an image at the cost of progress. Epictetus offers a blunt trade: accept being thought foolish, and you buy the freedom to learn. The beginner's mistakes are not shameful; they are the price of every skill worth having. Those who refuse to look stupid stay safe and stay the same, while those willing to look foolish keep improving, quietly, until one day the results speak for themselves.
