Great achievements look sudden from the outside, but they are built from small practices repeated over time. We want to start with the grand gesture, yet mastery begins with little things: a single page written, one honest conversation, a small promise kept. This quote urges us to practice deliberately in small matters, because the habits we build there carry over to greater ones. Do not wait for a big moment to begin. Begin with what is in front of you, and let the small practice prepare you for the large.
