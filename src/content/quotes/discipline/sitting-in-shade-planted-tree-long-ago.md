Shade is never an accident. Someone planted a tree years ago, watered it, and waited, long before anyone needed its shelter. The comfort we enjoy today often comes from work done by others in the past, just as the comfort of tomorrow will come from work we do today. Discipline is planting trees whose shade we may never sit in ourselves. It is the willingness to invest in a future we cannot yet see. Plant something today: a habit, a skill, a kindness. Years from now, someone will be grateful you did.
