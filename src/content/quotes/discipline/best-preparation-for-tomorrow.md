We often worry about tomorrow while wasting today, yet tomorrow is built entirely from what we do now. Brown's advice is refreshingly practical: instead of anxiously planning for a future we cannot control, give today's work our full effort and attention. Each day done well becomes a brick in the foundation of the future, and a series of good todays adds up to a good tomorrow. Preparation is not a separate activity from living; it is living well, one day at a time. Do your best today, and tomorrow will largely take care of itself.
