Strength is usually pictured as force, and gentleness as weakness, but this line turns both ideas upside down. Real strength does not need to shout, threaten, or crush; it is calm enough to be gentle, and that calm is exactly what makes it strong. Gentleness, in turn, is not softness but controlled power, the discipline to choose kindness when anger would be easier. A gentle person who cannot be moved is far stronger than a loud person who cannot control himself. True strength and true gentleness are the same thing seen from different sides.
