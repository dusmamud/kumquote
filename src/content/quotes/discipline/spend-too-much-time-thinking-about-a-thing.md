Thinking has its place, but it can become a hiding place. We plan, research, and rehearse until the planning itself feels like progress — yet nothing has actually moved. The mind loves this trap because thinking feels safe and doing feels risky. Lee's warning is blunt: thought without action is a delay dressed up as preparation. A good plan acted on today beats a perfect plan that never leaves the notebook. Discipline is not the absence of thought; it is knowing when thought has done its job and stepping forward anyway.
