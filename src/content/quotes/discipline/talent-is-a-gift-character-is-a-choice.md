Talent arrives unchosen: some people are born quick, strong, or gifted with an easy ear for music. Character is different, because nobody is born with it finished. It is built choice by choice, in daily decisions to be honest, to keep promises, to do hard things when no one is watching. That is why talent alone so often disappoints: without character, a gift is wasted on someone who cannot be trusted with it. Discipline is the tool that turns a gift into something lasting. You cannot choose your talent, but you can always choose the person you become.
