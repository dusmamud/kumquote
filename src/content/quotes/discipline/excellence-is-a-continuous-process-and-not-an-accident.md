Excellence is often treated as a gift or a lucky break, but this quote calls it a process: something built step by step through steady effort. Accidents happen once; excellence repeats. It comes from paying attention to details, correcting mistakes, and refusing to settle for good enough. The process is not glamorous, which is why so few people stay with it. But those who do discover that excellence is not a destination they arrive at; it is a way of working that becomes part of who they are.
