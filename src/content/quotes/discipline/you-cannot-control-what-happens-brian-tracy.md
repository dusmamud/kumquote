Tracy splits life into two territories: events, which you mostly cannot control, and attitude, which you always can. The discipline lies in refusing to confuse them — in spending your energy on the one territory that answers to you, instead of raging at the one that does not. A bad event with a steady attitude becomes a lesson; the same event with a bitter attitude becomes a wound that never heals. Guard your attitude like the one piece of ground you truly own, and discipline will follow from it.
