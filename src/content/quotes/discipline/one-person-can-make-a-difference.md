It is easy to believe that change belongs to crowds, governments, or heroes — anyone but us. Kennedy answers with two short claims. First, one person can make a difference; history is full of single individuals who shifted the course of events. Second, everyone should try, because the attempt itself matters even when the outcome is small. We do not need permission or power to begin; we need only the willingness to act where we stand. Try, and the difference you make may be larger than you think.
