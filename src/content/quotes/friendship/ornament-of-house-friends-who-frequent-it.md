A house can be filled with fine furniture and still feel empty, because what makes a home is not decoration but the people who come through its door. Friends turn rooms into places of laughter, long conversation and shared meals, and their frequent presence is the true ornament. Expensive things impress visitors for a moment, but friends make a house feel alive every day. The quote reminds us to invest less in how a home looks and more in who gathers there. Open the door often, keep the table ready, and let friendship furnish the house.
