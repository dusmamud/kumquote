Friends can disappoint, move away, or change, but a book waits patiently on the shelf, always ready when you return to it. It never judges your bad days, never betrays a confidence, and never asks for anything in return. A book offers companionship in loneliness, wisdom in confusion, and adventure when life feels small — and it does all this faithfully, every time you open it. This is not a dismissal of human friendship but a celebration of reading: books are the friends that stay.
