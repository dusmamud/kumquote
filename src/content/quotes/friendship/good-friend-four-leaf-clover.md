Four-leaf clovers are rare by nature — one in thousands — and that is exactly the point. Good friends are not found on every corner. Most people pass through our lives briefly; only a few stay, understand us, and show up when it matters. Finding one is luck, but keeping one is work: honesty, patience, forgiveness, and time. If you have a friend like that, you are already fortunate in a way money cannot buy. Hold them close, thank them often, and never forget how rare a four-leaf clover really is.
