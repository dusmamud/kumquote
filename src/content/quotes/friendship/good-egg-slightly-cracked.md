Real friendship is not about pretending to be perfect. Everyone is slightly cracked — a little odd, a little flawed, carrying a few dents from life. A true friend sees all of that clearly and likes us anyway. There is no audition, no need to hide the strange parts. That acceptance is a relief in a world that constantly asks us to perform. It is also what makes the friendship honest: it is built on the real us, not a polished version. The friends who know our cracks and stay are the ones worth keeping close.
