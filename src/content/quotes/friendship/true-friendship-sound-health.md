We notice our health most when it is gone, and friendship works the same way. While good friends are nearby, their presence feels ordinary — a message here, a shared joke there, someone to call. Only when distance or disagreement takes them away do we feel the size of the gap they leave. That is why gratitude matters now, not later. Tell your friends what they mean while they can still hear it. Call, show up, make the time. The value of friendship is obvious in hindsight; the wise recognize it before it is lost.
