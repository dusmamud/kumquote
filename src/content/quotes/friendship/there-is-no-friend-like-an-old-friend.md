New friends bring excitement, but old friends carry something irreplaceable: shared history. They were there for the mornings of our lives, the beginnings, the mistakes, the first attempts. They remember who we were before we became who we are. That shared past creates a comfort no new friendship can quickly match. This quote celebrates the friends who have walked with us longest. They are not merely companions; they are witnesses to our lives, and their presence reminds us of where we came from.
