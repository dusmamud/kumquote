True friendship is rare because it asks for honesty, loyalty, and time, yet it gives back more than it costs. Thomas Aquinas places it above wealth, pleasure, and fame, because none of those can listen to you in sorrow or celebrate you without envy. Such friendship cannot be rushed or manufactured; it grows through shared trials and faithful care. If you have it, protect it above all else, for there is truly nothing on earth more to be prized. Treasure such friends, and be one yourself.
