Most of us work hard to be liked, showing only our best side and hiding the rest. Real friendship begins where that performance ends. A true friend has seen your flaws, your mistakes, and your fears, and chooses to stay anyway. That kind of love is not blind; it sees clearly and still stays. It teaches us that we do not have to be perfect to be worthy of love. When someone knows us fully and loves us still, we feel safe enough to be ourselves, and that safety is one of the greatest gifts any friendship can give.
