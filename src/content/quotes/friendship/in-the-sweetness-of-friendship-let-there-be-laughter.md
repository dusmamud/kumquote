Kahlil Gibran reminds us that friendship is not only a duty in hard times — it is also a joy to be shared. Friends who only appear in crisis are valuable, but a friendship without laughter slowly becomes an obligation rather than a delight. Sharing pleasures, laughing at nothing, celebrating small wins together: these light moments are the sweetness that keeps a bond alive between the storms. A friendship fed only on seriousness grows heavy; one seasoned with laughter grows strong. Let joy be part of the friendship, not a reward for surviving it.
