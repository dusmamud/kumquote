Friendship is easy when it costs nothing: a shared laugh, a pleasant afternoon, a message at a convenient hour. Its truth shows when it costs something, when a friend sets aside comfort, plans, or sleep to be beside you. Wein's test is simple and honest. Anyone can enjoy your good days; a true friend chooses your hard ones. Presence in inconvenient moments is the clearest proof of loyalty. If someone shows up when they would rather be anywhere else, keep them close. That is what friendship truly means.
