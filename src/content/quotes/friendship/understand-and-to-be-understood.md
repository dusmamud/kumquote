Being understood is one of the deepest human needs. We spend much of our lives explaining ourselves — softening our words, hiding parts of our story, and hoping to be seen clearly. A true friend removes that labour. They understand what we mean even when our words are clumsy, and they do not need a performance to accept us. Just as valuable is the other direction: to understand a friend fully, to know their history and their fears, is a gift we give in return.
