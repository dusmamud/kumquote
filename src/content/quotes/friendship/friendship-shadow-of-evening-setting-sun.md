In the morning of life, friendships are easy and plentiful, but they are rarely tested. As the day goes on and the sun lowers, what remains is what truly matters: the friends who stay when youth, energy and convenience fade. Like an evening shadow, true friendship grows longer and stronger with time rather than shrinking. It deepens through shared years, losses and quiet loyalty. The quote asks us to value the friendships that last into the evening, because they are the ones that will walk with us until the day is done.
