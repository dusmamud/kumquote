Happiness is easy to share; everyone wants to be near joy. Trouble is the real test. When life falls apart, the friends who stay, who call, who help, and who simply sit with you in the dark, are showing love in its truest form. Words of affection cost nothing, but presence in hard times costs time, comfort, and effort. Euripides knew that love is proven by action under pressure. Remember this both ways: be the friend who shows up in trouble, and value the friends who showed up for you.
