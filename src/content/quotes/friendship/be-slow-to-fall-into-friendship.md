Real friendship deserves care in choosing, because a friend is someone you trust with your thoughts, your time, and your weaknesses. Rushing into friendship hands that trust to people who have not yet earned it, and the disappointment that follows is often blamed on friendship itself rather than on the haste. But once you have chosen well, the duty reverses: stay firm and constant through disagreements, distance, and hard seasons. A friendship that survives testing is worth more than a dozen shallow ones.
