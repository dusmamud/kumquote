Life has grey days we all know: the long winter, the slow week, the season when nothing seems to grow. Hay calls friends the sunshine of life, and the image is exact. Friends do not remove the winter, but they warm it. A visit, a call, a shared laugh, these are small suns that break through the clouds and remind us warmth still exists. Like sunshine, friendship asks nothing in return; it simply shines, freely and generously. Keep your friends close, and even the coldest season will have its bright hours. A life with friends is never entirely without light.
