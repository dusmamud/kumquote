Good times hide the truth about friendship, because fair weather friends cost nothing to keep. It is when fortune shifts, when money, health, or success suddenly change, that reliability is truly tested. Some friends disappear at the first sign of trouble, while others draw closer, proving that their loyalty was never about what you could give them. Hardship is an honest judge of friendship. It strips away flattery and convenience and shows who is real. Do not fear the test; welcome it, for it leaves you with friends you can truly trust.
