Numbers mean little in friendship; loyalty means everything. Euripides contrasts one loyal friend with ten thousand relatives to show that a crowd of fair-weather connections cannot match a single person who stands by you when it costs something. Relatives may share blood, but loyalty is proven in hard moments, not inherited. A loyal friend tells the truth, keeps confidence and returns when others drift away. Such a friend is rare, and rarity is part of the value. The quote invites gratitude for the loyal few rather than envy of those with larger circles.
