Austen's heroine speaks for a whole philosophy of friendship: love people wholly or not at all. Half-hearted friendship — the polite kind that keeps its distance — is not in her nature, and it should not be in ours. Real friends give everything they have: time, honesty, help, and patience, without keeping a careful account. The sentence is a promise and a standard at once. If we would do anything for our friends, then we should also expect — and offer — nothing less than wholehearted devotion.
