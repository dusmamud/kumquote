Cunningham catches the difference between politeness and friendship in one small habit: waiting for the answer. Most people ask "how are you?" as a greeting, already walking away before the reply. A real friend asks and then stays — they actually want to know, and they make room for the honest answer, even when it is not a cheerful one. That waiting is the whole gift: attention, patience, and the message that your feelings matter enough to pause for. Be that rare person for your friends, and notice who is that rare person for you.
