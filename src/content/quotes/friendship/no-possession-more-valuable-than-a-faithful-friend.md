Wealth can be lost, fame can fade, and possessions wear out, but a good and faithful friend holds a value no market can set. Such a friend offers honest counsel when you are wrong, steady support when you are weak, and quiet joy when you succeed — all without asking for payment. In a world that prices everything, friendship is the one treasure that grows more valuable the longer you keep it. If you count your possessions, count your true friends first, for they are the only ones that make all the rest worth having.
