Family is given to us by birth, but friendship is chosen freely, and that choice gives it a special meaning. Jess C. Scott reminds us that friends can become the people we turn to in joy and sorrow, the ones who know our history and accept our present. Chosen family offers belonging without obligation and love without conditions of blood. Honour the friends who feel like family by showing up, listening deeply, and treating their trust as precious. Family is not only who you are born to; it is also who you choose to stand beside.
