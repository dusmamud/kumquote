Sincerity is the test of friendship. With most people we filter our words, hide our doubts, and present a careful version of ourselves. But with a true friend, we can speak plainly, admit what we really think, and show what we really feel, without fear of judgment or rejection. Emerson's definition is simple because the quality it names is rare: the freedom to be completely honest with another person. A friend who allows us that honesty gives us something priceless, a place where we never have to pretend. That is what makes a friend a friend.
