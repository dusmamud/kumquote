We often measure richness by quantity: more friends, more followers, more possessions. Leo Buscaglia offers a gentler measure. One true friend can bring as much joy, comfort, and meaning as a whole garden brings beauty. A single rose, fully appreciated, is enough. Likewise, one loyal friend who listens, understands, and stays can make life feel full. You do not need a large circle to be rich in friendship; you need depth, honesty, and presence. Treasure the one friend who truly knows you. In a world that prizes more, learning to value one is a quiet form of wisdom.
