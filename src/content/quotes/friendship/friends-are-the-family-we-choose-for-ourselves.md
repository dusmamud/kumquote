Family is given to us by birth, but friendship is chosen, and that choice makes it special. We choose friends because they understand us, accept us, and make life richer. Over time, chosen friends often become as close as blood relations, sometimes closer, because the bond is built on mutual choice rather than obligation. This quote celebrates that freedom: we get to build our own family from the people we love. The family we choose reminds us that belonging is not only inherited; it can also be created.
