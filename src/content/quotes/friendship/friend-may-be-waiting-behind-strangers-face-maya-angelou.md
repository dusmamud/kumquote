Angelou turns every new meeting into a possibility. The stranger beside you is not only unknown — they may be a friend you have not met yet, carrying the humour, loyalty, or understanding you have been hoping for. Most friendships begin exactly this way, with two strangers who took a small chance on each other. The quote asks for openness rather than suspicion: greet people as though a friend might be hiding behind their face, because sometimes one is. Keep the door open, and let tomorrow's friendship walk in.
