Sorrow manages on its own — it arrives whether we want it or not. But joy is different: it reaches its full value only when it is shared. A victory celebrated alone feels half-finished, while the same joy divided with a friend doubles in warmth. That is the quiet purpose of friendship. Friends do not only comfort us in grief; they complete our happiness by sharing it. Keep your friends close in good times, not only in bad ones. Joy divided is joy multiplied, and a friend is the person who makes happiness whole.
