Friendships can feel accidental, as if they happened to us, but O. Henry insists otherwise. Every lasting friendship is built from choices: the decision to reach out, to listen, to show up after a disagreement, to remember a birthday, to make time when life is busy. None of that happens by chance. The quote invites us to treat friendship as something we actively create rather than something we passively receive. If a friendship matters, it deserves the same care as anything else we value, because neglect is a choice, and it ends what chance alone could never sustain.
