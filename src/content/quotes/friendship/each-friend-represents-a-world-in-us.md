Each friend opens a door to a part of ourselves we did not know existed. One friend brings out our humour, another our courage, another our quiet thoughtfulness. These worlds live inside us all along, waiting for the right person to arrive and wake them. That is why losing a friend can feel like losing a part of ourselves, and why new friendships can feel like becoming someone new. Treasure the friends who arrived and showed you who you could be. Each one gave you a world, and your life is larger because they came.
