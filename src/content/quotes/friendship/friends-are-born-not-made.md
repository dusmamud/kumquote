Henry Adams captures the strange instant recognition of true friendship. Some people enter our lives and feel familiar from the first conversation, as if the friendship was always waiting to happen. It cannot be manufactured by effort or scheduling; it arrives when two natures simply fit. This is why forced networking so rarely produces real friends, while a chance meeting can change a life. We do not build such bonds from nothing — we discover them, already formed, in the moment we meet the right person.
