We often measure our social lives by numbers, counting friends the way we count followers. But one true friend is already a great deal, and two are more than most people ever find. Three close friends is a rare gift, hardly possible in a single lifetime. Depth matters more than quantity: a friend who knows you fully and stays anyway is worth a hundred acquaintances. Do not chase a crowd. Treasure the one or two who truly know you, give them your time and honesty, and you will be richer than any number could make you.
