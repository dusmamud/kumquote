We plan our lives around careers, houses, and ambitions, yet the ingredient that gives life its flavour is often the simplest: friends. They turn ordinary days into memories, lighten heavy burdens, and celebrate our joys as if they were their own. Without friends, even the finest achievements taste plain. With them, even simple meals and quiet evenings become rich. Invest in friendship the way you invest in anything precious. It is the most important ingredient in the recipe of life, and no life is truly complete without it.
