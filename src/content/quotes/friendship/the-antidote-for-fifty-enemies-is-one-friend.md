Fifty enemies can make life feel small and fearful, yet a single true friend changes the balance entirely. One friend who believes in you, tells you the truth, and stands beside you is worth more than a crowd of opponents. Enemies attack from the outside, but a friend strengthens you from the inside, reminding you of your worth when others try to take it away. That is why friendship has always been counted among life's greatest defences. Choose your friends well, keep them close, and remember that one loyal friend is an antidote to a world of opposition.
