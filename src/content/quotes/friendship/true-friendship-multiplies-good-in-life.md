Friendship has a quiet arithmetic all its own: joy shared becomes larger, and sorrow shared becomes smaller. A true friend does not merely stand beside you in good times; they double your laughter and halve your burdens simply by being present. That is why friendship is worth more than wealth or status, because no achievement tastes as sweet when celebrated alone. Invest in people who multiply your happiness and divide your grief. Their presence turns ordinary days into celebrations and hard days into something you can survive together.
