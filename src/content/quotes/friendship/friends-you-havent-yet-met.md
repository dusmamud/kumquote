Strangers can feel like a wall — unknown, possibly unfriendly, best avoided. Yeats invites us to see them differently: not as strangers, but as friends we simply have not met yet. That small shift changes everything. It turns a crowded room from a threat into an opportunity, and a new city from a lonely place into a field of possible friendships. Most of our closest friends were once strangers, after all. The next one might be sitting beside us right now. All it takes is a hello.
