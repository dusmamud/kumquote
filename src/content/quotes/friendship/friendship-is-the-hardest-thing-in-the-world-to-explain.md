Friendship is hard to explain because it is not a transaction or a contract. It is built slowly, through shared time, honest conversations, and showing up when it matters. You cannot learn it from a book or buy it with money. It grows in the quiet moments: the call returned, the promise kept, the presence offered without being asked. This quote honors friendship as one of life's most mysterious gifts. It cannot be defined neatly, but everyone knows it when they have it, and everyone feels its absence when they do not.
