True friendship is so good that it can feel almost unbelievable — that another person would know you fully and still choose to stand beside you. Like the hope of the soul's immortality, it asks for a kind of faith: trust in something you cannot prove but deeply feel. A real friend's loyalty, honesty, and affection seem too generous to be real, and yet there they are, steady through the years. Perhaps that is why friendship has always been compared to the sacred things in life.
