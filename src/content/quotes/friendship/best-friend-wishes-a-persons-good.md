Aristotle distinguished three kinds of friendship: those based on usefulness, those based on pleasure, and those based on goodness itself. Only the last is true friendship, and this line explains why. A friend of usefulness wishes us well because it benefits them; a friend of pleasure wishes us well because we entertain them. But the best friend wishes our good for our own sake — our success is the goal, not a means to something else. That selflessness is the test of real friendship.
