Friendship is easy to measure in daylight, when everyone is available and cheerful. Dietrich measures it at four in the morning, when the call means trouble and the answer costs sleep. The friends who matter are the ones who pick up — not the ones who promise, but the ones who answer. A 4 a.m. friend has already decided, long before the phone rings, that your emergency is their business. Count those friends, not the crowd. They are the whole list that matters.
