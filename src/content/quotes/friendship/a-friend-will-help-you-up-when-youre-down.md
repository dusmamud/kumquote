A real friend does not only celebrate with us; they help when we fall. Sometimes help means practical action, pulling us back on our feet. Sometimes it means simply staying beside us and listening, without fixing anything. Both matter. This quote, from Winnie-the-Pooh, captures the gentle loyalty of true friendship. It does not demand heroics, only presence. The friend who stays when we are down, whether by lifting us or lying beside us, proves what friendship really is: not words, but quiet, steady company.
