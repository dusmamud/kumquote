Aristotle placed friendship above wealth, power, and pleasure, arguing that even a life filled with every other good would feel empty without friends. He was right: riches are meaningless without someone to share them with, and achievements feel hollow when there is no one to celebrate them. Friends give life its flavour, turning ordinary days into memories and hard days into survivable ones. No amount of success can replace the warmth of being known and loved. If we had to choose between all the goods of the world and our friends, the friends would win every time.
