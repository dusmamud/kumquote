Larson points out the harder half of friendship, the one nobody talks about. Overlooking failures is the easy part — good friends forgive mistakes and stay through bad patches. Tolerating success is harder: when a friend rises, earns praise, or grows rich, envy can quietly poison even a loyal heart. A true friend celebrates your wins without keeping score, and forgives your falls without keeping a ledger. If you want to test a friendship, look at both moments — the failure and the triumph. The friend who stands beside you in both is the true one. Be that friend too.
