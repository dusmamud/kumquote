Every creature builds something it needs to survive: the bird builds a nest, the spider spins a web, and human beings build friendship. This short line places friendship among the basic necessities of life, not a luxury to enjoy only when convenient. A nest shelters, a web feeds, and friendship sustains us in the same essential way — giving us safety, belonging, and a place to return to. Without it, life is exposed and thin, no matter how much else we have.
