Wine does not feed us, yet it makes a meal a celebration; friendship works the same way in a life. Our days can be full of duty and necessity, but friends add the warmth, flavour, and sparkle that turn mere living into something worth savouring. Like good wine, friendship also improves with time, deepening as years pass and shared memories accumulate. The comparison is simple and generous: life without friends would still function, but it would lose its richness. Friendship is not an extra to be fitted in when convenient; it is what makes the whole of life taste better.
