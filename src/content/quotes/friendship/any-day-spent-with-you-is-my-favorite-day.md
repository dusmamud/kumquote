Pooh's logic is simple and flawless: if every day with a friend is the favourite, then today — spent with that friend — must be the new favourite. The line captures what friendship does to ordinary time. A dull day becomes a good one simply because of who shares it. We often wait for special occasions to feel joy, but friendship turns unremarkable days into favourites without any occasion at all. The secret is presence, not planning. Today is not special because of what happens; it is special because of who is there.
