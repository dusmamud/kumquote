The best friend is not the one who agrees with everything you say, but the one who makes you want to be better. Their belief in you raises your standards, their honesty keeps you grounded, and their encouragement gives you courage to grow. You become kinder, braver, and more capable in their company than you are alone. Ford's definition is a useful test: look at who you become around your friends. The best ones bring out your best, and the truest way to honour them is to do the same for them.
