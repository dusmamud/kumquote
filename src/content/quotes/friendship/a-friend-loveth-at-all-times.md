A true friend does not love only in fair weather. Their love stays constant through good days and bad, through success and trouble alike. That is what separates a real friend from a fair-weather companion: the friend remains when times turn hard. Like family born to stand with us in adversity, a true friend shows up when we need them most. You can measure a friendship not by laughter shared in easy times, but by loyalty shown in difficult ones. A friend who loves at all times is one of life's rarest and greatest gifts.
