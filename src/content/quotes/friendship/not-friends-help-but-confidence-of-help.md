Help itself matters, but the confidence that help is there matters more. Knowing a friend will stand by you changes how you face trouble, even before any help arrives. That quiet certainty steadies the nerves, sharpens courage, and makes hard things feel possible. A friend who is merely willing to help is good; a friend whose help you can count on without asking is better still. Much of friendship's value is this invisible safety net. It does not need to be used to do its work. Simply knowing it is there is often help enough.
