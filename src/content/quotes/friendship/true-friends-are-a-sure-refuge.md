Prosperity attracts many companions, but hardship reveals who your true friends are. When money runs out or trouble arrives, fair-weather friends quietly disappear, while real friends become a refuge — a place of safety, comfort, and honest help. True friendship is tested not in good times but in bad ones, and it proves itself by staying. A friend who stands beside you in misfortune offers something no wealth can buy: knowing you are not alone. Guard such friends carefully, be the same refuge for them, and you will never be poor in the way that truly matters.
