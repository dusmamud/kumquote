Good friends do not need constant contact to stay close. Like stars, they are not always visible, but they are always there. Distance, busy lives, and passing years do not dim a true friendship; it waits quietly until it is needed. This quote is a comfort for anyone separated from old friends. The bond does not depend on daily messages or frequent visits. It depends on trust built over time. Knowing a friend is there, even unseen, is enough to make the dark feel less lonely.
