Pleasure shared is pleasure doubled. A meal tastes better with a friend across the table, a journey feels richer with a companion beside you, and even ordinary days gain colour when someone is there to share them. Without friends, the finest pursuits slowly lose their flavour, because joy needs a witness. That is why friendship is not a luxury added to a good life; it is one of the things that makes life good in the first place. Guard your friendships as you would guard any treasure, for they are the source of life's greatest pleasures.
