A faithful friend is protection no money can buy. When trouble comes, a loyal friend stands beside you, defends your name when you are absent, and tells you the truth when you need to hear it. That kind of friendship is rare, which is why finding one is like finding treasure: valuable, worth guarding, and impossible to replace. Treasures are not used up by sharing them; a faithful friendship only grows stronger the more it is relied upon. If you have found such a friend, hold on tightly, and be the same kind of treasure for them.
