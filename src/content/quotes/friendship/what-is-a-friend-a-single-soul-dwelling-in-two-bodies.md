A true friend feels less like another person and more like a second self. They know our thoughts before we speak them, share our values, and understand our choices without long explanations. This quote captures the rare closeness of deep friendship: two people, one soul. It is not about agreeing on everything, but about sharing the same inner life. Such friendship is rare because it requires honesty, time, and mutual trust. When we find it, we discover that we are no longer alone inside our own heads.
