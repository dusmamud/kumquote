We usually praise friendship for its visible kindness — the helping hand, the warm smile, the pleasure of company — but Emerson looks deeper. The true glory of friendship is quieter and more powerful: the moment we realize another person believes in us. Being trusted with someone's friendship is a vote of confidence that can change how we see ourselves. It lifts us, steadies us, and calls out our better nature. A friend's belief becomes a kind of inspiration, a spiritual gift that no material help can match. That trust is the heart of friendship.
