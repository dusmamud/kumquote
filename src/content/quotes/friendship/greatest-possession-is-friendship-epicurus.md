Epicurus placed friendship above wealth, fame, and every other pleasure, because friends are the one good that protects us in every season of life. Money can vanish, health can falter, and success can turn hollow, but a friend stays beside us through each of those losses. Friendship is not a luxury added to a good life; it is the foundation that makes the rest of life bearable and joyful. That is why the wisest investment is not in things but in people — in showing up, listening well, and keeping the bonds that carry us when nothing else can.
