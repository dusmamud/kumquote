True friendship is the rare place where we can drop the performance. With old friends, we need not be clever, successful, or impressive; we can be silly, honest, and imperfect, knowing we are loved anyway. That freedom is precious, because so much of life demands that we present a polished version of ourselves. Old friends have seen our mistakes and stayed, which makes their acceptance the most believable kind. Emerson's line celebrates this gift: the blessing is not just companionship, but the comfort of being fully known and still fully welcome.
