There are seasons when our inner fire simply goes out — exhaustion, grief, disappointment, or just the slow grind of ordinary life. In those moments, advice rarely helps. What helps is a person: someone whose energy, kindness, or belief in us reignites something we thought was gone. A friend's encouragement can do in minutes what months of effort could not. That is why Schweitzer says we should be thankful for those people. They do not just cheer us up; they relight us. And someday, we will get to do the same for someone else.
