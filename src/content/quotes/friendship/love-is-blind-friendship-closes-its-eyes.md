Love can overlook almost anything, but friendship sees clearly and chooses to stay anyway. A friend knows your flaws, your failures, and your foolish moments, yet refuses to let them define you. That gentle blindness is not ignorance; it is loyalty, the decision to value the whole person rather than tally their mistakes. Real friendship asks for honesty without cruelty and acceptance without pretending. When someone closes their eyes to your faults and opens their arms to your true self, they offer one of life's rarest gifts: to be fully known and still loved.
