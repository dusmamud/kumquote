True friendship is rare because it asks for something deeper than company or convenience. When two people share values, humour, and trust so completely that they feel like one soul in two bodies, the friendship becomes a second home. Such friends understand us without long explanations, and their presence makes us more ourselves, not less. This kind of friendship cannot be rushed or bought; it grows slowly, through shared days and honest conversations. If you find it, guard it well. A friend who is truly one soul with you is among life's greatest gifts.
