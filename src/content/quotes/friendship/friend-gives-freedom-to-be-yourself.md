Many relationships ask you to perform: to be impressive, agreeable, or different from who you are. A true friend asks none of that. In their company you can drop the act, speak honestly, admit weakness, and be exactly yourself without fear of judgement. That freedom is precious, because so much of life is spent managing how others see us. Morrison's definition captures what makes friendship feel like rest: it is the one place where you are free. Give that freedom to your friends, and you will never be without one.
