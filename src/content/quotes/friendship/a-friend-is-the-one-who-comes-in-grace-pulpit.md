Everyone is present when things go well. The test comes when the world goes out: the job is lost, the diagnosis arrives, the marriage ends, the money runs out. Pulpit's image is simple and exact. A friend is the one who comes in when the whole world has gone out, walking toward us while everyone else walks away. That kind of friend cannot be bought or impressed; they can only be earned, by being that kind of person ourselves. When the world goes out for someone you love, go in. It is the oldest definition of friendship, and the truest.
