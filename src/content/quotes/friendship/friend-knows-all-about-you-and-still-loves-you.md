Many relationships depend on presenting only our best side. True friendship is different: it survives honesty. Elbert Hubbard describes a friend as someone who has seen your flaws, mistakes, and fears, and chooses to stay. That acceptance is rare and precious, because it frees you from pretending. Being that kind of friend requires the same generosity: seeing someone fully and loving them anyway. Do not hide your imperfections from true friends. Let them know you completely, and let their acceptance remind you that you are worthy of love.
