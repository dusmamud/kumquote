Self-belief is hard to build alone. On bad days, our own voice turns doubtful, and every failure feels like proof that we are not enough. A good friend changes that conversation. They remind us of what we have done, what we can do, and who we really are — until we can believe it again ourselves. That is the quiet superpower of friendship: it lends us faith when ours runs out. We all need someone like that. And the best way to keep such a friend is to be one for someone else.
