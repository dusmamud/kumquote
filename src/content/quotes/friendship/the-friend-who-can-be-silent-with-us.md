Nouwen describes the rarest kind of friend: the one who does not try to fix you. In despair or grief, most people rush to offer advice or cheerful distractions, because your pain makes them uncomfortable. The true friend stays, says little, and simply shares the silence. That presence is more healing than any solution, because it tells you that you are not alone in the dark. You do not need to be cured to be loved. Friendship at its deepest is not about answers; it is about sitting beside someone in their powerlessness and not looking away.
