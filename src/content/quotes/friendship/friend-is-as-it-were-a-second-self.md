A true friend is not someone you merely enjoy; they are a second self. You trust them with your thoughts as you trust your own mind, and you care for their good as you care for your own. That is why losing a friend can feel like losing part of yourself, and why a friend's joy feels like your own. Such closeness does not happen quickly. It grows from honesty, loyalty, and years of shared experience. When you find a person you can call a second self, you have found something rare, and the bond deserves to be guarded.
