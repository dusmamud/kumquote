Ali admits that friendship resists definition. It is not taught in school, measured in exams, or explained in a formula — yet it is one of the most important things we ever learn. We understand it by living it: by standing beside someone, forgiving, laughing, and showing up when it counts. If we miss its meaning, Ali warns, we have missed something essential about life itself. The lesson is simple: friendship is learned in the heart, not in the classroom, and it is worth learning well.
