A good book is a friend that never tires of you. It waits quietly on the shelf, always ready when you need it, and it never judges or interrupts. Books give wise counsel drawn from the best minds of every age, and they teach with endless patience, repeating a lesson as often as you need it. Unlike people, they are always available, in the middle of the night or on a lonely day. If you want friends who never leave and never fail you, fill your life with books. They are the quietest, wisest, and most faithful friends of all.
