Voltaire compares friendship to a marriage of the soul — a deep, chosen bond between two inner lives. But he adds a warning that most romantic quotes leave out: this marriage can end in divorce. Friendships are not automatic or permanent; they need the same care as any marriage — honesty, attention, forgiveness, and time together. Neglect them, betray them, or take them for granted, and they can break. The warning is also an encouragement: a friendship we tend with the seriousness of a marriage can last a lifetime. Treat it as sacred, and it stays sacred.
