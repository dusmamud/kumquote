The surest way to defeat an enemy is not to destroy the person but to destroy the enmity — by turning them into a friend. Hatred keeps conflict alive, while friendship ends it completely. When you listen, understand, and show kindness to someone who opposed you, the opposition itself dissolves. There is no enemy left to fight, because the person who was once against you now stands beside you. This is a wiser victory than any battle. Making a friend of an enemy does not mean surrender; it means winning the only victory that truly lasts.
