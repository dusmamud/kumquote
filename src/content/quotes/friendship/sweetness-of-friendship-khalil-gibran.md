Gibran reminds us that friendship is not only loyalty in hard times — it is also delight in good ones. A friendship without laughter is incomplete, like a meal without salt: nourishing, perhaps, but missing its flavour. Sharing pleasures — a joke, a walk, a good meal, a small victory — is what turns acquaintance into friendship and keeps it warm over the years. So laugh with your friends on purpose, and share the good things freely. A friendship that celebrates together will also stand together when the hard days come.
