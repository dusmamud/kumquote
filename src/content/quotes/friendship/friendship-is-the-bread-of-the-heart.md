Bread is simple, daily, and necessary, and friendship plays the same role for the heart. We live on steady nourishment, and a friend provides it in small portions: a listening ear, an honest word, a shared laugh, a presence that asks for nothing. Without it, the heart grows hungry in a way success cannot feed. Friendship does not need to be dramatic to be essential. Like bread on the table, it is easy to take for granted until it is missing. So value friends daily, not only in crisis, and feed others with the same care.
