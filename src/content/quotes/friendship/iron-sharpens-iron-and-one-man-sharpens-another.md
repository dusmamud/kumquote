Good friends make each other better, just as iron sharpens iron. Honest conversation, gentle challenge, and shared effort polish our character the way a blade is honed on stone. A friend who only flatters you leaves you dull; a friend who speaks truthfully, even when it stings, helps you grow sharper and stronger. Choose friends who push you to think clearly, act bravely, and live well. And be that kind of friend in return. When two people sharpen each other with honesty and care, both come out stronger than they went in.
