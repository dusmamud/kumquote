Nobody gets through life alone, however strong or talented they are. The honest truth is that we all get by — not by sheer willpower, but with a little help from our friends. A friend who listens, a friend who shows up, a friend who lends a hand at the right moment: these small helps add up to a life that works. Admitting we need help is not weakness; it is wisdom. And offering help is one of the simplest ways to be a good friend. We get by together, a little help at a time.
