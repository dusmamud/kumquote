Helping someone else is usually described as selfless, but Emerson noticed a quieter truth: it helps the helper too. When we sincerely try to lift another person, we practise patience, we see the world through their eyes, and we discover strengths in ourselves we had not used. The act of giving expands the giver. This is not selfishness disguised as kindness; it is the natural design of human life. We are built to grow through service, and the growth is a compensation — a gift that arrives alongside the giving.
