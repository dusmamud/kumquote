Friendship rarely begins with a single dramatic moment. It grows the way a vessel fills — drop by drop, kindness by kindness, conversation by conversation. We cannot point to the exact day a stranger became a friend, because the change happens quietly, below the surface. Then one day, almost without noticing, we realise the vessel is full: this person matters to us, and we matter to them. That is why friendship cannot be rushed or forced. It is built from small, repeated acts of attention that seem unimportant at the time.
