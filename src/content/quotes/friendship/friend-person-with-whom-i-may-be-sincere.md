Real friendship is measured by how honestly you can speak. With most people we edit ourselves, soften opinions, and hide doubts, but a true friend creates a space where unguarded thoughts are safe. Emerson points to sincerity as the test: if you must rehearse every sentence, the bond is still shallow. Thinking aloud together builds trust faster than any compliment, because it shows you believe the other person will not judge you. Such friends are rare, and they deserve care. Guard the relationships where you can be fully yourself, since they keep you honest and sane.
