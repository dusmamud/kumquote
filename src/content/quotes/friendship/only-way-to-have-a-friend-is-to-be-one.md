Friendship cannot be demanded or bought; it grows from giving. Ralph Waldo Emerson reminds us that if you want loyal, caring friends, you must first practise loyalty and care yourself. Listen without judging, keep confidences, show up when it matters, and celebrate others without envy. People are drawn to those who make them feel seen and valued. Waiting for others to prove their friendship first keeps you lonely, while taking the first step often opens a door. Be the friend you wish to have, consistently and without keeping score, and genuine friendship will usually find its way back to you.
