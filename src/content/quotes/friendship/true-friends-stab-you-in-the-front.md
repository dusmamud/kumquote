Wilde's joke is a test of honesty. A real friend tells you the hard truth to your face, even when it stings, instead of whispering it behind your back. Enemies attack from behind because they want to hurt you without risk; friends confront you directly because they want to help you, and they accept the risk of your anger. The sting of an honest friend is a kindness in disguise. It means they respect you enough to be truthful. Choose the friends who stab you in the front — they are the only ones you can trust.
