Nature makes many wonders — mountains, oceans, forests — but its finest work may be a true friend. A friend is not manufactured or bought; a friend grows slowly, through shared time, honest talk, and loyalty tested over years, the way a tree grows ring by ring. No amount of planning can force a real friendship into being; it happens naturally when two people meet and choose each other, again and again. That is why it feels like a masterpiece rather than a product.
