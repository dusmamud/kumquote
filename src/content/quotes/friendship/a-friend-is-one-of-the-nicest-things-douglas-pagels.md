We treat friendship as something we receive, a gift from other people. Pagels turns it around: a friend is one of the best things you can be. Being a friend is itself a gift, to the other person and to ourselves. The listener, the helper, the one who shows up, these are the heart of friendship. The strange thing is that the more we give as friends, the more friendship we find. People are drawn to those who are generous with their presence. To have a friend, be one first. It is the simplest trade, and the most rewarding.
