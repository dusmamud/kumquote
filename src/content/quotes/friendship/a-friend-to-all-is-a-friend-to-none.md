True friendship requires depth, time, and loyalty — things that cannot be spread thin across everyone. If you try to be equally close to all people, you will be truly close to none. Real friendship asks for commitment: showing up, keeping confidences, and choosing someone even when it is inconvenient. That is not unkindness to others; it is honesty about what friendship means. Be kind to everyone, but reserve the name of friend for those with whom you share genuine trust and care. A few true friends are worth far more than many shallow ones.
