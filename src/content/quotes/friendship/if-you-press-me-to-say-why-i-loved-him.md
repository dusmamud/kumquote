Montaigne tried to explain his friendship with Étienne de La Boétie and found that no list of qualities could capture it. It was not about usefulness or shared interests, but about two people recognising each other completely. True friendship cannot be reduced to reasons, because its cause is the meeting of two selves, not a set of advantages. That mystery is part of its beauty. We do not choose our closest friends the way we choose tools. We find them, recognise something familiar in them, and the bond forms before we can explain why.
