We can claim any virtues we like, but the world judges us by our companions. The company we keep is visible in a way our private intentions are not, and people reasonably assume that we share the values of those we choose to spend time with. This is not unfair; it is how trust works. We choose our friends freely, so our choices reveal what we actually value, whatever we say. A person surrounded by honest, kind, and ambitious people is assumed to share those qualities.
