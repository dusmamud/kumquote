We work hard to acquire money, status, and possessions, yet the greatest blessing of all, a true friend, is the one we pursue least deliberately. Friendships often happen by accident, and we take them for granted until they fade. La Rochefoucauld's irony is a warning: the most valuable thing in life deserves the most care, not the least. Invest in your friendships the way you invest in your career. Call, listen, show up, and give. A true friend is a blessing, and blessings that are cared for last a lifetime.
