Montaigne is describing his friendship with Étienne de La Boétie, and he refuses to explain it with reasons like usefulness, pleasure, or shared interests. Those things describe many relationships, but they cannot capture this one. The bond simply was, because two souls recognised each other. True friendship often feels like this: no single quality explains it, and no checklist can say why this person matters so much. It is not built from reasons but from recognition. When someone asks why you love your closest friend, the honest answer is often just that it was them, and it was you.
