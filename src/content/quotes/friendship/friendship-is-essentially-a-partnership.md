Friendship is often pictured as warm feeling, but this quote describes it as a partnership: two people actively working for each other's good. Partners share burdens, celebrate wins, and hold each other accountable. The feeling matters, but the doing matters more. A partnership means showing up consistently, not only when it is easy. This view of friendship is practical and demanding, and also reassuring: friendship is not a mystery we wait for, but a shared project we build together, day by day, through action.
