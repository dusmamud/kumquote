Evelyn pictures friendship as a golden thread running through the whole world, tying heart to heart. It is a quiet image, but a large one: friendship is not only a private comfort between two people, but the bond that holds humanity together. Across distance, difference, and time, friends keep us connected to something larger than ourselves. The thread is golden because it is precious, and it is a thread because it is humble — not a chain that binds, but a tie that gently holds. Wherever it runs, the world is a warmer place.
