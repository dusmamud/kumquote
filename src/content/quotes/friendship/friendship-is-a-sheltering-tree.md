A tree gives shelter without asking for anything in return, and that is what a good friend does. In the heat of trouble or the rain of disappointment, friendship offers shade, quiet and a place to rest until the storm passes. The shelter is not loud advice or quick solutions; it is simply presence, steady and unhurried. Like a tree, a friendship grows slowly, putting down roots over years, and its strength is proven in bad weather rather than fair. Be that kind of friend, and be grateful for the ones who shelter you.
