Holmes pictures friendship as a rose that breathes, alive rather than cut and fading. Every fold of the flower holds sweetness, just as every layer of a true friendship holds something good: the shared joke, the old memory, the quiet understanding that needs no words. A rose is beautiful from a distance, but its fragrance is only found up close — and so is friendship. It rewards the person who comes near. Keep your friendships close enough to smell the rose, and they will keep sweetening your days.
