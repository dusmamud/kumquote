Thinking aloud is an act of trust. With most people we edit, polish, and hide the messy middle of our thoughts, afraid of sounding foolish. Before a true friend, that filter can fall away. You can say the half-formed idea, the unpopular opinion, the doubt you barely admit to yourself, and know it will be heard with generosity. Such a friend helps you think better, not by agreeing, but by listening honestly. A friend before whom you may think aloud is a gift beyond price, because they give your mind a safe place to breathe.
