Trust in friendship is beautiful precisely because it is fragile. A single betrayal, a careless word, or a broken promise can crack something that took years to build, and even when it is repaired, the line where it broke often remains visible. This does not mean broken friendships are worthless; some are mended with patience and become stronger. But it does mean friendship deserves handling with care. We should speak to friends as if their trust were glass, not stone, remembering that the easiest way to keep a friendship whole is never to test how much damage it can take.
