We call friendship leisure, as if it were rest, but Adler says it is the hardest kind of rest there is. Real friendship taxes us: it demands time, honesty, patience, and the willingness to be there when it is inconvenient. It asks us to listen to the same worry twice, to forgive the same fault again, to show up when staying home would be easier. That effort is the point. A friendship that costs nothing is worth little. The work we put into it is exactly what makes it precious.
