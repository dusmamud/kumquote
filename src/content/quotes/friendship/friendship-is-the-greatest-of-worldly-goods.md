Lewis ranks friendship above every other worldly good — above wealth, fame, and comfort — and calls it the chief happiness of life. That is a strong claim from a man who had all three, and it rings true. Friends are the people with whom we are most ourselves, the ones who share the joke and the burden alike. No possession can do what a friend does: understand us, forgive us, and still choose our company. If friendship is the greatest good, then it deserves the greatest care we can give.
