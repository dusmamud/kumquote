Most people like us for the version of ourselves we show them. A real friend knows the full version: the flaws, the mistakes, the unflattering truths, and likes us anyway. That kind of acceptance is rare because it asks for honesty on both sides. The friend must see clearly, and we must let ourselves be seen. When someone knows all about you and still chooses you, the friendship becomes a safe place where pretense can finally rest. That is the test of true friendship: not admiration from a distance, but liking that survives full knowledge.
