A real friend is one of the rarest things in life. Acquaintances are easy to find; people who stand by us when it costs them something are not. The phrase "really a friend" carries the whole point — it separates the fair-weather companion from the one who stays through trouble, boredom, and disagreement. Such a friend is worth more than wealth or success, because no achievement feels complete without someone to share it with, and no hardship feels as heavy when someone shares that too.
