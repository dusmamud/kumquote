We all carry abilities that stay dormant because nobody expects much from us. A true friend changes that by seeing more in us than we see in ourselves. Their belief is not empty flattery; it is a steady, honest confidence that calls our better self forward. When a friend says you can do it, and means it, you start to believe it too. That is the quiet power of friendship: it does not merely comfort us where we are, it draws out what we are capable of becoming. The best friends are the ones who make our potential feel reachable.
