Barbara Kingsolver reminds us that friendship is measured by showing up, not by saying the perfect thing. The friend who stays away may be protecting themselves from awkwardness, but their absence is felt far more than any clumsy word. A hand held in silence, a visit made without knowing what to say, a simple "I'm here" — these matter more than polished comfort. Real friends risk getting it wrong because being present matters more than being eloquent. When someone is hurting, your company is the gift; the words are only the wrapping.
