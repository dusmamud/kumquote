Light is usually the thing we want — clarity, safety, an easy path — yet Keller chooses the dark with a friend over the light alone. Hard times shared become bearable, while even good fortune feels empty without someone beside us. Friendship does not remove the darkness, but it changes what the darkness means: we are not lost, only walking together through it. The line measures what truly matters to us, and it is not comfort but companionship. Given the choice between an easy road walked alone and a hard road walked with a friend, the friend is worth more.
