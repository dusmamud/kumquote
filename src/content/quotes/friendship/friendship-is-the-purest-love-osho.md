Love is tangled with wanting: to be chosen, to be needed, to get something in return. Osho calls friendship the purest love because it is the least tangled. A friend loves us without demanding we become something else, without keeping score, without needing perfection. It is love in its simplest form: two people who enjoy each other's company and wish each other well. That simplicity is why friendship can last a lifetime while romances fade. It asks for so little and gives so much. If you want to know what love looks like without conditions, look at a true friendship.
