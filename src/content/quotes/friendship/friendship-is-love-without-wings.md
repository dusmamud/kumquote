Byron gives friendship the highest compliment by comparing it to love and then removing the wings. Love flies — it soars, it rushes, it sometimes crashes. Friendship stays on the ground, steady and close, walking beside us through ordinary days. It has the warmth of love without the turbulence, the devotion without the drama. That is why friendships often outlast romances: they are built for the long road, not the high flight. Love without wings is love that stays beside us.
