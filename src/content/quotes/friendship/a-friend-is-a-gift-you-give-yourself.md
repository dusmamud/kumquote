You cannot buy friendship, and you cannot force it. But you can choose it — by showing up, by listening, by being the kind of person others trust — and that choice is a gift to your own life. A true friend makes hard days bearable and good days brighter, and no amount of money can purchase what a friend freely gives. The best part is that the gift grows: the more you invest in friendship, the richer it becomes, for both of you. So give yourself this gift. Open the door, reach out first, and let a friend in.
