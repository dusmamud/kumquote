Eliot describes the rarest gift friendship offers: the feeling of being completely safe with another person. With a true friend, we do not rehearse our sentences, hide our doubts or measure every word for fear of judgment. We can think aloud, admit mistakes and simply be ourselves. That comfort is not laziness; it is trust built slowly over time, through honesty kept and confidences honoured. Such friendship gives us a place to rest from the careful performance daily life demands. When we find someone with whom silence feels easy, we have found something precious.
