A mirror shows your face; an old friend shows your life. Someone who has known you for years can see patterns you cannot: how you have changed, what you keep repeating, and where you are pretending. That reflection is more honest than any compliment or criticism from a stranger, because it comes from shared history and genuine care. Old friends remember who you were and can remind you who you meant to be. The image is gentle rather than harsh. A true friend reflects without cruelty, helping you see yourself clearly so you can grow.
