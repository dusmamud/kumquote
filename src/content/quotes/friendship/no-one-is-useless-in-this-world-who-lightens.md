Worth is not measured by wealth or talent, but by whether we make life lighter for another person. A friend who listens after a hard day, helps without being asked, or shows up when things are heavy has done something genuinely useful. Usefulness is not grand or rare; it is available to everyone, every day, in small acts of care. You do not need power or riches to matter. If your presence eases someone else's load, your life has purpose. Friendship is the simplest form of this truth, and even the quietest kindness can change a life.
