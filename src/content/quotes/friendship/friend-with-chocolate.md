Not every truth about friendship needs to be solemn. Grayson's playful line reminds us that friendship is also about simple shared pleasures: sitting together, laughing, and enjoying the small sweet things in life. A friend makes ordinary moments better, and chocolate is just the symbol of that sweetness. The deepest bonds are not only built in hard times; they are also built in the easy, joyful hours when nothing important is happening. Sometimes the best thing in life is gloriously simple: a good friend, and something sweet to share.
