Friends multiply the good and soften the bad. A joy shared with a friend feels twice as bright, because their delight adds to our own and the moment becomes a memory we hold together. A sorrow shared feels half as heavy, because another person carries part of the weight and reminds us we are not alone. This simple arithmetic is why friendship matters so much: it does not remove life's ups and downs, but it changes how they feel. With friends beside us, happiness grows larger and hardship grows smaller, and life becomes easier to bear.
