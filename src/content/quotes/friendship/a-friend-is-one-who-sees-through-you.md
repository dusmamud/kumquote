Most people show a polished version of themselves, hiding the doubts, mistakes, and moods underneath. A real friend sees past that performance and stays anyway, not because they missed the flaws but because they understand them. Being truly seen can feel frightening, yet it is also a relief: you no longer have to pretend. The friend who enjoys the view after seeing everything offers something rare, acceptance without conditions. That friendship gives us courage to be honest about who we are, and teaches us our worth was never in the mask, but in the person underneath it.
