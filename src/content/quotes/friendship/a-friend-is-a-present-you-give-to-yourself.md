We usually think of gifts as things we give to others, but friendship is a gift we give to ourselves. Choosing a good friend enriches your own life: their company, their wisdom, and their loyalty become part of your daily joy. No purchase can match what a true friend adds to life. And unlike things that wear out, a friendship grows more valuable the longer it lasts. So invest in friendship the way you would invest in anything precious. A friend is not an expense or a distraction; a friend is the finest present you can give yourself.
