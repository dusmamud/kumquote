We often blame a lack of love when a marriage struggles, but this quote points to a deeper cause. Love can be intense and still fail if friendship is missing: the easy conversation, the shared laughter, the comfort of being fully known. Passion fades and returns, but friendship is what carries a relationship through ordinary days and hard years. A marriage built on friendship has a foundation that passion alone cannot provide. The lesson applies beyond marriage: any lasting bond needs friendship at its core, not only feeling.
