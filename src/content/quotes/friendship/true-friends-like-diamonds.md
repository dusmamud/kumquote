Diamonds are rare, and so are true friends. Most people we meet are pleasant acquaintances; only a few become the kind of friends who stay through hard seasons and still shine years later. Like diamonds, true friends are formed under pressure — tested by distance, disagreement, and difficulty — and they come out brighter for it. They never go out of style because loyalty and honesty never do. We should treasure them the way we treasure anything precious: carefully, gratefully, and never taking them for granted.
