Life without friendship can be bitter: work grows heavy, sorrows grow lonely, and even success tastes flat when there is no one to share it with. Friendship sweetens everything it touches — hard days feel lighter, good days feel richer, and ordinary moments become memories worth keeping. A friend's laughter, a shared meal, a conversation that runs long into the night: these small things add a sweetness no wealth can buy. Like honey in tea, friendship does not replace the drink but transforms it. Keep your friendships close and tended, and the whole of life will taste better for it.
