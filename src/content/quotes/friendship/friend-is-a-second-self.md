A true friend is not a stranger who happens to be kind, but someone so close that their thoughts, hopes and cares feel like your own. This is why a friend's joy delights us and a friend's pain hurts almost as if it were ours. The idea sets a high standard: friendship at its best is not a transaction or a convenience but a second self, sharing life rather than merely observing it. Such closeness takes time, honesty and trust to grow. When you find it, treat it as one of the rarest things in life, because it is.
