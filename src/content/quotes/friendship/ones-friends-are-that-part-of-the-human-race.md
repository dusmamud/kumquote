George Santayana points out that much of life asks us to perform — to be polite, professional, composed, and careful. With real friends, the performance drops. We can be tired, confused, silly, or sad without fear of judgment, and that freedom is what makes friendship feel like home. Friends are the people before whom we do not have to be impressive or correct; we only have to be ourselves. In that sense, friendship is not a luxury of life, but the place where life is most honestly lived.
