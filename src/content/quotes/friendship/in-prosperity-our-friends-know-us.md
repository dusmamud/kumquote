Prosperity attracts many people, but it does not reveal who they are. When life is easy, everyone seems loyal. Adversity is the real test: it shows who stays when there is nothing to gain. This quote warns us not to confuse popularity with friendship. True friends are proven in hard times, when helping costs something. It also turns the mirror on us: we learn who our friends are in our own hard times, and they learn who we are in theirs. Hardship is the honest judge of every bond.
