Friendship often begins with a moment of recognition: you meet someone who sees the world the way you do. It is not about liking the same things, but about sharing the same truth, the same sense of what matters and what is real. That shared vision creates a bond that small talk never can. Friends are people who stand beside you looking at the same horizon, not people who simply stand facing you. When two people see the same truth, conversation becomes easy, silence becomes comfortable, and the friendship grows roots that shallow acquaintance can never match.
