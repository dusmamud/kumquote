Keller, who knew darkness in a literal sense, chooses companionship over clarity. Walking alone in the light sounds safer — we can see every step, avoid every obstacle. Yet she says the dark road shared with a friend is better, because friendship supplies what sight cannot: courage, laughter, and the comfort of not being alone. Hard times are easier when faced together, and even ordinary days feel richer beside someone we trust. The quote reminds us that the best journeys are measured by who walks with us, not by how bright the path is.
