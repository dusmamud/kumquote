Wiesel, who survived the darkest chapters of history, knew what truly sustains a life. Romantic love may burn brightly, but friendship endures across decades, through changes of fortune, distance, and loss. Friends witness our whole story, not just its brightest moments, and their steady presence shapes who we become more deeply than any single passion. Love can fade or change form, but a true friend remains, a constant mark on the soul. In the end, it is often friendship, quiet and faithful, that leaves the deepest imprint on a life.
