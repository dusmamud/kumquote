A person can lose money, health, or status and still feel worthless — until a friend reminds them otherwise. Friendship gives every life value, because to a friend you are never useless. You matter simply by being there, by listening, by caring, by sharing the road. Even when you feel you have nothing to offer the world, a friend's trust says otherwise. That is the quiet miracle of friendship: it gives worth to lives that the world might overlook. As long as you have a true friend, you are never useless, and never alone.
