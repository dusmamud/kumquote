Gracián describes friendship as the arithmetic of a good life. Joy shared with a friend grows — a celebration told twice is twice as sweet, and a small happiness becomes a memory the moment you share it. Sorrow works the other way: a burden carried by two people weighs half as much on each, because the friend's presence makes the load bearable. Keep your friends close in good times and bad alike. The good will multiply, the evil will divide, and the balance of your life will improve.
