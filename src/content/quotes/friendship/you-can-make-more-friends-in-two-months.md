Most of us try to win friends by talking about ourselves, hoping others will find us interesting. But friendship works the other way round. People are drawn to those who show genuine interest in them: who ask questions, listen carefully, and remember what matters to them. Interest is the fastest way to a person's heart, because everyone wants to feel noticed and understood. So if you want more friends, stop performing and start listening. Be curious about others, and you will find that friendship comes to you far faster than you expect.
