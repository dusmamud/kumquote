Friendship doubles joy and halves sorrow, and this line says exactly how. In good times, a friend's presence makes success shine brighter, because shared happiness is fuller than happiness kept alone. In hard times, a friend's help divides the burden: what would crush one person becomes bearable when two carry it together. This is the quiet mathematics of friendship — it multiplies the good and divides the bad. No one is meant to carry life alone, in triumph or in trouble.
