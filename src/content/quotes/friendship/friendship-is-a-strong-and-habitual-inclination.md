Friendship is not a single grand gesture but a steady habit of wishing another person well. It shows itself in small, repeated choices: checking in, listening properly, celebrating their wins, and standing by them when things go wrong. The word habitual matters, because friendship lives in what we do regularly, not in what we promise occasionally. Two people who make a habit of promoting each other's good build something that survives distance and difficulty. Be that kind of friend, and you will find that friendship returns to you many times over.
