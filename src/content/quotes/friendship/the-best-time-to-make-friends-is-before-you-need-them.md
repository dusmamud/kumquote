Friendship cannot be rushed into existence the day trouble arrives. It grows slowly, through shared meals, honest conversations, and small acts of loyalty repeated over months and years. The person who invests in friends during calm seasons is never truly alone when the storm comes, because the bonds are already strong. Wait until you are desperate, and you will find that trust takes time to build — time you no longer have. Make friends now, while life is easy, and you will have a circle of strength ready for the day life is not.
