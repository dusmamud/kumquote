Happiness shared does not split in half; it doubles, because a friend's delight in your good news adds a joy of its own. Grief works the opposite way: carried alone it feels unbearable, but shared with a friend its weight is halved, even when nothing about the situation has changed. This is the quiet arithmetic of friendship, and it explains why friends are most needed at both extremes of life. In celebration and in sorrow, friendship multiplies the good and divides the bad. Keep friends close in both seasons, and be the friend who does the same.
