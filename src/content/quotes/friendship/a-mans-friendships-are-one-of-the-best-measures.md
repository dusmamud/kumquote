Charles Darwin suggests that we can judge a person less by their wealth or titles than by the friends they keep. Friendships are chosen, not inherited, so they reveal what someone truly values. Loyal, generous, honest people tend to gather friends of the same kind, while selfishness slowly empties a person's circle. Look at who stands beside someone in hard times and you learn more than any résumé could tell you. Our friendships are a mirror of our character, reflecting back the person we have chosen to be.
