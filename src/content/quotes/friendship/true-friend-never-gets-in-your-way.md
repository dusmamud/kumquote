Glasgow defines a true friend by restraint as much as by support. A real friend does not crowd us, correct us constantly, or stand in the way of our choices. They give us room to live our own life — except when we are heading toward harm. Then, and only then, they step in, not to control us but to protect us. That balance is rare: freedom without indifference, honesty without interference. The friend who knows when to step back and when to step forward is the one worth keeping close.
