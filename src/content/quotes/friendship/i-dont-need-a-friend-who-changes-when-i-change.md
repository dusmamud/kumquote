A friend who only agrees with you is not a friend at all; they are a mirror. Real friendship includes honesty, even when honesty is uncomfortable. A true friend tells you when you are wrong, challenges a bad idea, and refuses to applaud a choice that will hurt you. That friction is valuable, because it keeps us from drifting into error while everyone around us stays silent. Agreement is cheap and easy to find. What is rare is a friend with an independent mind who cares enough to disagree, and whose honesty you can trust completely.
