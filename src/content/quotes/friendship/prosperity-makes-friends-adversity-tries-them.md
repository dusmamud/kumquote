Success attracts company the way light attracts moths. When life is going well, friends are easy to find, and the crowd around you grows. Adversity does the opposite: it tries them, testing which friendships were real and which were only enjoying the warmth of your good fortune. The friends who remain when there is nothing left to gain are the ones worth keeping. This is not a bitter truth but a useful one. Prosperity shows you many faces; adversity shows you which faces are true. Keep the ones who stayed.
