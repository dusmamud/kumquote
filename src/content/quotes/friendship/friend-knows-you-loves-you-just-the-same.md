Most people love the version of us we present, which makes real friendship rare and precious. A true friend has seen the flaws, the bad moods, the mistakes and the awkward truths, and still chooses to stay. That kind of knowing removes the need to perform or hide, and in that safety we become more honest versions of ourselves. The love described here is not blind; it sees clearly and stays anyway. If you have a friend like this, guard the friendship carefully, and try to be the same kind of friend in return.
