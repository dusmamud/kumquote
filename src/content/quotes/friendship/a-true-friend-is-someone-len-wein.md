Friendship is easy when it costs nothing: a laugh, a message, a good time. Wein's test is stricter. A true friend is there for you when they would rather be anywhere else, when it is late, inconvenient, or uncomfortable. That is the moment friendship stops being a pleasure and becomes a promise. Anyone can celebrate with us; few will sit with us in the hard hours. The friend who shows up anyway proves what the word means. Be that friend, and you will know your true friends, because they will do the same for you.
