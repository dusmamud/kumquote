Friendship is often described in soft terms — shared laughter, warm feelings, good times. Lincoln offers a sterner test: who stands beside you when you are opposed? Shared enemies reveal shared values. A friend is not just someone who enjoys your company; it is someone who shares your fights, defends your name when you are not in the room, and refuses to switch sides when it gets uncomfortable. Fair-weather friends vanish at the first sign of trouble. The real ones stay, because they are in it with you.
