John Selden says in four words what long essays struggle to prove: old friends are best. New friends are a delight, but old ones carry a shared history that cannot be hurried into being. They remember who you were before success or failure changed how the world treats you, and they love the person underneath the titles. With them there is no need to explain the backstory or impress anyone; a single reference, a shared joke, says everything. Time tests friendship the way fire tests metal, and the bonds that survive it are the ones worth keeping closest.
