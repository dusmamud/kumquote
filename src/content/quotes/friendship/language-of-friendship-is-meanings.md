True friends understand each other beyond words. A glance, a pause, or a half-finished sentence can carry a whole conversation, because the meaning lives in the shared history between two people. Thoreau reminds us that friendship is not measured by how much is said, but by how deeply it is understood. Words are only the surface; the real language is trust, attention, and knowing. When a friend understands you without explanation, that is a rare and precious thing. Cherish the friends who hear what you mean, not only what you say.
