Lincoln's wisdom turns conflict upside down: the surest victory over an enemy is not defeat but friendship. Defeating someone leaves resentment that breeds future conflict, while befriending them removes the enmity entirely. When we understand our opponents, listen to them, and treat them with respect, we often discover shared interests and common ground we never expected. The enemy disappears not because we destroyed the person but because we dissolved the hatred between us. It is the most complete victory possible, because it leaves no one defeated and both sides better off.
