Gibran draws a line between two ways of treating friendship: as a responsibility or as an opportunity. Treating it as an opportunity means asking what a friend can do for you, which turns the bond into a transaction. Treating it as a responsibility means asking what you owe the friendship: honesty, time, patience and presence when it is inconvenient. The word sweet matters, because this responsibility is not a burden but a joy. Caring for a friend enriches the one who cares. True friendship asks something of us, and that asking is exactly what makes it precious.
