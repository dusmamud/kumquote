True friendship is a relationship between equals. It does not ask one person to lead forever or the other to follow forever; it asks both to walk side by side, sharing the road as partners. The friend who walks behind you may admire you but never truly know you, and the friend who walks ahead may guide you but never truly join you. Walking beside someone means facing the same direction, at the same pace, through the same weather. That is friendship at its best — not above, not below, but beside, step for step, all the way.
