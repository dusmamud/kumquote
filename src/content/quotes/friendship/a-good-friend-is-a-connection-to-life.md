A good friend is not just someone to pass time with; they are a living connection to the whole of your life. They remember your past, walk beside you in the present, and believe in the future you are trying to build. In a world that can feel rushed and uncertain, that connection keeps us steady, reminding us who we are when we forget. Friendship of this kind is not a luxury but a necessity. Keep such friends close, answer their calls, and be the same steady connection for them. A friend like this holds your life together.
