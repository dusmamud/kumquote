Distance tests friendship, but it cannot defeat the real kind. Helen Keller knew that physical separation is only geography; the heart keeps its own map, and true friends remain close in spirit no matter how many miles lie between them. A phone call, a letter, or a memory can close the gap in an instant, because the bond lives in shared history and mutual trust, not in daily proximity. When friends meet again after years apart, the conversation often resumes as if yesterday. That is the mark of true friendship: distance may separate bodies, but never hearts.
