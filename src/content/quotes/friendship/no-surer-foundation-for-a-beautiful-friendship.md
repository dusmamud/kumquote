Shared tastes build the strongest friendships, and few bonds form faster than the love of the same books. When two people cherish the same stories, they already share a language of values, humour, and imagination before they have even swapped life stories. A favourite book is a window into a person's mind, and discovering that someone else loves it too feels like finding a kindred spirit. Wodehouse's lighthearted wisdom holds a deeper truth: friendship grows fastest where interests overlap, because shared passions give two people endless things to talk about and explore together.
