"How are you?" is usually a greeting, not a question. Most people ask it while already walking away. A real friend asks it and stays — really stays — to hear the honest answer, even when the answer is messy. That kind of listening is rare because it costs time and attention, the two things we guard most. But it is also what makes friendship feel like home. To be heard without being rushed is one of the kindest experiences in life. Good friends give it freely. We should try to give it back.
