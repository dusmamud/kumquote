Aristotle draws a line between real friendship and friendship that only serves a purpose. Many people wish us well because our success helps them, or because our company is useful to them. A true friend is different: they want good things for you simply because they are good for you, with no benefit to themselves. That selfless wish is the test of friendship. It shows when a friend celebrates your success without envy and supports choices that gain them nothing. Such friendship is rare, yet it is the only kind fully trusted through every season of life.
