We spend our lives chasing gifts that can be bought or earned, but the greatest gift cannot be purchased at all. Friendship is given freely, and its value grows with time. Humphrey's gratitude is worth imitating: instead of always wanting more, pause and recognise the friendships you already have. They are the wealth that matters most, the people who laugh with you, stand by you, and make life richer. If you have received the gift of friendship, you are already among the fortunate, and saying so is the proper response.
