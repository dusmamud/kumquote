Some words carry feeling before we even read the sentence around them, and friendship is one of them. Just seeing it reminds us of people who stood beside us, laughed with us, and made ordinary days feel lighter. The warmth comes from memory: shared meals, long conversations, and the comfort of being known without having to explain yourself. Friendship needs no grand definition, because its meaning lives in those moments. Long before we analyse what friendship is, we already feel what it means, and that feeling alone is enough to make us smile.
