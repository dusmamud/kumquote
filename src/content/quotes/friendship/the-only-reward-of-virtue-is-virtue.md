Friendship cannot be bought, earned, or demanded; it can only be given by being the kind of person others want as a friend. This quote turns the usual question around. Instead of asking how to find good friends, it asks how to be one: honest, loyal, generous, and present. Virtue is its own reward, and friendship works the same way. The act of being a friend is itself the prize, because it shapes us into better people. If we want true friends, the work begins with ourselves.
