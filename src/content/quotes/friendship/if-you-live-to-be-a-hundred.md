This is a child's wish expressed with perfect logic: if I must live a hundred years, let it be one day less than you, so I never have to face a day without you. It sounds simple, but it holds a deep truth about friendship. The thought of losing a friend is harder than any other loss, because friends make life itself worth living. The quote measures love not in grand gestures but in the wish to never be apart. It is playful, tender, and profoundly honest about what friends mean to us.
