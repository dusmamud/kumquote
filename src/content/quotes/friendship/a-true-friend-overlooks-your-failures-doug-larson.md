It is easy to be a friend in good times, when there is laughter and success to share. Larson's test is harder. A true friend overlooks your failures and tolerates your success. Overlooking failure takes grace: the friend does not hold our worst moments against us. Tolerating success takes generosity: the friend is not diminished by our wins, nor jealous when we do well. Both are rare. Most people can manage one or the other. The friend who can do both, who stays steady through our failures and our triumphs, is the one worth keeping for life.
