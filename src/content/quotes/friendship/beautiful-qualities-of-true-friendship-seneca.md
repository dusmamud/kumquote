Seneca names the quiet miracle at the heart of friendship: being understood without having to explain everything. With a true friend, half a sentence is enough — they know your history, your tone, your silences. And the gift runs both ways: understanding them, in turn, is its own pleasure. This mutual understanding is rare because it takes time and honesty to build; it cannot be bought or hurried. When you find a friend who understands you, guard that bond. It is one of the most beautiful things two people can share.
