Emerson celebrates the rare comfort of a friendship old enough to drop all pretence. With new acquaintances, every word is measured; with an old friend, you can be silly, wrong, or clumsy, and the friendship does not even notice. That freedom is the proof of the bond: years of shared history have built a trust no single mistake can break. It is a blessing because so much of life demands performance. If you have an old friend, thank them — and be that unguarded, forgiving friend for someone else.
