Epicurus studied what makes people happy, and his answer was not wealth, fame, or pleasure, but friendship. Riches can be lost and pleasures grow dull, yet a true friend stays steady through all of it. Friendship gives life safety and joy at once: someone to share good days with, and someone to lean on when days turn hard. Wisdom is not cleverness for its own sake but the skill of choosing what truly satisfies. By that measure, friendship is the wisest investment a person can make, because it pays back in every season.
