Seven words have carried this proverb through centuries because they contain a complete test. A friend in need is a friend indeed: the real friend is revealed not at the party but in the crisis. Anyone can be friendly when everything is fine; the true friend appears when help is needed and there is nothing to gain from giving it. Need is the moment that separates the real from the polite. Keep the friends who show up in hard times, and be the friend who shows up for others.
