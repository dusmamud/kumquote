Shakespeare describes a friendship measured not by years but by depth. A true friend has seen your past, including the parts you would rather hide, and stays. That kind of knowing is rare: most people love an edited version of us. A real friend loves the unedited one — mistakes, moods, unfinished growth — and still believes in who you are becoming. Such friendship asks for honesty in return. You cannot be fully known while hiding. The gift of a friend like this is that you can stop pretending, and in that safety, you can finally grow.
