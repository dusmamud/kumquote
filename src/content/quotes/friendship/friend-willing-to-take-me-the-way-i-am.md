Much of social life asks us to perform, to smooth our edges and present a polished version of ourselves. A friend is the person with whom that performance can stop. They do not require you to be more successful, more charming, or more agreeable before they accept you; they take you as you are, moods and flaws included. That acceptance is rare and restful. It gives us room to be honest about who we are and to grow without pretending. A friendship built on acceptance, rather than approval, is one of the few places where a person can truly breathe.
