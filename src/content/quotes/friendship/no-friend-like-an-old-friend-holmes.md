Holmes honours the friend who knew you before you became who you are. Morning days are the early ones — youth, beginnings, the time before reputation and responsibility layered over you. An old friend carries that version of you, and with it a kind of knowledge no new friend can buy: they remember what you dreamed of, what you laughed at, who you were before the world weighed in. That shared past makes the friendship irreplaceable. Keep them close, call them often, and remember that such friends are made slowly, over mornings that never come again.
