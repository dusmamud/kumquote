People disagree about almost everything: wealth, fame, pleasure and power all have their critics. Friendship is the rare exception, because everyone who has ever had a true friend knows its worth without needing to be convinced. Its usefulness is practical as well as emotional: friends share burdens, give honest counsel, celebrate victories and sit through defeats. No philosophy, culture or age has found a good argument against it. That universal agreement is itself the evidence. Whatever else divides us, friendship is one good that nobody disputes.
