We often wait too long to say kind things to our friends. We plan the perfect praise for a birthday, a farewell, or sadly, a funeral — when the person can no longer hear it. But a loving word spoken today is worth far more than the finest speech carved on a tombstone. Tell your friends what they mean to you while they can still smile at it. Appreciation delayed is appreciation wasted. Speak now, kindly and honestly, and let your friends hear, while they are still here, how much they matter to you.
