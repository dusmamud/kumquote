Medicine heals the body, but friendship and love heal something deeper. A friend's presence can steady us in grief, encourage us in fear, and remind us we matter when we doubt it. No prescription can do that. This quote names friendship as therapy in the truest sense: not treatment, but care. The healing comes from being known, accepted, and accompanied. In a world that often treats people as problems to fix, friendship offers something rarer: someone who stays, listens, and helps us heal by simply being there.
