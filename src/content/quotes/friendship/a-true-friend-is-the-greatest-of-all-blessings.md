Blessings are often counted in wealth, health, or success, yet a true friend may be the greatest of them all. A true friend brings comfort in sorrow, joy in celebration, wisdom in confusion, and strength in weakness. They ask nothing in return except your honesty and loyalty. Unlike possessions, which fade, or achievements, which pass, true friendship grows deeper with time. If you are blessed with such a friend, you already have something many people search for all their lives. Do not take it lightly. Thank them, cherish them, and be the same blessing to them.
