Franklin's list is practical, funny, and a little unsentimental. An old wife has stayed through the years; an old dog loves without conditions; ready money never abandons you in an emergency. All three are faithful in the same way: they are there when it matters, not only when it is easy. The joke carries a serious test for every friendship. Ask not who applauds you in good times, but who remains when the years pass and the money runs short. Faithfulness is proven by staying.
