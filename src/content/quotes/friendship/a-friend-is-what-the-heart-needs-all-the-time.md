The heart does not need a friend only in crisis. It needs one in ordinary days too — someone to share a quiet evening with, to laugh at nothing with, to sit beside without needing a reason. Friendship is not a service you call on in emergencies; it is a steady presence that makes the whole of life warmer. People who wait until they are lonely to seek friends find the door harder to open. Keep your friendships close in good times, and they will be there in bad times, because the heart has never stopped needing them.
