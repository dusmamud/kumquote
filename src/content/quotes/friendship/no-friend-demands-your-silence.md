Walker draws a firm line: a person who demands your silence or blocks your growth is not your friend, whatever they call themselves. True friendship makes room for your voice, your opinions, and your becoming. It may challenge you, but it never asks you to shrink. Silence demanded is control, not care, and growth denied is possession, not love. The quote is a reminder to examine our closest relationships honestly and to choose friends who celebrate our voice and cheer our growth, not those who fear it.
