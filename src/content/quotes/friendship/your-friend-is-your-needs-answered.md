A true friend is the answer to needs we sometimes cannot name ourselves. When we are lonely, they offer company; when we are lost, they offer perspective; when we are hurting, they offer comfort without being asked. Gibran's short line captures the quiet miracle of friendship: the right person arrives with exactly what we needed, often before we knew we needed it. This is not about using friends but about the natural generosity of people who care. Friendship answers needs because friends pay attention, and attention is the beginning of every kindness that follows.
