We usually choose friends for how they make us feel: comfortable, amused. Ford suggests a better test. The best friend brings out the best in you, making you braver, kinder, more honest than you are on your own. Such friends do not flatter us; they challenge us, gently, to become the person we could be. They celebrate our growth and tell us the truth when we need it. A friend like that is rare, and worth keeping close. Look at your friends and ask: do they bring out my best? The answer tells you who your best friends are.
