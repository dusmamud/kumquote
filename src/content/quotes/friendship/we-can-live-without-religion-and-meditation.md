Human beings need affection the way plants need water. We can live without many things — rituals, routines, even certain comforts — but we cannot thrive without warmth from other people. A kind word, a caring friend, a hand on the shoulder: these small acts of affection keep us alive inside. Loneliness, by contrast, slowly wears the spirit down. That is why friendship is not a luxury but a necessity. Nurture your friendships, offer affection freely, and accept it gratefully. Without human warmth, even the richest life feels cold and empty.
