Success attracts company. When the limo is running, everyone wants a seat, and it is easy to mistake that crowd for friendship. The real test comes when the limo breaks down, when the money, fame, or luck runs out. Who is still there, willing to ride the bus with you through the ordinary and the hard times? That person is your true friend. Winfrey's image is a warning against counting fair-weather companions as friends, and a reminder to value the ones who stay when there is nothing in it for them.
