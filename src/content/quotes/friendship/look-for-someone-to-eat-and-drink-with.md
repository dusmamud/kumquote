Epicurus puts friendship before food, and he means it literally. A meal shared with a friend nourishes more than the same meal eaten alone, because companionship is the real feast. We spend our lives chasing the things to eat and drink, the goods and pleasures, while forgetting the person to share them with. But pleasure without friendship is thin, and friendship makes even simple food taste better. Choose your company first, and the rest takes care of itself. A table with friends is richer than a feast eaten alone.
