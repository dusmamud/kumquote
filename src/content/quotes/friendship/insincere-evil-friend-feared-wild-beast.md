A wild beast is dangerous, but its danger is honest and visible. An insincere friend is worse, because the harm comes disguised as closeness. A beast may wound the body, which heals, but a false friend wounds the mind: trust broken, judgment clouded, and peace disturbed long after the moment passes. That is why choosing friends is one of life's most serious decisions. A good friend strengthens the mind, while a bad one quietly poisons it. Guard your company as carefully as your health, and keep close only those whose sincerity you have truly tested.
