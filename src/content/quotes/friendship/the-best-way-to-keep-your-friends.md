Friends are easy to lose and hard to replace. We give them away casually: by neglecting them, by taking them for granted, by letting pride or busyness keep us from calling back. The best way to keep friends is simply not to lose them in the first place. Answer the message, show up when it matters, forgive the small offence, and make time before time runs out. Friendship is a treasure that cannot be bought back once it is spent. Keep yours carefully, and remember that the friends you already have are the ones most worth keeping.
