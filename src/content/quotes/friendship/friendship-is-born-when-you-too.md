Friendship often begins with a moment of recognition. You discover that someone else shares your strange interest, your quiet fear, your odd humour, or your hidden hope, and suddenly you feel less alone. C. S. Lewis captures that spark perfectly. It is not admiration from a distance; it is the joy of finding a fellow traveller. That shared understanding creates instant trust, because you no longer have to explain yourself completely. Seek friendships where you can be honest about who you are. When you hear those words, you too, you have found the beginning of something real and lasting.
