Roberts describes the deepest kind of friendship with a musical image. Each of us carries a song — our hopes, our identity, the person we are at our best — but in hard times we forget the words. A true friend remembers them for us. They remind us who we are when grief, failure, or exhaustion has made us forget. That makes friendship irreplaceable: it is not only company in good times, but memory in bad ones. The friend who can sing your song back to you is holding your true self safe until you are ready to sing again.
