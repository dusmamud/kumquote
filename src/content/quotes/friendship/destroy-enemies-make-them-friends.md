Defeating an enemy usually creates another one, but turning an enemy into a friend ends the conflict entirely. Lincoln's line is a quiet piece of genius: the enemy is destroyed not by force but by friendship, because a friend is no longer an enemy at all. This takes more courage than fighting, since it requires listening, patience, and the willingness to see the other person as human. Revenge keeps the cycle turning; friendship breaks it. The strongest victory is not the one that leaves someone defeated, but the one that leaves two people standing together.
