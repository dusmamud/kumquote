Love changes form as life changes: romance fades and returns, families grow and scatter, passions cool. But friendship, when it is true, remains steady through all of it. It is the most constant part of love because it does not depend on excitement or novelty; it depends on trust, shared history, and the simple choice to stay. Friends who have seen us at our worst and stayed anyway teach us what love really means. Build friendships that can last, and you will find that even when other loves change shape, friendship endures beside them.
