Jong offers one of the most honest descriptions of courage ever written. She does not claim to have conquered fear or to have stopped feeling it; she simply stopped letting it decide for her. That distinction matters, because many people wait for fear to disappear before they act — and it never does. Courage is not the absence of fear but the refusal to obey it. The next time fear tells you to stay small, listen to it, thank it for its warning, and then act anyway. That quiet refusal is what freedom looks like.
