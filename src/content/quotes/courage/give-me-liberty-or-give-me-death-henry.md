He argued that a life lived in submission was no life at all, and that the choice was stark: liberty or death, with no comfortable middle ground. The words ignited a revolution because they named what many felt but had not dared to say, that some principles are worth the ultimate risk. Courage, in this moment, was not the absence of fear but the refusal to let fear decide. Henry's sentence still rings because it asks each of us what we value enough to risk everything for.
