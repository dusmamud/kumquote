Elizabeth Kenny contrasts two ways of living: a single day of lion-like courage against a lifetime of sheep-like submission. The lion's day may be brief and costly, but it is lived fully — choosing, risking, standing tall. The sheep's life is safe and long, but it is lived on others' terms. The quote asks which we would choose if we could see our whole life at once. Most of us would trade years of quiet compliance for even one day of true courage. Better to roar once than to bleat forever.
