Dante urges us to stand like a tower that does not bend its summit to the tempest. Storms will come: criticism, hardship, and pressure to compromise. The tower does not argue with the wind; it simply remains upright. Steadfastness is courage in its quietest form, the refusal to be bent by every gust. The quote is a model for integrity. Know what you stand for, plant yourself firmly, and let the tempest pass. The tower is still standing when the storm is spent.
