Fear is not a flaw; it is the natural response of every living thing to danger, and feeling it does not make you a coward. Courage begins exactly where fear does — in the decision to act anyway, with shaking hands and a racing heart. The people we admire most were afraid too; they simply refused to let fear make the decision. If you wait until you feel fearless, you will wait forever. Take the step while you are still afraid, and you will learn that fear shrinks once you move. True courage is not the absence of fear, but action in its presence.
