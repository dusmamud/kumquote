Churchill calls courage the first of human qualities because every other virtue depends on it. Honesty takes courage when the truth is unpopular. Kindness takes courage when cruelty is easier. Justice takes courage when power objects. Without courage, our values stay theoretical, pretty words we abandon under pressure. With it, they become real, tested in the moments that matter. That is why courage guarantees the others: it is the quality that turns principles into actions. Build courage first, and every other virtue gains a foundation strong enough to stand on.
