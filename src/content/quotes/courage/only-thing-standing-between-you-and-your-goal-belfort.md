Belfort points to the invisible barrier that blocks most goals: the story we tell ourselves about why we cannot succeed. We repeat tales of being too old, too late, too untalented, or too unlucky, until the story feels like fact and we stop trying. But the story is not the world; it is a narration we chose, and we can choose a different one. Courage, here, is the act of questioning our own excuses and rewriting the tale, so that the path to the goal finally opens.
