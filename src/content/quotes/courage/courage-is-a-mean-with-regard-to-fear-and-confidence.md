Aristotle defines courage as a mean between fear and confidence. Too little confidence becomes cowardice; too much becomes rashness. Courage sits in the middle: feeling fear honestly, judging the danger clearly, and acting as reason directs. It is not the absence of fear but the right amount of it, guided by judgement. The quote makes courage a skill of balance rather than a burst of boldness. The brave person is the one who has learned to calibrate fear and confidence well.
