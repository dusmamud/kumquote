Thompson gives courage its fullest definition: it is not one act but a sustained power — to face danger, endure misfortune, master fear, and resist injustice, all while still affirming that life is good. That last part is the hardest: courage is not only fighting what is wrong, but refusing to let the fight make us bitter. To keep believing in life's goodness while looking its sorrows in the face is the deepest bravery of all, and the one that sustains every other kind.
