Ali, a champion who risked his title and career for his beliefs, knew that safety has a price. Avoiding risk feels comfortable, but it guarantees a small life: no great achievement ever came without a gamble. Every meaningful goal — starting a business, speaking a truth, chasing a dream — carries the chance of failure, and that chance is exactly what makes the achievement matter. Courage is not recklessness; it is accepting the risk because the goal is worth it. Those who never risk anything accomplish nothing, and they lose the very safety they were trying to protect.
