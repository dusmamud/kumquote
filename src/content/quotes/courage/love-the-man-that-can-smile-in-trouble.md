Paine admires a rare kind of strength: not the loud courage of battle, but the quiet kind that holds steady when life turns hard. Smiling in trouble is not denial; it is refusing to let hardship write the whole story. Gathering strength from distress means treating pain as training, converting each setback into steadiness for the next one. Growing brave by reflection is the deepest part: thinking honestly about fear until it shrinks to its true size. This courage is available to anyone, because it is built in private, one hard day at a time.
