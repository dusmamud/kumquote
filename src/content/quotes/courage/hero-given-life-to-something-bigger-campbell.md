A hero is not simply someone who is brave in a crisis, but someone who gives their life, their time, energy, and heart, to something larger than personal comfort. That something might be a cause, a community, a craft, or another person, but it lifts the hero beyond self-interest. Courage enters because such giving always costs something: safety, ease, or recognition. Yet the exchange is what makes a life heroic, turning ordinary days into service of a greater purpose. In this view, heroism is available to anyone willing to live for more than themselves.
