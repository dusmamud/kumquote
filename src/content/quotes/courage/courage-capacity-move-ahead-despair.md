We often imagine courage as the absence of fear or doubt, but May describes something truer and harder. Despair does not disappear just because we decide to be brave; it walks alongside us, insisting that nothing will work. Courage is the capacity to keep moving anyway, to take the next small step while despair is still talking. This is not denial. It is a refusal to let a feeling have the final word. People who move ahead in spite of despair discover something important: action itself can change the feeling.
