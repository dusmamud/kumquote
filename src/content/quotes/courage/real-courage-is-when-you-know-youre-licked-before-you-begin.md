Atticus Finch's definition of courage, written by Harper Lee, turns the idea upside down. Courage is not the confidence of someone who expects to win; it is the decision to begin when defeat looks certain. That kind of bravery has nothing to do with glory and everything to do with character — doing the right thing because it is right, not because it will work. Most of us will face a moment like this: a hard conversation, an unfair fight, a cause that seems lost. Starting anyway is courage, and finishing what we started is what makes it real.
