Virgil's three words have survived two thousand years because they are still true: fortune favors the bold. The cautious may avoid failure, but they also avoid the opportunities that only come to those who step forward. Boldness does not mean recklessness; it means acting despite uncertainty, asking for the chance, and taking the risk when the odds are reasonable. Luck, it turns out, is often just preparation meeting a moment of daring. Sit still and fortune passes by. Step forward boldly, and fortune frequently turns its face toward you.
