Cervantes ranks our losses by what they cost. Wealth can be rebuilt, and even a lost friend leaves memories and lessons behind. Courage is different, because it is the tool we use to recover from every other loss. Lose money and courage remains, and you can earn again; lose a friend and courage remains, and you can love again. But lose courage itself, and nothing else can be rebuilt, because the will to try is gone. Guard your courage first, Cervantes says, for it is the foundation under everything else.
