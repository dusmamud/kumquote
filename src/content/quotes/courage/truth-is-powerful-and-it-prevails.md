Truth's line is both simple and fearless: truth is powerful, and it prevails. Lies may win for a season — they are loud, quick, and convenient. But truth has a patience that lies do not. It waits, it endures, and in the end it is the only thing left standing. Speaking the truth takes courage, especially when the truth is unpopular. But those who speak it join something larger than themselves. You do not need to be powerful to speak the truth; speaking the truth is what makes you powerful.
