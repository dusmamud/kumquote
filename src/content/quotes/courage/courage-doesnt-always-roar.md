We picture courage as loud and dramatic, but most of it happens quietly, at the end of an ordinary day. After a failure, a rejection, or a long stretch of effort without results, the brave act is simply to begin again tomorrow. No audience watches that decision; no one applauds it. Yet it is the courage that most lives depend on, because big victories are rare and small restarts are constant. Trying again is not glamorous, but it is how people rebuild, recover, and eventually succeed.
