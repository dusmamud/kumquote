Courage and faith are two sides of the same coin. To act bravely, we must believe in something beyond the present danger: a cause worth serving, a future worth reaching, or simply the conviction that our efforts matter. Without faith, courage has nothing to stand on, and fear wins by default. With faith, even a frightened person can step forward, trusting the step is right. This faith need not be religious; it can be faith in a friend, a principle, or one's own resilience. Courage begins where faith answers fear, and the brave are those who believe enough to act.
