Audre Lorde describes a turning point: the moment we stop waiting for fear to disappear and start using our strength anyway. Fear does not vanish; it simply becomes less important, because the vision we serve matters more than the fear we feel. Power, here, is not domination but the courage to act on what we believe, even while afraid. Many people wait to feel ready before they begin, and the waiting lasts a lifetime. Lorde's wisdom is that readiness follows action, not the other way around.
