Fear is cheap lodging. It costs nothing to move in, and many of us settle there for years, living small because the rent is low. But the rooms are cramped, dark, and far from everything worth seeing. This image asks us to notice the poor conditions we have accepted and to want better for ourselves. Courage is the decision to move out of that cheap room into a larger life, even though the rent is higher and the address is unfamiliar. We deserve better conditions than fear can offer, and the door is open whenever we choose to leave.
