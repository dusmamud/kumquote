Comfortable routines tell us very little about who we are, because they never test us. Adventure strips away the roles and habits that usually define us, leaving something more honest underneath. Some people discover courage they did not know they had; others discover limits they needed to respect. Either way, they learn something no amount of quiet reflection could have taught them. Gide's point is that self-knowledge is not found in safety. It is found on the far side of the step we are afraid to take.
