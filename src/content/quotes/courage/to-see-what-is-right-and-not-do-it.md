Knowing what is right is only half the matter; the other half is doing it. Many people can recognise the honest course, the kind action, or the necessary stand, yet stay silent. Confucius names that gap for what it is: not ignorance, but a failure of courage. Courage is not reserved for grand battles; it appears in daily choices — speaking up, admitting a mistake, or refusing to look away. When we see the right thing and fail to act, we betray our judgement. True courage is closing the distance between what we know and what we do.
