Dying takes no effort at all; living well takes courage every day. It takes courage to love after loss, to try after failure, to hope when the world gives you reasons not to. Many people survive their years without ever truly living them, hiding from risk, feeling, and change. Real bravery is choosing to engage: to speak your mind, to pursue what matters, to be present in your own life. Do not merely endure your days; inhabit them. Anyone can die, but only the brave truly live — and that choice is yours to make today.
