Life will hand you chapters you did not choose — loss, failure, unfairness — but it does not get to decide who you are in them. The victim waits for rescue and rehearses the wrong; the heroine acts, even in small ways, and writes the next scene herself. Being the heroine of your life does not mean pretending hardship did not hurt; it means refusing to let hurt be the whole story. Take one brave step: ask for help, set a boundary, start again. Your story is still being written, and you hold the pen. Choose the role of heroine, and live it.
