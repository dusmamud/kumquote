Reading about life is not the same as living it. We can study, plan, and imagine, but real understanding comes only from experience, and experience requires risk. Being brave means stepping beyond what is comfortable and certain, knowing we might fail or be hurt. That risk is the price of a full life. Coelho reminds us that no book, no advice, and no amount of thinking can replace doing. To be brave and take risks is to choose a life that is truly lived, not merely observed from a safe distance.
