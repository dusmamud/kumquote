Insecurity often dresses itself as toughness, putting others down to feel strong. True bravery works the other way. Acknowledging another's strength costs nothing and gains a great deal: respect, trust, and the chance to learn from people we might otherwise have dismissed. It also takes honesty, because it means giving up the comforting story that we are the strongest person in the room. Courage is not only facing danger; it is facing the truth about ourselves and others without flinching.
