Hemingway defines courage in five words, and the definition is all about composure. Grace under pressure is not the absence of danger or difficulty; it is the ability to stay steady, kind, and clear-headed when everything around you is falling apart. Anyone can be brave when the stakes are low. The test comes when fear, pain, or chaos press in and the easy response is panic or cruelty. True courage, Hemingway suggests, looks like dignity in those moments: a calm voice, a steady hand, and a refusal to let pressure decide who you are.
