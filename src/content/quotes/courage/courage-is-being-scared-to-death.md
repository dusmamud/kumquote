John Wayne gives courage a plain, cowboy image: you are terrified, and you saddle up anyway. There is no talk of conquering fear or feeling confident — only the simple act of moving forward while still afraid. That honesty is what makes the quote useful. Most of us wait for fear to pass before we act, but fear rarely passes on its own; it passes because we act. Courage, Wayne suggests, is not a quality you possess before you begin. It is what you discover in yourself once you have begun.
