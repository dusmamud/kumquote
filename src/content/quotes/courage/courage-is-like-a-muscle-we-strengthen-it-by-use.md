Gordon gives courage a practical shape: it is not a trait you are born with or without, but a muscle that grows with exercise. Every small brave act — speaking up once, facing one fear, taking one risk — makes the next one easier. And like a muscle, it weakens when unused; the longer we avoid fear, the harder it becomes to face. The good news is that the training starts small. Use courage daily, in little things, and it will be there when the big things ask for it.
