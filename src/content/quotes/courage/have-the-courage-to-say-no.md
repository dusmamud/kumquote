Saying no is harder than it looks, because it disappoints people and risks disapproval. Facing the truth is harder still, because it may show us a fault we would rather ignore. This quote ties both acts to a single standard: doing what is right simply because it is right, not because it is rewarded or popular. That kind of courage is quiet and daily. It shows up in small refusals, honest admissions, and choices no one applauds. Over time, those choices build a life we can stand behind without excuses.
