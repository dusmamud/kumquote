Atticus Finch defines courage in the simplest terms: beginning anyway. Real courage is not winning, and it is not the absence of fear. It is knowing you are beaten before you start — and starting regardless. Most of life's important battles are like this: the odds are against us, the outcome is uncertain, and quitting would be easier. Courage is the decision to see it through no matter what. It is quiet, stubborn, and unglamorous. But it is the only kind of courage that ever changes anything.
