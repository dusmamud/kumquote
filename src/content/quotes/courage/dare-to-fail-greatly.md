Great achievements and great failures share the same doorway: risk. The person who never risks failing can only attempt safe, small things, and small attempts produce small results. To achieve greatly, you must be willing to fail greatly, to put real effort and reputation on the line for something that might not work. That willingness is what separates bold lives from careful ones. Failure is not the opposite of achievement here; it is the price of admission. Dare greatly, and even the failures teach you how to reach further next time.
