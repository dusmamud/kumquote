We usually praise the courage of speaking out, and rightly so: saying an unpopular truth takes nerve. But this quote reminds us that listening takes courage too. To sit down, stay quiet, and truly hear another person means risking that we might be wrong, that our mind might change, or that we might have to admit a mistake. Both kinds of courage matter. One defends what we believe; the other keeps us open to learning. A person who can do both is harder to fool and easier to trust.
