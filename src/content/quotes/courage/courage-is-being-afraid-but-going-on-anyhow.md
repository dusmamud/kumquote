The simplest definition of courage is also the truest: feel the fear, and go on anyway. No hero waits until fear disappears, because it never fully does. The frightened heart still beats, the hands still tremble, and yet the feet keep moving. Going on anyhow is not recklessness; it is the disciplined choice to let purpose outweigh panic. Each step taken while afraid weakens fear's hold and strengthens our own. Over time, this practice becomes a quiet confidence, not the absence of fear, but the knowledge that fear does not get the final say.
