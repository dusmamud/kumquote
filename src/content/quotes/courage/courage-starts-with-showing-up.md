We often picture courage as a grand, dramatic act, but most of the time it is much quieter: walking into the room, raising a hand, telling the truth about what we feel. Showing up means refusing to hide behind excuses, perfection, or silence, even when being seen feels risky. It does not guarantee success, applause, or approval. What it guarantees is that we are actually in the game instead of watching from the sidelines. Courage begins the moment we stop waiting to feel fearless and simply appear, honestly and as we are.
