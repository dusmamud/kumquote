Shedd's image is gentle but firm: safety is not the purpose of a ship, and comfort is not the purpose of a life. A ship in harbor is protected and useless, fulfilling none of the purpose it was built for. We face the same choice — stay in the safe harbor of the familiar, or sail toward what we were made to do. The sea carries risk, but the harbor carries a quieter loss: a life never used. Courage is the decision to leave the harbor, knowing the voyage is the point.
