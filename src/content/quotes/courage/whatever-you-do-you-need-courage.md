No decision escapes criticism. Choose one path and someone will say you should have chosen another; stay still and someone will call you timid. Since disapproval is unavoidable, it is a poor guide for living. Courage means deciding by your own judgement and values, then standing by the choice while critics speak. Their voices are not evidence that you are wrong — they are simply proof that you acted. Listen to wise counsel, learn from fair criticism, but do not let the fear of being judged make your decisions for you. Every meaningful course requires courage.
