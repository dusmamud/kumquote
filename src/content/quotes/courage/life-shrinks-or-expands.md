Anais Nin draws a direct link between courage and the size of a life. Fear makes the world smaller: we avoid new places, difficult conversations, and unfamiliar people, and our world quietly contracts around us. Courage does the opposite: each brave act opens a new room in life — a friendship, an opportunity, a skill, a place we had never dared to go. The size of our life is therefore not fixed by circumstance; it is set by how much courage we are willing to practise. Small courage makes a small life, and great courage makes a large one.
