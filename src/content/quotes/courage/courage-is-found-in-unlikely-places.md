Tolkien, who sent small hobbits to do the work of great heroes, knew that courage rarely looks the way we expect. It is not the loud boast of the strong, but the quiet resolve of the ordinary person who refuses to turn away. When we look for bravery only in heroes, we miss it in ourselves — in the neighbour who speaks up, the friend who endures, the stranger who helps. Courage hides in unlikely places, and often the most unlikely place of all is within us.
