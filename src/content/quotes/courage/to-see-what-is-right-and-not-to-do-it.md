Confucius locates courage not in battle but in the quiet moment of knowing. Most failures of courage are not failures of knowledge; we usually know what is right long before we do it. The gap between seeing and acting is where courage lives. It takes little bravery to agree with the good in private and much more to stand for it when it costs something. Knowing the right thing and doing nothing is not neutrality; it is a choice. Courage, in this view, is simply knowledge that has grown legs and started walking.
