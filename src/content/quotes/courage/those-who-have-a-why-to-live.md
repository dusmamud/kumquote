Nietzsche's line, quoted by Viktor Frankl, gives suffering a purpose. A 'why' — a reason to live, a person to love, a work to finish — makes almost any 'how' bearable. People endure extraordinary hardship when they know why they are enduring it. Without a why, even small difficulties feel unbearable. Courage is not the absence of suffering; it is suffering with meaning. Find your why, hold it clearly, and the hardest days become survivable. The question is not whether life will be hard, but whether it will be worth it.
