Most of us wait for a guarantee before we begin, wanting proof that our effort will succeed, but no such proof exists at the beginning of anything worthwhile. Courage is the willingness to commit anyway, to take the first step while the outcome is still unknown. The guarantee we want would make courage unnecessary; it is precisely the absence of certainty that makes beginning brave. Once we start, we learn, adapt, and find our way, but none of that happens until we dare to begin without knowing how it ends.
