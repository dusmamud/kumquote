Twain dismisses the scoreboard before the contest begins. Size, title, resources, and reputation are the dog; the fight is the will inside it, and will does not always follow size. History is full of smaller fighters who outlasted larger ones simply because they refused to quit first. The line is a reminder not to count yourself out before you start, and not to count an opponent in before they have proven themselves. What matters is not how big you look standing still but how hard you are willing to keep going.
