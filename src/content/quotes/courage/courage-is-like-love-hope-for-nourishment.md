Courage cannot survive on willpower alone; it needs hope to feed it. Like love, it withers when starved of belief that things can improve. A soldier, an artist, or a patient all draw strength from the same source: the quiet conviction that effort matters and tomorrow can be better. Without hope, even the bravest heart grows tired, because there is nothing to fight for. With hope, ordinary people endure extraordinary hardship. Nourish hope, then, by remembering past victories, by keeping good company, and by holding a clear picture of the future you want. Courage will follow where hope leads.
