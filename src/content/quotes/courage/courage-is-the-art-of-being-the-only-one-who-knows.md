Wilson reveals the secret of the brave: they are frightened too, they are simply better at hiding it. Courage is not the absence of fear but the art of carrying it without letting it show — steady hands, a calm voice, and one foot in front of the other. The audience sees confidence; only the performer knows the terror backstage. That is the whole art: fear does not disappear, it is managed, and managing it well enough that others can lean on you is what makes a person brave.
