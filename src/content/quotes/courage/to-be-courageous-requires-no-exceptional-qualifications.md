Courage is not reserved for the strong, the trained, or the lucky. It asks for no special qualifications, only the willingness to act when the moment arrives, and that moment comes to everyone sooner or later. A quiet decision to speak up, help a stranger, or stand by a principle can be as courageous as any battlefield act. The opportunity is democratic; it visits the ordinary as surely as the great. What matters is recognition: seeing the moment for what it is, and seizing it. Courage is not a gift for the few, but a choice offered to all.
