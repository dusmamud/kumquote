Chanel names a courage that requires no battlefield: thinking for yourself, aloud. Private independence is easy; speaking an unpopular thought in public risks judgment, exclusion, and ridicule. Yet societies change only when someone says aloud what others merely think. The word "still" suggests this has always been the hardest courage, in every era. To think for yourself aloud is to accept being misunderstood for a while, in exchange for honesty. It is the courage behind every other kind: the refusal to let the crowd do your thinking.
