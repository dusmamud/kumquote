Growth has an uncomfortable price: it requires us to be bad at things before we can be good at them. Taking chances means accepting that mistakes are not a sign of failure but the normal cost of learning. Moore's advice is practical as well as brave. Each mistake, honestly faced, teaches something that success alone cannot. We grow not by avoiding errors but by collecting them, learning from them, and trying again with better judgment. Courage, in this light, is simply the willingness to be a beginner, again and again.
