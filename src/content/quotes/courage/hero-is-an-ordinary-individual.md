Reeve redefines heroism by removing the cape. A hero is not born special but becomes so by enduring what would stop most people. The strength he describes is not a single dramatic act but the daily decision to keep going when the obstacles feel overwhelming. After his own accident, Reeve spoke from experience: perseverance is a choice made again each morning. Ordinary people become heroes quietly, not by avoiding hardship, but by refusing to surrender to it one day at a time.
