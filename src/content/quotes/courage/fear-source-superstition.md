When people are afraid, they reach for easy explanations and easy enemies. Superstition offers false certainty, and cruelty offers false control, and both grow out of fear. A frightened mind prefers a simple story to an honest one, even if the story is untrue and the cruelty is unjust. The cure is not more fear but more courage: the courage to face uncertainty, to admit what we do not know, and to treat others decently anyway. Clear thinking and kindness both begin where fear ends.
