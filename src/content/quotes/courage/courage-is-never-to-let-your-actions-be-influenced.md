Fear is a feeling, and feelings deserve to be noticed, but they make poor guides for action. When fear decides for us, we avoid the conversation, skip the opportunity, and stay in the place that is safe but too small. Courage is not the struggle to stop feeling afraid; it is the decision to act on what we believe is right while the fear is still there. The feeling may shout, but it does not have to steer. Over time, acting in spite of fear teaches us something fear never will: that we are stronger than the danger we imagined.
