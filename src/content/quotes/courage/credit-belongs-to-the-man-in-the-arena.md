Critics sit safely outside the arena, pointing out every stumble while risking nothing themselves. The person in the arena, face marred by dust and sweat, has already done the brave thing: they showed up and tried. Credit belongs to them, not because they always win, but because they dared greatly, knowing failure was possible. This is a liberating truth. The opinions of those who never risk anything carry little weight, while the effort of those who try carries all of it. Step into the arena, accept the dust, and let your courage, not the critics, write the story.
