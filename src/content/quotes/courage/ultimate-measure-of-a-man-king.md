King argues that character is revealed not in easy times but in hard ones. Anyone can look principled when comfort and convenience agree with doing right; the real test comes when standing up costs something, when the crowd disagrees or the consequences are real. Moments of challenge and controversy strip away performance and show what a person truly values. It is there, under pressure, that courage and conviction are measured. The quote asks us to judge ourselves and others not by fair-weather behavior but by the stands we take when standing is difficult, because that is where character actually lives.
