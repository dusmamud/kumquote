Huffington speaks from experience: fearlessness is not a gift of the fearless few, but a habit built by repetition. The first time you speak up, start something, or stand alone, it feels unnatural and frightening. The tenth time, it feels like something you simply do. Fear does not vanish, but it stops being the deciding voice. Each exercise of courage rewires the habit, until the brave response becomes the natural one. Start small, repeat often, and fearlessness grows. The more you practise, the less fear decides for you.
