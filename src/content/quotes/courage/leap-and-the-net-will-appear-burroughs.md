Burroughs offers a short promise for anyone standing at the edge of a big decision: jump, and support will appear. We want to see the net before we leap, but life rarely works that way; the net is woven by the leap itself, by the people and chances that only show up once we commit. Waiting for certainty keeps us standing on the cliff forever, while leaping teaches us we are more resourceful than we believed. The net is not a guarantee of a soft landing but trust in our own ability to adapt. Courage is the leap itself.
