Cohen describes courage as invisible. It is not applause or display, but the quiet act of returning to what you threw away: a hope, a dream, a belief you abandoned. Others cannot see this work because it happens inside, without witnesses. Courage is the decision to pick up the discarded thing and try again. The quote honours private bravery. The most courageous moments are often the ones nobody notices, when you choose to come back for what matters. Nobody applauds the return, but the person who comes back knows exactly what it cost.
