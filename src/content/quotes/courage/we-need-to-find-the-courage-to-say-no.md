De Angelis names a courage we rarely celebrate: the courage to say no. We praise the bravery of taking on challenges, but refusing what drains us — an overcommitment, a toxic relationship, a demand that costs us ourselves — takes equal nerve. Saying yes is easy; it pleases people and avoids conflict. Saying no risks disappointment and disapproval. Yet every no to what does not serve us is a yes to the life we actually want. Boundaries are not selfishness; they are self-respect in action.
