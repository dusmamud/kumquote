We admire honesty, kindness, generosity, and justice, yet each of these virtues requires courage to practise when it is costly. Telling the truth can risk approval. Being kind can risk being hurt. Standing for justice can risk comfort and safety. Without courage, our values remain ideas we hold in private but abandon under pressure. Angelou places courage first because it protects all the other virtues. It is the strength that lets us act on what we believe, even when the moment is hard.
