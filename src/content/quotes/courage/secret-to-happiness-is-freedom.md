Thucydides chains three ideas together in a short, powerful logic. Happiness requires freedom, because a life ruled by fear or oppression cannot be truly happy. Freedom requires courage, because it must be defended, claimed, and sometimes fought for. Put together, the secret of happiness is not comfort or wealth but the bravery to live freely. That means speaking honestly, choosing our own path, and refusing to trade liberty for safety. The chain is unforgiving: lose courage, lose freedom, lose happiness. Guard the first link, and the rest follows.
