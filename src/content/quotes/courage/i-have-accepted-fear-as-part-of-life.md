Erica Jong does not pretend fear goes away. She accepts it as a permanent part of life, especially the fear that comes with change, and then describes what courage looks like: moving forward while the heart pounds and every instinct says turn back. This is a more honest picture than the myth of the fearless hero. The pounding heart is not proof that we should stop; it is the body's alarm, and it rings loudest exactly when something matters. Courage, in Jong's telling, is not the absence of fear but the decision to walk with it rather than obey it.
