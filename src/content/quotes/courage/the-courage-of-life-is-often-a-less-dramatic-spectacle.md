Kennedy distinguishes the courage of life from the courage of a final moment. Everyday courage is less dramatic but no less magnificent: doing what you must despite personal consequences, obstacles, dangers, and pressures. It is the courage of the parent, the worker, the citizen who keeps going. This steady bravery is the basis of all human morality, because character is built in ordinary days, not only in crises. The quote honours the quiet courage that sustains a whole life. It is the courage nobody films and nobody applauds, and it is the courage that matters most.
