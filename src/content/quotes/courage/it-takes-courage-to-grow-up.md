Cummings names one of the quietest forms of courage: becoming yourself. Growing up is often described as fitting in — learning the rules, pleasing others, and wearing the mask that is expected. But real maturity is the opposite: shedding those masks and living as the person you truly are. That takes courage because the world rewards conformity and punishes difference. People may misunderstand you, or disapprove, or walk away. Yet the alternative is worse: a life spent pretending. To grow up, in Cummings' sense, is to choose honesty over approval, again and again.
