Dreams are tender things, and sharing them means risking doubt, laughter, or indifference from people whose opinion matters to us. That is why so many dreams stay hidden, safe but untested. Bombeck names the courage in opening up: to show someone what we truly want is to trust them with a part of ourselves. The reward is real, though, because a dream spoken aloud becomes easier to pursue, and the right listener can offer help, belief, or simply the encouragement to begin. Courage here is not about facing danger, but about being honestly seen.
