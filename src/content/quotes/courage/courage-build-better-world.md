It is easy to believe the world is fixed, that its problems are too old and too large for us to touch. This line answers that resignation with warmth and urgency. It does not deny how hard the work is; it simply says the time has not run out. Building a better world is not a task for heroes alone but for ordinary friends, neighbours and citizens acting together. Courage here is the refusal to give up on improvement. As long as we are alive, it is not too late to make something fairer and kinder than what we found.
