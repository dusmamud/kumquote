Thucydides, recording Pericles, links three great ideas in a chain: happiness needs freedom, and freedom needs courage. We cannot be happy while living in fear, because fear narrows every choice. And we cannot be free without the courage to speak, to act, and to stand apart when needed. Courage is therefore not a luxury for heroes; it is the foundation of a good life for everyone. Each brave act, however small, widens the space in which happiness can grow. Freedom is the reward of courage, and happiness is the reward of freedom.
