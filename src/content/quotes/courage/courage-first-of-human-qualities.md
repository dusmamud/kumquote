Courage comes first because every other good quality depends on it. Honesty means little if fear stops you telling the truth, and kindness means little if fear stops you acting on it. A person may know what is right, yet still stay silent when speaking up carries a cost. Courage is what turns private conviction into public action. It does not mean feeling no fear; it means moving forward while fear is present. Once that first step is taken, patience, integrity and generosity finally have room to appear. Without courage, the rest remain wishes rather than habits.
