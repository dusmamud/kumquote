Fear is often misread as a warning to stop, when it is really a sign that something matters to you. We fear the things we care about: the interview for the job we want, the conversation that could change a relationship, the leap toward a dream. If nothing were at stake, there would be nothing to fear. So instead of treating fear as an enemy, treat it as a compass — it points toward what is worth doing. Feel the fear, recognise what it is telling you, and go forward anyway. The things worth having are usually on the far side of fear.
