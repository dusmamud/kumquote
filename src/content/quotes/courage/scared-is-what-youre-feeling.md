Donoghue separates the feeling from the action with surgical precision. Being scared is not a flaw; it is simply what fear feels like in the body. Being brave is not the absence of that feeling but what you do while it is happening. Too many people wait to stop feeling afraid before they act, and they wait forever. Courage means moving forward with shaky hands and a racing heart. The fear may stay for the whole journey, and that is fine. Brave is the verb, not the feeling, and it belongs to anyone willing to move.
