Anne Frank, writing in hiding at fifteen, understood something many adults miss: you do not need permission or perfect conditions to begin improving the world. Nobody need wait a single moment — not for the war to end, not for the right time, not for someone else to act first. Improvement starts with the smallest step, taken now, where you are. A kind word, a shared meal, a truth spoken — these are the beginnings. The world changes one small act at a time, and the first act can be yours, today.
