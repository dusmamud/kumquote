Billy Graham observes that courage spreads like any other strong emotion. One person's stand gives others permission to stand too; spines stiffen across a room when a single person refuses to bend. This is why public courage matters so much — it is never only personal. The brave act we admire in private becomes, when witnessed, an invitation to others. Fear is also contagious, which makes the choice of which contagion to spread a moral one. A single stand can change the posture of everyone watching.
