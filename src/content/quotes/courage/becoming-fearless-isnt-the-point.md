Veronica Roth corrects a common misunderstanding about bravery: the goal is not to become fearless, because that is impossible. Fear is wired into us, and chasing its absence only leaves us disappointed and stuck. The real work is learning to control fear — to feel it, name it, and choose anyway — until it no longer decides for us. Freedom, in this view, is not a life without fear but a life where fear has lost its vote. Roth's insight is liberating: we can stop waiting to feel brave and start practicing the small acts of control that make bravery real.
