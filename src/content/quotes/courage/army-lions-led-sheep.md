Strength is not only about numbers or natural talent; it is about the spirit at the front. A group of brave people led by a timid leader will hesitate, while ordinary people led by someone bold will find courage they did not know they had. Leadership sets the tone for everyone behind it. One courageous person at the head can turn fear into resolve, and one fearful leader can drain the fight from the strongest team. The lesson is clear: if you want a brave group, start by being brave yourself, because courage flows downward from the front.
