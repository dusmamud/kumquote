Heinlein corrects a popular misunderstanding: a fearless person cannot be courageous, because courage needs fear to exist. If nothing frightens you, there is nothing to overcome, and bravery is just temperament. Courage begins exactly where fear does, in the moment you feel it and act anyway. The two are companions, not enemies, and the presence of fear is proof that courage is possible, not that it is absent. So stop waiting to feel fearless. Feel the fear, recognize it as the doorway, and walk through it like a person who knows what courage actually is.
