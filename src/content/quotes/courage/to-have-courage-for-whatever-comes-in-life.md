Life does not ask our permission before bringing hardship; it arrives unannounced, in illness, loss, and sudden change. What matters is not avoiding these trials, which no one can, but meeting them with courage. Teresa's insight is that everything lies in this single readiness: to face whatever comes, without knowing what it will be. Courage here is not a single act but a posture, a settled willingness to endure and adapt. With it, even the hardest days become bearable, because we meet them standing. Without it, even small troubles overwhelm us. Everything, truly, lies in that.
