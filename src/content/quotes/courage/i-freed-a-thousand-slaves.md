Tubman's words carry the weight of her life. She freed a thousand people from slavery, yet knew that many more remained in chains — not only physical chains, but the chains of believing they were not free. Freedom begins in the mind: until people know they are slaves, they cannot seek liberation. Her courage was not only in the dangerous journeys, but in the belief that freedom was possible for everyone. The greatest chains are the ones we cannot see. Breaking them starts with knowing they are there.
