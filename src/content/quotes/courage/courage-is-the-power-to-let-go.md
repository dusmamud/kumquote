Letting go is where courage actually lives. The familiar feels safe even when it no longer serves us — a job that drains us, a habit that holds us back, a story we have outgrown. Holding on costs nothing in the moment, which is exactly why it is so tempting. Lindquist reminds us that bravery is not always a dramatic leap; often it is the quiet decision to release what we know so we can reach for what we might become. Growth begins the moment we loosen our grip on the comfortable and trust ourselves to handle the unknown.
