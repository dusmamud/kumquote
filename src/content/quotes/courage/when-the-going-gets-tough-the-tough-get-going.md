Kennedy describes what toughness actually looks like: not the absence of difficulty but the response to it. When things go smoothly, almost anyone can keep going; the test arrives when they do not. The tough are not those who never struggle but those who start moving precisely when movement is hardest. Getting going is a choice made in the worst moment, not a mood that waits for better weather. Difficulty, in this view, is a filter: it separates those who were only comfortable from those who were truly committed to the road ahead.
