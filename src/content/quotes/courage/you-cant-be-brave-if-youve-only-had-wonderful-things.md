Moore's line is a gentle truth about how bravery is made. If everything has been wonderful — easy, comfortable, safe — you have never had the chance to be brave, because bravery appears only when something is hard. It is a reminder that courage is forged in difficulty, not comfort. The struggles you wish to avoid teach you what you are capable of. A person who has faced hardship and kept going knows something a sheltered person does not. Do not resent the hard chapters. They prove — to yourself most of all — that you are braver than you believed.
