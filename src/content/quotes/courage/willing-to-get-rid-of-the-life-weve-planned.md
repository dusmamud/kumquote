Campbell names the hardest trade in a lifetime: releasing a plan we have loved for years in exchange for a life we cannot yet see. The planned life feels safe because it is familiar, even when it has stopped fitting. The waiting life is frightening because it is unknown, yet it is the one that actually belongs to us. Courage here is not dramatic but quiet, the willingness to let go of a good story for a true one. Every new chapter begins with the same act: loosening our grip on the old plan.
