Herbert's line from Dune has become a mantra against fear. Fear is the mind-killer because it clouds judgement and paralyses action. The speaker resolves to face fear, let it pass through, and see what remains when it is gone. Only the self endures. The quote offers a practical method: do not fight fear head-on, but observe it, let it move through you, and continue. Fear is temporary; the person who outlasts it is not. Let fear pass through, keep your footing, and you will find yourself still standing on the other side.
