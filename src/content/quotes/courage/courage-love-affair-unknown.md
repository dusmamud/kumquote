Most of us treat the unknown as a threat, something to avoid or control. This line suggests the opposite relationship: fall in love with it. The unknown holds every new beginning, every surprise, and every version of ourselves we have not yet met. Courage, in this view, is not grim endurance but attraction, a willingness to step toward mystery with curiosity instead of dread. When we love what we do not know, fear loses its grip. Life becomes an exploration rather than a fortress, and each uncertain step feels like the start of a romance with possibility.
