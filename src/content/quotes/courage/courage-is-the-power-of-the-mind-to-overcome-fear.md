King describes courage as the power of the mind to overcome fear. Fear is a feeling, but it does not have to be the ruler. The mind can examine fear, name it, and choose to act despite it. This is not denial but discipline: thinking clearly in the presence of danger. The quote locates courage in our inner life, where it can be trained like any other strength. When the mind refuses to be mastered by fear, the person is free to act rightly.
