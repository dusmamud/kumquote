Standing with the crowd costs nothing; the group carries us, and no one notices us individually. Standing alone is different: every eye is on you, and the pressure to conform is heavy. Gandhi knew that the most important stands in life are often lonely ones — telling an unpopular truth, refusing an unjust order, or choosing the right path when everyone else chooses the easy one. Courage is not loud; it is the quiet decision to stay standing when stepping back would be simpler. If you believe something is right, stand for it, even alone.
