Bravery is not the same as immortality, and no amount of courage can guarantee a long life. But caution offers no safety either; a life spent avoiding every risk is a life barely lived. The cautious may escape danger, yet they also escape adventure, discovery, and the deep satisfaction of trying. Living fully means accepting risk as the price of experience. Better to attempt something bold and fail than to spend a lifetime wondering what might have been. The reward for courage is a life fully lived.
