Facing an enemy is frightening, but facing a friend takes a different kind of bravery. With an enemy, you expect opposition; with a friend, you risk the relationship itself — their anger, their disappointment, perhaps losing them. Yet true friendship needs honesty more than agreement. Telling a friend they are wrong, or refusing to go along with something harmful, is an act of loyalty, not betrayal. The friends worth keeping are the ones who can hear a hard truth and respect you for it. Stand up to your friends when it matters; that is how trust is truly built.
