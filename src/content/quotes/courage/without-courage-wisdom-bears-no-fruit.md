Gracián warns that wisdom without courage is a beautiful tool never taken out of its box. You can know the right decision, see the danger clearly, and understand exactly what must be done, and still do nothing. Knowledge alone changes no outcomes; action does. Courage is the bridge between seeing and doing, between a wise plan and a real result. The world is full of wise people who never acted. Do not join them. Let wisdom point the way, then let courage carry you across, because a truth never acted upon bears no fruit at all.
