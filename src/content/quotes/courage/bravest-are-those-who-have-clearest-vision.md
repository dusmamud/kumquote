Real bravery is not blindness to danger; it is seeing the danger clearly and going forward anyway. The person who rushes in unaware may be reckless, but the one who counts the cost, understands the risk, and still chooses to act is truly brave. Clear vision makes courage harder, because it removes the comfort of ignorance, yet it also makes courage meaningful. We admire such people because their choice is deliberate, not accidental. In our own lives, the same applies: understand what is at stake, weigh the glory and the danger honestly, and then step forward with eyes open.
