Every day offers a choice between courage and comfort, and it is tempting to believe we can have both. Comfort asks for safety, silence, and the familiar path. Courage asks for honesty, risk, and the willingness to be seen. Choosing one means letting go of the other, at least in that moment. A life built entirely on comfort stays small, while a life shaped by courage keeps growing. The two cannot share the same decision, so we must decide which one we truly want.
