Fear comes like a flood — sudden, powerful, and able to sweep away our best intentions. King knew this from experience, and his answer was not to pretend fear did not exist, but to build against it deliberately. Courage, in his image, is not a feeling that arrives on its own; it is a structure we construct, day by day, through small acts of standing firm. Each time we speak up, show up, or refuse to retreat, we add another stone to the dike. Build your courage before you need it, and fear will not carry you away.
