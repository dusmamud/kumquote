Baldwin states a hard truth about change: nothing changes until it is faced. We can avoid a problem for years, but avoidance is not a solution — it is a delay that makes the problem larger. Facing something does not guarantee we can fix it, but it is the only way fixing becomes possible. Courage is the willingness to look directly at what is true: the mistake, the conflict, the fear, the loss. What we face, we can begin to change. What we refuse to face owns us from the shadows.
