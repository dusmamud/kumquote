Courage is often treated as a private victory, but its effects spread further. When one person speaks honestly, admits a mistake, or tries something hard, others watching feel a little safer to do the same. Bravery is contagious in the best way: it lowers the cost of honesty for everyone nearby. This idea reminds us that our choices are never only about us. Each time we choose courage over comfort, we give the people around us permission to be braver too, and that small ripple is how a family, a workplace, or a community becomes more open and alive.
