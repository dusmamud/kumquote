Lorde's declaration is short and absolute: deliberate, and afraid of nothing. It is not a claim that fear does not exist, but a decision about how to live with it. To be deliberate is to choose your steps on purpose, rather than drifting where fear pushes you. Fear shrinks the world; deliberateness widens it. When you decide what matters and move toward it steadily, fear loses its authority. You may still feel afraid — but fear no longer decides. That is what courage looks like: a person walking deliberately toward what matters, afraid of nothing.
