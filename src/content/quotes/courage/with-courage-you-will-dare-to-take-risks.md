Courage is not only about facing danger; it is the root of every other virtue. It takes courage to take a risk, to be kind when cruelty is easier, and to admit you were wrong when pride says stay silent. Without it, integrity collapses at the first pressure, and compassion retreats at the first cost. That is why courage must be practised in small things: speaking honestly, standing up for someone, choosing the right path when it is unpopular. Each brave act strengthens the foundation. Build courage daily, and honesty, kindness, and humility will stand firmly upon it.
