Fear arrives uninvited, a reflex of the mind when danger appears. It is automatic, and feeling it says nothing about a person's character. Courage is different: it is a decision made in full awareness of the fear, to act anyway. No one can choose whether fear appears, but everyone can choose what happens next. That choice, repeated in small moments and large ones, is what turns ordinary people into brave ones. Bravery is not the absence of fear but the decision that something else matters more.
