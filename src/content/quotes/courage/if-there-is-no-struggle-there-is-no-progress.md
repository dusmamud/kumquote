Douglass speaks from a life of struggle: progress without struggle does not exist. Every freedom, every right, every improvement was won by people willing to endure difficulty. Struggle is not a sign that something is wrong; it is the price of anything worth having. Those who avoid all struggle also avoid all growth. The rain and the storm are part of the harvest. When we accept struggle as the path rather than the obstacle, we stop resenting it and start using it. Progress belongs to those who keep going through it.
