Wanting something is only the first step; saying it out loud is where the real courage begins. Many people keep their desires quiet because naming them makes rejection possible, and silence feels safer than hearing no. But nobody can help us, hire us, love us, or agree with us if they never know what we are asking for. The world cannot answer a question that was never asked. Speaking plainly does not guarantee we will get it, yet it is the only way to give ourselves a chance, and asking gets easier every time.
