Gandhi links two qualities we rarely connect: love and courage. It is easy to be kind when everything is safe, but real love often demands risk: speaking an unpopular truth, forgiving an enemy, or standing with someone others have abandoned. The coward cannot pay that price, so love stays small and conditional. The brave can love widely because they are willing to face the consequences. Love, then, is not soft or passive; it is one of the bravest acts a person can perform. To love deeply, first find the courage to be vulnerable.
