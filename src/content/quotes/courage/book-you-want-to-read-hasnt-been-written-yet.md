Morrison turns courage into creativity. If the book you need does not exist, do not wait for someone else to write it — write it yourself. The same is true of every missing thing: the change, the conversation, the project, the apology. Waiting for permission is the opposite of courage. The world is full of unwritten books and undone work, waiting for someone willing to begin. You do not need to be the most qualified person in the room. You only need to be the one who starts. The book begins with a single sentence.
