Angelou says courage begins with standing up for yourself, then grows into standing up for somebody else. Self-respect comes first: without it, we cannot help anyone. But courage does not stop at self-defence. The person who has found their voice can lend it to those who have not yet found theirs. The quote traces a path from personal bravery to solidarity. Stand up for yourself, then turn and stand beside others. That is how courage multiplies. One voice raised for another is how the timid become brave and the silent find their words.
