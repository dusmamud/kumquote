Freedom is not handed out to those who wait politely for permission; it belongs to those willing to act boldly in spite of the risk. Playing it safe keeps us comfortable, but comfort slowly becomes a cage, and the life we wanted stays just out of reach. Boldness does not mean recklessness; it means choosing to speak, to try, and to stand up when staying quiet would be easier. Each bold step widens the space we are free to live in. The price of freedom is the courage to take the first step before we feel ready.
