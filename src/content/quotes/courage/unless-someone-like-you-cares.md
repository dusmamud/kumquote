The Lorax's warning is a call to courage: nothing improves unless someone cares enough to act. Waiting for others to fix things is comfortable, but it changes nothing. The world gets better one caring person at a time — someone who picks up the litter, speaks up, plants the tree, or simply refuses to look away. You do not need to be powerful or famous; you only need to care a whole awful lot. Caring is the first brave act. Everything else — the work, the change, the hope — follows from it.
