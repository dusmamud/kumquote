Fear is not the opposite of courage; it is the material courage is made from. A person who feels no fear faces no real test, while the one who trembles and still steps forward proves something genuine. This line reframes bravery as action taken in the presence of fear, not after fear has gone. Waiting until we feel fearless only postpones the work that matters most. Growth lives on the far side of the frightening step, so do the scary thing anyway. Repeated often enough, that choice turns courage from a rare gift into a steady habit.
