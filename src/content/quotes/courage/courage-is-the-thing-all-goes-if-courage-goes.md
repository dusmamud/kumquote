Talent, wealth, and plans all matter, but without courage they collapse at the first real test. A person can possess every advantage and still achieve nothing if fear keeps them from acting. Courage is the thing because it is the foundation on which everything else stands: it turns ability into action, and intention into result. When courage goes, ambition fades, honesty wavers, and dreams quietly die. Guard it, therefore, as the most precious quality you own. Feed it with small brave acts daily, and it will hold firm when the great tests come.
