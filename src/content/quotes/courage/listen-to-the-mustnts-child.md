Silverstein's poem is a lesson in courage for children — and for adults too. The mustn'ts, don'ts, and shouldn'ts are the voices that tell us what is impossible. They sound like wisdom, but they are often just fear wearing a sensible coat. Real courage begins when we listen close to a different voice: the one that says anything can happen. The world is full of people who were told no and did it anyway. Listen to the warnings, by all means — but do not let them be the last voice you hear.
