Paulo Coelho warns that life's real tests rarely arrive on schedule. They come without warning, in moments we did not prepare for, and they measure two things at once: our courage and our willingness to change. That pairing matters, because courage without change is just endurance, and change without courage never begins. The challenge is not a punishment but a question — will we stay where it is safe, or step into something new? Coelho's point is gentle but firm: the test will come whether we are ready or not, and how we answer it shapes everything that follows.
