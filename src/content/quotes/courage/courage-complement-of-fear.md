Courage only has meaning because fear exists first. Without something to be afraid of, boldness costs nothing and proves nothing, so the fearless person can never be called brave. This quote treats the two as partners rather than enemies: fear supplies the test, and courage supplies the answer. The point is not to eliminate fear, which is a natural signal, but to refuse to let it decide for you. When you act despite a racing heart, you turn a weakness into evidence of character. That is why the bravest people are often the ones who admit they were afraid.
