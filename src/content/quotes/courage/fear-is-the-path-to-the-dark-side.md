Yoda traces a chain that starts small and ends in ruin. Fear on its own feels harmless, but left alone it curdles into anger at whatever frightens us; anger hardens into hate; hate becomes suffering for ourselves and everyone near us. The lesson is to catch the chain at its first link. Courage is not the absence of fear but the refusal to let it set the direction. Face the fear early, while it is still only fear, and the rest of the chain never forms. Small fears, faced early, stay small.
