Dr. Seuss gives a child's map for a grown-up truth: you already have what you need. Brains in your head, feet in your shoes — the equipment for choosing your own direction is yours. No one else can steer your life for you, and no one else should. The road will have turns, but the choosing is yours at every step. Courage is not about knowing the destination in advance; it is about trusting yourself to decide, to walk, and to adjust as you go. You are the driver. Start the journey.
