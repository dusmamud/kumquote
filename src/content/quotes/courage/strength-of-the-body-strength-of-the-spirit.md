Tolkien reminds us that the body's strength has limits, while the spirit's strength does not. Muscles tire, age, and fail, but resolve can carry a person through trials no physique could survive. The smallest hobbit can outlast the strongest warrior when the spirit refuses to quit. What counts is not size or power but the inner decision to keep going, to stand firm, and to do the right thing when it costs. Train the body, by all means, but train the spirit harder. In the end, it is the spirit that decides who we are.
