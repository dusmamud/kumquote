Heroes are not made of different material; they simply endure a little longer. The ordinary person and the hero feel the same fear, the same urge to quit, the same tiredness. The difference is five more minutes: one more attempt, one more step, one more honest word when quitting would be easier. That small margin is available to anyone. When you feel you cannot go on, try lasting just five minutes more — you will be surprised how often the fear breaks first. Bravery is not the absence of fear; it is fear outlasted by a few stubborn minutes.
