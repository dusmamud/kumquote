Fear feeds on avoidance: the longer you put off the dreaded task, the larger it grows in your mind. The cure is direct action. Make the call, give the speech, have the conversation — and you will usually find the fear was far bigger than the reality. Fear dies when it meets experience, because experience replaces imagination with fact. You do not need to feel ready or fearless first; you only need to begin. One brave act can end months of worry. Do the thing you fear most, and watch the fear lose its power over you.
