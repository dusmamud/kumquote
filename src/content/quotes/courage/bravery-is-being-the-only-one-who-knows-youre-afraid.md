Brave people often look calm from the outside, which makes others think they feel no fear at all. The truth is usually the opposite: they feel afraid just like everyone else, but they act anyway. Bravery is private in that way; the fear is real, and the choice to move forward happens quietly inside. This idea frees us from waiting to feel fearless before we begin. We do not need our fear to disappear. We only need to carry it with us and take the next step, even while our hands shake and our heart races.
