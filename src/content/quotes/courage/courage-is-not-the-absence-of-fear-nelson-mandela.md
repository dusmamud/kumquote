Mandela is correcting the movie version of courage. In films, the
brave person feels nothing. In real life, Mandela says, the brave
person feels the fear fully — and acts anyway. The fearless person
has nothing to overcome, so their action proves nothing. Courage
begins exactly where fear is present.

That reframes every scary thing you are avoiding. You do not need
to wait until you feel ready, calm, or confident, because those
feelings may never arrive before the act. You need to feel the fear,
name what it is protecting you from, and take the smallest step
that counts as triumph over it. Do that once, and the next fear
gets a little less say in your life.
