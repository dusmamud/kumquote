Every bold step carries a moment of unsteadiness, when the old ground is gone and the new ground is not yet sure. That brief loss of footing is the price of daring, and it frightens many people into staying still. But the quote warns that the greater loss is different: a life spent never daring slowly loses the self, as choices are made by fear instead of desire. We trade a moment of wobble for years of regret. Daring is risky, but refusing to dare is riskier, because it costs the very person we might have become.
