Brave people rarely look the way we expect. From the outside, they seem calm and certain, as though fear had never touched them. Jones reveals the secret: they are afraid, just like everyone else, but they carry it privately and act anyway. Courage is not the absence of fear but the refusal to let fear be visible in our choices. This is strangely encouraging. The audience sees confidence. Only we know the truth, and that private knowledge is the real measure of bravery.
