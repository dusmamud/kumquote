Safety feels comfortable, and a ship tied up in harbor will never sink. But Shedd reminds us that safety is not the purpose of a ship — it was built to cross oceans, face storms, and reach distant shores. The same is true of us. Staying where it is safe protects us from failure, but it also protects us from discovery, growth, and achievement. A life spent only in harbor is a life never truly lived. Courage means leaving the shore knowing the sea is uncertain. The ship was built for the journey, and so were you.
