Fear narrows our thinking, shuts down judgment, and leads to decisions we would never make calmly, a little death of reason that can obliterate everything we are. The remedy in the novel is to face fear directly, let it pass through, and remain standing on the other side. Courage, then, is not the denial of fear but the discipline of letting it move through us without taking command. When we refuse to let fear rule our minds, we keep the very thing it threatens to destroy: ourselves.
