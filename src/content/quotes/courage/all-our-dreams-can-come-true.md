Dreams alone change nothing; it is the pursuit that gives them life. Disney's words are a reminder that every great achievement began as a dream someone was brave enough to chase. The courage to pursue means facing doubt, risking failure, and starting before we feel ready. Many people keep their dreams safely locked away because pursuing them is frightening. But a dream left unpursued slowly fades into regret. The distance between dreaming and doing is courage. Take the first step, however small, and the dream begins to move from imagination toward reality.
