Virgil's line, written two thousand years ago, still explains why bold people seem luckier than cautious ones. Fortune is not a lottery; it rewards those who act, because action creates chances that waiting never does. The brave person applies, speaks, starts, and asks, collecting opportunities while the timid wait for a guarantee that never comes. Bravery does not remove risk, but it multiplies the number of doors that open. Luck, in this view, is simply what happens when courage meets a world full of chances.
