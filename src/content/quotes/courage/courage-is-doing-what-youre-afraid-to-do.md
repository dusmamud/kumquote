Rickenbacker, a fighter pilot who knew fear intimately, defines courage as action in the presence of fear. It is not a trait you are born with or a mood you wait for; it is a decision you make while afraid. The pilot who flies anyway, the speaker who speaks anyway, and the patient who endures anyway all share the same secret: they stopped negotiating with fear and started moving. Fear will shout that you cannot do it, but fear is a poor judge. Do the thing you fear, and you will find courage waiting on the other side.
