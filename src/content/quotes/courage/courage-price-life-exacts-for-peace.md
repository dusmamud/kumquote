Peace is often imagined as the absence of conflict, a quiet state that arrives on its own. In truth, peace is earned through courage: the courage to face fear, speak honestly, and act in spite of uncertainty. Without that price paid, fear rules the heart, and no comfort is truly restful. The calm that follows a brave act is deeper than any comfort bought by avoidance. Those who pay courage's price discover a peace that cannot be shaken by the next storm.
