J.R.R. Tolkien's line, spoken by Galadriel in The Lord of the Rings, is a quiet rebuke to the feeling of being too small to matter. History is usually told as the story of great figures, but it is often changed by ordinary people who refuse to look away — a hobbit carrying a burden, a single act of refusal. The quote's power is that it does not promise success; it promises possibility. The future is not fixed, and its course can turn on the smallest person willing to act. That is why courage belongs to everyone, not only to heroes.
