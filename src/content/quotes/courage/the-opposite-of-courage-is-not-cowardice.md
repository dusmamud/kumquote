The real enemy of courage is rarely panic or running away; it is the quiet pressure to blend in, agree, and do what everyone else is doing. Conformity feels safe because nobody singles out the person who follows the crowd, yet it costs us our own judgment and voice. Standing apart does not require shouting or rebellion for its own sake. It only requires thinking for oneself and being willing to be the one who disagrees. A society where everyone conforms loses the very people who could improve it, so courage often looks like refusing to copy the crowd.
