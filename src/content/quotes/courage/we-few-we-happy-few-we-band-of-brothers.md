Shakespeare gives King Henry a speech that turns a hopeless battle into a privilege. Outnumbered and facing a far larger army, Henry tells his men that being few is not a shame but an honour: those who stand here will be bound to each other forever, and those who stayed home will wish they had come. Courage, in this moment, is shared. No one fights alone; each man draws strength from the man beside him. A band of brothers is not made by numbers but by the choice to stand together when leaving would be easier.
