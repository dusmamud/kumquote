Being brave is often misunderstood as never feeling afraid, but fear is a natural response to danger, and even the boldest people feel it. What sets a brave person apart is not the absence of fear but the decision to act in spite of it. Fear warns us; it does not have to command us. When we face what frightens us, its power shrinks, and confidence grows from surviving difficulty. Courage is therefore a practice, not a trait handed to a lucky few. Each time we conquer a small fear, we prepare ourselves for larger ones ahead.
