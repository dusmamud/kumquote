Campbell warns that the life we planned can become a cage. We cling to our plans because they feel safe, even when a better life is waiting beyond them. Letting go takes courage: it means admitting the plan was only a draft, not a destiny. The life waiting for us may look different from what we imagined, but it is real and ready. The quote invites trust. Release the old map, and step toward the life that is actually calling you.
