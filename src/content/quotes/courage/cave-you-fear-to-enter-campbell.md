Campbell captures a pattern found in myths around the world: the thing we most want lies behind the thing we most fear. The cave is dark and frightening, so we circle it for years, yet the treasure, growth, freedom, or truth we seek, waits inside it. Fear, in this telling, is not a stop sign but a compass, pointing toward the very place we need to go. Entering the cave does not mean the fear vanishes; it means we walk in anyway, and discover that the darkness was guarding the prize.
