Bravery is not a trait reserved for some future, better version of ourselves; it is a choice available today. The wings we fear to extend are already there, folded and waiting, and they only prove their strength when we use them. Trust is the heart of this dare: we cannot know we will fly until we leap, yet the leap itself is what teaches us. Many people wait for certainty before acting, but certainty comes after courage, not before. Dare today, extend your wings, and discover that the air holds you. Flight begins with a single brave decision.
