Rosa Parks speaks from the experience of a decision that changed history. Fear is powerful while we are still deciding, because uncertainty feeds it; every possible consequence crowds the mind at once. But once the mind is made up — once we have committed to what is right — fear loses much of its force. The decision itself becomes a kind of shelter. Parks knew this from her refusal to give up her seat, a choice made long before that day through years of resolve.
