Most of us go through life believing we are more fragile than we really are. We picture the brave as a different kind of person, born fearless, while we wait for a feeling of readiness that never arrives. Carnegie reminds us that courage is not a rare gift handed to a few; it is a reserve already sitting inside ordinary people, discovered only when life demands it. The interview, the apology, the leap into something new — each one draws on strength we did not know we had. Act first, and the courage reveals itself.
