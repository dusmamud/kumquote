Brown names a fear that hides behind many smaller ones. People say they are afraid of failing, of looking foolish, of losing security, but underneath often sits a deeper fear: that life is short and the dream might be the last chance. So they choose the safe life, not because they love it but because it postpones the reckoning. The irony is that the safe life still ends, and the dream dies quietly with it. Courage here means accepting that time is limited and letting that fact push you forward instead of holding you still.
