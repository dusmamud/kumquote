Eriksson, who coached at the highest level, knew that talent rarely decides who succeeds. The greater obstacle sits inside: the fear of failing in front of others. It makes players hesitate, choose the safe pass, and avoid the shot that matters. The same fear keeps the rest of us from starting businesses, applying for jobs, or speaking up. Failure itself is survivable and often instructive; it is the fear of it that paralyses. Remove that fear, or act despite it, and the real barrier falls away.
