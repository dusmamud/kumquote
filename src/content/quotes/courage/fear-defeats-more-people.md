Most dreams do not die because they were impossible. They die because fear talked their owners out of trying. Fear defeats more people than lack of money, talent, or opportunity ever will, because it strikes before the attempt even begins. It convinces us the risk is too great, the timing is wrong, and we are not ready. Most of those fears never come true. Recognising fear as our main opponent changes the battle. We do not need to be the most talented person in the room; we need to be the one who acts despite being afraid.
