Luce places courage at the foundation of character. Kindness, honesty, patience, and generosity are all admirable, but each of them sometimes demands courage to practise. Honesty requires the courage to tell an uncomfortable truth; kindness requires the courage to reach out first; patience requires the courage to wait. Without courage, our other virtues stay locked inside us as good intentions. With it, they climb into action. If you want to grow as a person, do not start by collecting virtues — start by building courage, and the rest will follow. Courage is the first rung, and it lifts everything else.
