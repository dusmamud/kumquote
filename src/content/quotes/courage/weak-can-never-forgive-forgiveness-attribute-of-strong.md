Gandhi turns the usual idea of strength upside down. We often think forgiveness is surrender, something the weak do because they cannot fight back. He says the opposite: holding a grudge is easy, while letting go of it takes real power. Forgiveness does not excuse the wrong or pretend it did not happen; it refuses to let the wrong keep ruling your life. The strong can forgive because they are not afraid of looking soft. It is the weak who must keep hating, because hatred is the only strength they have left.
