Disney calls courage the main quality of leadership, wherever it is exercised. Leading always involves risk, especially in new undertakings, where the outcome is unknown and critics are ready. Without courage, a leader avoids decisions, follows the crowd, and lets fear set the direction. With it, a leader can commit to a vision and carry others through uncertainty. The quote applies beyond business. Anywhere someone must lead, courage is the quality that makes the rest possible. Wherever people must be led, the leader's courage is the first thing they will lean on.
