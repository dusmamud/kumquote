Andrew Jackson's bold claim is that courage outweighs numbers. One person willing to stand, speak, and act can shift a room, a movement, or a nation, because conviction is contagious and most majorities are only waiting for someone to go first. The quote is not about ignoring others but about the power of a single principled stand to break paralysis. History is full of majorities that began as one courageous voice. When we feel outnumbered, the reminder matters: numbers count votes, but courage starts movements.
