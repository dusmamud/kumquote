Before a hard thing happens, our minds rehearse it again, and each rehearsal adds pain that the real event may never bring. The dread of suffering can stretch for weeks, while the suffering itself is often shorter and more bearable than we imagined. Coelho's insight frees us from this trap: much of what we fear is the fear itself, not the thing. When we step forward and meet the difficulty, we usually find it smaller than the shadow it cast. Courage, then, is refusing to let imagined pain keep us from living the real life in front of us.
