We often wait to act until we can see the whole path clearly, every step mapped out and every risk removed. That clarity rarely comes. King describes faith as the courage to take the first step anyway, trusting that the next step will become visible as we move. The staircase appears one step at a time, not all at once. This is true for justice, for personal change, and for any large goal. Waiting for certainty keeps us standing still. Taking the first step, even in darkness, creates movement, learning, and hope.
