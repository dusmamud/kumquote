A flower does not bloom everywhere; it needs good soil, light, and care. Pope Francis suggests gratitude is the same. Nobility here has nothing to do with rank or wealth. It is the quiet dignity of a person who notices goodness and responds to it. Ingratitude, by contrast, grows in neglected soil, where entitlement and resentment take root. Gratitude is therefore both a sign and a practice: a sign of a well-tended inner life, and a practice that keeps that life in bloom.
