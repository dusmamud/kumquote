Cummings opens his poem with thanks before he has named anything to be thankful for, and that order matters. Gratitude here is not a response to good fortune but a way of seeing: the day is amazing because he meets it with an open, thankful attention. Trees, sky, and ordinary light become gifts the moment we notice them. The prayer is short because it does not need to argue its case. Saying thank you first changes the whole day, turning what might have been routine into something worth celebrating.
