Bethke offers a shortcut to joy that costs nothing and takes only a moment. We often search for happiness through achievement, purchases, or grand experiences, but thankfulness reaches it faster, by changing how we see the life we already have. The moment we name something we are grateful for, the mind shifts from scarcity to abundance, and joy follows close behind. No waiting, no earning, no perfect conditions required, only attention to a gift already present. If joy feels far away, Bethke suggests, the quickest path is not forward but inward, toward thankfulness for what is here now.
