Bibesco describes the perfect balance of giving and gratitude. To give without remembering is generosity without scorekeeping — the gift is complete the moment it is given, with no debt recorded. To take without forgetting is gratitude without expiry — kindness received stays remembered and honoured. Most of us get this backwards, remembering every favour we did and forgetting every favour we received. Blessed are those who reverse it, because they live lightly and gratefully at once. That is a freedom money cannot buy and time cannot take away.
