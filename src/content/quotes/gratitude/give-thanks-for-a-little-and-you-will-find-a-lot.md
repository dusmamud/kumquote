Thankfulness changes how much we see we have. When we give thanks for a little — a meal, a friend, a safe night — our eyes open to the abundance already around us. The ungrateful heart always finds something missing, no matter how much it owns; the grateful heart finds plenty in very little. This is not settling for less, but recognising that much of what we call "not enough" is already more than enough. Start with the small things, thank them honestly, and you will discover that your life was richer than you thought.
