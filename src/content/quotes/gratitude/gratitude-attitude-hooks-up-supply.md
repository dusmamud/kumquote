Proctor's image is electrical: gratitude is the connection, and without it the current cannot flow. A person who feels entitled to everything and thankful for nothing is disconnected from the very sources that sustain them — other people, opportunities, and their own good fortune. Gratitude plugs us back in. It makes us notice the help we receive, which in turn makes others more willing to help again. Attitude is the switch, and gratitude is the current that runs through a well-lived life.
