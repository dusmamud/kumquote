Every day arrives only once. No morning has ever looked exactly like this one, and no evening will repeat it. Angelou greets each day with wonder, as if it were a gift never seen before. That attitude turns ordinary days into something precious. When we treat today as new, we notice more: the light, the faces, the chances that are here now and gone tomorrow. Gratitude grows naturally from this seeing. A wonderful day is not one without problems. It is one we choose to see clearly.
