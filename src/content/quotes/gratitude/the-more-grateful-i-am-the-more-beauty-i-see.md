Gratitude changes what our eyes can see. A grateful person notices the light in a room, the kindness in a stranger, the colour in an ordinary street — beauty that was always there but passed unnoticed. Complaint narrows the world; thankfulness widens it. The more you practise gratitude, the more your attention is trained to find what is good and lovely, and the world answers by offering more of it. Beauty is not scarce; it is only hidden from ungrateful eyes. Be grateful, and watch the world grow beautiful.
