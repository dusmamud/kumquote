Painter lifts thankfulness out of etiquette and into the spiritual life. Manners say thank you because it is polite; spirituality says it because it is true. To thank someone is to admit that we did not do it alone, that our life is held up by hands we often ignore. That admission is a form of humility, and humility is the beginning of every spiritual path. A thank you spoken honestly is a small prayer: it names the gift, honours the giver, and reminds us that we are recipients before we are achievers.
