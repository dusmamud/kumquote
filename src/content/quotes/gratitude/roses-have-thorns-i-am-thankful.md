The same rose can be a reason to complain or a reason to give thanks, and the difference lies in where we look. Some people fix their eyes on the thorns and miss the flower; others see the flower first and accept the thorns as part of it. Karr's line is a lesson in perspective: life will always offer both beauty and difficulty together. Gratitude is the habit of noticing the rose without denying the thorn. When we practise that shift, ordinary days start to look generous, because we stop waiting for perfection and start appreciating what already blooms.
