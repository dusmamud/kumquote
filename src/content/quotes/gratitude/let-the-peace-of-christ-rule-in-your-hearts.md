Paul ties thankfulness to peace, and the order matters. When peace rules the heart, gratitude follows naturally; when anxiety rules, thanks feels impossible. He reminds his readers that they were called to peace together, as members of one body, so gratitude is not only personal but shared: we are thankful for each other as well as for ourselves. The verse ends with a simple command, and be thankful, as if it were the natural conclusion of a peaceful heart. Peace and gratitude, in this view, grow from the same root.
