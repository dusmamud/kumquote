We often believe happiness hides in extraordinary moments, a grand vacation, a major achievement, a once-in-a-lifetime event. But chasing the extraordinary leaves us missing the happiness already in front of us: a child's laugh, a shared meal, a quiet morning. Gratitude is the practice that reveals it. Paying attention turns ordinary moments into gifts, and thankfulness turns gifts into joy. Happiness was never far away; it was waiting in the present, unnoticed. Stop chasing, start noticing, and the life you already have will begin to feel like the one you were searching for.
