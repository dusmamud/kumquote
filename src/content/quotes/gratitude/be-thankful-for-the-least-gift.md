Thomas à Kempis says to be thankful for the least gift, so that you may be fit to receive greater ones. Gratitude for small things trains the heart to notice grace everywhere. A person who despises small gifts is not ready for large ones; appreciation is a capacity that grows with use. The quote reverses our instinct to wait for something big before feeling thankful. Start with the smallest gift, a meal, a morning, a kind word, and the larger gifts will find a grateful home.
