Thankfulness is the first step — a feeling that rises when we notice something good. Gratitude, in this view, is what happens when that feeling matures into a way of living. It is not a single moment of thanks but a practice: returning to appreciation again and again until it shapes how we see the world. Steindl-Rast suggests that gratitude is completed when it changes our behaviour — when we care for what we have, share it generously, and treat life itself as a gift. Thankfulness begins the journey; gratitude is arriving at a thankful life.
