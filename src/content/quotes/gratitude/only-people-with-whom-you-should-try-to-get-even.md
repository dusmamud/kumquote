Southard turns revenge into a joke with a serious point. Getting even usually means repaying harm, and it keeps us chained to the people who hurt us. But there is one kind of getting even worth pursuing: repaying kindness. The people who helped us deserve to be repaid in full, with interest, because gratitude is a debt that honours the debtor. Chasing revenge shrinks a life; chasing repayment of kindness expands it. Choose your debts carefully, and settle only the generous ones.
