The deepest feelings are often the hardest to put into words, and gratitude is the best example. A simple thank you can sound too small for the kindness it tries to repay, so people fall silent instead, or repeat the same few phrases and hope they are enough. Yet silence is not ingratitude; it may mean the feeling is too large for language. That is why gratitude is best shown in deeds as well as words: returning the kindness, remembering it years later, and passing it on. When words fail, a thankful life says what the tongue cannot.
