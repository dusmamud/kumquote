Nietzsche calls gratitude the essence of all beautiful art. Great art is not made from complaint or emptiness, but from a deep appreciation of life — its light, its sorrow, its strange beauty. Gratitude is what turns experience into art: the artist notices what is good and true, and gives it form. The same is true of a good life. When we look at our days with gratitude, even ordinary moments become beautiful. Art and gratitude share one root: the willingness to see the world as a gift rather than a burden.
