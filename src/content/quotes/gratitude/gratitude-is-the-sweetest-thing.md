Chinmoy calls gratitude the sweetest thing in a seeker's life, because it sweetens everything it touches. A seeker is someone looking for meaning, peace, or truth, and gratitude is the simplest practice on that path. It asks for no special skill, no money, and no permission, only attention to what is already good. The taste is immediate: a grateful moment feels warmer, lighter, and more alive than an ungrateful one. Keep practicing, and the sweetness spreads from single moments into whole days. The search for meaning may end with something as simple as saying thank you.
