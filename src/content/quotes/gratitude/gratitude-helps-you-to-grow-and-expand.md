Caddy describes gratitude as a force that opens a life instead of shrinking it. Complaint narrows our attention to what is missing, and the world feels small and hostile. Gratitude does the opposite: it turns attention toward what is already here, and the world grows larger, warmer, and more generous. Joy and laughter follow naturally, not because problems vanish, but because a grateful mind notices the good that was always present. Growth, in this sense, is not about acquiring more but about seeing more clearly.
