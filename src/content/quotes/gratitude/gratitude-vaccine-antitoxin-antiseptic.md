Jowett reaches for medical metaphors to describe gratitude's protective power. Like a vaccine, it prevents bitterness from taking hold; like an antitoxin, it neutralizes the poisons of envy and resentment; like an antiseptic, it cleans the wounds of disappointment so they can heal. Gratitude does not deny hardship but protects the heart from its infections. Practiced regularly, it builds immunity against cynicism. The image is practical: gratitude is not only a feeling but a daily hygiene of the soul, keeping us healthy from the inside.
