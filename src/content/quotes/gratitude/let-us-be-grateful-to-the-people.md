Proust pictures the people who make us happy as gardeners, tending our souls the way a gardener tends a garden. We do not blossom on our own: our growth depends on the care, patience, and attention of others, often given quietly and without reward. The image is gentle and true — a garden needs someone who waters it, prunes it, and waits for it to flower. Gratitude, then, is simply recognising the gardeners in our lives: the friends, family, and teachers whose kindness helped us grow.
