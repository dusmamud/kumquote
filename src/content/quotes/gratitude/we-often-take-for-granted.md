Ozick points to the strange blindness of ordinary life: the things we should be most grateful for are the ones we notice least. Clean water, a working body, a friend who answers the phone, these become invisible through familiarity. We treat them as background, until they disappear and we realize what they were worth. Gratitude is the practice of seeing the background again, of naming the gifts hiding in plain sight. Look around today and list five things you take for granted. Each one is a reason for thanks, and each one was never guaranteed.
