Wisdom is often imagined as knowledge, but this line says most of it is appreciation. Knowing facts matters far less than noticing the people and gifts already around us. The second sentence turns that insight into action: go and squeeze someone's hand. Gratitude is not complete as a thought; it needs a gesture, a touch, a spoken word, delivered while there is still time. The urgency is gentle but real. The people we appreciate will not always be within reach, so wisdom is saying thank you now, with our hands as well as our hearts.
