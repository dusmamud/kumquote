Chesterton says gratitude is happiness doubled by wonder. Happiness alone is pleasant, but when it is joined by wonder at the gift itself, it doubles. Wonder is the recognition that the good things in life are not owed to us; they are given, and that makes them precious. The quote turns thankfulness into a lens. Look at your life with wonder, and ordinary happiness deepens into gratitude. The doubling happens the moment we stop taking the gift for granted. Wonder turns a common day into a gift, and gratitude turns the gift into joy.
