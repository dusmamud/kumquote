Oprah Winfrey describes gratitude as a kind of magnet: focus on what you have, and you will find you have more. The mechanism is attention. A grateful mind notices opportunities, kindness, and resources that a complaining mind walks straight past. It also treats people better, and people respond to that warmth with generosity of their own. The opposite is equally true — fixate on what you lack, and even plenty feels like poverty. Gratitude does not magically create wealth, but it changes how we see and use what we already hold, and that changes everything.
