Every good thing we enjoy has a source, and most of those sources are people we never see. The water in our cup came from a spring, carried by hands and work we did not witness. The same is true of our comforts, our education, and our opportunities: someone dug the well, built the road, or taught the teacher. Remembering the spring means refusing to take the water for granted. Gratitude is simply that memory kept alive, the habit of tracing a blessing back to where it began and honouring the source.
