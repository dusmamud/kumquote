We walk through life receiving far more than we notice: the food on our table, the kindness of strangers, the patience of friends, the simple gift of another morning. Yet we often keep score only of what we lack, and life feels thin. Gratitude changes the accounting. When we pause to count what has been given, the same ordinary life suddenly looks abundant. Richness, in this sense, is not about owning more but about seeing more clearly. A grateful heart turns small blessings into wealth, and turns an ordinary day into something worth celebrating.
