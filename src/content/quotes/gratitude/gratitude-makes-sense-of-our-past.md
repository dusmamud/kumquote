Melody Beattie gives gratitude a role in every tense of life. Looking back, it helps us make sense of our past, finding meaning even in difficult chapters by noticing what they taught us. In the present, it brings peace, because a grateful mind is not consumed by what is missing. Looking forward, it creates a vision, because a person who sees the good in today can imagine more good tomorrow. Gratitude is therefore not a single moment of politeness but a way of living that touches past, present, and future alike.
