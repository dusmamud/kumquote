Wilde punctures the comfort of good intentions. Grand plans to help, thank, or give — held in the mind but never acted on — change nothing for anyone. The smallest real act, a note, a call, a minute of help, is worth more because it actually reaches another person. Gratitude, like kindness, is measured in deeds rather than feelings. The quote urges us to shrink our ambitions to the size of action: do the small kind thing now, instead of planning the grand one forever. Intentions are free; acts are what count.
