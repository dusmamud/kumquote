It is easy to be thankful for blessings, but she asks for gratitude toward everything, even the problems that hurt and frustrate us. Accepting them with love does not mean enjoying hardship; it means trusting that even difficulties carry meaning and refusing to meet them with bitterness. This kind of gratitude transforms suffering from an enemy into a teacher, and frees the heart from resentment. When we can receive the whole of life, good and hard alike, with love, thankfulness stops being a reaction to good fortune and becomes a steady way of living.
