Socrates draws a line between two kinds of wealth. Contentment, the satisfaction with what we have, is natural wealth because it needs nothing extra to feel complete. Luxury, by contrast, creates endless new wants, and each want makes us feel poorer until it is satisfied. The more we chase, the more we lack. This idea invites us to see gratitude as true riches: a person who is thankful for enough already owns a great deal, while a person who always needs more stays poor no matter how much they gather. Real wealth begins with appreciating what is already ours.
