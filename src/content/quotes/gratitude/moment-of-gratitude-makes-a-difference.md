Wilkinson points out how small the doorway to gratitude really is: a single moment. Not a retreat, not a new philosophy, just one pause to notice something good. That moment interrupts the machinery of complaint, and once interrupted, the attitude begins to shift. One grateful thought makes the next one easier, and a day built from such moments feels different by evening. Attitude is not changed by force but by accumulation, and gratitude is the smallest unit of that change. A moment is all it takes to begin.
