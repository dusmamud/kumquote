Gratitude and despair cannot occupy the same moment. The mind that is fully present to a blessing — warmth, friendship, a breath of fresh air — cannot at the same instant drown in hopelessness. This does not cure deep sorrow, and it is no substitute for help when you need it, but it offers a foothold: one thankful thought can break the spiral, even briefly. In a dark hour, naming one small good thing is a real act of strength. It will not fix everything, but it proves that light is still reachable, one grateful moment at a time.
