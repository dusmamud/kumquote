Giving thanks in good times is easy; the challenge is to give thanks when circumstances are hard. This short command does not ask us to pretend that suffering is pleasant, but to keep gratitude alive inside it. Even in difficulty there is usually something to be thankful for: a friend who stayed, strength we did not know we had, or a lesson that will serve us later. Thankfulness in all circumstances is not denial. It is a steady anchor that keeps hardship from defining the whole of our lives.
