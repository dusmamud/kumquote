Goodness does not grow on its own; it needs soil, and that soil is appreciation. When we notice and value the good in others, we encourage more of it, both in them and in ourselves. A kind act that is appreciated is more likely to be repeated, while one that goes unnoticed may wither. Appreciation is therefore not a small courtesy but the ground from which generosity, patience, and honesty grow. If we want a better world, the work begins by recognizing the goodness already around us and tending it like a garden.
