Feeling grateful in private is a pleasant sensation, but it does nothing for the person who helped us. They cannot see inside our hearts, and silence looks exactly like indifference. Brault is blunt about this: gratitude that is never expressed is not gratitude at all, but ingratitude wearing a nicer name. Saying thank you is a small act, and that is precisely why its absence is so noticeable. Gratitude completes itself only when it leaves our thoughts and reaches the person it belongs to.
