Berry refuses both denial and despair. He does not ask us to ignore the facts: the world is troubled, the news is heavy, and honest people know it. Joy, in his view, is not the opposite of facing facts but what comes after facing them fully. Once we have considered everything, we can still choose gratitude for what is good, still find beauty, still be glad to be alive. This joy is tougher than optimism because it has looked at the worst and decided to live well anyway. It is gratitude with its eyes open.
