Yoga is often imagined as postures and breathing, but its highest form is an attitude, a way of meeting life. Gratitude is that attitude: the steady practice of receiving each moment with thanks, whether it brings joy or difficulty. This is harder than any pose, because it must be practiced everywhere, not only on a mat. A grateful attitude steadies the mind, softens the heart, and turns even hardship into a teacher. It unites us with life as it is, rather than as we wish it were. In that union lies the deepest peace, and the highest yoga of all.
