Gratitude costs nothing, yet it is among the most valuable gifts we can offer. Unlike money or possessions, it is available to everyone, rich or poor, and giving it never leaves us with less. In fact, the act of thanking enriches the giver, opening the heart to joy and deepening our sense of connection. Tozer reminds us that gratitude is not a small courtesy but a precious offering, worthy of the highest regard. When we give thanks sincerely, we join a circle of generosity: the more we offer, the more we receive in return.
