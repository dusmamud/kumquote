James identifies the deepest human craving, and it is not money or fame: it is the need to be appreciated. We all want our efforts noticed, our presence valued, and our worth acknowledged. A word of genuine appreciation can lift a person's whole week, while its absence can quietly drain years of effort. This is why gratitude matters so much: appreciating others meets their deepest need, and being appreciated meets ours. The exchange costs nothing and changes everything. Notice people, thank them specifically, and watch how much stronger every relationship becomes.
