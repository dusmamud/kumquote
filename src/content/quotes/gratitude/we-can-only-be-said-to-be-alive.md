We move through most days on autopilot, barely noticing the people, health, and comforts that sustain us. Wilder suggests that we are truly alive only in the moments when we wake up to our treasures — when the heart recognises what it already holds. Those moments of awareness are not rare by nature; they are rare because we rush past them. Gratitude is the practice of pausing long enough for the heart to catch up with the eyes. When we become conscious of our blessings, ordinary days reveal themselves as rich, and life feels fuller without anything needing to change.
