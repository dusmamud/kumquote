Waking with thanksgiving is a way of beginning the day by naming what matters most. Friends, old and new, are the people who shape our lives through conversation, loyalty, and shared laughter, yet we often treat their presence as ordinary. This line reminds us that friendship is not guaranteed; it is a gift that arrives each morning, if we notice it. Old friends carry our history, and new friends open new possibilities. To greet the day with thanks for both is to recognize that we are never truly walking through life alone.
