Voltaire finds a quiet magic in appreciation: when we genuinely admire what is excellent in another person, some of that excellence becomes ours. This is not theft but sharing — by studying a great artist, a wise friend, or a skilled colleague with real attention, we absorb something of their quality. Envy, by contrast, pushes excellence away and leaves us poorer. Appreciation is generous in both directions: it honours the other person and enriches us at the same time. To appreciate well is to learn well, and the habit turns everyone we meet into a teacher.
