Yet she chose to look at what remained: the beauty of nature, the kindness of those helping her, the small mercies of each day. Gratitude, for her, was not denial of misery but a deliberate act of attention, refusing to let suffering have the last word. Her example gives the idea its full weight: if thankfulness could survive in a hidden annex, it can survive anywhere. Beauty remains even in dark times, and noticing it is both an act of hope and an act of courage.
