To be present in all things is to truly arrive where we are, instead of living in the past or the future, and presence makes gratitude possible, because we cannot be thankful for what we have not noticed. Thankfulness, in turn, deepens presence, giving our attention a warm reason to stay. Together they form a complete way of meeting life: show up fully, and receive whatever comes with a grateful heart. The instruction covers everything, not only the pleasant parts, and that is its strength. A life met with presence and gratitude is a life fully lived.
