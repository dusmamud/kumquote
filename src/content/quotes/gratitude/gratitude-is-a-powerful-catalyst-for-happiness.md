Gratitude does not just sit quietly beside happiness; it sparks it. A single moment of genuine thanks can shift a whole day, turning an ordinary morning into something warm and bright. Like a spark catching dry wood, one thankful thought leads to another, and soon joy is burning steadily. This is why gratitude works best as a daily habit rather than an occasional feeling: small sparks, lit often, keep the fire alive. Take a minute today to name something you are thankful for, and feel the warmth it lights in you.
