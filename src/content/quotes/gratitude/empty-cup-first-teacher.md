We often learn the value of something only after it is gone. The cup is full and we drink without thinking; only when it is empty do we realise what we had. This line urges us to learn sooner. Do not wait for loss to teach you what gratitude could have taught you while the blessing was still in your hands. Notice the full cup now, while you can still enjoy it. Gratitude given in time is worth far more than regret afterwards, because it lets us live with our blessings instead of only mourning them.
