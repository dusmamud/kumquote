Kak Sri treats gratitude as an art, not a feeling that arrives on its own. Adversity gives us a rough canvas: loss, failure, illness, or disappointment, none of which looks lovely at first. The art lies in what we do with it, finding the lesson, the strength, or the unexpected kindness hidden inside the hardship, and painting over the darkness without pretending it was never there. The picture is not fake; it is the same life seen with a grateful eye. Gratitude does not erase adversity, it reframes it into something worth keeping.
