Bonhoeffer, writing from a prison cell, knew how much of life is gift rather than achievement. We receive far more than we give: our language, our health, our friendships, the food on the table, all built by countless others. Yet ordinary life hides this dependence, and we walk around imagining we earned everything ourselves. Gratitude is the lens that reveals the truth. When we see how much we have received, life becomes rich, not because we own more, but because we finally notice what was always there, freely given and quietly sustaining us.
