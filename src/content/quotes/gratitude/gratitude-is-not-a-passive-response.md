Steindl-Rast corrects a common misunderstanding: gratitude is not something that happens to us when good fortune arrives; it is something we practise by paying attention. The gifts of life are always present — the air, the light, the people beside us — but they pass unnoticed by an inattentive mind. Gratitude arises the moment we wake up to what is already given. Attention is the doorway, and thankfulness is what waits on the other side. Wake up to the gifts already around you, and gratitude will follow naturally.
