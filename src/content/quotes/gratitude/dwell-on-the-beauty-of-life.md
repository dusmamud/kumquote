We spend much of our attention on problems, flaws, and fears, and life begins to look as narrow as our worries. Marcus Aurelius invites us to dwell instead on the beauty of life. Look at the stars, the sky, the sea, and the simple wonders that ask nothing from us. Seeing ourselves running with the stars reminds us that we belong to something vast and beautiful, not just to our daily troubles. Gratitude grows from this kind of attention. When we dwell on beauty, we stop taking the world for granted.
