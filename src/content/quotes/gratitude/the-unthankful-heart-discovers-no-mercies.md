Beecher contrasts two ways of moving through the world. The unthankful heart walks past blessings without seeing them, because it is looking only for what is missing. The thankful heart finds something to be grateful for in every hour, because it is looking for what is present. The world does not change between the two; only the seeing changes. Gratitude is therefore a kind of vision — the practice of noticing the mercies that were there all along. Look for the blessings first, and you will never run out of reasons to give thanks.
