Twain jokes that he can live for two months on a good compliment. A kind word is small and costs nothing, yet its effect lasts far beyond the moment it is spoken. Compliments feed something deep in us: the need to be seen and valued. The quote is a reminder of how powerful encouragement is. We often withhold praise, thinking it unnecessary, while the people around us are hungry for it. Speak the good word; it may sustain someone for weeks.
