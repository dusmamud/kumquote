Peale describes a quiet upward spiral. Thankfulness is a practice — something you do deliberately, like exercise, until it becomes habit. Like any habit, it strengthens with use: the more you look for things to be thankful for, the more you notice. The grateful see gifts everywhere — a kind word, a warm meal, a healthy body — that a less thankful person walks straight past. The world does not change; your attention does. That is why the thankful seem to have more to be thankful for: they trained themselves to see it. Practise thankfulness, and the practice will repay you daily.
