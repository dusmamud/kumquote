Mother Teresa observes that kind words are short and easy to speak, yet their echoes are endless. A gentle sentence takes seconds to say and can be remembered for a lifetime. Kindness is the cheapest gift and the most durable. The quote challenges the idea that making a difference requires grand gestures. Often it is a small, kind word at the right moment that changes a day, or a life. Speak kindly; the echo will travel farther than you know.
