Thoreau finds that a walk at dawn blesses the whole day. The early morning has a quiet that the rest of the day never recovers — birdsong, cool air, light arriving slowly. To walk in it is to begin the day with gratitude rather than hurry. The blessing is not magic; it is attention. When we start by noticing the world, we carry that noticing into everything else. A day begun in gratitude is harder to spoil. The walk is small, the effect is large: a whole day shaped by its first quiet hour.
