Winfrey says living in the moment brings a sense of reverence for all of life's blessings. Presence is the doorway to gratitude: when we actually notice the day we are living, its gifts become visible. Rushing through life, we miss them entirely. Reverence is the feeling that follows attention, a quiet awe at how much has been given. The quote links mindfulness and thankfulness. Slow down, live in this moment, and the blessings you already have will come into view.
