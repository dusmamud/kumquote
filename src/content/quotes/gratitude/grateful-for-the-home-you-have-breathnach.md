We are trained to want a bigger house, a better address, or finer things, and that wanting makes the present home feel insufficient. But gratitude reframes it: the roof, the warmth, and the shelter we enjoy right now are already enough for this moment. All you have is all you need, not because ambition is wrong, but because contentment begins with seeing what is here. When we stop measuring our home against someone else's, we discover that enough was never far away; it was waiting in the rooms we already live in.
