Epicurus warns that enough is a decision, not a number. For the person to whom enough is always too little, no amount of wealth, praise, or pleasure will ever satisfy. The hunger grows with the feeding. Gratitude is the opposite habit: learning to call something enough. When we can look at our life and say 'this is enough,' the chase ends and contentment begins. It is not that we stop wanting better things; it is that we stop believing happiness lives only in the next thing. Enough is a doorway, and gratitude is the key.
