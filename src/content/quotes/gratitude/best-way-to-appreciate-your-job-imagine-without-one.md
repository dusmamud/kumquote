Wilde offers a quick cure for taking work for granted: picture it gone. The daily irritations that fill our complaints, the early mornings, the difficult colleagues, the tedious tasks, all look different when set against unemployment and empty days. Appreciation rarely comes from listing benefits; it comes from imagining their absence. The exercise works for more than jobs: imagine a day without health, without a friend, without a home, and the ordinary suddenly looks like good fortune. Gratitude grows fastest in the shadow of loss.
