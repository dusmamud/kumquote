Disappointment feels like a tide coming in, covering everything with what went wrong. But attention is a choice, and when we turn it toward gratitude, the water begins to move the other way. Remembering what is good does not erase the loss, yet it makes room for love, connection, and hope to return. The image is honest: tides move slowly, and feelings change gradually, not all at once. Still, the direction matters. Each moment spent noticing what we are thankful for pulls the tide of love a little closer to shore.
