Albert Schweitzer reminds us that gratitude is not only for gifts and good fortune, but for the people who revive us when our own light has gone out. Everyone has seasons when motivation fades and the inner fire burns low, and in those moments a single conversation, a word of encouragement, or someone simply believing in us can relight everything. Such people rarely know what they did. That is why Schweitzer calls for thanks: not as politeness, but as recognition that our strength is often borrowed, and that the kindness of others deserves to be named before it is forgotten.
