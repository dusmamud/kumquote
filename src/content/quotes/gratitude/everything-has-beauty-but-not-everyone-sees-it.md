Confucius points out that beauty is everywhere, but seeing it is a skill. Two people can walk the same street: one sees only traffic and noise, the other sees light, faces, and small kindnesses. The difference is not in the street but in the eyes. Gratitude trains the eyes. When we practise noticing what is beautiful — in people, in nature, in ordinary moments — the world quietly becomes more beautiful. Beauty is not scarce; attention is. The person who learns to see beauty will never be poor in wonder.
