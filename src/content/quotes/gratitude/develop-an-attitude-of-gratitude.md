Gratitude is not a mood that arrives by chance; it is an attitude we can deliberately develop, like a muscle strengthened by daily use. Giving thanks for everything, even setbacks, reframes our experience: difficulties become lessons, delays become preparation, and small steps become progress toward something bigger. This does not mean pretending hardship is pleasant, but trusting that each step forward carries us somewhere better. A grateful attitude changes what we notice, and what we notice changes how we act. Practice thanks daily, and the path ahead begins to look like an opportunity.
