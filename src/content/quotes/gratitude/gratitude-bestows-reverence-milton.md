Without thankfulness, a morning, a meal, or a friend's kindness passes unnoticed, filed away as routine. With it, the same moments open up, and we encounter what he calls everyday epiphanies: sudden glimpses of meaning in the commonplace. Gratitude is the lens that makes the sacred visible in the ordinary, teaching us to see depth where habit sees only surface. The world does not change; our way of seeing does, and reverence is the reward for paying grateful attention to a life we too often rush past.
