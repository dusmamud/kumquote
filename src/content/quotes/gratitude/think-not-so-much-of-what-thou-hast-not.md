Aurelius advises us to think less of what we lack and more of what we have. For each blessing, imagine how eagerly you would have sought it if you did not have it. This exercise turns familiar gifts, health, friends, a home, into wonders again. We take them for granted only because they are constant. The quote is a daily practice for contentment. Count what you have, picture life without it, and gratitude follows naturally. The blessings were always there; the exercise only teaches your eyes to see them again.
