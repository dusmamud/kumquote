Wanting more is a habit that never ends, because every achievement quickly becomes the new normal. Gratitude breaks that cycle by turning attention toward what is already here. When we notice the good we already possess, it stops feeling small and starts feeling sufficient. Enough is not a number; it is a way of seeing. A grateful heart finds abundance in ordinary things, and that quiet contentment is worth more than any further accumulation. That shift in vision changes everything, without changing a single circumstance.
