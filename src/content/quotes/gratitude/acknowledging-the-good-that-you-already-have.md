Abundance is usually imagined as having more, but Tolle argues it begins with noticing what is already here. When we acknowledge the good in our lives — health, friendship, shelter, small daily joys — we shift from scarcity to sufficiency. That shift changes everything: a mind that feels it has enough can give freely, create boldly, and welcome more without desperation. A mind that feels empty, no matter how much it owns, will always grasp for more. Gratitude is not settling for less; it is the foundation on which a richer life is built.
