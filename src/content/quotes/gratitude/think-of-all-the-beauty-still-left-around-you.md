Anne Frank wrote these words while hiding from a war that had taken almost everything. Yet she could still see the beauty left around her — the sky, the trees, a child's laugh. Gratitude is not denial; it is the choice to notice what remains when much is lost. Her words remind us that beauty does not disappear in hard times; it simply becomes quieter, waiting to be noticed. Even in the darkest room, there is light somewhere. To be happy in such a moment is not foolishness. It is the deepest kind of courage and thanks.
