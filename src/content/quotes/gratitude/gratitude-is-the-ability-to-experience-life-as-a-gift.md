Nothing we have was strictly owed to us: not the morning light, not the breath in our lungs, not the people who love us. When we see life as a gift rather than an entitlement, everything changes. Ordinary things regain their wonder, and complaints give way to thanks. Gratitude is the ability that makes this shift, the practiced skill of receiving each day as something given, not taken for granted. It needs no perfect life, only an open eye. Those who experience life as a gift find abundance in the simplest places, and joy in what others overlook entirely.
