A thankful heart does more than feel good; it gives birth to the rest of a good character. People who are grateful tend to be generous, because they know they have received more than they earned. They tend to be patient, because they notice what is going well instead of only what is missing. They tend to be kind, because appreciation softens resentment. Gratitude is called the parent of virtues because the others grow out of it. Start with thankfulness, and honesty, generosity and courage have fertile ground to grow in.
