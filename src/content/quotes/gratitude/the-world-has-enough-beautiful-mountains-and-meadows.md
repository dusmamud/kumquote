Josephson reminds us that the world is already full of beauty; what it lacks is not more splendour but more people willing to notice it. Mountains, skies, lakes, forests, stars, sunrises — all of it is given freely, every day, and mostly ignored. Appreciation costs nothing and changes everything: the same walk becomes a gift when walked gratefully. The world does not need to be improved before we can enjoy it; it needs us to slow down, look up, and say thank you.
