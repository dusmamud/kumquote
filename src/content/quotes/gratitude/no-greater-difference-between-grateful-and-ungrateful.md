Two people can have the same job, the same health, and the same good fortune, yet live in completely different worlds. One notices every kindness and counts each gift, while the other takes everything as owed and finds fault with the rest. The difference is not luck or wealth; it is the habit of gratitude. A grateful person turns ordinary days into something rich, because attention itself becomes a form of wealth. An ungrateful person stays poor no matter how much arrives. Character shows most clearly not in what we own, but in whether we remember to be thankful.
