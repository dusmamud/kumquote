Ward uses a perfect image: unexpressed gratitude is a gift that never arrives. Feeling thankful in private is a good start, but it changes nothing for the person who helped you — they cannot see inside your heart. Only when gratitude is spoken, written, or shown does it complete its journey, bringing warmth to the giver and joy to the receiver. So many thank-yous sit unwrapped in our minds, delayed until a better moment that never comes. The remedy is simple: say it now, while it still matters. A present is only a present when it is given.
