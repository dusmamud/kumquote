Joy does not grow from having more, but from noticing what is already here. Gratefulness is the root that feeds it: when we recognise a gift — a sunrise, a kind word, a second chance — joy rises naturally. Without that root, even great good fortune feels empty, because we are too busy wanting the next thing to enjoy this one. The practice is simple: pause, notice, and say thank you, silently or aloud. Roots are hidden, but they decide whether anything blooms. Tend your gratefulness, and joy will grow from it.
