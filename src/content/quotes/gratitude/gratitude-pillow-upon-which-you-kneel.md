Maya Angelou gives gratitude a place in the most intimate moment of the day — the nightly prayer. A pillow supports the head in rest; gratitude, she suggests, should support the spirit in prayer. Ending the day by kneeling on gratitude means reviewing the day through thankfulness rather than complaint, and carrying that softness into sleep. The image is tender and practical: make gratitude the last thing you touch each night, and it will shape your dreams, your rest, and the morning that follows. Gratitude is the gentlest way to close a day.
