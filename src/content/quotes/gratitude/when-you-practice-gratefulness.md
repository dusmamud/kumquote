Gratitude is often treated as a private feeling, but the Dalai Lama shows its social power. When we practise gratefulness, we start to see how much of our lives depends on others: the farmer who grew our food, the teacher who shaped us, the friend who listened. That awareness naturally grows into respect, because we recognise the effort and kindness behind what we receive. An ungrateful person takes others for granted; a grateful person honours them. Practising thanks, then, is not just good for our own mood — it makes us kinder, humbler, and more respectful toward everyone around us.
