Robinson distinguishes two kinds of gratitude, and the second is the larger one. The sudden kind arrives when we receive something: a gift, a favor, a rescue. It is quick, warm, and easy to feel. The larger kind grows when we give: our time, our care, our resources. Giving gratitude deepens it, because generosity reminds us how much we have to share. People who only take eventually feel empty, while people who give feel rich. Practice both kinds, but lean into the larger one. The more gratitude we give away, the more of it we seem to keep.
