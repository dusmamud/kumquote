Each morning is a fresh gift, yet we often greet it with complaint instead of thanks. Tecumseh's morning prayer lists the simplest blessings, light, life, strength, food, and the joy of living, none of which we earned, and all of which we could lose. Beginning the day with thanks sets its tone: a grateful morning makes a generous day. And if we see no reason to give thanks, the fault lies in our own attention. Look closer, and reasons appear everywhere. Gratitude is not found by searching far away; it begins the moment we open our eyes.
