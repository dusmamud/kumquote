Gratitude is often shown with words, but this line asks for something harder: accepting everything, including our problems, with joy. That does not mean pretending problems are pleasant. It means trusting that even hard things have a place, and refusing to let resentment be our response to life. Accepting with joy turns gratitude from a feeling into a way of living. When we meet each day, easy or difficult, with an open and thankful heart, gratitude stops being something we say and becomes something we are.
