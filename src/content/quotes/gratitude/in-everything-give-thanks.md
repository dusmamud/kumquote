Paul gives the shortest possible instruction on gratitude, and its boldness is in the word everything. He does not say to give thanks only when life is easy or when prayers are answered; he says in everything, including the hard seasons when thanks feels unnatural. This is not a denial of pain but a discipline: looking for what remains, what can be learned, and who is still beside us. Gratitude in difficult times does not pretend things are good; it insists that goodness has not entirely left the room.
