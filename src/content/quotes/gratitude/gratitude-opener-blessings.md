Blessings are often already present in our lives, locked away behind our inattention. We have health, friends, food, and small daily kindnesses, yet we walk past them without noticing. Gratitude is the key that opens those locked doors. The moment we pause to appreciate what is already here, it stops being ordinary and becomes a gift. Nothing outside has to change first. The blessings were waiting all along; gratitude simply lets us in to enjoy them, and keeps the door open.
