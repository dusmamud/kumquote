This verse from Psalm 100 pictures thanksgiving as the way in, not an afterthought. We do not arrive first and then decide whether to be grateful; gratitude is the door itself, the attitude with which we enter. To come with thanksgiving is to come already aware of what has been given, already ready to praise rather than complain. The image is simple but demanding: bring your gratitude with you, before you ask for anything else. A thankful approach changes the whole visit, and the one who enters this way rarely leaves empty-handed.
