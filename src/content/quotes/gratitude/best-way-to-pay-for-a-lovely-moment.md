Beautiful moments ask for nothing except our full attention, yet we often rush past them while planning the next thing. Bach suggests that enjoyment itself is the payment: to truly notice a sunset, a laugh, or a quiet morning is to honour it completely. A lovely moment does not need to be saved, shared, or improved; it only needs to be lived. When we slow down and let a good moment sink in, it stays with us far longer than any photograph. Gratitude begins right there, in the simple act of being present for what is already good.
