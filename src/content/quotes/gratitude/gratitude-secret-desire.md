Not all gratitude is pure, and this sharp maxim asks us to be honest about our own. Sometimes a thank you is less about appreciation and more about keeping the gifts coming, a polite investment in future favours. Recognising that in ourselves is uncomfortable but useful. It separates real gratitude, which asks for nothing more, from the kind that is really a bargain. The test is simple: would we still be thankful if no further benefit followed? If the answer is yes, the gratitude is genuine; if not, it was only desire wearing a polite mask.
