Nothing in this line changes except the way we see it, yet everything is transformed. A simple meal becomes a feast when we taste it with thanks; a house becomes a home when we appreciate the shelter and the people in it; a stranger becomes a friend when we meet them with openness. Gratitude works like light in a room: it does not add furniture, it reveals what was already there. This idea shows that abundance is less about having more and more about noticing more. When gratitude is present, ordinary life turns rich, warm, and full of connection.
