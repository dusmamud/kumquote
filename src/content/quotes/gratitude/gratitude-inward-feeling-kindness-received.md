Van Dyke draws a useful distinction between gratitude and thankfulness. Gratitude is inward — the quiet feeling when kindness is received, the heart registering a gift. Thankfulness is the outward impulse — the words, the note, the returned kindness that expresses the feeling. One without the other is incomplete: unexpressed gratitude stays private, and expression without feeling is empty politeness. The quote reminds us to complete the circle — to let the inward feeling become an outward act, so that the person who gave knows their kindness was received.
