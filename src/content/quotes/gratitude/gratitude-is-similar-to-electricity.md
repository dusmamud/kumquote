Gratitude kept inside us is like electricity stored in a wire that leads nowhere; it has potential but no power. It only comes alive when it flows outward, in a spoken thank-you, a returned kindness, or a generous act. Like current, it must be generated fresh each day, used, and shared, or it fades away. This is why silent appreciation, however sincere, feels incomplete: gratitude is meant to move. When we express it, the energy multiplies, warming both the giver and the receiver. Let gratitude flow, and it will light up the whole circuit of our lives.
