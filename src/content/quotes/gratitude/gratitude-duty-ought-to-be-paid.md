Rousseau captures the strange double nature of gratitude. On the other side, no one can demand it. A gift given in the expectation of gratitude stops being a gift and becomes a transaction. True generosity expects nothing in return, and true gratitude is freely offered, not extracted. This balance keeps relationships honest. We give because giving is good, and we thank because thanks are due — but neither act should be performed as a payment. Gratitude lives in that free space between obligation and expectation.
