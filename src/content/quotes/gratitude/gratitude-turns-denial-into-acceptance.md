Beattie describes gratitude as a force that changes how you see everything. Denial becomes acceptance: instead of refusing to face what is true, you find a way to receive it, even when it is hard. Chaos becomes order: the same messy life, seen through grateful eyes, starts to make sense, because you notice what is holding together rather than what is falling apart. Confusion becomes clarity: when you name what you are thankful for, your priorities sharpen. Gratitude does not change the facts of your life. It changes your relationship to them — and that change, quietly, changes everything.
