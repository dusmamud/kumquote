Wealth is usually measured in money, but this quote measures it in attitude. A grateful person feels rich because they see what they already have; a complaining person feels poor because they see only what is missing. The same life can feel abundant or empty depending on which lens we use. Complaint narrows our world to what is wrong, while gratitude widens it to what is good. Choosing gratitude does not ignore real problems, but it refuses to let them define everything. In that sense, thankfulness is a kind of wealth anyone can hold.
