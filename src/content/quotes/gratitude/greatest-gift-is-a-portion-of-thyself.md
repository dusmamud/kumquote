Emerson says the finest thing we can give is not money or objects but a piece of ourselves: our time, attention, and genuine presence. A bought gift can be generous, but it costs us nothing personal; it is a transaction, not a sharing. Giving a portion of ourselves means sitting with someone, listening fully, and being truly there, which cannot be wrapped or priced. That is why the gift matters: it carries the giver inside it. When we give ourselves, we give what no shop can sell.
