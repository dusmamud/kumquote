Blake promises a plentiful harvest to the thankful receiver, and the farming image is deliberate. Gratitude is not passive; it is a way of tending what we have so that it grows. People who notice and appreciate their blessings take better care of them: relationships deepen, work improves, and health gets attention. The ungrateful, by contrast, let good things wither from neglect. Thankfulness is the water and sunlight of a good life. Receive what comes with an open, grateful heart, and the harvest keeps returning, season after season.
