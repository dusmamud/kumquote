Emmons traces all of that back to a single root: gratitude. A grateful person recognises that they depend on others, and that recognition breeds decency. It is hard to be cruel to someone you are thankful for, and hard to cooperate with someone you take for granted. Gratitude is therefore not a private luxury but a public good. When it spreads, trust grows, and when trust grows, everything else — cooperation, concern, and simple civility — follows naturally in its wake.
