Polite words of thanks are easy to say and easy to forget, because they cost nothing and often mean little more than good manners. Real thanksgiving is different: it comes from the heart, which means it changes how we feel and how we act toward the person who helped us. A heartfelt thanks remembers the gift long after the moment has passed and looks for a way to return the kindness. Words alone can be spoken without thinking, but thanks from the heart is remembered, and it deepens the bond between giver and receiver.
