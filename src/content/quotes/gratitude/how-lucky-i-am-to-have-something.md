Milne turns goodbye into a measure of gratitude. Saying farewell hurts in direct proportion to how much the person or place meant to us, and that pain is itself proof of a gift. We are lucky not because goodbyes are easy but because we had something worth missing. The sadness and the luck are the same fact seen from two sides. Gratitude here does not cancel grief; it stands beside it and says the love was worth the cost. A hard goodbye is the price of a good hello.
