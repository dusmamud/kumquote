Gratitude pulls us out of regret about the past and worry about the future, and plants us firmly in the present moment. When we notice something to be thankful for right now, a warm cup, a friend's laugh, sunlight through a window, we are fully here, not lost in thought. The more we practice this, the more present we become, and presence is itself a gift: we listen better, love better, and notice more. Gratitude is therefore not just a feeling but a way of paying attention. Each thank-you is a doorway back into the only moment we truly have.
