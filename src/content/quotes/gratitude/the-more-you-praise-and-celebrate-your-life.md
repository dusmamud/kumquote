Oprah describes a happy cycle: celebrating what we have does not use up our joy, it multiplies it. When we pause to praise the good already in our lives — a friend, a meal, a small victory — we train our attention to notice more of it. Gratitude works like light in a dark room: the more we let in, the more we can see. Celebration is not denial of hardship; it is the deliberate choice to feed the good, and the good, well fed, grows.
