William Arthur Ward describes gratitude as a power of transformation. It does not change the facts of our lives — the common days remain common, the routine jobs remain routine — but it changes how those facts feel. A day seen with gratitude becomes a thanksgiving; a job done with thanks becomes a source of joy; an ordinary chance, noticed with appreciation, becomes a blessing. Nothing outside has moved, yet everything inside has changed. This is the quiet magic of gratitude: it does not require new circumstances, only new eyes.
