Henry Ward Beecher pictures gratitude as a flower growing from the soul — the fairest of all its blossoms. The image suggests that gratitude is not imposed from outside but grows naturally from within, when the soul is healthy and open. Like a blossom, it is beautiful, fragile, and a sign of life: a soul that can feel gratitude is alive to the good in the world. Other qualities may be more useful or more impressive, but gratitude is the fairest because it is pure appreciation, expecting nothing in return.
