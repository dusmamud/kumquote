Ban Breathnach calls gratitude the most passionate transformative force in the cosmos. It is not a polite habit but a power that changes everything it touches. Gratitude turns scarcity into sufficiency, complaint into contentment, and ordinary days into something rich. The transformation is passionate because it engages the whole heart, not just the mind. The quote treats thankfulness as a creative force. Practise it, and the world itself seems to change, because the person seeing it has changed. Begin with one thankful thought, and watch how quickly the whole landscape of a day changes.
