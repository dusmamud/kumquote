Confucius gives a simple rule for a peaceful mind: forget the injuries, remember the kindnesses. Injuries, when nursed, grow larger in memory; a small slight replayed a hundred times becomes a wound. Kindnesses work the opposite way — remembered, they warm you again and remind you the world has been good to you. The rule is not about pretending harm never happened; it is about choosing which memories get to live in your mind rent-free. Forget injuries, and you free yourself. Remember kindnesses, and you stay grateful. A mind that keeps the right ledger is a mind at peace.
