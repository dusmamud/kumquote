Thich Nhat Hanh turns an ordinary act — walking — into a practice of gratitude. To walk as if kissing the Earth is to move with attention and tenderness, aware that each step rests on something that supports us without asking for anything. Most of the time we rush across the ground as if it were only a surface between destinations, never noticing the gift beneath our feet. This quote asks us to slow down and feel it: the Earth holds us up, feeds us, and asks for nothing in return. Gratitude begins with noticing, and walking is where it can start.
