Tony Robbins points to a simple switch in the mind: gratitude and fear cannot occupy it at the same time. Fear grows from focusing on what might go wrong or what we might lose; gratitude grows from noticing what is already here and already good. When we shift our attention to the second, the first loses its grip, and the world suddenly looks abundant rather than threatening. This is not wishful thinking but a change of focus. We cannot always change our circumstances, but we can change what we notice — and what we notice becomes the life we experience.
