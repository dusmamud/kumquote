Of all the emotions a person can cultivate, gratitude may be the healthiest. Anger tightens the body, envy poisons the mind, and fear drains the spirit, but gratitude does the opposite: it relaxes, opens, and restores. A grateful heart sleeps better, relates more warmly, and recovers more quickly from setbacks. It is not naive optimism but a practical habit with real effects on health and happiness. Choosing gratitude daily is one of the simplest investments in wellbeing. It costs nothing, yet it returns more than it asks.
