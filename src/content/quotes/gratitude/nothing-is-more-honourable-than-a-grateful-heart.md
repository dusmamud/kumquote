Seneca calls nothing more honourable than a grateful heart. Honour, for the Stoics, is not about rank or reputation but about character. A grateful heart recognises what it has received, remembers who gave it, and responds with decency. That quality is rare and noble because it requires humility: admitting that we did not achieve everything alone. The quote sets gratitude among the highest virtues. Whatever else a person achieves, a grateful heart is the truest mark of honour. A grateful heart needs no title and no wealth; it carries its honour wherever it goes.
