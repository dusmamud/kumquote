Schwab, who built his career managing people, found that appreciation develops talent better than criticism. People grow toward what is noticed and encouraged; they shrink under constant fault-finding. Appreciation is not flattery — it is honest recognition of what someone does well, and it gives them confidence to do more of it. Encouragement supplies the energy for the next attempt. Leaders, parents, and friends who practice this discover a simple truth: the best in people appears when it is appreciated, and gratitude is the tool that brings it out.
