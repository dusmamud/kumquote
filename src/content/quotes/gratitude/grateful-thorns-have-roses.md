Karr shows how the same fact supports two opposite attitudes. Roses have thorns — that is undeniably true — and one person grumbles about the thorns while another gives thanks for the roses. Nothing about the flower changes; only the focus does. Gratitude is this deliberate choice of attention: noticing the rose rather than the thorn. It does not deny difficulty — the thorns are real — but it refuses to let difficulty be the whole story. A grateful eye finds the gift inside the imperfection.
