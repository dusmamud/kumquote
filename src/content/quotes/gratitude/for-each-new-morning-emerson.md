Emerson's grace is a simple catalogue of daily gifts, each one ordinary and each one worth naming. A new morning with its light, rest and shelter through the night, health and food, love and friends: none of these is guaranteed, yet all of them arrive, day after day, so quietly that we forget to notice. The prayer works by listing them, turning vague thankfulness into specific attention, and reminding us that goodness sends everything, not just the grand events. Gratitude, in this form, is a daily inventory of blessings, a habit of counting what is given before asking for more.
