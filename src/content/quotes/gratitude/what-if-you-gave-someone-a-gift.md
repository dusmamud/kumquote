Imagine giving a thoughtful gift and hearing nothing back: no thanks, no smile, no sign it mattered. Most of us would hesitate before giving again. Maraboli suggests life works the same way, not because life keeps score, but because gratitude changes what we are able to receive. When we notice and appreciate what we already have, we become open to more of it; when we ignore it, good things pass unnoticed. Thankfulness is therefore not just good manners. It is a way of staying awake to life's gifts, so that more of them can reach us.
