Beattie describes gratitude as a key that opens a door we did not know was locked. Without it, even a full life can feel empty, because we are always looking at what is missing. With it, the same life suddenly feels rich: what we have becomes enough, and often more than enough. Gratitude does not add anything to our circumstances; it changes how we see them. A meal becomes a feast, a house becomes a home, a stranger becomes a friend. The fullness was always there, waiting for us to notice it.
