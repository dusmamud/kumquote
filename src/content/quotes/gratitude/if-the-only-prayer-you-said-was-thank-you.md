Prayer is often imagined as a long list of requests, but Eckhart reduces it to a single word: thank you. If gratitude were our only prayer, he says, it would be enough — because thankfulness already contains everything prayer is meant to do. It acknowledges that life is given, not owed; it turns attention toward goodness; and it quiets the constant hunger for more. A life that begins each day with thanks needs little else. The quote invites us to try the simplest spiritual practice of all: notice what is good, and say thank you for it.
