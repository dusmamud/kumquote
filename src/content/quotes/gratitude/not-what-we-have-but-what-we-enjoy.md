Epicurus separates possession from enjoyment, and the gap between them is where most unhappiness lives. We collect houses, clothes, and gadgets, then wonder why abundance never arrives. The answer is that abundance is not a pile of things but a capacity to enjoy what is already ours. A simple meal eaten with attention can feel richer than a feast eaten in distraction. Wealth, in this view, is measured not by the size of the storehouse but by the quality of attention we bring to it.
