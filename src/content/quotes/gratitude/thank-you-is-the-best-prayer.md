No prayer is simpler, or more honest, than "thank you." It asks for nothing, admits that we have received more than we earned, and turns the heart toward the giver — whether God, a friend, or life itself. Long prayers can hide pride or bargaining; two words of thanks hold only humility and love. Said with real feeling, they cover everything a prayer should: awareness of blessing, and a heart open to more. Whatever your faith, this prayer belongs to everyone. When words fail you, let "thank you" be enough — it always is.
