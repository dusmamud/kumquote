Bhajan's line is short because the mechanism is simple. An attitude of gratitude changes what we notice, and what we notice changes what we pursue. A grateful person sees openings, helpers, and small blessings everywhere, and so moves through the world collecting opportunities. An ungrateful person sees only obstacles and closed doors, and moves through the same world finding nothing. The great things do not arrive by magic; they arrive because a grateful attitude makes us ready to receive them, and ready people are the ones who catch what life offers.
