Politeness can be practiced by habit, but gratitude goes deeper: it is courtesy with a heart behind it. To thank someone is to say, I saw what you did, and it mattered to me. That recognition is the finest form of respect we can offer another person, because it honors both their effort and their dignity. A society rich in gratitude is also rich in kindness, since people who feel appreciated give more freely. The smallest sincere thank-you can lift a weary day. Courtesy opens doors, but gratitude opens hearts.
