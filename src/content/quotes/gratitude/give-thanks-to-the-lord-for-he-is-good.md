This verse from Psalm 136 gives gratitude two reasons: goodness and endurance. Thanks here is not for a single favour but for a love that does not run out, a goodness that holds across seasons and failures. The phrase endures forever lifts gratitude beyond the moment; even when circumstances change, the reason for thanks remains. That steadiness is the point. Feelings come and go, but a gratitude rooted in something enduring can survive hard days. We give thanks not because everything is perfect but because goodness itself has not left.
