Success without gratitude often feels hollow: the goals are met, yet the satisfaction never arrives. Gratitude changes the recipe entirely, because it turns attention toward what is already working instead of what is still missing. A person who practices thanks daily finds meaning in ordinary progress and strength in hard seasons. It costs nothing, needs no special occasion, and quietly improves every other ingredient in life, from relationships to ambition. That is why Canfield calls it the single most important one: without gratitude, even a full life can feel empty, and with it, even a simple life feels rich.
