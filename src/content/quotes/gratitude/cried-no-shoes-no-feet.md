Self-pity grows when we compare what we lack with what others have. This line breaks that habit with a single encounter. The speaker's complaint about shoes collapses the moment she meets someone without feet, and the lack suddenly looks like a blessing. It is not about feeling guilty for having shoes; it is about perspective. Most of us have far more than we admit, and our complaints fade when we look honestly at the lives around us. Gratitude often begins with that shift of attention, from what is missing to what is already ours.
