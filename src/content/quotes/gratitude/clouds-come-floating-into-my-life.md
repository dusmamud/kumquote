Tagore turns even clouds into a gift. They no longer come to bring rain or storm, but to add colour to his sunset sky. This is gratitude at its finest: the ability to receive whatever arrives and find the beauty in it. Clouds are not always welcome — they darken, they delay, they disappoint. But seen with grateful eyes, they become part of the picture, adding depth and colour that a clear sky could not. Life brings clouds to everyone. Gratitude does not remove them; it lets them colour the sky.
