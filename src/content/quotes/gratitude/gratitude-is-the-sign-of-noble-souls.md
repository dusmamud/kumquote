Aesop, the ancient fabulist, reduces nobility to a single sign: gratitude. It is not birth, wealth, or power that makes a soul noble, but the willingness to acknowledge what others have given. Ungrateful people take kindness for granted and soon stop receiving it, because no one enjoys giving to someone who never notices. Grateful people, by contrast, honour every gift, however small, and that honour draws more kindness toward them. Nobility, Aesop suggests, is not something you are born with but something you practise. A simple thank you, sincerely meant, is the clearest proof of it.
