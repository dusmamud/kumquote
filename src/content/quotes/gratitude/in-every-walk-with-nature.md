Muir's observation is simple and true: every walk in nature gives back more than we bring. We go out carrying worry, tiredness, or grief, and we return carrying something lighter — a clear thought, a calm heart, a reminder of scale. Nature asks nothing and gives freely: clean air, open sky, the sound of water, the patience of trees. Gratitude for these gifts is the natural response. The more we receive, the more we realise how little we needed to bring. Nature's generosity is quiet, constant, and entirely undeserved — which is exactly why it deserves our thanks.
