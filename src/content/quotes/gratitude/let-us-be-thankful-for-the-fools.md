Success is usually credited to talent, hard work, or good timing, but it also depends on the mistakes of others. Every careless rival, every bad decision by a competitor, and every foolish choice in the room quietly clears the path for someone else. Twain's joke carries a serious point: our achievements are never ours alone, and some help arrives disguised as other people's errors. Being thankful for fools is really about admitting how much of life depends on luck and on the people around us. A little humility makes success sweeter, and a little humor makes it easier to bear.
