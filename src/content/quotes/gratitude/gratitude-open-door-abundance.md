Gratitude opens them. When we notice what we already possess — health, friends, skills, small daily comforts — the world begins to look generous rather than stingy. This is not magical thinking. A grateful person notices opportunities that a resentful person walks past, and people are drawn to those who appreciate them. Abundance, in this sense, is not about having more things. It is about seeing clearly what is already here. Gratitude is the door, and it opens from the inside.
