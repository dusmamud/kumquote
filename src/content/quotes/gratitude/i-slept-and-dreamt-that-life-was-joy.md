Tagore traces a journey in three steps: dreaming that life is joy, waking to see that life is service, and discovering through action that service itself is joy. Dreams are not enough; they must be tested against waking life. And service, which can sound like duty or sacrifice, turns out to be the deepest joy of all. Gratitude grows when we stop asking what life owes us and start asking what we can give. The joy we dream of is found not in receiving but in serving. Act, and behold — the service becomes the joy.
