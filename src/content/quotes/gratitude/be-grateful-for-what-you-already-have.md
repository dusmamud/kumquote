Bennett refuses to make gratitude the enemy of ambition. Being grateful for what we have does not mean settling or giving up on our goals. It means pursuing them from a place of fullness rather than emptiness, of appreciation rather than desperation. People who chase goals while resenting the present arrive exhausted, even when they succeed. People who stay grateful along the way enjoy the journey and make clearer decisions. Hold both at once: thankfulness for today and hunger for tomorrow. The two do not compete; they complete each other, and together they carry us forward.
