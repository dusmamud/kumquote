Oprah describes gratitude as a self-fulfilling outlook. Focusing on what we have trains attention on abundance, and abundance, once noticed, seems to grow — opportunities appear, relationships deepen, and contentment follows. Focusing on what we lack has the opposite effect: no achievement ever fills the gap, because attention keeps moving to the next missing thing. The quote is not a promise of wealth but a law of perception. Gratitude changes what we see, and what we see shapes what our life becomes.
