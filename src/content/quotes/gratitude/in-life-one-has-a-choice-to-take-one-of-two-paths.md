Many of us save our joy for special occasions, waiting for a birthday, a holiday, or a milestone to feel grateful and celebrate. Ogunlaru offers a different path: treat each ordinary day as special in itself. The choice is ours, and it costs nothing to make. A Tuesday morning can hold a good breakfast, a kind message, or a moment of sunlight, and noticing those things turns a plain day into a small festival. Waiting for special days means missing most of life; celebrating each day means finding reasons to be thankful now, in the life we are already living.
