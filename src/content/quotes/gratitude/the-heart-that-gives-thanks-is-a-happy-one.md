Douglas Wood makes a simple claim about how feelings work: thankfulness and unhappiness cannot occupy the heart at the same moment. The two states pull in opposite directions — one looks outward at what has been given, the other inward at what is missing. That is why gratitude is such a practical tool, not just a pleasant sentiment. When a day feels heavy, pausing to name even one thing we are thankful for shifts the frame, and the shift is immediate. We may not fix the problem, but we change the position from which we face it, and that changes everything.
