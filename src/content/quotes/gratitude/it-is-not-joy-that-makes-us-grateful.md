We usually wait for something joyful to happen before we feel grateful, as if joy were the cause and thanks the result. Steindl-Rast reverses the order and says gratitude comes first. When we pause to notice a gift in the ordinary, whether a kind word or a warm meal, joy follows naturally from that noticing. This means joy is not something we must chase or wait for; it grows out of attention. By practising gratitude in small moments, we open the door to joy again and again, discovering that happiness was never far away, only waiting for us to look.
