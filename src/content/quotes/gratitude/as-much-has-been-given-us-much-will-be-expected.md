Receiving much is not only a blessing; it is also a trust. A person who has been given health, freedom, education, or prosperity owes something back to the world that provided them, and the debt is paid in service, not only in words. Saying thank you is a beginning, but true homage is proved by how we live: by using what we have been given for the good of others. Gratitude that moves the hands and guides the choices becomes a real contribution, and it is the only kind that truly honors the gift.
