Prayer is often imagined as a long list of requests, but this line reduces it to two words. Saying thank you is a complete spiritual act on its own, because it recognises that life itself is a gift rather than something we are owed. It needs no eloquence, no ritual, and no special occasion. A life that keeps saying thank you, for food, for friends, for another morning, is already a life of prayer. If gratitude is the whole of it, then the simplest words are enough.
