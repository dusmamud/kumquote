Faust calls a grateful heart the beginning of greatness, and the word beginning matters. Greatness does not start with talent, wealth, or luck; it starts with the attitude that notices what is good and builds from there. A grateful person sees resources where others see scarcity, and that vision makes action possible. Ingratitude, by contrast, blinds us to our own advantages and leaves us complaining in the middle of plenty. Start with gratitude, and the path to doing great things opens up. It is the first step, not the last reward, of a meaningful life.
