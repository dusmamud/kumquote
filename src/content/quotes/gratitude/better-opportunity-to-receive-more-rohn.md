Rohn describes a paradox of receiving: the way to gain more is to be thankful for what you already hold. Gratitude trains the eye to see value, and people who see value take better care of what they have, attract trust, and notice new chances that complainers miss. Thankfulness is therefore not just good manners but good strategy, the habit that prepares us to receive more. The best opportunity for abundance, Rohn argues, is not out there somewhere but in appreciating what is already in our hands.
