Fuller warns that ingratitude is the worst of vices, even though gratitude seems the least of virtues. Thankfulness looks small beside courage or justice, yet its absence poisons everything. The ungrateful person takes gifts as entitlements and relationships as transactions, and slowly loses both. Gratitude, though modest, keeps the heart open and the bonds between people strong. The quote is a reminder to take the small virtue seriously. Neglect it, and the worst vice quietly takes its place. Keep the small virtue alive, and the heart stays open to everyone who has ever helped you.
