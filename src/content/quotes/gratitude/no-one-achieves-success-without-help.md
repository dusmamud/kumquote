Whitehead punctures the myth of the self-made person. Behind every success stand teachers, friends, family, colleagues, and strangers whose help made it possible — and the wise know it. Acknowledging that help is not weakness; it takes confidence to admit you did not do it alone. Insecure people claim all the credit, while secure people share it gladly. Gratitude, in this view, is a mark of strength and honesty: it tells the truth about how success really happens. No one climbs alone, and the wisest climbers are the first to thank the hands that lifted them.
