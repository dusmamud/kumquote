Angelou greets each day as something entirely new, a day no one has ever seen before and will never see again. The wonder lies in that freshness: however ordinary the morning looks, it has never happened before in the whole history of the world. When we see the day as a first edition, small things regain their shine, and the habit of thankfulness follows naturally. Every sunrise is an unrepeatable gift, and the wise response is simply to receive it with open eyes and a grateful heart.
