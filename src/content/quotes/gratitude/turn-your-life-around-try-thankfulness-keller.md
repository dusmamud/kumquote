We usually wait for circumstances to improve before we feel grateful, but he reverses the order: start with gratitude, and the circumstances begin to shift. Thankfulness changes what we notice, turning our attention from what is missing to what is already present, and that shift changes how we act. A grateful person spots opportunities, treats people better, and meets setbacks with steadier nerves. The turnaround is not magic but attention: try thankfulness for a week, and the life you are living starts to look, and feel, like a different one.
