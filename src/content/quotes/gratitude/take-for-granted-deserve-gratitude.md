The things we rely on most are the ones we notice least. Clean water, a working body, a friend who answers the phone, a roof that keeps out the rain — these fade into the background precisely because they are always there. We only recognise their value when they disappear. Ozick's observation is a gentle warning against this habit. Gratitude is not only for the extraordinary; it is most needed for the ordinary, the steady blessings that hold our lives together.
