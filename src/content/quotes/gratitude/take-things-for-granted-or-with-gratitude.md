Two people can live the same life and experience it completely differently. One takes everything for granted, seeing food, shelter, and friendship as things owed to them, and feels constantly short-changed. The other receives the same things with gratitude, and finds the world full of gifts. The difference is not in circumstances but in attention. Gratitude transforms ordinary life into something rich, while taking things for granted turns even abundance into emptiness. The choice between them is made fresh every single day, in a hundred small moments.
