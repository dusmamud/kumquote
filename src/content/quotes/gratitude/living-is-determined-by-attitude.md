Gibran shifts the focus from what life gives us to what we bring to life. Two people can receive the same day — the same weather, the same news, the same work — and live entirely different days. The difference is attitude: one meets life with gratitude and openness, the other with complaint and suspicion. Life does not decide our experience for us; our attitude does. Gratitude is the attitude that turns ordinary days into good ones. What we bring to life is, in the end, what life gives back to us.
