Ziglar calls gratitude the single most powerful source of inspiration, stronger than ambition, fear, or reward. When we are thankful, we see our lives as full of gifts rather than gaps, and that sense of abundance moves us to act, to give, and to grow. Inspiration drawn from gratitude is steadier than inspiration drawn from wanting more, because it rests on what is already true rather than on what is missing. A grateful heart finds energy in ordinary days and turns small blessings into fuel for big efforts.
