Counting blessings sounds simple, almost too simple to change anything, yet it shifts the direction of attention. When we look for what is going well, we begin to see how much already supports us: health, friends, work, small kindnesses that pass unnoticed. The problems do not vanish, but they stop being the whole story. A life turned around is not a life without trouble; it is a life where gratitude has the louder voice. The habit costs nothing and needs no special skill, only the willingness to notice what is already good.
