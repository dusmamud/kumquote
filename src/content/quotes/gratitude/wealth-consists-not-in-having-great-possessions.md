Epictetus redefines wealth: it is not having great possessions, but having few wants. The person with endless desires is poor no matter how much they own, because enough never arrives. The person with few wants is rich with very little, because contentment is already theirs. Gratitude is the practice of shrinking our wants until what we have is enough. This is not settling for less; it is discovering that less was already more. Freedom begins when our happiness no longer depends on acquiring the next thing.
