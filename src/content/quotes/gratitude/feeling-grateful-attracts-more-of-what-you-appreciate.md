Attention is a magnet: what you appreciate, you notice more of, nurture more of, and invite more of into your life. A person who is grateful for their friendships invests in them, and the friendships deepen. Someone thankful for their health takes care of it, and it lasts longer. Gratitude is not magic; it is focus, turned toward the good. By appreciating what you value, you give it your time, energy, and care — and cared-for things grow. Choose to appreciate the good in your life, and you will find more of it arriving.
