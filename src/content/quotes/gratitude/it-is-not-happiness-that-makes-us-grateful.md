David Steindl-Rast reverses the order we usually assume. We tend to wait for happiness to arrive — a better job, a bigger win, a calmer season — and expect gratitude to follow. He argues the opposite: gratitude comes first, and happiness grows out of it. When we notice what is already good, the present moment becomes richer without anything changing. This is not a trick of positive thinking but a shift of attention. Grateful people are not luckier; they are looking more carefully, and what they see makes them happy.
