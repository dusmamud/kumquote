Lao Tzu offers a paradox that runs against everything the world teaches us: the moment you realize nothing is lacking, everything becomes yours. Contentment is not resignation or giving up; it is the recognition that the chase for more is what keeps us feeling poor. When we rejoice in the way things are, the world stops being a list of shortages and becomes a place of abundance. Lao Tzu's insight is that ownership is a state of mind — the person who needs nothing possesses everything, while the person who always wants more owns nothing, no matter how much they gather.
