Emerson uses the word cultivate deliberately: gratitude is not a feeling that arrives on its own, but a habit to be grown, like a garden. Every good thing that comes to us — a kindness, a meal, a moment of beauty — is a chance to practise. The habit grows stronger with use, until thankfulness becomes the mind's natural response rather than a forced effort. Giving thanks continuously does not mean ignoring hardship; it means keeping the habit alive even on difficult days, when gratitude is hardest and most needed.
