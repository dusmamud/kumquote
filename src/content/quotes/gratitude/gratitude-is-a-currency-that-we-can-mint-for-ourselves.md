Van Amburgh offers a delightful image: gratitude is money we print ourselves, and it never runs out. Unlike real wealth, it grows the more we spend it — every thank-you given makes us richer, not poorer. There is no budget to balance, no debt to repay, no fear of bankruptcy, because gratitude is minted in the heart and spent in words and deeds. It is the one currency available to everyone, regardless of fortune, and the only one that increases with use.
