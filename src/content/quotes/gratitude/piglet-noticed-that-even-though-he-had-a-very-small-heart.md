Milne puts a profound truth in the mouth of the smallest character: gratitude is not limited by the size of the heart that holds it. Piglet, timid and tiny, discovers that even his small heart has room for a large amount of thanks. It is a gentle reminder that gratitude does not require grandeur — no great wealth, no great occasion, only a willingness to feel thankful. The smallest heart, opened wide, can hold as much gratitude as any other.
