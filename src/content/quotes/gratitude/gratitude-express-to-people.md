Gratitude to God and gratitude to people are not separate duties; they are the same habit practised at different heights. The blessings we receive from above almost always arrive through human hands: a parent, a teacher, a stranger who helped at the right moment. If we cannot thank the person in front of us, our thanks to heaven rings hollow. This teaching grounds gratitude in everyday manners. Saying thank you to people is not a small courtesy; it is the training ground where genuine thankfulness to God is learned and proved.
