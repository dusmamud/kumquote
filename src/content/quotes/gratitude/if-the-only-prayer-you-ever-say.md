Meister Eckhart reduces the whole of prayer to two words: thank you. Gratitude, in his view, is not one spiritual practice among many; it is the heart of all of them. To say thank you is to admit that we have received more than we earned, and to meet life with humility, not entitlement. It needs no special place, no ritual, and no eloquence — only an honest recognition of the good that has come to us. If that is all we ever manage, it will be enough, because a life lived in thanks is a life lived in truth.
