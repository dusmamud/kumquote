Gratitude that stays inside us helps no one, least of all the person who deserves to hear it. We often assume others know we appreciate them, but unspoken thanks is easily missed, and the warmth we feel never reaches its destination. Stein reminds us that gratitude is a gift only when it is given — spoken, written, or shown in action. Expressing thanks costs little and means a great deal: it strengthens bonds, encourages kindness, and lets others know their efforts mattered. Feeling grateful is the beginning; saying so is what makes it real.
