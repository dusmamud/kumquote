For Merton, gratitude was not a polite habit but a way of seeing. Recognising a gift means recognising a giver, and for Merton that giver was God, present in everything. Even readers who do not share his faith can take something from this view. Gratitude transforms ordinary moments into encounters with something larger than ourselves. A meal becomes more than fuel; a kindness becomes more than an accident. To be grateful is to live awake to the giftedness of life itself.
