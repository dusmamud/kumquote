Gratitude is not a single polite word but a way of moving through the world. Worn like a cloak, it covers every part of life, warming the cold corners where complaint and resentment usually gather. A grateful person finds nourishment in small kindnesses, steady work, and ordinary days. Over time, that attitude feeds the whole of life, softening hardship and deepening joy. What we wear daily shapes who we become, and gratitude is the warmest garment of all. Wear it every day, and watch how it changes the way you see the world.
