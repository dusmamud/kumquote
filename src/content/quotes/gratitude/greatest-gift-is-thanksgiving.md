A gift costs us only what we can spare, and it is often chosen with little thought. That attention is a gift of ourselves: our time, our awareness, our willingness to be moved. Steindl-Rast's distinction explains why a sincere thank-you can matter more than an expensive present. The present says we remembered an occasion; the thanks says we noticed a person. Gratitude given freely is the most personal gift there is, because it cannot be bought, wrapped, or faked. It can only be offered.
