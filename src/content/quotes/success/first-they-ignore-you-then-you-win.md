New ideas rarely arrive to applause. At first they are ignored, because the world is busy with what already works. Then they are mocked, because mockery is easier than understanding. Then they are opposed, because once an idea gains strength, it threatens those who benefit from the old way. Each stage feels like a defeat, but it is actually a sign of progress: opposition only arrives when an idea has become impossible to ignore. The pattern teaches patience. If we measure success by the first reaction, we will quit too soon.
