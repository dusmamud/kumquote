The ordinary is comfortable, and comfort is a powerful argument for staying put. But nothing remarkable was ever built by someone who refused to risk anything. Risk does not mean recklessness; it means accepting that a better life may require an uncertain step. The usual routine guarantees the usual result. If we want something different, we have to do something different, and that always involves a little fear. The choice is simple, even if it is not easy: risk the familiar, or settle for the ordinary.
