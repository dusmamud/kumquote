Success and failure begin on the same road, with the same effort, the same uncertainty, and the same long stretch of work. The difference appears only near the end: one person keeps going when it gets hard, and the other stops. Until that moment, the two journeys are indistinguishable. That means you cannot always tell whether you are on the road to success or to failure, and the only sensible response is to keep walking. Persistence is what turns an ordinary road into the road to success.
