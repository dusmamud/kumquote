Setbacks invite us to stop and explain why stopping is reasonable. Obama's answer is to keep moving anyway. Pressing on does not mean ignoring pain or pretending everything is fine; it means refusing to let complaint become the place where we live. Energy spent on self-pity is energy taken from the work that could change things. The future, he suggests, belongs to those who continue despite the difficulty. When progress feels slow, press on — the next step may be the one that matters most.
