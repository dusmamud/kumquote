We wait for extraordinary opportunities while overlooking the ordinary work in front of us. Rohn's definition of success is plain: doing ordinary things extraordinarily well. The phone call returned promptly, the promise kept, the work checked twice, the customer treated with care. None of these moments makes a headline. But added up over months and years, they become a reputation, and a reputation becomes opportunity. Greatness is rarely a single act. It is the habit of bringing care and attention to the small, repeatable tasks that everyone else rushes past. Master the ordinary, and the extraordinary takes care of itself.
