Talking about a plan can feel like progress, but it is not the same as doing the work. Endless discussion, planning, and waiting for approval can quietly become a way of avoiding the first difficult step. Walt Disney points to the simple cure: begin. A small action breaks the spell of hesitation and gives you real information about what works. You will make mistakes, but mistakes made while doing teach far more than perfect plans made while waiting. Start with one concrete task today, finish it, and let that momentum carry you into the next one.
