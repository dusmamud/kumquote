We treat success as a finish line, a single moment when everything is won. But Ashe, who knew both triumph and hardship, saw it differently: the daily doing, the practice, the struggle, and the discipline are where success actually lives. A destination is reached once and then fades; the journey shapes you every single day. If you only value the ending, you will miss the life happening along the way. Enjoy the work itself, and the outcome becomes a bonus rather than the whole point.
