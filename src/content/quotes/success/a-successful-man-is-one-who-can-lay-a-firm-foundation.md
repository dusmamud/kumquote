Criticism, rejection, and insult are painful, but they do not have to destroy you. Every harsh word or unfair setback can become material for building something stronger, if you choose to use it that way. Successful people are not those who never faced opposition; they are those who turned opposition into foundation. The bricks thrown at you — doubt, failure, criticism — can become the base of your character and your work. Do not waste energy throwing them back. Pick them up, place them carefully, and build. What was meant to hurt you can become what holds you up.
