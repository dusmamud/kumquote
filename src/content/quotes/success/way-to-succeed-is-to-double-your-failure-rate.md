Failure is not the opposite of success but the fastest route to it. Watson, who built IBM, understood that every failure carries a lesson, and the more lessons we collect, the closer we come to the answer that works. People who fail rarely stay safe but also stay still; people who fail often are experimenting, learning, and moving toward success at twice the speed. Doubling your failure rate means doubling your attempts, your courage, and your learning. The goal is not to fail for its own sake, but to fail forward, quickly, until success becomes inevitable.
