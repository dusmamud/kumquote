Success breeds success, and Dumas captures the cycle in five words. One win brings confidence, and confidence brings bolder attempts; bolder attempts bring more wins, and the momentum builds on itself. The same is true in reverse for failure, which is why the first success matters so much. People trust winners, doors open for them, and opportunities gather around them. The lesson is to chase the first small victory with everything you have, because nothing will help the second one arrive like the first one did.
