Failure is usually treated as the opposite of success, but this line treats it as the raw material of success. Every failure carries information: what did not work, what was misunderstood, and what needs to change. Discouragement is the same — a signal to adjust, not a verdict to quit. People who succeed are not those who avoid failure; they are those who mine it for lessons and step forward on what they learned. The path to success is paved with attempts that did not work out.
