Every great work begins as something hidden inside ordinary material, and the artist's job is to reveal it. Michelangelo saw the finished figure before a single chip fell, and then did the slow, patient work of removing everything that was not the angel. Success often works the same way: the goal is clear long before it exists in the world, and progress means cutting away distraction, excess, and doubt. Vision without craft is only a daydream, and craft without vision is only labor. Hold the image steady, keep carving, and trust that the form is waiting inside the stone.
