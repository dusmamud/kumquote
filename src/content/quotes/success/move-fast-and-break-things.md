Perfectionism is a slow way to fail. This motto argues for speed over polish: ship the idea, learn from what breaks, fix it, and move again. The things that break are rarely fatal — and each break teaches something that months of planning never would. Moving fast does not mean moving carelessly; it means valuing learning over looking flawless. In fast-changing fields, the team that tries ten things learns more than the team that perfects one. Break things, learn fast, and let momentum do what hesitation never can.
