Disraeli traces success back to a single parent: boldness. Cautious plans rarely fail loudly, but they also rarely succeed greatly, because every worthwhile opportunity asks you to act before you feel fully ready. Audacity is not recklessness — it is the willingness to attempt what others call too big, too soon, or too unlikely. Timid people keep their dignity and lose the prize. If you want success, give it a bolder parent — dare a little more than feels comfortable, and let audacity open the door.
