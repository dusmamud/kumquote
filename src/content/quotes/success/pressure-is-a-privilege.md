Pressure feels heavy, but it is also a sign that you are somewhere that matters. Only people who have earned a big moment get to feel its weight. Instead of fearing pressure, you can welcome it as proof that your work has brought you to a place worth being. The athlete in the final, the speaker on the stage, the student at the exam — all of them feel pressure because the moment counts. Reframe it: pressure is not a punishment. It is a privilege, a sign that you have earned the chance to do something important.
