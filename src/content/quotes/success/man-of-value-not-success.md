Success is about what you gain, but value is about what you give. A person of success is measured by wealth, status, and recognition, all of which can fade. A person of value is measured by usefulness, honesty, and the difference they make to others, and that reputation endures. Einstein's advice is to aim at the deeper target: build character, share knowledge, solve real problems. Success may follow, but it will be built on something solid. Become valuable first, and success becomes a side effect rather than the goal.
