Success deserves its moment. We should pause, enjoy it, and thank those who helped. Gates warns that celebration is a teacher. Success flatters us; it tells us our methods were right and our judgment was sound, even when luck did much of the work. Failure is a stricter teacher. It points at the weak joint, the wrong assumption, the skipped step, and refuses to let us look away. The wise treat success as a rest stop and failure as a classroom. They enjoy the win, then ask a harder question: what did the loss teach that the win never could?
