We usually measure success by the height of the position — the title, the wealth, the applause — but this line asks us to measure the climb instead. Two people can reach the same place, yet one crossed far harder ground. The obstacles overcome reveal the real achievement: the discipline, the endurance, and the courage it took to keep going. Judging by position alone rewards luck and starting advantage; judging by obstacles rewards character. It also frees us from comparing our finish line with someone else's, because the true measure is how far we travelled from where we began.
