Mistakes are unavoidable for anyone doing anything worth doing. The first mistake is tuition — it teaches you what does not work, and that knowledge is worth the price. The tragedy begins only when the same mistake is repeated, because that means nothing was learned. Smart people keep a record of their errors, in their head if nowhere else, and change their method before the next attempt. Success is not a life without errors. It is a life where every error appears once, teaches its lesson, and is never invited back again.
