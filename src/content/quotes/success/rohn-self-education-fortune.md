School gives us the foundation to earn a living, and that matters, but the learning that changes a life usually happens after the classroom. Self-education — the books we choose, the skills we practice, the mentors we seek — is driven by curiosity rather than a syllabus, and it compounds over a lifetime. The person who keeps learning keeps growing, spotting opportunities that others miss and adapting when the world changes. Formal education opens the first door; the habit of teaching ourselves opens all the rest. Invest in your own learning, and the returns follow.
