Great achievements are rarely the result of single dramatic moments; they are the sum of ordinary days lived well. What we do each morning, how we spend our hours, and which tasks we prioritise quietly decide where we end up. A daily agenda filled with purposeful actions compounds over months and years into extraordinary results, while days spent drifting lead nowhere in particular. Maxwell's point is empowering: success is not hidden in some distant future but written into today's schedule. Change what you do today, and you change where you arrive tomorrow.
