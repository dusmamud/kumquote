Quitting has a logic to it. It promises relief, rest, and an end to the discomfort of trying. Lombardi's line is blunt because the choice it describes is blunt: winners are not the people who never wanted to quit, but the people who wanted to and kept going anyway. Persistence is not a talent; it is a decision made again and again, on the worst days. There is wisdom in knowing when to stop a wrong path. But most dreams do not die from a wise decision. They die from an ordinary Tuesday when quitting felt easier than continuing.
