The future does not belong to the lucky or the loud; it belongs to those who are genuinely good at what they do. Competence is a form of power that cannot be taken away: skills, knowledge, and judgement built through steady practice. Getting good is the first step, getting better is the daily discipline, and being the best is the result of refusing to settle. In a world full of noise and shortcuts, real ability stands out more than ever.
