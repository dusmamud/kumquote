Dreams feel magical when we first hold them, but Powell reminds us that magic is not the method. A dream becomes real through sweat, the physical effort of showing up; determination, the mental refusal to quit; and hard work, the steady repetition of both. None of these is glamorous, and none can be skipped. People who wait for a magical moment usually wait forever, while people who work see their dreams take shape one ordinary day at a time. The magic, if there is any, is simply what disciplined effort looks like from the outside.
