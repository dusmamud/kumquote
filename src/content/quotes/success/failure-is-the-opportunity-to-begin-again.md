Failure feels like an ending, but it is usually a beginning with better information. The first attempt teaches us what does not work, which is knowledge we could not have gained any other way. Starting again is not starting from zero; it is starting from experience. The intelligent restart keeps what worked, discards what did not, and moves forward with clearer eyes. People who fear failure avoid this gift entirely, protecting themselves from the lesson by never attempting the thing at all.
