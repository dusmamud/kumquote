Every achievement begins as a want — a clear, burning desire for something better than the present. Without desire, plans have no fuel and effort has no reason; with it, obstacles become problems to solve rather than excuses to stop. Desire is what turns a casual wish into a goal, and a goal into a plan. It does not have to be loud or dramatic; it only has to be genuine and specific enough to guide daily choices. If you want to achieve something, first ask whether you truly desire it.
