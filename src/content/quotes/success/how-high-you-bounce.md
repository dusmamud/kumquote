Everyone eventually hits bottom; the fall itself is not the measure of a person. What matters is the bounce: how quickly you gather yourself, learn from the blow, and rise again. Patton, a soldier who knew defeat and recovery, measured success not by how high someone climbs but by how high they rebound. A strong bounce needs honesty about what went wrong and the courage to try again differently. The bottom is not the end of the story unless you decide to stay there. Rise, and the fall becomes part of your strength.
