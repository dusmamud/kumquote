Success feels like luck or mystery, but Wilde treats it like a science experiment: set up the right conditions, and the result follows. That is encouraging news, because conditions can be built. Study the field, practice the skill, find good mentors, protect your time, and repeat the process. None of this guarantees a specific outcome on a specific day, but over time the odds shift heavily in our favor. Stop waiting for a lucky break and start building the conditions. Results are not magic; they are the natural product of the right setup.
