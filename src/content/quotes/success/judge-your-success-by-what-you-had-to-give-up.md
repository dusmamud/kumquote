The price of an achievement reveals its true worth. A success that cost our health, family, or integrity may look impressive yet feel hollow within. The Dalai Lama invites us to measure success not only by what we gained but by what we sacrificed to gain it, because a prize bought too dearly is no prize at all. Wise ambition counts the cost before paying it and refuses trades that betray what matters most. The best successes are those we can look back on with pride, knowing the price was fair and the gain was real.
