This is a complete strategy for facing an uncertain future in three short moves. Expecting the best keeps hope and energy alive, because people who expect good outcomes work harder toward them. Preparing for the worst keeps that hope honest, so a setback becomes a problem to solve rather than a shock that ends the effort. And capitalizing on what comes is the skill that ties them together: whatever actually arrives, good or bad, becomes material to use. Optimism without preparation is fragile, and preparation without optimism is grim. Together, they make a person ready for almost anything.
