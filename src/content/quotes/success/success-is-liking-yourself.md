Real success is measured from the inside, not by applause or wealth. It is the quiet satisfaction of liking who you are, finding meaning in your work, and being proud of how you do it. Money and recognition can be borrowed or faked, but self-respect cannot. When you can look at your life and honestly like all three, you have achieved something most people chase their whole lives without finding. Angelou's definition is simple and demanding: succeed in a way you can be proud of, or it is not success at all.
