Waiting to feel ready is the most common way to never begin. Greatness is not a starting requirement; it is a result of starting. The first attempt will be rough, the first version will be flawed, and that is exactly how it should be. Skill grows through doing, not through preparing to do. Every expert was once a beginner who refused to wait for permission. The only real qualification for starting is willingness. Begin badly if you must — but begin. Greatness is built by those who start before they feel great.
