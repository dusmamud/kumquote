Carnegie gives success a three-part recipe, and enthusiasm leads it. Not talent alone, not luck, but a burning energy for the work, steadied by common sense and carried by persistence. Enthusiasm without judgment burns out or runs off course; judgment without enthusiasm never starts. Persistence holds the other two together through the long middle, when excitement fades and obstacles pile up. Together they form a combination that beats raw brilliance more often than we expect. The quote is practical encouragement: bring your fire, keep your head, and refuse to stop.
