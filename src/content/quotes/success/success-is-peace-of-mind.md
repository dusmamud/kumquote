Most definitions of success depend on things we cannot control: the scoreboard, the market, other people's opinions. Wooden's definition depends on only one thing — whether we did our best. That makes success available to everyone, every day, regardless of the outcome. Peace of mind follows effort, not victory. When we know we gave our full effort, we can rest, win or lose. When we know we held back, no trophy quiets the doubt. Measure yourself by effort, and success stops being a moving target.
