A detour is annoying, but it is not the end of the journey. It forces a different route, not a different destination. Failure works the same way: it closes one path and asks us to find another, often teaching us something the easy road never would. People who treat failure as a dead end stop traveling; people who treat it as a detour keep moving and usually arrive wiser. This quote offers a gentler way to think about setbacks. We do not have to pretend failure feels good, only to remember that it rarely means the journey is over.
