Success is often described as if it were a destination reached by the lucky or the gifted. Hilton points to a simpler pattern: successful people act, and they keep acting after mistakes. Motion creates opportunity, feedback and momentum, while waiting creates nothing. Mistakes are not proof of failure here; they are the natural cost of moving forward. Quitting is the only move that ends the process. The quote encourages starting before conditions feel perfect and treating errors as information rather than verdicts. Keep moving, learn quickly, and let persistence do the work that talent alone cannot.
