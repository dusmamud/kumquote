People often call success luck, but luck is only the moment when opportunity arrives. Unser, a racing champion, knew that opportunity means nothing without preparation: the hours of practice, the study of the track, the discipline of the pit crew. When the chance comes, the prepared person is ready to take it, while the unprepared one watches it pass. You cannot control when opportunity appears, but you can control how ready you are. Prepare quietly and thoroughly, and when your moment arrives, you will not need luck.
