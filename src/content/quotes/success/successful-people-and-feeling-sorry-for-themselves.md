Setbacks hurt everyone equally. The failed launch, the lost client, and the rejected idea sting no matter who you are. The difference is not the pain itself but the length of the stay. Some people move through disappointment quickly: they grieve it, learn from it, and return to work. Others set up camp inside it, replaying the unfairness until it becomes the story of their life. Feeling sorry for ourselves is natural for a day, but as a habit it quietly steals the energy that recovery needs.
