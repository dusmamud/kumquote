Going far in life is less about talent than about willingness. The people who travel furthest are the ones who accept the risk, take the chance, and act while others wait for certainty. Doing and daring mean speaking up, starting the project, asking the question, and facing the possibility of failure. Each bold step teaches something, even when it does not work out. Caution has its place, but a life of only caution goes nowhere. Dare a little, do a little, and the distance you travel will surprise you.
