People love to search for a secret formula, as if success were hidden behind a trick no one has shared. There is none. The ingredients are plain and well known: prepare carefully, work hard, and learn from every failure instead of hiding from it. Each part matters. Preparation without effort is just planning, effort without preparation is wasted energy, and neither grows without learning from mistakes. Accepting that there is no shortcut is freeing, because it means the path is open to anyone willing to do the work.
