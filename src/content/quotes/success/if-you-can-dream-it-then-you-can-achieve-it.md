Dreams are often dismissed as idle wishes, but this quote treats them as the first step of achievement. Everything built by human hands began as an image in someone's mind: a building, a business, a book, a better life. Dreaming gives direction to effort; without it, hard work has nowhere to go. The danger is not dreaming too big but never letting a dream become a plan. When a dream is clear enough to guide daily choices, it stops being fantasy and starts being a destination. Believe the dream, then work toward it step by step.
