Gyms build muscles, but they cannot build the desire that makes a champion. That desire lives inside a person: a dream strong enough to survive early mornings, injuries, and defeats. Two athletes can follow the same training plan and get different results, because one wants it more deeply. This quote reminds us that the most important training happens in the mind and heart, not in the body. Equipment and coaches help, but they cannot replace vision. Champions are made first by what they carry inside, and only second by where they train.
