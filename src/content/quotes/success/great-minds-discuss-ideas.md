What we talk about shapes what we think about, and what we think about shapes what we become. Conversations about ideas — how things work, what could be built, what should change — pull the mind toward creation and understanding. Conversations about events keep us informed but passive, watching life happen to others. Conversations about people, especially gossip, shrink the mind to the size of other people's business. None of these is forbidden, but the balance matters. People who spend most of their talk on ideas tend to build things; people who spend it on people tend to stay stuck.
