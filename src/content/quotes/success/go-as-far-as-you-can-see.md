You do not need to see the whole road before you start walking. It is enough to see the next stretch clearly and move toward it. Each step forward reveals a little more of the path: new ideas, new people, new chances that were hidden before. Waiting for a full map is just another way of never beginning. Trust that the view will widen as you move. Go as far as you can see now, and when you arrive, the horizon will have moved with you. Progress is made one visible step at a time.
