Trying to please everybody is a strategy with no finish line. Every audience wants something different, and every concession to one group disappoints another. The result is a life shaped by other people's preferences, with no centre of its own. Success, whatever its key may be, requires the opposite: a clear sense of what matters to us, and the willingness to disappoint some people in service of it. Not everyone will approve, and that is not a failure — it is the price of having a direction.
