Excuses are comfortable, and that is precisely why they are dangerous. Each one feels reasonable in the moment, yet together they build a life of stalled plans and unkept promises. Nightingale transformed nursing in the face of indifference and opposition, and she did it by refusing excuses from herself and from others. Accountability is not harshness; it is honesty about what you control. When you stop explaining why something cannot be done, you free your energy to find how it can. Success follows those who own their part.
