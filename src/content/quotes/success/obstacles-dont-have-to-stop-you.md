Every worthwhile goal puts a wall in front of us sooner or later: a rejection, a lack of money, a skill we do not yet have. The instinct is to turn back and choose an easier road. But walls are rarely as solid as they look, and they often mark the exact place where growth begins. Going around, climbing over, or patiently working through takes longer. This quote reminds us that obstacles are not stop signs; they are part of the route, and persistence is how we pass them.
