It is strange to hear the most successful basketball player in history talk about failure, but that is exactly the point. His success was not built despite failure but through it: thousands of missed shots, lost games, and moments when he fell short. Each failure was information, not a verdict. Most people treat failure as proof they should stop; he treated it as practice. This quote reverses the usual story. Success does not belong to those who never fail. It belongs to those who fail often, learn quickly, and keep showing up anyway.
