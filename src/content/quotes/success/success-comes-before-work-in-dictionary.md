Everyone wants success first and work second, but life arranges them the other way around. The work always comes before the reward: the practice before the performance, the preparation before the result, the effort before the recognition. There is no version of the story where success arrives before the work is done. Sassoon's joke carries a serious truth. If you want the outcome, accept the order. Do the work first, do it well, and let success take its proper place after it.
