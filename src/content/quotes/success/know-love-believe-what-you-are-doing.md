Success advice is usually complicated, but Will Rogers reduces it to three plain ingredients. Know what you are doing: learn the craft, understand the work, be competent. Love what you are doing: passion carries you through the dull stretches that competence alone cannot. Believe in what you are doing: conviction keeps you going when others doubt. Remove any one of the three and success wobbles — skill without love burns out, love without skill frustrates, and both without belief collapse at the first setback. Simple, yes. Easy, no. But the formula holds.
