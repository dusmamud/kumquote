Esar jokes that luck has four parents, and the joke carries a serious point. Aspiration points you at something worth having; desperation supplies urgency when comfort would let you drift; perspiration is the plain work no shortcut replaces; and inspiration is the spark that keeps the work from going stale. Call the result good fortune if you like, but notice how much of it you can manufacture. If success has not found you yet, check which ingredient is missing — a clearer aim, a sharper reason, harder work, or a fresh idea — and add it first.
