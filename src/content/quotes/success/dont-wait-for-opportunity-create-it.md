Opportunity rarely knocks on the door of those who sit waiting for it. Shaw's advice is to stop watching for the perfect opening and start building one: learn a skill, start a project, reach out to someone, or solve a problem nobody else has noticed. Creating opportunity means taking initiative before conditions are ideal, and trusting that effort will open doors that waiting never would. The people we call lucky are usually those who created their own luck by acting first. If the opportunity you want does not exist yet, you are free to create it.
