Success begins with a decision, not a wish. Hunt insists on exactness: decide precisely what you want, because a vague goal produces vague effort. Then comes the second half of the sentence, the part most people skip — resolve to pay the price. Every achievement has a cost in time, discipline, and sacrifice, and the price is known before the reward arrives. People who succeed are not those who avoid the cost but those who agree to it in advance. Decide clearly, then pay willingly, and the goal stops being a dream.
