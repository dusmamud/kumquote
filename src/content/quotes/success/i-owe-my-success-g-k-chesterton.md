Good advice is easy to find and hard to use, because it arrives dressed as other people's experience. Chesterton confesses something many successful people quietly know: he listened respectfully to the best advice, and then did the opposite. The point is not to ignore wisdom. It is that advice is a starting point, not a command. Other people's experience can show us the map, but only we know the ground under our feet. Listen carefully, weigh it honestly, then trust your own judgment. Success belongs to those who think for themselves, even when it means disagreeing with the experts.
