Without a plan of your own, you will drift into whatever plan the world hands you: someone else's schedule, someone else's priorities, someone else's definition of success. Drifting feels easy because it asks nothing of you, but it quietly spends your years on goals you never chose. Designing your life does not require a perfect map, only an honest decision about what matters and a few deliberate steps toward it. Write your own plan, however simple, and follow it. A small plan you own is worth far more than a grand one you borrowed.
