This definition takes success out of other people's hands. Trophies, promotions and applause depend on judges, markets and luck, but knowing you gave your best depends only on you. It asks a harder question than whether you won: did you prepare honestly, work with full attention, and become a little more capable than you were? When the answer is yes, the result stops mattering so much, because the growth itself is the achievement. Measure yourself against your own capacity, not against someone else's scoreboard, and success becomes something you can claim every single day.
