Success looks mysterious from the outside, but up close it is usually unglamorous: the same good habits, repeated daily, without applause. Successful people are not necessarily smarter or luckier; they have built routines that make progress automatic — planning, practising, finishing what they start, and learning from mistakes. Habits decide what you do when motivation fades, and motivation always fades. The encouraging part is that habits can be chosen and built one at a time. Start with one small habit that serves your goal, repeat it until it is easy, then add another.
