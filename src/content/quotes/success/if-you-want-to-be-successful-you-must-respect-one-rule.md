Coelho reduces success to a single rule of honesty, and it is the hardest one to keep. We lie to ourselves in small, comfortable ways: about why we failed, about what we really want, about whether we are doing our best. Each self-deception feels harmless, but together they build a life on a crooked foundation. Success requires a clear view of reality, including the parts we would rather not see. The person who tells himself the truth can fix what is broken and choose what is real. Self-deception guarantees we keep solving the wrong problem.
