Every finished thing looks obvious in hindsight. Before it is done,
it looks impossible — that is not evidence that it cannot be done,
it is just what unfinished work feels like from the outside. Mandela
spent twenty-seven years in prison before leading his country, so he
knew the feeling from the inside, not from a poster.

The use of this quote is a test. When a goal feels impossible, ask
whether anything has actually proved it impossible, or whether you
are only standing before it. If no proof exists, the next step is
not confidence — it is one small, verifiable piece of work. Do it,
and the word impossible gets a little smaller. Repeat until it is
gone, and the thing is done.
