Success rarely arrives on schedule, and impatience is one of its quietest enemies. Many good ideas fail not because they were wrong but because their owners gave up too early, while many bad ideas survive simply because their owners waited. Patience lets compound effort do its work: skills deepen, trust builds, and small gains add up into something large. It is not passive waiting but active endurance, staying in the game while results gather. Those who can wait well outlast those who can only rush. Patience is a key, and it opens doors speed cannot.
