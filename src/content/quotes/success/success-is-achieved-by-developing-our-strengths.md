We spend most of our energy patching what we are bad at, and wonder why we feel average. Vos Savant flips the strategy: success grows from what is already strong in us, sharpened until it becomes exceptional. Weaknesses still need managing, but they are rarely the source of greatness. A singer does not become great by fixing her accounting; she becomes great by singing better. Find the talent that comes most naturally, invest in it deliberately, and let it carry you further than a lifetime of fixing flaws ever could.
