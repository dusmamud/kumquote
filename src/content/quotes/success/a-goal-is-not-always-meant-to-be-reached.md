Some goals exist less as destinations and more as directions. Aiming at something far away gives our days purpose, focus and a standard to measure ourselves against, even if we never fully arrive. The value is in the aiming itself: the discipline it builds, the skills it demands and the person we become while reaching. If we judge a goal only by whether we reach it, we miss what it gave us along the way. So choose goals that stretch you, pursue them seriously, and let the pursuit matter as much as the arrival.
