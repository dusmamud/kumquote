Trust is contagious, and it begins with self-trust. When we carry ourselves with quiet confidence, others sense it and feel safer following our lead, believing in our ideas, and giving us responsibility. Doubt, by contrast, spreads just as quickly: if we do not believe in ourselves, others hesitate to believe in us too. This is not about arrogance or pretending to know everything, but about a steady belief in our ability to learn, adapt, and deliver. Confidence earns trust, and trust opens doors that talent alone cannot.
