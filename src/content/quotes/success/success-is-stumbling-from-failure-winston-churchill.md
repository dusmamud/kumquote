Success is often imagined as a staircase, each step higher than the last. Churchill knew better. Real success stumbles: a failed product, a lost election, a plan that fell apart in public. The difference between those who succeed and those who do not is rarely the number of falls. It is the enthusiasm they carry into the next attempt. Enthusiasm here is not naive cheerfulness. It is the refusal to let a failure harden into a verdict about the future. Keep your curiosity and your energy alive through the stumbles, and each failure becomes a step rather than a stop.
