Bennett challenges the usual scoreboard of success — money, titles, and status — and replaces it with a better one: the difference we make. Climbing high means little if no one is better off for it, while even a modest life can be a great success if it lifts others. This view does not dismiss ambition; it gives ambition a purpose beyond ourselves. When we measure success by contribution rather than accumulation, our work gains meaning, our relationships deepen, and our achievements last longer than applause. True success is measured by what we give, not only by what we gain.
