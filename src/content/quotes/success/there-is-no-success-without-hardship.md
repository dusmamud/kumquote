Comfort rarely produces anything lasting. Sophocles reminds us that hardship is not merely an obstacle to success but often the material from which it is made. Difficult seasons force us to learn, adapt and discover strengths we never needed in easy times. The struggle itself teaches patience, resourcefulness and grit, qualities no shortcut can provide. When hardship arrives, it is worth asking what it is training us for, rather than only wishing it away. Endure it well, and success built on it will stand far longer.
