Success leaves a trail, and Robbins tells us to follow it instead of wandering. Every achievement has a method behind it: habits, choices, and routines that can be observed and repeated. Copying is not cheating; it is the oldest way humans learn. Find the person who already has what you want, study what they actually do day by day, and practise the same moves. Their results came from their actions, not from their luck. Borrow the method, adapt it to your own life, and the same causes will tend to produce the same effects.
