Circumstances can open doors or close them, but they do not decide the kind of person who walks through. Washington's line places the weight on character: honesty, diligence and courage matter more than luck or background. Two people can face the same setback and end in very different places, because one lets conditions dictate the outcome while the other responds with steady integrity. Character is built in small, unseen choices long before it is tested in public. Build it well, and no circumstance can fully define you.
