Most dreams are not blocked by lack of talent, money, or opportunity, but by the absence of two simple things: the will to try and the belief that trying could work. Without the will, the dream stays a daydream; without belief, the first difficulty ends the attempt. Together, they are enough to begin, and beginning is usually the hardest part. Once we try, we learn, adapt, and discover resources we did not know we had. The distance between dreaming and doing is shorter than it looks; it is crossed by a single decision to believe and begin.
