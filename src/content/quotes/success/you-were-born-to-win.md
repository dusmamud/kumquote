Potential is the starting point, not the finish line. Being born with talent, energy, or opportunity means little until it is organised into a plan. Planning turns a vague ambition into steps; preparation turns those steps into ability; expectation turns ability into the confidence that carries us through difficulty. Each stage matters, and skipping one weakens the rest. A plan without preparation is a fantasy, and preparation without expectation falters at the first setback. Winning, in this view, is not luck or magic.
