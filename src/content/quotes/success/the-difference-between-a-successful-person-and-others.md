Talent and knowledge are common; what separates those who succeed is the will to keep going when effort gets hard. Strength fades, knowledge becomes outdated, but will is the decision to act again after every setback. Most people stop not because they cannot continue, but because they choose comfort over the next difficult step. Success belongs to the person who makes that step anyway, day after day. Cultivate your will the way you would train a muscle. It is the one advantage nobody can take from you, and it compounds with every use.
