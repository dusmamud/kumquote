Where we start is not our fault, but staying there is a choice we make daily. Our background, our neighborhood, our family's expectations — all of them shape us, but none of them has to define us. The first step toward success is a mental one: refusing to believe that our starting point is our destination. That refusal is not denial; it is determination. People who escape limiting circumstances do not wait for permission to leave them. They decide, then they move. Your environment is a beginning, not a verdict.
