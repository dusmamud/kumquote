Education is the strongest prevention a society has. When a child learns to read, to think, and to hope, whole futures open up that once looked closed. Ignorance, by contrast, leaves people with few choices, and few choices often lead to trouble. Opening a school is not just about books and classrooms; it is about giving young people a path toward work, dignity, and a better life. Every door opened to learning is a door closed to despair. Invest in teaching, and you invest in safer, fairer communities for everyone.
