Success without struggle tastes bland. It is the failures along the way — the rejections, the wrong turns, the plans that fell apart — that give achievement its depth and meaning. Without them, we would never know what we overcame, and we would value the result far less. Failure also seasons us: it sharpens judgment, builds humility, and teaches lessons that smooth sailing never could. Instead of fearing failure as the opposite of success, we can see it as an ingredient. A little of it makes the final taste richer.
