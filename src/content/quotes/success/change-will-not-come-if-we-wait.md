Waiting feels safe because it costs nothing today, but it quietly hands our future to someone else. Obama's call is for ordinary people to stop waiting for a hero or a perfect moment and to become the change themselves. History moves when enough people act where they stand — in their families, workplaces and neighbourhoods. The ones we have been waiting for are simply us, once we decide to begin. Change does not arrive from elsewhere; it starts the moment we take responsibility for it.
