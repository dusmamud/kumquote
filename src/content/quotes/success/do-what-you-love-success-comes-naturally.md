Frost warns that chasing success directly is a trap: the harder we grasp at it, the more it slips away. Success is a byproduct, not a target. It follows naturally when we pour ourselves into work we love and believe in, doing it well and honestly over time. Passion brings the energy that effort requires, and belief brings the patience that results demand. Instead of asking how to become successful, Frost suggests asking what we love enough to do well — and letting success arrive on its own, as a consequence rather than a goal.
