Before success appears in the world, it first appears in the mind. People who see themselves as capable act like capable people: they take on challenges, recover from setbacks, and spot opportunities others miss. People who see themselves as failures do the opposite, hesitating until the chance passes. Thinking of yourself as a success is not pretending you have already arrived; it is choosing the identity that makes the right actions natural. The mind follows the story it is told. Tell it a story of competence and persistence, and your behaviour will slowly rise to match it.
