Victory tastes ordinary when it comes easily, but after defeat it carries a depth that easy wins never have. Defeat teaches what comfort cannot: where we are weak, what we truly want, and how much we are willing to give. The person who has known loss does not celebrate loudly; they celebrate deeply, because they understand the cost of the moment. Do not fear defeat, then. Let it sharpen you. When the win finally comes, it will be sweeter precisely because you know what it took to earn it.
