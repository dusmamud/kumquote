Greatness is never reached by a smooth, easy road. The path climbs through difficulty, doubt, and hard work, and that roughness is not a sign that you are lost — it is a sign that you are climbing. Easy roads lead to ordinary places. The heights demand effort, patience, and the willingness to keep going when the ground is steep. If your journey feels rough, take heart: the struggle is part of the climb. Those who reach the top are not the ones who found the easiest way, but the ones who kept walking the rough road.
