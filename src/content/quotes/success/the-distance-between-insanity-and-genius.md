The same bold idea looks like madness before it works and genius after it works. Feirstein's joke carries a serious truth: the world judges unconventional thinking by its outcome, not by its logic. That means innovators must endure being called foolish while their work is still unproven. Success is the only measurement the crowd trusts, so the distance between ridicule and admiration is simply the result. The lesson is not to fear looking foolish. Every breakthrough was once an idea that sensible people dismissed, until it succeeded.
