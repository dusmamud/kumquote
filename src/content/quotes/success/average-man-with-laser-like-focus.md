Greatness does not require being extraordinary at birth. Lee, who studied focus as much as fighting, believed the edge comes from concentration: one goal, pursued without scattering attention in a dozen directions. The average person with laser-like focus outperforms the gifted person whose energy is spread thin, because focus turns ordinary ability into relentless progress. Distraction is the real enemy of achievement. Choose one thing that matters, give it your full attention, and let the narrow beam of your focus do what talent alone cannot.
