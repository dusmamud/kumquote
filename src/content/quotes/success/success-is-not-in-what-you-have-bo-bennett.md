Bennett separates the scoreboard from the person. Possessions can be gained, lost, and counted, but they say little about the quality of a life; a person can own a great deal and still be small in patience, honesty, or kindness. Character, by contrast, travels with you everywhere and decides how you handle both fortune and failure. If you want a durable kind of success, invest less in acquiring and more in becoming — the person you are is the only wealth you never have to guard against losing.
