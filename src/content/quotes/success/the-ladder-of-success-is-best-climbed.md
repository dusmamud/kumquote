Opportunity rarely arrives as a grand invitation; it shows up as a small rung, a chance meeting, a task nobody else wants, or a skill worth learning. The ladder of success is climbed one rung at a time, and each rung is an opportunity used well. People who wait for the perfect opening often wait forever, while those who step onto whatever rung is available keep rising. Do not despise small chances. Take the rung in front of you, stand on it firmly, and reach for the next. That is how high places are reached.
