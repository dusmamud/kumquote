Stone's definition has no room for talent or luck, and that is the point. Trying once is common; keeping on trying after the first failure, and the fifth, is rare — and rarity is what separates the successful from the rest. Each attempt teaches something, even the failed ones, so persistence is also education. If your efforts have not worked yet, the question is not whether you are capable but whether you will try again with what you have learned. The people who keep trying are the ones still in the game.
