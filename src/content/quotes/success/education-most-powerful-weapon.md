Weapons force change on others; education changes the person who holds it, and through that person, the world. Learning gives people the ability to see their situation clearly, to question what they were told to accept, and to imagine something better. It opens doors that birth and circumstance kept shut, and it cannot be taken away once it is earned. A single educated mind can lift a family, and a generation of educated minds can lift a nation. That is why those who fear change often fear schools first.
