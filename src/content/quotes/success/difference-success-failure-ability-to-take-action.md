Ideas alone decide nothing, and this line draws a sharp line between dreaming and doing. Two people can share the same talent, the same plan, and the same opportunity, yet only the one who acts will move forward. Action turns knowledge into experience, and experience into results. Waiting for certainty is the most common form of standing still, because certainty usually arrives only after the first step is taken. Failure follows action sometimes, but so does success, and neither follows inaction at all. The difference is rarely ability; it is the willingness to begin.
