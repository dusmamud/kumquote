Brown narrows the distance between you and your dream to two things, and both live inside you. No wall, rival, or circumstance is named, because the real obstacle is usually the combination of unwillingness to begin and disbelief that it could work. Together they are enough to start, and starting is how evidence replaces doubt. Each small attempt proves a little more is possible, which feeds the will to try again. The dream is not far away in the way a distant city is far away. It is one decision and one act away, repeated.
