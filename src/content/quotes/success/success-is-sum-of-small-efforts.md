Great achievements rarely come from one dramatic act. They are built from ordinary actions repeated with care: one page written, one lesson studied, one healthy choice made, one customer served well. Robert Collier reminds us that consistency matters more than intensity. A burst of effort followed by weeks of neglect achieves little, while modest daily effort compounds into skill, trust, and results. The secret is to make the small effort easy enough to repeat and important enough to protect. Over months and years, those quiet repetitions add up to a life that looks, from the outside, like sudden success.
