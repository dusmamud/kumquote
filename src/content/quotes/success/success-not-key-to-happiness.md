Many people treat success as the doorway to happiness, promising themselves joy once the promotion, the money, or the recognition arrives. Schweitzer reverses the order: happiness is the foundation, and success grows from it. People who enjoy their work learn faster, persist longer, and attract others, which makes achievement more likely. Chasing success while miserable often leads to reaching the goal and finding it empty. The practical lesson is to build a life you enjoy now, doing work you care about with people you like, and let success follow as a by-product rather than a master.
