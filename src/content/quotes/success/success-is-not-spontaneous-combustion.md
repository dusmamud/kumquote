Success rarely arrives by accident, and waiting for it to happen is not a plan. A fire needs a spark and someone willing to strike it, and the same is true of any ambition worth pursuing. Setting yourself on fire means bringing your own energy, urgency and enthusiasm instead of expecting circumstances to provide them. It is the decision to act with heat: to start before conditions are perfect, to work with visible passion, and to keep feeding the flame when progress is slow. Purpose that burns attracts help and momentum. The spark is yours to light.
