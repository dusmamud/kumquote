We search for complicated secrets of success, yet the formula is plain and has never changed. Rise early, while the day is still yours and the mind is clear. Work hard, doing the real work rather than talking about it. And strike oil: recognise the opportunity in front of you and act on it decisively. Most people wait for luck to find them, but luck favours those who are already awake and already working. Keep the formula simple, repeat it daily, and let the results arrive in their own time.
