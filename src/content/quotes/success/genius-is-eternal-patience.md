Genius looks like magic from the outside, but Michelangelo reduces it to a single word: patience. The masterpieces that seem effortless took years of slow, careful work, correcting, waiting, and returning to the same stone again and again. Talent matters, but talent without patience burns bright and fades fast. Eternal patience means staying with a problem longer than anyone else is willing to, until the work finally gives up its secret. What the world calls genius is often just patience that refused to quit. Stay with it, and time will do the rest.
