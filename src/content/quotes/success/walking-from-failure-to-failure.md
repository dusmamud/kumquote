The road to success is not a straight line; it is a series of stumbles. What separates those who arrive from those who quit is not the absence of failure but the refusal to let failure steal their enthusiasm. Each setback is a lesson, not a verdict, and the person who keeps their energy and curiosity alive through the hard parts eventually reaches the goal. Enthusiasm is fuel. Protect it when things go wrong, because it is the thing that carries you from one failure to the next until the failures finally run out.
