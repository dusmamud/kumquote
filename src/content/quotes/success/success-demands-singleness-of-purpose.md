Divided attention produces divided results. Lombardi's demand is for singleness of purpose: one clear aim pursued with full energy, rather than many aims pursued halfway. Distraction is the quiet enemy of achievement, because it feels busy while producing little. When purpose is single, decisions become simpler, effort compounds, and small daily actions point in the same direction. Choose the one thing that matters most right now, and give it your undivided attention. Success follows focus far more often than it follows talent alone.
