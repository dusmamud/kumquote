Everyone wants the elevator: the shortcut, the lucky break, the overnight rise. Zig Ziglar reminds us that no such lift exists. Success is climbed one step at a time, through ordinary days of effort that nobody applauds. The stairs are slow, and that is the point — each step builds the strength, skill, and judgement that the destination demands. People who skip the climb often cannot hold what they reach, because they never grew into it. So stop waiting for the doors to open. Put one foot on the first step, then the next, and keep going. The climb itself is what makes you ready.
