A reputation is built slowly, one honest decision at a time, over years of showing up and keeping promises. It is the sum of countless small moments when nobody was watching. Yet a single careless act can undo it in minutes, because trust is far easier to break than to build. Remembering this changes how we behave under pressure. When a shortcut tempts us, the question is not only what we gain today but what twenty years of steady work is worth. People who protect their name the way they protect their savings rarely regret it.
