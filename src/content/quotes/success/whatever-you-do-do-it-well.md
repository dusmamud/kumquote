Excellence is a habit, not a special occasion, and this short line makes it a rule for everything. Whatever you do, from the smallest task to the largest project, do it well. Careless work in small things becomes careless work in large things, because habits do not respect size. Doing things well takes longer and asks more, but it builds a reputation, a skill, and a pride that shortcuts never can. People notice quality even when they cannot name it. Whatever the task in front of you, give it your best, and let the work speak for itself.
