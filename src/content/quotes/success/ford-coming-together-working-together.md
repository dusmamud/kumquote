Great work is rarely a solo performance, and this line maps the three stages of turning a group into a team. Coming together is only the beginning — the easy part, full of good intentions. Keeping together is harder, because it demands patience, trust, and working through disagreement without falling apart. Working together is the real test, where shared effort produces something no one could build alone. Each stage depends on the one before it, and skipping ahead never works. Lasting success belongs to people who can gather, endure together, and then truly collaborate.
