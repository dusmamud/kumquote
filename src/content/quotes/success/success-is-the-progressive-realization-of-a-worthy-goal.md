Nightingale defines success not as a finish line but as movement toward something worthwhile. That changes who counts as successful: anyone steadily advancing toward a goal they value is already succeeding, whether or not the world has noticed yet. The word progressive matters, because it turns success into a daily practice rather than a single event. A worthy goal gives direction, and each step toward it adds meaning. This definition is generous and demanding at once. It asks only that the goal be worth pursuing and that progress be real, however small the steps may seem.
