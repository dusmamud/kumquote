We often picture success as a solo achievement, one person's talent and effort standing alone. Chopra reminds us that the picture is incomplete. Lasting success always involves other people: the colleague who shared an idea, the friend who opened a door, the team that carried the work through a hard week. Failure, by contrast, often happens in isolation, when we are too proud or too afraid to ask for help. The lesson is practical. Build relationships before you need them, share credit, and let others into the work. Together we go further than any of us can go alone.
