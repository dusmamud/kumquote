Lauder, who built a cosmetics empire from a kitchen table, dismisses the romance of dreaming. Dreams are pleasant, but they do not build anything on their own. Her success came from long hours, persistence, and a refusal to wait for opportunity to arrive. The quote is a reminder that ambition without effort is only wishing. We can admire our goals for years, or we can work for them daily. Lauder chose work, and the work — not the dream — is what turned her vision into something real.
