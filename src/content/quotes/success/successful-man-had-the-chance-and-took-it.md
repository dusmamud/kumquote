Opportunity visits everyone, but it rewards only those who act on it. Many people recognise a good chance yet hesitate, waiting for a better moment or a guarantee that never comes. Meanwhile, others seize the moment with whatever they have and learn as they go. Success rarely belongs to the person with the most chances; it belongs to the one who takes the chance in front of them. Readiness matters less than willingness, because action turns a fleeting opening into a lasting result. The difference is not luck, but courage.
