Esar's playful list hides a serious truth: success is rarely one thing. Aspiration gives it direction, the dream that points us forward. Desperation supplies urgency, the pressure that makes us act. Perspiration is the daily work, the unglamorous effort repeated until it compounds. And inspiration is the spark that keeps the whole mix alive when the work gets hard. Call the result good fortune if you like, but it is fortune of a particular kind — the kind that arrives only for those who bring all four ingredients together.
