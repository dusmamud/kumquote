Morgan built an empire on steel and railroads, yet he credits his success to two words: know people. Deals, talent, trust, and opportunity all flow through human beings, and the person who understands people can move all of them. Knowing people means more than remembering names; it means understanding what motivates them, what they fear, and what they need. Money and strategy matter, but they follow relationships. In the end, every business is a people business, and the one who knows people best holds the real advantage.
