Good is comfortable, and that comfort can become a trap. A decent job, a safe routine, or a satisfactory result can quietly hold you back from something far better. Reaching for great means risking what is good, and that risk frightens most people into staying put. But settling for good when great is possible is its own kind of loss. Letting go is not reckless when the goal is clear and the effort is real. Sometimes the only way to reach great is to release your grip on good.
