Before the world can trust you, you must trust yourself. Self-trust is the quiet confidence that you will keep your word, learn what you need, and recover when things go wrong. Without it, even great talent hesitates, second-guesses, and waits for permission. With it, you act decisively and learn quickly from mistakes. Self-trust is not arrogance; it is built slowly, by keeping small promises to yourself day after day. Each promise kept adds a brick to the foundation. Build that trust first, and success has something solid to stand on.
