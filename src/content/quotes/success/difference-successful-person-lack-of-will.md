Lombardi, who coached champions, insists that success rarely comes down to talent or knowledge. Most people have enough strength and enough understanding to succeed; what they lack is will — the stubborn determination to keep going when it gets hard. Strength fades without will, and knowledge sits unused without it. The will to persist, to practise, and to refuse defeat is the true dividing line. It is good news, too: will is not something we are born with or without, but something we can choose and strengthen every day.
