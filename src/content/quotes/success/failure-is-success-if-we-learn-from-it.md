A failed attempt is only wasted if we walk away having learned nothing. Every mistake carries information about what does not work, and that information is worth more than a lucky win that teaches us nothing. People who treat failure as a lesson keep moving forward with better judgement each time, while those who fear it stop trying altogether. The difference between a setback and a defeat is simply what we do next. Study what went wrong, adjust, and try again. When failure becomes a teacher, it stops being an ending and starts being a step toward success.
