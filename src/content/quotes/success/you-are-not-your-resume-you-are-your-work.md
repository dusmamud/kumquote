A resume lists where you have been, but it says little about what you can actually do. Your work is the real record: the projects finished, the problems solved, the people helped, and the quality you bring when nobody is watching. Employers and clients may glance at a resume, but they remember the work. So invest less energy in polishing your credentials and more in doing work worth remembering. Build something real, ship it, and let it speak for you. In the end, your work is the only introduction that truly matters.
