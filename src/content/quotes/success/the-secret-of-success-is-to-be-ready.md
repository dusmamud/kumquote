Opportunity rarely announces itself with a fanfare, and it seldom waits for us to get ready. Disraeli's secret is preparation done quietly, before the moment arrives: skills learned, relationships tended, work completed without an audience. When the chance finally appears, the prepared person can move at once, while others are still getting started. Readiness is not luck; it is the habit of doing the work in advance. Stay ready, so that when your opportunity comes, you can meet it fully and well.
