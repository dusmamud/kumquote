Doing only what you are paid for keeps you exactly where you are. The people who advance are usually those who give a little extra before anyone asks: the better work, the solved problem, the finished job. That extra effort is noticed, even when it is not immediately rewarded, because it builds a reputation for reliability and skill. Over time, the world tends to pay for value already demonstrated, not value merely promised. So do more than the minimum now, not as a favour, but as an investment. The reward follows the work, not the other way around.
