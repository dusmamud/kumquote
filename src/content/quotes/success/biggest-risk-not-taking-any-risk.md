Playing it safe feels like the cautious choice, but in a world that changes fast, standing still is the riskiest move of all. Skills go stale, markets shift, and opportunities pass to those willing to move. Taking no risk guarantees slow decline — it just feels comfortable while it happens. Smart risk is different from gambling: it is a calculated step toward something better, with the downside understood. The people who thrive are not the ones who avoided risk. They are the ones who chose their risks wisely and took them early.
