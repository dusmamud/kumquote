McEntire packs a whole philosophy into three bones. The wishbone is the dream — without wanting something, there is nothing to aim at. The backbone is courage, the part that stands up to fear, criticism, and long odds when wishing alone is not enough. And the funny bone is the one people forget: humour keeps you light enough to survive the setbacks that would crush someone who takes every failure personally. Check which bone is weakest in your own life, and feed it. Most people have the wish; fewer keep the backbone and the laugh.
