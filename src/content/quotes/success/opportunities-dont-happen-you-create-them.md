Waiting for the right opportunity is a passive strategy that rarely pays off. Opportunities are not found lying on the ground; they are built by people who take initiative, start conversations, learn new skills, and put themselves forward. Every project you begin, every person you help, and every risk you take creates new openings that did not exist before. The people who seem to get all the breaks are usually the ones creating them. Stop waiting for your chance, and start building it today.
