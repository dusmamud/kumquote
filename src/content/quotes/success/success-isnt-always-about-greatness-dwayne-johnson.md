Greatness has a glamour that hides how it is made. We picture the highlight, not the quiet mornings that built it. Johnson points to the unglamorous truth: success is mostly consistency. The workout done when motivation is gone, the rehearsal repeated until it is boring, the small promise kept again and again. None of these moments feels great while it is happening. But consistency compounds. Day after day, the small efforts stack into something no single effort could achieve. Greatness does not arrive in a moment; it arrives as the sum of ordinary days, done well, one after another.
