Praise feels good, but it rarely teaches us anything new. Happy customers confirm what we already believe about our work. Unhappy customers do something far more valuable: they point at the exact place where our work fell short, in plain words. Gates built a huge company on listening to complaints rather than dismissing them. The criticism stings, but the information is free, specific, and honest. Anyone who wants to improve, in business or in life, should treasure the people willing to say what went wrong. They are not the enemy of success; they are its most honest advisors.
