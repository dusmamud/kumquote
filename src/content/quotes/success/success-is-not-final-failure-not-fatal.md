Success can mislead us into thinking the work is finished, while failure can mislead us into thinking the story is over. Neither is permanent. Churchill reminds us that both are only chapters, and what matters most is the willingness to keep going. A victory should be enjoyed without becoming complacent, and a defeat should be studied without becoming defeatist. Courage is the bridge between the two. It means returning to the work after praise and after disappointment alike. The people who endure are not those who never fall; they are those who rise, learn, and continue with steady resolve.
