Sills, whose voice was built on years of disciplined practice, states a truth that every craft confirms: the destinations that matter cannot be reached by a shortcut. A shortcut skips the very work that makes arrival worthwhile, the learning, the endurance, the slow accumulation of skill. The places worth going demand the long road, and the long road is not a punishment but the price of admission. This is reassuring: the struggle is not a sign that we chose wrongly, but that we chose something valuable. If the path feels long, it is probably leading somewhere good.
