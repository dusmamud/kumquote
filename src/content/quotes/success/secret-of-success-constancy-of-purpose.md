Talent and luck both help, but neither can replace a steady aim. Constancy of purpose means choosing a direction and returning to it day after day, even when progress is invisible and distractions are loud. Many people start with energy and abandon the work at the first difficulty, scattering their effort across too many goals. A constant purpose acts like a compass: it turns small daily actions into a single accumulating force. The secret is not intensity in one moment but loyalty to one aim over time. Keep the purpose fixed, and let the methods bend around it.
