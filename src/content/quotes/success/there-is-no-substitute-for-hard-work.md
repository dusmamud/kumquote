Talent, luck and clever shortcuts all have their place, but none of them can replace sustained effort. Edison, who tested thousands of materials before a working light bulb, knew that most breakthroughs arrive after long stretches of unglamorous labour. Hard work does not guarantee success, yet success without it is rare and fragile. The line is a warning against waiting for inspiration or advantage to do the heavy lifting. Progress belongs to those who show up daily, practise deliberately and keep going when results are slow. Effort is the one ingredient nobody can supply on your behalf.
