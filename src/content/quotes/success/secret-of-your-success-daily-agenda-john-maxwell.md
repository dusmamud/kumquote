Maxwell hides no mystery in success: it is the agenda you actually follow, not the goals you announce. A day is a small unit, easy to waste and easy to repeat, which is why its contents decide the year. The fix is unglamorous — write down the few things that matter, put them first, and protect them from the noise. Look at yesterday's agenda and you can predict next year's results with surprising accuracy. Change the day, and the future follows without needing a grand speech.
