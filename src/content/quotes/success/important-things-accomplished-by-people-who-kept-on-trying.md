History's greatest achievements were not made in easy moments but in dark ones, by people who refused to stop when hope seemed gone. Persistence is what separates those who finish from those who merely start: the willingness to try once more after every reasonable person would have quit. Hope is not always a bright feeling; sometimes it is simply the stubborn decision to continue. Every attempt teaches something, even the failed ones, and those lessons quietly accumulate until the breakthrough comes. When there seems to be no hope at all, keeping on trying is itself the hope.
