Good fortune favours those who work with what they are given instead of complaining about what they lack. Wooden's wisdom is that success depends less on ideal circumstances than on our response to real ones. Plans fail, opportunities vanish, and unexpected problems appear; the people who thrive are those who adapt, find the advantage in the situation, and keep moving. Making the best of things is not passive acceptance but active creativity: turning setbacks into lessons and limitations into focus. Life rarely gives us perfect conditions, but it always gives us something to work with.
