Talent gets the headlines, but these three quiet qualities do the actual work. Patience keeps us steady when results are slow. Persistence keeps us going when the plan hits trouble. Perspiration — plain, honest effort — turns ideas into something real. Any one of them alone is not enough: patience without effort is just waiting, and effort without patience burns out fast. Together, though, they are hard to beat. No setback can stop a person who is willing to wait, keep going, and keep working. That combination is available to anyone.
