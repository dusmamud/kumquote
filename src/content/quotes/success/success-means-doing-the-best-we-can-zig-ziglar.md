Ziglar moves success from the finish line to the starting line. If success is only the getting — the prize, the promotion, the applause — then most of life is failure, because most days bring no prize. But if success is the doing, then every honest effort counts, even when the result disappoints. Judge the day by whether you tried fully and fairly. The triumph may come or not, but a day spent truly trying is already a successful day.
