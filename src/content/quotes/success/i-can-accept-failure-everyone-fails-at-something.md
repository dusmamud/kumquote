Failure is part of every honest attempt. Anyone who tries something difficult will fail at times, and that is nothing to be ashamed of. What deserves regret is never trying at all: the idea never tested, the chance never taken, the talent never used. Effort is the one thing fully within our control, and refusing to try wastes it. This quote draws a clear line between failing, which is human and useful, and not trying, which is a choice we will regret. The lesson is to measure ourselves by effort, not only by outcome.
