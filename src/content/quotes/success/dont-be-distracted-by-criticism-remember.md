Criticism stings most when it arrives just as progress begins, and it is tempting to stop and answer every critic. Ziglar warns that distraction is the real cost: attention spent defending yourself is attention taken from the work itself. Some criticism is useful, but much of it comes from people who have not risked anything of their own. Their harshest words often reveal frustration with their own lack of progress. The healthiest response is to keep building, let results speak, and refuse to let someone else's bitterness set your direction or pace.
