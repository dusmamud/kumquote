Luck is often misunderstood as a random gift, but Jefferson noticed a pattern: prepared, hardworking people seem to meet more good fortune. That is no coincidence. Hard work increases the number of chances you take, the skills you build, and the people you meet. It also helps you recognise an opportunity when it appears and act on it quickly. Others may call the result luck, but it is usually the visible end of invisible effort. If you want more good fortune, increase your effort, your learning, and your willingness to show up. Luck favours those who are already moving.
