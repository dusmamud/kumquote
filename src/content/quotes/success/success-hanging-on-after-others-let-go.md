Most people do not fail because they lack talent; they fail because they stop. Early enthusiasm fades, obstacles pile up, and quitting starts to look reasonable, which is exactly when persistence matters most. Hanging on does not mean stubbornly repeating what is not working. It means staying in the game, adjusting the approach, resting when needed, and returning the next day. The people still standing at the end are rarely the most gifted; they are the ones who refused to let go. Success often belongs to whoever outlasts the moment everyone else walks away.
