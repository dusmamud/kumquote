Success has three ingredients, and most people only think about the first two. Desire supplies the fuel: without a strong want, effort fades at the first difficulty. The size of the dream sets the scale: small dreams produce small results, however hard we work. But the third ingredient decides everything — how we handle disappointment. Every large dream meets setbacks, and the difference between those who arrive and those who quit is rarely talent; it is the ability to absorb a blow, learn from it, and keep moving.
