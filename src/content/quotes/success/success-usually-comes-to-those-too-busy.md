Chasing success directly often leads to anxiety, shortcuts, and constant comparison. Thoreau suggests a better path: become absorbed in useful work and let success follow as a by-product. When you focus on doing something well, serving others, and improving a little each day, recognition and reward often arrive without being forced. Being too busy to look for success does not mean being careless; it means your attention is on the craft, not the applause. Do the work with care, keep learning, and trust that meaningful results grow from consistent, honest effort over time.
