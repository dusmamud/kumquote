Aiming high changes what happens even when you fall short. A small goal reached exactly is worth less than a great goal that stretches you beyond what you thought possible. Big aims force bigger effort, deeper learning, and bolder choices, so the attempt itself leaves you further along than a safe target ever would. Missing the moon is not failure when the journey carries you among the stars. The real risk is aiming so low that success teaches nothing. Set your sights high, give the attempt everything, and let the reach itself become the reward.
