Easy victories tell us little about a person, because anyone can succeed when nothing resists. Real achievement is measured by what stood in the way and how it was faced: the disadvantages, the doubters, the setbacks and the odds that said it could not be done. Opposition is not proof of failure; it is the scale on which success is weighed. The harder the struggle maintained with courage, the more the outcome means. Do not envy smooth paths. The worth of an accomplishment grows with the resistance it overcame, and that resistance is part of the story.
