Most people wait to feel great before they begin, and that waiting is the trap. Greatness does not arrive before the first step; it grows out of the first step, and the second, and the hundredth. Beginners are allowed to be clumsy, slow, and uncertain, because starting is the only way skill is ever built. The order matters: action first, greatness later. Nobody starts great, but everyone who became great once started exactly where you are now. Stop waiting to be ready. Begin badly if you must, but begin.
