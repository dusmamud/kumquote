Everyone makes mistakes, but not everyone learns from them. A mistake that teaches us something has value: it shows us what does not work, what we misunderstood, or where we need to grow. The only true failure is repeating the same error without reflection, because then the cost was paid for nothing. This quote reframes mistakes as tuition rather than tragedy. The question after any setback is not how to hide it, but what it has to teach. People who treat mistakes as lessons keep moving forward; people who ignore the lesson stay stuck in the same place.
