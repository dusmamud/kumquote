Washington, who rose from slavery to found an institute and advise presidents, knew that position alone is a poor measure of a life. Two people can stand on the same rung of the ladder having climbed very different distances, and the one who started lower has achieved more. This quote asks us to judge success by the resistance overcome, not by the height reached. Obstacles are not interruptions to the story of success; they are the story, the evidence of how much a person had to grow, endure, and learn.
