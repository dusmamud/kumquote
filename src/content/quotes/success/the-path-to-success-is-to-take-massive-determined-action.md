Small, timid steps rarely change a life, because they are easy to abandon at the first obstacle. Massive action works differently: it creates momentum, and momentum carries us through the days when motivation fades. Robbins pairs size with determination, because effort without resolve burns out quickly, and resolve without effort never starts. The path to success is not a secret map; it is a habit of acting boldly and refusing to stop. Take the big step, then the next one, and let the weight of your own motion pull you forward.
