Ambition points the way, but wanting something is only the beginning. Many people can describe the destination clearly and never move toward it, because desire alone does not carry anyone forward. Persistence is the vehicle: the daily showing up, the patient repetition, the refusal to stall when the road gets rough. A grand ambition with no persistence is a map without a journey, while modest ambition carried forward steadily can travel surprisingly far. Choose the direction with ambition, then trust persistence to do the carrying, mile after mile.
