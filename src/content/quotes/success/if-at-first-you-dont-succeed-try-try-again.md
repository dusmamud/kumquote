Failure feels like a verdict, but it is usually only a first draft. The people who succeed are not those who never fail; they are those who treat each failure as a lesson and return to the work with a better plan. Trying again does not mean repeating the same mistake stubbornly. It means adjusting, learning, and taking the next step with clearer eyes. Every attempt teaches something the previous one could not. So when you fall short, rest briefly, learn what you can, and try again. Persistence, not perfection, is what carries you forward.
