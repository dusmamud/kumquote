We like to imagine success as a smooth climb, but it is usually built from setbacks. Failure teaches what success cannot: where our plans were weak, what we misunderstood, and how much we actually want the goal. Frustration, too, has a role; it tests whether our desire is real or only a passing wish. People who quit at the first failure never discover what they could have built from it. This quote reminds us that failure is not the opposite of success but its raw material, and that persistence turns disappointment into foundation.
