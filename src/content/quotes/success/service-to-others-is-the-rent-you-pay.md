Success is usually measured by what we collect: money, titles, applause. This quote measures it by what we give. Serving others, through kindness, time, attention, or help, is not an extra on top of a good life; it is the price of belonging to one. Everyone occupies space in the world, and service is how we earn our place in it. The reward is not only moral but practical: people who serve build trust, friendship, and purpose, which are the things that make a life feel rich. Giving is how we pay for the room we occupy.
