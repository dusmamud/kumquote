Talent and strategy get all the attention, but most success starts with something far simpler: being there. Show up for the meeting, the workout, the class, the deadline — again and again. So many people never even arrive that presence alone puts you ahead. Showing up also compounds: each appearance builds skill, trust, and opportunity, even on days when nothing dramatic happens. The secret is not mysterious. Most of the battle is attendance. Be present, be consistent, and let the other twenty percent take care of itself.
