Success is usually pictured as a race, with everyone fighting for the same prize. This quote turns that picture upside down. Helping someone else succeed does not use up our own chances; it multiplies them. When we share knowledge, open a door, or lend encouragement, we build trust and goodwill that come back to us in unexpected ways. Generous people also learn faster, because teaching deepens understanding. The most durable success is not won alone. It grows out of a circle of people who lift one another.
