Success is often pictured as having the most talent, money, or opportunity, but this quote measures it differently. It asks only that we use fully whatever we already have: our time, our skills, our energy, and our character. Comparing ourselves with others is a trap, because we cannot control what they were given. We can control how honestly we apply what we were given. A person who gives their best with modest means has succeeded in the only sense that matters. The standard is personal, not competitive, and it is within everyone's reach.
