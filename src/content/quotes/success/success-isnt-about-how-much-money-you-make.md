Money can measure a career, but it is a poor measure of a life. Michelle Obama redirects success toward its real test: whether other people's lives are better because of what we did. A difference can be small — a child encouraged, a colleague supported, a community strengthened — and still count deeply. Chasing wealth alone often leaves people successful on paper and empty inside. Judge your days by the good they carry into others' lives, and success becomes something richer than any salary can show.
