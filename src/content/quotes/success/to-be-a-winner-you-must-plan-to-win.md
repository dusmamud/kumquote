Winning is usually decided long before the contest begins. It starts with a plan that turns a vague wish into concrete steps, continues with preparation that builds the skill and stamina to execute, and finishes with expectation, the quiet belief that the effort will pay off. People who skip these stages hope for luck; people who follow them create their own chances. Expectation matters because it shapes how we practice and how we respond to setbacks. This quote lays out a simple sequence: plan it, prepare for it, expect it, and the result often follows.
