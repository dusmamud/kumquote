This short, startling line turns our usual picture of success upside down. We imagine winners gliding from triumph to triumph, but behind almost every success lies a long trail of failures, rejected ideas, and attempts that did not work. Failure is not the opposite of success here; it is its main ingredient. Each failed attempt teaches something the next attempt needs, and the person who keeps going collects enough lessons to finally get it right. The message is blunt and freeing: if you want to succeed, expect to fail often, and treat every failure as part of the price.
