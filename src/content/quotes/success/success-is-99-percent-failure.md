Honda knew failure from the inside. His early factories were destroyed, his designs were rejected, and his company nearly collapsed more than once — yet he kept building, learning from each breakdown. Calling success 99 percent failure reframes every setback as part of the process rather than proof that you should stop. Each failed attempt teaches something success cannot: what does not work, what needs changing, and how much you want the goal. If failure is the raw material of success, then the only real mistake is quitting early. Keep going, and the failures will add up to something.
