Many people delay happiness until they achieve success, believing that a promotion, a prize, or wealth will finally make them content. Schweitzer reverses that order. When you enjoy your work, you bring energy, patience, and creativity to it, and those qualities make success more likely. Hard days become challenges rather than punishments. If your current path leaves you miserable, ask what part of it could be done with more meaning, craft, or service. Happiness is not a reward after success; it is often the fuel that makes success possible.
