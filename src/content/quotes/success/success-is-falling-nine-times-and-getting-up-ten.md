Success is rarely a straight climb; it is a long series of falls followed by the decision to stand up again. The people who succeed are not those who never stumble, but those who refuse to stay down after the ninth fall. Each time we rise, we bring back something useful: experience, patience and proof that we can survive a setback. The count matters less than the habit of getting up one more time than we fall. If you are standing after your latest failure, you are still in the game, and that is where success is built.
