Success has a price, and it is paid in advance, in daily installments of hard work and dedication. There is no discount for talent and no shortcut around the effort. The determination to give your best whether you win or lose is what separates real achievement from mere luck. Many people want the reward but refuse the payment, and then wonder why success never arrives. Pay the price willingly, in full, every day. When you have given your best to the task, the result, whatever it is, will carry a satisfaction that no shortcut could ever buy.
