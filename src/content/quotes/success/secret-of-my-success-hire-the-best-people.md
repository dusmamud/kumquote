Success is rarely a solo achievement, and this line gives the credit where it belongs: to the people around you. Great work comes from great teams, and great teams are built by choosing people who are better than you in the areas that matter. Hiring well is not about collecting impressive résumés; it is about finding people who care deeply, think clearly, and raise the standard of everyone near them. One exceptional person can change the direction of a whole project.
