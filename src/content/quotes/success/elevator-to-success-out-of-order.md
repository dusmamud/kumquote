There is no shortcut to a lasting achievement, however much we wish there were. The stairs are slow, unglamorous, and tiring — and they are the only way up. Each step is small on its own: one more call, one more page, one more honest day of work. But steps add up in a way that waiting never does. People who look for the elevator spend their energy searching for a faster route, while the stair-climbers quietly arrive. Success is not a single leap. It is hundreds of ordinary steps taken in the same direction.
