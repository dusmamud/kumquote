Enthusiasm alone can burn bright and fade fast, which is why this line pairs it with two steadying partners: common sense and persistence. Passion supplies the energy to begin and to inspire others, horse sense keeps that energy aimed at what actually works, and persistence carries it through the long stretches when excitement runs low. Success rarely comes from any one of these alone. The combination matters: fire to start, judgment to steer, and staying power to finish. When all three are present, ordinary ability can produce results that talent alone never reaches.
