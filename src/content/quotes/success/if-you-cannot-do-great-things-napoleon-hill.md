We often wait for a grand stage before we try to be excellent, as if greatness needs a large audience. Hill's advice is the opposite: excellence is built in the small things, where almost no one is watching. A careful email, a tidy workbench, an honest answer, a detail done right. These are not lesser tasks; they are the training ground where quality becomes a habit. Whoever does small things in a great way will do great things the same way. Greatness is not a different skill. It is the same care, applied at a larger scale.
