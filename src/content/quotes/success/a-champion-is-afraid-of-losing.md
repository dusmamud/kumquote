This line turns our usual idea of fear upside down. Most people hold back because they fear winning: the attention, the responsibility, the higher bar that follows. A champion feels the opposite fear — the fear of losing — and uses it as fuel. That fear keeps training sharp, focus steady, and effort honest. The difference is not talent alone; it is what you do with fear. Let the fear of losing push you to prepare fully, and let the fear of winning stop holding you back. Winners are not fearless; they are afraid of the right thing.
