Doubt rarely comes from people who have done the thing themselves. Goforth sorts the naysayers into two groups, and both are driven by fear. The first group is afraid to try, so your attempt reminds them of their own hesitation. The second group is afraid you will succeed, because your success would prove that change is possible. Neither group is describing your ability; both are describing their own fear. Once we see that, their warnings lose their weight. Listen to people who have tried, not to people who are afraid.
