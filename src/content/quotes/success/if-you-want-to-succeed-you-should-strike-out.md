Following the exact route others took can only lead where they have already been. The worn paths are crowded, and by the time a route is called safe, its best rewards have often been claimed. New paths are riskier, but they are also where fresh opportunities hide, because nobody has mapped them yet. Striking out on your own means thinking for yourself, testing ideas others ignored, and accepting that you may stumble. That independence is not recklessness; it is how new ground gets broken. The surest way to stand out is to stop walking in everyone else's footsteps.
