Hilton strips achievement of its mystery and ties it to something unglamorous: motion. Successful people are not those who never err; they are those who keep moving after the error. The quote makes a quiet distinction between mistakes and quitting. Mistakes are part of the route, expected and survivable, while quitting is the only true dead end. Action does not require certainty or perfection, only the willingness to take the next step and learn from where it lands. Waiting for the perfect plan is itself a form of standing still.
