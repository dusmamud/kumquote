Success is often described in heavy, serious terms, yet this playful recipe names three simple ingredients. The wishbone stands for dreams and hope — without a vision of what could be, there is nothing to aim for. The backbone stands for courage and resolve, the willingness to stand firm when the work gets hard. And the funny bone reminds us that humor keeps us sane through setbacks, letting us laugh instead of breaking. Together they form a balanced approach: dream boldly, stand strong, and do not lose your sense of humor along the way.
