Lombardi, famous for demanding victory, makes a careful distinction here. The scoreboard is not fully in your control — opponents, injuries, and luck all have a vote — so making winning everything would hand your happiness to chance. But the desire to win is entirely yours: it decides how hard you prepare, how carefully you practise, and whether you keep fighting when the score turns against you. You may lose the game and still have lived up to the standard, because you wanted it fully and gave it everything. Want it that much, and the wins tend to follow.
