Nostalgia makes the past look golden and the future look uncertain, but Lewis turns that feeling around. Whatever we leave behind, however good it was, the best is still ahead. This is not a promise that the future will be easy, only that it holds better things for those willing to move toward it. Clinging to what is gone keeps us from reaching what is coming. Hope is the decision to believe that tomorrow can be richer than yesterday. Let go of the past with gratitude, face forward, and walk toward the better things waiting ahead.
