Success and happiness are often confused, but Carnegie separates them cleanly. Success is about achievement: setting a goal and reaching it. Happiness is about appreciation: learning to value what is already in your hands. Many people succeed by the first measure and fail by the second, always hungry for the next prize. The wisest life holds both: strive for what you want, and practise wanting what you have. When gratitude joins ambition, success stops feeling empty and starts feeling like a life well lived.
