Waiting for the perfect opportunity is a passive strategy that leaves your future in other people's hands. Chris Grosser reminds us that opportunities are usually made, not found. Creation starts with initiative: noticing a need, offering value, and taking a risk before you feel ready. Not every attempt will succeed, but each one teaches you something and expands your network. Do not wait to be chosen. Build something useful, reach out, and create the chance you have been waiting for.
