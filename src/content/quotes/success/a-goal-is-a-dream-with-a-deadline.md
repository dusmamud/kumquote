A dream without a date is easy to admire and easy to postpone forever. It stays comfortable because it asks nothing of us today. A deadline changes that entirely: it turns a vague wish into a commitment with a shape, a plan and a sense of urgency. Once a dream has a date, every day asks what must be done now to meet it. That pressure is useful, because it turns longing into action. If there is something you truly want, give it a deadline. Until you do, it is only a pleasant thought, not yet a goal.
