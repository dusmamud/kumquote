It is easy to become fixated on our limits: the skills we lack, the resources we do not have, the doors that are closed. That fixation is paralyzing, because it turns attention away from everything we can actually do. The wiser move is to work with what is in our hands. Small, possible actions taken today matter more than perfect actions imagined for someday. Limits are real, but they are not the whole story. Progress comes from focusing on our strengths and our next step, not from mourning what lies beyond our reach.
