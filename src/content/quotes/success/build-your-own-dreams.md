Time passes either way. The hours we spend working will be spent on something — the only question is whose vision they serve. Building our own dreams is harder at the start: the path is unclear, the pay is uncertain, and nobody hands us the instructions. But the alternative is not safety, it is simply building someone else's dream with the same hours and the same energy. Both routes demand effort; only one returns ownership of the result. This is not a call to quit every job, but a reminder to reserve some of our time for what we want.
