There is no secret formula for success, only this: stay constant to a purpose. Talent and opportunity matter, but they scatter without a steady aim. Constancy means returning to the same purpose day after day, even when progress is slow and distractions are loud. It is the quiet refusal to abandon the goal for the newest excitement or the easiest path. Purpose gives effort its direction, and constancy gives purpose its power. When you hold to one clear aim long enough, small daily actions accumulate into results that look, from the outside, like luck or genius.
