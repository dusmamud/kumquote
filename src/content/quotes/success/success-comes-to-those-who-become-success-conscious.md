The mind finds what it is trained to look for. A person who thinks constantly about obstacles will notice obstacles everywhere; a person who thinks about success will notice chances, allies, and openings. Hill calls this becoming success conscious — filling the mind with the goal until it starts working on the problem even in quiet moments. It is not magic; it is attention. What we focus on shapes what we attempt, and what we attempt shapes what we achieve. Keep success in mind, and the mind will keep working toward it.
