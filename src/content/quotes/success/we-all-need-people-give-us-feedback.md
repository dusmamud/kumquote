Nobody improves in a vacuum, and this line states a plain truth about growth. We cannot see our own blind spots, so honest feedback from others is often the only mirror that shows us what needs to change. Praise feels good, but useful criticism is what actually moves skill forward. The key is to ask for it, listen without defending yourself, and treat it as information rather than attack. People who welcome feedback learn faster than those who avoid it. Improvement is not a solo project; it is a conversation with people who care enough to tell the truth.
