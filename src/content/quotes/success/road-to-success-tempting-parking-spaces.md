Success is a long road, and this joke carries a serious warning about comfort. Along the way there are countless pleasant places to stop: a small win that feels enough, a routine that pays the bills, a plateau that asks nothing more of you. Each stop looks harmless, and each one is, until stopping becomes the habit. The danger is not a single rest but the slow decision to settle. Keep driving past the tempting spaces, rest when you truly need it, and remember that the destination is still ahead, waiting for those who refuse to park early.
