Success is often made to sound mysterious, but Glasow reduces it to three clear conditions: the right action, done well, at the right moment. Doing the wrong thing brilliantly still fails, and doing the right thing badly or too late wastes the chance. Integrity guides the choice of what is right; skill and care determine the way; judgment decides the timing. When all three align, results follow naturally. The simplicity is the point: we do not need a secret formula, only the discipline to choose well, execute carefully, and act when the moment is right.
