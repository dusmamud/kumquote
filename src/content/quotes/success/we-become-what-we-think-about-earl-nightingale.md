Our thoughts are not idle background noise; they are the architects of our days. What we return to again and again, worry or possibility, resentment or gratitude, slowly shapes what we notice, what we attempt, and what we become. Nightingale's line is not magic thinking. It is a practical observation: attention is a resource, and wherever we spend it, our life follows. A person who thinks about obstacles will find them everywhere. A person who thinks about what can be built will start building. We cannot always choose our first thought, but we can choose which thoughts we keep feeding.
