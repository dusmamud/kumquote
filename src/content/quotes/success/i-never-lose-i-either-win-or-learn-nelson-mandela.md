Losing hurts, and no honest person pretends otherwise. But Mandela reframes what losing means. If every outcome is either a win or a lesson, then failure stops being a verdict and becomes information. A missed promotion teaches us what the role required. A failed project shows us where our planning broke down. The lesson is only useful if we are willing to look at it instead of turning away. People who grow fastest are rarely the ones who fail least; they are the ones who learn most from each fall. Treat every setback as tuition, and no experience is wasted.
