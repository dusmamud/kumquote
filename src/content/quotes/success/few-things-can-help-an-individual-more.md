People grow fastest when someone trusts them with real responsibility. Being trusted tells a person, "I believe you can do this," and that belief often becomes the fuel for doing it well. Responsibility is not a burden to avoid; it is a gift that builds confidence, skill, and pride. When leaders give responsibility instead of only giving orders, they create stronger teams and stronger people. The same is true for ourselves: take responsibility for your own work and your own growth, and you will discover strengths you did not know you had.
