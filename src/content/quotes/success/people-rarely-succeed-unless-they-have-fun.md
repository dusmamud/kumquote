Enjoyment is not a distraction from success; it is often the fuel for it. Work that feels like drudgery drains energy and patience, while work we genuinely enjoy keeps us curious, creative and willing to stay with a problem longer. People who love what they do practise more, notice more and bounce back faster from setbacks, because the activity itself rewards them. That does not mean every task will be pleasant, but finding the part of the work you enjoy changes everything. Choose work you can love, and success becomes a natural side effect rather than a forced outcome.
