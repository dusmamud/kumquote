Behind every smooth success story is a pile of failures nobody saw. The first attempt collapsed, the second disappointed, and the third taught the lesson that finally made the fourth one work. People who fear failure avoid the very material success is made of, while those who study their failures turn each one into a brick. Frustration and even catastrophe are not the end of the road; they are often the ground on which something stronger gets built. Do not hide your failures. Build on them, and they will hold more weight than any easy win ever could.
