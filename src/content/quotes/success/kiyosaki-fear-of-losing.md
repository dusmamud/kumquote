Fear of losing is a powerful force, and it quietly keeps many people from ever playing the game. We weigh the pain of a possible loss more heavily than the joy of a possible win, so we choose safety and call it wisdom. But a life built only on avoiding loss is a small life, and the excitement of winning is worth the risk. Courage is not the absence of fear; it is letting the pull of what could go right outweigh the dread of what could go wrong. Take the chance, and let excitement lead.
