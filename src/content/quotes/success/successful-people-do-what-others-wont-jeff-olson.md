No secret ingredient separates successful people from everyone else, only a difference in what they are willing to do. The work that matters is rarely glamorous: early mornings, extra repetitions, boring consistency, the choice to keep going when no one is watching. Unsuccessful people are rarely less talented, only less willing to do the unglamorous parts. Olson's line is a challenge to stop wishing the path were easier and start asking whether we are willing to be better. The gap between where we are and where we want to be is filled with work we already know we should do.
