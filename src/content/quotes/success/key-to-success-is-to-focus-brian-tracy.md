Fear is a skilled thief of attention. It pulls our thoughts toward everything that could go wrong, until our imagination rehearses disasters instead of planning victories. Tracy's advice is to reverse that habit deliberately. Focus is a practice: choosing, again and again, to think about what we desire rather than what we dread. Desire pulls us forward; fear only freezes us in place. The two cannot occupy the mind at the same time. Guard the focus, and the fear loses its grip. Aim the mind at the goal, and the hands learn what to do next.
