Spencer reminds us that education aims at action, not at the mere storage of facts. Knowledge that never changes what we do is decoration. The purpose of learning is to live better — to make wiser choices, to work more skilfully, to treat people more fairly. A person who knows much but acts poorly has missed the point of their education. Schools and books matter because they prepare us to act, not because they fill us with information. The test of learning is always the same: what do you do differently because you know it?
