Dante links wisdom directly to how we value time. The wise understand that time is the one resource that cannot be earned back, and so they feel its loss sharply. Wasted hours annoy them not from impatience but from clarity: every hour spent on what does not matter is an hour taken from what does. This quote is a gentle warning to examine where our days actually go. If we guarded our time the way the wise do, we would choose more carefully, act more deliberately, and waste far less of the life we have.
