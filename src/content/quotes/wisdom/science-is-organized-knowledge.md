Knowledge and wisdom are related, but they are not the same thing. Knowledge can be collected, organised, and tested, like books arranged neatly on a shelf. Wisdom is what happens when that knowledge is lived — when understanding shapes the choices we make, the way we treat others, and the person we become. Kant draws a clear line: information alone does not make a life well lived. A life becomes wise when learning is woven into character and conduct. Study gives us facts; wisdom gives those facts a home in how we live.
