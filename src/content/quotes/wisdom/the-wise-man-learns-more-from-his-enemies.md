Gracián points out an uncomfortable truth: our enemies are often better teachers than our friends. Friends, out of kindness, tell us what we want to hear. Enemies have no such restraint — they study our weaknesses and attack exactly where we are vulnerable. That hurts, but it also shows us precisely where we need to grow. A wise person does not waste energy hating the critic, but listens for the truth inside the attack. Your friends will comfort you, and you need that too. If you want to improve, listen to your enemies — they watch you more carefully than anyone.
