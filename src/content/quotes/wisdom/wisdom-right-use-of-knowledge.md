Spurgeon separates knowing from understanding. Knowledge can be collected like coins, yet a person may hold a fortune of facts and spend them foolishly. Wisdom is the judgment that decides which fact matters, when to speak it, and when to keep it quiet. It turns information into action that helps rather than harms. The difference shows in daily choices: the knowledgeable person can describe the road, but the wise person knows which turn to take and why it matters now.
