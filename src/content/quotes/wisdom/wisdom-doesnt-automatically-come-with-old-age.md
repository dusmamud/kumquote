Van Buren punctures the comforting idea that years alone make us wise. Time passes for everyone, but wisdom is not a reward for waiting; it is the result of paying attention, reflecting, and changing course when we are wrong. Plenty of people grow older without growing wiser, repeating the same mistakes with more confidence. The only thing age guarantees is a changing body. If we want the years to teach us, we have to do the learning ourselves, on purpose, one honest look at a time.
