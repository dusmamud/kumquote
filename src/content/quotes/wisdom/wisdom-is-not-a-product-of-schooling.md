Einstein separates schooling from wisdom. Classrooms can teach facts, methods, and qualifications, but wisdom comes from a lifetime of paying attention, making mistakes, and reflecting on them. A person with many degrees can still act foolishly, while someone with little formal education can show deep judgement. Schooling ends; the attempt to acquire wisdom does not. It continues through work, relationships, failure, and age. The quote reminds us that education is a tool, not a finish line. What matters is whether we keep learning long after the lessons stop.
