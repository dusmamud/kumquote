Einstein was not dismissing knowledge — he had more of it than
almost anyone alive. He was ranking the two. Knowledge tells you
what is already known. Imagination is what lets you ask a question
nobody has asked yet, and without the question, knowledge has
nowhere new to go. Every theory he is remembered for began as a
thought experiment, not a textbook.

For the rest of us, the lesson is practical. When you are stuck,
do not only search for more facts. Spend ten minutes imagining the
problem solved, or the situation from the other person's side, or
the opposite of what everyone assumes. Knowledge is the map of
where we have been. Imagination is the only way to draw the next
part of it.
