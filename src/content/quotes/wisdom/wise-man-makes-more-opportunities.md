Waiting for the right opportunity is a passive strategy, and Bacon calls it unwise. Opportunities rarely arrive fully formed; they begin as small openings, half-formed ideas, or problems nobody wants. The wise person does not wait for perfect conditions but creates openings through effort, curiosity, and persistence. Making opportunities means asking, trying, building, and learning from failure. Luck favors those who move. The world rewards initiative more than patience, and wisdom lies in knowing that the door you open yourself counts just as much as the one you find.
