Socrates turns ignorance into the starting point of wisdom. The person who believes they already know everything stops asking questions, stops listening, and stops learning — and so their understanding quietly freezes. Admitting "I know nothing" is not despair; it is honesty about how vast the world is and how small any one mind remains beside it. That admission keeps curiosity alive and makes room for better ideas to enter. True wisdom is not a pile of answers, but the humility that keeps the search going.
