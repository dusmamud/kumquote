Da Vinci, a master of complexity, argues that true refinement ends in simplicity. Anyone can add ornament, features, and noise, but it takes deep understanding to strip a thing down to its essence without losing its power. Simplicity is not emptiness or laziness; it is the result of careful thought, of removing everything that does not serve the purpose. A simple design, sentence, or solution is harder to achieve than a complicated one, because it demands that we truly understand what matters. The ultimate sophistication is making the difficult look effortless, and the profound feel plain.
