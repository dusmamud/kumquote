The first mistake is usually honest: a wrong turn, a misjudgment, a moment of carelessness. What turns it into something worse is the refusal to face it. Correcting a mistake takes humility, because it means admitting we were wrong in front of ourselves and sometimes in front of others. But every uncorrected mistake quietly becomes a habit, and habits become character. Wisdom is not the absence of mistakes. It is the willingness to stop, own the error, and set things right while there is still time.
