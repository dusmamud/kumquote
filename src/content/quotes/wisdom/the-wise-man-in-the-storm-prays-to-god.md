Emerson reframes prayer in a storm. The wise person does not ask to escape danger, because danger is part of life and cannot always be avoided. Instead, he asks for deliverance from fear itself. Fear distorts judgement, freezes action, and makes every problem larger than it is. Courage is not the absence of danger but the mastery of the response to it. When fear loosens its grip, a person can think clearly and act well even in the middle of the storm. That inner freedom matters more than safety.
