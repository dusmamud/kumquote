Tolle suggests that wisdom begins where noise ends. A busy mind reacts quickly and mistakes its first opinion for the truth, while a still mind can see what is actually there. Looking and listening without judgment lets the situation speak before the ego answers. That pause is where understanding grows, because it leaves room for facts to arrive. Stillness is not emptiness; it is attention without hurry. The wiser person is often the one who waits a moment longer before deciding.
