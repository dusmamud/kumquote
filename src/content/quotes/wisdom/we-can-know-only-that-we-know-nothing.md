True wisdom begins with admitting how little we truly know. The world is vast, and every answer opens new questions; anyone who claims certainty about everything has stopped learning. Humility about our knowledge keeps us curious, open to correction, and kind toward people who disagree. It also protects us from the worst mistakes, which usually come from overconfidence rather than ignorance. To say "I do not know" is not defeat — it is the doorway to understanding. The highest wisdom is not having every answer, but knowing the limits of your own.
