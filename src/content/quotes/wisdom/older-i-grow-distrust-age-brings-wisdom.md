Mencken warns against a comforting myth: that birthdays automatically deliver wisdom. Age brings experience, but experience only becomes wisdom when it is examined honestly. Plenty of people grow older while repeating the same errors, defending the same prejudices, and learning nothing. The familiar doctrine flatters us into waiting passively for insight that never arrives on its own. Real wisdom comes from questioning our assumptions, admitting mistakes, and staying curious at every age. Growing older is guaranteed; growing wiser is a choice, and it must be made deliberately, year after year.
