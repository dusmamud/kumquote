Two short words carry a lifetime of work. To know ourselves means looking honestly at our motives, our fears, and the habits that quietly steer our choices. Without this knowledge, we blame others for our troubles and repeat the same mistakes in new disguises. Self-knowledge is not vanity; it is the ground of every good decision, because we cannot choose wisely for a person we do not understand. It also breeds humility, since an honest look inward reveals how much we still lack. The examined life begins here, with the simple, difficult act of seeing ourselves clearly.
