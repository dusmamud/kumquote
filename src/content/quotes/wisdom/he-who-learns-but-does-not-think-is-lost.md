Learning without thinking produces a mind full of borrowed facts and empty of judgment. Such a person can recite what others have said but cannot decide what is true. Thinking without learning is equally dangerous: it spins clever theories from thin air, unchecked by the wisdom of those who came before. Real understanding needs both, study and reflection in balance. We must gather knowledge humbly, then test it against our own reason and experience. Only then does learning become wisdom. The two belong together like breathing in and breathing out; either alone leaves the mind lost or in peril.
