Michel de Montaigne offers a surprising test for wisdom: not solemnity, but cheerfulness. The truly wise are not weighed down by their knowledge; they carry it lightly, with good humour and ease. A bitter, anxious cleverness may impress in debate, yet it suggests a mind still at war with itself. Cheerfulness, by contrast, shows that a person has made peace with uncertainty, accepted human limits, and learned to enjoy the world as it is. If you want to spot wisdom in a room, Montaigne says, look for the person who can smile without pretending.
