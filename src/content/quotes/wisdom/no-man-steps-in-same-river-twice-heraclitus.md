Heraclitus uses a river to show that change is the only constant. The water flows on, so the river you stepped into yesterday is never the same river today, and you, shaped by every passing moment, are never quite the same person either. Nothing stands still, however solid it seems, and clinging to what was is a losing struggle against the current. Wisdom lies in accepting this flow rather than fearing it, learning to move with change instead of resisting. When we stop demanding permanence, we can meet each new moment as it truly is: fresh, unrepeatable, and alive.
