Epicurus notices a common form of foolishness: always preparing to live and never actually living. The fool plans the perfect moment, the right conditions, the finished preparation, while life slips past. Wisdom is recognising that the present is the only time we truly have. Getting ready has its place, but when preparation becomes permanent, it is just procrastination dressed up as prudence. Begin now, with what you have, and let living itself teach you the rest. The present moment is the only one guaranteed, and it deserves to be lived rather than endlessly rehearsed.
