Answers can become a cage when they arrive too early. A person armed with all the answers stops asking, stops wondering, and stops growing. Questions, by contrast, keep the mind alive: they open doors, challenge assumptions, and invite new understanding. Knowing the right questions matters more than collecting quick answers, because a good question can guide a lifetime of learning. The wise are not those who speak with certainty on every subject, but those who know which questions are worth asking. Curiosity outlasts certainty, and the best questions never truly grow old.
