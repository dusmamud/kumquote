Newton credits his discoveries not to solitary genius but to the work of those before him. Every great achievement stands on earlier efforts — the experiments, the failures, the ideas that made the next step possible. Humility is not false modesty here; it is accuracy. No one begins from nothing. The giants beneath us are teachers, rivals, and friends whose work we extend. Recognising this changes how we create: we read more, credit others, and build for those who will stand on our shoulders next. Progress is a relay, not a solo race.
