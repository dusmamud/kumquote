Bacon's short sentence carries a practical truth: what we know shapes what we can do. Ignorance leaves us at the mercy of chance, rumour, and other people's decisions, while knowledge gives us choices, tools, and the ability to judge for ourselves. Power here does not mean ruling others; it means having command over our own lives. A person who understands how things work can fix, build, heal, or persuade. Learning is therefore not a luxury but a form of strength, and every fact honestly gained adds to our freedom.
