Jung notes that understanding grows through mistakes as much as through facts. Every error corrected adds something that a correct guess never could: the knowledge of why the wrong path fails. Science, craft, and self-knowledge all advance this way, by trying, failing, and adjusting. A person who has never been wrong has only a narrow kind of certainty. The wise accept error as part of learning, not as a shame to hide, because each mistake quietly marks the boundary of what is true.
