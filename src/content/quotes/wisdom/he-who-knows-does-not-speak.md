True understanding rarely needs to announce itself. Those who genuinely know something feel no urge to prove it in every conversation, because their knowledge is settled and quiet. Constant talking, by contrast, often fills the space where understanding should be, a way of hiding uncertainty behind noise. Silence is not ignorance; it is the patience to listen, to observe, and to let truth reveal itself slowly. The wise speak when words are needed and useful, not to display themselves. In a loud world, restraint in speech becomes a mark of depth, and listening becomes the surest path to learning.
