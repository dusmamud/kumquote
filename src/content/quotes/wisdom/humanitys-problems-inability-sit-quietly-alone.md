Pascal traces most human trouble to restlessness. Unable to sit quietly, people chase noise, quarrels, and distractions, and many of their worst decisions are made simply to escape silence. Sitting alone is not idleness; it is the moment when a person must face their own thoughts without disguise. Those who can bear that meeting learn what they actually want and fear. The room is a test, and passing it means finding peace without needing the world to entertain you. Those who learn to sit quietly find that peace was waiting in the room all along.
