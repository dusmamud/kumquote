Lao Tzu contrasts two opposite movements. Knowledge grows by adding: new facts, new skills, new ideas, each day a little more. Wisdom grows by subtracting: letting go of assumptions, cravings, and noise until what matters stands clear. A crowded mind cannot see straight, however much it holds. Removing what is unnecessary is harder than collecting more, because it asks us to admit that much of what we carry does not serve us. Wisdom is not a bigger shelf but a cleaner room.
