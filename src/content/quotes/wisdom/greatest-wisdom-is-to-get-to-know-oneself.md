Galileo spent his life studying the heavens, yet he called self-knowledge the greatest wisdom of all. We can map stars and measure planets while remaining strangers to our own motives, fears, and habits. Knowing ourselves means noticing what triggers us, what we truly want, and where we deceive ourselves. Without that, even great knowledge is used blindly. With it, our choices grow steadier and our judgements kinder. The hardest subject to study is the one closest to home, and the most useful.
