Descartes separates the gift from the use of it. A sharp mind left idle, or spent on quarrels and vanity, does no more good than a dull one. What matters is method: thinking carefully, doubting what is uncertain, and following reasons step by step. The main thing is not talent but discipline, the daily habit of applying reason instead of impulse. Anyone can improve by using the mind they have with care. Wisdom begins when a good mind is put to good work.
