Pope warns that a little knowledge can be worse than none at all, because it brings confidence without understanding. A person who has tasted one idea often speaks as if they have mastered the whole subject, and that false certainty closes the door to further learning. True beginners know they know little, and experts know how much remains; the danger sits in between. Wisdom is recognising where we stand on that road and staying curious. A little learning should make us humble, not certain.
