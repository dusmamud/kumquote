Molière teaches that dignity is defended by refusing to be dragged down. An insult only wounds when it is answered on its own terms; patience takes away its power by refusing the contest. Moderation is not weakness but control, the choice to respond from calm rather than from anger. The wise person understands that a loud reply often proves the insult true in the eyes of others. Silence and steadiness, by contrast, leave the offender with nothing but his own words.
