Steinem observes that the hardest part of growing is not gaining new ideas but letting go of old ones. We absorb beliefs early about who we are, what we deserve, and how the world works, and those beliefs quietly run our choices long after they stop being true. New knowledge piles up on top of them without changing anything underneath. Unlearning means questioning what we were taught, noticing which ideas were never ours, and making room for better ones. Growth begins not with adding more, but with clearing space.
