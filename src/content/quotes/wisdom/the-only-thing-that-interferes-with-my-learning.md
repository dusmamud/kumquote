Einstein suggests that formal schooling can sometimes block real learning. Education can fill the mind with facts and formulas while quietly discouraging curiosity, doubt, and independent thought. True learning needs room to question, to experiment, and to be wrong. When teaching becomes only memorisation and examination, it interferes with the very understanding it claims to build. The quote is a reminder to keep curiosity alive beyond the classroom, because the love of learning, not the certificate, is what makes a mind grow.
