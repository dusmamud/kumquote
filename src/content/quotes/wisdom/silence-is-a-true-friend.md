Words, once spoken, cannot be taken back, and a hasty remark can wound a friendship or ruin a reputation. Silence, by contrast, never betrays us; it holds our thoughts safely until we are ready to speak with care. This is not hiding the truth, but a reminder that restraint is a form of wisdom. The pause before speaking lets anger cool, lets facts settle, and lets kindness shape the message. Mastering silence gains time to think and earns the trust of others. In the end, knowing when not to speak is as valuable as knowing what to say.
