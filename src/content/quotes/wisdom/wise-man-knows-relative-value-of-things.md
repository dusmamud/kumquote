Inge points to the heart of good judgment: knowing what matters most. Life constantly asks us to choose between things that all seem urgent, and the wise person weighs them against one another instead of treating them as equal. A friendship is worth more than a quarrel; health is worth more than a deadline; peace is worth more than being right. Knowing relative value means giving the best of your time to the best of your aims, and letting the rest take its proper, smaller place.
