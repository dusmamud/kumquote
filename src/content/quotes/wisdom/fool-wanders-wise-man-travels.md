The difference lies inside. The wanderer drifts without direction, blown by whatever mood or fashion is strongest that day. The traveller moves with intention, knowing where they are headed and learning as they go. Fuller compresses a whole philosophy into a single contrast. Life will carry us somewhere either way, but purpose turns mere motion into a journey. Wisdom, then, is not about staying still or knowing every answer in advance. It is about moving through life deliberately, with our eyes open and our destination in mind.
