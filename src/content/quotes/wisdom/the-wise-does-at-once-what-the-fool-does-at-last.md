Gracián points out that wisdom and folly often reach the same conclusion; the difference is timing. The wise person sees what must be done and does it now, while the fool delays, argues, and drifts until delay itself forces the same action under worse conditions. The apology, the repair, the honest conversation, or the hard decision rarely changes with waiting; only the cost grows. Acting promptly is not rushing. It is recognising the inevitable early, when there is still time to do it well and with grace.
