This Akan proverb pictures wisdom as a baobab — a tree so vast that no single person's arms can reach around it. The image humbles anyone who thinks they have figured life out alone. Wisdom lives spread across people, generations, and cultures: the elder's patience, the child's honesty, the stranger's different view. We gather it by listening widely and admitting how much we cannot hold ourselves. Nobody owns wisdom; we each carry a branch of it, and the tree is only whole when many hands reach for it together.
