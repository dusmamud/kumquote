Emerson distinguishes two kinds of prayer: one asks for the danger to disappear, the other asks for freedom from fear. The wise person knows danger cannot always be removed, but fear can be faced. Deliverance from fear is the deeper request, because a fearless person can act clearly in any storm, while a frightened person is lost even in safety. Wisdom accepts what cannot be controlled and works on what can — our own response. Courage, not comfort, is the wise person's true shelter.
