Cicero sorts learners into four kinds, and the ranking is a challenge to us all. The wise learn by reason, thinking things through before acting. Average minds need experience, which teaches through mistakes and costs time. The stupid learn only by necessity, when pain forces change. And the brute learns by instinct alone, never rising above reaction. The question is which kind we are becoming. Reason is available to everyone, but it requires the discipline to pause and think. Choose reason first, and we can skip the expensive lessons that experience and necessity would otherwise charge us.
