Adams, a man who spent his life exploring landscapes, saw life itself as the same kind of journey. Every experience — even the painful or ordinary ones — is a territory to be explored rather than a verdict to be endured. Seen this way, setbacks become field notes and surprises become discoveries. Wisdom gathers slowly, not from collecting years but from paying attention to each one. The curious person never stops exploring, and so never stops growing wiser. Keep exploring, keep noticing, and wisdom will keep gathering around you.
