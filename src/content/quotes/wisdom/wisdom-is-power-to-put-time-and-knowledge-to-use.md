Knowledge sitting unused is like money buried in a field; time spent without purpose slips away unnoticed. Wisdom is the bridge between the two: knowing what matters, and giving your best hours to it. Many people are busy all day yet achieve little, because effort without direction is only motion. The wise person pauses, chooses the right task, and applies what they know at the right moment. Ask yourself each morning what deserves your time today, and use your knowledge there. Used well, even one focused hour can matter more than a week of distraction.
