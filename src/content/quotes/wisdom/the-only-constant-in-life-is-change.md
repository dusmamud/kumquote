Heraclitus observed, more than two thousand years ago, that everything flows and nothing stays still. People, seasons, fortunes, and feelings all shift, and resisting that movement only causes suffering. We cling to how things were, or demand that good moments last forever, and disappointment follows when they do not. Accepting change does not mean liking every change; it means recognising that change itself is the one reliable feature of life. When we stop fighting it, we can adapt, let go, and find new footing. Peace comes not from freezing life in place but from learning to move with it.
