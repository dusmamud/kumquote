Real listening goes beyond the words themselves. A wise person hears not only what is said, but what is meant: the worry behind the question, the hope behind the complaint, the silence behind the answer. This kind of understanding cannot be rushed. It requires attention, patience, and a willingness to set aside our own reply while someone else is still speaking. Most misunderstandings come not from malice but from hearing only half of what was offered. Wisdom begins in the ear, not in the mouth.
