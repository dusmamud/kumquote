Some problems cannot be solved by staring at them harder. The mind, exhausted and anxious, circles the same dead ends all evening, growing more frustrated with each lap. Sleep offers a different kind of work: the unconscious mind sifts, sorts, and connects ideas without our interference. By morning, the knot often loosens on its own, and the answer seems obvious. This is not laziness but wisdom, knowing when effort has reached its limit. Rest is part of thinking, and patience part of solving. Sometimes the best move is to step away and trust the quiet committee of the night.
