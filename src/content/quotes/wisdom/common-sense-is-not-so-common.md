Voltaire's joke hides a serious observation: the things everyone claims to know are the things few people actually practice. Common sense should mean judging plainly, without fashion, panic, or pride, yet crowds regularly abandon it for whatever is loudest. The phrase stings because it is true in every age: we nod at obvious truths and then act against them. Real common sense is rarer than it sounds, because it requires the discipline to pause, ignore the noise, and do the plainly sensible thing when everyone else is doing something else.
