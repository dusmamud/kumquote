Socrates traces wisdom back to a feeling we often outgrow: wonder. A child asks why the sky is blue or why people act as they do, but adults, busy and certain, stop asking and start assuming. Wonder is the crack in that certainty; it admits that something is not yet understood and invites us to look closer. Every inquiry, every science, and every honest philosophy starts there. Keep the capacity to be surprised, and wisdom keeps growing. Lose it, and learning stops.
