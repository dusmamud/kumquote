Jung points out that real thinking is hard work. It demands attention, honesty, and the willingness to change our minds. Judging, by contrast, is easy — it is quick, certain, and requires no effort. That is why most people prefer it. But a judgement is not a thought; it is a shortcut that skips the thinking. When we judge, we stop learning about the person or situation in front of us. Wisdom asks us to do the harder thing: to pause, to examine, to understand before we decide. Thinking is difficult precisely because it is the work that matters.
