Jordan draws a sharp line between knowing and doing. Many people know exactly what they should do: exercise, save money, apologize, start the project. Wisdom is seeing the right next step clearly. But virtue begins only when the step is actually taken. Knowledge without action is just decoration, and good intentions without follow-through change nothing. The gap between knowing and doing is where most lives stall. Close it by making the next step small enough to start today. Wisdom points the way; character walks it, one ordinary action at a time.
