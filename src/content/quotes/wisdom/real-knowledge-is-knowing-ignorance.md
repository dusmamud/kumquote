Confucius draws a line between knowing facts and knowing yourself. Real knowledge is not a pile of information; it is an honest map of what you understand and what you do not. People who mistake a little learning for mastery make the biggest errors, because they act with false confidence. Those who know the edges of their ignorance ask better questions, seek help sooner, and learn faster. This kind of self-knowledge is rare because it requires honesty about our limits. Yet it is the foundation of every other kind of learning, and the mark of a genuinely educated mind.
