Confucius points out that claiming to know everything is proof of the opposite. Life keeps presenting new situations, and anyone who has faced them all has simply not been asked enough yet. The person who thinks they have every answer stops listening, stops learning, and slowly falls behind. Real understanding comes with humility: recognising that each question answered opens the door to harder ones. Growth belongs to those who admit what they do not know. The wisest people are not those with all the answers, but those who keep asking better questions.
