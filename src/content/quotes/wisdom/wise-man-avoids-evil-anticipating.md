Most trouble announces itself quietly, long before it arrives in full force. The wise person pays attention to those early signs and acts while the cost of acting is still low. This is not fear or pessimism; it is foresight. It is far easier to step around a hole than to climb out of one. Anticipation turns wisdom into something practical, a habit of looking ahead rather than merely reacting. The best way to survive a storm is often to see the clouds gathering and prepare before the rain begins.
