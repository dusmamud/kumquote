Anyone can memorize an answer, but a good question opens a door that no answer could have found, reframing the problem so that new understanding becomes possible. The wise person knows that answers age quickly while the right question keeps working, guiding inquiry long after the first reply has faded. Asking well takes humility, curiosity, and the courage to admit what we do not know. In a world full of confident answers, the rare skill is the question that makes everyone think more clearly, and that is where wisdom truly lives.
