We usually treat our wounds as flaws to hide, as proof that something went wrong. Rumi sees them differently. The broken place is also the opening through which understanding, compassion, and light can enter. Pain cracks our certainty and our pride, and through that crack we begin to see ourselves and others more honestly. People who have suffered often become kinder, because they know what hurt feels like. It means letting the wound teach us, softening us, and becoming a doorway to deeper wisdom, empathy, and a more honest way of living.
