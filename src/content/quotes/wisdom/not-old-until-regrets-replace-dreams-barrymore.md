Barrymore measures age not in years but in the balance between dreams and regrets. A person stays young while the future still holds promise, while there are plans to make, things to learn, and hopes to chase. Old age begins when we stop looking forward and start living backward, replaying what might have been instead of imagining what still could be. Regret is not the enemy by itself; it becomes one only when it crowds out dreaming. The remedy is simple and lifelong: keep a dream on the horizon, however modest, and let the future stay larger than the past.
