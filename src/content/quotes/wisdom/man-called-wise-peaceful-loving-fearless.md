The Buddha measures wisdom by character, not by talk. A person can speak endlessly about truth and still live in anger and fear, and all that speech proves nothing. True wisdom shows itself in how someone lives: peaceful when provoked, loving when it is difficult, and fearless when it matters. Words are easy; conduct is the test. This quote redirects our admiration from the eloquent to the steady, reminding us that wisdom is something you are, not something you say.
