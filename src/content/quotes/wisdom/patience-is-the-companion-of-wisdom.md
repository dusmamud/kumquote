Augustine pairs two qualities we rarely connect: patience and wisdom. Quick answers feel strong, but they often come from irritation or fear rather than understanding. Wisdom needs time to see clearly, to hear all sides, and to let feelings settle. Patience is the quiet companion that makes that waiting possible. When we rush a decision, we usually choose the loudest option, not the best one. The patient person can sit with uncertainty, gather facts, and act only when the picture is full. In a hurried world, patience is not passivity; it is wisdom protecting itself from haste.
