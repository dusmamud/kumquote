Franklin jokes that ignorance is natural but staying ignorant takes effort. We are all born knowing nothing, yet the world constantly offers lessons, books, conversations, and mistakes to learn from. To remain stupid, a person must actively refuse all of it, ignoring evidence and avoiding reflection. Wisdom, by contrast, requires only openness: listening, asking questions, and admitting when you are wrong. The quote is a gentle warning that foolishness is rarely an accident; it is a choice to stop learning.
