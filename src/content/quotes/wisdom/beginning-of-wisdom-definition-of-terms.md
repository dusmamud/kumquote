Arguments collapse not because people disagree, but because they use the same words to mean different things. Before debating justice, success, or happiness, we must first agree on what those words actually mean. Clear definitions turn vague quarrels into honest discussions, where real differences can be examined instead of hidden behind confusion. Wisdom begins with this simple discipline: define your terms, listen to how others define theirs, and only then begin to reason together. Without that foundation, even the cleverest argument builds on sand, and both sides leave convinced they have won while understanding nothing.
