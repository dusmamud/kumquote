Shaw redirects wisdom from looking backward to looking forward. Remembering the past is easy; anyone can collect memories. Wisdom begins when we accept responsibility for what comes next — when we stop using the past as an excuse and start using it as a guide. The person who asks, "What will I do with what I know?" is wiser than the one who merely recites what happened. Our future is shaped less by where we have been than by what we choose to carry forward.
