Goethe reminds us that wisdom cannot be borrowed. We can repeat a true saying without understanding it, the way a child repeats a word without knowing its meaning. A thought becomes ours only when we test it against our own life, argue with it, and watch it hold up under pressure. That honest rethinking is slow work, but it is the only way an old truth becomes a living one. The thoughts that change us are the ones we have earned for ourselves.
