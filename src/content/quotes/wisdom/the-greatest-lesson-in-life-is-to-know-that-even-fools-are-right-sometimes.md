Winston Churchill warns against dismissing people entirely. It is easy to label someone a fool and then ignore everything they say, but truth does not check credentials before it speaks. A broken clock is right twice a day, and a person we consider foolish may still see one thing clearly that we have missed. The greatest lesson is humility: separating the idea from the person who offers it. Listening to everyone costs little, while refusing to listen can cost us the one insight we needed most. Wisdom keeps its ears open, even toward unlikely teachers.
