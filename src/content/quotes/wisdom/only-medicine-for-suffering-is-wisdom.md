Force and punishment can restrain harm, but they rarely heal its causes. Suffering, crime, and cruelty grow from ignorance, fear, and short-sighted choices — conditions that only understanding can change. Wisdom teaches people to see consequences, to value others, and to choose better paths before harm is done. That is why education, honest conversation, and good example matter more than anger. A wiser society is a kinder one, not because people are forced to behave, but because they understand why goodness matters. The cure for the world's troubles begins in the mind.
