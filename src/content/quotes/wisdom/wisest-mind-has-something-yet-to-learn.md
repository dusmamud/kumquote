Santayana makes learning a lifelong condition, not a phase that ends with school. The wisest people are not those who have finished learning but those who have learned how much remains. Arrogance closes the mind by declaring the case settled; humility keeps it open, curious, and able to change. Every year adds new evidence, new tools, and new mistakes to understand, so the expert who stops learning is soon overtaken by the beginner who has not. To admit there is something yet to learn is not a confession of ignorance; it is the mark of a mind still alive.
