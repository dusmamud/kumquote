Proust insists that wisdom cannot be handed over like a gift. Advice and books can point the way, but understanding must be earned through your own experience. No one else can take the journey for you or spare you its difficulties. The detours, mistakes, and slow discoveries are the actual material of wisdom. This is both humbling and freeing: no shortcut exists, but the path is yours alone. What you learn by living through it stays with you in a way that borrowed wisdom never can.
