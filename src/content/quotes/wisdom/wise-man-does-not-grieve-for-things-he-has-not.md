Much of our unhappiness comes from staring at what we lack: the bigger house, the better job, the life someone else seems to have. The wise person turns that gaze around and counts what is already present — health, friends, freedom, a skill, a roof. Rejoicing in what you have does not mean giving up ambition; it means refusing to let missing things poison the good ones. Gratitude is the practical form of wisdom here, a daily habit of noticing blessings before they are gone. The person who rejoices in what they have is already rich.
