Enemies are rarely generous teachers, yet they teach us things our friends never will. A friend softens criticism; an opponent points straight at our weak spots, often with uncomfortable accuracy. This is not about agreeing with critics or surrendering to them. It is about refusing to waste a useful lesson simply because of who delivered it. Every clash reveals something: a blind spot, a bad habit, a gap in our thinking. Those who can learn from opposition grow faster than those who only learn from praise.
