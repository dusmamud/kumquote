Learning can quietly feed pride: the more we know, the easier it is to look down on those who know less. Wisdom corrects that pride by reminding us how much remains unknown. The more deeply you study any subject, the more you see its mysteries, and the humbler you become. That humility is not false modesty; it is honest sight. It makes you a better teacher, a better listener, and a better friend, because people open up to those who do not judge them. Let your knowledge make you useful, and let your awareness of its limits make you wise.
