Solomon ibn Gabirol lays out wisdom as a sequence, and silence comes first. Before we can learn, we must stop talking — to others and to ourselves. Listening follows: real attention to what is being said and what is being left unsaid. Memory holds what we have heard, practice turns it into skill, and teaching others completes the circle by revealing what we still do not understand. Wisdom is not a single insight. It is a discipline of stages, each depending on the one before. Skip the silence, and the rest never quite works.
