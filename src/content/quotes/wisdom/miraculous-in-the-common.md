Wisdom is often pictured as something rare and distant, found only in great books or faraway places. Emerson says its true mark is simpler: the ability to see the miraculous in the common. A sunrise, a seed growing, a stranger's kindness — these ordinary wonders surround us daily, yet most people walk past them without noticing. The wise person has not lost the capacity for wonder. They look closely at everyday life and find it extraordinary. Keeping that sense of awe alive is not childish; it is the deepest form of understanding the world.
