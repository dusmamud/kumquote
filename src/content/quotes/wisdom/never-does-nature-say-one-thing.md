Juvenal reminds us that wisdom and nature speak the same language. Nature runs on honest cause and effect: neglect a plant and it withers, rest a body and it heals. Wisdom simply agrees with these facts instead of fighting them. We get into trouble when we demand exceptions, chasing shortcuts that nature never grants. The wise person studies how life actually works and aligns choices with reality, rather than with wishes. There is no conflict between the two voices, only our reluctance to listen. Trust what is real, act accordingly, and life stops arguing back so loudly.
