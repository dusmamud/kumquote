Voltaire describes the strange arithmetic of learning: every book opens new rooms and reveals more doors we had never noticed. The beginner mistakes a little knowledge for mastery, while the serious reader grows more aware of the vastness beyond. This is not despair but honesty. Knowing how much we do not know keeps us curious, humble, and willing to change our minds. Real education does not end in certainty; it ends in a deeper respect for mystery and a quieter confidence about what we actually understand.
