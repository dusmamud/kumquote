A life worth remembering is built in one of two ways: by creating something meaningful for others, or by living so boldly that the story itself inspires. Franklin challenges us to choose one, or both, rather than drifting through days that leave nothing behind. Writing something worth reading means thinking clearly, sharing honestly, and adding real value. Doing something worth writing means taking risks, serving others, and pursuing work that matters. Both paths demand effort and courage. The alternative is a life spent consuming without contributing. Wisdom asks us to leave something behind that was worth the time it took.
