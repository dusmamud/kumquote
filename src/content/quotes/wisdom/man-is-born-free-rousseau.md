We are born free, yet everywhere we find ourselves bound by laws, customs, debts, and expectations we never chose. The chains are not only iron ones; they include social pressure, economic necessity, and the opinions that shape our choices before we notice. Rousseau's point is not that all chains are evil, but that we should ask which of them are legitimate and which merely serve the powerful. A free society, he argues, is one where people obey only laws they have a hand in making, so that obedience and freedom become the same thing.
