Coelho ties wisdom to love in a way that flips our usual thinking. We tend to treat wisdom as a matter of intellect, something gained through study and analysis. He argues that wisdom grows from the heart: loving deeply teaches us patience, humility, and the limits of our own understanding. The fool, by contrast, believes love can be mastered like a formula, dissected and explained. But love refuses to be reduced to logic; it must be lived. Wisdom begins where certainty about love ends.
