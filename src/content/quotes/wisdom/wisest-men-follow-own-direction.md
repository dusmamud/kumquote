Crowds are loud, and their opinions arrive with confidence, which makes following them feel safe. Yet the crowd is often wrong, or simply answering a different question than the one we face. Euripides points to a quieter kind of courage: thinking for oneself. Following our own direction does not mean ignoring advice or dismissing experience. It means weighing what we hear against our own judgment and values, then deciding for ourselves. Their path may look lonely at times, but it is genuinely theirs, and that is why it holds.
