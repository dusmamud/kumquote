Growing older is easy; growing old well is one of life's hardest arts. It asks us to accept changing strength, to let go of old roles, and to find new purpose in each season. The wise elder does not cling to youth or curse the years, but gathers what age alone can give: patience, perspective, and freedom from the need to impress. Every stage of life has its own work, and the final chapters deserve the same care as the first. Learn to age gracefully now, by holding things lightly and valuing people over pride, and the years will add depth instead of bitterness.
