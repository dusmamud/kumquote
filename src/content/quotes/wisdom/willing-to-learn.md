We like to define ourselves by what we already know — our degrees, our experience, our certainties. Bateson says our real identity lies elsewhere: in what we are willing to learn. Knowledge goes stale quickly in a changing world, but a willingness to learn never does. The person who stays curious keeps growing long after the expert who stopped learning has been left behind. What matters is not the size of what you know today, but the openness of your mind tomorrow. That willingness is what truly defines who you are.
