Talking feels powerful, especially when we want to be seen as clever, but words spoken too quickly can undo us. Silence, by contrast, costs nothing and protects everything. A person who listens first learns what the room actually needs, and when they finally speak, their words carry weight. The fear of seeming foolish keeps many people talking, yet the quote suggests the greater risk lies in the opposite direction. A foolish remark, once spoken, cannot be taken back, while silence leaves room to learn. Wisdom often looks like restraint: knowing that not every thought needs to be said aloud.
