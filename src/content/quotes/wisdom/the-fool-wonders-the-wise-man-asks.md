Wondering is easy; anyone can stare at a mystery and feel amazed. Asking is harder, because a real question demands courage, honesty, and the willingness to hear an answer we may not like. The fool stays in wonder, enjoying the feeling without ever seeking understanding. The wise turn wonder into inquiry, putting their confusion into words and following where the answers lead. This is the difference between admiring a locked door and searching for its key. Curiosity becomes wisdom only when it moves from passive amazement to active questioning, and from questioning to learning.
