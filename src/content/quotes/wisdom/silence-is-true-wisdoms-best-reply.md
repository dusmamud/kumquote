Not every insult needs an answer, and not every argument needs a winner. Silence can say what words cannot: that you refuse to be drawn into anger, gossip, or a fight that helps no one. The wise person knows that a sharp reply often feels good for a moment and costs far more later. Choosing silence is not weakness; it takes real strength to hold your tongue when pride demands a response. In meetings, in families, and in public debate, the quiet pause often carries more authority than the loudest voice. Speak when words help, and let silence answer the rest.
