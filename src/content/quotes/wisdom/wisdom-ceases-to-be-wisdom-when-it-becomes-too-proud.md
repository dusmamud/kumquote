Gibran gives wisdom a human test. If knowledge makes you too proud to weep, too grave to laugh, or too selfish to care for others, it has stopped being wisdom. Real wisdom stays tender: it can feel sorrow, enjoy laughter, and reach beyond itself. The moment learning hardens into superiority, it loses its value. Wisdom is measured not by how much you know, but by how fully you remain human while knowing it. Keep your heart soft, and your understanding will stay true.
