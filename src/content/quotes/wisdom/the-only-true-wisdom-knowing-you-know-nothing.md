Socrates turns wisdom upside down: it begins not with answers but with admitting how little we know. The person who believes they know everything stops asking questions, stops listening, and stops learning — and that certainty is the real ignorance. Recognising our limits, by contrast, keeps the mind open and curious. It makes us better listeners, better learners, and better decision-makers, because we check our assumptions instead of defending them. True wisdom is humble. It is the quiet confidence to say "I don't know" and then go and find out.
