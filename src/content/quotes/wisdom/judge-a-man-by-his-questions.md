Voltaire suggests that answers can be rehearsed, borrowed, or faked, but questions reveal the mind behind them. A good question shows curiosity, honesty, and the courage to admit what is unknown. It shows what a person cares about and how deeply they think. Answers, by contrast, often tell us only what someone has memorised or what they want us to believe. In a conversation, a job interview, or a friendship, listen to the questions. They are the truest window into a person's character — and asking better questions is one of the fastest ways to become wiser ourselves.
