Wilde gives failure a kinder name. When we call our failures 'mistakes,' we can pretend they are accidents. When we call them 'experience,' we admit they taught us something. Every error carries a lesson, but only if we are willing to read it. Experience is not simply the passing of time; it is the record of what we tried, what went wrong, and what we did differently next. The wisest people are not those who never failed. They are those who turned their failures into a curriculum and kept studying.
