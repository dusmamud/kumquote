Longfellow argues that wisdom is caught, not just taught. Books can inform us, but a conversation with a wise person does something books cannot: it answers our questions, challenges our assumptions, and shows us how wisdom actually lives in a human being. Ten years of solitary study can leave us full of facts and empty of judgement, while one honest evening across a table can change how we see everything. Seek out wise company; it is the shortest road to wisdom.
