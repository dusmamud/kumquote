Caen jokes that wisdom is not handed out with age; it is earned by overreaching. The first time we take on more than we can handle, we discover our limits the hard way, and that discovery is the beginning of judgment. Before that moment, confidence is mostly untested guesswork. After it, we know what too much looks like, and we measure the next task against real experience. Failure here is not wasted effort but tuition. Wisdom teeth, like real ones, arrive late and hurt a little, but they mark the moment a person starts chewing carefully.
