Thoreau defines wisdom by restraint. The wise do not rush into desperate measures; they wait, observe, and choose carefully. Desperation is usually a sign that earlier, calmer choices were avoided. When we act from panic, we trade a bad situation for a worse one. Wisdom is the patience to sit with difficulty without making it worse. It is knowing that not every problem needs an immediate answer, and that some problems dissolve when given time. The strongest move is often the quietest: to pause, to think, and to act only when the action is sound.
