Wisdom is usually earned the hard way: by making mistakes and living with the results. We gain experience precisely because we did not yet know better, and that experience slowly teaches us what we lacked. The circle is humbling, but it is also hopeful — every error is raw material for understanding. The wise person is not someone who never failed, but someone who paid attention when they did. So treat your mistakes as tuition rather than shame. Ask what each one taught you, and you will find that your worst days often became your best teachers.
