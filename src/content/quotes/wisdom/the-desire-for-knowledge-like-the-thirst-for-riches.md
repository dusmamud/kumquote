Sterne compares the hunger for knowledge to the thirst for wealth: both grow as they are fed. Each answer opens new questions, and each discovery reveals how much remains unknown. This is not a warning but a description of curiosity's nature. The more you learn, the larger the world becomes. A wise person accepts this endless appetite without frustration, enjoying the pursuit itself. Knowledge does not end in satisfaction; it ends in wonder, and wonder keeps the mind alive and moving forward.
