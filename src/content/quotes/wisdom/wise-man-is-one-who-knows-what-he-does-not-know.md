Knowing what we do not know is the beginning of real wisdom. The fool rushes to answer every question, certain of things he has never studied, while the wise pause and admit the limits of their understanding. This honesty is not weakness; it is strength, because it opens the door to learning. A person who knows their ignorance asks better questions, listens more carefully, and changes their mind when evidence demands it. Pretending to know everything freezes growth entirely. The wisest people are those who can map their own ignorance and treat it as the starting point of discovery.
