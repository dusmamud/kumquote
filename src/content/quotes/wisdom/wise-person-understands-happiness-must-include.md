Happiness pursued alone is fragile. A person who seeks only their own pleasure soon finds it hollow, because joy deepens when it is shared. The wise understand that our lives are woven together: the happiness of our family, friends, and neighbors shapes our own. Helping others flourish is not self-sacrifice but self-interest rightly understood, since a kinder world is a happier place to live in. When we include others in our happiness, we multiply it. True wisdom, then, is not cleverness but connection, the recognition that no one is truly happy while those around them suffer.
