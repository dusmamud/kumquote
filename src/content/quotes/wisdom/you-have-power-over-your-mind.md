Marcus Aurelius, an emperor who faced war and plague, found his strength in a single distinction: the mind is ours, the world is not. We cannot command events, other people, or the future, and struggling to do so only exhausts us. But we can always choose how we interpret what happens and how we respond. That inner freedom is real power. Realising this is not a one-time insight but a daily practice — catching ourselves when we rage against what we cannot change, and returning attention to what we can. Strength grows from that return.
