Mirth is pleasant, but it rarely teaches us anything. We laugh, we relax, and we move on unchanged. Sorrow, on the other hand, slows us down and forces us to ask what matters. The wise do not seek sadness for its own sake, but they do not run from it either. They know that the hardest rooms in life are often the classrooms where character is formed. A person who has faced grief honestly returns to joy with deeper gratitude and clearer eyes.
