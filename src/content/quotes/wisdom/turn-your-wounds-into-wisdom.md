Winfrey reframes pain as raw material for growth. Wounds are unavoidable: loss, failure, betrayal, and disappointment touch every life. What differs is what we do with them. We can let them harden into bitterness, or we can study them until they teach us something. The person who reflects on a painful experience gains judgement that no easy life could provide. Turning wounds into wisdom does not mean pretending the hurt was good. It means refusing to waste it, and letting the hardest chapters become the source of our deepest understanding.
