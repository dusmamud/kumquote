This old proverb admits an uncomfortable truth: wisdom is expensive, and the price is usually paid in mistakes. We learn the deepest lessons not from books alone but from failures, losses, and the consequences of our own choices. The cost can feel high at the time, yet the purchase is worth it, because wisdom bought through experience stays with us in a way that borrowed advice never does. Every costly lesson, fully learned, becomes an asset that protects us for the rest of our lives.
