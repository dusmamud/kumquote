Gandhi warns that certainty is a trap. The moment we believe our wisdom is complete, we stop listening and start making mistakes. Even the strongest can weaken and the wisest can err, so humility is not weakness but clear sight. Holding our convictions lightly lets us correct course when new evidence arrives. This quote asks for a balance: act on what you believe, but remember you might be wrong. That reminder keeps the mind open, the conscience awake, and wisdom alive instead of frozen into dogma.
