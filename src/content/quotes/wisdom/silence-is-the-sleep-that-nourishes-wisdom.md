Wisdom does not grow in noise. Bacon compares silence to sleep because both do their best work quietly, out of sight: the mind sorts, settles, and strengthens what it has learned. In a world that rewards constant talking and instant opinion, silence feels like wasted time, yet it is often the most productive pause we can take. When we stop filling every moment with words, we finally hear our own thoughts and notice what matters. A wise person knows when to speak and when to be still. Protect your quiet hours, and wisdom will have room to grow.
