Bacon reminds us that not every book deserves the same attention, and wisdom lies in knowing the difference. Some books need only a taste, a quick look to catch their flavor and decide whether they merit more time. Others can be swallowed whole, read quickly for their story or information. But a few rare books deserve to be chewed and digested, studied slowly, argued with, and returned to again and again. Reading well means matching our effort to the book's worth, and giving our deepest focus to the few that truly feed the mind.
