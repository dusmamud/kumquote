Wisdom cannot be handed over like a parcel or learned from a single lesson. It grows slowly out of lived experience: the mistakes made, the risks taken, the years of watching how things actually work. Books can point the way, but only experience turns knowledge into judgment. That is why the wise often speak quietly and choose carefully; their understanding was earned over time. Anyone seeking wisdom should seek experience first, and let patience do the rest of the work.
