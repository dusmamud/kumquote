Cicero ranks the ways people learn, and reason comes first. The wise learn by thinking — they understand principles and avoid mistakes before making them. Average minds need experience, learning only after the fact. Those who learn by necessity wait until pain forces them, and those ruled by instinct never learn at all. The ladder is clear: reason is the fastest teacher, experience the slowest honest one. Wisdom is the choice to learn from thought rather than waiting for life to teach the same lesson more harshly.
