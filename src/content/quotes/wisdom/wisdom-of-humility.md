Humility is not thinking less of ourselves, but thinking of ourselves less, and admitting how much remains unknown. Every answer we gain opens new questions, so the wiser we become, the larger the mystery appears. Arrogance, by contrast, closes the mind; it declares the search finished and mistakes confidence for knowledge. True wisdom grows in the opposite direction, through the quiet admission that we may be wrong. This humility is endless because learning never ends, and each year adds fresh reasons to listen more and assume less. The humble mind stays teachable forever.
