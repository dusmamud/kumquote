It is easy to dismiss a foolish question, but Bruce Lee sees hidden value in it. A wise person can learn from anything — even a silly question reveals how people think, what they misunderstand, and where they are coming from. The fool, meanwhile, gains nothing even from a brilliant answer, because he is not truly listening. The difference is not intelligence; it is attitude. Wisdom is a way of approaching the world, open and curious, while foolishness is a closed door. What you learn depends less on what is offered and more on how you receive it.
