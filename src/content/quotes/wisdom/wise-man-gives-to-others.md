Common sense says that giving things away leaves you with less, but Lao Tzu describes a different kind of wealth. Wisdom, kindness, and knowledge do not shrink when shared — they grow. A teacher who gives away understanding gains deeper insight; a friend who gives generously gains richer bonds. Hoarding treasures of the mind only lets them gather dust. The wise person knows that what is given returns multiplied, not in the same form, but in trust, gratitude, and connection. Generosity is not a loss; it is the wisest investment a person can make.
