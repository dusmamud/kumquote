Facts and skills can earn you a salary, but they cannot tell you how to live well. Knowledge answers questions; wisdom decides which questions matter, when to speak, and when to stay silent. A person may hold many degrees and still make poor choices about money, relationships, or time. Making a life requires judgement, patience, and care for others — things no textbook can hand you. Gather knowledge eagerly, but measure yourself by wisdom: by the quality of your decisions and the peace they bring. A good living feeds you; a good life fulfils you.
