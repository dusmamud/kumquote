Shaw rejects the popular idea that a finished self is hiding somewhere, waiting to be discovered. There is no buried treasure of identity to dig up; instead, we make ourselves through choices, habits, and acts of will, day after day. Finding yourself sounds passive, as if life were a treasure hunt, while creating yourself puts the responsibility squarely in our hands. The invitation is liberating: you are not stuck with the person you were yesterday, because today offers fresh material for the person you choose to become.
