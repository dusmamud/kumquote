Franklin places humility at the entrance of wisdom. Before we can learn anything real, we must admit how much we do not know. A person who believes they already understand everything has closed the door to growth, while the person who knows their own ignorance stays curious, asks better questions, and listens more carefully. Recognising our limits is not weakness; it is the first act of clear thinking. Every field of knowledge rewards those who approach it as students. Wisdom begins the moment we stop pretending to be certain and start being honest about what we still need to learn.
