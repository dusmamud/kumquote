Quinault warns that wisdom can become a fault when it is displayed too eagerly. Knowing more than the moment requires can breed argument, suspicion, and a habit of correcting others for no good reason. True judgment includes knowing when to stay silent and let a small error pass. A wise person measures not only what is true but what is useful to say, and when. Restraint is part of wisdom, because knowledge without timing does more harm than ignorance ever could.
