Amiel describes teaching as an art of suggestion rather than command. A good teacher does not force conclusions on a student; she guesses what is already forming in the student's mind and offers the next step. Suggestion respects the learner's freedom and invites thinking instead of memorising. The wisest guidance is often a question, a hint, or an example that lets understanding arrive on its own. True teaching awakens what is already there rather than pouring in what is not.
