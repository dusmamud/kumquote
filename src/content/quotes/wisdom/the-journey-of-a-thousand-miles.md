Every great undertaking looks impossible from the starting line. A thousand miles is too far to imagine walking, and so many people never begin at all. Lao Tzu's wisdom is that the whole journey is never taken at once; it is taken one step at a time, and only the first step is ever required of us right now. That first step is small, ordinary, and entirely possible. Once taken, the second step becomes easier, and momentum quietly builds. Large goals are not achieved by heroic leaps but by a long series of modest moves.
