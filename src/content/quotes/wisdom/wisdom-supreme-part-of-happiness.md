Happiness built on luck or pleasure alone is fragile, because luck changes and pleasures fade. Sophocles places wisdom at the top of happiness because wisdom is what makes happiness last. A wise person chooses well, avoids needless trouble, and knows what is truly worth wanting. In this view, wisdom is not cold or detached; it is the skill of living well. Happiness without wisdom can be reckless, chasing whatever feels good in the moment. Wisdom gives happiness roots, so that it survives the seasons and grows deeper over time.
