Cousins defines wisdom as foresight, not knowledge. Anyone can learn facts; wisdom is the habit of asking what happens next before acting. A clever person wins the argument today and loses the relationship tomorrow; a wise person sees both outcomes and chooses differently. Anticipation is a skill built from attention: noticing patterns, remembering how similar choices ended, and pausing before the easy move. The future is not a mystery to the wise, it is the shadow of the present, and those who study the shadow make fewer mistakes in the light.
