Emerson captures the slow work of time: a single day rarely reveals what it is teaching us. The meaning of an event often appears only years later, when the pattern finally comes into view. What feels like failure today may prove to be the turn that saved us; what feels like delay may be preparation. Patience is therefore a form of intelligence — the willingness to let the years do their teaching, trusting that understanding arrives on its own schedule, not ours.
