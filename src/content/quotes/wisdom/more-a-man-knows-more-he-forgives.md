Catherine suggests that understanding and forgiveness grow together. The more we know about people, their motives, their fears, their circumstances, the harder it becomes to judge them harshly. Ignorance breeds quick condemnation, because we see only the offence and not the story behind it. Knowledge widens our view: we begin to see why people act as they do, and that understanding softens anger into compassion. Forgiveness, then, is not weakness or forgetting. It is the natural result of seeing clearly. The wiser we become, the more room we find in our hearts to let others be human.
