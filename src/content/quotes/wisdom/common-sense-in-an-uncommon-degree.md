Coleridge demystifies wisdom: it is not some rare, mysterious gift, but ordinary good sense carried further than most people carry it. The wise person simply notices what is obvious, thinks clearly when others panic, and acts on plain truths that everyone knows but few follow. Wisdom looks extraordinary only because common sense is so uncommonly practised. Cultivate clear judgement in small daily matters, and the world will eventually call it wisdom. Practise clear judgement in small daily matters, and wisdom will follow on its own.
