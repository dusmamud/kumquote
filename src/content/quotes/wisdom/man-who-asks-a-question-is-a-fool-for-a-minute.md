Confucius weighs a small embarrassment against a lasting one. Asking a question may expose what we do not know for a moment, and pride hates that moment. But silence protects the pride and keeps the ignorance, which costs far more over a lifetime. Every expert was once someone willing to look foolish in front of others. The price of a question is a minute of discomfort; the price of never asking is years of guessing. Wisdom is choosing the smaller cost.
