Mencken punctures a comforting myth: years alone do not make anyone wise. Time can just as easily harden opinions, confirm prejudices, and turn experience into an excuse for refusing to learn. Real wisdom is not the automatic product of birthdays; it comes from reflection, from admitting mistakes, and from staying curious when it would be easier to settle. Age gives us the raw material of experience, but only an open mind turns that material into understanding. Stay curious, keep reflecting, and the years will teach you well.
