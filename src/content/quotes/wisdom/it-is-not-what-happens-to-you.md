Epictetus, born into slavery and later a teacher of Stoicism, knew that events are often beyond our control. What remains ours is the response. Two people can face the same setback: one is broken by it, the other grows through it, and the difference lies in how each interprets what happened. Reaction is not about pretending pain does not exist; it is about choosing what the pain will mean and what we will do next. This teaching returns power to us in situations where we feel powerless.
