This ancient verse puts wisdom first among all the things a person might pursue. Wealth, status, and pleasure all fade or fail, but wisdom guides every other choice we make. The verse is also practical: it does not merely praise wisdom, it commands us to seek it actively, and to seek understanding alongside it. Knowledge without understanding is information; wisdom without understanding is guesswork. Make wisdom the priority, and everything else finds its proper place. Seek wisdom first, and every other pursuit will fall into its proper place.
