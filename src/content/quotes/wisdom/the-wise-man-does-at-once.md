Procrastination often wears the disguise of careful planning. The fool waits for the perfect moment, the perfect conditions, or more courage, and the moment never comes. The wise person recognises that delay has its own cost and acts while action is still possible. Machiavelli is not praising haste; he is praising decisiveness. Doing at once what must be done frees the mind from the weight of unfinished business and turns intention into reality. Wisdom shows itself not in endless deliberation but in knowing when thought has done its work and the time to act has arrived.
