Epictetus finds a lesson in the design of our own bodies: listening should take twice the time speaking does. Most of us reverse the ratio, rushing to reply while the other person is still talking, and so we learn little and misunderstand much. Those who listen well gather knowledge, build trust, and speak with far greater weight when they finally do. Wisdom begins with the humility to keep our mouth closed and our ears open, letting the world teach us before we offer our verdict.
