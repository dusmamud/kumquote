We resist change by standing outside it, analyzing and worrying, yet Watts insists understanding comes only from participation. Change cannot be mastered from a distance; it must be entered, moved with, and danced with. Plunging in does not mean recklessness but willingness — accepting that life flows and learning to move with its current rather than against it. Those who join the dance discover rhythm where others see only chaos. Sense is not found before the change but inside it.
