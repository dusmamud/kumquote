Darwin's insight reaches far beyond biology: in life, as in nature, the winners are rarely the strongest or the cleverest, but the most flexible. Strength breaks when the world shifts, and intelligence stalls when it clings to old answers. The adaptable person treats change as information rather than threat, and adjusts course without losing purpose. Whether in a career, a relationship, or a crisis, survival belongs to those willing to learn, let go, and begin again in new conditions. Stay flexible, keep learning, and you will survive whatever changes come.
