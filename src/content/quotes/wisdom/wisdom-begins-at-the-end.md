We usually think wisdom comes before action, guiding us to choose well, but Webster suggests it often arrives afterwards. That is why the end of an experience is such a powerful teacher. Hindsight is not just regret; it is understanding finally catching up with events. The quote invites us to look back deliberately, to harvest the lessons of finished seasons instead of rushing into the next one. Wisdom begins at the end, because only then can we see the whole shape of what happened.
