McKee draws a sharp line between knowing and doing. Wisdom shows us the right path, but knowledge alone changes nothing; it is integrity that moves our feet. The gap between insight and action is where character is tested. A life guided by both wisdom and integrity is consistent: what we believe, we practise. This quote reminds us that understanding is only half the work. The other half is courage, the willingness to walk the path we know is right, even when it costs us comfort or approval.
