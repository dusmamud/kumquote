Maya Angelou offers wisdom without judgement. We cannot be blamed for what we did not yet understand, and the past is not a courtroom. But the moment we learn something new — about the world, about other people, about ourselves — we carry a responsibility to act on it. Knowing better and doing the same is a choice, and a poor one. This quote frees us from guilt over old mistakes while refusing to excuse present ones. Growth is simply this cycle, repeated: do your best, learn, and then do better. That is all wisdom asks of us.
