Knowledge and wisdom are often mistaken for the same thing, but Cowper draws a sharp line between them. Knowledge collects facts and wears them proudly, eager to show how much it has gathered. Wisdom, by contrast, knows the limits of what it understands and stays humble before what it does not. The more a person truly learns, the more aware they become of how much remains unknown. Real wisdom is not the loud display of facts but the quiet honesty of admitting uncertainty. It listens more than it speaks and grows precisely because it never assumes it has finished learning.
