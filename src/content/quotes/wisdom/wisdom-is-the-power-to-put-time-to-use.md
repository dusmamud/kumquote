Watson, who built a business empire, defines wisdom in practical terms: using time and knowledge well. Knowledge alone is inert; a shelf of unread books changes nothing. Time alone is just passing hours. Wisdom is the skill of combining them, spending our hours on what matters and applying what we know where it counts. Most waste comes not from ignorance but from misdirected effort, busywork that feels productive while the important work waits. The wise person asks two questions daily: what do I know, and where should I spend today? Answer both honestly, and the days begin to add up.
