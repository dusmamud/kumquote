Confucius draws a sharp line between knowing facts and knowing ourselves. True knowledge includes an honest map of our ignorance — knowing where our understanding ends. People who pretend to know everything stop learning, while those who admit gaps stay curious and careful. This self-knowledge prevents costly mistakes and opens the door to real growth. Wisdom is not having every answer; it is knowing which questions remain open and being honest about them, with others and with ourselves. It is a discipline worth practising daily, because honest ignorance is the beginning of every real understanding.
