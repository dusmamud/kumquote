A new idea does not simply add information; it changes the shape of the mind that receives it. Once a person has truly grasped a fresh perspective, the old way of seeing the world no longer fits. The mind stretches to hold the new understanding, and even if the details fade, the wider horizon remains. That is why a single book, conversation, or insight can alter a life. Growth is not reversible, and every genuine expansion of thought leaves us permanently larger than we were before.
