Lippmann says wisdom can only be recognised by the wise. Advice, insight, and truth are like music: they mean nothing to an audience that cannot hear them. A closed mind dismisses what it does not understand, while an open mind can receive even difficult truths. This is why the same words can change one person and leave another untouched. To benefit from wisdom, we must first cultivate the ability to listen, to consider, and to admit we might be missing something. Receptivity is the beginning of understanding.
