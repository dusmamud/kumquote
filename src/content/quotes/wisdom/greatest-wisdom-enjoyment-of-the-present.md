Lubbock argues that the wisest goal in life is not wealth or fame but enjoying the present moment fully. We spend so much energy regretting yesterday or fearing tomorrow that today slips past unnoticed. Yet the present is the only time we ever actually live in. Making its enjoyment our supreme object does not mean chasing pleasure recklessly; it means paying attention, savoring small things, and refusing to trade today for a future that may never arrive. The past is gone and the future is unwritten. Wisdom is learning to be glad, right now, for what is already here.
