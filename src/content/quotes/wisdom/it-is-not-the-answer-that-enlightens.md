We spend most of our lives hunting for answers, yet Ionesco suggests the real power lies in the question itself. A good question opens doors that a ready answer would keep shut: it makes us look again, doubt our assumptions, and think for ourselves. Answers can end a conversation, but questions keep it alive. The person who asks well learns more deeply than the person who simply collects facts. So before rushing to be right, pause and ask better questions. What am I missing? What if the opposite were true? In that honest curiosity, real understanding begins.
