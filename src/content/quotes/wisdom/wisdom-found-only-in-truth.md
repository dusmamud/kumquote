Goethe ties wisdom to honesty at its root. Advice built on flattery, comfort, or convenient fiction may soothe for a day, but it fails when it meets reality. Only what is true can guide a decision that must survive contact with the world. Seeking truth is therefore not an abstract duty but a practical one: it is how we avoid building a life on sand. The wise person prefers an uncomfortable fact to a pleasing falsehood, because only facts can be trusted to hold.
