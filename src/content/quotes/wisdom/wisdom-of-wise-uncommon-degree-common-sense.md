Inge brings wisdom down from the clouds and places it in everyday judgment. The wise are not those with the strangest ideas but those who apply ordinary sense more consistently than the rest of us. They notice what is plainly in front of them, weigh consequences before acting, and avoid mistakes that look obvious afterward. Common sense is called common because everyone has some; it is uncommon when it is practiced without exception. Wisdom, then, is not a mystery but a discipline of paying attention.
