Colton points out a quiet advantage we all share: the present is the only time that truly belongs to us. The past is gone beyond recall, and the future is promised to no one. Yet we mortgage the present constantly, trading today for worries about tomorrow or regrets about yesterday. Ownership of the present means using it deliberately: a conversation we actually attend to, work done with care, rest taken without guilt. This hour is ours in a way no other hour is. Spend it well, because it is the only time we can ever truly call our own.
