We often want answers right away, but some questions can only be settled by waiting. Time has a way of revealing what was hidden: a promise kept or broken, a plan that was sound or foolish, a person who was true or false. What looks impressive today may be exposed as empty in a year, and what seems small now may prove lasting. The wisdom here is patience. Instead of rushing to judge, we can let events unfold and trust that time will show what things really are.
