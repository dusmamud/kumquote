A circus arrives with colour and noise, dazzles for a night, and is gone by morning, leaving only an empty field. Hecht sees time the same way: a travelling show that never stays in one place. This is not a reason for despair. The circus will return, in new forms, with new wonders, as long as we are willing to watch for it. The quote asks us to enjoy the show while it is in town, because nothing in life pitches its tent permanently.
