There is always a reason to wait: the moment is not perfect, the resources are not ready, or the risk feels too great. Mandela rejects that excuse. The time is always ripe to do right, because doing right does not depend on ideal conditions — it depends on a decision. Waiting for a better moment often means the moment never comes, and injustice or opportunity simply passes by. Using time wisely means recognising that now is the only time we truly possess. Right action taken today, however small, is worth more than perfect action postponed forever.
