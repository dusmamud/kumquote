Warhol punctures the comforting myth that time alone fixes things. Time passes, but broken relationships, bad habits, and unfinished work do not heal themselves merely by aging. Change requires an agent, and the only agent reliably available is us. Waiting for time to do the work is a polite form of surrender. The quote hands responsibility back to us: if something must change, we must change it. Time provides the opportunity, but we must supply the action, and the sooner we start, the sooner change begins.
