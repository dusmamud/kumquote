A clock does not merely tell the time; it announces how little of it remains. Its ticking is a small, steady voice reminding us that every hour passes whether we use it well or not. Olsen's speaker does something startlingly human: she silences the voice rather than heeding it. The quote is funny, but it is also sad. Throwing away the clock does not stop time; it only stops the reminder. The braver act is to let the clock talk, and to answer it by living deliberately.
