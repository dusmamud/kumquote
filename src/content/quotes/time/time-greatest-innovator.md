We credit inventors and visionaries with changing the world, but Bacon gives the honour to time itself. Given enough time, customs soften, empires fall, ideas once dismissed become obvious, and the impossible quietly becomes routine. This is both a comfort and a warning. What seems permanent today may not survive the decade, and what seems hopeless may yet turn. Patience, then, is not passivity. It is working with time, trusting its slow power to do what force cannot do overnight.
