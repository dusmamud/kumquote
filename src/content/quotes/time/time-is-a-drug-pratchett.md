Time is the one drug no one can refuse, administered daily in equal doses to everyone, and its overdose is certain. We joke about it because the alternative is to be frightened, yet the humor carries a warning: time is not neutral, and it does not wait while we decide how to use it. Every dose brings us closer to the end, which is exactly why the present dose matters so much. The wit makes the medicine easier to take, reminding us to spend our time as though it were precious, because it is.
