In physics, past, present, and future are not separate rooms but one continuous landscape, and the sharp division we feel between them may be a trick of human perception. What has happened and what will happen are, in this view, equally real, laid out together like places on a map. The thought is both humbling and comforting: nothing truly vanishes, and the moments we love are never entirely lost. Time, for Einstein, was less a river carrying us away than a dimension we are still learning to see clearly.
