We talk about time as if it were a road stretching behind and ahead of us, but we never actually live anywhere except this moment. The past survives only as memory, and the future only as imagination; both can mislead us into missing the life happening right now. Regret pulls us backward and worry pulls us forward, yet neither place is real in the way the present is. To notice the now, the breath, the room, the person in front of us, is to find the only time we truly own. Everything else is a story we tell about it.
