Herbert pictures time as a rider that breaks youth, as a horseman breaks a spirited horse. Youth feels invincible, but time steadily tames it: strength softens, beauty fades, and ambitions change shape. The image is not cruel but honest. Time disciplines us all, and resistance only makes the ride harder. The wise response is to accept the training, keep what matters, and let go of what youth alone could carry. Broken in by time, we can still run far. The rider is patient and fair, and those who learn his pace discover strength they never knew they had.
