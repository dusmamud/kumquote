Ali turns a common habit upside down. Counting the days is passive — marking time until something happens, watching life pass. Making the days count is active: filling each one with effort, purpose, and presence so that it matters. The difference is between a life measured and a life lived. Ali's own career embodied the line; he made his days count in the ring and beyond it. The quote asks us to stop tallying time and start investing it, so that when we look back, the days add up to something worth remembering.
