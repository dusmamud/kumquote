Watts, in his famous hymn, pictures time as an ever-rolling stream that bears all its sons away. Generations pass like water: each one arrives, lives briefly, and is carried off. The image is humbling, reminding us how small our span is against the flow of ages. Yet it also brings comfort. The stream has carried countless lives before us and will carry countless after. Our part is to live well within the current, not to resist it. The stream asks only that we live well while it carries us, and leave something good for those who follow.
