Hawthorne pictures time as something that passes overhead, swift and unstoppable, yet never truly gone. What remains is its shadow: the memory of how those hours were used, the habits formed, the words spoken, the work left behind. The image is gentle rather than frightening. It does not warn that time is an enemy, only that it leaves a trace. Every day adds to that shadow, whether we notice it or not. The quote asks for a quiet kind of awareness, living so that the shadow time leaves is one we can look back on without regret.
