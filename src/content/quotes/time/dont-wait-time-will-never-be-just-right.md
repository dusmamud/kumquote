Hill attacks the most popular excuse in the world: waiting for the perfect moment. The right time never arrives, because conditions are never flawless and fear always finds a new reason to delay. People who wait for perfect weather never leave the house. The truth is that imperfect action beats perfect waiting every time. Start small, start messy, start now, and adjust as you go. Momentum is a better teacher than planning, and the first step creates the second. The time will never be just right, so the right time is whenever you begin.
