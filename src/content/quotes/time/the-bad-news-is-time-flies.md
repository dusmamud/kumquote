Time will pass whether we steer it or not. Days, months, and years fly by, and we cannot slow the plane down. The good news is that we are not helpless passengers. We are the pilot. We choose the direction, the altitude, and the destination, within the limits of the journey. That means deciding what matters, setting priorities, and correcting course when we drift. A pilot cannot control the weather, but can still fly with skill and purpose. Altshuler's image is hopeful: time moves fast, but the controls are in our hands, and how we fly makes all the difference.
