A life is not a single grand decision but the sum of ordinary days, and each day is the sum of hours spent. If your days are filled with distraction, hurry, and other people's urgencies, that becomes your life, whatever your intentions were. The good news is that the equation works in reverse: change one day, and you begin to change the life. Spend an hour on what you love, give real attention to one person, and the day takes on a different shape. We do not get a separate life later; this day is the life we are living.
