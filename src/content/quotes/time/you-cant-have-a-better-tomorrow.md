Kettering names the hidden tax on the future: thinking about yesterday all the time. The past deserves reflection, not residence. Every hour spent replaying old mistakes or old glories is an hour stolen from tomorrow's possibilities. A better tomorrow is built in the present, with today's choices, not with yesterday's regrets. That does not mean forgetting the past; it means learning from it quickly and moving on. Release yesterday's grip, and the future suddenly has room to grow. Tomorrow belongs to those who spend their attention on today.
