Brown's list of names is deliberately humbling: Helen Keller, Pasteur, Michelangelo, Mother Teresa, da Vinci, Jefferson, Einstein — people of wildly different gifts, eras, and circumstances, all given the same twenty-four hours. The excuse of not having enough time collapses once we see it plainly, because the difference was never the hours but what each person did with them. Time is the one resource handed out equally to everyone, and it cannot be saved, borrowed, or bought back. The question is not whether we have enough of it, but whether we are spending it on what actually matters to us.
