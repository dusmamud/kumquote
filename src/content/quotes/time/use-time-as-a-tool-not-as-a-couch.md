Kennedy's contrast is blunt: time can either build something or hold us while we rest. A tool is picked up with purpose, aimed at a task, and put to work; a couch is where we sink in and stay comfortable. Most of us treat time as the second, drifting through hours and calling it a busy day. The quote does not demand constant labour; it asks for intention. Use the hours deliberately, even for rest, and they become a tool. Waste them without noticing, and they become a couch that quietly steals the years.
