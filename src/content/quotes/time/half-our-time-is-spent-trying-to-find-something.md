Rogers captures the absurd loop of modern life: we hurry through everything in order to save time, and then stand around wondering what to do with the hours we saved. We rush the meal, the commute, and the conversation, treating each moment as an obstacle to the next one. Then, with time finally in hand, we are bored, because we never learned to enjoy the moments we were rushing past. The joke is a diagnosis. Saving time is pointless if we do not know how to spend it well.
