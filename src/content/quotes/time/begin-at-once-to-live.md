Seneca's advice is urgent: start living now, and treat each day as a complete life of its own. We often treat days as rehearsals for a real life that will begin someday. But there is no someday — there is only today, and then another today. A day lived fully, with attention and purpose, is a small life in itself. If we live each day this way, we will not reach the end feeling unprepared. The art of living is the art of beginning, again and again, every single morning.
