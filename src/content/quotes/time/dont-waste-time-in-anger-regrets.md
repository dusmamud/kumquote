Bennett lists four thieves of time, and none of them steals hours — they steal attention. Anger replays the offense, regret replays the past, worry rehearses a future that may never come, and grudges keep old wounds open. Each feels urgent, yet none changes anything. Time spent in these states is time lost to life itself. The quote is not a demand to feel nothing, but an invitation to notice where our hours go and to spend them instead on what builds, heals, and matters. Letting go is how we get our time back.
