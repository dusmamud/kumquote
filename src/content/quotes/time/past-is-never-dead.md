We like to imagine the past as finished, filed away behind us. But old wounds still ache, old promises still bind, and old choices still shape the present in ways we barely notice. The past lives on in our habits, our fears, and the stories we tell ourselves. Understanding this is not a burden; it is freedom, because what is still alive can still be addressed. We can apologize, forgive, and choose differently today. Time does not erase history, but it gives us the chance to answer it. The past is only dead when we stop letting it teach us.
