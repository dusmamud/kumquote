An idle hour is not free; it is money left unearned, work left undone, and opportunity quietly lost. The comparison works because both time and money are finite, and both reward those who spend them with intention. Wasting an afternoon feels harmless in the moment, yet the hours add up into days, months, and years that can never be recovered. Remembering that time is money does not mean working every waking minute, but spending each hour as though it had a price, because in truth it does.
