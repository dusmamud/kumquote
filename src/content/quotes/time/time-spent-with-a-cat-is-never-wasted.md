Not every hour has to produce something to be well spent. Sitting quietly with a cat, watching it sleep in a patch of sun or chase a shadow across the floor, is a small lesson in being present. Animals do not hurry, plan, or keep score, and their calm is catching. The time feels wasted only if we measure life by output alone. Rest, play, and simple company refill the energy that work draws down. An afternoon given to a creature that asks for nothing but warmth is not lost; it is time returned to us as peace.
