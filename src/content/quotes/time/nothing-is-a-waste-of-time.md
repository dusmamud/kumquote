We often call an hour wasted when it did not produce what we expected, but experience rarely works that way. A failed project teaches judgment, a long walk clears the mind, and even a mistake can become useful if we are honest about what went wrong. The value of time is not only in what we finish; it is also in what we learn along the way. When we use every experience as material for growth, nothing is truly lost. The question is never whether the time was spent, but whether we paid attention to what it gave us.
