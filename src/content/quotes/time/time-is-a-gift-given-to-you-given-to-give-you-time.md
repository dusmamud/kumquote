Juster turns time into something given, not owned. A gift is not earned or bought; it arrives in our hands, and the only question is what we do with it. His playful repetition hides a serious point: time is given to us so that we can have the time of our lives, and wasting it is like leaving a present unopened. The hours will pass whether or not we use them well. Treating each day as a gift rather than a burden changes how we spend it, and a life spent well is the best thank-you we can offer.
