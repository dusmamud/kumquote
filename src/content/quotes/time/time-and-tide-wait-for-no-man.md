Chaucer reminds us that time moves whether we are ready or not. The tide does not pause for the sailor who is unprepared, and the hours do not slow down for the person who keeps delaying. Opportunities pass in the same way: a chance ignored today may not return tomorrow. The quote is a call to act while action is still possible, rather than assuming the moment will wait for us. Preparation matters, but endless preparation becomes another form of delay. Time is the one resource we cannot save up, so the wise use it while they have it.
