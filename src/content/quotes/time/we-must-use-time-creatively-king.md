King warns against treating time as a passive force that will fix things on its own. Time is neutral, and it can serve justice or injustice depending on how people use it; waiting for the right moment is often only a polite way of doing nothing. To use time creatively is to act now, with imagination and moral courage, shaping the hour rather than being shaped by it. The call is urgent and practical: the time we have is the time to do right, and creativity means finding a way to use it well.
