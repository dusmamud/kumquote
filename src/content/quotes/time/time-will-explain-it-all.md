When we are in the middle of confusion or pain, answers feel urgent and out of reach. Euripides offers a gentler view: time itself does the explaining. What seems unfair today may make sense months from now, when we can see the full shape of events. Waiting does not mean doing nothing; it means giving life room to reveal what haste would hide. This idea asks for patience in hard seasons, trusting that understanding often arrives on its own schedule. Some questions are not solved by thinking harder, but by living forward until time quietly makes the meaning clear.
