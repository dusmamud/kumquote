Van Dyke shows how time changes shape with our feelings. Waiting makes it crawl; fear makes it rush; grief stretches it; joy shortens it. The clock measures the same minutes for everyone, but our experience of them is entirely personal. Only love, he says, makes time feel like eternity — not because it stops, but because it becomes full. A life rich in love does not count the hours. This is why the same day can feel endless to one person and too short to another. Time is not only measured; it is felt.
