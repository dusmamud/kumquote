Peters trusts time as a teacher that needs no words. In the middle of a crisis, everything feels urgent, and we cannot tell the important from the noisy. But time sorts things ruthlessly: the insult we thought unforgivable fades, the possession we craved loses its shine, and the people who truly matter remain standing. What survives the years is what was real all along. We do not need to force this clarity; we only need to wait. Time reveals our priorities by simply outlasting everything that was never important.
