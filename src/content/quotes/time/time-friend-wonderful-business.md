Buffett's line is about investing, but its logic applies to anything built to last. Time rewards quality and punishes weakness: a sound business, a strong habit, or a honest reputation grows more valuable with each passing year, while a fragile one is exposed by the same passage. Mediocrity cannot hide forever, because time strips away luck and marketing. The advice is to choose things that improve with age rather than decay, and then give them the years they need. Patience is not passive waiting; it is the active choice to let compounding do its quiet work.
