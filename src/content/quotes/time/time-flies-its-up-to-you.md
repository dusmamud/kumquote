Time moves quickly no matter what we do, and that speed can feel frightening or freeing depending on how we meet it. A passenger simply watches the days pass, while a navigator chooses a course and steers toward it. The quote reminds us that we cannot slow time down, but we can decide where it carries us. Small daily choices about work, rest, and relationships set the direction of a life. Taking the wheel does not mean controlling everything; it means refusing to drift, and guiding our limited hours toward the things we truly want to reach.
