Aurelius pictures time as a river of passing events, each one flowing by and gone. Nothing stays still; people, moments, and empires all move downstream. The image teaches acceptance: fighting the current only exhausts us, while noticing the flow brings calm. Each event arrives, passes, and is replaced, and our task is to meet the present one well. The river does not pause for regret or hurry for ambition. Wisdom is learning to stand in the stream, steady and attentive, while time carries everything forward.
