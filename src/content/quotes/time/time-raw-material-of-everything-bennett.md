Bennett calls time the raw material of everything, the strange substance from which all of life is made. Unlike money or goods, time cannot be earned back, stored up, or borrowed, yet it is handed to everyone in the same daily measure. We shape our days from it, and the quality of our lives depends on how skillfully we work this mysterious material. It is inexplicable because we cannot fully grasp it, only spend it, and each moment used well becomes part of something lasting.
