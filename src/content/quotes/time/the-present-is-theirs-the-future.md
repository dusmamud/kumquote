Tesla knew that the work that matters most is rarely rewarded in its own moment. The present belongs to those who chase quick applause, while the future belongs to those who build patiently for what is yet to come. His words are a comfort to anyone whose efforts go unnoticed today: the value of real work is not measured by immediate praise. Plant seeds now, and trust that time will bring the harvest. But that is how progress happens — one person, working quietly today, for a tomorrow that belongs to them.
