Time is the one treasure nobody can buy back, yet we spend it as if it were endless. Gompers lists its true uses — thinking, acting, building friendships, and becoming better people — because every hour is a choice about who we are becoming. No fortune can purchase an extra day, and no regret can refund a wasted year. The question is never whether we have time, but what we do with the time we have. Spend it on growth, on others, and on becoming a better citizen of the world.
