We often speak as though time is the thing that slips away, but this line turns the idea around. Time does not leave; it is we who move through it, changing as the years pass. The days remain steady and indifferent, while our strength, our youth and our chances quietly fade. That reversal is what gives the words their sting. It asks us to stop blaming the clock for what we have not done. The hours will keep coming whether we use them or not, so the real question is what we do with the brief passage we are given.
