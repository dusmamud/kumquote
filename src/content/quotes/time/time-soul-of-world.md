The soul is what gives a body life, movement and meaning, and this line says time plays that role for the whole world. Without time, nothing could grow, change, or happen at all; everything would be frozen in a single still moment. It is time that lets seeds become trees, strangers become friends, and ideas become realities. To call time the soul of the world is to say it is not an empty background but the living force inside everything. Our days are not just passing; they are the very life of the world unfolding.
