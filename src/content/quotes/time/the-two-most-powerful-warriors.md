Tolstoy, who wrote epic novels of war, names patience and time as the strongest warriors of all — stronger than armies. Force can win a battle, but lasting victories belong to those who wait, endure, and let time do its work. Patience is not passivity; it is the strength to keep going without demanding instant results. Time, meanwhile, heals wounds, matures plans, and reveals truths that haste cannot see. Together they defeat problems that no amount of rushing or force could solve. When you feel powerless, remember these two allies: wait well, and let time fight for you.
