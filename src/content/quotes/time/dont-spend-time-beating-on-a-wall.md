Persistence is a virtue, but it becomes stubbornness when the path is closed. Some obstacles yield to effort, while others do not move, however long we push. Wisdom lies in telling the difference: knowing when to keep trying and when to step back and look for another way. A wall that will not become a door is not a test; it is information. Time spent pounding on it is time taken from building, learning and finding the entrance that opens. Letting go of a closed route is not giving up; it is choosing to spend energy where change is possible.
