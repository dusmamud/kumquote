Einstein calls time an illusion, and physics agrees more than our calendars do. We experience time as a river flowing one way, but the deeper reality is stranger: past, present, and future may all exist together, and our sense of flow is partly a trick of perception. The thought is humbling and freeing at once. If time is not quite what it seems, then our urgency, our regret, and our rush may also be softer than they feel. We cannot master time's mystery, but we can stop worshipping the clock and start living inside each moment fully.
