Many dreams die not because they are impossible but because they look too long. Learning a language, writing a book, or changing a career might take years, and the years ahead seem like a wall. Nightingale points out that the time will pass anyway — the only question is whether we will have something to show for it. Five years from now will arrive whether we start or not, and starting today means arriving there with the goal already achieved. The fear of the duration is a trick of the mind.
