Bacon calls time the author of authors because it writes the final judgement on every book and idea. Writers may praise or attack each other, but only time decides what survives. Works that flatter the moment fade, while those with real substance are read centuries later. The quote humbles every author and every critic. Our task is not to chase the verdict of today but to write something true enough that time will keep. Time is the only critic that cannot be bribed.
