Franklin states a hard truth in six words: time, once spent, cannot be recovered. Money lost can be earned again, a broken object can be repaired, but a wasted hour is gone forever. That finality is what makes time our most precious possession, and also the one we treat most carelessly. The quote is not meant to frighten us but to wake us up. Use time as if it were irreplaceable — because it is — and the life you build with it will be worth the care.
