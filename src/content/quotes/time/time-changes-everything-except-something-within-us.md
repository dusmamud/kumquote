Years pass and everything around us shifts: places, people, habits, and even our own faces in the mirror. Yet some part of us keeps expecting things to stay as they were, and feels startled every time they do not. That surprise is not foolishness; it shows how deeply we attach ourselves to the present moment. We live as if today will last, even though we know it will not. Accepting change does not mean giving up that feeling. It means learning to greet each new season without resentment, and to carry forward what still matters.
