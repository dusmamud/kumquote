Years pass and people change, yet the picture we carry of them often stays frozen at the moment we knew them best. A childhood friend remains forever young in our mind; a parent remains as they were decades ago. Time transforms the real person while memory preserves the old image, and the gap between the two can surprise us when we meet again. This is why reunion can feel strange: we greet a memory, not the person before us. Loving someone well means updating the picture, letting them change, and meeting them as they are now.
