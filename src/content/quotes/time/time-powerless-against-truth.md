Time wears down nearly everything it touches. Buildings crumble, reputations fade, and even the strongest empires fall apart under its slow pressure. Yet truth stands apart from that decay. A fact that is genuinely true does not grow weaker with age; if anything, the passing years strip away the falsehoods around it and leave it clearer. This is a quiet encouragement to anchor our lives in what is real rather than what is fashionable. Fashions pass, but honest ideas and honest work outlast the centuries.
