Time only ever moves forward, carrying us toward a future we cannot see. Memory, though, travels backward, pulling us toward moments that no longer exist. We live suspended between these two currents: one pushing us on, the other pulling us back. This tension explains why the past can feel so vivid, even as it slips further away, and why we can feel both young and old at once. Wisdom lies in honoring memory without living inside it. Let the past inform you, but keep walking forward, because time will not wait for anyone.
