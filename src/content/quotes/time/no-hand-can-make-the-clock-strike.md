The past cannot be replayed, no matter how much we wish it could. Hours that have gone are gone, and no effort, regret, or longing can call them back. Byron's line is a warning against living in yesterday, whether through nostalgia or remorse. Since lost time stays lost, the only honest response is to value the present hour while it is still here. This idea does not ask us to forget the past, but to learn from it and turn our attention to the time we still hold, which is the only time we can truly use.
