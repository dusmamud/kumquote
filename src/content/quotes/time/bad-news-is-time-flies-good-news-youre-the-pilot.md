Time will pass whether we steer or not; that much is certain, and no one can slow it down. But we are not helpless passengers. We hold the controls, choosing the direction, the altitude, and the destination of our days. A pilot who ignores the controls drifts wherever the wind blows, while one who steers with purpose arrives somewhere worth being. The bad news is real, but the good news is better: every morning hands us the controls again. Fly deliberately, choose your course, and the speed of time becomes less frightening, because you are the one flying.
