Tolkien's Gandalf speaks these words to Frodo, who wishes he lived in a different age. The reply is a lesson for anyone who resents their circumstances: we do not choose the times we are born into, the troubles we inherit, or the length of our days. What we do choose is what to do with the time given to us — and that choice is enough. It frees us from wishing for another era and focuses us on the present, where our decisions actually matter. The past and the future are not ours to command.
