The future arrives for everyone at exactly the same speed, no matter how rich, powerful, or clever we are. We cannot hurry it, bribe it, or slow it down, and worrying about it does not change its pace. What we can change is how we meet each hour as it comes. Sixty minutes will pass whether we spend them well or waste them, so the real question is not how fast time moves but what we do with the time we are given. The future is coming anyway; our job is to be ready for it.
