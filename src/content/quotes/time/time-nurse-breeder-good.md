Good things rarely arrive fully formed. Like a child raised by a careful nurse, they need time, attention and steady care before they can stand on their own. A friendship deepens over years, a skill matures through practice, and a reputation is built one honest act at a time. Time does not simply pass; it nurtures whatever we feed it. The reminder here is gentle: plant good things, tend them patiently, and let the slow work of time bring them to life.
