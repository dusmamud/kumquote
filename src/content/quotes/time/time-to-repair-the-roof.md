A leaking roof cannot be fixed in a storm, when the rain is pouring in and every repair is urgent and difficult. The wise time to act is while the sun shines, when the problem is small and the work is easy. This applies far beyond houses: health, relationships, and finances all reward early attention and punish delay. Preparation feels unnecessary until it becomes impossible. The best moment to mend what is broken is before it breaks. Small repairs made early prevent large damage later.
