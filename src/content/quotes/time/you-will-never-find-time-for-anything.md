Time does not appear on its own, no matter how long we wait for a free afternoon. Our days fill themselves with small demands, and the things that matter most are usually the ones without a deadline. Making time means choosing deliberately: saying no to something small so we can say yes to something important. It might mean waking earlier, closing a screen, or letting a minor task wait. The hours are already there; the question is whether we will claim them for what we truly care about.
