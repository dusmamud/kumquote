Thoreau measures cost not in money but in life. Everything we buy, earn, or chase is paid for with hours — the only currency we cannot earn back. A cheaper price in money can still be expensive in time. Before taking on a new commitment, a new possession, or a new ambition, ask what portion of your life it will consume. If the exchange is fair, pay gladly. If not, walk away. Wealth is not having much; it is keeping enough of your life for the things that matter most. Spend your hours like they are precious, because they are.
