Carroll puts a comic truth into the mouth of the White Rabbit, and it holds up outside Wonderland. Hurrying feels like progress, but rushing usually creates the very delays it tries to escape: mistakes, forgotten keys, wrong turns, and the need to do things twice. The faster we push, the more the day slips out of order. Slowing down is not laziness; it is the shortest route to getting things right the first time. Sometimes the quickest way forward is to stop running and walk carefully instead.
