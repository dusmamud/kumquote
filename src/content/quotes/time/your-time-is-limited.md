Time is the one resource we can never refill, and spending it on a life chosen by others is the deepest waste. Other people's expectations, opinions, and plans can quietly become our own, until we wake up living a story we never chose. Jobs reminds us to listen to our own inner voice, even when it is quieter than the noise around us. That does not mean ignoring advice or responsibility. It means checking whether the path we are on reflects what we truly value.
