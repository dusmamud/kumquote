Sandburg turns time into money, but with a warning no bank can give: you hold only one coin, and you alone decide how it is spent. Nobody can save your hours for you, lend you theirs, or refund the ones you wasted. Every day hands us the same amount, and every day we choose, whether we notice or not. Spending well means matching hours to values: family, craft, health, learning. The coin is spent either way, wisely or carelessly. Choose deliberately, because the purse empties at the same rate for everyone.
