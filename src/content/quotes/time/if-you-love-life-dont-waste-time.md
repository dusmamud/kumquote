Bruce Lee connects two things we usually treat as separate: loving life and using time well. Time is not a container that life happens inside; it is the very material life is made of. Every wasted hour is not a pause in living but a piece of living spent on nothing. That is why the quote stings — it asks whether our days actually reflect what we say we love. Loving life, in Lee's sense, is not a feeling but a practice: choosing, again and again, to spend our hours on what matters instead of letting them slip away unnoticed.
