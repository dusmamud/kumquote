Theophrastus, successor to Aristotle, names time as our most precious possession, ahead of wealth or reputation. We guard our money carefully yet spend our hours casually, as if they were free — but time is the one thing that can never be replaced or refunded. Every choice about how we spend an hour is a choice about what our life is made of. Spend it on what matters, and the life becomes valuable; spend it carelessly, and the value leaks away unnoticed. Treating time as a treasure does not mean being anxious about every minute.
