Stowe collapses those walls. The past lives inside us as memory and habit, shaping every choice we make today. The future lives inside us too, as hope and fear, pulling our decisions in one direction or another. Only today is real, and it contains both. This is not a call to forget history or abandon planning. It is a reminder that the only moment we can ever act in is now. Yesterday's lessons and tomorrow's dreams are useful only insofar as they change what we do today.
