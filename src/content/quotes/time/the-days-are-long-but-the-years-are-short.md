A single day can feel endless, full of small chores, waiting, and tired stretches that seem to drag on forever. Yet when we look back, the years have slipped past almost unnoticed, and the seasons we thought would last forever have already gone. This strange double speed of life is why the ordinary days deserve more attention than we give them. The long afternoon is not an obstacle between us and real life; it is real life. Noticing it while it is happening, instead of only in hindsight, is how the short years become full ones.
