Cummings treats time as the great organiser, spreading events into a sequence instead of a pile. Without it, every moment would collide: birth and death, beginning and ending, all at the same instant, and nothing could be lived or understood. The quote makes the order of life feel like a gift rather than a limit. Because things happen one after another, we can choose, prepare, and recover. Respect the sequence: do not demand every result now, and do not grieve every loss at once. Time gives each thing its turn, and that spacing is what makes a life possible.
