Millman strips away the two favourite hiding places: later and elsewhere. The time is now, not when conditions improve; the place is here, not in some imagined setting where life will finally begin. Staying in the present is not passive. It means giving full attention to the task, the person, or the moment in front of us, instead of rehearsing the past or fearing the future. Life only ever happens in the present tense. Everything else is a story we tell about it.
