John Wooden, the most successful coach in college basketball, kept his wisdom simple: the clock is always running, so today is the only day you can play. Yesterday's wins and losses are already on the scoreboard, and tomorrow's game has not started. What matters is what you do with the hours in front of you — the practice, the effort, the kindness, the work. Make the most of today, because it is the only day that is ever truly yours to use.
