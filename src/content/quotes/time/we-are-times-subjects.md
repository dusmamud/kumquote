Shakespeare reminds us of a humbling truth: no one commands time. Kings and commoners alike must answer when time calls them onward. The line sounds dark, but it can also be freeing — because our days are limited, they matter more, not less. Knowing that time will one day bid us be gone should sharpen our choices, not sadden them: spend hours on what deserves them, speak the words that need saying, and do not postpone the life you want. We cannot rule time, but we can rule how we use the time we are given.
