Mother Teresa strips time down to the only day we truly own: today. Yesterday is finished, whatever it held, and tomorrow is a promise no one can guarantee. Only today can be acted on, loved in, and changed. The quote is a gentle rebuke to procrastination and regret alike — both are ways of living in days that do not exist. "Let us begin" is the heart of it: not a grand plan, but a small start, now. Whatever good you have been waiting to do, the time for it is not someday. It is today, and today is enough.
