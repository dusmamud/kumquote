Pericles reminds us that time settles questions no argument can. In the heat of the moment, we rush to judge people, decisions and events, certain that we already know how the story ends. But months and years reveal what anger and excitement hide: who was honest, which choice was wise, and what truly mattered. Time does not take sides; it simply lets consequences speak. The counsel it offers is patient and free of bias. When a decision feels urgent, it is often worth waiting and letting time do its quiet work.
