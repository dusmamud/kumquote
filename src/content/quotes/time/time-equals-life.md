Lakein reduces time management to an equation: time is life, so how we handle time is how we handle life itself. Wasting time is not a small fault; it is wasting the very material of existence. Mastering time, on the other hand, is not about cramming every minute with work — it is about choosing deliberately, so that our days reflect what we actually care about. The person who masters their time decides in advance what matters, protects it from interruption, and lets the rest go.
