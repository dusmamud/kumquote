Two places can sit a short distance apart on a map, yet feel impossibly far when the hours between them stretch on. Time creates distance that no road can shorten: the wait for news, the years between meetings, the slow drift of a friendship. We measure separation in miles, but we feel it in time. Understanding this changes how we treat absence, because every delay carries its own weight, and every reunion must cross not only space but all the time that passed in between.
