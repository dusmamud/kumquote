Buffett's investment wisdom applies far beyond business: time rewards quality and exposes mediocrity. A wonderful company — or a wonderful skill, habit, or relationship — grows stronger with the passing years, compounding quietly while no one watches. A mediocre one slowly falls apart, because time strips away the illusion that held it up. The question is not how much time we have, but whether what we are building is the kind of thing time will treat as a friend. Build what time will love, and time will do the rest.
