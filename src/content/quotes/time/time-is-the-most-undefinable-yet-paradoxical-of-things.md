Colton captures the strange nature of time: the moment we try to hold it, it has already moved on. The past exists only in memory, the future only in imagination, and the present vanishes even as we name it. Yet this slippery thing governs everything we do. The paradox is not a puzzle to solve but a reminder to stop analysing time and start living in it — because while we debate what time is, it is quietly passing, and it will not wait for our answer.
