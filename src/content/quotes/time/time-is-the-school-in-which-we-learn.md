Time teaches us through experience, but its lessons come at a price. Every year adds understanding — about ourselves, about others, about what truly matters — yet every year also takes something away. Schwartz captures this double nature: time is both a school and a fire. We cannot learn without passing through time, and we cannot pass through time without being changed by it. The wisdom is to attend the lessons while we can, because the fire does not pause. What time teaches, it teaches once; our task is to listen carefully and live accordingly.
