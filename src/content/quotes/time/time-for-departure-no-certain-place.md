Sometimes a job, a place, or a relationship has run its course, and staying only because the future is uncertain is its own kind of trap. Departure without a map is frightening, yet it is also honest: it admits that the present has nothing left to teach us. The road ahead may be unclear, but movement itself creates new possibilities. A departure is not an escape. It is a declaration that we trust life to meet us somewhere beyond the familiar.
