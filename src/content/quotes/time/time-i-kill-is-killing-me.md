Cooley turns a casual phrase into a warning. We say we are killing time as if time were the victim, but the truth runs the other way: every hour we waste is an hour of our life spent. Time does not wait politely while we scroll, delay, and drift; it passes, and with it goes the only currency we cannot earn back. The line is short because it needs no argument. Read it once, and the next idle hour feels different, as if each wasted minute were quietly taking something from us.
