Einstein wrote these words to console a grieving friend, and they carry both physics and comfort. In his view of the universe, past, present, and future exist together, and the sharp line we draw between them is a trick of our perspective rather than a fact of nature. The people and moments we have lost are not erased; they remain part of the whole. Time feels like a river carrying us away from what we love, but Einstein suggests the river is an illusion, and nothing truly loved ever leaves the fabric of things.
