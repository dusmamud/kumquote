Darwin, whose work required decades of patient observation, saw a wasted hour as a sign of a deeper misunderstanding. Someone who can throw away an hour lightly has not yet grasped that life itself is made of hours — to waste one is to waste a small piece of living. The word dares is telling: wasting time is an act of boldness against our own interests, and it suggests we believe we have time to spare. We do not. A single hour can hold a conversation, a lesson, a walk, or the beginning of a project.
