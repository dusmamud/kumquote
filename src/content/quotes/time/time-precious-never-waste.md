Few lines are as short, and few are as hard to argue with. Time is precious precisely because it cannot be bought back, stored up, or borrowed from tomorrow. Every hour we spend is an hour we have chosen to give to something, whether we chose consciously or not. The warning is not about working every minute; rest and joy are also good uses of time. It is about carelessness, about letting days drift away on things that do not matter to us. Used with attention, even ordinary time becomes precious.
