MacKay reminds us that time is the one resource handed out with perfect fairness. Rich and poor, young and old, famous and unknown, everyone receives the same twenty-four hours, and no one can buy, borrow, or store a single extra minute. What differs is not the gift but the use of it. The successful and the struggling stand before the same clock; the difference is what they do while it ticks. Equality of time is both a comfort and a challenge: nobody is cheated, and nobody is excused.
