Einstein's line is playful, but it carries a serious comfort. Time spreads life out so we can meet it one moment at a time, instead of facing everything in a single overwhelming instant. The past is behind us, the future has not arrived, and only the present asks anything of us right now. When life feels like too much, remember that time is doing its job — spacing things out so we can handle them. Take the moment in front of you; the rest will come when it is ready.
