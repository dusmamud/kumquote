Unlike a road, time has no signposts, borders, or lines drawn across it to show where one part ends and another begins. The hours flow into each other without pause, and only we decide that a new year, a birthday, or a Monday means a fresh start. Those markers are inventions, but useful ones: they give us a chance to stop, look back, and choose a direction. Change does not arrive on a schedule. It happens quietly, day by day, whether we mark it or not, and the only true divisions in time are the ones we notice.
