Seneca warns that waiting is itself a way of losing life. We postpone happiness until the promotion, the holiday, the perfect moment — and while we wait, the days pass anyway. Life does not pause for our preparations. The future we are waiting for arrives already spent, because we spent the present waiting for it. To live is not to wait for life to begin; it is to begin now, with what we have, where we are. The present is the only time we ever truly own. Spend it, don't save it for later.
