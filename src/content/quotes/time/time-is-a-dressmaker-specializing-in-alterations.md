Time quietly alters everything it touches, taking in here, letting out there, reshaping what we thought was finished. Our bodies change, our opinions soften, our priorities shift, and the fit of our old life no longer suits us. Like a good dressmaker, time does not ask permission; it simply works, stitch by stitch, year by year. Resisting the alterations only leaves us uncomfortable in clothes that no longer fit. The wise accept the fitting, letting time adjust them gracefully. A life well altered is not a life diminished, but one tailored, over decades, to fit who we have become.
