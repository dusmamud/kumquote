A schedule can serve us, or it can rule us, and the difference lies in who makes the decisions. When the clock governs, every hour is a demand, and we rush from one obligation to the next without asking whether any of them matter. Governing the clock means deciding in advance what deserves our time and protecting those hours from interruption. It is a small act of freedom: choosing the shape of a day instead of letting appointments choose it for us, and remembering that we own our time before anyone else does.
