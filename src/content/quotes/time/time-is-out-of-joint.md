Sometimes life feels wrong in a way that is hard to name: plans break, values shift, and the world seems out of step. Shakespeare gives that feeling four short words. To say the time is out of joint is to admit that something in the present is disordered, and that setting it right will take effort. Yet the line carries hope, because naming a problem is the first move toward fixing it. When our own time feels out of joint, the task is to notice it clearly and take the small, brave steps that bring things back into place.
