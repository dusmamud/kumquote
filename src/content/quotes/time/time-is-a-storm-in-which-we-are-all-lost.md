Time does not move gently or ask permission; it sweeps through our lives the way a storm sweeps across open ground, scattering plans and rearranging everything it touches. We shelter ourselves with routines, savings, and careful plans, yet none can hold back the years. To be lost in this storm is not a failure but the common condition of being human. Nobody finds a way out of time. What we can do is stand in it honestly, holding on to what we love while it lasts, and letting the storm remind us that every calm day is precious.
