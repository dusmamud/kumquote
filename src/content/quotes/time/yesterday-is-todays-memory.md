Gibran folds time into memory and dream. Yesterday lives on as today's memory; tomorrow exists already as today's dream. The past is not gone — it shapes how we see now. The future is not distant — it grows from what we hope for now. This means the present is the meeting place of all three: memory, experience, and hope. We cannot change yesterday, and we cannot control tomorrow, but we can choose what we remember and what we dream. Time is not a straight line; it is a circle we carry within us.
