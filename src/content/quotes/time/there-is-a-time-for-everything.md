Life moves in seasons, and each one has its proper work: a time to plant and a time to harvest, a time to speak and a time to listen, a time to grieve and a time to celebrate. Frustration often comes from demanding one season's fruit in another's time — hurrying a harvest or mourning a winter that has not yet passed. Patience is trusting the rhythm: what is not right today may be right in its season. Instead of fighting the calendar of your life, learn to read it. Do today's work today, and let each season bring its own gift.
