We rush to finish our stories, desperate for resolution, but time writes better endings than impatience ever could. Given enough time, misunderstandings soften, wounds close, and the true meaning of events becomes clear. What feels like a disaster today may reveal itself, years later, as the turning point that made everything work. Time is the best author because it has the one thing we lack: perspective. It knows how the chapters connect. Trusting time does not mean doing nothing; it means doing our part and letting the ending arrive when it is ready, not when we demand it.
