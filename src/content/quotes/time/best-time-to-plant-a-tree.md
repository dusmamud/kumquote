This old proverb answers the most common excuse for not starting: it is too late. Yes, the ideal moment may have passed twenty years ago — the best time to learn, to save, to build, or to begin. But that lost chance is not a reason to lose another twenty years regretting it. The second best time is now, and now is always available. The same is true of every worthwhile project. Stop mourning the perfect start, and take the good one that is standing right in front of you.
