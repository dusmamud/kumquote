Smiles lists the things we can recover and the one we cannot. Money can be earned again, knowledge can be relearned, and health can often be rebuilt with care. But lost time is gone forever, and no amount of wealth or effort buys it back. That makes time the most precious thing we own, even though we treat it as the cheapest. We guard our wallets carefully and give away our hours without thinking. Spend time like the irreplaceable currency it is: on people who matter, work that matters, and rest that restores.
