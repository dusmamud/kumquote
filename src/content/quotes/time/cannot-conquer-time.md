Time has a way of flattering us into believing we have beaten it. A good year, a strong body, a plan that worked, and we start to think we are in charge. This line warns against that deception. No one conquers time; at best we make peace with it and use it well while it lasts. The warning is not meant to frighten but to wake us up. If time cannot be conquered, then the wise move is not to fight it but to live deliberately inside it, choosing what matters before the hours run out.
