Every age complains that its own time is uniquely difficult, yet each era holds the same raw material: hours to use, work to do, and choices to make. Whether this time is good depends less on circumstance than on what we do with it, since a difficult hour used well beats a golden hour wasted. The past is gone and the future is unmade, so the present is the only time we can ever spend. Knowing what to do with it, acting with purpose now, is what turns any time into a very good one.
