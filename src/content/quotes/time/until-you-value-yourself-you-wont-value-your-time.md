People who give their hours away to every demand usually do not believe their own time is worth protecting. But time is the one currency you cannot earn back, and spending it carelessly is a quiet way of saying your life does not matter. Valuing yourself comes first: once you believe your goals and rest deserve respect, guarding your time follows naturally. Say no to what drains you, yes to what grows you, and watch how much more your days contain. Self-respect and time-respect are the same habit, practised one hour at a time.
