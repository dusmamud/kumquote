Kennedy refuses to treat tomorrow as something that simply arrives. A gift is given; an achievement is earned, and the future belongs to those willing to work for the shape they want it to take. Waiting for a better time is itself a decision, and it usually produces the future nobody chose. The present is the only material we have, and what we build with it becomes the future we inherit. If we want a different tomorrow, the work starts today, in the hours we already hold.
