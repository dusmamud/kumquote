Saint-Exupéry reveals why the things we tend matter most. The rose is not important because it is rare or beautiful; it is important because the prince watered it, sheltered it, and gave it his hours. Time spent is what creates value: the people and projects we invest in become precious precisely because they carry our days. Wasted here does not mean lost; it means given freely, without counting the return. Love is measured not in words but in time, and the hours we give are what make anything ours.
