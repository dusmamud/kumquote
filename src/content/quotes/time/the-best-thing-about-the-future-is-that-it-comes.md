Abraham Lincoln finds comfort in a simple fact we often forget: the future does not arrive all at once. It comes one day at a time, and that pace is a kindness. We cannot solve next year today, but we can handle today, and then tomorrow when it comes. This steady rhythm turns overwhelming plans into manageable steps and makes even the hardest seasons survivable. Lincoln's line is not naive optimism; it is a practical reminder that time itself does the heavy lifting if we let it. We only ever have to live one day, and that is always enough.
