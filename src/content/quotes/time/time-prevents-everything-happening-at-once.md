Wheeler's definition sounds like a joke, but it captures time's essential gift: sequence. Without time, everything — birth and death, effort and reward, cause and effect — would collapse into a single instant, and nothing could unfold or be understood. Time separates events so that stories can happen, growth can occur, and choices can matter. It is the space in which life takes shape. Remembering this reframes waiting and delay: they are not wasted time but the structure that lets meaning exist at all.
