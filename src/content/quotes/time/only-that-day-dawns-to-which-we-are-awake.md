Thoreau says only that day dawns to which we are awake. The sun rises for everyone, but a day only truly begins for those who notice it. Sleepwalking through life, distracted and hurried, we miss the dawn entirely. Being awake means paying attention: seeing the light, hearing the morning, feeling the day as it arrives. The quote makes awareness a daily practice. Each morning offers a fresh beginning, but only to those who are present enough to receive it. Wake up to the morning, and the day will wake up to you.
