Time mends what nothing else can: grief softens, anger cools, and wounds that once felt fatal become scars we can live with. That is its great healing power, and it asks only patience from us. But time is no beautician; it does not preserve youth or smooth away the years. It leaves its marks on every face and every body, honestly and without apology. The bargain is fair: inner healing in exchange for outer change. Those who accept it gracefully find that the lines time draws are not flaws but a record of a life fully lived.
