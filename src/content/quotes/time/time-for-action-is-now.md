Saint-Exupéry rejects the excuse of timing. We tell ourselves we will act when conditions improve, when we are ready, when the moment is right — and the moment never arrives. The time for action is now, not because conditions are perfect but because now is the only time we actually have. And it is never too late: beginnings can happen at any age, in any circumstance. The quote is a gentle push against delay, reminding us that the smallest step taken today outweighs the grand plan postponed until tomorrow.
