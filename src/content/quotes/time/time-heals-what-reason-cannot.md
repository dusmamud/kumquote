Seneca acknowledges the limits of logic: some wounds cannot be argued away. Reason can explain a loss, but it cannot make the pain leave; only time can do that, slowly and without our permission. This is not passivity but patience — the understanding that healing has its own pace, and that trying to rush it often makes things worse. When reason has said all it can say, the kindest thing we can do is give time its room to work.
