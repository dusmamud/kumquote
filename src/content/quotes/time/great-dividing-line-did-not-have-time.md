Field locates the difference between success and failure in a single excuse. "I did not have time" sounds like a fact about the world, but it is usually a confession about priorities. Successful people have the same hours as everyone else; they simply refuse to let the excuse stand. The five words mark the moment effort stopped, not the moment time ran out. Eliminating that sentence from our vocabulary does not create more hours, but it forces honesty about what we chose instead — and honesty is where change begins.
