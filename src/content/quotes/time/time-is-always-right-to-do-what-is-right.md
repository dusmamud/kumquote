There is never a perfect moment to do what is right. Waiting for easier conditions, better timing, or safer circumstances is often just fear dressed as patience. Justice, kindness, and honesty cannot be postponed until they are convenient, because the moment of decision is always now. The time to act is the time when the need is seen. Every day offers a chance to choose what is right over what is easy, and that choice is what defines a life.
