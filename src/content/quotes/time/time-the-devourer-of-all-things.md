Ovid gives time a fearsome name: the devourer of all things. Empires, beauty, youth, and memory all pass through its jaws eventually. The line sounds bleak, but it carries a useful urgency. If time consumes everything, then the things we love deserve our attention now, not later. Call the friend, write the book, take the trip, say the kind word. Nothing we postpone is safe from the devourer. The antidote is not denial but action: use time while we have it, and let what we create outlast us.
