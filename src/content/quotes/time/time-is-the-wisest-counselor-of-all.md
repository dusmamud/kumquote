Time has a way of settling questions that feel urgent today. What seems like a crisis in the morning often looks smaller a week later, and a decision that felt impossible becomes clearer once the noise fades. Patience is not the same as doing nothing; it is giving reality a chance to speak. When we rush, we decide from fear and incomplete information. When we wait wisely, patterns emerge and the right path often reveals itself. Trusting time means trusting that understanding grows, that feelings settle, and that the wisest answer is sometimes the one we have not hurried into.
