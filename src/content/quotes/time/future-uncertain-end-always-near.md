Morrison pairs two facts we prefer not to hold together: the future is uncertain, and the end is always near. Uncertainty tempts us to postpone, as if tomorrow were guaranteed; nearness warns us it is not. The tension creates urgency without panic — a reminder to live deliberately now rather than waiting for a clearer moment that may never come. The quote is dark but energizing: because the end is near, the present matters; because the future is uncertain, our choices today carry real weight. Live as if time were precious, because it is.
