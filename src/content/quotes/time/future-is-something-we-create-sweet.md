We do not enter the future; we build it with every decision, habit, and act of imagination in the present. Waiting for tomorrow to arrive is a misunderstanding, since tomorrow is being shaped right now by what we choose to do or neglect. This makes the present far more powerful than it feels: each day is raw material for a future that does not yet exist. The invitation is to stop asking what the future holds and start asking what we are making of it, because we are its authors, not its audience.
