Franklin's shortest famous saying equates time with money, and the comparison still stings. We would never throw coins into the street, yet we scatter hours on things we do not value — endless scrolling, pointless meetings, and tasks that lead nowhere. Franklin was a practical man, and his point is practical: treat your hours as a budget, spend them where they earn the most meaning, and audit them honestly. A life is, in the end, only the sum of its hours.
