Laughter is often treated as a break from real life, a pleasant distraction from serious work. This proverb reverses that judgment completely. Time spent laughing is not wasted time; it is among the most sacred uses of time there is. To laugh is to step briefly outside the weight of the everyday and touch something larger and lighter. The gods, in this image, are not distant judges but companions in joy. When we laugh, we are not escaping life. We are meeting it at its best.
