Khayyam's moving finger writes the record of our lives and moves on, never returning to erase a line. The past is fixed; no tears or pleading can undo what has been written. The verse is a warning against regret and a call to live carefully now, since each moment becomes permanent the instant it passes. It also frees us: what is done is done, and energy spent on rewriting the past is wasted. Write the next lines well instead. The finger keeps moving either way, so let the lines it writes next be ones worth keeping.
