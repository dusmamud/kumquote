Money can be earned again, people can be hired, and mistakes can often be corrected, but a spent hour never returns. That is why managing time comes before managing anything else. A leader who guards the calendar protects the work itself, because every project, meeting, and decision draws on the same limited supply of hours. The discipline is simple to state and hard to keep: spend time only on what truly matters, and say no to the rest. When time is treated as the precious resource it is, everything else begins to fall into order.
