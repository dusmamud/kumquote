Kennedy warns against using time as an excuse — the habit of saying "the time is not right" while years pass. Time is a tool, and like any tool it only works in the hands of someone willing to pick it up. Used as a crutch, it props up delay and indecision, letting us blame the calendar for our own reluctance. The difference is not in the clock but in us: whether we spend our days building something or leaning on them while waiting for a perfect moment that never arrives.
