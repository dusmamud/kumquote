Oliver refuses a life of mere visiting. To visit the world is to pass through it without touching anything — seeing sights, collecting days, but never really living in them. She wants more: to be immersed, to pay attention, to be astonished, to take part. A life only visited is a life half-lived. The world offers itself to us daily — in light, in weather, in the people we meet — but only if we stop and receive it. Do not be a tourist in your own life. Live here, fully, while you can.
