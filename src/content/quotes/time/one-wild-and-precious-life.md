Oliver asks the question that makes everything else quiet: what will you do with your one wild and precious life? There is only one, and it is already passing. Wild, because it cannot be fully tamed or planned; precious, because it cannot be replaced. The question is not a test with a right answer — it is an invitation to pay attention. What we do with our days is what we do with our lives. Answer honestly, and the answer will tell you what to change, what to keep, and what to begin today.
