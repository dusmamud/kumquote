Mackay lists the strange contradictions of time: it costs nothing, yet it is the most valuable thing we have. We cannot own it or keep it, only use it while it passes. This makes time unlike any other resource — there is no saving it for later, no buying more when we run out. The only choice is how to spend it now. Knowing this should change how we treat our hours: guard them from trivial demands, give them generously to what matters, and waste none on what we will not remember.
