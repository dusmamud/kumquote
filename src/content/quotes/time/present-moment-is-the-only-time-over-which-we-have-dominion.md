Thich Nhat Hanh draws a clear boundary around our power. The past is finished and cannot be revised; the future has not arrived and cannot be controlled. Only this moment answers to us, and even it slips away quickly if we spend it elsewhere. Dominion here does not mean domination but stewardship: we can choose what to do with now, how to breathe, how to speak, how to listen. A life is built one present moment at a time. Claim this one fully, and the next will follow from it.
