Money can be earned again, but time, once spent, is gone forever. Each of us holds the same daily allowance, yet we spend it so differently, and often carelessly, letting other people's demands decide our days. Sandburg's image of a coin makes the point concrete: every hour is a small fortune, and we are its only rightful owners. Guarding it means learning to say no, protecting the hours that matter, and spending them on what we truly value. No one else should hold the purse strings of our lives; the spending is ours to choose.
