Thoreau warns that killing time is not a harmless pastime. Time and eternity are connected: every hour wasted is a small piece of forever, lost. We speak of killing time as if it were an enemy, but time is the very material of our lives. To injure it is to injure ourselves. The hours we throw away — scrolling, waiting, complaining — never return. Eternity is not somewhere else; it is made of these very moments. Treat each hour as part of something endless, and it becomes harder to throw any of them away.
