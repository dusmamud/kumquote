Time passes whether we use it well or not, so the question is what each hour returns to us. Spending time treats it as something to get through, while investing time treats it as something that compounds: skills learned, relationships deepened, health protected and work that matters. An investment does not always pay off at once, which is why it is tempting to choose the easier spend. But small, steady deposits add up into a life that feels purposeful rather than busy. Before giving an hour away, it helps to ask what it will be worth a year from now.
