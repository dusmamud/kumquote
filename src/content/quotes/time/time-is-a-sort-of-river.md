Aurelius, a Roman emperor writing to himself, pictures time as a river carrying everything past us. Events arrive, rush by, and disappear downstream, and nothing stays still for long. The current is strong, and we cannot dam it or step into the same water twice. The wise response is not to fight the river but to stand in it attentively, doing good work while the moment lasts. What we build, how we treat people, and who we become are what the river leaves behind. Flow with it, and use each passing moment well.
