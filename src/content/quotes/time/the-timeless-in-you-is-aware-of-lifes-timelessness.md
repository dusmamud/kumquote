Beneath the rush of days and dates, there is a quieter part of us that does not feel bound by clocks at all. It is the part that remembers childhood as if it happened yesterday, and senses that love, beauty, and meaning do not expire the way appointments do. We are more than the number of years we have lived; something in us reaches beyond the calendar. Remembering this does not make daily life less important, but it frees us from fearing that time can take everything away. What is truly timeless in us remains, even as everything else changes.
