Time is the great finisher of what we begin and the great healer of what wounds us. Plans that seem stuck today may ripen tomorrow; grief that feels endless slowly softens; truths hidden now will surface in time. This is both a comfort and a warning: time brings good things to pass, but it also passes by while we wait. Use its patience wisely — plant the seeds, do the work, and let time do its part. Nothing stays frozen forever. Given enough time, everything changes, and change is where hope lives.
