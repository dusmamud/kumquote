Feeling guilty about wasted hours only wastes more of them. The clock does not rewind because we punished ourselves for yesterday's idleness; it simply keeps ticking while we regret. The moment you notice time slipping away is the moment to act, not to mourn. Start the task, make the call, open the book — one useful minute now is worth an hour of self-blame. Regret has a proper use: a brief warning to change course. Beyond that, it is just another way of wasting the very time you wish you had back.
