Tagore finds freedom in the butterfly's way of counting. Months are a human invention; moments are what life is actually made of. The butterfly does not worry about how long it has, only about how fully it lives each moment — drinking nectar, riding the wind, resting on a flower. We, by contrast, count years and fear their passing, while neglecting the moments inside them. Time enough is not a matter of quantity but of attention. A short life lived in full moments is richer than a long life spent waiting.
