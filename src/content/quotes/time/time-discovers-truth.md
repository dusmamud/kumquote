Seneca says time discovers truth, and experience proves him right. Falsehoods can shine briefly, but they cannot survive the years. Time tests every claim: rumours fade, fashions pass, and what is real remains standing. The quote warns against judging too quickly. A new idea or a new person deserves time before we decide their worth. Patience is the ally of truth. Wait, watch, and let time reveal what argument and excitement cannot. Truth does not hurry, and it does not need to; it has time on its side, while falsehood runs out of it.
