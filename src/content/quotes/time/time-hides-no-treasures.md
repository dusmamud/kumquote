Thoreau says time hides no treasures; we want not its then, but its now. The past holds nothing we can recover, and the future promises nothing we can grasp. Only the present moment is real and available. People who chase the past or wait for the future miss the treasure that is already here: this day, this hour, this breath. The quote is a call to attention. Stop digging in yesterday or tomorrow. The now is where life actually happens.
