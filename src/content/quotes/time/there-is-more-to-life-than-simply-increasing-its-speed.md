Modern life rewards speed. Faster answers, faster travel, faster results. Yet moving faster does not always mean living better. When everything accelerates, we lose the time to notice, to reflect, and to be present with the people around us. Gandhi reminds us that life is measured not by how quickly we move, but by how deeply we live. Slowing down is not laziness. It is a choice to give full attention to the meal, the conversation, the walk, and the work at hand.
