Benjamin Franklin asks a question that leaves no room for evasion: do you love life? If the answer is yes, then the logic follows — do not squander time, because time is the stuff life is made of. The old-fashioned wording only sharpens the point. We claim to love our lives while spending our hours carelessly, as if life and time were two different things. Franklin insists they are one and the same. Every hour spent well is life lived; every hour squandered is life thrown away. Loving life, then, is a daily practice measured in minutes.
