Colton points out a simple fact we constantly forget: the past is gone and the future is not ours, but the present belongs to us entirely. We spend our lives regretting what was and worrying about what might be, while the only time we can actually use slips past unnoticed. The present is not a waiting room for a better moment; it is the whole of our life, delivered one instant at a time. To own the present is to own everything we will ever truly have.
