Groucho Marx takes a familiar proverb and trips it up with wordplay, turning a solemn warning about time into a joke. The humour works because the first half is true — time really does fly, faster every year — and the second half reminds us not to take that truth too heavily. Life is short, but dread is not the only response; laughter is another. If time insists on flying past, we might as well enjoy the flight, and a little nonsense along the way is a fair price for the ride.
