Rohn states a simple fact that most people live as if it were false. We trade our time for money every day, yet we treat money as the scarcer resource, guarding it while giving hours away carelessly. Money can be earned again after it is lost; time cannot be recovered at any price. This does not mean money is unimportant, only that the exchange should be made with open eyes. Before spending hours on something, ask whether it is worth the time, not just whether it pays.
