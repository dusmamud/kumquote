Da Vinci, who produced an extraordinary body of work, insists that time is generous to those who use it. The excuse that there is never enough time usually means we have not decided what matters, so the hours scatter. But time does not run away from the person who is working; it stays, hour after hour, offering itself to anyone willing to begin. The key is use, not possession — time cannot be stored, only spent, and it rewards the spender who acts. Stop saying there is no time, and start using the time that is already here.
