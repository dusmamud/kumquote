Life is too short to spend on dull routine that serves no purpose. Chanel divides our hours into two worthy uses: work that creates something meaningful, and love that connects us to others. Everything else, the empty habits, the pointless worry, the days lived on autopilot, is time wasted. Monotony is not rest; it is life slipping by unnoticed. The challenge is to fill our days deliberately, choosing effort and affection over drift. When we look back, we will not miss the hours spent in routine, but we will treasure every hour spent working well and loving deeply.
