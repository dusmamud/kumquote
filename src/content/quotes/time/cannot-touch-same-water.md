A river never stands still, and neither does a life. The water that flows past your hand is gone in an instant, replaced by water that has never been there before. In the same way, no moment ever repeats itself exactly. The morning you wasted cannot be recovered, and the chance you missed will not return in the same form. This image teaches us to treat each day as fresh water rather than something we can store. We cannot step into the same moment twice, so we should meet each one while it is here.
