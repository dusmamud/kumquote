A second seems too small to matter, which is exactly why we waste them so freely. Goethe's claim sounds extravagant until we consider what a single second can hold: the word that changes a relationship, the decision that alters a life, the glance that becomes a memory. Treating each second as infinitely valuable does not mean living in constant urgency. It means living with attention, so that the small moments receive the respect they deserve. A life is, after all, only a collection of seconds.
