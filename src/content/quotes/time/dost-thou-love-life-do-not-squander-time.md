Life is not measured in years but in the moments we actually use. Every hour spent drifting is an hour of life spent, because time is the very material life is made of. To love life, then, is to guard your time: to spend it on people, work, and rest that matter, and to refuse the distractions that quietly consume it. This does not mean filling every minute with labour; rest and joy are also good uses of time. It means choosing deliberately. Love your life by loving your hours, and your years will take care of themselves.
