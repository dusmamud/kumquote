Jones gives time many jobs at once, and all of them are true. It flies when we are happy and marches on when we are waiting. It heals wounds we thought would never close, and it runs out just when we need a little more. It will tell, too: time exposes what was real and what was pretence, settling arguments that words cannot. We cannot control its pace, but we can respect its many roles. Time is not one thing but the stage on which everything else performs.
