The past can feel familiar because we carry its memories, but Hartley reminds us that it is a different world. People in other eras thought differently, valued different things, and lived by rules that now seem strange. Judging the past by today's standards misses the point; understanding it requires entering its own logic. This quote invites humility toward history — and toward our own past selves, who also did things differently. We cannot return to that country, and its customs no longer apply. All we can do is visit it through memory and learn from what we find there.
