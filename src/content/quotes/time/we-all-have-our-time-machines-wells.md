Wells, the inventor of the time machine in fiction, reminds us that we already own two of them. Memories carry us backward, letting us revisit childhood rooms, lost friends, and former selves with a vividness no machine could improve. Dreams carry us forward, sketching the futures we hope for and fear, and giving direction to the present. The past lives in memory and the future lives in dreaming, which means time travel is not a fantasy of science but a daily fact of being human.
