Almost everything lost in life can be recovered with effort. Money can be earned again, knowledge relearned, health often restored through care. Time alone admits no replacement; once an hour passes, no amount of work brings it back. This makes time the most precious thing we own, and the easiest to waste, because its loss is silent. Smiles does not mean to frighten us, but to sharpen our choices: spend time as if it were irreplaceable, because it is. The hours we give to what matters become a life well lived; the rest simply disappear.
