Time gives life its order: one thing after another, instead of everything crashing down at once. That spacing is a gift. It means we can face troubles one by one, enjoy pleasures one by one, and grow step by step. When we wish everything would happen now — success, answers, healing — we are really wishing away the very structure that makes life liveable. Be patient with the sequence. What you want may be on its way, just not all at once. Time's slow ordering is not a delay; it is how a life holds together.
