A file shapes metal slowly, shaving it away grain by grain without any sound or drama. Time works the same way on our lives. It does not announce itself with alarms; it quietly wears down what is weak, smooths what is rough, and changes us a little each day. We may not notice the change from one morning to the next, but over years the difference is unmistakable. The image warns us not to ignore slow processes. Small daily habits, like a silent file, are always shaping who we become.
