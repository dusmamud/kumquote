Most people drift through their days without feeling time's pressure, content to let hours pass uncounted. But those with a gift, or a calling, feel time as a spur: there is work to do, and the years are short. This urgency is not anxiety but purpose, the sense that something valuable must be made before the chance fades. Talent without this drive often stays dormant, while driven talent, even modest, achieves remarkable things. The lesson is not to fear time, but to let its passage focus us. A deadline, honestly faced, can be the kindest teacher of all.
