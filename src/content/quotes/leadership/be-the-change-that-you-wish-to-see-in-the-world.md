We often wait for others to change first: the government, the company, the family, the world. Gandhi turns that waiting upside down. Change begins with the person in the mirror. If we want honesty, we must be honest. If we want kindness, we must practise it. If we want peace, we must live peacefully, even when others do not. Leading by example is slower than giving speeches, but it is far more convincing. People watch what we do far more closely than what we say.
