Titles can be granted in a day, but influence is earned slowly, one interaction at a time. A person with a grand title and no influence leads no one, while a person with no title but deep trust can move an entire team. Leadership lives in the quiet moments: a word of encouragement, an honest conversation, an example set when no one is watching. It is one life touching another, and through that touch, changing its direction. Forget the flowchart; the real organization chart is drawn in relationships. Whoever influences others for good is a leader, whatever the title says.
