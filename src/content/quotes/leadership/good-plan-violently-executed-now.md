Patton values speed over perfection. A plan that is good enough, carried out with full energy today, beats a flawless plan that arrives after the moment has passed. Waiting for certainty often costs more than acting with incomplete information, because the world keeps moving while the planners deliberate. Violent execution here means wholehearted commitment, not cruelty: every resource thrown behind one clear decision. Leaders who move decisively shape events; those who wait for perfection are shaped by them. Perfection can wait; the moment cannot, and leaders who understand that difference win.
