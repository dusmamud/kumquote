Strategy matters — plans, tactics, and clear thinking win battles and build companies. But Schwarzkopf insists that character matters more. A leader without strategy can still earn trust, admit mistakes, and rally people around a shared purpose. A leader without character, however clever, will eventually lose the people who matter most, because no one follows someone they cannot trust. Character is what holds a team together when the plan falls apart. So build your strategy carefully, but guard your integrity even more carefully. In the long run, who you are decides where you can lead.
