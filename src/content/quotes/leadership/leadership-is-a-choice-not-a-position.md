Stephen Covey strips leadership down to its simplest truth: it is a choice, not a position. You do not need a title, an office, or permission to lead — you need the decision to take responsibility, to speak up, to help, to set an example. Positions are given; choices are made, every day, by anyone. This is liberating for people who think leadership belongs to someone else, and challenging for those who already hold a title, because a position without the choice to lead is empty. Leadership begins the moment we decide to act as if it is ours to do.
