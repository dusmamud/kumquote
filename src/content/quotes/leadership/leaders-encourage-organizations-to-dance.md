Bennis asks leaders to prepare their people for music that has not been heard yet. The future does not arrive on schedule, and the organisations that thrive are those ready to dance to new rhythms — new technologies, new markets, new ideas. A leader who only rehearses the old songs leaves the team unprepared. Encouragement matters here: people need permission to experiment, to learn unfamiliar steps, to stumble without punishment. Leadership is not about preserving the past. It is about building a team flexible enough to move gracefully into whatever comes next.
