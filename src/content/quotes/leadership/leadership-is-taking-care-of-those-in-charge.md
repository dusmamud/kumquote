Sinek draws a sharp line between authority and leadership. Being in charge is a position; taking care of people is a responsibility. A title gives you the right to make decisions, but it does not make you a leader — that comes from how you treat the people who trust you with their time, effort, and livelihoods. Leaders who protect their teams, listen to them, and put their welfare first earn loyalty that no org chart can command. Those who use authority only for themselves soon find they are in charge of no one. Leadership, in the end, is service.
