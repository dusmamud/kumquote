Godin reframes leadership as giving people a stage. Ideas that work deserve to spread, and leaders create the conditions for that spreading — the tools, the trust, the audience. A platform is not a position of power; it is a space where others can stand and be heard. The leader's role is to build that space and then step aside. When people have a platform, their best ideas reach further and their confidence grows. Leadership, in this view, is generous: it multiplies voices instead of hoarding the microphone for one.
