Nelson contrasts two kinds of motivation with a single image. Lighting a fire beneath people means pressure, threats, and urgency, which may produce effort but also fear and resentment, and the effort stops the moment the pressure lifts. Building a fire within them means igniting purpose, pride, and genuine enthusiasm, which burns on its own long after the leader has left the room. The best leaders do not drive people; they inspire them, connecting daily work to something meaningful. When people feel their work matters, they give their best freely. Inner fire outlasts outer force every time.
