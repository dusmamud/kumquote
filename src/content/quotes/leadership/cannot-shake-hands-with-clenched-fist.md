Gandhi reduces diplomacy to a single image: an open hand or a closed one, never both. A clenched fist is ready to fight, and no honest agreement can begin while one side is still armed with anger. Peace requires a gesture first, a deliberate loosening of the grip that says the other person is not an enemy. That gesture costs pride but buys possibility. Leaders who want reconciliation must show their hands are empty before asking anyone else to open theirs.
