Eisenhower puts integrity above every other leadership skill, and he does it deliberately. Talent, strategy, and charisma can all be learned or hired, but integrity is the foundation they stand on. Without it, success is an illusion: people may obey for a while, yet they will not trust, and trust is what turns a group into a team. His list of settings matters too — a railway gang, a football field, an army, an office — because the principle does not change with rank or industry. Wherever people follow someone, they watch whether that person's words and actions match.
