Sinek warns against the most common leadership trap: doing everything yourself because it is faster. A leader who solves every problem becomes the bottleneck, and the team never learns to stand on its own. Real leadership is the slower, harder work of coaching — helping people think, decide, and grow until they can succeed without you. The reward is a team that achieves things beyond what any one person could do, including beyond what they believed possible for themselves. That is how ordinary people achieve extraordinary things together.
