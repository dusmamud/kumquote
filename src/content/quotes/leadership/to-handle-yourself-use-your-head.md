Eleanor Roosevelt draws a line between managing ourselves and leading others. Handling yourself takes clear thinking: discipline, honesty, and the ability to master your own reactions. Handling others takes something different — the heart. People are not problems to be solved with logic alone; they need to feel understood, respected, and valued. A leader who uses only the head becomes cold; one who uses only the heart becomes sentimental. The art is knowing which tool each moment requires. Lead yourself with reason, and lead others with warmth.
