People do not need a leader who merely gives orders; they need someone whose calm presence steadies them when the stakes are high. When a team looks to its leader and sees steadiness instead of panic, their own fear shrinks and their focus returns. Confidence is contagious in both directions, and the leader sets the emotional tone for everyone else. This is why composure matters as much as strategy: the leader who stays sure under fire gives the whole team permission to believe, and belief is often what turns effort into victory.
