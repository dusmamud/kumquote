A boss pushes people from behind with orders and fear; a leader walks ahead and shows the way. The difference is direction and example. People follow a leader because they trust the path, not because they fear punishment. Driving gets short-term obedience, but it breeds resentment and kills initiative. Leading — sharing the work, the risk, and the credit — builds loyalty that lasts. If you manage people, ask yourself whether they follow you or merely obey you. The answer tells you whether you are leading or only driving.
