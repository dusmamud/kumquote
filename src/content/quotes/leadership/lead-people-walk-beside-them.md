Leading from the front can look impressive, but it can also leave people feeling driven rather than guided. Walking beside them is different. It means sharing the same ground, the same difficulties, and the same pace, so that followers know they are not alone. A leader who walks beside their people learns what the journey actually feels like, instead of guessing from a distance. This closeness builds a trust that orders and speeches cannot. People follow a leader they believe understands them.
