Firestone, who built a great company, placed people above profit as the purpose of leading. Results matter, but they are the by-product of people growing — gaining skill, confidence, and character. A leader who treats people as tools will get short-term output and long-term emptiness; a leader who develops people builds something that outlasts any single quarter. The highest calling is not to use people well, but to leave them better than you found them. Invest in people first, and the results will take care of themselves.
