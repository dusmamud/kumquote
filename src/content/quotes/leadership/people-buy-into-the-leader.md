Maxwell's point is simple and often missed: people follow people, not plans. A brilliant vision presented by someone nobody trusts goes nowhere, while a trusted leader can rally people around an ordinary idea and make it extraordinary. Trust is built slowly, through honesty, consistency, and genuine care, and it is spent quickly by broken promises. Before selling any vision, a leader must earn belief in themselves. That means showing up, keeping commitments, and admitting mistakes. Win the person first, and the vision follows naturally, carried by people who believe in the one who carries it.
