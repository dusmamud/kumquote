Everyone faces the same wind, but people respond to it in very different ways. The pessimist complains, spending energy on what cannot be controlled. The optimist waits, hoping conditions will improve on their own. The leader does neither. Leaders accept the wind as it is and adjust the sails, turning an unfavourable breeze into forward motion. This is the heart of leadership: working with reality instead of fighting it or wishing it away. Circumstances are rarely ideal, and waiting for perfect conditions usually means waiting forever.
