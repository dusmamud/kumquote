Kouzes argues that visions of the future fail when they ignore the present. A leader can paint a splendid tomorrow, but if people feel unseen, unheard, and unvalued today, they will not follow. Deep connection in the present, listening, understanding hopes and fears, showing genuine respect, builds the trust that makes the future believable. People follow leaders they believe care about them now, not leaders who only promise later rewards. The road to tomorrow therefore runs through today: meet people where they are, honor who they are, and the future becomes a journey they will gladly take with you.
