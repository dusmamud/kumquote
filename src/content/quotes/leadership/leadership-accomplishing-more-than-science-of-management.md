Powell draws a line between management and leadership. Management works within known limits, measuring what can be done with the resources at hand. Leadership begins where those limits end, asking people to reach past what the charts say is possible. That reaching is an art, not a formula: it depends on trust, purpose, and the belief that the team is capable of more. When people feel that belief, they often prove the numbers wrong, and the impossible becomes the record.
