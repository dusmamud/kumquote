Covey separates two skills that are often confused. Management is about climbing well: working efficiently, meeting deadlines, and improving each rung. Leadership asks a prior question that efficiency cannot answer: is this ladder leaning against the right wall at all? A team can climb brilliantly and still arrive somewhere worthless if the goal was wrong. The leader's work is therefore to pause, look up, and choose the destination before urging everyone to move faster. Speed without direction is only a quicker way to the wrong place, and true leadership begins by making sure the climb is worth making.
