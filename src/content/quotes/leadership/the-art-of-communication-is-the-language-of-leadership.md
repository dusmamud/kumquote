Humes puts communication at the centre of leadership, not as a soft skill but as the skill itself. A vision that cannot be explained cannot be followed; a decision that cannot be understood cannot be trusted. Leaders speak to give direction, to listen for what is unsaid, and to make people feel part of something larger than their own task. The art lies in clarity and honesty, not in fine phrases. When a leader communicates well, people know where they stand, where they are going, and why the journey matters.
