A vision held by one person is only a dream; shared by many, it becomes a movement. The greatest leaders do not impose their picture of the future, they gather people around it, letting each person see their own part in the story. When a team shares a vision, effort multiplies, because everyone pulls together. Mobilizing others is not about commanding harder, but about communicating clearly and listening well, until the vision belongs to everyone. A shared vision turns a group of individuals into a team, and a team into a force that can achieve what no one could alone.
