Lippmann measures leadership not by what happens while the leader is present, but by what remains after they are gone. A leader who depends on personal charisma alone leaves a vacuum when they depart. A true leader plants conviction in others, the belief that the work matters and the will to continue it. The final test is therefore a test of generosity. Did the leader build followers, or did they build other leaders? Only the second answer lasts beyond a single lifetime.
