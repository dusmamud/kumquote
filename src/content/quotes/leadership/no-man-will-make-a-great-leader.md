Carnegie knew that leaders who try to do everything themselves become the ceiling of their own organizations. The desire for total control and total credit feels like strength, but it is really fear wearing a suit. Great leadership means hiring people who are better than you in their fields, giving them real authority, and sharing the credit generously. A leader's job is not to be the hero of every story; it is to build a team that can win without constant supervision. Step back, trust others, and the work grows larger than any one person's hands could ever hold.
