People do not follow titles or job descriptions; they follow the example set by a leader's daily conduct. A leader who demands honesty but cuts corners, or preaches hard work while avoiding it, is judged by actions, not words. Standards are set quietly, through the choices made when no one is watching. The quality of a leader shows in the behaviour they model, because teams quickly become a reflection of the person at the top. Raise your own standards first, and the standards of everyone around you will rise too.
