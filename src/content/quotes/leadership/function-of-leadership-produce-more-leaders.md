A leader who only gathers followers builds something that depends on them, and it weakens the moment they step away. Real leadership multiplies itself by teaching others to think, decide and take responsibility. That means sharing knowledge instead of guarding it, trusting people with real work, and letting them learn from mistakes. A team full of leaders is stronger than a team waiting for orders, because initiative no longer sits with one person. The true measure of a leader is therefore not how many people follow them, but how many people grow capable of leading others in turn.
