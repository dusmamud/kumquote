Roosevelt understood that expertise alone never wins people over. A leader can be brilliant, experienced, and full of good ideas, but if the team doubts whether the leader cares about them, none of that knowledge matters. People listen with their hearts before their heads: they ask, "Does this person value me?" before they ask, "Is this person right?" Caring is not softness; it is the foundation of trust, and trust is what makes guidance welcome instead of resented. Show people you care first, and your knowledge will finally have an audience willing to hear it.
