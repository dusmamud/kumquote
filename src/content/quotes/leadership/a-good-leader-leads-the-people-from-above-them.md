Arnold contrasts two kinds of leadership. A good leader directs from above, giving orders and watching results. A great leader works from within, standing beside people, sharing their effort, and earning trust through presence. Leading from within means understanding the work firsthand and the people doing it. It builds loyalty that orders never can. The quote reminds leaders that position alone is not enough; the best influence comes from being close to the people you serve, not distant above them.
