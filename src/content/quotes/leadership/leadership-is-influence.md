Maxwell strips leadership down to a single word, and the simplicity is the point. Influence is not a title, a salary, or an office; it is the ability to change what other people think and do. A manager with authority but no influence is obeyed and ignored at the same time, while a person with influence needs no rank to move a room. Influence is earned daily through trust, example, and consistency, not granted by a promotion. If you want to lead, the question is not what position you hold, but whom you move and in which direction.
