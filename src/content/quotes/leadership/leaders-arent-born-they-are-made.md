Lombardi, one of the most successful coaches in American football, refused the myth of the born leader. Leadership, in his view, is a craft: it is learned through practice, failure, and persistent effort, just like any other skill. This is good news for anyone who doubts themselves, because it means leadership is not reserved for the naturally confident or charismatic. It is built in the daily work of listening, deciding, taking responsibility, and learning from mistakes. Hard work is not glamorous, but it is reliable. Anyone willing to do it, year after year, can become the leader their team needs.
