Simon Sinek draws a line between authority and leadership that many people never see. Being in charge is a position: it comes with a title, a budget, and the power to decide. Taking care of those in your charge is a responsibility: it means the people who follow you feel safe, supported, and valued. The first can be granted by an organization; the second must be earned by leaders who put their people first. When leaders take care of their teams, the teams take care of the mission — and that is the exchange on which all lasting leadership rests.
