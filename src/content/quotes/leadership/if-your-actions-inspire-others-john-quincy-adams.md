This definition quietly fires most managers. Leadership, here, is
not a title, a team, or a loud voice. It is an effect: after you
act, do the people around you dream bigger, learn faster, and try
harder — or do they shrink, wait for orders, and play safe? The
answer is the test, and it works on parents, teachers, and friends
just as well as on bosses.

It also makes leadership available today, without permission. You
do not need a promotion to inspire one person. Do the work well,
share what you learn, and encourage someone else's attempt instead
of correcting it. If they leave the conversation braver than they
arrived, you led — whether or not anyone ever called you a leader.
