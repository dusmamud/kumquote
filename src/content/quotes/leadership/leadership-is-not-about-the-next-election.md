Leaders who think only about the next election, quarter, or headline always choose the move that looks good now, even when it costs the future dearly. Real leadership plays a longer game, asking what those who come after us will inherit from today's choices. That changes everything: patience over quick wins, investment over extraction, trust over applause. The next generation cannot vote, complain, or undo what we break, which is why they deserve our most careful thought. Leadership measured in decades builds things that last, and the leaders worth remembering planted trees they knew they would never sit under.
