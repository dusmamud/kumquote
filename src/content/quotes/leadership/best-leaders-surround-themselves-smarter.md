Insecure leaders hire people who will not threaten them, and the whole organization pays the price in weak thinking. Great leaders do the opposite: they seek out talent brighter than their own, because they know the mission is bigger than their ego. Surrounding yourself with smart people is not a confession of weakness; it is the wisest strategy available. A strong team challenges assumptions, catches blind spots, and carries the work further than any single person could. The leader's real job is not to be the smartest in the room, but to build the room where the smartest can thrive.
