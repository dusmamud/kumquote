Tracy strips leadership down to its truest test: would people follow you if they did not have to? A title can compel obedience, but it cannot create loyalty, enthusiasm, or trust; those are earned by character, competence, and care for others. Voluntary followers choose a leader because they believe in the person and the direction, not because an org chart says so. The challenge is to build yourself into someone worth following, so that your influence rests on who you are rather than on the position you happen to hold.
