Blame is easy and useless: it points backwards, shames people, and fixes nothing. A leader's job is to look forward and find the remedy — the change in process, the missing resource, the honest conversation that prevents the problem from repeating. Teams that hunt for faults stop taking risks, while teams that hunt for remedies keep improving. The next time something goes wrong, resist the urge to ask whose fault it was and ask instead what will fix it. Remedies build trust and progress; fault-finding builds only fear. Choose the question that moves things forward.
