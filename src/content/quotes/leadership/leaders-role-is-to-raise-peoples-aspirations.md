Most people aim lower than they could, not from laziness but from never having been asked to reach higher. A leader's role is to lift that ceiling, to show people a version of themselves they had not imagined. But raising aspirations is only half the work; the leader must also release the energy to pursue them, removing obstacles, granting trust, and celebrating progress. When people feel both inspired and free, they surprise even themselves. Leadership, at its best, is this double gift: a bigger dream, and the permission and power to chase it.
