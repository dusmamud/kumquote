Indra Nooyi admits what many leaders quietly know: leadership resists a tidy definition, and good leadership is rarer still. Titles, budgets, and org charts are not the measure; the test is whether people will follow you somewhere difficult, simply because they trust you. That kind of loyalty is not bought with perks or demanded by authority — it is earned through consistency, care, and a vision people believe in. Her standard is deliberately extreme, to the ends of the earth, because it strips away every shortcut and leaves only the one thing that counts: willing followers.
