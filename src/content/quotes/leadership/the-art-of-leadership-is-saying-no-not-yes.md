Blair reveals that leadership is mostly refusal. Saying yes is easy and popular; every request, idea, and demand sounds reasonable in the moment. But a leader who says yes to everything scatters focus and exhausts the team. Saying no protects priorities, time, and energy for what truly matters. It takes courage, because refusal disappoints people. Yet the organisations that achieve most are those whose leaders guard their focus fiercely. Leadership is deciding what not to do, and holding that line.
