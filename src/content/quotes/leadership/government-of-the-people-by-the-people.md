Lincoln defines democracy in three short phrases: it belongs to the people, it is run by the people, and it exists for the people. Remove any one of those and the whole structure falls. The promise at the end is a hope and a warning: such a government can endure, but only if the people keep it alive. His words turn citizenship into a responsibility, because a government of the people perishes the moment the people stop tending it. The line endures because it states the whole idea in a single breath.
