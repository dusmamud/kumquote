Jobs draws the line between leaders and followers at innovation. A follower copies what already exists; a leader imagines something new and takes the risk of building it. Innovation does not always mean inventing a product — it can mean a better process, a braver idea, or a refusal to accept the way things have always been done. Jobs built his career on that risk. Leaders, in this view, are simply the people willing to try something untested and to keep going until it works.
