Blanchard draws the line that separates modern leaders from mere bosses. Authority can compel obedience, but it cannot win commitment; people do what they must under authority and no more. Influence, built on trust, competence, and genuine care, earns willing effort — the kind that shows up early, stays late, and solves problems without being asked. In a world where talent can leave, authority is a weak currency. Influence is the only leadership that truly lasts. Earn trust daily, and people will follow you because they want to, not because they must.
