Power can force compliance, but it cannot create greatness, because people obey power only while they must. Empowerment works differently: a leader who shares authority, credit, and opportunity grows people who can act without being pushed. Those people, in turn, carry the mission further than any single leader could alone. Maxwell's measure of a leader is therefore not how much power they hold, but how much power they give away wisely. Great leaders leave behind stronger teams, braver decisions, and new leaders. Their legacy is measured in the people they lifted, not the control they kept.
