A good leader can win trust and rally people behind a cause, and that is no small achievement. But a great leader goes further, shifting the confidence from themselves to their people. When followers believe in themselves, they stop waiting for instructions and start solving problems, taking risks, and leading others in turn. This is leadership that multiplies: it creates not dependents but new leaders. The greatest compliment a leader can receive is a team that no longer needs them. True greatness lies not in being followed, but in helping others discover they are capable of leading themselves.
