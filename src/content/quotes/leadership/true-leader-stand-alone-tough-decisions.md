MacArthur joins three qualities that rarely sit together: confidence, courage, and compassion. Standing alone takes confidence when the crowd disagrees. Tough decisions take courage when every option costs something. Listening takes compassion when the leader already knows the answer. Each quality checks the others — confidence without compassion becomes arrogance, courage without listening becomes recklessness, and compassion without courage becomes indecision. True leadership is not one strength but the balance of all three, held together under pressure. Leaders who master this balance earn trust precisely because people see all three qualities working at once.
