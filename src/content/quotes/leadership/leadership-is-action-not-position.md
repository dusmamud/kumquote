McGannon compresses leadership to its essence: it is what you do, not what you are called. A title can sit on a door while its owner leads nothing; meanwhile, someone with no title at all can lead a team through a crisis by acting first and acting well. Waiting for a promotion before leading is a misunderstanding of the job. Leadership begins the moment you take responsibility and act — the position, if it comes, only catches up later. Start leading today, wherever you stand, and the title will follow.
