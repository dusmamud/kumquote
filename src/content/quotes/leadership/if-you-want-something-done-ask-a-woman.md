Thatcher draws a sharp line between talking and doing. Saying is easy and often ends with the saying itself, while doing requires follow-through, patience, and the willingness to be judged by results. Her line praises the habit of finishing things, a quality she associated with the women around her, who carried the work while others debated it. The point is not about men or women in general, but about valuing action over announcement. In the end, what matters is who delivers, not who speaks first.
