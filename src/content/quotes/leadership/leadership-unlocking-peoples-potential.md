A leader's real work is not giving orders or taking credit, but helping people grow beyond what they thought possible. When a leader removes obstacles, shares knowledge, and trusts others with real responsibility, the whole group becomes stronger. Potential is often hidden by fear or lack of opportunity, and unlocking it requires patience and belief. The measure of leadership is not the leader's own achievements, but the achievements of the people they helped become better. That is the truest test of a leader's worth.
