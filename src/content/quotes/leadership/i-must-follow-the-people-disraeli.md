A leader who marches too far ahead of the people ends up alone, commanding no one, while a leader who listens and follows the people's will keeps their trust and their company. Leadership is not dragging followers toward a private vision but reading the currents of their hopes and giving them shape and direction. The art lies in the balance: to lead without losing touch, and to guide without abandoning those you serve. The best leaders walk with their people, not merely in front of them, and earn the right to lead by staying close.
