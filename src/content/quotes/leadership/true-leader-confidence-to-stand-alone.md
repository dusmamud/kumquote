MacArthur describes a leader who is both strong and soft in the right places. Standing alone takes confidence, because popular decisions are easy and lonely ones are not. Tough decisions take courage, because someone will always be unhappy. And listening takes compassion, because people need to feel heard before they will follow. Most leaders master one of these; the great ones hold all three together. Strength without compassion becomes tyranny, and compassion without strength becomes drift. The complete leader stands firm, decides honestly, and still has the humility to listen before acting.
