Patton, a general known for bold action, valued flexibility over rigid rule-following. Principles matter: they give a leader a compass and keep decisions honest. But circumstances change, and a principle applied blindly can become a liability. The skilled leader understands the spirit behind the rule and knows when the situation demands a different approach. This is not the same as abandoning values whenever it is convenient; it is judgment, the ability to tell the difference between a principle worth defending and a habit worth breaking. Leadership is knowing what to hold firm and what to bend.
