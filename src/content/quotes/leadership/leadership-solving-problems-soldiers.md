Powell reframes complaints as a sign of trust. When people bring you their problems, they believe you can help and that you care — that is leadership working. The dangerous day is not the one filled with problems but the quiet one when people stop coming, because they have concluded that speaking up changes nothing. Silence is not success; it is disengagement. A leader's job is to stay approachable enough that problems keep arriving, and to solve them visibly enough that people keep believing it is worth bringing the next one.
