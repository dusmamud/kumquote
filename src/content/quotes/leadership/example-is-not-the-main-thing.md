People follow what they see far more than what they hear. A leader can give speeches about honesty, hard work, or kindness, but if daily behaviour contradicts those words, the words lose their power. Example is the only argument that cannot be ignored, because it is lived in full view of others. Schweitzer reminds us that influence is earned through conduct, not claimed through titles or slogans. The standard a leader sets with their own actions becomes the standard everyone else follows. To lead well, first live well — the example does the teaching.
