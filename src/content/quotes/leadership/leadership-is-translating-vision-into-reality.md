Bennis captures the difference between dreaming and leading in a single sentence. Vision alone is cheap — almost everyone can imagine a better future. The leader's real work is translation: turning that picture into plans, decisions, and daily actions that other people can follow. It requires patience with detail, courage to make hard choices, and the ability to keep a team moving when the vision feels far away. Without translation, a vision stays a speech. With it, ordinary effort becomes real change. Leadership, then, is less about having the vision than about doing the unglamorous work of making it real.
