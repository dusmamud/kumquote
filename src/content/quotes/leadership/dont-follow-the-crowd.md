Thatcher champions the courage to stand apart. Following the crowd feels safe, because the crowd absorbs blame and offers easy approval. But crowds drift toward comfort, not toward what is right or necessary. A leader's job is to see the better path, walk it first, and let others decide to follow. That requires conviction, thick skin, and patience, because the crowd often mocks the first steps. Yet history remembers the ones who led, not the ones who merely followed. Walk your own road with integrity, and the crowd that matters will eventually walk with you.
