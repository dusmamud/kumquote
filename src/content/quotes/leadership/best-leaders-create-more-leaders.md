Peters measures leadership by multiplication, not by headcount. A leader who collects followers builds dependence: everything waits for their decision, and the organisation stalls whenever they are away. A leader who develops other leaders builds capacity that outlasts them, because each person they train goes on to train others. This requires generosity, since it means sharing credit, giving away the interesting work, and accepting that others may surpass you. The legacy of a great leader is not a loyal crowd but a room full of people ready to lead.
