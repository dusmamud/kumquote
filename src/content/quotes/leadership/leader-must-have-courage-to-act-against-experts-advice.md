Experts are valuable, but their advice is not a command. A leader who always follows the experts abdicates the very responsibility that defines leadership: the final decision. Sometimes the data points one way while judgment, values, or a longer view point another, and the leader must have the courage to choose. This is lonely work, because if the decision fails, the blame is theirs alone. Yet leadership exists precisely for these moments, when no expert can decide for you. Listening carefully, then deciding independently, is the mark of a leader who truly leads.
