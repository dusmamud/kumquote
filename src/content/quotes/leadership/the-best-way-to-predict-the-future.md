Drucker, the father of modern management, rejects the idea that the future is something that simply happens to us. Predictions are guesses, and waiting for certainty is a way of giving up control. The alternative is to act: to build the product, start the project, or make the decision that shapes what comes next. Leaders do not sit around forecasting trends; they create them through deliberate work. This is both empowering and demanding, because it makes us responsible for the future we get. If you want to know what tomorrow holds, start building it today.
