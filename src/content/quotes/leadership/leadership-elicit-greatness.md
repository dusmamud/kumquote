Most leadership advice assumes people are empty vessels waiting to be filled with skill, motivation, or vision. Buchan reverses the assumption entirely. Greatness, he argues, is already present in ordinary people; it simply needs to be drawn out. That changes how a leader treats people. Instead of asking what is wrong with them, the leader asks what is waiting inside them. This view is both humbling and hopeful. It means leadership is less about the leader's brilliance and more about their ability to recognise the quiet greatness in others.
