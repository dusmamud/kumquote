Maxwell's line is a reminder that leadership is never a side issue — it is the factor that decides whether everything else works. A brilliant strategy fails under weak leadership, while an ordinary plan can succeed when someone leads with clarity, honesty, and care. When leadership rises, morale rises, decisions improve, and people grow. When it falls, trust erodes and even the best ideas stall. That is why the most important work a leader does is not managing tasks but growing themselves and growing others, because every result in the organisation traces back to the quality of its leadership.
