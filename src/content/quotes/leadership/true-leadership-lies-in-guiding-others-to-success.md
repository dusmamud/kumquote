Owens defines leadership as a service, not a spotlight. The leader's job is to guide: to make sure each person knows what they pledged to do, has what they need to do it, and is performing at their best. That means paying attention to people, not just results, and removing obstacles before they become excuses. Success here is collective; the leader wins only when the team wins. Guiding others well takes patience and humility, because the credit belongs to those doing the work while the leader's satisfaction comes from watching them succeed.
