Blair points out that saying yes costs nothing in the moment: it pleases everyone, avoids conflict, and postpones the hard choice. Saying no is where leadership actually lives. Every yes to a distraction is a no to the priority, and a leader who cannot refuse ends up leading nothing in particular. The word no protects time, focus, and standards; it tells a team what matters most. Used well, it is not rejection but direction. The art is knowing which requests deserve it and having the nerve to say it clearly.
