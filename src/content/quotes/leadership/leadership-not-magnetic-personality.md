Drucker strips leadership of glamour. A magnetic personality can impress a room, and smooth talk can win a round of applause, but neither guarantees that people are led anywhere worth going. Real leadership rests on results, trust, and the discipline to do the work that others avoid. Charm without substance eventually disappoints, while quiet competence earns loyalty over time. The quote is a warning against choosing leaders for style instead of character and judgment. Look past the performance, and ask what the person actually builds, decides, and stands for when nobody is watching.
