Covey jokes that most organisations are busy cutting through the wrong jungle, faster every year. Workers chop efficiently, managers sharpen axes, and everyone is proud of the progress, yet nobody has checked whether the destination is right. The leader's job is different: climb above the daily noise, look at the whole landscape, and say the unpopular thing when the direction is wrong. It takes courage, because the crew would rather keep cutting. But speed in the wrong direction is not progress, and only the leader can see that.
