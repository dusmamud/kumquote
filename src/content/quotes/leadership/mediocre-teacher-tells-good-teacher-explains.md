Ward ranks four ways of leading people, and the ranking applies far beyond the classroom. Telling passes on information; explaining adds understanding; demonstrating shows how it is done. But inspiring does something the others cannot: it lights a fire in the learner that keeps burning after the lesson ends. The great teacher, like the great leader, does not just transfer knowledge but awakens ambition. People forget what they were told and even what they were shown, but they rarely forget the person who made them believe they could do more.
