Leadership is not a throne above the team; it is a role within it. A person who has never followed well does not understand what followers need: clear direction, honest feedback, and respect for their work. Knowing how to follow teaches patience, loyalty, and the discipline of putting the goal ahead of ego — the very qualities a leader must model. The best leaders still follow others when someone else knows more, and their teams trust them for it. If you want people to follow you, first show them you know how to follow.
