Eimes describes leadership as a kind of vision that works in three directions at once. Seeing more than others means noticing what is hidden: the quiet contributor, the coming problem, the unspoken fear. Seeing farther means looking past the next quarter toward the horizon others cannot yet picture. And seeing before means acting early, while there is still time to prepare. Most people manage one of these; leaders practice all three. Vision is not magic or prophecy. It is attention, imagination, and courage combined, and it can be trained by anyone willing to look longer and think deeper.
