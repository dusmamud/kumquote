Firestone puts people development at the very top of leadership's work. Products, profits, and processes all matter, but they follow from people who are growing. A leader who invests in others, through mentoring, training, honest feedback, and real opportunity, builds something that outlasts any single quarter. The highest calling is not hitting targets but helping people become more capable, confident, and fulfilled than when they arrived. Organizations that treat people as costs to manage stay small; those that treat people as lives to develop grow in ways spreadsheets cannot predict.
