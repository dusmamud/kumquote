Too many people think leadership means giving orders and watching others work. Townsend turns that idea upside down. A real leader serves the team, removing obstacles and handling unglamorous work so others can focus on what they do best. Carrying water is not a small job; it is the job. When a leader puts the team's needs first, people feel trusted and give their best in return. Authority that only commands breeds resentment; leadership built on service earns loyalty. A leader's success is measured not by power held, but by how well their people can do their work.
