It is tempting to believe leaders are born with some rare gift that the rest of us lack. Lombardi rejects that excuse. Leadership is made through hard work, study, practice, and the willingness to learn from failure. It is built in small moments: keeping a promise, listening when it is easier to speak, admitting a mistake, and staying steady under pressure. No one arrives fully formed as a leader. People grow into it by choosing responsibility again and again. That is good news for everyone. It means leadership is not reserved for a chosen few.
