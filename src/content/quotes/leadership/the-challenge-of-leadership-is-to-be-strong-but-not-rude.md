Jim Rohn's challenge captures the balance at the heart of leading people. Every strength a leader needs has a shadow side: strength can harden into rudeness, kindness can slide into weakness, boldness can become bullying. The art is holding both sides, pairing confidence with humility and pride with humor. Rohn is not asking leaders to be less, but to be more precise about how they show up. Authority does not come from one extreme or the other; it comes from the discipline of staying in the middle, where people feel both guided and respected, and where trust can grow.
