MacArthur reduces leadership to a single discipline: wisdom in the use of power. Power itself is neutral; it can build or destroy, protect or oppress, and the difference lies entirely in how it is used. A wise leader spends power sparingly, in service of a purpose larger than themselves, and knows when restraint is stronger than force. The unwise spend it to prove they have it, and soon have nothing left but fear. Leadership is not collecting power but stewarding it, so that it leaves people stronger than it found them.
