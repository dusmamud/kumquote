Anyone can lead people where they already want to go. Real leadership begins where comfort ends, guiding people toward a place they would never reach alone. This requires trust, because followers must believe the destination is worth the discomfort and the guide is worth following. A leader earns that trust through honesty, competence, and care, not through titles or orders. The journey may be difficult, but people follow willingly when they sense a purpose larger than themselves. That is the test of leadership: not how many follow, but how far they are willing to go together.
