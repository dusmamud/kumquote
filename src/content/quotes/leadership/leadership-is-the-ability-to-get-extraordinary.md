Great leaders do not need a team of geniuses; they need the skill to bring out the best in ordinary people. Tracy reminds us that leadership is not about hiring stars but about creating conditions where regular people do remarkable work: clear goals, honest encouragement, and trust. When people feel believed in, they stretch beyond what they thought possible. A leader's real achievement is measured not by personal brilliance but by the growth of the team. Look at your people not for what they are today, but for what they could become with your guidance.
