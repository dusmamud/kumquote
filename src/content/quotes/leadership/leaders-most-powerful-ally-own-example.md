Wooden says a leader's example does more than any speech or rule. People watch what a leader does far more closely than what he says, and they copy the conduct they see every day. A leader who is punctual, prepared, and honest sets a standard that needs no enforcement, because the team absorbs it. The example is an ally because it works even when the leader is silent, shaping behavior in every room he enters. To lead well, then, is first to live the standard yourself.
