Charm can win a room, but it cannot build a team. A glib speaker may impress for an hour, yet people soon judge a leader by what changes in their own lives. Real leadership lifts others: it shows them a goal worth reaching, demands more than they thought they could give, and helps them grow beyond their old limits. This work is quieter than charisma and far more lasting. Anyone can practice it, because it depends on service, not sparkle. The measure of a leader is not applause, but people who leave stronger and more capable than they arrived.
