Bezos turns team size into a simple test you can picture: if two pizzas cannot feed the group, the group is too big to work well. Small teams move faster because everyone knows the goal, speaks plainly, and can decide without layers of approval. Large teams drift into meetings, confusion, and slow compromise, and the best ideas get lost on the way. Keeping a team small is not about saving money; it is about protecting focus, ownership, and speed. A few committed people, well fed and well led, achieve more.
