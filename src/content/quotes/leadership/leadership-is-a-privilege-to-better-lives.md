Kibaki defines leadership as service, not reward. Power can easily become a tool for personal gain, status, and comfort, but that betrays its purpose. A leader is entrusted with influence so that other people's lives can improve, whether that means fairer decisions, better opportunities, or simply being heard. Treating leadership as a privilege keeps a leader humble and accountable, because a privilege can be withdrawn when it is misused. The test of any leader is simple: are the people they serve better off because of them? If the answer is no, the position has been wasted.
