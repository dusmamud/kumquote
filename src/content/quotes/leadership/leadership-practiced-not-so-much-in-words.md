Geneen warns that speeches are cheap and people know it. Teams do not watch what a leader says in a meeting; they watch what the leader does when things go wrong, who gets credit, and whether the stated values survive pressure. Attitude is the daily evidence: calm under stress, fairness in small decisions, and respect shown when nobody important is watching. Words can announce a culture, but only actions build one. A leader's real message is written in behaviour, repeated every day, and read far more carefully than any memo.
