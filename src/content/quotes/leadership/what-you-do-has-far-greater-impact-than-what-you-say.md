Covey reminds leaders that people watch behaviour far more closely than they listen to speeches. A leader can talk about honesty, hard work, or respect, but if their actions tell a different story, the actions are what the team believes. Words are cheap and quickly forgotten; conduct is remembered, copied, and passed on through a whole organisation. This is both a warning and an opportunity: every ordinary choice, kept promise, and calm response under pressure is a message. Lead by example, because example is the message.
