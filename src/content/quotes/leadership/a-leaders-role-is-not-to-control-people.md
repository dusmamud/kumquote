Welch rejects the image of the leader as controller. Staying on top of things through supervision and pressure produces compliance, not commitment. Real leadership guides, energises, and excites people, giving them reasons to care about the work. Energy is contagious: a leader who believes in the mission can pass that belief on. The role is to remove obstacles, spark enthusiasm, and help people do their best work. People who feel excited about their work bring more of themselves to it, and that extra effort is where great results come from. Control manages the present; excitement builds the future.
