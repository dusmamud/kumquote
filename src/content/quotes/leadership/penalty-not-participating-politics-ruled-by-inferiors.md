Plato warns that refusing public life does not free a person from government; it only hands power to those who wanted it most. The capable often step aside because politics seems dirty or beneath them, and the vacancy is filled by the ambitious and the unfit. The penalty is not a fine but a government you did not choose, making choices on your behalf. Participation is therefore a duty, not a hobby: the price of staying out is being ruled by someone worse.
