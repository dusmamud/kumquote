Maxwell reminds us that titles do not make leaders brave; passion does. A position can give someone authority, but it cannot give them the courage to stand by a vision when it is unpopular, costly, or slow to arrive. Passion supplies that courage, because the leader cares more about the vision than about comfort or approval. People follow that kind of leader not because they must, but because they can feel the fire. Passion, not rank, is the true source of a leader's nerve.
