Lee says honourable leadership starts inside. A leader who exercises power with integrity begins by governing himself: his motives, his honesty, and his consistency. Power used from the outside in, through force or manipulation, corrupts both leader and followers. But power that flows from inner character earns willing trust. People follow someone they believe in, not someone they fear. The quote reminds leaders that authority is only as good as the person holding it, and self-mastery comes first. The work of leadership, then, begins with self-examination before it ever touches anyone else.
