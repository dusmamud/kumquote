Goethe observes that great people are magnets for other great people — talent recognizes talent, and character attracts character. But attraction alone is not leadership; the second half matters just as much. Knowing how to hold great people together requires humility, because talented people have strong wills and different visions. The leader's task is to make the whole greater than the sum of its strong parts, giving each person room while keeping everyone aimed at a shared purpose. Greatness in leadership is measured by the company it keeps — and keeps.
