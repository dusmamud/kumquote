Authority can force obedience, but only inspiration creates willing effort. A leader's real work is to connect people to a purpose larger than themselves, so that struggle feels meaningful rather than imposed. When people choose the shared goal as their own, they bring creativity, courage, and persistence no order could ever command. That is why great leaders spend less time giving commands and more time sharing vision, listening, and building trust. That shared struggle turns a group into a team. Mobilise hearts first, and hands will follow willingly.
