Barker defines leadership by trust rather than by title. Anyone can order people to go somewhere safe and familiar, but a true leader earns the willingness to follow into unknown territory. People go to places they would not go alone because they believe in the leader's judgment, character, and vision of what lies ahead. That belief is not bought with authority; it is built through consistency, honesty, and care over time. Leadership, then, is measured by how far others will trust you to take them, and the greatest leaders are those who make the uncertain journey feel worth taking together.
