Sinek separates leadership from position: no title, badge, or promotion makes someone a leader, and lacking one does not stop anyone from leading. Leadership is a daily choice to take responsibility for others, to look after the people in our care, and to act when it would be easier to stay quiet. Rank can command obedience, but only choice earns trust. That is good news for anyone without authority, because the work of leading is available to everyone, every day, wherever they stand.
