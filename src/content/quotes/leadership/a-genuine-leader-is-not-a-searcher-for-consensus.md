King argues that real leadership is not about pleasing everyone. A searcher for consensus waits until all objections fade, which often means waiting until the moment for action has passed. A molder of consensus, by contrast, persuades people toward a shared conviction, shaping agreement around what is right rather than what is easy. This takes courage, because it means standing firm before others agree. Leaders who only follow opinion are really followers. Genuine leaders help people see a better course, and then unite them behind it.
