Morrison reminds us that leadership is not only strategy and systems; it is also the feeling a leader brings into a room. The magic she describes is small and human: energy when the team is tired, belief when the goal looks distant, humour when tension is high. Anyone can manage a meeting, but a leader changes how the moment feels, and people carry that feeling into their work. Magic here means presence, not tricks. It is the difference between a team that merely shows up and one that leaves believing something good is possible.
