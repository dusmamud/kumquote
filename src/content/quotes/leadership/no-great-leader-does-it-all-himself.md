The leader who insists on doing everything becomes the bottleneck of their own organisation, and the one who grabs all the credit soon leads an empty room. Great achievements are always team efforts, built on many hands and many minds. A wise leader hires people stronger than themselves in key areas, trusts them with real work, and shares the praise generously. Credit shared is not credit lost; it multiplies loyalty and effort. Step back, let others shine, and your leadership will be measured by what the whole team accomplished, not by what you did alone.
