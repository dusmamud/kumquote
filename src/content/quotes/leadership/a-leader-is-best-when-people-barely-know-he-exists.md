The loudest leader is not always the best one. A great leader works quietly, guiding, supporting, and clearing obstacles, so that the team itself grows strong and confident. When the work succeeds, people feel ownership because they truly did the work. They learned, they decided, and they delivered. The leader's success is invisible, and that is the point. Leadership built on ego needs applause and credit. Lao Tzu teaches that the highest form of leadership is the one that empowers others so fully that they forget they were led at all.
