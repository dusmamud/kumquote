Booker separates leadership from rank. Titles can be granted, but leadership must be demonstrated — through action and example, day after day. A person with no title who acts with courage and integrity leads, while a titled person who does nothing follows the crowd. This definition democratizes leadership: anyone can lead from wherever they stand, by doing the right thing visibly. It also raises the standard for those with titles, reminding them that authority is borrowed and leadership is earned, one action at a time.
