Great leaders do not simply hire the best and take the credit; they teach, coach, and set standards that lift everyone around them. Showing average people how to do superior work means sharing knowledge, modeling excellence, and believing in their capacity to grow. The result is a team that outgrows its starting point and a leader whose impact multiplies through others. This is leadership as teaching: the measure of success is not what you achieve alone but how much better the people around you become because of you.
