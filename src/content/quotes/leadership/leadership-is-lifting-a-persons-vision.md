Drucker defines leadership not as command but as elevation. A true leader does three things at once: shows people a higher aim than they imagined, raises the standard of what they expect from themselves, and helps them grow past the limits they assumed were fixed. This is the opposite of managing by fear or by routine; it is the work of believing in people before they believe in themselves. When a leader lifts vision, performance and personality rise with it.
