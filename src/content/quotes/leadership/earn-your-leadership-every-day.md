Leadership is not a title you win once and keep forever. Jordan, who led by example on the court, knew that respect has an expiry date: it must be renewed daily through effort, discipline, and consistency. A leader who coasts on past achievements quickly loses the team's trust. People follow those who show up prepared, work hard, and hold themselves to the same standard they demand of others. Every day is a fresh test. Earn your leadership today, and earn it again tomorrow — that is the whole job, repeated without end.
