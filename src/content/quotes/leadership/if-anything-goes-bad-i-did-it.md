Bryant describes leadership as the art of carrying blame and giving away credit. When things go wrong, the leader stands in front and takes responsibility, which frees the team to fix the problem instead of hiding from it. When things go well, the credit belongs to the people who did the work, and naming them builds loyalty and pride. This division seems unfair, but it is the bargain of leadership: the leader's reward is the team's trust, and trust is earned exactly this way.
