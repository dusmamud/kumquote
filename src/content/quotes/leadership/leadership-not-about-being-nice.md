Kindness matters in leadership, but it is not the whole job. A leader must sometimes deliver hard truths, make unpopular choices, and hold people to standards they would rather avoid. Being nice keeps everyone comfortable; being right and strong moves the group forward. The best leaders earn respect not by pleasing everyone but by deciding clearly, acting firmly, and accepting responsibility for the outcome. When your choices are honest and your resolve is steady, people will follow — even when the path is difficult.
