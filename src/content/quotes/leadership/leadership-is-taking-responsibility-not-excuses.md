Romney reduces leadership to a single habit: taking responsibility. Excuses are easy and always available — the market, the team, the timing, the luck. But a leader who blames circumstances teaches everyone else to do the same. Responsibility, by contrast, is contagious in the best way: when the leader owns the outcome, the team owns its part too. Taking responsibility does not mean accepting blame for things beyond your control; it means owning your response. Leaders who make no excuses create cultures where problems get solved instead of explained away.
