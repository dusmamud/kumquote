Real leadership never needs force. Hitting people — with fists, threats, or humiliation — produces obedience only while the threat lasts, and it destroys the trust that makes a team work. People led by fear do the minimum, hide mistakes, and leave at the first chance. People led by respect give their best, speak honestly, and stay through hard times. A leader's power comes from earning trust, explaining the purpose, and sharing the burden — not from striking anyone down. If your authority depends on fear, it is not leadership at all.
