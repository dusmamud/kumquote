Sinek separates the label from the relationship. A title is handed down by an organisation chart, and it buys compliance for as long as the paycheque lasts. Having the people is different: it is earned trust, freely given, and it survives bad quarters and hard decisions. People follow a boss because they must; they follow a leader because they want to, because they believe the leader cares about them. The test is simple and unforgiving: take away the title, and see who still follows.
