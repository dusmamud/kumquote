Thatcher's line is about power and the need to announce it. Real authority does not need a label; it shows in decisions, steadiness, and results. The moment someone must insist on their own importance, the importance is already in doubt. This applies to leaders, experts, and anyone in a position of influence. Quiet confidence persuades more than loud claims. People follow those whose actions speak clearly, not those who keep reminding everyone of their title. True power is recognised by others; it is never something you have to declare yourself.
