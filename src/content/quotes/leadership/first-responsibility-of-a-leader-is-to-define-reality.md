Leadership is often confused with authority or vision, but De Pree reduces it to two honest duties. First, a leader must tell the truth about where things really stand, even when the truth is uncomfortable, because people cannot act wisely on illusions. Second, a leader must say thank you, recognising that no result belongs to one person alone. Between those two bookends sits all the daily work of serving others. The quote suggests that leadership is less about commanding and more about clarity and gratitude: seeing reality plainly, and honouring the people who help you face it.
