Sun Tzu places example above command. Force can make people obey for a day, but it breeds resentment and fails the moment the threat is gone. Example works differently: when a leader shares the hardship, keeps the same standard, and does the hard task first, followers choose to follow. Their effort comes from respect rather than fear, and it lasts. The strongest authority is the quiet kind, earned by conduct that others can see and wish to match. That is why the best leaders are remembered for how they lived, not only for what they ordered.
