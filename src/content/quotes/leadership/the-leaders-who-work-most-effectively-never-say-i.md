Drucker notices that the most effective leaders rarely use the word "I", and it is not a trick of language. Their thinking genuinely starts with the team: what we can do, what we need, and how we succeed together. A leader who thinks "I" takes the credit, makes the decisions alone, and leaves people feeling like helpers in someone else's story. A leader who thinks "we" shares ownership, and ownership makes people care. The pronoun reveals the mindset, and the mindset decides whether a group works as a team or merely as an audience.
