Patton's advice is about trust. Telling people exactly how to do a job assumes they cannot think; telling them what must be achieved invites them to think. Good leaders define the outcome clearly and then step back, letting capable people find the path. The results often surprise — people solve problems in ways the leader never imagined. Micromanagement kills initiative and teaches dependence. Clear goals plus real freedom produce ownership, creativity, and pride. A leader's job is not to control every step but to set the destination and trust the team to get there.
