Fiedler argues that leadership quality decides an organisation's fate more than any other factor. Strategy, resources, and talent matter, but without good leadership they scatter. A strong leader aligns people, sets standards, and keeps the group moving through difficulty. A weak one lets confusion and drift take over. This places a heavy responsibility on anyone in charge: the success or failure of the whole effort rests largely on how well they lead. It is a reminder to take the role seriously.
