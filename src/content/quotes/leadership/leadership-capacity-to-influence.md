Influence is the true currency of leadership, and Munroe traces it back to its source. It begins with purpose, which ignites conviction, which produces a vision, which generates passion, which finally inspires others. Each link in that chain matters. The quote also makes clear that influence is not manipulation. It flows outward from something genuine inside the leader, and people follow because they sense that authenticity. Leadership, then, is less about techniques of persuasion and more about living so clearly from purpose that others want to follow.
