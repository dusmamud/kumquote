McGannon strips leadership down to its essence: it is something you do, not something you are given. A title can grant authority, but it cannot grant leadership; that must be earned through action, through stepping forward, taking responsibility, and helping others move. Conversely, a person with no title at all can lead by acting with courage and clarity when it matters. Waiting for a promotion before leading is a misunderstanding of the role. Leadership begins the moment someone decides to act for the good of the group, whether or not anyone has formally put them in charge.
