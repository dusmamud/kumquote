Barker defines leadership by trust. A leader takes people somewhere they would not go alone — into change, risk, or unfamiliar territory — and they follow because they trust the person, not merely the plan. That trust is earned through character, clarity, and consistency, not commanded by rank. The destination matters, but the relationship matters more: people follow leaders they believe will bring them back safely, or at least will not abandon them on the way. Leadership is the willingness to go first, and the trust that makes others willing to come along.
