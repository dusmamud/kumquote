Gandhi, who led millions without ever holding office or an army, knew that leadership had changed. In an earlier age, strength of arm might have been enough to command. In the modern world, leadership is the art of getting along with people — listening, understanding, persuading, and uniting. Force can win a moment, but only human connection can sustain a movement. The leader's true muscle is empathy, patience, and the ability to bring different people together around a shared purpose.
