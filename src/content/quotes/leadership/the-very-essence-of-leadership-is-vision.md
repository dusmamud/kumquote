Hesburgh says vision is the non-negotiable core of leadership. Without a clear picture of where to go, a leader cannot inspire confidence or action. An uncertain trumpet confuses people; they cannot follow a sound they cannot hear clearly. Vision gives direction, meaning, and energy to the whole group. It does not need to be grand or detailed, but it must be genuine and steady. A leader's first job is to see the destination clearly enough that others can see it too.
