Sandberg measures leadership by what remains after the leader leaves. Making people better while you are present is only the first half; the real test is whether that improvement lasts without you. A leader who creates dependence has failed, however impressive their results look in the moment. The true achievement is building capability, confidence, and systems that endure — mentoring people, sharing knowledge, and shaping a culture that keeps working. Presence is temporary, but impact can be permanent. Lead in a way that your absence does not undo your work, and you will have led well.
