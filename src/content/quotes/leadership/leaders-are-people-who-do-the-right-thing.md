Bennis draws a clear line between two roles that are often confused. Managers focus on efficiency: doing things right, following processes, hitting targets. Leaders focus on direction: doing the right thing, choosing the right goals, and asking whether the work matters at all. Both skills are needed, but they are not the same. A team can execute flawlessly in the wrong direction and still fail. Leadership begins with judgment about purpose and values, then uses management to carry it out. The best organisations need people who can do both, but they must never mistake smooth execution for true leadership.
