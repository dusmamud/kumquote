Believing leaders are born is dangerous because it excuses the rest of us from trying. If leadership were a gift of birth, most people could simply wait for born leaders to appear, and never develop their own capacity to guide, decide, and inspire. The truth is kinder and more demanding: leadership is a set of skills, learned through practice, failure, and reflection like any craft. Anyone willing to study, to listen, and to take responsibility can grow into it. The myth flatters the few and limits the many; rejecting it opens the door for every person to lead.
