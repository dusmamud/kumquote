Churchill mocks leaders who lead by following. Keeping an ear to the ground means chasing opinion polls and popular mood instead of standing for something. Such leaders may be liked in the moment, but they cannot inspire, because they offer no direction of their own. People look up to leaders who show conviction and a clear view of where to go. Leadership requires the courage to stand above the noise, decide what is right, and persuade others to follow, even when it is unpopular.
