Bill Gates looks past the old image of the leader as the person with all the answers and points to a different skill: empowerment. In a world that changes faster than any single person can follow, the leaders who matter are those who make the people around them stronger, smarter, and more capable. Command and control can deliver short-term results, but it caps what a team can achieve at the leader's own limits. Empowerment removes that ceiling. When others grow, the whole effort grows with them, and that is the leadership the next century rewards.
