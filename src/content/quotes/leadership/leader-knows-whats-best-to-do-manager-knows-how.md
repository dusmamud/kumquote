Adelman draws the line between two different questions. The manager asks how: how to organise, how to execute, how to do a thing well. The leader asks what: what is worth doing, what direction matters, what should be attempted at all. Both questions are necessary, but only the leader's question sets the destination. A team can execute brilliantly in the wrong direction and arrive nowhere. Knowing what is best to do takes judgement, courage, and a willingness to be wrong; knowing how is a skill that follows once the direction is right.
