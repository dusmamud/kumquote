Leadership is not a title on a door; it is a responsibility anyone can pick up. It begins with seeing what others miss, the quiet talent in a colleague, the better way hidden inside a broken process. But seeing is not enough; developing potential takes courage, because growth requires honest conversations, patience, and the willingness to let people stumble while they learn. A leader who invests in others multiplies their own impact, creating capability that outlasts any single project. The reward is a team that grows stronger every year, and a culture where people become more than they thought possible.
