Mandela, who led a nation out of division, describes leadership as the opposite of the spotlight. Leading from behind means giving others the front — the credit, the visibility, the chance to grow — while you guide quietly from the back. Mandela understood that a leader's job is not to be the hero but to make heroes of others. When people in front feel trusted and capable, they achieve more than any single leader could alone. True power, he shows us, is shared power.
