Orders can organize a workforce, but they cannot create devotion. People who are only following instructions will stop the moment the instructions stop. Saint-Exupéry's insight is that great work begins with longing, not logistics. The leader's real task is therefore not to divide work but to awaken desire. A shared vision does what no amount of supervision can do: it makes people want to build the ship, not because they were told to, but because they cannot imagine not sailing.
