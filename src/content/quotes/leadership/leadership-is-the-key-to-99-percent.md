Bowles makes a bold claim: almost every success, whatever the field, comes down to leadership. Good plans fail without someone to carry them through, and ordinary plans succeed when someone leads them well. Leadership supplies the missing ingredients — direction when the path is unclear, courage when morale dips, and accountability when excuses pile up. Talent and resources matter, but without a leader they sit idle. The point is not that leaders do everything themselves; it is that nothing lasting gets done without someone willing to take responsibility for the result.
