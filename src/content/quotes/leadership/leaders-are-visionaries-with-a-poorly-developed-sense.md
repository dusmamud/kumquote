Jarvik, who invented the artificial heart against enormous odds, describes the leader's necessary blindness. Visionaries see what could exist so clearly that the obstacles in front of them seem smaller than they are. A fully developed sense of fear would stop most bold ventures before they began; the leader's gift is to feel the fear and still press forward, or barely to notice it at all. This is not recklessness but a different kind of arithmetic, where the value of the goal outweighs the weight of the risks. Without that quality, the world would keep only what already exists.
