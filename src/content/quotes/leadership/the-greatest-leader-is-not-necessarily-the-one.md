Reagan shifts the measure of leadership from personal achievement to the achievements of others. A leader who does everything themselves may look impressive, but they leave no stronger team behind them. The greatest leaders multiply their impact by bringing out the best in the people around them, giving them responsibility and credit. Their success is measured not by what they did alone, but by what their people went on to do. True leadership, in this view, is a form of service: building others up until the team can achieve things no single person could.
