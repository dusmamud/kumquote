Eisenhower led armies and then a nation, and of all the qualities a leader needs — courage, intelligence, vision — he names integrity as supreme. Without it, the others become dangerous: a clever leader without honesty manipulates, and a bold one without principle destroys trust. Integrity means doing the right thing when no one is watching and keeping your word when it costs you. People follow leaders they trust, and trust is built slowly through consistent honesty and lost in a single lie. Skills can be learned, but integrity is the foundation everything else stands on.
