Leadership is often described as strength, but Rohn describes it as balance. Strength without kindness becomes cruelty; kindness without strength becomes weakness. Boldness without humility turns into bullying; pride without humour becomes arrogance. The challenge is to hold opposite qualities together — to be firm and gentle, confident and modest, serious and light — at the same time. This is difficult because each virtue, pushed too far, becomes a vice. Great leaders are not those who pick one quality and exaggerate it, but those who keep many qualities in proportion, adjusting each to the moment and the people before them.
