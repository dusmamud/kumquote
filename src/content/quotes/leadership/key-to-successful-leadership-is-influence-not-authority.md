Blanchard separates the title from the work. Authority can compel obedience for a day, but it cannot buy trust, effort, or loyalty; people follow orders and keep their hearts closed. Influence works the other way: it is earned through consistency, listening, and care, and it moves people because they want to move. A leader with influence does not need to threaten or demand, because the team already believes in the direction. Authority is given by an organisation. Influence is given by people, and it is the only kind that lasts.
