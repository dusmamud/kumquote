Hesburgh strips leadership down to a single requirement: a vision. Without one, a leader is only a manager of the present, keeping things running but going nowhere. A vision gives people a reason to follow — a picture of a future worth working toward, clear enough to guide daily decisions and bold enough to inspire sacrifice. Everything else a leader does — planning, persuading, organising — serves that vision. If you cannot say where you are going, you are not leading; you are only busy.
