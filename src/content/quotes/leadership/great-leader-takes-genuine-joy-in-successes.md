Nance makes joy the test of leadership. A leader who feels threatened by a team member's success will quietly hold people back, take credit, or set limits no one can see. A leader who genuinely delights in others' wins does the opposite: they clear paths, share credit, and watch people grow beyond them. That joy is not softness; it is security. Teams can feel the difference at once. When the leader celebrates wins as their own, everyone works harder, trusts more, and dares to aim higher than before.
