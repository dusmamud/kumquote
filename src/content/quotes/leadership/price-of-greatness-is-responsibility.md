Greatness is not a gift; it is a bill that comes due in responsibility. Anyone who wants to lead, to build, or to matter must accept blame when things fail, make hard calls, and carry burdens others set down. Many people want the title and the applause, but few want the weight that comes with them. That weight is the real price, and paying it is what separates the truly great from the merely ambitious. If you want a larger life, prepare to be answerable for it. Responsibility is not the cost of greatness — it is the proof of it.
