Eimes gives leadership three kinds of sight: more, farther, and before. Seeing more means noticing what others miss in the present — the quiet talent, the early warning, the hidden opportunity. Seeing farther means holding a longer horizon, thinking beyond the quarter to the decade. Seeing before means anticipating change while others still assume the present will continue. Together these form foresight, the leader's defining gift. It is not magic but disciplined attention: looking carefully, thinking ahead, and speaking honestly about what is coming while there is still time to prepare.
