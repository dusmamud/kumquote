Hesburgh argues that vision is not a luxury for leaders but their essence. Without a picture of where the group is going, even the hardest work scatters in different directions, and people grow tired without knowing why. Vision gives the work a destination, turning separate tasks into one journey. It does not need to be grand or original; it needs to be clear, honest, and repeated until everyone can see it. A leader without vision can manage the present, but only a leader with vision can lead anyone into the future.
