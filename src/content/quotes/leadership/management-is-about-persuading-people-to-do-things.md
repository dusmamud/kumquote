Steve Jobs draws a sharp line between managing and leading. Management, in his words, is persuasion — getting people to do things they would rather not do. Leadership is something else entirely: inspiration that makes people attempt what they believed was beyond them. The difference is not in technique but in what it awakens. Persuaded people comply; inspired people stretch, invent, and surprise even themselves. Jobs built his career on the second kind of influence, and his point is that the best leaders do not push people toward tasks — they pull them toward possibilities they had never imagined.
