Leadership unfolds in three movements, and each one matters. First, know the way: understand the goal, the path, and the obstacles ahead, so your direction is clear and honest. Second, go the way: walk it yourself, face the same difficulties, and prove through action that the path is possible. Third, show the way: guide others along it with patience, encouragement, and clear teaching. A leader who only knows the way is a theorist. Maxwell's picture is complete only when all three come together: knowledge, personal example, and generous guidance that helps others travel the same road with confidence.
