Managing the present is administration; leadership is movement toward a future that does not yet exist. Kissinger defines the leader's task as taking people somewhere they have never been — beyond their habits, their comfort, and their current understanding of what is possible. That requires vision strong enough to see the destination and courage enough to start walking before the path is clear. People resist change, so the leader must also carry them through doubt. Anyone can keep things running as they are. The leader's real work is guiding people into new territory.
