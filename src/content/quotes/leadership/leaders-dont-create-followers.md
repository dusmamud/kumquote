Peters draws the line between a boss and a leader with a single test: what do you leave behind? A boss who collects followers builds dependence; when the boss leaves, everything stalls. A leader who builds more leaders creates independence, and the work continues and multiplies. Developing leaders means sharing knowledge, delegating real decisions, and celebrating when others outgrow you. It takes security to want people to surpass you, but that is precisely the point. The greatest legacy is not a loyal crowd but a generation of people ready to lead others in turn.
