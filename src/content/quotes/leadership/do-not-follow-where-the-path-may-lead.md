Emerson speaks to the heart of leadership: the courage to go first. Following a worn path is safe, but it only takes us where others have already been. A leader must be willing to step into unknown ground, guided by conviction rather than by the footprints of the crowd. That first step is lonely and uncertain, yet it is how new possibilities open for everyone else. The trail a leader leaves becomes a road for others to follow. If you believe in the direction, walk it — and let others find their way by following yours.
