Brown defines leadership as accountability for potential. A leader is responsible for finding what people and processes could become, not just managing what they are. That means paying attention, asking hard questions, and creating conditions where growth is possible. It is not about control or credit; it is about stewardship of talent. The work is often quiet and unglamorous: coaching, listening, and removing barriers. Leaders who take this responsibility seriously help everyone around them become more than they were. That is the quiet work that makes teams and organisations genuinely better over time.
