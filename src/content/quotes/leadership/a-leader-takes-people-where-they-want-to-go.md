Rosalynn Carter distinguishes between a good leader and a great one. Taking people where they already want to go is helpful, but it asks little of anyone. Great leadership sometimes requires guiding people toward a destination they resist — a hard reform, an honest change, a future they cannot yet see. This takes courage, because it means asking for trust before the result is visible, and accepting unpopularity in the meantime. The great leader does not impose blindly; they see further and carry others with them toward what is right.
