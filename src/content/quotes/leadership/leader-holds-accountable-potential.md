Leadership is often confused with authority, but Brown defines it by responsibility. Holding yourself accountable for finding potential means refusing to write people off and refusing to accept broken systems as inevitable. It is hard work, because potential is not always obvious, and drawing it out requires patience, honest feedback, and trust. Yet this is what separates a leader from a boss. Anyone can occupy a position. A leader takes ownership of growth — their own, their team's, and the work itself.
