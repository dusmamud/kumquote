Eisenhower, who commanded vast armies, knew that orders alone do not create willing effort. People can be forced to obey, but forced work is slow, resentful, and fragile. Real leadership aligns the task with what the other person actually wants — purpose, pride, growth, or belonging — so that the work becomes their own choice. The word art matters: this alignment cannot be reduced to a formula, and it requires understanding people, not just giving instructions. A leader who masters it does not need to push, because the team is already pulling in the same direction.
