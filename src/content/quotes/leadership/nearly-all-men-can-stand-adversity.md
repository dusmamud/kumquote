Hardship tests endurance, but power tests character — and it is the harder test. Suffering often brings out patience and courage, because there is little choice. Power offers a choice every day: to serve or to exploit, to listen or to silence, to share credit or to take it all. The person who stays honest, humble, and fair while holding power has passed the truest examination of all. That is why we should judge leaders not by how they survive trouble, but by how they behave when nothing can stop them. Power reveals who we really are.
