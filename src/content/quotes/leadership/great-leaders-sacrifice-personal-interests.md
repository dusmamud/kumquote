Wooden's test of leadership is sacrifice. A leader who protects their own comfort, credit, or position while the team struggles is not leading — they are using the team. Great leaders give first: they take the difficult conversations, share the credit, and absorb the blame. Sacrifice is not martyrdom; it is the price of trust. When people see a leader putting the team's good ahead of personal gain, they follow willingly and give their best. Leadership is measured by what you give up for others, not by what you gain from them.
