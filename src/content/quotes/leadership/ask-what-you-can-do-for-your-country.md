Kennedy reverses the usual question of citizenship. Instead of listing what the nation owes you, he asks what you owe the nation, turning the citizen from a customer into a partner. The reversal works because it gives people a role: service, sacrifice, and participation are not burdens but the price of belonging. A country built on taking soon runs out of givers. His line endures because it asks each generation to answer for itself, in action rather than in words.
