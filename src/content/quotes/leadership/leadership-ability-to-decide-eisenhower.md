First comes decision: cutting through noise and doubt to choose what must be done, a lonely act of judgment that cannot be delegated. Second comes motivation: turning that decision into shared will, so that people want to do it rather than merely obey. Orders can force compliance, but only inspiration creates commitment, and committed people do far better work. The leader must therefore be both clear-headed and persuasive, a decider who can also win hearts. Decide well, then make others glad to follow, and the work will carry itself forward.
