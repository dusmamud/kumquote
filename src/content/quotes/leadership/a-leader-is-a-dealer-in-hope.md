Hope is the fuel that keeps people moving through difficulty, and a leader's first job is to keep that fuel from running out. Plans, resources, and orders matter, but without hope they fall flat, because no one gives their best to a cause they believe is already lost. Napoleon understood that leadership is largely emotional work: helping people see a future worth striving for, even when the present is hard. A dealer in hope does not deny problems; they show that problems can be overcome. When people believe tomorrow can be better, they find the strength to act today.
