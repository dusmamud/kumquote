Anyone can describe the present; it takes a leader to describe a better future clearly enough that others can see it too. Welch's point is that leadership is fundamentally forward-looking. Seeing what should be requires honesty about what is wrong and imagination about what is possible. It also requires the courage to say so out loud, even when the gap between present and future is uncomfortable. A team that can see where it is going will endure far more difficulty than one that is merely busy.
