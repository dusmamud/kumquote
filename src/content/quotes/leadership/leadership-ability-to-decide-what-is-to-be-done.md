Deciding is only half of leadership; the other half is making people want to follow the decision. Orders can force compliance, but they cannot create commitment, and a team that merely obeys will do the minimum. A true leader explains the purpose, connects the task to something larger, and earns willing effort. This requires clarity first, knowing what truly matters and what can wait, and then the human skill of inspiring trust. When people understand the why and feel respected in the how, they bring energy no command can produce. Leadership, then, is decision joined to persuasion.
