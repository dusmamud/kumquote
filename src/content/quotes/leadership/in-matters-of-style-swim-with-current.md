Wise leaders know what to bend on and what never to bend. Manners, methods, tastes, and small preferences can adapt to the people around you; flexibility there builds goodwill and keeps teams moving. But principles — honesty, fairness, loyalty to what is right — must hold firm against pressure, fashion, and convenience. A leader who compromises on everything stands for nothing, while one who fights over everything exhausts everyone. The art is in telling the two apart. Swim with the current on style, and people will trust you to stand like a rock on principle.
