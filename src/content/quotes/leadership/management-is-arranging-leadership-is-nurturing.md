Tom Peters draws a sharp line between managing and leading. Arranging and telling are about control: organising tasks, giving orders, checking compliance. Nurturing and enhancing are about growth: developing people, removing obstacles, and helping them do their best work. Both have their place, but only one is leadership. A team that is merely arranged will do what it is told. A team that is nurtured will do more than it was asked. The leader's real work is not to build a perfect machine, but to grow people who can build the future.
