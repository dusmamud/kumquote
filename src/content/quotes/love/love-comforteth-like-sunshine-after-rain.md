After rain, the sun feels warmer than ever, and that is exactly how love feels after sorrow. Love does not stop the storms of life, but it brings the warmth that follows them. A kind word, a gentle presence, or a loved one simply being near can dry the tears and lighten the heaviest day. Like sunshine, love asks for nothing in return; it simply shines, and everything it touches grows. When life has rained on you, seek the people who love you. Their love will comfort you the way sunshine comforts the earth after rain.
