Generations pass on many things: money, property, names, and traditions. But the greatest gift one generation can leave the next is not material at all; it is love, shown in how we raise our children, how we treat our neighbours, and how we live our own lives. Love taught by example outlives every inheritance, because it shapes the people who will shape the world after us. What we give in love becomes part of those who receive it, and they carry it forward. Leave love behind you, and you leave something that never runs out.
