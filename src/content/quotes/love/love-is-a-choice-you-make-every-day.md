Feelings come and go, but love endures because it is a decision renewed daily. Chapman, who spent his life counselling couples, knew that lasting love is not maintained by passion alone but by the quiet choice to be kind, patient, and faithful even on difficult days. Choosing love means listening when we would rather argue, forgiving when we would rather keep score, and showing up when we would rather withdraw. Each day's choice strengthens the bond a little more. Love is not something that happens to us; it is something we choose, today and every day after.
