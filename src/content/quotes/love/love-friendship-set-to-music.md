Campbell captures what makes love different from mere affection: it has rhythm, harmony, and a beauty that ordinary friendship alone does not reach. Friendship gives love its foundation — trust, laughter, and honest conversation — while love adds melody: tenderness, longing, and delight in the other person. When the two combine, the relationship becomes something greater than either alone. The quote reminds us that the best romances are built on genuine friendship, and that love, at its finest, is friendship raised to music.
