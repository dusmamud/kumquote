Love changes how we see another person. Their flaws do not disappear, but we see past them to something deeper: their worth, their goodness, their humanity. In that moment of clear seeing, something sacred becomes visible. This quote suggests that love is not only a feeling but a way of seeing, one that reveals the divine in ordinary people. We do not need a temple to meet the sacred; we need only to love someone truly. When we do, the face of God is not far away; it is looking back at us.
