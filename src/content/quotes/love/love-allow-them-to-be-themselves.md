Real love does not come with a blueprint for the other person. It does not say: be who I need you to be. It says: be who you are, and I will love that person. This is harder than it sounds, because caring makes us want to shape, fix, and protect those we love. But control dressed up as care is still control. Allowing someone to choose their own path is one of the bravest forms of love. It trusts them, respects them, and keeps the relationship honest. Freedom is love's proof.
