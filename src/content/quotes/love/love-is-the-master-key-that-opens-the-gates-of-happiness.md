Happiness has many doors, and most keys fit only one of them. Wealth opens one door, fame another, but neither reaches the rooms that matter most. Love is different: it opens the gates that lead to belonging, to being known and still accepted, to mornings that feel worth waking for. A life without love can be full of achievements and still feel locked from the inside. Hold the master key gently, use it every day, and the gates of happiness will open far more often than any other key could manage.
