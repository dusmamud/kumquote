Love changes what we see. When we love someone, their faults soften in our eyes, and the follies that others notice clearly become invisible to us. This blindness is not a flaw in love; it is part of its nature. Love looks with the heart rather than with cold judgment, and that generosity is what allows two imperfect people to belong to each other. The warning in the line is gentle: remember that love's vision is partial. Enjoy its kindness, but do not be surprised when the rest of the world sees what love has hidden from you.
