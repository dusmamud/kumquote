Sheen describes love as an exchange, not a loss. Giving ourselves to another person can feel frightening, as if we are giving ourselves away. But real love works the other way: in serving someone else, we discover parts of ourselves we never knew. The giver is not emptied but enlarged. Selfish love keeps score and grows bitter; generous love returns to us changed, carrying a deeper sense of who we are. That is why love feels both like surrender and like coming home. We lose ourselves only to find ourselves again, fuller than before.
