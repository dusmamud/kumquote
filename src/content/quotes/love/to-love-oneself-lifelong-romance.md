We are the one person we can never leave, yet we often treat ourselves with a harshness we would never show a friend. Wilde, with his usual wit, points out that self-love is not vanity but the foundation of every other love. A person who accepts themselves can love others without needing them to fill an emptiness. The relationship with yourself lasts a lifetime, through every change and failure. Tend it kindly. When you learn to enjoy your own company and forgive your own faults, every other romance in life becomes healthier and more honest.
