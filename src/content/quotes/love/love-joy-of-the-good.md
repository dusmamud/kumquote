Plato describes love as something that delights everyone who encounters it — the good rejoice in it, the wise marvel at it, even the gods are amazed by it. Love, in this view, is not a weakness or a distraction from serious life. It is the highest thing: the recognition of goodness in another person, and the joy that recognition brings. To love is to see what is best in someone and to celebrate it. That is why love feels sacred even in ordinary moments. It connects us to the good, and the good connects us to everything.
