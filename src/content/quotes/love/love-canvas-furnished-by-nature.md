Voltaire pictures love as a work of art with two makers. Nature furnishes the canvas — the attraction, the tenderness, the human need for closeness that we are born with. But imagination does the embroidery: the dreams we weave, the meaning we find in small gestures, the future we picture together. Without nature there is no canvas; without imagination the canvas stays blank. Love, then, is both given and created. It begins with what nature provides and becomes beautiful through what our imagination adds to it.
