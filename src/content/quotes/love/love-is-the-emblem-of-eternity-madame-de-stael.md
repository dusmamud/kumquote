Madame de Staël says love is the one thing that refuses to obey the clock. Years pass, faces change, and ordinary feelings fade on schedule — but real love keeps its own time, feeling as present after decades as it did on the first day. That is why lovers speak of forever without embarrassment: inside love, the idea of an ending feels foreign, almost nonsensical. Hold that feeling gently, and it will outlast every measure of time you set against it.
