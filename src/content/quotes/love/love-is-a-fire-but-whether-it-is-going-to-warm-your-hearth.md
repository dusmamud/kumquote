Love brings warmth into a life, but it also carries risk, and no one can predict which it will be. The same fire that warms a home can burn it down if it is handled carelessly. That uncertainty is not a reason to avoid love, but a reason to tend it with honesty and care. Love asks for attention the way a fire asks for fuel and watchfulness. When we care for it well, it lights our days. When we neglect it, a spark can do great damage. Love is worth the risk, but only when we respect its power.
