Love does not shrink when it is shared; it grows. Juliet is describing a kind of giving that breaks the usual rules of scarcity, where handing something away leaves you with less. Real affection works the other way: the more generously it is offered, the more of it there seems to be. This is why love feels boundless, because it is not a possession to guard but a current that strengthens as it flows. When we stop counting what we give and simply give, we discover the heart has no fixed budget, only a deeper capacity waiting to be used.
