Campbell follows one word through a whole lifetime and finds it changing shape at every stage. In youth, love is excitement — new, urgent, and electric. In middle age, it becomes habituation, the quiet comfort of a life shared day after day. In old age, it turns into mutual dependence, two people leaning on each other through the final years. The word stays the same, but the experience does not. Love is not one thing we learn once; it is a companion that grows old with us, taking a new form at every age.
