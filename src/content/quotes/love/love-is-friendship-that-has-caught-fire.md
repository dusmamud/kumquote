Lasting romance is not built on excitement alone; it grows from friendship. Ann Landers describes love as friendship that has caught fire, warm, bright, and alive, yet rooted in something steady. Friends know each other honestly, laugh together, support each other, and enjoy simply being together. When that friendship deepens into love, passion adds heat without removing trust. Relationships that lack friendship often burn out quickly, because there is nothing solid beneath the spark. Build love on friendship: talk openly, share interests, respect each other, and let affection grow from genuine companionship and care.
