Rumi joins two elements that should not mix: water and fire. Love is the water of life, the thing that nourishes, refreshes, and keeps everything growing. Yet the lover burns like a soul of fire, alive with heat and longing. Together they describe love's beautiful contradiction: it sustains us and consumes us at the same time. Water without fire is calm but cold; fire without water burns out of control. Love holds both, giving life while demanding everything. To love is to be both quenched and set alight, and that is why it changes us so completely.
