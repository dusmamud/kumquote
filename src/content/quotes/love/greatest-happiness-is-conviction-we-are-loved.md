Wealth, fame, and achievement can please us for a moment, but Victor Hugo points to a deeper happiness: knowing, without doubt, that we are loved. That conviction gives safety in uncertainty and courage in hardship. When you believe you are loved for who you truly are, not for what you achieve, you can rest, grow, and take risks. If you love someone, do not assume they know. Say it, show it, and repeat it, because the certainty of being loved may be the greatest gift you can give.
