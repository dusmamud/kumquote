Romance is often pictured as two people staring into each other's eyes, but Saint-Exupéry offers a deeper image: two people standing side by side, looking toward the same horizon. Lasting love is less about fascination with each other and more about shared direction: common dreams, values, and a life built together. Couples who only gaze at each other can lose themselves; couples who look outward together grow in the same direction. True partnership is a shared journey, not a mutual admiration. Love is strongest when it faces the world together.
