Brown describes love not as a feeling that arrives, but as a shift in what matters most. When we truly love someone, their wellbeing begins to count as much as — sometimes more than — our own. Their joy becomes our joy, and their pain becomes something we cannot ignore. This is not self-erasure; it is the widening of the self to include another person. Real love shows itself in small daily choices: listening fully, sacrificing comfort, and celebrating their happiness as if it were our own, because in a sense it is.
