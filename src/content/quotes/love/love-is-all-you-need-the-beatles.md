It sounds too simple to be true, and that is why it stays. The Beatles sang that love is all you need, and generations have repeated it because it names what we keep forgetting. We chase money, status, and security, believing they will make us feel safe. Yet people who have the most of those things say the same: without love, none of it is enough. Love is the foundation everything else rests on. Give a person love, and they can face almost anything. Take it away, and a full life feels empty. Simple, but not easy, and not wrong.
