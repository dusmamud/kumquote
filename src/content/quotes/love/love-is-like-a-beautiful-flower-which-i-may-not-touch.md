Love does not always arrive as something we can hold or prove. Like a flower we may not touch, it can still fill the whole garden with its fragrance. Its presence is felt in small things: patience, attention, a hand offered without being asked. We often demand that love show itself in grand gestures, but its real power is quieter, changing the atmosphere of a life simply by being there. Do not dismiss a love you cannot grasp. If its fragrance makes your days brighter, it is real, and the garden of your life is better for it.
