Most of our happiness depends on our own fortunes, but love changes the equation: another person's joy becomes necessary to our own. That is what separates love from mere affection or habit. When someone matters this deeply, their sorrow weighs on us and their triumph lifts us, not out of duty but because our hearts have become joined. This definition is demanding, yet it is also clarifying — it shows us what real love asks. It is not about needing someone to complete us, but about caring so much that their happiness is part of ours.
