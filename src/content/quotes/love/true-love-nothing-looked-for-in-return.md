Much of what we call love is really an exchange: affection for affection, loyalty for loyalty, attention for attention. There is nothing wrong with wanting love returned, but true love starts somewhere deeper. It begins the moment we stop keeping score and love simply because the other person matters. That is when love stops being a transaction and becomes a gift. It does not mean ignoring our own needs; it means our giving is not conditional on getting paid back. Love without a price tag is the only kind that lasts.
