We like to think of love as a feeling that arrives and stays, but De Angelis describes it as a choice repeated moment by moment. Feelings rise and fall with mood, weather, and tiredness; choice is steadier. Every day offers small chances to choose love again: to listen instead of interrupting, to forgive instead of keeping score, to stay kind when irritation would be easier. A lasting relationship is not one lucky feeling but thousands of small choices added together. Love endures because it is chosen, again and again.
