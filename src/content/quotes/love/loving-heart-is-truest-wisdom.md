Knowledge can fill books, and cleverness can win arguments, but Charles Dickens points to a deeper wisdom: a loving heart. Intelligence without love can become cold, proud, or cruel. A loving heart, by contrast, knows when to listen, when to forgive, and when to help without being asked. It understands people not as problems to solve, but as lives to honour. Wisdom is not only what you know; it is how you treat others. If you must choose between being right and being kind, choose kindness. A loving heart will guide you truly even when facts alone cannot.
