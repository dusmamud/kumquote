Some things can be explained and taken apart. Love is not one of them. Tagore calls it an endless mystery, not because it is confusing, but because it has no explanation outside itself. It does not need a reason to exist; it simply is, and its presence changes everything it touches. We try to define it, measure it, and predict it, and it slips past every definition. Perhaps that is the point. Love is not a problem to be solved but a wonder to be lived. Accept the mystery, and love becomes deeper than any explanation could make it.
