Anouilh reduces love to its simplest form: not a gift we buy, but the gift of ourselves. Presents can be bought by anyone, but giving yourself — your time, your attention, your honesty, your presence — costs something real and cannot be faked. That is why the gift of oneself is the only gift that truly proves love. When we hold back the self and offer only things, we are keeping the best part for ourselves. Love asks for the giver, not just the gift.
