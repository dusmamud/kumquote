We chase many kinds of wealth: money, status, applause, and possessions. Yet none of them travel with us, and none of them keep their shine when life grows hard. Love is the only gold because it alone keeps its value in every season. It cannot be spent, stolen, or worn out, and the more we give it away, the richer we become. When everything else is measured and counted, love remains beyond price. Hold it carefully, give it freely, and remember that a life rich in love is rich in the only currency that truly lasts.
