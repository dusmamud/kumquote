Frost's line is a quiet defence of loving things as they are, not as we wish them to be. We do not love the river for flowing straight, or a person for matching our plans; we love them for their own nature, quirks included. True affection accepts the whole thing: the beauty and the rough edges together. Look at what you love today and ask whether you love it, or only your improved version of it. Loving what is, exactly as it is, is the simplest and hardest kind of love.
