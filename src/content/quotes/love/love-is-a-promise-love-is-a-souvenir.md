Some gifts fade the moment they are given, but love is different: it stays with us after the moment has passed. A promise made in love is not a debt to be repaid but a gift that keeps its warmth in memory. Once given, it can never be taken back, and that permanence is both its beauty and its responsibility. We should give love carefully, knowing it will live on in the person who receives it. And when love is given to us, we should treasure it, because a love given never disappears. It becomes part of who we are.
