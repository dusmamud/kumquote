Greatness is not reserved for the famous or powerful; it is available to anyone willing to love in small ways. A kind word, a shared meal, a patient ear, or a helping hand may seem ordinary, but done with great love they become extraordinary. Mother Teresa knew that the world is changed less by grand gestures than by countless small acts of care, repeated daily by ordinary people. We do not need a stage or a title to matter. Whatever our hands find to do, doing it with love is enough to make it great.
