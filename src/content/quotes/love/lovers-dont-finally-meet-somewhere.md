Real love rarely feels like meeting a stranger; it feels like recognising someone you have somehow always known. Two people can meet for the first time and yet sense a familiarity that goes beyond the moment — shared values, a shared way of feeling, a quiet sense that they belong together. This line says that lovers were never truly apart; the meeting only reveals a connection that was already there. It is a gentle view of love as discovery rather than invention.
