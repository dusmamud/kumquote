Dr. Seuss describes the surest sign of love with a playful image: when being awake with someone feels better than any dream. Dreams are safe and perfect because we control them, yet real love outperforms them — not by being flawless, but by being real. The laughter, the conversation, and the simple presence of the other person become more vivid than anything sleep can offer. Staying awake stops being a sacrifice and becomes a choice. When reality beats our dreams, we know we have found something rare.
