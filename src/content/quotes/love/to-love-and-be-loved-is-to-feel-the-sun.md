Being loved warms you from the outside, like sunlight on your skin. Loving someone warms you from the inside, giving your days purpose and light. David Viscott captures the rare completeness of mutual love: both giving and receiving at once. Many people know one side only, loving without being loved in return, or being loved without daring to love back. True fulfilment comes when both flows are open. Love is not a single act but an exchange, a steady sharing of care, attention, and presence. When love flows both ways, life feels brighter from every direction.
