Mencken's comparison is deliberately sharp, and it carries a warning worth hearing. Falling in love feels effortless: a glance, a conversation, a sudden certainty. But love, once begun, develops its own momentum, binding two lives together in ways that are not easily undone. The difficulty of stopping is not only about endings; it is about the depth of what love creates, shared memories, habits, hopes, and a future imagined together. This is why love deserves more care at its beginning than we often give it. Easy to start does not mean light or casual.
