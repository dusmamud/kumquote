Love is described as a feeling. Fromm describes it as an activity: the active concern for the life and growth of what we love. Concern here is not worry; it is attention, care, and the willingness to help something become itself. A gardener loves a plant by tending it, a parent loves a child by helping them grow, a friend by showing up. In each case, love is visible in what we do, not only what we feel. To know whether you love something, do not ask your heart. Ask your hands, your time, and your attention.
