Genuine love refuses to stop at the walls people build between each other. Differences of background or distance can slow it, but rarely defeat it when the feeling is real. Love finds a way around what stands in its path, the way water finds a crack in stone. This does not mean love ignores real problems or pretends they do not exist. It means love treats obstacles as things to cross, not reasons to quit. Steady, hopeful affection keeps showing up, keeps trying, and keeps believing the other person is worth reaching. That persistence turns feeling into devotion.
