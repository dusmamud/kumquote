The first rush of love feels like madness because it is intense, sudden, and beyond control. It erupts like a volcano, shaking everything familiar, and then, just as surely, it subsides. That cooling is not the end of love but the beginning of its real test. When the eruption passes, what remains is a choice: to stay, to care, and to build something steady out of the fire. Passion starts love, but only commitment keeps it alive. The madness fades, and what we do after it fades is what love truly becomes.
