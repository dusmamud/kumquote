Love is often confused with possession: holding on tightly, setting rules, and fearing loss. But love that grips too hard suffocates. This quote, from The Prophet, asks lovers to stay close without binding each other, like two shores with a living sea between them. Closeness does not require merging into one person; it requires room to breathe, grow, and remain oneself. A bond that allows freedom is stronger than one built on control. Love thrives not when two people own each other, but when they choose each other freely, every day.
