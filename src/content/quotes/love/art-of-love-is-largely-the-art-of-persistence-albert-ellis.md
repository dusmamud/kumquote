We imagine love as a feeling that arrives and stays on its own, as if it were weather. Ellis disagrees. The art of love is the art of persistence, the choice to stay, to listen, to forgive, to try again. Feelings rise and fall, but lasting love is built on the decision to keep showing up, when it is not easy. Anyone can be loving when things are easy. Persistence turns affection into a relationship, and a relationship into a life shared. Love is not only something we fall into; it is something we practice, one day at a time.
