It is easy to love an idea of someone. It is much harder to love the real person, with thoughts, fears, and needs that have nothing to do with us. That is what makes love difficult: it asks us to step outside ourselves and accept that another person is as real, as complex, and as important as we are. Selfish love treats the other as a mirror. Real love treats them as a world. The moment we see someone else — not as a role in our story, but as a person in their own right — love begins.
