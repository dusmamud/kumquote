Picasso, a man who lived for creation, calls love the greatest refreshment — the thing that restores us when life has worn us down. Like cool water after a long walk, love revives our energy, clears our thinking, and returns colour to days gone grey. It refreshes not because it removes our troubles, but because it reminds us why the troubles are worth facing. Work, worry, and routine can exhaust the spirit; love quietly refills it. In that sense, love is not a luxury of life but its renewal.
