We often wait to be loved, as if love were something others owe us. Franklin reverses the order: love first, and become the kind of person love naturally gathers around. Being loveable is not about charm or appearance; it is about kindness, reliability, generosity, and the willingness to give without keeping score. People are drawn to those who make them feel valued and safe. When we offer love freely, we create the conditions for it to return — not as a transaction, but as a natural response.
