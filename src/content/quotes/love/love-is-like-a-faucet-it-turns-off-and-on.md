Holiday's image is simple and honest: love flows like water from a tap, and sometimes it stops. A relationship can run warm for months and then suddenly run cold, not because the feeling died but because something in the plumbing changed — attention, honesty, or care. The good news in the image is that a tap can be turned on again. Love is not a well that runs dry forever; it responds to what we do. Keep the pipes clear, turn the handle with intention, and the water returns.
