Everything in the world can be doubted: the stars, the sun, even truth itself. But this love is presented as more certain than all of them. The speaker is not arguing with logic; he is declaring a feeling so steady that it outranks the facts of nature. That is how deep love feels from the inside: not like a guess or a hope, but like the surest thing we know. This quote captures the confidence of real devotion. When love is true, doubting it would be stranger than doubting the stars.
