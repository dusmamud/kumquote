Pascal, a mathematician, admits that the heart follows a logic that reason cannot map. We love people, places and pursuits for reasons that resist tidy explanation, and demanding a full justification often misses the point. This is not an attack on thinking; it is a recognition that human feeling has its own knowledge. The heart notices goodness, beauty and connection before the mind can name them. Trying to force every feeling into a rational argument can flatten what makes it real. Some truths are felt first and understood later, and love is chief among them.
