Shakespeare gives love two faces at once: it is familiar, close as a friend, and yet it can act like a devil, turning people foolish. That is the truth about love. It comforts us and unsettles us in the same breath, making us braver and more vulnerable than we were before. We should not be surprised when love behaves this way. Its power lies in that contradiction. To love is to welcome something both tender and dangerous into your life, and to accept that you cannot control which face it shows. That risk is part of what makes love real.
