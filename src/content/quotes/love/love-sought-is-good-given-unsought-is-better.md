There is a quiet difference between love that is chased and love that is freely offered. When affection must be begged for, negotiated, or earned through effort, it can feel like a prize rather than a gift. But love that arrives unasked, given simply because the giver wants to give, carries a warmth that no pursuit can match. It tells the other person they are valued for who they are, not for what they provide. The best love does not wait to be requested. It notices a need, moves first, and gives without keeping score.
