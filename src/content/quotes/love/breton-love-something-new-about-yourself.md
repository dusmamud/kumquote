We usually think love is about learning everything about another person, but Breton turns it around: love teaches us about ourselves. The right person sees parts of us we had never noticed — a courage we denied, a tenderness we hid, a dream we had abandoned. Through their eyes, we become new to ourselves, and that discovery feels like falling in love twice over. A relationship that only confirms what we already know soon grows stale; one that keeps revealing us to ourselves stays alive. Love, at its best, is a mirror that shows us who we might become.
