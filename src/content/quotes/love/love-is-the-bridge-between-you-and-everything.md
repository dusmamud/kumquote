Without love, we stand apart from the world, separated by fear, pride, and indifference. Rumi's image of the bridge captures how love connects us to everything beyond ourselves: to other people, to nature, to beauty, and to the divine. Crossing that bridge means leaving the small island of the self and entering a wider world where we belong to something larger. Love is not merely a feeling between two people but the very path that joins us to life itself. Build the bridge, cross it often, and the world on the other side becomes home.
