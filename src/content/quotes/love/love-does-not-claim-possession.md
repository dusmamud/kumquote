Tagore draws the line between love and ownership with a single sentence. Possession says mine, and builds walls; love says go, and opens doors. To love someone is to want their freedom as much as their company, to celebrate their growth even when it leads them somewhere new. The beloved is not a prize to be kept but a person to be freed, again and again. This is a demanding vision of love, because it asks us to trust rather than control, to find security not in holding tight but in the strength of the bond itself.
