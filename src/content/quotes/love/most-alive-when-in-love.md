Love wakes us up. Colours seem brighter, food tastes better, music moves us more, and even ordinary days feel charged with meaning. When we are in love, we pay attention, we take risks, we feel deeply, and we are fully present in our own lives. The rest of the time we can drift through days half asleep. Updike's observation is that love returns us to life itself. It reminds us what it feels like to be alive, not merely to exist, and that aliveness is something worth seeking and protecting.
