We often say we love someone for their qualities: their kindness, humor, or beauty. But this quote points to a deeper truth. Love is measured by who we become in another person's presence. The right person brings out our best self: braver, kinder, more honest than we are alone. That is the real test of love, not how impressive the other person is, but how much they help us grow. A love that makes us more ourselves, and better ourselves, is the rarest and most valuable kind.
