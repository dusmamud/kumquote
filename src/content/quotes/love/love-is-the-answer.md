In a world full of complicated problems, this line sounds almost too simple. Yet its simplicity is the point. When we do not know what to do — in a fight, in a family, in a divided world — love is the direction that rarely leads us wrong. It does not solve every problem instantly, but it changes how we approach them: with patience instead of anger, with curiosity instead of judgment. Lennon was not offering a policy; he was offering a compass. And deep down, most of us already know he is right. Love is the answer.
