If love were only a feeling, it would come and go with our moods, and no promise to love someone forever could ever be kept. Fromm insists that lasting love rests on something steadier than emotion: a conscious decision, a clear-eyed judgement about who the other person is, and a promise to stand by that choice. Feelings matter, but they are the weather, not the foundation. A relationship survives hard seasons because both people decided it was worth keeping, not because they always felt like it. That decision, renewed daily, is what turns affection into commitment.
