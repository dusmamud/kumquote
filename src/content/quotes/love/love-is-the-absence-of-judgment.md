Judgment divides people into worthy and unworthy, right and wrong, acceptable and not. Love, at its deepest, does the opposite: it receives the other person as they are. That does not mean ignoring harm or pretending faults do not exist. It means refusing to let a verdict replace the person. When judgment falls away, listening improves, patience grows and genuine closeness becomes possible. The line is challenging because judgment feels protective, as if it keeps us safe. Yet it often builds walls where bridges belong. Love begins where the need to judge ends.
