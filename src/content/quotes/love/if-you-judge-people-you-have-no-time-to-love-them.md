Judgment and love cannot occupy the same moment. When we are busy measuring people's faults, comparing them to our standards, or deciding whether they deserve kindness, we have no attention left for loving them. Mother Teresa's words ask us to choose: we can spend our time judging, or we can spend it loving, but not both. Love begins where judgment ends, in the decision to accept people as they are and to care for them anyway. The moment we stop judging, we discover how much time and tenderness we actually have to give.
