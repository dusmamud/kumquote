Duty can make people obey, but it rarely makes them grow. Einstein saw that love teaches more deeply than obligation, because love reaches the heart rather than merely the will. A child who is loved into learning will go further than one driven only by rules. Duty says you must; love says you want to, and that difference changes everything. When we act from love, the work itself becomes the reward. The best teachers, parents, and leaders know this: inspire affection for the task, and duty becomes unnecessary.
