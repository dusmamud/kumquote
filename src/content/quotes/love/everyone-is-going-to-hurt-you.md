No relationship is free of hurt. Even the people who love us most will disappoint us, misunderstand us, or fail us at some point — not always from malice, but from being human. Expecting a pain-free relationship is expecting something that does not exist, and it leaves us perpetually disappointed. The honest question is not whether someone will hurt us, but whether they are worth the hurt. The right people are worth it because they also bring joy, growth, and loyalty that outweigh the pain.
