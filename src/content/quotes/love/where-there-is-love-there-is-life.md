A life without love can feel busy yet empty, full of activity but lacking warmth and meaning. Gandhi connects love directly with life itself: where love is present, life feels alive, hopeful, and worth living. Love gives courage in hardship, patience in conflict, and joy in ordinary moments. It is not limited to romance; it includes love for family, friends, neighbours, and even strangers. To love is to participate fully in life rather than merely survive it. If your days feel flat, ask where love is missing, and begin there. Love is what turns existence into a life.
