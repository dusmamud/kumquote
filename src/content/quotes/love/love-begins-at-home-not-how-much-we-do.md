Love is not measured by the size of the deed but by the heart behind it. A small act done with real love — a meal cooked with care, a word spoken gently, a moment of patience — can matter more than a grand gesture without feeling. And love starts closest to home, with the people we see every day, not with strangers far away. If we cannot be loving in our own house, our love elsewhere is a show. Begin where you are, with the people around you, and put love into small actions. That is where love begins.
