Love rarely arrives on our terms. It calls us toward vulnerability, change, and sometimes pain, and our instinct is to refuse. But this quote urges us to follow even when the way is hard and steep. Love asks for courage because it asks us to open up, to risk loss, and to grow beyond our comfort. The path is not always smooth, yet it leads somewhere worth going. Those who follow love's call discover that the difficulty was part of the gift. Love is not found by playing safe; it is found by following.
