Love behaves like a habit: once it takes root, it shapes our days — the morning message, the shared joke, the person we turn to first. Breaking that habit hurts precisely because love rewires our routines. And love is hard to satisfy because it always wants more closeness, more certainty, more time. Yet that difficulty is also the proof of its depth. A habit that is hard to break is one that mattered. The ache of missing someone is the shadow of having loved them well. It is painful, but it is also a kind of evidence.
