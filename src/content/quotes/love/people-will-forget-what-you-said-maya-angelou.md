We rehearse our words and polish our achievements, but Angelou
says the audience remembers something else entirely: the feeling
in the room after we leave. A clever sentence fades by dinner.
A moment of being truly heard, or truly dismissed, stays for years.

That is why this quote belongs under love and not under speaking
skills. Love, in practice, is mostly how people feel around you —
safe or judged, welcome or tolerated, seen or managed. If you want
to be remembered well, the work is not a better speech. It is
patience in one conversation today: listen fully, answer kindly,
and leave the other person feeling a little more human than before.
