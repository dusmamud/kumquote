Arguments about right and wrong divide us, even when both sides mean well. Each person defends their position, and love gets lost in the debate. This quote imagines a place beyond those categories, a field where judgment stops and meeting begins. It is an invitation to drop the need to be right and to be present with another person instead. Real connection happens when we stop sorting each other into categories. The field is always there, waiting, whenever we are willing to step beyond our opinions and simply meet.
