Yogananda pictures love as light falling on the walls we build between ourselves and others — walls of pride, fear, suspicion, and old hurt. Light does not argue with a wall or knock it down by force; it simply shines until the wall no longer matters. When we love someone truly, the distance between us shrinks: their pain becomes our concern, their joy becomes our joy. The same light works between strangers, too, whenever kindness replaces judgement. Walls are built slowly, brick by brick, but love can dissolve them in a single honest moment.
