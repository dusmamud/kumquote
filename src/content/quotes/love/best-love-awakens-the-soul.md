Real love does two things at once: it stirs us up and it settles us down. It plants a fire — ambition, longing, the urge to become more than we are — and at the same time it brings a deep peace, the feeling of being at home with another person. Love that only excites soon burns out; love that only comforts can grow dull. The best love holds both. It pushes us toward our better selves while giving us rest. When love awakens the soul and calms the mind, we know we have found something rare.
