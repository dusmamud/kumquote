We tend to think of love as something that happens between two particular people, as if it belonged to one relationship alone. Fromm argues the opposite: love is a way of being in the world, an attitude that a person either carries or does not. Someone genuinely loving brings patience, care, and respect to every encounter, not only to a partner. A loving person loves widely; one who cannot love one person well rarely loves another well either. Love is less about finding the right object and more about becoming the kind of person who loves.
