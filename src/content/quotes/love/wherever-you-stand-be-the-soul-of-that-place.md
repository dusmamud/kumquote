Wherever life places you — a home, a workplace, a friendship — you have a choice: drift through it half-present, or become its soul. To be the soul of a place means bringing warmth, attention, and love to it, so that the place itself feels more alive because you are there. It is not about status or control; it is about presence. A person who loves fully changes the atmosphere of a room simply by being in it. You do not need to wait for the perfect place to belong.
