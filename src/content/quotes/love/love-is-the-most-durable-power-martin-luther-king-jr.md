Power is usually pictured as force: armies, money, the ability to make others obey. King offers a different kind of power, one that outlasts them all. Love is the most durable power in the world because it does not depend on fear or control. It works slowly, changing hearts instead of breaking them, and what it builds cannot be taken away by the next election or the next army. Hatred burns bright and burns out. Love endures, quietly, through generations. In the long story of the world, it is love, not force, that has the final word.
