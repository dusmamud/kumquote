Most games need a loser, which is why love is so different: when it is played well, nobody has to lose. Each person gives, and each person receives, and the giving itself becomes part of the reward. The moment love turns into keeping score, someone starts losing, because affection measured out carefully stops feeling like affection at all. A good partnership is not a contest but a shared game where both players root for each other. Play generously, celebrate the other's wins as your own, and discover that in love, winning together is the only way to win.
