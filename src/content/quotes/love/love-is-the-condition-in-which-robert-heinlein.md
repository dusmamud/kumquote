We measure love by how it makes us feel: the warmth, the excitement, the comfort. Heinlein offers a stricter test. Love is the condition in which another person's happiness becomes essential to your own. Not pleasant, but essential. When their joy lifts you and their pain weighs on you, that is love doing its work. It is the moment the boundary between two lives grows thin, and their wellbeing becomes part of yours. This is why love asks so much of us. To love someone is to tie your happiness to theirs, willingly, and to carry that tie with care.
