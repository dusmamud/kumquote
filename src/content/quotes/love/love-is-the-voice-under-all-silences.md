Beneath every silence between people, Cummings hears a voice that never stops speaking: love. It is present even when words fail, when distance grows, or when fear tries to drown it out. The second half of the line makes a bold claim, that hope has no opposite in fear, because love carries hope within it. Fear and hope may seem like opposites, but where love is real, fear cannot cancel hope entirely. The quote treats love as something quieter and deeper than speech, a current running underneath everything, steady even when it cannot be heard.
