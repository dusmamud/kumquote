Most love stories begin with a flame: intense, bright, wild. Lee follows that flame through time. In the beginning, love is a fire, pretty and fierce, but still only light and flickering. What matters is what it becomes. As hearts mature, love turns to coals, deep-burning and unquenchable, warm without the drama of the flame. That is the love that lasts: not the blaze that consumes everything in a season, but the steady heat that warms a home for years. Friendship is the wood that keeps those coals burning. Tend the friendship, and the fire never truly goes out.
