True love does not come with conditions that change when circumstances change. It does not fade when beauty fades, when money runs out, or when hardship arrives. The marriage of true minds is a union of equals who remain steady through time and trouble. This quote, from Sonnet 116, defines love by what it is not: it is not fickle, not convenient, not temporary. Real love is constant, and its constancy is what makes it trustworthy. If it alters with every difficulty, it was never love in the first place.
