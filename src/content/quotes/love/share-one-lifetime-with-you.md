Eternity means nothing without someone to share it with, while a single lifetime beside the right person is worth more than all the ages of the world. This is love at its most devoted: the choice of togetherness over length, of a shared ordinary life over endless lonely years. It says that the value of time is not in how much of it we have but in whom we spend it with. Love turns a finite lifetime into something immeasurable, because every day shared adds a meaning that years alone could never hold.
