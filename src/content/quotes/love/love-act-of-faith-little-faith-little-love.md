Love cannot be proven in advance, and that is why it requires faith. To love someone is to trust what cannot be guaranteed: that they will stay, that they mean what they say, that the future will hold. People with little faith protect themselves by loving little, keeping one foot out the door and their hearts half closed. But guarded love stays small, because love grows only where trust is given freely. Faith is not blind; it is the courage to commit without certainty. The more faith we bring, the more love becomes possible.
