Love does not always begin with grand gestures. Often it begins with something as small as a smile. A smile costs nothing, yet it changes the air between two people: it says you are welcome here, I am glad to see you, you matter to me. In a world where so many feel unseen, that small welcome is a powerful act. Mother Teresa understood that great love is built from tiny, repeated kindnesses. A smile is the first of them. Offer it freely — to family, to strangers, to anyone — and watch how often love follows.
