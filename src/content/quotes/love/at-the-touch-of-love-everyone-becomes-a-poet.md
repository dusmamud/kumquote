Love changes how we see. Before it, the world can look ordinary and plain; after it, the same streets, the same mornings, and the same small moments seem full of meaning. That is why even people who have never written a verse suddenly find words, images, and songs pouring out of them. Love does not teach poetry as a skill; it opens the senses so wide that ordinary life starts to look like art. The lover becomes a poet not by studying language but by feeling deeply enough that language becomes necessary.
