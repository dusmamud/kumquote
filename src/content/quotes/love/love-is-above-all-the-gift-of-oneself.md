Love is not proven by what we buy or promise; it is proven by what we give of ourselves. Time, attention, patience, honesty — these are the true gifts of love, because they cost something real and cannot be faked for long. Anyone can offer presents, but only love offers the self: being present, listening fully, and standing beside someone in hard times. That gift asks for vulnerability, and that is why it means so much. To love is to hand over a piece of yourself and trust the other person to hold it gently.
