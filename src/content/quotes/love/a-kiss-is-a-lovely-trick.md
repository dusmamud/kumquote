There are moments when words run out — not because there is nothing to say, but because language is too small for what we feel. A kiss belongs to those moments. It says what sentences cannot: presence, tenderness, and a closeness that needs no explanation. Bergman's line is playful, calling it a trick of nature, but it captures something true about love. Speech is for thinking and negotiating; touch is for the feelings that arrive before thought. When words become superfluous, silence is not empty — it is full. A kiss is simply the most honest way to fill it.
