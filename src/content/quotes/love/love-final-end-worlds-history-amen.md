Novalis gives love the largest possible place: not a feeling among others, but the final word of everything. History moves through wars, empires, and ideas, yet the line suggests that all of it ends in love, as a prayer ends in Amen. Love is what remains when the noise falls away, the thing that gives the whole story its meaning. If that is true, then loving well is not a small part of life but its deepest purpose. Everything else passes; love is the conclusion toward which the world has been moving all along.
