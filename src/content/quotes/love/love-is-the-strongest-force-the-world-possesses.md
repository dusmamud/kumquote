Love's strength looks nothing like the strength of armies or empires. It does not shout, threaten, or demand; it serves, forgives, and endures, and in doing so it outlasts every force that opposes it. Gandhi knew this from experience: humble love moved millions where violence could not, because it changes hearts instead of breaking them. The paradox is the point. Love is strongest precisely because it is humblest, needing no display of power to achieve what power never can. The gentlest force in the world is also the most unstoppable.
