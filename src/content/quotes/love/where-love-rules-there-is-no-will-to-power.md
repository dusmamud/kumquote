Love and the desire to control cannot share the same space. Where love truly rules, there is no need to dominate, manipulate, or win, because both people feel safe and valued as they are. But where power takes over, love quietly disappears, replaced by fear, resentment, and the need to keep score. Jung's insight helps us recognise unhealthy relationships: if someone must always be right or in charge, love has already left the room. Real love does not seek power over another; it seeks partnership, trust, and the freedom for both people to grow.
