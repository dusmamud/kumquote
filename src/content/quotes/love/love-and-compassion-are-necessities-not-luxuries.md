We treat love and compassion as luxuries, nice to have when life is comfortable. This quote insists they are necessities, as essential as food and shelter. Without them, individuals grow cold and societies grow cruel. Human beings survive through connection: babies need affection to thrive, adults need belonging to stay well, and communities need compassion to hold together. A world without love is not merely sad; it is unlivable. The quote asks us to treat kindness not as an extra, but as the foundation everything else depends on.
