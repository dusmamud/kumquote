We often enter love like a bargain, quietly keeping score: what did I receive, what am I owed, was it enough? That arithmetic slowly poisons even good relationships. Love measured by what we get is never satisfied, because no person can fill every need. Love measured by what we give is different. Giving freely — attention, patience, kindness — creates warmth that comes back on its own, without being demanded. The happiest couples are not the ones who got the most. They are the ones who gave the most, and discovered that giving was the gift.
