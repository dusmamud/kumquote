Love is not proved by grand gestures or pretty words. It is proved by the hard days two people survive together, the arguments they work through and the losses they carry side by side. Shared experience is the real test, because anyone can be charming when life is easy. What matters is who stays when the plan falls apart and the mood turns sour. Over time, those moments become a history that belongs to no one else. That history becomes the bond itself, stronger than attraction and deeper than promises, because it was earned one day at a time.
