We often think of love as something we find or lose, a possession that comes and goes. De Angelis describes it as a flow, an energy moving through us, nourishing whatever it touches. Like water, love cannot be held in a closed fist; it has to move, to be given and received. When we dam it with fear or resentment, it goes stale. When we let it move, it extends beyond us, touching strangers. Love is not a thing we own. It is a current we join, and the more freely it flows, the more alive everything becomes.
