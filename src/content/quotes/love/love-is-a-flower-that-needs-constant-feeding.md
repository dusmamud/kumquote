Love is often treated as a feeling that either exists or does not, as if it needs nothing from us. But like a flower, love is a living thing, and living things need feeding. Attention, kindness, honesty, and time are its daily food, and without them even the strongest love begins to fade. The good news is that the feeding is simple: small, steady acts of care, repeated day after day. Do not wait for grand occasions to nourish your love. Tend it daily, in ordinary moments, and it will keep blooming for years.
