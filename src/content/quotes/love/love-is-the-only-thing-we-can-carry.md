At the end of a life, money, titles, and possessions all stay behind. What travels with us is love: the relationships we built and the kindness we gave. That is why love matters more than achievement; it is the one treasure time and loss cannot take away. A life rich in love ends more peacefully, because the person leaves knowing they mattered. This also guides how we should live now. If love is what lasts, the hours spent caring for others are never wasted, and every act of love becomes a legacy no one can steal.
