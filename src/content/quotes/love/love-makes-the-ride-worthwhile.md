The world turns on its own, driven by work, duty, and necessity, whether love is present or not. But a world that only turns is just a machine, and life inside it can feel mechanical and empty. Love does not power the world; it gives the journey meaning. It turns ordinary days into memories, hardships into shared stories, and the long ride of life into something worth taking. Without love the ride still happens, but with love it becomes worthwhile, and that makes all the difference.
