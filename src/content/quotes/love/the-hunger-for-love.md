Mother Teresa, who fed the hungry daily, names a deeper hunger that bread cannot touch. The body asks for food and is satisfied; the heart asks for love and can starve for a lifetime, quietly, in a crowded room. Loneliness, she learned, is the most terrible poverty, harder to relieve than an empty stomach because it cannot be filled with a meal or a coin. This quote asks us to notice the invisible hunger around us: the person who needs to be seen, the child who needs to be held, the elder who needs a visit.
