Love is hard to define in words, but easy to recognize in a person. Before meeting the right someone, love is an idea, a word in books and songs. Afterward, it has a face, a voice, and a thousand small memories. Hesse's line is a quiet thank you to the person who made an abstract idea real. It says that love is not learned from definitions but from experience, from being known and cared for by another person. If you understand love at all, it is because someone showed it to you.
