Hatred answered with hatred only multiplies the darkness. King's words remind us that the only force strong enough to end hate is love, because love alone changes hearts rather than merely defeating enemies. When we respond to cruelty with kindness, we break the cycle instead of feeding it. This is not weakness or surrender; it takes far more strength to love an enemy than to hate one. History shows that movements built on love outlast those built on revenge. Light does not argue with darkness; it simply shines, and the darkness disappears.
