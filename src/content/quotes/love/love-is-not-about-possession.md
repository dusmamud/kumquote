Many people treat love as ownership, as if caring for someone gives the right to control or change them. Osho draws a clear line between the two: possession clings and fears loss, while appreciation notices and values what is already there. Real love does not tighten its grip; it pays attention, listens, and lets the other person remain themselves. When love becomes appreciation, it grows lighter and steadier, because nothing is being held hostage. The test is simple: if love makes you want to own someone, it has drifted; if it makes you grateful for them, it is love.
