We often dismiss love as a soft feeling, something pleasant but less solid than money, work, or ambition. Tagore turns that judgement around: love is the most real thing there is, because it is what connects us to others and gives the rest of life its meaning. A career or a fortune can be lost overnight, but the bonds we form shape who we are long after circumstances change. When we treat love as a passing sentiment, we undervalue the force that holds a life together. Seen clearly, love is not decoration on reality; it is the foundation underneath it.
