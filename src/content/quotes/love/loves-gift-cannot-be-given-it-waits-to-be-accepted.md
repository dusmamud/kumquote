Love cannot be forced on anyone. You can offer it freely, but it only becomes real when the other person opens their heart and accepts it. That is what makes love both beautiful and fragile: it depends on two wills, not one. The giver must be patient, and the receiver must be willing. When acceptance finally comes, the gift is complete, and both hearts are changed by it. Do not try to push love where it is not yet welcome. Offer it honestly, wait gently, and let acceptance come in its own time.
