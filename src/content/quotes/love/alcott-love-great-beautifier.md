Three words hold a whole truth: love changes how things look. A plain room feels warm when someone we love is in it, an ordinary day gains colour, and even our own reflection seems kinder when we feel loved. The beautifying is not a trick of the eye but a change in the heart — love teaches us to notice what is good, to forgive what is flawed, and to find grace in the everyday. It beautifies the lover as much as the beloved, softening harshness into patience. Whatever love touches, it leaves more beautiful than it found, including us.
