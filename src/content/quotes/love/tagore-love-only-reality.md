Tagore lifts love far above a passing feeling and places it at the centre of everything real. Sentiments come and go, but love is the ground beneath them all — the truth that gives the world its meaning. When we love, we touch something that does not depend on circumstance or reward; it simply is, steady and enduring. The line invites us to treat love not as a decoration on life but as its foundation. If love is the ultimate truth, then living lovingly is not a soft choice but the most serious and honest way to live.
