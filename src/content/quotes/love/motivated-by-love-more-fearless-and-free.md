Fear makes us small: we protect ourselves, avoid risks, and hold back. Love does the opposite. When we act from love, whether for a person, a cause, or a community, we stop calculating the danger to ourselves and start focusing on what needs doing. That shift is liberating. A parent defending a child, a friend standing up for another, a stranger helping in a crisis: none of them feel fearless, but love carries them past fear. This quote reminds us that love is not soft; it is one of the bravest forces there is.
