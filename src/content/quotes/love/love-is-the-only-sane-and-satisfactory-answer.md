Human beings face a real problem: we are separate, aware of our aloneness, and hungry for connection. Fromm argues that love is the only answer that truly solves it, because it joins people without erasing them. Wealth, power and fame offer distraction, but they leave separateness intact. Love alone creates union while preserving individuality. Calling it the sane answer is deliberate: in a world full of anxious striving, loving well is the most rational thing a person can do. It is also the most satisfying, because it answers the deepest human need directly rather than around it.
