Feelings of love can be strong, but they rise and fall with mood, stress, and circumstance. David Wilkerson reminds us that real love is also a choice expressed in action: listening patiently, keeping promises, helping without being asked, and staying present when things are hard. Anyone can feel affection on a good day; love is proven on difficult days. When you act lovingly even when you do not feel like it, the feeling often follows. Do not wait for perfect emotions to love well. Love through small, faithful actions, and let those actions keep the relationship strong and alive.
