True love is measured not only by admiration for the other person but by what their presence brings out in us. Some people make us smaller — more guarded, more anxious, less ourselves. Others make us braver, kinder, and more honest than we are on our own. That transformation is the quiet test of a relationship. When we are with the right person, we recognise a better version of ourselves, one we could not quite reach alone. Loving someone, then, is also a kind of gratitude: for who they are, and for who they help us become.
