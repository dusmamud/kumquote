Some connections go deeper than liking or habit; they feel like recognition, as if two people were made of the same substance. This line captures that rare bond where another person understands you without long explanations, shares your way of seeing the world, and feels like a part of yourself. Such love is not built on convenience or appearance but on a deep sameness of spirit. It is why losing that person feels like losing a part of you, and why finding them feels like coming home.
