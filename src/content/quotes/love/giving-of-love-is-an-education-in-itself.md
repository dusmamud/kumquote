Loving someone teaches us things no book ever could. In giving love, we learn patience when the other person disappoints us, courage when we make ourselves vulnerable, and generosity when we put another's needs before our own. Love stretches us beyond selfishness and reveals strengths we did not know we had. Each act of giving deepens our understanding of ourselves and of other people. The education is never finished, because every relationship brings new lessons. To love is to keep learning, and the learning itself is one of love's greatest gifts.
