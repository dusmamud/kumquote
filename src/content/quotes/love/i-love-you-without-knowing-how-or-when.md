The deepest love resists explanation. We cannot always say when it began, how it grew, or why this person and no other. Trying to trace its causes feels beside the point, because love is not a decision made after weighing evidence — it is a recognition that arrives before the reasons do. That mystery is part of its power. A love that can be fully explained can also be argued away; a love that simply is, stands on its own. Neruda's lines accept this without apology: love needs no map, no timetable, and no justification.
