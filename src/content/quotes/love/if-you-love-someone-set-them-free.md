Bach turns the instinct to hold on into its opposite: real love opens the hand instead of closing it. Possession is not devotion; it is fear dressed as affection. To set someone free is to trust that love, if it is genuine, does not need chains to survive. The return is the proof, not because it was forced but because it was chosen, freely, by someone who could have gone anywhere and came back. And if they do not return, the parting is painful but honest: what was held was never truly owned.
