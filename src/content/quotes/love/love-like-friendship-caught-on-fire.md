Lee describes love as friendship with heat added. The friendship underneath supplies trust, laughter, and honest conversation, while passion gives the bond its intensity and urgency. Without friendship, desire burns out quickly, leaving little to hold two people together. Without fire, friendship stays comfortable but never becomes love. The image explains why the strongest romances often begin as close friendships: the foundation is already tested before the flame arrives. Look for a partner who is first a friend, because when the excitement cools, the friendship is what keeps the warmth alive.
