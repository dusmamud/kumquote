Six words carry one of the most demanding ideas ever spoken. Loving your neighbor as yourself means treating others with the same care, patience, and generosity you naturally give yourself. It does not ask for feeling alone but for action: feeding, forgiving, helping, and showing up. The measure is yourself, which is exactly why it is so hard; we excuse our own faults easily and judge others harshly. Reverse that habit, and the world changes. Love your neighbor as yourself, and the simplest rule becomes the hardest and most important one to live.
