Love is often imagined as rare or seasonal, something that arrives only under perfect conditions. But this line reminds us that love is always in season, always within reach, for anyone willing to offer it. It costs nothing to give, yet it nourishes whoever receives it. A kind word, a moment of attention, a small act of help: these are fruits anyone can pick, at any time. We do not need wealth or occasion to love well. We only need willing hands. Love is the one harvest that never fails, because it grows wherever it is planted.
