To everyone else, the person we love may look ordinary. But love changes the eyes that look. Mauriac says that loving someone is seeing a miracle that others cannot see — the particular courage, kindness, or light that hides inside an ordinary life. This is why love feels so personal: it is a private revelation, not a public fact. The miracle is not that the person is perfect; it is that love lets us see what is best in them. To love is to be given new sight.
