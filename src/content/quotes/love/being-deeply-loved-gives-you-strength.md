Love works in two directions, and each gives a different gift. Being deeply loved gives strength, because knowing someone believes in you helps you stand firm when life is hard. Loving deeply gives courage, because caring for another person makes you willing to face fear, sacrifice comfort, and act bravely on their behalf. Lao Tzu captures this balance beautifully. Love is not weakness or dependence; it is a source of power. When you both give and receive love fully, you become stronger and braver than you could be alone. That is why love changes people for the better.
