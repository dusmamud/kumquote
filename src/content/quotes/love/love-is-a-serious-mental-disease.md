Plato's provocative line captures how love overturns our reason. When we fall in love, our thoughts, sleep, appetite, and judgment all change, as if some pleasant fever had taken hold. We do things we would never normally do and feel things we cannot explain. Yet this madness is not a flaw but a glimpse of something beyond ordinary logic: love rearranges our priorities and shows us what truly matters. The wise response is not to resist love's intensity but to let it teach us, because the disorder it brings often reveals the deepest order of the heart.
