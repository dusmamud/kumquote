Bruno saw love not as a feeling between two people but as the force that holds the world together. Just as gravity pulls planets into orbit, love draws people toward one another — across distance, difference, and disagreement. It is the affinity that turns strangers into friends, rivals into partners, and isolated lives into a shared world. When we love, we are not escaping reality; we are participating in the very bond that keeps reality whole. To love is to join the pattern that connects everything.
