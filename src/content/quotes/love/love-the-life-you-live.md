The two sentences form a circle. Loving the life we live means accepting it — finding value in what we already have instead of always reaching for something else. Living the life we love means shaping it — making choices that bring us closer to what matters. Neither half works alone. Acceptance without action becomes resignation; action without acceptance becomes endless chasing. Together they describe a balanced life: grateful for the present and honest about what we want to change.
