Love is often described as a feeling, but feelings rise and fall, while forgiveness is a choice made again and again. Every close relationship collects small hurts, misunderstandings and disappointments, and without forgiveness those pile up until affection is buried. Endless forgiveness does not mean accepting harm without limits; it means refusing to keep a ledger of every fault. The tender look that becomes a habit is the result: kindness practised until it is natural. Love lasts not because two people never fail each other, but because they keep choosing to forgive.
