Shakespeare compresses love into a single burning image: a spirit made entirely of fire. Fire gives warmth and light, but it also consumes, spreads, and cannot be held still. Love is the same. It warms everything it touches, yet it refuses to be contained or controlled, flaring up when least expected. A spirit all compact of fire has no cool, safe part; it is passion through and through. That is why love feels both wonderful and dangerous. It lights the room, and it can burn the house down, and we welcome it anyway.
