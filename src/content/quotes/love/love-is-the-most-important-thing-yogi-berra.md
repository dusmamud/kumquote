It is a joke, and it is true. Berra puts love first, where it belongs, and admits that baseball is pretty good too. The humour works because it is honest. Life is full of things we love in different ways: people first, then the games, the songs, the pleasures that make days worth living. Love does not demand we give up everything else. It asks only to be first, the thing we protect when everything competes for our time. Keep love at the centre, and there is still room for baseball, for laughter, and for all the things that follow.
