This definition of love is funny because it is honest. Love is vast and deep like an ocean — full of joy, longing, tenderness, and occasional storms. And yes, it comes with expenses: dinners, gifts, weddings, and the countless small costs of caring for another person. But the humor hides a truth worth keeping. The expenses are not the price of love; they are the evidence of it. We spend on what we value. An ocean surrounded by expenses is still an ocean — and most of us would not trade it for a cheaper, smaller sea.
