Mother Teresa removes every excuse for withholding love. It is not a rare delicacy that appears once a year, nor a luxury reserved for the wealthy or the worthy. It is always in season and within reach — a kind word, a shared meal, a moment of attention costs nothing and is available to anyone, anywhere. If you have ever thought you had nothing to give, this quote answers you: love is the one gift that grows by being given. Reach out your hand today — the fruit is already there.
