Mother Teresa states a paradox that only makes sense once we have loved deeply. Loving until it hurts means giving beyond comfort — forgiving when it is hard, staying when it would be easier to leave, and caring without keeping score. The pain is real, yet it does not diminish love; it deepens it. Hurt that comes from loving is not the same as hurt that comes from bitterness. When love is the cause, even pain becomes a doorway to more love, because a heart stretched by giving grows larger, not smaller.
