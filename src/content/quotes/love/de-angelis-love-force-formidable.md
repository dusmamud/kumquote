We respect money, armies, and laws as the great powers of the world, yet none of them moves people the way love does. Love makes strangers care for each other, gives the frightened courage, and holds families and friendships together through hardship. It can outlast anger, survive distance, and rebuild what seemed broken beyond repair. Calling it formidable is not sentimentality; it is a plain observation of how much love actually accomplishes. When we doubt what we can endure or change, remembering love's strength reminds us of a power we already carry inside.
