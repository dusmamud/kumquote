Love is often treated like a finished thing, something we find complete and keep. Lennon's image says otherwise. Love is a flower, a living thing that needs light, water, and time, and it dies quickly when it is neglected or gripped too tightly. You cannot rush a flower into bloom by pulling at it, and you cannot keep love alive by demanding it stay the same. Growth takes patience, attention, and the willingness to let the other person change. Tend it gently, give it room, and it will surprise you with how much it can grow.
