Love cannot be grasped or kept like a possession; it only lives when it is given freely. The moment we try to hold love tightly — controlling it, guarding it, demanding it stay exactly as it is — it slips away, and we are left clutching only ourselves. Open arms mean trust: letting the other person be free, accepting them as they are, and offering affection without conditions. This kind of love is risky because it can be hurt, but it is the only kind that grows.
