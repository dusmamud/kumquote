Some loves go beyond ordinary affection. They are deeper than friendship, stronger than attraction, and larger than words can hold. Such love changes the people who feel it, binding them deeply. It is not measured by gifts or promises, but by devotion — the willingness to stand by each other through joy and sorrow alike. A love that is more than love does not fade when circumstances change, because it lives in the heart, not in conditions. It is rare, intense, and unforgettable, and those who have known it carry it forever.
