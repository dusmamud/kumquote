We are trained to notice differences: other backgrounds, other habits, other opinions. Love works in the opposite direction. Adorno says love is the power to see similarity where others see only dissimilarity — to recognise the same fear, the same hope, the same humanity in someone who seems entirely unlike us. That recognition is what turns a stranger into a friend and a rival into a partner. Love does not erase differences; it looks past them to what is shared. In that seeing, connection begins.
