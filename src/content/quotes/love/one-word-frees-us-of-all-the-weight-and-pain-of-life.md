Life can feel heavy: grief, failure, regret, and the slow accumulation of pain. We carry that weight alone until love arrives. Love does not remove the hard facts, but it changes how heavy they feel. To be loved is to be seen and accepted, and that acceptance lightens everything. One word, love, names the force that lifts the burden. This quote suggests that no philosophy or achievement frees us the way love does. When we are loved, the weight of life becomes something we can carry, and sometimes even set down.
