Love arrives looking like light, full of warmth and promise, and at first we rarely notice its shadows. The same feeling that lifts us can make us vulnerable, because caring deeply means risking loss and pain. That is the trap: love draws us in with beauty, then asks us to carry its weight. But the shadows are not a reason to run. They are part of what makes love real, since anything worth having asks something of us. The wise lover walks in with open eyes, accepting light and shadow alike, and finds the risk itself gives love depth.
