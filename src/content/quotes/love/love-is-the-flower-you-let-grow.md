Love cannot be forced open like a bud pulled apart by impatient hands; it grows in its own time, like a flower. Lennon compares love to something living that needs light, water, and patience rather than control. When we rush it, demand it, or try to shape it into what we want, we damage the very thing we are trying to keep. Real love asks for trust in the slow process of growing together. Give it room, tend it gently, and let it become what it was meant to be.
