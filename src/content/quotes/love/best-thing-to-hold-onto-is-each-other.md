Possessions, achievements, and plans can all fade or change, but human connection endures. Audrey Hepburn reminds us that what we hold onto most tightly should not be things, but people. In hard times, it is not wealth that comforts us; it is a hand to hold, a voice that listens, and a presence that stays. Love is not only romance; it includes family, friendship, and community. When life feels uncertain, turn toward the people you love and let them turn toward you. Holding onto each other is how we survive loss, celebrate joy, and remember what life is truly for.
