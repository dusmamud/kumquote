Six words, and a complete reversal of how we usually think about ourselves. We spend so much effort listing our own qualities — our work, our looks, our achievements — as if the best thing about us must be something we own. This line says the opposite: the best thing about me is not mine at all, it is you. It is the person who makes ordinary days brighter, who brings out the patience and kindness we did not know we had. Love, at its best, works this way.
