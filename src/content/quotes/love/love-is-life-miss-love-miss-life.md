A life without love can be busy, successful and comfortable, yet still feel empty, because love is what gives the rest of it meaning. Achievements fade, routines grow dull, and possessions lose their shine, but love keeps renewing ordinary days with purpose. To miss love is not simply to miss romance; it is to miss connection, warmth and the feeling of mattering to someone. That is why the quote pairs the two so plainly: love is not a decoration on life but life itself. Make room for it, and everything else finally makes sense.
