Sparks compares love to the wind because both are invisible yet impossible to deny. We cannot hold love in our hands or point to it on a shelf, but we feel its effects everywhere — in a quickened heartbeat, in patience during hard days, in the quiet urge to protect someone. Doubting love because it cannot be seen is like doubting the wind because it has no shape. Its reality is proven by what it moves. Trusting that feeling, even without evidence we can measure, is part of what makes love both mysterious and true.
