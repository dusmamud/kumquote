This is a complete guide to living well in three short commands. Love all: keep your heart open to everyone, without hatred or prejudice. Trust a few: be wise about whom you let close, because trust is precious and not everyone deserves it. Do wrong to none: whatever your feelings, harm no one. Together they balance openness with wisdom and kindness with boundaries. Shakespeare packs a whole moral philosophy into one line: be generous in love, careful in trust, and harmless in action, and your life will be both good and safe.
