Four words that strip love of all its excuses. Hugo refuses to let love be only a feeling we carry in private; if it does not move into action, it is not yet love. To love is to show up, to help, to forgive, to stay, to give time and attention when it costs something. Feelings are the seed, but action is the tree. A love that never acts is only an intention, and intentions feed no one. The test of love is simple: what did it do today.
