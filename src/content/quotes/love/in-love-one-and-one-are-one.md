Arithmetic says one and one make two, but love follows a different kind of math. Two people remain themselves, yet in love they become something single: a shared life, a shared purpose, a "we" that is more than the sum of its parts. This oneness does not erase either person; it joins them without removing what makes each one distinct. That is the quiet miracle of love. It does not add people together like numbers on a page. It blends two separate lives into one story, where each person's joy and pain belong to both.
