It is tempting to guard love the way we guard something fragile, keeping it safe by keeping it small. But love kept under lock and key slowly starves, because it needs openness to stay alive. To love someone is to accept that you might be hurt, misunderstood, or left behind, and to choose them anyway. That risk is not a flaw in love; it is what makes love meaningful. A heart that never risks anything never truly connects. The bravest thing we do is let someone matter enough that losing them would actually hurt.
