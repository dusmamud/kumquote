Water is not a luxury; it is what keeps a living thing alive, and Rumi says love plays the same role in a human life. We can survive for a while on routine, ambition, and distraction, but without love we dry out inside, growing brittle and numb. The invitation here is not to sip cautiously but to drink deeply, with heart and soul. Love is meant to be taken in, not measured out in small doses. A life soaked in love stays supple and awake, while a life without it hardens no matter how comfortable it looks.
