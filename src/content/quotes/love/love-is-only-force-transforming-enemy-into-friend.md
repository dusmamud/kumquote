Hatred and revenge may win a battle, but they rarely end a conflict. They create new wounds and new enemies. Martin Luther King Jr. understood that only love can truly transform a relationship, because love changes how we see the other person. When we choose to listen, understand, and forgive, an enemy can become a human being with fears and hopes like our own. Love is not soft or naive. It is the strongest force for lasting peace, because it heals what force alone cannot.
