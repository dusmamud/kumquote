Life can feel heavy and grey, and we often look for energy in the wrong places: caffeine, noise, distraction. Browning points to a simpler source. Love is the energy of life, the current that runs underneath everything worth doing. When we love someone, or something, we find strength we did not know we had. The early mornings get easier, the hard work feels lighter, and even ordinary days take on colour. Love does not remove the difficulty; it gives us the energy to meet it. Fill a life with love, and you will rarely find it running on empty.
