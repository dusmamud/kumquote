Happiness comes and goes, but love is measured by what you are willing to give up for another person. A feeling can fade on a bad day, yet love remains when someone gives their time, comfort, or pride for another's sake. Sacrifice sounds heavy, but in practice it is often small: listening when you are tired, apologising first, or putting another need ahead of your own. These choices prove love is real: they cost something. Anyone can enjoy love when it is easy. Loving means staying generous when it is not, and that willingness turns affection into trust.
