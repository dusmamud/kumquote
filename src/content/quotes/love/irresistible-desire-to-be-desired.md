Frost distils love to its most human core: not the wish to possess, but the longing to be wanted in return. Love is rarely one-sided for long; it seeks an answer, a mirror, a mutual recognition. To desire someone is only half the feeling. The deeper half is the hope that they desire you just as fiercely. This is why unreturned love aches so sharply and why mutual love feels so complete. At its heart, love is a conversation of longing, each side hoping to be chosen as surely as it chooses.
