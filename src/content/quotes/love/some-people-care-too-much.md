Caring too much is often treated as a flaw — a sign of being soft, naive, or easily hurt. Winnie-the-Pooh's simple wisdom turns that judgement around. The people who care too much are the ones who notice when someone is struggling, who remember the small things, and who show up without being asked. That excess of care is not weakness; it is the very substance of love. A world of people who cared exactly the right amount would be a colder place. The ones who care too much are the ones who keep it warm.
