Young love does not pause to weigh right and wrong; it rushes forward. This line captures that innocence, and its danger, in a single image. Love in its early days is too young to know conscience, too full of feeling to stop and ask whether it should. That is part of its charm and part of its risk. The heart leads, and reason arrives later, too late. Growing older teaches love to listen to conscience as well as feeling. But there is something honest about love before it learns caution, a reminder of how completely the heart can believe.
