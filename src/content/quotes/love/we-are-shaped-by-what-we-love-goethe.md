Goethe warns that love is not only something you feel — it is something that remakes you. What you love decides what you spend your time on, what you forgive, what you become. Love something small or cruel and it shapes you downward just as surely. We choose our loves more than we admit, and our loves then choose who we become. So the question is not only whom or what you love, but what that love is making of you. Choose your loves carefully: they are carving you even now.
