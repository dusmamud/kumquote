True devotion gathers a whole life into a single focus: one person who becomes the heart, the life, and the constant thought. This is not about losing yourself but about finding the centre around which everything else makes sense. When someone holds this place, ordinary days gain colour, decisions gain a quiet purpose, and even absence is felt keenly. Such love is total, yet it does not imprison; it gives the beloved the freedom of knowing they are deeply, steadily loved.
