Everything we gather in life eventually has to be left behind: possessions, titles, plans and even places. Love is different, because it is not something we own but something that becomes part of us, carried in memory, character and the way we treat others. At the end, what comforts is not what we achieved but whom we loved and who loved us. That is why love makes even a difficult ending easier: it is the one thing that cannot be taken away. Invest in it while there is time, because it is the only luggage that travels with us.
