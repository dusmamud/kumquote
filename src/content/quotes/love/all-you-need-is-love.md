Four words, and a whole philosophy. Lennon was not claiming that love pays the rent or solves every problem; he was pointing at what remains when everything else is stripped away. Wealth, status, and success can all be lost, yet a life held together by love still stands. Love is the need beneath every other need — the reason we seek connection, build families, and keep going through hard times. The line endures because it is both simple and radical: it insists that the one essential thing cannot be bought. Everything else is extra. Love is the foundation.
