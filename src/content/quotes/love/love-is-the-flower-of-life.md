Love does not follow our schedules or obey our plans. It arrives unexpectedly, in ordinary moments, and blossoms without asking permission or following any law we set for it. That wildness is part of its beauty. We cannot force love to appear, and we cannot command it to stay, yet when it comes it colours everything around it. The wisest response is not to control love but to welcome it when it arrives and to care for it while it lasts. Like a flower, it grows best where it is free, noticed, and gently tended.
