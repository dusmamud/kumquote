Faith, hope, and love are the three things that remain when everything else passes, and Paul places love above them all. Faith looks forward, hope waits patiently, but love acts now, in the present, toward real people. Gifts, knowledge, and achievements fade with time, while love endures because it is the one thing that connects us to each other and to what is good. To choose love is to choose what lasts. When all else is stripped away, faith and hope point toward love, and love is the greatest because it is the reason the other two exist.
