Real love does not shrink either person. It expands both. When two people truly love each other, each one grows to include the other's dreams, fears, and hopes, and neither loses themselves in the process. You become larger, not smaller — more patient, more curious, more alive — because the other person's world has become part of yours. Love that demands shrinking is not love at all; it is control wearing love's name. The truest sign of love is growth: two natures, each enriched, each still themselves, and both more than they were alone.
