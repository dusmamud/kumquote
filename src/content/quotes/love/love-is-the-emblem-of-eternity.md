Madame de Stael gives love a scale beyond ordinary measurement. Time counts our days in hours and years, but love refuses that accounting. A single moment with the beloved can feel endless, and years apart can pass like a breath, because love lives outside the clock. To call love the emblem of eternity is to say that it touches something in us that does not age, expire, or obey calendars. This is why grief for a lost love feels so strange: the feeling remains present even when the person is gone.
