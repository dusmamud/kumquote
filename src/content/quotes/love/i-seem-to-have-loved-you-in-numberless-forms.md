Some love feels older than a single lifetime, as if it has returned again and again across ages and forms. This line gives voice to that feeling: love so deep that one life cannot hold it. Whether taken literally or as poetry, it speaks of a bond that time cannot wear out. Real love does not feel new and fragile; it feels known, steady, and endless. It is the sense of having found, in another person, something the soul has always recognised. Such love does not count its days, because it believes it has forever.
