Losing love hurts, and the pain can make us wish we had never loved at all. But a life without love is colder and smaller than a life that loved and lost. Love, even when it ends, leaves behind warmth: memories, lessons, and a heart that knows how to feel deeply. The person who never loves stays safe, but also stays untouched by one of life's greatest gifts. Grief is the price of having loved, and most hearts, given the choice again, would still pay it. To have loved is always better than never to have loved.
