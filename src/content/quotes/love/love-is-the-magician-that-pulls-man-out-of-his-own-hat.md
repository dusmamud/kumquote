Love works like a magic trick, except the person pulled out of the hat is you — a better version of yourself you did not know was there. Before love, we are often trapped in our own habits, fears, and narrow routines. Love reaches in and lifts us out, showing us that we can be braver, kinder, and more alive than we thought. It does not change us by force; it changes us by making us want to be worthy of the person who loves us.
