Love cannot be explained the way a fact can. It is felt in the senses — in the sound of a familiar voice, the sight of a face across a room, the warmth of a hand held without a word. Poetry works the same way: it does not argue, it moves you. That is why love resists definitions and spreadsheets of reasons. It arrives as an experience, not a conclusion, and it is understood best by living it rather than analysing it. Treat love as poetry, not as a problem to solve, and its beauty will speak for itself.
