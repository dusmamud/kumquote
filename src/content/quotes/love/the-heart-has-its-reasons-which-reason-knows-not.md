Reason is a wonderful tool, but it cannot explain everything, and love is its favorite exception. We often love people for reasons we cannot fully name, and attempts to justify love with logic usually miss the point. The heart notices things reason overlooks: a tone of voice, a shared silence, a small kindness. This quote does not dismiss reason; it simply admits its limits. Some of the truest things we know are known by the heart first. Love belongs to that knowledge, and it needs no permission from logic to be real.
