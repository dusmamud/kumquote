Goethe draws a sharp line between two kinds of love. Dominating love controls — it decides, restricts, and calls the control care. Cultivating love does the opposite: it tends, waters, and makes room for the other person to grow into themselves. The difference shows in how the loved one feels — smaller and watched, or freer and stronger. Real love never needs to shrink someone to feel safe. If your love is making the other person smaller, it has drifted toward control. Tend the garden instead: trust, patience, and space will grow far more than any fence ever could.
