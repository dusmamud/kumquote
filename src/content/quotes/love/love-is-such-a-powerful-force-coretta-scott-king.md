We reserve love for the people closest to us, as if it were a resource that runs out when shared. Coretta Scott King widens the circle. Love is a powerful force, there for everyone to embrace, an unconditional love for all humankind. This is not a soft idea; it is the force that sustained a movement through danger and loss. Love for humanity asks us to see strangers as neighbours and enemies as people who can change. It is the hardest kind of love, and the most necessary. Embrace it, and it changes us, and the world we live in.
