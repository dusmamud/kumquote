Real love does not change when circumstances change. Shakespeare's famous sonnet defines love as a constant force: it does not fade when beauty fades, when fortune turns, or when the beloved changes. If love disappears the moment things get difficult, it was never love but infatuation or convenience. True love stands firm through time, illness, distance, and hardship, because it is rooted in the person, not in conditions. This is why the sonnet has endured for centuries: it sets the highest standard for love, and invites us to measure our own by it.
