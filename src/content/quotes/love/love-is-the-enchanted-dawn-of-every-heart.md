Dawn does not change the landscape; it changes the light, and suddenly everything familiar looks new. Love works the same way in the heart. The world is the same world, but it is lit differently — colours are brighter, ordinary moments carry weight, and the future feels open. That is why love feels like an awakening rather than an addition: it does not give us a new life, it reveals the life we already had. Lamartine's image also carries a warning, gentle as dawn itself: like daylight, love must be lived in while it lasts.
