Some loves begin long before the lovers meet. From the first story heard, the heart starts searching for someone it has not yet found, guided by a feeling it cannot explain. Rumi calls that search blind, because we do not know who we are looking for, only that we are looking. Love stories plant a longing that ordinary life cannot satisfy, and the search itself becomes part of the journey. Whether the beloved is found or not, the looking changes us. The heart knew what it wanted before the eyes ever saw it.
