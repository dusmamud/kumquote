Among all the treasures life can offer, love stands alone. Wealth fades, fame passes, and plans change, but having loved — truly loved — is enough to make a life rich. We often ask life for more: more success, more comfort, more certainty. Yet the heart knows a simpler truth. To have loved someone deeply is the pearl hidden in the folds of ordinary days, the one thing that gives everything else its shine. Ask nothing further than love, and you will find you already hold the greatest treasure of all.
