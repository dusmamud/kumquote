In love, as in every close relationship, it is tempting to raise your voice when you feel unheard — but volume never grows anything. Thunder is loud and frightening, yet it is the quiet rain that feeds the flowers. Gentle, honest words land softly and take root; shouting only makes people close themselves off. Raising your words means choosing better words — clearer, kinder, more truthful — instead of louder ones. Love grows in the climate of patience, not pressure. Speak softly, mean what you say, and let your words do the quiet work of rain.
