This is love stated as the one certainty in an uncertain world. You may doubt the stars, doubt the sun, doubt even truth itself — but never doubt this love. It is a promise that stands firmer than the facts of nature, because it comes from the heart rather than from the senses. Real devotion does not waver with moods or circumstances; it stays steady when everything else is questioned. That is what makes love trustworthy. When someone loves you like this, you can lean on it. Doubt the world if you must, but never doubt a love like this.
