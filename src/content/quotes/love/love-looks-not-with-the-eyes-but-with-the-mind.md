We think we fall in love with what we see, but attraction runs deeper than appearance. Love sees with the mind: it notices character, humor, kindness, and the way a person thinks. That is why love can seem blind to outsiders; it is looking at things the eyes cannot measure. Beauty fades and circumstances change, but the qualities the mind loves endure. This quote reminds us that lasting love is built on understanding, not appearance. When we love with the mind as well as the eyes, the feeling has roots that can survive time.
