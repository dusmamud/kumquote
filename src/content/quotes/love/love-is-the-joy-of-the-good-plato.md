Plato gives love the highest praise he can: it delights the good, astonishes the wise, and would amaze even the gods. The good find joy in it because love is goodness made personal; the wise find wonder in it because love keeps exceeding every theory about it. Even beings who have seen everything would stop and stare, because love is the one force that never becomes ordinary. Treat it as the great matter it is, and let it keep amazing you.
