Almost everyone experiences love at some point — a crush, a romance, a deep attachment. Far fewer truly enjoy it, because enjoying love takes more than feeling it. It takes patience when the excitement fades, honesty when things get hard, and the willingness to stay when leaving would be easier. Many people experience love and lose it through neglect or fear. The few who enjoy it are the ones who tend it like a garden: daily, quietly, and with care. Feeling love is common. Keeping it is the rare art.
