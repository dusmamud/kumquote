Bradbury makes a bold claim: love is not one answer among many, but the answer to everything. Work done without love becomes drudgery, and success without love feels empty when it finally arrives. Love gives action its reason, turning routine into purpose and effort into joy. It is the only reason to do anything, because anything done without it loses its meaning along the way. This does not mean every task must feel romantic; it means the best work flows from caring deeply. Do what you love, love what you do, and the doing itself becomes enough.
