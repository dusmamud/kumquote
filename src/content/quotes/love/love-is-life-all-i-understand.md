Tolstoy spent a lifetime asking what life means, and his answer came down to this: love is not a part of life; it is life itself. We understand the world not by analyzing it from a distance but by loving within it — our family, our work, our neighbors, the beauty around us. Without love, knowledge stays cold and abstract. With love, even ordinary things gain meaning. If we want to understand life more deeply, the path is not more information. It is more love. Love first, and understanding follows.
