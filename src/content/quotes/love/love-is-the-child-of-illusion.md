Love often begins with an idealised picture of another person, a beautiful illusion that draws two people together. But no real person can live up to a perfect image forever, and when the illusion fades, disappointment follows. This is not a reason to give up on love; it is an invitation to love more honestly. The second stage of love is deeper because it is chosen with open eyes. When we accept someone as they truly are, flaws included, affection stops depending on fantasy and becomes something steadier, kinder, and far more real.
