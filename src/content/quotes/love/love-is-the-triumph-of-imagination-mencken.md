Mencken, the great sceptic, admits that love beats logic — and says so with a grin. Intelligence weighs the risks, counts the flaws, and advises caution; imagination sees the person as they could be, the life that might be built, the joy waiting on the far side of reason. In love, imagination wins the argument, and that is not a failure of thinking but a different kind of knowing. Love asks you to imagine more than the evidence supports, and then to build toward that imagined thing. The triumph is real, and it is worth the risk.
