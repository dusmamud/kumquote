We spend much of our lives feeling like pieces: a role here, a fragment there, always a little incomplete. Rumi says love is the whole thing, and we are only pieces of it. The love we feel for one person is not separate from the love that holds the world together; it is the same love, seen through a single window. When we love deeply, we touch something larger than ourselves, a wholeness that was there all along. We are not building love from nothing. We are remembering it, piece by piece, until the whole comes back into view.
