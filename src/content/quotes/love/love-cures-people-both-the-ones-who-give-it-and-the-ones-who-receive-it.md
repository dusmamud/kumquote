Love has a healing power that medicine alone cannot provide. For the person who receives it, love brings comfort, safety, and hope in times of pain. It reminds them they are not alone and that they matter. For the person who gives it, love also heals — it softens bitterness, gives purpose, and fills the heart with meaning. Giving love is not a loss; it is a cure for loneliness, anger, and emptiness. When you love someone, you help them heal, and in the same act, you heal yourself. Love is medicine that works both ways.
