True love fills the mind so completely that thoughts of the beloved never stop coming. This image turns each thought into a flower and each flower into a garden without end, showing how love can make ordinary life bloom. It is a tender way of saying: you are always on my mind, and that constancy is itself the proof of love. Real affection is not a single grand moment but a thousand small thoughts, returning again and again. When someone lives in your thoughts like flowers in a garden, that is love in its quietest, most faithful form.
