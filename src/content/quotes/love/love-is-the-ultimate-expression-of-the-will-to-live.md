The will to live is usually imagined as survival, but Wolfe says its fullest form is love. To love is to reach beyond mere survival toward connection, creation, and joy — the things that make life worth the effort of living it. A person who loves is saying yes to the world: yes to another person, yes to the future, yes to being part of something larger than the self. Love is not a decoration on life; it is life at its most alive. Where love is present, the will to live has found its purpose.
