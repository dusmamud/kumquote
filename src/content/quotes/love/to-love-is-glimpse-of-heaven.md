Heaven is often imagined as somewhere far away, but love brings a glimpse of it into ordinary life. In a moment of real love, time slows, selfishness loosens its grip, and another person's happiness matters as much as your own. That glimpse does not last forever, and it was never meant to: its purpose is to show what life can feel like at its best, so we keep reaching for it. Even a brief taste changes how we see everything else. Love, in this sense, is not an escape from life but a preview of its highest possibility.
