We spend years learning skills, collecting knowledge, and chasing achievements, yet Ahbez claims the greatest lesson of all is simpler: to love and to be loved. It is a lesson no school teaches and no exam measures, but it shapes a life more than any other. Loving asks for openness and courage; being loved asks for trust and the willingness to receive. Both are harder than they sound. In the end, what we remember most is not what we owned or won, but who loved us and whom we loved in return.
