Keller, who could not see the flower or hear the garden, describes love through what it gives rather than what it looks like. Some loves cannot be held or possessed — distance, circumstance, or respect keeps them just out of reach. Yet their presence still fills the whole place, like a scent you cannot grasp but cannot miss either. You do not need to touch a thing to be glad it exists. Sometimes the finest love is simply knowing the flower is there, and letting its fragrance make your whole garden better.
