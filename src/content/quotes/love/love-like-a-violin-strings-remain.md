Love is not always loud. There are quiet seasons — distance, disagreement, exhaustion — when the music seems to stop. That silence can feel like the end, but it is not. The strings remain: the shared history, the trust, the deep knowledge of each other. A violin does not stop being a violin when it is not being played. And when the music returns, it is richer for the pause. Real love survives the silent stretches because it is built into the instrument itself. The music may stop. The strings remain forever.
