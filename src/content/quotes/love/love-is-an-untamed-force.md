Love cannot be managed the way we manage a schedule. The harder we try to control it, the more it fights back, because love grows from freedom. Trying to imprison it, by demanding proof or keeping someone close from fear, only turns affection into resentment. Trying to fully understand it leaves us confused, since love follows no neat logic. The wiser response is to respect its nature: to give it room, to trust it, and to let it change us. Love asks for courage rather than control, and those who accept it find a force that reshapes life.
