Falling in love can feel like a force of nature, as unstoppable as gravity itself. But this playful line reminds us that love is not an accident of physics. Gravity may pull objects together, but it cannot choose who we love or how we love them. The choice, the courage, and the care are ours. Love feels effortless when it arrives, yet keeping it alive takes real effort and real decisions. So enjoy the feeling of falling, but remember that love is not something that merely happens to you. It is something you choose, again and again.
