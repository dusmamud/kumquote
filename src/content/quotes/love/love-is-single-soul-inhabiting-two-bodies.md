True love creates a closeness that goes beyond words or shared routines. Aristotle describes it as two people becoming so deeply connected that they feel like one soul in two bodies. That does not mean losing yourself; it means finding someone whose joys feel like your joys and whose pain feels like your pain. Such love grows from trust, honesty, and time spent truly knowing each other. It is not built in a moment of excitement, but in countless small acts of care. When love reaches this depth, separation feels incomplete, and togetherness feels like coming home.
