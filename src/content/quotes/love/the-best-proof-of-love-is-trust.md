Words are easy, and gifts are easier still. Anyone can say they love you, but trust is the proof that cannot be faked for long. Trust means believing someone with your fears, your failures, and your future, and knowing they will handle them gently. It is built slowly, in small kept promises, and it is broken quickly, in one careless betrayal. Where trust grows, love grows with it; where trust dies, love becomes only a word. If you want to know whether love is real, do not listen to the promises. Watch the trust.
