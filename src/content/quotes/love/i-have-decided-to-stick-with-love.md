Hate feels powerful at first. It gives us energy, a target, and a sense of being right. But it is a heavy load to carry. It demands constant feeding, it colours every thought, and it slowly exhausts the person who holds it. Love, by contrast, is lighter — not because it is easy, but because it does not consume the one who practises it. Choosing love is therefore not only a moral decision but a practical one: it frees us from a burden that would otherwise shape our whole life.
