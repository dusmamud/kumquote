In a single image, Shakespeare captures how love begins in the body before the mind can name it: sighs rising like smoke from a fire we cannot see. Love is not presented here as calm or reasonable, but as something that escapes us, drifts upward, and fills the air. The sigh is the smallest sign of longing, and from many sighs a whole atmosphere forms. That is often how love arrives — not with a declaration but with restlessness and a feeling we cannot quite hold. The line reminds us that love is felt first and understood only later.
