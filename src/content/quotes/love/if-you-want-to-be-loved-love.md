It is easy to wait for love to find us, to sit back and wonder why nobody reaches out first. Seneca cuts through that passivity with a simple rule: the way to receive love is to give it. People are drawn to warmth, generosity, and genuine attention, and they naturally return what they are offered. This is not a bargain or a trick; it is how human connection works. When we love first, without demanding a guarantee, we create the very thing we were waiting for. The shortest path to being loved is to become a loving person yourself.
