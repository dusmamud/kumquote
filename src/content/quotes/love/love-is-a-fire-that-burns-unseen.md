Some fires announce themselves with smoke and flame, but love often burns quietly, out of sight, even from the person who carries it. Camoes describes a feeling that consumes from within while leaving the surface calm, a heat that others cannot see and the lover cannot always name. This unseen burning is love at its most private: the ache of missing someone, the warmth of a memory, the slow glow of devotion that needs no audience. Because it is invisible, it is easily underestimated, by others and by ourselves. Yet unseen does not mean unfelt.
