Every real relationship meets obstacles, and that is not a sign that the love is wrong. Shakespeare knew that misunderstandings, distance, family objections, and plain bad timing are part of love's story, not proof of its failure. Couples who expect a smooth road are shaken by the first bump, while those who expect difficulty meet it with patience and humour. The bumps test love and, when handled with care, strengthen it. A love that has survived rough ground is deeper than one never tested. Smooth seas never made skilled sailors, and easy love never made a lasting bond.
